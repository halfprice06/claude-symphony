# EastWest Hollywood Orchestra (Opus Edition): reference material

> **Not in this repository.** The manuals (`pdfs/`, `text/`), the saved web pages (`web/`) and the YouTube transcripts (`youtube/`) belong to their authors and were removed from the repository and its history. This page stays as the list of what was read and where to find it (manuals: `media.soundsonline.com`; the web pages and videos by the titles and links below). `DIGEST.md` is Claude's own summary of them.

Manuals, reviews, forum threads and YouTube transcripts about EastWest's Hollywood Orchestra Opus Edition, the Opus engine and Hollywood Orchestrator. Collected 2026-10-10.

## Manuals (`pdfs/`, text in `text/`)

| File | What it is | Version |
|---|---|---|
| `EW-Hollywood-Orchestra-Opus-Edition-User-Manual.pdf` | Library manual: instruments, articulations, moods, Hollywood Orchestrator (119 pp) | Dec 2025 |
| `EW-Opus-Software-Manual.pdf` | Opus engine: browser, Play page, mixer, MIDI tools, keyswitch editor, automation (138 pp) | Oct 2025 |
| `EW-Libraries-Getting-Started-HD.pdf` | Installation Center, authorization, first load (16 pp) | May 2024 |
| `EW-Hollywood-Orchestra-Opus-Edition-User-Manual-2021.pdf` | Earlier combined edition (engine and library, 228 pp), added 2026-11-06 from Dan. Much more per-instrument detail than the 2025 manuals: velocity, expression (CC11) and vibrato (CC1) behaviour, mic positions, releases, and the Hollywood Solo Violin and Solo Cello libraries (sections 3.5-3.6). Where it disagrees with the 2025 manuals, trust the 2025 ones and the patch files. | May 2021 |

All three come from `media.soundsonline.com`. Older versions (2021 and 2023) are on retailer sites but were left out. In those versions the engine and library documentation were combined in one manual. `text/` has `pdftotext -layout` output for searching.

## Web pages (`web/`)

- `sos-review-ho-opus.md`: Dave Stewart, Sound on Sound review, July 2021. Covers the new instruments, Opus and Orchestrator in detail.
- `music-connection-ho-opus.md`: Barry Rudolph, Music Connection "New Toys" review, Aug 2021.
- `vi-control-groove3-course-review.md`: a buyer's video-by-video review of the Groove3 course.
- `vi-control-orchestrator-walkthrough-thread.md`: an Orchestrator discussion (Arranger Mode, comparison with Sonuscore's The Orchestra, MIDI export). Only the on-topic posts are kept.
- `steinberg-forum-hollywood-orchestrator.md`: using Orchestrator in Cubase.
- `musehub-groove3-course-outline.md`: Eli Krantzberg's "Hollywood Orchestra Opus Edition Explained" (owned on MuseHub; the videos are not included here).
- `routledge-guide-to-midi-orchestration.md`, `routledge-acoustic-midi-orchestration.md`: publisher descriptions of the two general books on orchestrating with samples (Gilreath; Pejrolo and DeRosa). The books are not included.

The order of the EastWest videos for learning: Product Quickstart Guide, then the Opus Engine Walkthrough, the HO Opus Edition Walkthrough, the Hollywood Orchestrator Walkthrough, Composing with Hollywood Orchestrator, and the 2026 Update. The EastWest Academy series (1–7) covers orchestration, MIDI programming and reverb in general terms using EW libraries. The Scoring With Opus series shows full cues built from start to finish.

Not collected: "Know the EastWest Opus Engine - Really Well (Part 1)" by SecondTierSound (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3HEpekcrPkc) and "3 minute Hollywood Orchestrator Preset Guide" by Soundbed Videos (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h_vUyFMQxTI). YouTube rate-limited both caption downloads.
