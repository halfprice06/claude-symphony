# Manual reading plan for the Finale's critic cycles 1-20

Dan (2026-11-06, for the mockup loops): use the library manual in depth. Each critic reads the pages in its row of the May 2021 Hollywood Orchestra Opus manual in full (`guides/eastwest-hollywood-orchestra/text/EW-Hollywood-Orchestra-Opus-Edition-User-Manual-2021.txt`; pages with tables or pictures as images from `../pdfs/` with `pdftoppm -f N -l N -r 110 -png`). Page numbers are PDF pages. Where the 2021 text and the 2025 manuals disagree, the 2025 manuals and the installed patch files win; `guides/eastwest-hollywood-orchestra/DIGEST.md` section 11 lists the known conflicts, and `loop/MANUAL-GAPS.md` lists what the manual offers that the mockup does not use.

Movement I's critics read the same pages in its cycles 36-47 (`symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/loop/MANUAL-READING.md`; their "Manual notes" tables are in `symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/cycles/c36`-`c47/reports/`). Read the Movement I report for your row's pages first, then check each note against the Finale: the tools carry Movement I's answers, so say which apply here, which do not, and what the Finale needs that Movement I did not (percussion, mutes, harp glissando, hammered repeats, many timpani rolls).

Slots: A = articulation and phrasing; B = balance and levels; C rotates with the cycle (workflow.js): blend and space (c1, 5, 9, 13, 17), dynamics over time (c2, 6, 10, 14, 18), tempo, timing and transitions (c3, 7, 11, 15, 19), holistic (c4, 8, 12, 16, 20).

Skipped as not relevant to this mockup: 1-9, 12-31, 35-42, 47-48, 55-60, 65-67 (front matter, installation, browser, menus, presets), 75-81 (Solo Violin), 82-88 (Solo Cello: not installed, no solo cello in the Finale), 118-120 (tam-tam, suspended cymbal: not in the Finale), 127-132 (tambourines, metals, woods), 152-153, 156-157, 163-187, 133, 193, 201, 205-207, 209-218, 224-228.

## Second round, cycles 7-12

Cycles 7-12 re-read the first round's A and B rows, shifted by six cycles (c7 = c1 rows, c8 = c2, ..., c12 = c6; same slot, same pages). C re-reads the first-round row of its own lens: c7 (tempo) the c3 C row, c8 (holistic) the c4 C row, c9 (blend) the c1 C row, c10 (dynamics) the c2 C row; c11 (tempo) and c12 (holistic) pick their own pages. On a second reading, open the first-round report for that row (cycles/cNN/reports/<slot>.md, its "Manual notes" table) and check each note against the current render: which are now followed, which are not, and why. Add new notes only for what the first reading missed. Put MANUAL-GAPS.md items still open ahead of everything else.

## Third round, cycles 13-20

No fixed pages. Each critic picks the pages behind its lens's top open problem (DIGEST.md gives the page for each instrument and controller), reads them in full, and lists them in its "Manual notes" table.
