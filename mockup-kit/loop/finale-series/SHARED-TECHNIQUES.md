# Finale techniques for the other movements (sent 2026-11-09 after c09; update after each cycle)

Measured on the Finale (symphony/mvt3/reaper-v600). Details are in cycles/c07-c09/CHANGES.md and loop/LEARNINGS.md; the code is in tools/score_to_parts.py and tools/build_reaper.py.

1. Dry hall within Dan's "too wet" limit:
   - Spaces II irSetup on every return: envelopeMode 2, endPos 0.5, decayLength 0.5 (tools/fxchunk.py, with read-back).
   - Sends -10 dB on violins, violas, celli, horns and trumpets; upper winds -7 dB.
   - No sends on basses, trombones, tuba, bassoons or contrabassoon.
   - Return faders -1.97 / -1.97 / -1.97 / -2.34 dB; R Strings high-pass at 160 Hz.
   - Result: tutti stops fall 20 dB in about 0.39 s, RT60 1.26 s, returns about 13 dB under the dry sum.
2. sfp / fp:
   - The crescendo printed on the same onset ("sfp <") was overwriting the spike, so every led note started on the p layer and swelled into the sf.
   - FP_SPIKE_AT_MARK writes the sf value at the moved note-on. SFP_SPIKE_S = FP_SPIKE_S = (0.10, 0.30); FP_VEL 96 (fp notes had played velocity 53).
   - Each part whose p is less than 10 dB under its ff gets a p row in roles.json of (10 minus the measured drop), up to -8 dB; the stem fix went deeper where needed (flutes -12).
   - Judge each part's own drop, not the master's.
   - Result: master drop at the sfp 6.2 → 10.5 dB; each part's drop 10-17 dB.
3. sub. p:
   - PHRASE_SHAPE type "drop": no positive offset on a phrase that starts on a subito or on a drop of 6 CC or more.
   - HELD_DECRESC_CARRY keeps a held decrescendo's level through the subito.
   - -4 dB roles on fresh brass entries at the subito.
   - With post-fader returns, a volume-envelope step drops the dry signal while the hall keeps ringing (BRIEF S3 is satisfied).
   - Result: master drop 1.7 → 4.2 dB.
4. Low-end stereo width:
   - Side-only EQ: a 60 Hz -3 dB low shelf plus a 180 Hz -5.5 dB bell (1.5 octaves), on the 12 low tracks, the 4 returns and the harp.
   - A deeper side cut over-monos: 400 Hz -8 dB gave 0.72 below 250 Hz.
   - Result: correlation below 250 Hz 0.54, 250 Hz-5 kHz 0.56, side/mid -5.6 dB (references 0.54-0.62 / 0.47-0.56 / about -5).
5. Brass seating:
   - New mix.json key track_width (REAPER track width, settable with --mix-only, no Opus rebuild).
   - Trumpets width 0.5 at pan +0.30 / +0.33 / +0.36; trombones width 0.6 at +0.26 / +0.30 / +0.34.
   - The muted Trumpet 1 then sits with the cor anglais it doubles.
6. "Mushy" low mids:
   - Horn family bell 315 Hz -5 dB (1 octave) with horns +1.0 dB; violas 315 Hz -3 dB with +0.8 dB.
   - Result: 315 Hz excess over the recordings 5.8 → 4.3 dB. Part of what is left is horn fundamentals, which EQ should not chase.
7. Final chord, FINAL_RING: the last chord's notes ring 0.45 s past the written end under a falling volume shape (-8 / -16 / -24 dB).
   - Result: drop at +0.3 s 21.9 → 15.3 dB; -20 dB at 0.42 s (recordings 0.43-0.55 s).
8. Metric accents in ostinatos:
   - Reset the round robin with CC16 just before each ostinato span. This sets which bow stroke comes first, so the strong stroke lands on the beat.
   - Then a per-span level table (start: down +1.5, mid +1.0, off -2.5), refit after the render.
   - In 2/2 the half bar is a strong beat.
   - Result: downbeat minus off-beat +3.0 to +3.8 dB in every span.
9. Harp: HARP_VEL_MAX 123. Velocity 124 and up triggers the Nail Pick layer (HO manual p.69); an ff chord there was 10 dB over everything.
10. Measurement: the brass-accent "2-5 kHz jump" counts entries after rests, not brightness. Use the tilt jump (2-5 kHz minus 0.5-2 kHz) or accents where brass is already sounding.
11. Tone of the template EQ:
    - brass 821 Hz bell -4 plus a 3.2 kHz -4 dB bell;
    - strings 1 kHz -2.5;
    - winds 1 kHz -1.55;
    - make-up gains to keep the level.
    - Result: 500 Hz-1 kHz and 2-8 kHz within about 1 dB of the references.
12. Basses' template high-pass 53 → 35 Hz (adds about 2.3 dB at 50 Hz).

## Added after c11 (2026-11-09)
18. Repeated low-string eighths: measure them at the fundamental (30-120 Hz). The basses hold most of that band but only 6-10 % of their harmonic band in a tutti. If the strokes before the beats are weak (on HO Basses Sus MAX the up-bow off-beat swells through its note-off), add a volume dip that lies only in the played gap before each beat (-9 dB, from note-off + 6 ms to next onset - 3 ms) and start the downbeat's metric step at the onset. Result: on-beat dips 2.3-10.4 -> 8.2-18.9 dB on the stem and 4.9-8.8 -> 7.8-10.6 dB on the master, off-beats and level unchanged.
19. sf of an sfp on winds and brass: the strings' post-fader bump (item 13) works there too. +3 dB over 0.35 qn (+4 on the weakest front) put the first 0.1 s within -1.6..+1.9 dB of the ff before (was -2.8..-4.5) and four of five peaks at +0.00..+0.09 s (was +0.17..+0.28). It needs no new row and no memory, so try it before the Sus Accent row.
20. Legato lead tables: a negative per-onset row must be added to the total lead (base + velocity extras) before the clamp. Clamping the base first hid most of the row (-0.07 s realised -0.03 s); after the fix the early espressivo joins went from -53..-128 ms to -24..+19 ms.
21. Chord repeats in strings: a tenuto chord repeat inside one slur (louré) and one that starts a new slur need different gaps. Check slur starts in the event list (`slur -1` = start). Here 0.06 s louré and 0.12 s at the new bow gave dips of 6.2 and 12.5 dB (from 5.1 and 8.5).
22. Note trims ramp in over 0.6 s, so on eighths they barely act (+3 dB realised +0.5). Use a level shape over the run instead.
23. Delivery ride after a subito: hold the row at 0 through the subito bar and ramp it in over about 10 qn. Starting it at the mark halved the f -> p step in the mp3 (6.5 dB, master 9.0; now 8.6), and a 6 qn ramp made the next bar louder than the subito bar. Simulate with a parts.json copy next to mix.json.

## Added after c12 (2026-11-09)
24. Same-pitch string repeats that must take a new bow at any dynamic: a "force" list of onsets in the new-bow rule (`STR_REP_NEW_BOW["plain"]["force"] = {track: [onsets]}`; acts under a slur and inside detach spans; an onset that cannot take it prints a WARNING) gives a 74-90 ms data gap. It is level-only, so it can go in by stem fix after the render.

Measuring slurred joins on a sustain row: per pitch, not by wide-band RMS (`symphony/mvt3/reaper-v600/cycles/c12/actor_scripts/joins6b.py OLD_RENDER OLD_PARTS NEW_RENDER NEW_PARTS`: envelopes of harmonics 2-7 of the old and the new pitch; it reports the depth at which they cross and the time with both within 6 dB of their bodies). Result on HO Solo Trumpet "Mute Sus": a +30 ms overlap (`ROW_SLUR_OVERLAP_S`) doubled the two-pitch time (median 34 -> 70 ms) and fixed only 4 of 10 deep joins, so keep overlaps on such rows at 0-10 ms.
