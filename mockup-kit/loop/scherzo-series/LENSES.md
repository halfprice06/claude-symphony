Each cycle has three critics: slot A (articulation and phrasing), slot B (balance and levels) and slot C, whose lens rotates through the four C lenses below. Cycle N uses C lens number (N - 1) mod 4: c01 Blend and space, c02 Dynamics over time, c03 Tempo, timing and transitions, c04 Holistic comparison, c05 Blend, c06 Dynamics, c07 Tempo, c08 Holistic, and so on (c17 Blend, c18 Dynamics, c19 Tempo, c20 Holistic).
