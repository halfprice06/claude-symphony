# Manual reading plan for critic cycles c01-c20 (movement III, Scherzo)

Dan (2026-11-06, for the mockup loops): use the library manual in depth. Each critic reads the pages in its row of the May 2021 Hollywood Orchestra Opus manual in full (`guides/eastwest-hollywood-orchestra/text/EW-Hollywood-Orchestra-Opus-Edition-User-Manual-2021.txt`; pages with tables or pictures as images from `../pdfs/` with `pdftoppm -f N -l N -r 110 -png`). Page numbers are PDF pages. Where the 2021 text and the 2025 manuals disagree, the 2025 manuals and the installed patch files win; `guides/eastwest-hollywood-orchestra/DIGEST.md` section 11 lists the known conflicts, and `loop/MANUAL-GAPS.md` lists what the manual offers that this mockup does not use.

Slots: A = articulation and phrasing; B = balance and levels; C rotates (`loop/LENSES.md`, C lens (N - 1) mod 4): blend and space in c01, c05, c09, c13, c17; dynamics over time in c02, c06, c10, c14, c18; tempo, timing and transitions in c03, c07, c11, c15, c19; holistic in c04, c08, c12, c16, c20.

Every relevant page is read once in the first pass of each slot (A: c01-c07, B: c01-c06, C: c01-c12); later cycles repeat the groups with a fresh render, so a second reader checks what the first one's findings changed. The groups are Movement II's (`symphony/mvt2/reaper-v600/loop/MANUAL-READING.md`) with the scherzo's passages. Skipped as not relevant to this mockup: 1-9 (cover, credits, contents), 12-31 (overview, editions, system requirements, installation, activation), 35-42 and 47-48 (download locations, UI navigation, menus, Logic), 55-60 and 65-67 (browser, downloads, Orchestrator presets), 72-74 (harp glissando key maps; the scherzo has no harp glissando), 116-122 and 127-132 (tam-tams, cymbals, drums, tambourines, metals, woods: the scherzo's percussion is tacet), 152-153 and 156-157 (arpeggiator, chord tools, echo, recorder, scale, transpose), 163-187 (Perform page, Hollywood Orchestrator), 133 and 193 (chapter contents), 201 (EQ picture), 205-207 and 209-218 (SSL strip internals, gate, tape, distortion, modulation, harmonics, delay), 224-228 (licence).
