measurements, code, score or sources). Nobody heard the audio. Actor 6's NOTES.md was treated as claims to check.

**Verdict.**

## 8. Research (with sources)

1. **Accents in mock-ups.** Practitioners keep the accent in the attack (velocity / a short controller push) and
   return to the marked dynamic within the note; a level raised over the whole note sounds unnatural on held notes, and
   harder velocities pick a harsher, shorter attack (https://vi-control.net/community/threads/questions-about-prepping-ahead-for-dynamics-and-volume-for-orchestral-mock-ups.159951/ ;
   https://forums.steinberg.net/t/help-with-realistic-mock-ups/157194?page=6 ;
   https://vi-control.net/community/threads/bbcso-expression-vs-dynamics-vs-velocity.103333/ ;
   https://vi-control.net/community/threads/the-cc-control-elephant.122175/). In recorded performance, intensity rises
   are shorter and steeper than falls (Dean, Olsen and Bailes on Haydn recordings,
   https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/is-there-a-rise-fall-temporal-archetype-of-intensity-in-the-music/).
   Consequence: r06's shape (40 ms rise, 300 ms fall) is right; where a velocity step crosses a sample layer (cellos,
   basses) the step should shrink and the bump carry the weight. SFZ velocity crossfades (`xfin_lovel`/`xfin_hivel`)
   remove hard layer switches (https://sfzformat.com/opcodes/xfin_lovel/).
2. **Free brass, cor anglais and contrabassoon samples.**
   - University of Iowa MIS pre-2012 brass: Bb trumpet (vib / no vib), tenor trombone, bass trombone and tuba are
     complete at pp, mf and ff; the horn set lacks pp C2-B3 and mf C3-B3 (the files return 404; I checked the URLs).
     The 2012+ per-pitch sets (horn Bb1-F5, ff only) are stereo Decca-tree recordings, 24/96. Terms: "may be
     downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions" (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html ;
     https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISFrenchhorn.html ; https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISBbtrumpet.html ;
     https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIStenortrombone.html ; https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISbasstrombone.html ;
     https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIStuba.html ; https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS-Pitches-2012/MISHorn2012.html).
   - Philharmonia Orchestra samples include French horn, cor anglais and contrabassoon; free to use in any work,
     including commercial, but not to be sold or redistributed "as is" (keep them in assets/, never publish the files)
     (https://philharmonia.co.uk/audio-sound-samples/ ; https://github.com/skratchdot/philharmonia-samples/).
   - Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra: 4 horns, 3 trumpets, 3 trombones, tuba, sustain and staccato, stereo, minor-third
     sampling, CC Sampling Plus 1.0 (https://os.zynthian.org/factory_sfz/Other/sonatina/Sonatina%20Symphonic%20Orchestra/Readme.html ;
     https://www.synthtopia.com/content/2011/01/31/free-creative-commons-orchestral-sample-library-sonatina-symphonic-orchestra/).
     VPO 3 packages these and recommends a common reverb to make the sources sit together
     (https://www.virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/).
3. **Timing and section spread.** Editors of real orchestras nudge by about 10 ms and keep variation within about
   ±10 ms; pure random onset "humanize" sounds like bad players; sections drift together, and slow string attacks need
   a per-patch latency correction rather than randomness (https://vi-control.net/community/threads/how-precise-in-terms-of-milliseconds-and-db-are-professional-musicians-vs-the-score.90243/post-4513796 ;
   https://forum.vsl.co.at/post/290360 ; https://vi-control.net/community/threads/quantising-slow-attack-strings.155143/ ;
   https://musescore.org/en/node/376281). Consequence: a small, fixed per-player offset inside each section (player 2
   of an a2 map 5-15 ms late, divisi halves offset), not random jitter per note.
4. **Staging, depth and solos.** Section-wise early reflections: strings closest, woodwinds behind, brass further back,
   one common hall (https://vi-control.net/community/threads/early-reflections-positioning-and-panning.148432/ ;
   https://forum.vsl.co.at/post/143512 ; https://beatkitchen.io/guides/orchestral/11-orchestral-mixing/). Brass and
   percussion reflections from the back wall can arrive before the strings' direct sound and mask them (stage
   acoustics, https://iml.esm.rochester.edu/polyphonic-archive/article/stage-acoustics-for-symphony-orchestras-just-black-magic-part-ii/index.html).
   Orchestral recordings use spot microphones on soloists and woodwinds, ridden with the score (mix guides above;
   https://imusician.pro/en/resources/blog/how-to-record-mix-and-master-classical-music).
5. **Timpani rolls.** Library rolls are looped and shaped with a crossfade controller; a roll is one continuous event
   whose dynamics come from the controller, with the first and last strokes slightly accented; repeated new notes inside
   a roll give audible bumps (https://forum.vsl.co.at/topic/53560/best-practices-to-get-dynamic-timpani-rolls-in-sychron-sound-nice ;
   https://forum.vsl.co.at/post/250901 ; https://forum.vsl.co.at/post/288710 ;
   https://forums.steinberg.net/t/timpani-roll-dynamic/107904). Consequence: chained roll notes must be one roll.
6. **Mastering loudness.** Classical masters sit at about -20 to -16 LUFS; LRA has no standard ceiling (a Mahler 1
   release measured 23.5 LU), so the slow-movement references are a guide, not a rule; fix range in the dynamics, not
   with a compressor (https://violetrecording.com/how-to-master-classical-music/ ;
   https://vi-control.net/community/threads/what-db-is-your-masters-peak-and-also-low.156562/page-2 ;
   https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/tech3342).
