## Findings

### 1. 247–254: the owed "fast ff full-brass combination of the two heads" has only one head in the brass. Applied.

- **Problem.** cyclic-notes ("Owed" item 6) and mvt1.md ("kept for the finale") reserve "a fast ff full-brass combination of the first- and second-theme heads in C major". The plan (v01, 243–250) also says "combined ff in full brass". In v70 only movement I's S head is in the brass (Tpt 1·2, Hn 1·2). The P head is in Fl 1·2 and Vln I·II alone. Tbn 1·2 hold f harmony notes that Hn 3·4, the bass trombone and the tuba already supply. At 269–272 the P head is in the brass, but there it is set against F1, not against the S head. So the combination the symphony saved for this point never sounds in the brass.
- **Evidence.**
  - Rimsky-Korsakov, PDF 72: the melodies "best suited to the brass quality are those of an unmodulated diatonic character, rousing and triumphant in the major key". The P head in major is such a melody.
  - A&S PDF 392 (principle 7): the mode change on a repeated idea is the point. Movement I's two heads, heard together only at f in A♭ and in minor keys (mvt1.md), now return in major in the instruments that were held back for them.
- **Earlier decision.** c059 kept the P head in one octave because the octave above puts Fl 1 and Vln I on E7, and the octave below (G4) crossed the trumpets' S head. Two octaves below avoids both: trombones at G3–A4, under Tpt 1·2 (E5–C6) and the Hn 1 S head. They meet Hn 1·2 only in unisons (E4 at 247, F4 at 249).
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-070/work/sections/finale.ily).** Tbn 1·2 a2 on the P head and its continuation 247–254, in tenor clef, back to bass clef at 255:

  ```

  ```

  finaleTbnTwo is identical in 247–254, then keeps its own notes from 255.
- **Effect on the rest of the passage.**
  - The harmony stays complete: Tbn 2's old F at 250 is still in Hn 3 and in the line itself, and the bass F at 252 is still there.
  - The hammered layout (owed item 7: head in octaves, Cb eighths, held brass) is kept. Hn 3·4, the bass trombone, the tuba and Tpt 3 still hold or hammer at f.
