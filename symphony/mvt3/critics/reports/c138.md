## Findings

### 1. 217-218: violas, cellos and basses stay f under the echo of theme B (applied)
**Problem.** In c108, every wind and brass part on the held F/A♭ chord diminishes f > mp, so that the echo in Hn 1-2 a2 and the cor anglais (A♭3-D♭4-F4-G♭4, f espress.) comes through. The strings on the same chord were left at f: violas A♭4/D♭5 just above the echo, cellos F3 inside it, basses F2. The cor anglais is in its lower-middle register, under f strings in the same register.
**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 290: a quiet line is heard only when the other parts are made quieter, and in a hall the score has to do this (Principle 11). PDF 160: the held chord should move, not stand still. c108's own reason (Rimsky PDF 129, 133: an echo is weaker, and the harmonic basis is of inferior power) covers these strings as much as the low brass.
**Fix.**
- Vla: `q\> q | q\mp q | <c' dis'>1\p^\markup \italic "div."`
- Vc: `f1~\cbaw\> | f1\mp | aes,1\p`
- Cb: `f,1~\cbaw\> | f,1\mp | aes,,1\p`

Vln I-II (F6/D♭6, above the echo) keep f. The trumpets, upper woodwinds, timpani and violins keep the sub. p at 219, so the protected sub. p → fff path is unchanged. The low strings enter 219 at p, as the low brass have since c108.

### 2. 188-190 (and 68-70): F2's violin statements have no swell at the phrase's peak (applied)
**Problem.** At 187-194 Vln I-II, the violas (mp) and Hn 1 (p dolce) play eight bars of F2 at one level, and the line rises to its peak (B♭5 at 189) with no dynamic shape. The cor anglais statement eight bars earlier shapes its peak (180 <, 182 mf >, 183 p), so the string statement, which should sing more, is the flatter of the two. The exposition at 67-70 is the same.
**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 155: the lyrical string legato is the one that "transitions ... into a gentle swell on the note of arrival". PDF 150 and 152: the expressive crescendo and diminuendo are what notation must ask for, because a flat level reads as lifeless (Principles 3 and 6).
**Fix.** recS.ily, in Vln I, Vln II, Vla and Hn 1 (inside the existing `\transpose c aes,` blocks): `d''4(\< c''4 e''4 g''4) | d'''2(\> c'''4 b''4) | a''2(\! g''2)`, with the same pattern an octave lower in the violas and Hn 1. sTwo.ily, Vln I-II 68-70: `f''4(\< ... | f'''2(\> es'''4 d'''4) | c'''2(\! bes''2)`. The 196-198 crescendo and the c118 bow plan are unchanged.

### 3. 234-238: movement I's second-theme head without its slurred pickup and swells (applied)
**Problem.** The owed violin statement of movement I's S head ("molto cantabile") starts with the pickup G4 detached from the E5 it rises to, and its four bars carry no dynamic shape. Movement I phrases this theme with a slurred pickup and swells on the long notes: v99 155-159 (`g'4(\mf` into the head, crescendo at 157); v221 Hn 1 46-50 (< > on the held note, < on the tenuto).
**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 151: legato makes the line a gesture, and the transition between notes carries the expression. The rising sixth from the pickup is that transition. PDF 155: swell on the note of arrival (Principles 5 and 6).
**Fix.**
- arrival.ily 234, Vln I-II: `g'4(`.
- sHead.ily 235-238, Vln I-II: `e''2.~\startTextSpan\< e''8\> d''8) | c''4(\! a''2--\< g''4\!) | f''4.(\< e''8 d''4 f''8 a''8 | c'''2(\> b''2)\!\stopTextSpan`

The diminuendo falls on the C6 appoggiatura over V7 into its resolution B5. Pitches, rhythm, "sul A" and "ord." at 239 are unchanged (themecheck 36/36). This is expression only, not a change to the quotation's notes. The cyclic threads may want to know that the finale now phrases the theme as movement I does.

### 4. 217: "espress." is not printed on the horns' a2 echo (applied)
**Problem.** Only Hn 1 carries `^\markup \italic "espress."`. On the part-combined Hn 1·2 staff in unison, LilyPond printed "a2" and dropped the text, so the score shows "espress." over the cor anglais only (v138 page 12).
**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 152, 156: expressive nuance is what the written line has to carry to the player (Principle 3). The horns are two of the three players on the echo.
**Fix.** codaTwo.ily Hn 2: `aes2(^\markup \italic "espress." des'2`. The scratch PDF now prints it over "a2". Both horn parts carry it.

### 5. 223 as the first true peak: confirmed, no change

At 211 the low brass are ff for one bar, then f. 219 sub. p widens the range just before the arrival. 223 is the first fff, the first cymbal, bass drum, string tremolo and C7, with a sustained timpani roll.
