## Findings

### 1. 229: the canon's last diminuendo has no target level (applied)
Problem: at 226–229 the canon (Fl, Ob, Cl 1·2, Vln I·II) plays ff with swells on its two suspensions. The last diminuendo, from D6 into the C6 resolution at 229.3, stops on a bare `\!`. The next printed level for these parts is f at 231, after the breath. The violas, bassoons, contrabassoon, cellos, basses and timpani are at f throughout 229–230, and the brass return at f at 231. A player reading 229–230 cannot tell whether the last hairpin returns to ff, which would make 231 a subito f, or falls toward f.
Evidence: MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10) ends every hairpin that changes the level on a printed level (dim. to p in bars 4–5; the swell in bars 12–14 continues to mp). PDF 8 asks for the vertical lines of the score to be proofread: at 229.3 every other part sounding is at f. The swells at 227–228 do return to ff, and their source comment says so. The swell at 229 is the canon's last, and its level changes.
Fix (arrival.ily): the hairpin ends on f at 229.3 in all eight canon parts. Cl 2 takes the f on a spacer, so the shared staff keeps one simultaneous dynamic:
```
arrivalFluteOne: ... d'''2\> ... c'''4\f des'''4 | c'''1 \breathe | ...
arrivalFluteTwo: ... a''2\> ... bes''2\f | ...
arrivalOboeOne:  ... d'''4.\> r8 c'''4\f des'''4 | ...
arrivalOboeTwo:  ... a'2\> bes'2\f | ...
arrivalClOne:    ... d'''2\> c'''4\f des'''4 | ...
arrivalClTwo:    ... | << g'2. { s2\> s4\f } >> f'4 | e'1 \breathe | ...
arrivalVlnOne / arrivalVlnTwo: ... d'''2\> c'''4\f des'''4 | c'''1 | ...
```
The coda's descent after the arrival is now printed in steps: fff 223, ff canon 226, f at 229.3, then f > mp at 233–234 and p at 235. The restated f at 231 is kept, as the violas restate theirs at the start of the plagal close. Notes, ties, the swells at 227–228, the chorale and its top line, and the timpani pulse are unchanged. Page 13 shows no collisions.

### 2. 94.1: Hn 2's re-attack has no accent in the codetta's last chord (applied)
Problem: 23 parts strike the codetta's last chord at 94.1. All of them carry an accent except Hn 2, which re-attacks E♭4 (`es'4-> | es'2 r2`). Hn 3·4 play the same rhythm and accent both 93.4 and 94.1.
Evidence: MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11). In Beethoven's tutti the sf is printed in every staff, and the editor adds a bracketed [sf] where a part lacked it. PDF 8 asks for the vertical lines to be proofread.
Fix (clos.ily, closHnTwo): `es2. es'4-> | es'2-> r2`. The page-5 image shows the accent under Hn 2's half note next to Hn 1's tied E♭. No other mark moves.

### 3. 104.4: the basses' D♭ lacks the staccato of every doubling part (tested, not applied)
Problem as found: F1's tail at 103–104 is `es4-.) des4-.` in Vc, Vla, Bn 2, Tbn 1 and Hn 3·4, but `des,4` without a staccato in the basses. At 100, the same place in the first statement, the basses do have it.
Test: with `des,4-.` the build reported a new outer-voice octave at 104 (C♯6→E♭6 over C♯2→E♭5). The reason is that at 104 the violins enter with their run (D♭6–E♭6 on beat 4), which they do not do at 100. With every bass short, nothing low sounds under the run's last eighth. The basses' full quarter is the only bass under that eighth, so the difference has a function. Decision: keep `des,4`.

### 4. 274.1: Bn 2's accent on the pedal C (not applied)
Bn 2 accents its re-attacked C3 at 274, while the bass clarinet, bass trombone, contrabassoon, tuba and cellos re-attack the same pedal without one. Bn 1 accents its moving F4 on the same staff, so the part combiner sets both as one chord with one accent. Taking Bn 2's accent off would split the staff into two voices for a single bar. Decision: keep.

### 5. Dynamics on the eighth rest before a pickup at 199, 265, 269 (not applied)
Fl 1, Ob 1 and Cl 1 at 199, Cl 1 at 265 and Bn 1 at 269 have their ff on `r8\ff` before the first note. MOLA Ex. 3 places a dynamic at the first sounding note, and c068 adopted that rule. In the score, each of these staves is shared with a second player who strikes on the downbeat at the same ff, so the one printed ff is correct there. Only the extracted parts show the ff under a rest. A part-only move (`\tag #'part`) belongs to the engraving lens. Decision: no change in this cycle. The score is correct, and the parts still give the right level one eighth before the note.

### 6. Harp pedal diagrams and "Ped." texts (not applied)
MOLA PDF 5 says harp pedalling should be left to the performer. The score prints diagrams at its harp entries and advance pedal texts at 115 and 247 (c006, c566). They were added after a relay from movement II, so the harp writing matches across movements, and for the chromatic entries at 123, 243 and 277. Removing them is a lens-5/6 question and would undo two accepted cycles on an engraving argument alone. Decision: keep. They tell a player what each passage needs, and they do not affect the sound.
