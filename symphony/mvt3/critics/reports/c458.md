## Findings

### 1. Bar 74: the counter-melody has no dynamic shape in the one bar where it carries the motion (applied)

Bars: 74 (exposition, F2 in E♭ major).

Problem: at 74 the tune (Vln I·II, mf) holds F5, tied from 73.4, for a bar and a quarter. The only motion in that bar is the counter-melody in violas and cellos (with Hn 1 pp), which c051 already changed to quarters B♭–A♭–F–G (Sebesky Ex. 273) to push into 75. The counter keeps its flat mp, so the bar where the focus should pass to it for a moment (Sebesky PDF 14: the background "can absorb the soloist and rise to the primary level temporarily") is marked exactly like the bars where it must stay under the tune.

Evidence: Sebesky PDF 207 (Ex. 269: the lead is static, "it needs some help. The counter melody in the violas lends the right amount of melodic interest", and the violas carry their own hairpins); PDF 215–216 (Ex. 281, note C: one part moves alone while the others sustain, "in order to create a little motion"); PDF 18 (Ex. 2A: each short line has its own mp < mf > swell). The score does the same for its other counter-line: the chromatic counter at 236–237 and 240–241 swells on its held note and recedes as the tune moves (c278, c378, Adler PDF 212, Ex. VII-63). The recapitulation's parallel bar 194 already has its own fill (Ob 1's echo, c337), so it stays as it is and the two statements remain different.

Fix (sections/sTwo.ily, violas and cellos only; notes, slurs and the mp level unchanged):

```
sTwoVla: ... | bes4(\< aes4 f4\> g4~ | g2\! aes4 bes4) | ...
sTwoVc:  ... | bes4(\< aes4 f4\> g4~ | g2\! aes4 bes4) | ...
```

The swell peaks on 74.3 and is back to mp at 75.1, where the tune moves again. No printed peak level, as at 236 and 240.

Tested: I also put the same hairpins on Hn 1 (its pp dolce doubling). That made the part-combiner reassign the horn voices, and the MIDI then played Hn 1·2's shared notes at 76.3 at velocity 62 (they should be 37, pp) and dropped Hn 1's notes at 73–74 from the "solo" voice. I took the horn hairpins out. Hn 1 stays an unshaped pp colour under the strings, which also keeps Rimsky-Korsakov's balance (one wind under a string section). With the horn unchanged, the only MIDI differences from v458 are the six viola and cello attacks at 74.2–74.4 (velocities 50→59/67/59 and 63→72/80/72).

### 2. Bars 46–48: a swell on the inner voices' move to G♭/C♭ (tested, not applied)

The transition's one harmonic event is Vln II F–D–G♭ and Vla D–C–C♭ at 46.3–47, which Sebesky Ex. 272 (PDF 207) would mark as a "chromatic inner melody" that "changes chord quality". I considered pp < p > pp on Vln II and Vla from 46.3 to 48. I decided against it: the violas at 46–49 are on the do-not-touch list (c163, c206, c228, with the 9–8 at 49), the oboe enters with the figure on 47.2, so a peak at 47.1 would compete with it (Sebesky PDF 13–14), and at pp the change of chord is already plain. No change.

### 3. Bars 11–26: F1 stays at one level, f, for sixteen bars (checked, kept)

### 4. Bars 119–122: the bassoon solo's second phrase has no swell (checked, kept)

The first phrase (115–118) swells p < mp > p, and the second phrase is flat p until 122. That follows the focus rule: at 119 and 122 the "4 soli" violin runs fill the bassoon's held notes and briefly take the primary level (Sebesky PDF 14, PDF 211–212, Ex. 280). A swell in the bassoon there would cover them. No change.
