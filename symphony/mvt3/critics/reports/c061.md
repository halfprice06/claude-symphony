## Findings (ranked)

### F1. The development's climax melody has no rhythm of its own (110–113) — applied
- **Problem.** The goal of the development is the ff F-minor cadence. Its top line is G6 (whole) | A♭6–F6 (halves) | F6–E6 (halves) | F6. Every note falls on a beat and every note is consonant with the bass (5, 3, 3, 5, 3, 1). The lament just before it (107–109) has 7-6 suspensions in the violins. So the climax is rhythmically the plainest bar group in the development, and the tutti covers it rather than it leading the tutti. A listener cannot hum a line that consists only of chord tones in even values.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 346: a suspension "animate[s] and individualize[s]" its voice, and "rhythm rather than melody assumes paramount importance".
  - PDF 348: dissonant suspensions "bring into prominence the voice in which they occur".
  - PDF 350, §8: the cadential V4-3 "enhance[s] the effect of a cadence by emphasizing the leading tone".
  - PDF 358–359, §19 and Ex. 21-25: in minor, 2 suspended over I rises to 3 by the half step.
  - Exercise 2 (PDF 370) builds a whole melody from tied downbeat notes.
- **Fix (applied).**
  - 110 G tied into 111, as a 9 over F minor resolving up by half step to A♭ on beat 2.
  - A♭ held across the middle of the bar, which makes it a rhythmically strong syncopation at the change to D♭ (PDF 363, Ex. 21-32).
  - 112 F tied from beat 2 into beat 3 over C, a cadential 4-3 resolving to E on beat 4.
  - The line is now G–(G)–A♭–F | F–(F)–E | F, with two dissonances that resolve. Pitches, harmony, dynamics and scoring are unchanged.

  In dev.ily, Vln I `g'''1\ff~ | g'''4( aes'''2 f'''4 | f'''2~ f'''4 e'''4) | f'''2->`. The same rhythm goes into Vln II, Fl 1, Fl 2 and Ob 1 (`g''1\ff~ | g''4 aes''2 f''4 | f''2~ f''4 e''4`). Tpt 1 and Hn 2 take the 4-3 at 112 (`f''2~ f''4 e''4`, `f'2~ f'4 e'4`), so no E sounds against the suspended F.
- **Remaining clash.** The A♭s of Hn 1, Tpt 3 and Tbn 2 at 111 sound under the G 9th. They are brass at f under ff strings and winds, which is the contrasting texture A&S PDF 352 allows. Tpt 3's B♭→A♭ (c060) is kept.
- **Protection.** Cycle 58 protected "climax 107–113" for its dynamics and articulation. Those are untouched, and the lament 107–109 is unchanged.
