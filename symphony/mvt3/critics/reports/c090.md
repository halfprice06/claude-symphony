## Findings

### 1. 275–276: held E5 in the oboes and Vln II covers the end of the cyclic chain (applied)
**Problem.** The finale's close is meant to finish the movements' shared 5–♯4–4–3 close. finale.md says the chain's E5 in the oboes and Vln II "passes to Tpt 1·2's E5 in the scherzo quotation, so the line runs G–F♯–F–E–D–D♭–C at the E5 level". In v90 that hand-over does not happen. Ob 1·2 (`e''1~ | e''1`) and Vln II (`<g' e''>1:32`) hold E5 through 276. Meanwhile Tpt 1·2 and Cl 1 play the quotation's tail E5–D5–D♭5–C5 in the same octave. Two things follow:
- The voice that carried G–F♯–F–E stops on E. The D–D♭–C (the motto's chromatic tail and the scherzo's tail) is heard only in another instrument, against a held E5.
- E5 sounds against D♭5 in the same octave, an augmented-second clash that blurs the D♭, the one chromatic note the cyclic link depends on.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 274: "try to keep the accompanying forces registrally distant from the soloist whenever possible… especially when dealing with woodwind solo instruments". Adler PDF 288: Haydn moves the flute's doubling onto the inner line that needs it. Cycle 39 removed held Cs from this bar for the same reason; the held E5 is the same problem one step higher.

**Fix (applied, finale.ily).** The oboes and Vln II continue the chain into the tail, so 5–♯4–4–3–2–♭2–1 sounds in one voice:
- finaleOboeOne and finaleOboeTwo, 275–276: `e''1\< | e''4( d''8 des''8 c''4-.) r4 |` (was `e''1~\< | e''1 |`).
- finaleVlnTwo, 276: `e''4^\markup \italic "unis." d''8( des''8) c''4-. r4 | <e' c''>4->^\markup \italic "div." …` (was `<g' e''>1:32`).

The quotation itself is unchanged: the record stays at 36/36, and Fl 1 + Vln I C6 and Tpt 1·2 + Cl 1 C5 are as before. G and E stay in the chord (Hn 1·2, Hn 4, Bn 1 and upper violas on E4; Fl 2, Cl 2, Hn 3 and Tbn 1 on G). This changes how the chain is scored, not any theme's notes. Tell the scherzo and movement II threads only that the finale's chain now runs on through D–D♭–C in the oboes and Vln II.

### 2. 220: Tpt 3 leaps onto the thread pitch E4 at sub. p (applied)
