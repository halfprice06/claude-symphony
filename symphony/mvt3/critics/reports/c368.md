## Findings

### 1. 211-215: theme B's swell ends on levels that only the MIDI plays (applied)

**Problem.** c338 shaped movement II's theme B recall at 212-214. Winds and trumpets go ff (211), < (212), peak at 213 with no mark, then > (214). The violins go f < (212) into the peak at 213 and > (214). Because an open hairpin left the MIDI level low afterwards, c338 restored the levels with hidden dynamics: `-\tweak stencil ##f \ff` at 215 in Fl 1·2, Ob 1·2, Cl 1·2 and Tpt 1·2, and `\ff` at 213 and `\f` at 215 in Vln I and Vln II. The page therefore shows a diminuendo at 214 with no target, nothing at 215, and then a second diminuendo at 216 "to f" at 217 (page 12). A player who has dimmed at 214 does not know whether to come back to ff at 215. If they stay low, the printed 216-217 "dim. to f" means nothing. The MIDI plays ff at 215, so the conductor reads one plan and hears another. In the violins the f < at 212 has no printed peak, and their new quarter-note line at 215 starts with no level.

**Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10): every hairpin ends on a printed level (mf > p, pp < mp). Ex. 6 (PDF 11): an editor prints a missing level, in brackets if need be, instead of leaving it to be guessed. PDF 8: the vertical line is proofread, so the same attack carries the same mark in every staff. c338's own report (lines 55-58) says the hidden marks "only fix the MIDI", so the printed and the played plans differ by design.

**Fix (applied, codaTwo.ily).** The twelve hidden marks are now printed. They change nothing in the MIDI, whose 211-218 bytes are identical to v368:
- `codaTwoTptOne` etc.: `es''2.(\ff f''4 | des''1~\> | des''1~\f` (215), and the same in Tpt 2, Fl 1, Fl 2, Ob 1, Cl 1. Ob 2 has `es''1\ff`, Cl 2 has `f'1\ff`. Every shared staff has equal marks.
- `codaTwoVlnOne`: `f'''2.(\f\< ges'''4) | aes'''2(\ff bes'''4 aes'''4) | ges'''2.(\> f'''4) | es''4(\f f'' ges'' aes'')`. Vln II the same on its line.

The page now reads: winds ff < (peak) > ff, then > f at 217; violins f < ff > f. The ff at 215 and the f at 217 keep c338's levels, and no strong mark is added (c008).

### 2. 243-246: the stringendo's crescendo, written as "cresc. molto" and ended on the ff (applied; the qualified word crescendo is new to the score)

**Problem.** Under the horn call in augmentation (Hn 1·2 ff, 243) the strings drop to mf, divided, and build for four bars under the stringendo (printed at 245, c348) into Più allegro ff at 247. Their hairpins stopped with `\!` at 246.3 (violins, on the G6 half note that ends their scale) and 246.4 (Vla, Vc, Cb), two beats or one beat before the ff. So the build never reached a printed level (MOLA Ex. 3). The violas did not take part in the drop. They kept the f of 239 under the mf of every other string part, and the cellos and basses had a diminuendo in 242 that the violas, playing the same inner chord, did not have. A two-level build (mf to ff) over four narrow bars was shown only as a thin wedge, the same sign the score uses for one-bar swells.

**Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 7 (PDF 12) writes such a build as words, "cresc. molto", with "rit." printed above it as a separate word. Here "stringendo" above and "cresc. molto" below would sit the same way. Ex. 3 (PDF 10): the change ends on its level. Ex. 6 (PDF 11) and PDF 8: one attack, one mark (the violas at 243). Adler part 2, PDF 201, item 3: a crescendo is clearer when written out. The score had only the bare "cresc." (c268, 141-145). "molto" tells the strings that this is the largest string build before Più allegro, mf to ff. The timpani (p < f), Fl 1 (f at 245) and the brass already have their own levels.

**Fix (applied).**
- global.ily: `crescMolto = #(make-music 'CrescendoEvent 'span-direction START 'span-type 'text 'span-text "cresc. molto")`. It plays in the MIDI like a hairpin.
- sHead.ily: Vln I `<gis' gis''>2\mf\crescMolto ... g'''2` (the `\!` removed, so the dashes run to the ff at 247.2). Vln II `<e' b'>2\mf\crescMolto ... g''2`. Vc `e,2\mf\crescMolto ... d4--`. Cb `e,2\mf\crescMolto ... d,4--`. Vla `c'2(\> d'2) | b2\mf\crescMolto c'2 | ... g'4 g4`, which drops at 242 with the cellos.
- Bn 1·2 (shared staff): the hairpin now ends on `f1\f \breathe` / `d1\f` at 246 instead of `\!`. The MIDI is unchanged.
- Cl 1 is left as it was. If its hairpin runs on to its ff at 247, the part-combiner splits Cl 1·2 at 247-253 (I tested this and reverted it).
- global.ily: bar 243 gets score-only `\newSpacingSection` with `spacing-increment = #5.0`, reverted at 244, so "mf cresc. molto" ends before the strings' continuous 243|244 barline. At default spacing it crossed it (MOLA PDF 7-8; Ex. 7).

MIDI: only the five string tracks change, in 242-246. Violin velocities are 1-2 lower on the way up, and the violas dip from 62 to 56 at 243 and rise to 67 at 247.
