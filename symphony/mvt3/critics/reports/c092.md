## Findings

**1. 131–132: the development's one perfect cadence (D7–G) doubles the seventh and moves it in octaves. Applied.**
Problem: at 131 the seventh C sounds in Hn 4 (C4, held), Bn 1 (C4), Vla (C4) and Ob 1 (C5 on beat 3). Ob 1 C5→B4 and Hn 4 C4→B3 then resolve it in parallel octaves into 132. In the same change Cl 1 falls D5→G4 while the bass falls D→G, which is another pair of octaves.
Evidence: A&S PDF 76: "The chord 7th forms a dissonance, so it must never be doubled." PDF 83 (Ex. 5-18): parallel octaves give "not enough [contrast] to give the feeling of two individual voices". These are independent lines (Ob 1 has its own descent, Hn 4 its repeated-note layer from 129, and Cl 1 a held D), so the free-texture exception of PDF 84 does not apply. This is the cadence that closes the G area, so it is heard.
Fix (dev.ily): `devOboeOne` … `fis''2\f d''2 | b'4-> r4 r2` (was `fis''2\f c''2`). The new line arpeggiates F♯–D–B in thirds, as Ex. 5-12 (PDF 82) allows. `devClOne` … `d''1\f | d''4-> r4 r2` (was `g'4->`): the common tone is held. The G chord at 132 stays complete (B in Ob, Bn, Hn 4, Vla; D in Cl, Vln II, Hn 3, Vla).

**2. 185–186 (and its model 65–66): F2's closing cadence moves the tenor pad in octaves with the bass. Applied.**
Problem: at 185 Bn 1 leaves B2 for G2 (beat 3), and Cl 1 holds G3. At the deceptive cadence both go to A♭ together with the bass G→A♭ (Vc G2→A♭2, Cb G1→A♭1). That makes octaves (V–VI with the 5th of V rising beside the bass). In the exposition model, Bn 1 holds D3 under the trumpet's D4 at 65.3, so the leading tone is doubled, and both rise D→E♭ in octaves at 66.
Evidence: PDF 83, Ex. 5-18 (parallel octaves); PDF 76 (the leading tone is never doubled). Cl 1 doubles Bn 1 an octave higher by design (c074), so the pair counts as one doubled tenor line. That line moves in octaves with the bass, which is a real error under PDF 83, not a colour doubling.
Fix (recS.ily): `recSBsnOne` bars 185–186 `b,2 d2 | c1` (was `b,2 g,2 | aes,1`); `recSClOne` 186 `c'1` (was `aes1`). V7 is complete at 185.3 (G pizz, D3, F3, B3 in the solo), the leading tone B3→C4 sounds only in the solo, and VI doubles its third (C3, C4) with root A♭ in the basses and violas. Exposition (sOne.ily) `sOneBsnOne` bar 65 `d2 bes,2` (was `d1`): Bn 1 joins the cellos' B♭2 and goes with them to E♭3, so the solo's D4→E♭4 is no longer doubled. I tried `d2 f2` first. It is correct in the voice leading, but analysis.txt then reported a top/bass "octave" at 66 (V2 pizz F4 against Bn F3), so I chose B♭2, which the analyser passes.

**3. 111–112: the bass trombone leaps more than an octave twice in the climax. Applied.**
Problem: `devBassTbn` reads `f,2 des2 | bes,,2 c2` (F2–D♭3–B♭1–C3), a tenth down and then a ninth up within one bar, at ff. The tuba, contrabassoon and basses already sound B♭1, so the low note adds no weight. The tenth followed by the ninth makes the line jump around.
Evidence: PDF 81: "Leaps larger than an octave are not permitted"; PDF 89 point 7; PDF 75 (lines lie mostly in the middle of the range).
Fix: `bes,2 c2` (B♭2–C3, a step). B.tbn now doubles Bn 2 at the unison on D♭3–B♭2–C3 and the tuba an octave above. Checked against Tbn 1·2: no new 5ths or octaves.

**4. 134: the G7 before the retransition loses its third for a full bar. Applied.**
Problem: Hn 3·4 leave at the end of 133 (c039, the brass leave first in a diminuendo), and with them goes the only B. Bar 134 then holds G2–G3–F4–D5–G5, an empty fifth with the seventh and no third, at pp into 135.
Evidence: PDF 76: the 3rd "is never omitted except for special effects", and "root-position seventh chords frequently omit the chord 5th and double the root". Nothing here asks for an open sound: B♮ is the same pitch in C minor and C major, and it is the bass of the stop chord three bars later.
Fix (dev.ily): `devVlnTwo` end `d''1\> | b'2. r4\! |` (was `d''1\>~ | d''2. r4\!`). Vln II moves to B4 and the chord becomes G–B–F–G, with the 5th omitted as the reading allows. Vln II re-enters at 135 on D5. c039's order (brass, then wind, then strings) is kept.

**5. 196–197: Hn 2 moves in octaves with the tune at the A♭ statement's last phrase. Applied.**
Problem: Hn 2 holds E♭3 through 196 and leaps to A♭3 at 197. The violins' pickup E♭5 (196, beat 4) leaps to A♭5 at the same moment, so there are octaves with a leap in both voices.

Fix (recS.ily): `recSHnTwo` 197 `\transpose c aes, { g2\< a2 }` (E♭3–F3, was A♭3–F3). Hn 2 holds the common tone, then F3→A♭3 at 198 in sixths and fifths with Hn 1's C4–D♭4–E♭4. The dynamics on the shared horn staff are unchanged.

**6. 107–109: the lament's alto–tenor gap (checked, not changed).**

The lament is protected (cycles 53 and 72), and the gap is the p string-only start of a crescendo that the winds fill at 110. I keep it under PDF 79's expressive exception and propose no change.
