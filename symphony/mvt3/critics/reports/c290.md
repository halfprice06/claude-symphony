## Findings

### 1. 178: the timpani join F2's two recapitulated phrases with the scherzo's motive x, pp (applied)
- **Bars:** 178, recS bar 8. Muted Tpt 1 and cor anglais hold D4 over V (G) at the seam between F2's antecedent (171–178) and consequent (179–186).
- **Problem:** the finale uses the scherzo's timpani motive x only as loud dominant preparation (9, f in the opening crescendo; 143–145, mf < f under the stretto). The scherzo's own last use of it was soft: pp–ppp, near the rim, under a held horn note (scherzo 363–371). The finale never takes up that soft colour. This is the only material from the end of the scherzo that the finale has not yet used in its own character. The recapitulated second theme (171–186) is the movement's quietest solo before the coda, and its timpani are silent from 167 to 198.
- **Evidence:** Adler2 PDF 273, Ex. XVII-13 (Beethoven, Violin Concerto i, 99–109): "The timpani is very important because it plays the motive that joins the two phrases of the melody." In the image the drum is alone in the bar where the woodwind melody pauses, four repeated quarters on one pitch. Motive x in its repeated-note form (G–G–G–G, the record's motiveXretr) is the same kind of figure. PDF 270 (Bartók: "quietly pulsating timpani" as part of a transparent background) supports the pp level. The exposition's seam at 57–58 cannot take it: its half cadence is on B♭ (V of E♭ minor), which is not among the drums (G, A♭, C, E♭). So the device appears once, in C minor on the dominant G, and the recapitulation differs from the exposition.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-290/work):**
  - recS.ily, recSTimp: `R1*7 | g,4\pp_\markup \small \italic "near the rim" g,8 g,8 g,4 r4 | R1*18 |` (was `R1*26 |`). The cue bars 197–198 are unchanged.
  - codaOne.ily, codaOneTimp at 199: `c4\ff_\markup \small \italic "ord." r8 c8 …`. This adds text only; the notes of the protected quotation 199–204 are unchanged.
- **Protection:** 171–186 are protected as "trumpet solos with pp accompaniment" (c004, c019, c168). The stroke keeps that reason. It is pp, lies under the held D4 (it neither doubles nor covers the tune) and adds no doubling to the solo. The tune, the C.a./Tpt pairing, the peak at 182 and the pad are unchanged. The precedent is c217's "dal niente", accepted against the same protection.
- **Tell the scherzo thread:** this is a new quotation of motive x (same form and pitch as 9 and 143–145; no finale theme changes). ### 3. finale.md does not record one link with movement I (record only; no score change)
- **Bars:** 171–178.
- **Problem:** mvt1.md lists movement I 171–174 (the minor second theme as cor anglais and solo cello, "like the finale's single-voice minor statements of its second theme"). finale.md does not record the other side: the finale's recapitulated F2 at 171 is again the cor anglais in duet (with the muted trumpet), in C minor, at the same bar number. That is the finale's only C-minor statement of F2. The cor anglais is the solo colour of movement I's motto answer (I 5) and of movement II's theme B, so this is a cyclic colour link (Adler2 PDF 263–264: a contrasting colour keeps a solo fresh after earlier movements).
- **Fix:** in finale.md, under F2, after "C minor with muted trumpet and cor anglais at 171": add "(answers movement I 171–174, cor anglais and solo cello in the minor second theme; the cor anglais is also movement II's theme-B instrument; keep the cor anglais on this statement)". The note at bar 9 in item 13 also needs a wording correction: the finale's bar 9 takes the closing figure's instrument and rhythm but has the repeated-note form G–G–G–G (motiveXretr), not the closing pitches B–B–B–E. Write "the scherzo's closing timpani motive x (rhythm and instrument; repeated-note form on G)".
