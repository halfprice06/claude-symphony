The earlier passes all recorded rule 17 (violins and cellos in octaves) and the cello form of rule 23 as not used, and c191 found no theme statement in the score that uses them. In the neo-Romantic models this layout is the usual one for a big string tune at its peak. Examples are Tchaikovsky's Romeo and Juliet love theme in its later statements, and the peaks of the second themes in Rachmaninoff 2/iii and Dvořák 8/i. In Ex. 29 and 30 Rimsky shows it in an f cantabile and in a mezzo-forte appassionato.

## Findings

### 1. F2's climax 77-81: the cellos take the tune an octave below Vln II, which forms double octaves Vln I / Vln II / Vc. APPLIED. This changes the scoring of the finale's own theme F2, not its notes.

**Bars:** 77-82, cellos (and violas at 78 beats 3-4).

**Problem:** F2 at 67-82 is the movement's main lyrical statement and its highest melodic point (B♭6 at 78). From 67 to 82 the tune is in Vln I / Vln II octaves, with Fl 1 and Ob 1 from 77. The violas and cellos play the counter-melody in unison for all sixteen bars. At the f peak the tune therefore has no tenor octave. The string body under it is the same Va+Vc counter that was there in the pp opening bars, so the scoring gives the peak no more weight than the start. The growth from statement to statement comes from the woodwind joining (rule 20), and within the statement only Fl 1, Ob 1 and the clarinet thirds add anything. In the whole finale no theme ever sounds in violins and cellos together. Rimsky says this layout's instances "are frequent", and it is the usual sound of a Romantic big tune at its climax.

**Evidence:** Rimsky PDF 60-61 (e): violins and cellos in octaves are "used in very expressive passages where the 'cellos have to play on the A or D strings. This method produces a more resonant tone than the preceding one" (that is, more resonant than violas and cellos). Ex. 29 (PDF 206) has Vln I+II in unison over the cellos in an f cantabile. Ex. 30 (PDF 207-208) has Vln I over Vln II + cellos, then Vln I+II over the cellos. PDF 62 (a): double octaves with "Vns I / Vns II / 'Cellos" serve "full cantabile melodies extremely tense in character, and in forte passages for choice". Bars 77-79 are the f climax of the movement's lyrical theme. PDF 56: cellos are "more often entrusted with tense passionate cantabile", "usually laid out for the top string (A)". The cello line here is E♭4-F4 | B♭4-A♭4-G4-F4 | E♭4-D4, entirely on the A string. PDF 54: crossing the cellos above the violas is one of the natural means of relief. At 77-79 the cellos lie above the violas' counter. Ex. 32 (PDF 210) has the woodwind holding chords under the string double octaves, as Fl 1, Ob 1 and the clarinet thirds already do here.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-281/work/sections/sTwo.ily, comment "c281" above sTwoVc):**
```
% sTwoVc, bars 77-82, was:
bes2(\mf c'4 f'4) | es'2( d'2) | c'2( aes4 c'4) | es'2( d'2) | aes4( ces'4 bes4 f4) | g1\< |
% now:
\clef tenor es'2.\mf\< f'4\upbow | bes'4.(\f\downbow aes'8 g'4 f'4) | es'2.( d'4) | c'4( bes4 aes4 g4~) | g4( f4\> d2) | \clef bass g1\mp\< |
```
The cellos follow the violins' notes, bowing (up-bow 77.4, down-bow 78) and dynamics (mf < f at 78, > at 81). They keep the c241 suspension G tied into 81 resolving to F, and they rejoin the violas on the counter-melody's G3 at 82, mp < as the violas. The violas keep the counter alone, so it is still heard. Rimsky PDF 55: a viola line is doubled by other strings or woodwind when it is a cantabile melody, and this is a secondary line under a pad of Hn 1·2 and bassoons.

One supporting change: sTwoVla bar 78, `es'2( d'2)` → `es'2( g2)`. With the tune in the cellos just above the violas, the dotted A♭4-G4 against the violas' E♭4-D4 made audible parallel fifths between adjacent parts. Before this change they were hidden as compound fifths with the violins, which the all-pairs check also flagged. D4 stays in Hn 1. B♭3 was tried and rejected because it moves in octaves with Cl 1's B♭4-C5 into 79.

**Protection answered:** "67-82 balance" (c094, c098; restated in c211) protects the level of the counter-melody against the tune: the counter was lowered to mp because two sections in unison outweighed the tune in octaves. This change takes weight from the counter and adds it to the tune at the peak only, which is the direction of those fixes. No dynamic of the violins, winds or pad changes. The recapitulation 187-198 (strings protected, cellos mf on the counter confirmed by c121/c124) is left as it is, so that statement keeps its own colour: unison violins, Ob 1, violas and Hn 1 below.

**Checks:** build 0 warnings (build-warnings.txt empty), 16 pages, page 4 checked as an image. The tenor clef falls at the system break before 77 and the bass clef at 82, and the system is not crowded. analysis.txt is unchanged except two density lines (0 outer-voice parallels). par8.py on 75-83 gives the same output as v281 (bassoon octaves with the bass only). The old viola-violin compound fifths at 78.3 and the harp-cello octaves at 79 are gone. Theme record 38/38.
