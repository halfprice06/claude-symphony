## Findings

### 1. 269-272: movement I's head is outweighed and buried in the same register (applied)
**Problem.** In v24 the "swap" moved F1 to the top (Fl, Ob, Cl, Tpt 1-2, Vln I-II). However, Hn 3-4 and Tbn 1-2 still play F1, copied from 265-268, at C4-E3. Movement I's head is left to Hn 1-2, Bn 1, violas and cellos, at G3-E4: the same octave as the trombone and horn F1.
**Evidence.** Rimsky's weights (PDF 51) give F1 about 15 horn-units (2 Tpt 4, 2 Tbn 4, Hn 3-4 2, six woodwinds 3, two violin sections 2) against 4.5 for movement I's head (Hn 1-2 2, Bn 0.5, Vla 1, Vc 1). Both lines sit in the same register, so the cyclic theme cannot be heard. The appendix chords (505-507) and Ex. 204/211 (367, 378) keep each group in its own register. Ex. 205/206 (368-369) shows the rescored return. In a true exchange the brass mass carries the lower theme.
**Fix (applied).** At 269-272, Hn 3-4 and Tbn 1 play movement I's head with Hn 1-2, and Tbn 2 plays it an octave lower:
```
% finaleHnThree, finaleHnFour, finaleTbnOne, bars 269-272
r8\ff g8( e'4.->) d'8( c'8 b8 | c'4->) r4 r2 | r8 g8( e'4.->) d'8( c'8 b8 | c'4->) r4 r2 |
% finaleTbnTwo, bars 269-272
r8\ff g,8( e4.->) d8( c8 b,8 | c4->) r4 r2 | r8 g,8( e4.->) d8( c8 b,8 | c4->) r4 r2 |
```
Now F1 (winds, Tpt 1-2, violins: 9 units) stands above movement I's head (four horns, two trombones, Bn 1, Vla, Vc: 10.5 units), with no register overlap. The texture in 270 and 272 (movement I's head rests after beat 1) mirrors 266 and 268.

### 2. 265-268: the doubling brass under F1 at ff (applied)
**Problem.** Eight brass play F1 ff (Tpt 1-2 and Hn 1-2 at C5, Hn 3-4 and Tbn 1-2 an octave below) against movement I's head in woodwind and violins ff.
**Evidence.** Ex. 209 (375): the supporting brass is written f under the ff melody. PDF 51: 2 Tpt + 4 Hn + 2 Tbn ≈ 12 units against 5 for the woodwind and violins.
**Fix (applied).** Hn 3-4 and Tbn 1-2 enter f at 265 (`c'2->(\f g4. a8 | …`), with equal dynamics on both shared staves. Tpt 1-2 and Hn 1-2 stay ff on F1, so cycle 21's change (trumpets on F1) is kept.

### 3. 263-264: the tonic chord of the last cadence has no fifth in the upper parts (applied)
**Problem.** Count at 263: C 16 times, E 7, G only 3 (Hn 4 G3, Tbn 2 G3, Tpt 3 G4). Above G4 the woodwind and strings play only C and E (Fl C6/E5, Ob C5/E4, Cl C5/E4, Vln I C6, Vln II E5). The sixth from E5 to C6 is empty.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 85-86: harmonic notes must not be missing in the upper parts, and an empty interval inside a forte chord is the worst fault. Every ff/fff chord in the appendix (505-507) has the complete triad in the woodwind and in the divided violins.
**Fix (applied).** Cl 2 keeps G4 from 262 (`g'1\fff~ | g'1`). Vln II takes the double stop `<e'' g''>1\fff~ | <e'' g''>1`. The G5 enters as an added part where the voices diverge (PDF 86). The chorale top line, Tpt 1 B→C, Tpt 2's E and the fff are unchanged.

### 4. 278: Tpt 1 below Tpt 2 in the final chord (applied)
**Problem.** Tpt 1 holds C5 and Tpt 2 E5, so the parts cross in the last chord. The part-combined staff hides this.
**Evidence.** In all 20 appendix chords (505-507) the trumpets are a close chord with the first on top, inside a close brass block.
**Fix (applied).** Tpt 1 keeps C5. Tpt 2 holds G4 from 277 (`g'1->\fff~ | g'4->`), and Tpt 3 holds E4 from 277 (`e'1->\fff~ | e'4->`). The trumpets now form C5-G4-E4 above the horns' C5-G4-E4-C4, with no leaps into the last chord. The crossing at 263 is protected (Tpt 2's E natural) and was left alone.

### 5. 274: timpani A♭ against the C pedal (applied)
**Problem.** Over the C pedal (tuba, Cbn and Cb at C2; B.tbn, Bn 2 and Vc at C3), the timpani rolls A♭2 under iv6/4. The drum is the strongest bass-register sound in the bar, so the low register then holds C2-A♭2-C3. That blurs the pedal, and the bar sounds like A♭ in the bass rather than a 6/4 over C.
**Evidence.** In the appendix the timpani always rolls on the bass note or root of the chord (505-507). PDF 124: close intervals in the lowest octaves sound bad. The A-A♭-G line already sounds in Ob 1, Hn 1, Tbn 1, Tpt 2 and the violins.
**Fix (applied).** `finaleTimp` at 274: `c1:32->` instead of `aes,1:32->`. The timpani holds C from 273 to 278.
