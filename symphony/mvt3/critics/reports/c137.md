## Findings

### 1. 159-165: the clarinets' broken chords duplicate the counter-line's figure in the same register (applied)
Problem: from 159 Vln II and Ob 2 play the counter-line in continuous slurred eighths, mostly broken chords (161 B4-G4-B4-D5 | F5-D5-B4-D5; 163, 164, 165 the same kind). Under it the clarinets keep their eighth-note broken chords (161 Cl 1 D4-F4-G4-F4 | D4-G4-B4-G4). Two layers in the same note value and the same figure meet in G4-C5. The counter-line is then heard as a second accompaniment, not as a line.
Evidence: Ravel (PDF 217): when melody and accompaniment are both eighth-note movement, "the highly contrasting colours throughout the piece are very necessary to separate the two elements." Dvořák (PDF 209): a light accompaniment with sustained "harmonic counterpoint." Schoenberg (PDF 230, items 2 and 5): sustained chords stand for the pedal, then solid chords, then one bar of arpeggiation to lead on; and (PDF 230-231) a countertheme made of the bar's pitches with its own rhythm. The score already has the clarinets hold at 160 and 164, so the fix only extends what the passage does every other bar.
Fix (recP.ily, concert pitch): clarinets hold the harmony 159-165 and take the eighths back at 166, where the counter-line ends.
```
recPClOne 159-166: g'1 | f'2( aes'2) | g'1 | f'1 | es'2( aes'2) | g'2( f'2) | d'1 | es'8( g' c'' g') r2
recPClTwo 159-166: es'1 | d'2( c'2) | b1 | d'2( b2) | c'2( f2) | d'2( b2) | g1 | g8( c' es' c') r2
```
Voice leading checked: the seventh F (162) falls to E♭ (163); A♭ over the N6 falls to G (164); no B sounds against the suspended C at 165 (as c062 required); Cl 2 avoids the octaves D-C with the bass at 162-163 and F-G at 163-164. Vln II/Ob 2 and the 151-158 layout are unchanged.

### 2. 197: the cellos' and violas' counter-melody runs into octaves with the tune (applied)
Problem: at 197 beats 3-4 the tune (Vln I A♭5-B♭5, Vln II A♭4-B♭4) and the counter-melody (violas and cellos A♭3-B♭3) move A♭→B♭ together. The counter-melody gives up its own line on the last two notes before the f at 198. The exposition does not do this (77: cellos B♭-C-F under E♭-F). c092 removed the same octaves from Hn 2 in this bar; the strings were left.
Evidence: Schoenberg's countertheme keeps its own line (PDF 230-231); Brahms's independent cello part (PDF 205); A&S PDF 83 (parallel octaves).
Fix (recS.ily, C template inside `\transpose c aes,`): violas and cellos 197 `g2(\mf\< a2)` and `g2(\< a2)` (were `g2( c'4 d'4)`): real E♭3-F3 → A♭3 at 198, under the tune's A♭-B♭-E♭. My first attempt (F3-D♭4) made octaves D♭-A♭ with the basses into 198; this version has none. The cellos' mf and the crescendo to f at 198 (protected) are unchanged.

### 3. 169: Hn 1 G4 a semitone under Cl 1's A♭4 in the link's V♭9 (applied)
Problem: at 169 (bass B, V6/5) Cl 1 holds A♭4 (the ♭9, held from 168 to 170) while Hn 1 moves A♭4→G4. The ninth and its tone of resolution sound together a minor second apart in two neighbouring wind voices, in an inverted ninth chord. At 170 the root is G3 in Vln II, an octave and a semitone below the A♭, which is the right spacing; 169 is the only bar with the clash.
Evidence: A&S Unit 27, PDF 469-470 (quoted above); Adler PDF 203 (fix the order of the layers: the held A♭ is the pad's top line and C minor's G arrives with the cor anglais at 171).
Fix (recP.ily): `recPHnOne` 168-169 `aes'1\>~ | aes'1\p` (was `aes'1\> | g'1\p`). 169 becomes B-D-F-A♭ with G only as a passing note in the violins; the violas' D, Hn 2's D and the diminuendo order of 167-170 (protected) are unchanged.

### 4. 126: Bn 1's counter-line meets the violas' melody at the unison (applied)
Problem: in the G-major hand-over Bn 1's counter (125-130) rises C4-D4 at 126 while the violas' F2 phrase falls E4-D4, so the two meet on D4 for a half note. In the same register and dynamic, bassoon and violas at the unison merge, and the counter-line drops out for that beat.
Evidence: Schoenberg (PDF 229): doubling at pitch, two colours "would swallow each other up." Stravinsky's bassoon counterpoint realises the harmony without touching the melody (PDF 228).
Fix (dev.ily): `devBsnOne` 126 `c'2 g2` (was `c'2 d'2`), so the bassoon goes C4-G3-A3 into 127. B3 was rejected because it makes octaves C-B with the cellos (126 C3-B2). The 123-130 hand-over itself (protected) is unchanged.
