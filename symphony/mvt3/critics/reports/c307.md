## Findings

### 1. Bars 3-4: the cellos join the scale figure (applied)

Problem: the ff chord at 1-2 is answered at 3-4 by the scale figure in Vln I (from G5), Vln II and the violas (both from G4, violas "unis."). The cellos and basses rest. The answer to a full-orchestra chord therefore has nothing below G3, and its last note, the accented G at 4.4, has no octave below it. The same happens again at 5-6 → 7-8, where the cellos then enter with a different line.

Evidence: Adler's Ex. V-17 (Hindemith, PDF 139-140) prepares the tutti chords with a forceful string unison over the full range of the section, not in the upper strings alone. Adler PDF 137 builds intensity by adding octaves. In Ex. V-19 (Shostakovich 6, PDF 141) the cellos double the violins as the violins reach their low register. In bar 4 the violins and violas descend to G4 and G3, exactly where a cello octave belongs. The movement already uses the full string unison at 9-10, so 3-4 is the place where it is still missing.

Fix, applied in /tmp/critic-307/work/sections/intro.ily (`introVc`, bars 3-4 only):

```
r4 g8(\f f8 es8 d8) c8( b,8 | c8 d8 es8 f8) g4-> g,4-> |
```

This replaces `R1*2`. The cellos play an octave below the violas with the same slurs, dynamic and accents. The double basses still enter at 9 (c279), so 9 keeps a new bottom octave. The cellos' line at 7-8 with Bn 1 is unchanged. I added a c307 comment in the file citing Adler PDF 139-141.

Protections answered:

- c058's bowing at 3-4 is unchanged. The cellos take the violas' slur pattern and start up-bow after the bar-2 down-bow.

In v307 the same figure already showed octaves between Vln I and the violas. The 6.1 entries are the same as in v307. analysis.txt still reports 0 outer-voice parallels, and the lowest pitch in 1-4 is still G1.

### 2. Bars 115-122: the pizzicato on/off-beat formula is used for the third time (not applied)

Problem: the development's bassoon solo is accompanied by cello and bass pizzicato on the beat and Vln II pizzicato chords off the beat (`r4 <bes' g''>4\pp ...`). This is the same formula as under the trumpet solo at 51-66 and 171-186. Adler presents on/off-beat figures as one of several formulas (PDF 158-159). He shows the agitated imitative inner-string accompaniment of the *Figaro* overture (PDF 160) as a different one, and the score never uses it.

Fix, if the protection is ever lifted: in `devVlnTwo` at 115-118, replace the off-beat chords with a quiet imitative eighth figure answering the bassoon. For example, write `r4 g'8(\pp a' bes'4) r4` per bar, with the violas following one beat later. Keep the cello and bass roots.

Not applied. The passage is listed as "115-118 and the bassoon solo" and "115-130" in HISTORY, without a stated reason, and the brief does not allow me to change it without answering one. The bassoon solo is already exposed and works as written. This is recorded for a future critic.

### 3. Bars 9-10: the full string unison confirmed (no change)

With finding 1 in place, the octave build-up now goes: upper strings (1-2 chord), upper strings plus cellos (3-4), the same again (5-6 → 7-8), then basses and contrabassoon added at 9. This is the order Adler gives on PDF 137: add octaves step by step towards the tutti. No change needed.
