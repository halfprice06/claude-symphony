## Findings

### 1. 195–198: the clarinets' pp return doubles the horns at the unison (applied)
Problem: cycle 57 rested the clarinets 187–194 and brought them back pp at 195. There they play `c'1 | b2 c'2 | c'2 c'2 | c'1` and `g1 | g2 g2 | g2 a2 | aes1`, at the same pitches as Hn 1 (`c'1 | d'2 c'2 | …`) and Hn 2 (`g1 | g2 g2 | c'2 a2 | aes1`), which enter p in the same bar. So one chord tone has two dynamics (pp and p), and the clarinets add no note the horns and bassoons do not already have. In the exposition the parallel bars 75–78 have horns alone: cycle 47 removed the clarinets there because "clarinets should rarely be set inside the horns" (Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 108). The recapitulation now repeats the exact layout cycle 47 removed.
Evidence: Sebesky ch. 1, PDF 12–13 (economy; Ex. 1 removes the trombone line that only thickened the lower middle); ch. 6, PDF 211–212 (a background line that only shadows another part distorts the focus; it should supply missing chord tones).
Fix: recS.ily `recSClOne = { … | aes1 | R1*12 | }` and `recSClTwo = { … | es1 | R1*12 | }` (clarinets rest 187–198, as 67–82).

### 2. 87–90: the 6/3 bridge is flat f with no push into the ff codetta (applied)
Problem: in 87–90 every part is f: the violins' and flute's scale figure (the line the listener should follow), the violas' 6/3 double stops, the bass, and from 89 the four horns' accented off-beats. Then 91 is ff with the full brass, and nothing leads to it. The double-stopped violas and the accented horns sit in the register of Vln II's figure (A3–E♭5) at the same level.
Evidence: Sebesky ch. 1, PDF 13–14 (one primary element at a time; the background must not compete); ch. 6, PDF 207–208 (carry one bar into the next; Ex. 273's quarters "give extra push into the next phrase").
Fix (clos.ily): violas `<bes es'>2\mf … | <es aes>2\< <f bes>2 | <es bes>2-> r2\! |`; horns 1–4 `r4 aes'4->\mf\< … | … bes'4->\! |` (same in each horn); `\<` at 89 ending at 91 in Vln I, Vln II, Fl 1 (`r4 f''8(\< …`), Bn 1·2, Vc, Cb, Cbn. Shared staves stay equal (Bn 1·2 both f with the same hairpin; Hn 1·2 and 3·4 all mf). The violins, flute and bass stay f.

### 3. 114: the D7 pivot leaves the climax as an ff chord fading in 1.4 seconds (applied)
Problem: after the ff cadence on F (113, half-bar rest), bar 114 holds D7 (V of G) in the strings and Bn 2 with a diminuendo and no starting level, so the players attack it at the last dynamic, ff, and must reach the pizzicato pp of 115 within one bar at 2 = 84. The way out of the climax sounds like a second ff hit and a fast fade, not like a turn to the new key and to the bassoon solo.
Evidence: Sebesky ch. 6, PDF 217–218, Ex. 284: after the forte tremolo lift, the drop is printed (to mp) and coincides with the change of colour; ch. 1, PDF 18, Ex. 2A: every level change is printed.
Fix (dev.ily): 114 `fis''1\p\>` (Vln I), `c''1\p\>` (Vln II), `a1\p\>` (Vla), `d2.\p\> r4\!` (Vc), `d,2.\p\> r4\!` (Cb), `d1\p\>` (Bn 2). The half-bar rest at 113 makes the p a clean subito drop. This keeps c018's bass rest on beat 4.

### 4. 9–10: the crescendo into the theme has no printed goal (applied)
Problem: winds, horns and strings play mf < over 9–10, and each hairpin stops on the rest after the accented quarter of 10, with no level printed. Only the timpani print f there. Then Hn 1·2, Cl and Bn enter at 11 mf under the strings' f, so the horns, for example, crescendo to an unknown level and then play mf.
Evidence: Sebesky ch. 1, PDF 18, Ex. 2A, and ch. 6, PDF 205, Ex. 269: each hairpin ends on a printed dynamic (mp < mf, p < mf).
Fix (intro.ily): `\f` on the accented quarter of 10 in every crescendo part, e.g. `f'''2 d'''4->\f r4`, Bn 1/Vc `d'4 es'4 f'4->\f d'4->)`, Vln I `… b''8 d'''4->\f) r4`; the `\!` on the rests is removed. The mf at 11 then reads as a deliberate step under the strings.

### 5. 3–4: the scale figure is bowed differently from its return at 87–90 (applied)
Problem: the strings' figure at 3–4 is f with ten eighths and the accented G under one slur (`r4 g''8(\f … b'8 | c''8 … f''8 g''4->) g'4->`). At 87–90 the same figure at f is four notes per bow with the accented top note bowed separately (c018 applied Adler's rule, PDF 39, there). c018 left 3–4 as "short enough", but 3–4 is the same length and level as 87–90, which c018 split for exactly that reason.
Evidence: Sebesky ch. 6, PDF 214–215, Ex. 281: every voice that plays one figure has one articulation; ch. 6, PDF 206: the recurrent figure unifies the score only if it is recognisably the same. Adler part 1, PDF 38–39.
Fix (intro.ily, Vln I, Vln II, Vla): `r4 g''8(\f f''8 es''8 d''8) c''8( b'8 | c''8 d''8 es''8 f''8) g''4-> g'4->` (and the same an octave lower). This touches the strings' answer at 3–4, not the protected bars 1–2.

### 6. 19: the contrabassoon enters with no dynamic (applied)
Problem: pOneCbsn enters at 19 on the bass line after eight bars' rest. The last printed level is the mf < of 9–10. The bass in Vc/Cb is f and Bn 2 mf.
Evidence: Sebesky ch. 1, PDF 18 (every entry carries its level); the entry script found no other case.
Fix (pOne.ily): `pOneCbsn = { R1*8 | es,2\mf c,2 | …`, mf like Bn 2 on the same line.
