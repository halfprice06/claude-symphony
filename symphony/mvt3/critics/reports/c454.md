## Findings

### 1. 259–260: the seventh of iiø6/5 has no brass, string or tenor part, and the bass has a tenth gap above it (applied)

**Problem.** In the last cadence (I6–vi–iiø6/5–Ger6–V7–I), the chord at 259–260 is F–A♭–C–D, ff with a crescendo. The parts are:
- **D**: Tpt 1·2 (D5), Hn 1·2 and Hn 3 (D4), Tbn 1 (D4), Fl 1·2, Ob 1, Cl 2, Bn 1 and Vln I.
- **F**: tuba, contrabassoon, basses, B.tbn, B.cl., Bn 2 and cellos, plus Tpt 3 and the violas.
- **A♭**: Hn 4 and Tbn 2, plus the arpeggios.
- **C**: only Ob 2's held C5.

The chord therefore sounds mostly as a diminished iio6. Above the bass's F2 octave, the next held note is A♭3, a tenth higher, at ff. The weighted scan gives C 0.5 against D 9.5 and F 7.5, the weakest held tone of any long loud chord in the score.

**Evidence.** PDF 51: in forte one trombone equals two horns, and one horn equals two oboes, so Ob 2 cannot hold its note against a brass D. PDF 85: "The bass should rarely lie at a greater distance than an octave from the part directly above it … It is necessary to make sure that the harmonic notes are not lacking in the upper parts". PDF 86: "Nothing is worse than writing chords, the upper and lower parts of which are separated by wide, empty intervals, especially in forte passages". PDF 82 (note in the image): doubling the bass "creates an effect of heaviness".

**Fix (finale.ily, finaleBassTbn, with a c454 comment).** Change `a,1~ | a,1 | f,1~ | f,1 \breathe | aes,1\<` to `a,1~ | a,1 | c1~ | c1 \breathe | aes,1\<`.
- The bass trombone plays C3 at 259–260. It moves by a third in each direction (A2–C3–A♭2), and returns to the bass with the tuba at 261, as c393 set it.
- The brass chord is now complete: C3, A♭3, D4, F4, D5 over the tuba's F1. The C has the weight of a trombone, and the gap above the bass becomes a fifth, F2–C3.
- F stays in six bass parts (tuba, contrabassoon and basses on F1; Bn 2, B.cl. and cellos on F2).

**Protection.** "Last cadence 259–263" (c192, c199, c200) protects its harmony and, per c189, Hn 3's D4→E♭4 and Hn 4's A♭3. The harmony is unchanged: this change makes the protected iiø6/5 audible. Hn 3, Hn 4, the Tpt 1 B→C at 262–263, all dynamics, the breath and the 263 fff are unchanged. c404 and c428 also changed parts inside 259–263 on the same terms.

**Checks.** par8.py over 256–264 shows only the bass octave doublings, and fewer than before because the bass trombone leaves them. The all-pairs check adds only parallel fourths (compound 11ths) between the bass trombone and the violas at 258→259, which is not a fault between upper parts. The gap and balance scans no longer flag 259–260.

### 2. 40–42: the flutes hold a tritone p as the chord thins out (applied)

**Problem.** At the end of the counterstatement's diminuendo, Fl 1 holds A♭5 (protected since c201) over Fl 2's D5 for three bars. This is a diminished fifth in one colour, and from 41 the flutes are the top of a thinning chord.

**Evidence.** PDF 90, rule 4: "Concords (octaves, thirds and sixths) and not discords … should be given to instruments of the same kind or colour, except when discords are to be emphasised". The diminuendo is not meant to emphasise a discord. PDF 91: in widely divided four-part harmony, use pairs of two colours in register order.

**Fix (pTwo.ily, pTwoFluteTwo, with a c454 comment).** Change `d''1~\f | d''1~\> | d''1~ | d''1\p` to `d''1\f | f''1~\> | f''1~ | f''1\p`.
- At 40 the two flutes move in contrary motion: B♭5→A♭5 over D5→F5, from a sixth to a third.
- At 40 the woodwind now lie in overlaid pairs: Fl A♭5/F5, Ob D5/A♭4, Cl F4/D4, Bn D4/B♭2.
- D is still held in Bn 1 (39–42), Ob 1 (40) and Cl 2 (to 41).
- Fl 1's A♭5, the drop-out plan of c237, the dynamics and the transition's F5 at 43 are unchanged. Fl 2's F5 now leads into the flute's own F5 there.
- The bass is held, so there are no parallels with it.

### 5. 259–260: Tpt 3 and the violas double the bass F of an inverted seventh chord (kept)

### 6. 40: oboes in a tritone for one bar, D5 over A♭4 (not applied)

### 7. PDF 53, compound colours (observation, no change)

### 8. PDF 93–94, three of a family with the low member (not used)
