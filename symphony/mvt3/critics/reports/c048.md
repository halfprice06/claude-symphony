## Findings

### 1. 243–246: the horn call stays f while the rest of the orchestra crescendos into 247 (applied)
**Problem.** Tpt 1·2 and Hn 1·2 play the owed horn call (E–C–B→C) at f with no hairpin. The strings, woodwinds and the timpani roll (p<f) all crescendo under the stringendo, and the brass then enter ff at 247. The melody is the one part that does not grow towards the goal.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 53–54: 243–246 is a four-bar hypermeasure, and its goal is the next strong bar, 247 (I after V4/3, Allegro molto). The crescendo belongs in the line that carries the phrase. The page image (p. 14) shows hairpins in every staff except the two that have the tune.
**Fix (sHead.ily).** Add a hairpin from the B at 244 into the ff at 247. Hn 1·2 end theirs together on beat 4 of 246, so the part-combiner keeps them a2 at 247. Without that, Hn 2's pickup G3 split the staff into two voices at 247–250 in my first build.

sHeadTptTwo = { R1*8 | e'2->(\f c''2~ | c''4 b'2.~\< | b'2 c''2~ | c''2.) r4 | }

### 2. 273–274: the held C on top and the C pedal are re-struck with accents on the weak bar 274 (applied)
**Problem.** At the Largamente, Fl 1 (C6), Tpt 1 and Hn 3 (C5) play `c1-> | c1->`, and the timpani play two separate accented rolls. The bass winds and cellos also re-accent C at 274. Cycles 32 and 34 describe this C as "held" (do-not-touch: "held C6 over 273–274"), but it is written as two attacks of equal weight. In 274 the event is the iv6/4 (F♯→F, A→A♭ in the chain), and the re-struck C covers it.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 50: the start of a note is stronger than its continuation, and chromatic notes carry their own accent. PDF 53: bar 2 of a four-bar group (273–276) is weak. The c045 rule in this score also says that rolls on one pitch are tied. 273–274 was the only untied roll left in the coda.
**Fix (finale.ily).** Tie Fl 1 `c'''1->~ | c'''1`, Tpt 1 `c''1->\ff~ | c''1` and Hn 3 `c''1->~ | c''1`. The timpani become one roll, `c1:32->~ | c1:32~ | c1:32\<~ | c1:32`. B.cl, B.tbn, Tuba, Cbn and Vc re-strike C at 274 without an accent. Bn 2 keeps its accent, because it shares a staff with Bn 1's moving F. The chain voices keep their accents at 274. All pitches, the quotation at 275–276 and fff at 278 are unchanged.

### 3. 255–262: Hn 3 and Tpt 3 accent every half note under the last chorale (applied)
**Problem.** Since c019, Hn 3 and Tpt 3 repeat the chorale's notes as marcato minims with `->` on both beats of all eight bars. When every beat is accented, the accent no longer separates strong from weak. Bar 1 of each pair of bars, where the doubled-value chorale changes note, is no stronger than any other beat.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 50: metre is a pattern of accented and unaccented beats, and in 2/2 the first half note is the strong one. PDF 53: the chorale's notes, one per two bars, make two-bar groups. The pulse is already continuous in the violas' quarters and the basses' eighths.
**Fix (finale.ily).** Keep `->` on the first minim of each bar and drop it on the second, e.g. Tpt 3 `c'2->\ff c'2 | c'2-> c'2 | e'2-> e'2 | …` and Hn 3 `e'2->\ff e'2 | …`. Hn 4 is held and unchanged. This keeps c019's repeated minims and changes only the accent pattern.

### 4. 62 and 182: the diminuendo after F2's peak sits on the last quarter only (applied)
**Problem.** At the climax of the trumpet solo (62) and of the cor anglais and muted trumpet (182), the peak note is mf. The `\>` is attached to beat 4 and then cut by the breath mark, so beats 1–3 stay mf, beat 4 gets a one-quarter diminuendo, and then comes p at 63/183. The flute doubling has the same placement. On the page (p. 3) the hairpin is a stub under the last note.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 54–55: the accented dissonance (cycles 1 and 11 made F2's peak an appoggiatura) carries the stress, and its resolution is lighter. PDF 52: B♭–A♭–G♭–F is one falling group from the peak into 63. The diminuendo should cover that group.
**Fix.** In sOne.ily, Tpt 1 `bes''4\mf aes''4\> ges''4 f''4) \breathe | es''2.(\p` and Fl 1 `bes''4\mf aes''4\> ges''4 f''4) | es''2.(\p des''4)`. In recS.ily, the cor anglais and Tpt 1 get `g''4\mf f''4\> es''4 d''4) \breathe | c''2.(\p`, and Fl 1 gets the same placement. The breath marks are kept.
