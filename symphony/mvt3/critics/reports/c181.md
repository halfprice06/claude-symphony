## Findings

### 1. 126-127: parallel 5ths between the bassoon's counter-line and F2 in the violas (applied)
- **Problem.** In the G-major hand-over, the violas play F2 (E4-D4 at 126, E4 at 127). Bn 1's dolce counter-line leaps C4 to G3 on 126 beat 3 and then moves to A3 at 127. The result is G3/D4 followed by A3/E4: consecutive perfect 5ths in half notes, p, between two legato lines, with only the harp underneath.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 331: "5ths and octaves created by chordal skips count as faulty voice leading and must be avoided. Never write chordal skips without checking for forbidden parallels."
  - A&S PDF 343, points 8 and 12: the unaccented forms are too weak to break 5ths.
  - The scan finds these as the only 5ths between a tune and a counter-line outside tutti doublings.
  - An earlier comment in dev.ily already warned against G3 here. c137 brought it back to avoid two other faults: a half-note unison with the violas, and C-B octaves with the bass.
- **Fix.** On 126 beats 3-4, Bn 1 plays D4-A3 instead of a G3 half note. The A3 is tied into 127 (an anticipation):

  ```
  devBsnOne … | c'2 g2 | a2 b4 c'4 | … → … | c'2 d'4 a4~ | a2 b4 c'4 | …
  ```

  The 5th A3/E4 is now reached by oblique motion (A&S PDF 342, Ex. 20-27). The fix also keeps c137's two concerns away:
  - B3 is not used, because C4-B3 against the bass C-B would be 8ves.
  - D4 meets the violas' D4 for one quarter only, not a half note.

  I also tried a rest on beat 3 (Bn 1 resting while Cl 1 takes the tune). It pushes the score to 17 pages, so I rejected it.
- **Protected passage.** The hand-over 123-130 (Vc → Vla → Cl 1 → Vln I/Fl 1, and the harp) is protected. That protection covers the order of the theme's entries and the harp. Neither changes here: the fix alters one beat of the bassoon's accompanying counter-line, and F2 is untouched.

### 2. 25-26: the violas' figure doubles the leading tone and falls an augmented 2nd at the deceptive cadence (applied)
- **Problem.** On 25 beats 3-4 the violas' eighths are G3-C4-D4-B3. This doubles two notes of the tune: the suspended C (Vln II C4 at 25.3) and its resolution, the leading tone B3 (Vln II B3 at 25.4). The violas' B3 then falls to A♭3 at 26, as V7 moves to VI. That is raised 7 dropping to ♭6, an augmented 2nd, while the other B3 rises to C.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 340, section 15 (Ex. 20-24): figuration in minor uses the melodic-minor forms by direction unless the harmony requires otherwise. Nothing here does.
  - A&S PDF 202, Unit 13: in V-VI the 3rd of VI is doubled so that 7 moves to 8, which "prevents parallel 5ths or (in minor) an augmented 2nd".
  - A&S PDF 341, section 16: avoid unisons made by figuration. The violas' B3 joins Vln II's B3 in unison.
  - The violas play mf under the f tune at the movement's first cadence, so this is not hidden.
- **Fix** (pOne.ily, pOneVla):

  ```
  … | b8( d' f' d') g8( c' d' b) | aes8( c' es' c') r2 | → … | b8( d' f' d') g8( d' f' d') | c'8( es' aes' es') r2 |
  ```

  The figure now arpeggiates V7 without the suspended C or a second B. D4 steps to C4, the doubled 3rd of VI, as A&S prescribes.
- **Checks.** No new 5ths or 8ves with Vln II, Cl 1, the horns or the bass (I checked the alternatives F4-E♭4 and B3-C4; both make unisons with Cl 1 or Vln II). The protected F1 articulation and bowing 11-26 belong to the tune and are unchanged.
- **Themes.** F1, F2 and the chorale top line are unchanged by both findings, and so are all quotations. There is nothing to tell the other movement threads.

No other principle in the reading is broken by the tunes themselves. F1, F2, theme B and both chorale lines use figuration as the unit describes, and A&S does not support a change to them. Most of the remaining melodic material is on the do-not-touch list.
