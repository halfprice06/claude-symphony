## Findings

### 1. 141–145: the dashed "cresc." lines run across the page turn 143|144 with no word after the turn (applied)
**Bars:** 141–145, page 7 → page 8. Parts affected: Bn 1·2, Cbn, timpani, Vla, Vc, Cb.
**Problem:** c268 turned the long hairpins over this turn into "cresc." with dashes (MOLA PDF 12). LilyPond does not repeat the word on a broken text spanner. Page 8 therefore opens with six bare dashed lines running to the f at 145, and a conductor who has just turned the page cannot tell what they mean. The parts book has the same behaviour wherever such a line crosses a turn.
**Evidence:** MOLA PDF 7 says the page layout must be planned around what the player reads after the turn. PDF 8 says to proofread the horizontal lines of the score, not just the vertical ones. Ex. 7 (PDF 12) writes the change as words, "cresc. molto". A continuation that drops the word loses what the word was there to say. The usual engraver's practice is to restate it in parentheses.
**Fix (applied):** global.ily now defines `c405-broken-cresc`. It runs the default `dynamic-text-spanner::before-line-breaking` and sets `bound-details.left-broken.text` to "(" + the spanner's text + ")". score.ly Staff context: `\override DynamicTextSpanner.before-line-breaking = #c405-broken-cresc`. parts-book.ly gets the same override in its Staff layout. It is a general rule, but in the score it changes only bar 144: all six staves now read "(cresc.) - - - f". Hairpins and MIDI are unchanged.

### 2. Harp "étouffez" crosses the barline at 134, 243 and 279 (applied)
**Bars:** 134 (text ran into the 134|135 double bar and key change), 243 (into the 243|244 barline), 279 (the 278|279 barline cut through the word).
**Problem:** these are the anchored-text collisions MOLA Ex. 7 shows. The word is about 9 mm wide, and in these bars it started at the rest and ran past the next barline (at 279 it was right-aligned and ran back across the previous one). The damping points at 82 and 131 already fit and are left alone.
**Evidence:** MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7, PDF 12. Each damping point is unchanged: 134.3 (c206), 243.1 (c116), 279.2 (c386).
**Fix (applied):**
- dev.ily 134: `r2 r2-\tweak self-alignment-X #CENTER ^\markup \italic "étouffez"`
- sHead.ily 243: `r1-\tweak extra-offset #'(-1.2 . 0) ^\markup \italic "étouffez"` (CENTER moved it across the 242|243 barline, so I tested it and replaced it).
- finale.ily 279: `r4 r4-\tweak self-alignment-X #CENTER -\tweak extra-offset #'(0.6 . 0) _\markup \italic "étouffez" r2\fermata`. The word is now below the LH staff, where the bar has no other mark; above the staff it would hit the fermata.

At 220 dpi all three now sit inside their bars. Page count and system breaks are unchanged.

### 3. 187: Ob 1's "1." is in plain type; every other solo number in the score is bold (applied)
**Problem:** recS.ily wrote `\markup \column { "1." \italic "dolce" }`. Every other entry ("1." from the part-combiner, and the `\markup \bold "1."` instances in clos, dev, sOne, sTwo and recS) is bold. MOLA PDF 8 asks for proofreading for consistency.
**Fix (applied):** `\markup \column { \bold "1." \italic "dolce" }`.
