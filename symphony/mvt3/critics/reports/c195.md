## Findings

### 1. Rests 128-134 and 135-142 are split into single bars in every part (root cause of the cor anglais compression) — applied
Bars 128-142, all parts that rest there. c187 fixed the score's breaks with `\repeat unfold 7 { s1 \tag #'score \noBreak }` (128-134) and `\repeat unfold 8 { ... }` (135-142) in global.ily. After `\removeWithTag #'score`, each unfolded copy is its own sequential block, so `\mergeRests` (c095) cannot join the skips and every part prints fifteen single-bar rests there. The cor anglais part spent a whole system on 129-138 and its page was over-full by 10 staff-spaces; the bass clarinet lost another system. MOLA PDF 5 and 7 want one numbered rest per silence and no crowding; Gould PDF 31-41 assumes uncompressed spacing for every rule above (beam and ledger spacing are fixed proportions of the stave-space, which compression does not respect for the systems around them).
Fix (global.ily lines 40 and 43): write the repeats out flat, as 205-210 already are:
`s1*5 \tag #'score \break s1 \tag #'score \noBreak` ×7, and `s1 \tag #'score \noBreak` ×8 `s1 \tag #'score \pageBreak ...`.
The score is unchanged (all 16 pages compared as images: identical). The cor anglais now has single rests 123-134 and 135-144 and fits its one page with no compression. Consequences checked: Tpt 1 and Tpt 3 moved their page breaks and became over-full by 0.0 and 0.2 staff-spaces, so they take `system-system-spacing.padding = #0.5` (as Flute 2 and the cor anglais already do); the timpani part drops from 3 pages to 2 with its one turn in the rest 160-162.

### 2. Bass clarinet part over-full by 20.5 staff-spaces — applied
Whole part. Since c187's doubling at 27-34 the part no longer fits one A4 page; LilyPond squeezed it and the compression put letters D and E on the notes of 31-32, J into the 129 system, N under the staff, and "Più allegro" and "poco a poco stringendo" under the 199 system (MOLA PDF 8). `ly:page-turn-breaking` ignores `page-count`, so it kept choosing one page. Fix in parts-book.ly: `\paper { page-breaking = #ly:optimal-breaking first-page-number = 2 }` (MOLA PDF 6: a part that starts on page 2 reads as one open spread with no turn, as Bassoon 1 already does) and `{ s1*150 \pageBreak }` in its score, so page 2 holds 1-150 and page 3 holds 151-279 (the recapitulation). Every mark now stands at its bar. One added A4 sheet side in one part; no score page.

### 3. Cor anglais part: "poco a poco più animato" (205) printed under the solo at 183-184 — applied
In the one-page cor anglais part the 199-204 and 205-210 rests are short, so the 205 tempo text was stacked above "Tempo I" of 199 and ended up just below the 176-184 system, under the hairpin of the cor anglais' own melody, where it reads as an instruction for 183 (MOLA PDF 8: floating markings). Spacing tempo marks horizontally for the whole part (as `\markLengthOn`) pushes it to two pages, so I used a local break instead: `{ s1*204 \break }` in the cor anglais \score. The text now sits over 205-210; still one page, no warning.

### 4. Percussion part: tempo texts at 205, 243 and 247 stacked over earlier marks — applied
Same fault as finding 3, three times: "poco a poco più animato" (205) hung under 149-170, "poco a poco stringendo" (243) and "Più allegro" (247) under 211-220. The part is one page with room to spare, so its \layout takes `\override MetronomeMark.extra-spacing-width = #'(0 . 1.0)` and `extra-spacing-height = #'(-inf.0 . +inf.0)` (the MetronomeMark half of `\markLengthOn`). Each tempo text now stands over its own bar; still one page. (Applied to all parts, the same override added pages to ten parts and compressed the cor anglais, so it stays local.)

### 5. Flute 1 at 222: G6-A♭6-B6 under a horizontal beam — applied
Bar 222 (last bar before the arrival), Fl 1 `r8 g'''8( aes''' b''')`. All three notes lie beyond the first ledger line and span a third, so Gould PDF 35 asks for a 1/2-space slope, PDF 36 for the beam to rise with the interval, and PDF 35 ("beams outside the stave ... matching angles look better") for the same angle as the other beams. Vln I plays the same notes at the same pitch in the same bar with the 1/2-space rise (override since c105); the flute's beam was flat in the score (stems up on the shared staff) and in the part (stems down). Fix in codaTwo.ily: `r8 \once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25) Beam g'''8( aes''' b''')`. `\offset` adds the slope to LilyPond's default position, so one command serves both the stems-up score and the stems-down part (c105 needed separate tagged values at 199). Score page 12 changes in this beam only; no sound change. This is inside 216-223 only in the sense of bar numbers: no pitch, rhythm or dynamic of the protected approach changes.

### 6. Score beams otherwise correct — no change
Every manual `Beam.positions` override (199, 201, 206, 210, 222, 242, 246, 247-252, 264-267) still matches its notes: seconds beyond the ledger lines slope 1/4 space, wider intervals 1/2, and each end sits on, hangs from or centres on the middle line (Gould PDF 31-35). LilyPond's own quanting handles the rest (rows 1-8, 13-22). I found no beam in the middle of a space, no beam crossing more than one stave-line in a short group, no divided wide-interval group, and no ledger-line collision in shared staves. A further change here would be marginal, so I made none.
