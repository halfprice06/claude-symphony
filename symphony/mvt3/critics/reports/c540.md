### 3. Theme B at 211–216 in three octaves (tested on paper, declined)
- Adler PDF 189 calls a three-octave spread "most desirable" for power. Theme B is in two octaves (Tpt 1, Ob 1, Cl 1 and Vln II's upper half; Fl 1·2 and Vln I an octave higher). The only free part for a third octave at movement II's own pitch is the violas.
- Declined. The violas would leave the held D♭-major chord, and the echo at 217–218 (C.a. and Hn 1·2) would lose its point: it is now the one statement at movement II's pitch, in movement II's instrument (Adler PDF 188, the solo chosen for its register and intent). The ff two-octave statement already carries over the held brass.
