The appendix itself contains no rule about breathing. The breathing findings below apply the range tables' footnote (what is "usually called for in orchestral playing") together with the breathing passages in Adler part 1: flute PDF 188 ("the flute requires a great deal of breath ... give the flutist time to rest after especially difficult or sustained passages", with Brahms 1/iv dovetailing the two flutes so the first can breathe), oboe PDF 198 ("frequent rest periods mandatory"), contrabassoon PDF 230 ("the amount of breath it takes to produce these notes is considerable ... provide quite a few periodic rests"), brass PDF 292 ("allow frequent intervals of rest"; "in a loud passage one should not phrase too many notes on one breath in a slow tempo"), and tuba adler2 PDF 8 ("frequent rests are strongly recommended").

## What else I studied

I measured every staff's longest stretch without a rest from score.midi with a script (/tmp/critic-066/scripts/breath.py; part-combined staves merge both players, so I checked each candidate in the .ily files), checked the focus passages bar by bar (110–113 Fl 2/Ob 2, 115–122 bassoon solo, 171–186 violas, 247–279 high oboe and clarinet, bass clarinet and contrabassoon throughout), and viewed pages 2–4 and 12 of the scratch build.

Focus passages with no fault: Fl 2 G5 and Ob 2 E5–C5 at 110–113 sit in the middle register and are easy at ff (PDF 303–304). The bassoon solo 115–122 stays between B♭2 and G4, has its breath at 118, and Bn 1 has bar 114 free after the ff at 113. The violas' sul tasto eighths 171–186 (E♭3–C4, four to a bow, 0.8 s per slur at 2 = 72) are easy, follow arco playing at 167–170, and return with "ord." at 187 as PDF 312 gives it. The high oboe and clarinet notes in the coda are inside the professional column. The bass clarinet plays only 18 notes, so endurance is not an issue. Range is not a problem anywhere in the score. Breathing is: in seven places a wind or brass part holds for 20 to 58 seconds with no rest or breath mark.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 211–230: low winds and lower brass hold for 34–38 s at ff–fff with no breath (applied)
- Problem: Bn 1·2 and Cbn play whole notes from 211 to 230 with no rest (37.9 s; 211–222 at 2 = 72 with allargando, 223–230 at 2 = 56). Tuba, B.tbn, Tbn 1·2, Hn 3·4 and Tpt 3 hold 211–228 (33.6 s) and include ff/f at 211–218 and fff at 223. Only the melody (Tpt 1·2, Fl, Ob 1, Cl 1) has a breath, at 214. Fl, Ob and Cl hold 215–222 (tied D♭ 216–218, then 219–222) without a breath before the fff at 223.
- Evidence: Adler PDF 230 (contrabassoon low notes need "quite a few periodic rests"), PDF 292 (loud, slow passages: not too many notes on one breath), adler2 PDF 8 (tuba), PDF 188 (flute).
- Fix (applied): `\breathe` at the end of 214 in every held part that lacked it (Bn 1·2, Cbn, Tpt 3, Hn 3·4, Tbn 1·2, B.tbn, Tuba, Ob 2, Cl 2): `codaTwoTuba = { des,1\ff\> | des,1\f | ges,,1 | aes,,1 \breathe | f,,1 ...`. `\breathe` at the end of 218 in every wind and brass part, before the sub. p (for example `des'''1) \breathe | c'''1\subP` in Fl 1). `\breathe` at the end of 225 (the repeated C) in Bn 1·2, Cbn, Tuba and B.tbn: `arrivalCbsn = { c,,1\fff | c,,1\f | c,,1 \breathe | b,,,1 ...`. The breaths fall where the harmony changes or a note is repeated; nothing is removed from the sound, and 222→223 is untouched.

### 2. 51–86: Bn 1 holds a pp pad for 58 s; Cl 1·2 for 27 s (applied)
- Problem: Cl 1·2 and Bn 1 hold whole notes pp under the trumpet solo through all of 51–66. Bn 1 continues through 67–82 and into the f at 83–86 with no rest (36 bars, 58 s). The recapitulation already fixed this (cycle 6: Cl 1 rests at 178, Cl 2 and Bn 1 at 182). The exposition did not get the same fix.
- Evidence: Adler PDF 230 (periodic rests for low winds), PDF 198. A pad at pp cannot be broken audibly, so the rests have to be written in and staggered.
- Fix (applied): as at 178/182, a quarter rest on beat 4 for Cl 1 at 58 (`d'2. r4`), Cl 2 at 62 (`ges2 bes4 r4`) and Bn 1 at 62 (`es2 d4 r4`). These fall where the solo already breathes (breath marks at 58 and 62). In 67–82: Bn 1 at 70 (`c2 es4 r4`) and 78 (`c2 d4 r4`), Bn 2 at 74 (`bes,2. r4`; the double basses carry the bass).

### 3. 27–42: Fl, Ob and Cl 1·2 hold an f pad for 23 s (applied)

### 4. 167–204: Cl 1·2 and Bn 1·2 have no break into the second theme; Bn 1 plays 183–204 without a rest (applied)

### 5. 239–254: clarinets and Bn 1 lack the breaths the oboes and brass have (applied)
