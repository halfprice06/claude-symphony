## Findings

### 1. Concave beamed groups are not horizontal: Gould's concave rule applied to the whole score (applied)

Bars (groups that change on the page): 27 Vln I, Vln II, Cl 1·2 (F5-D5-B4-D5, stems down); 36-38 Bn 1, Vla, Vc; 75 Vln I; 110 Vc; 161-164 Vln II (D-B-G-B, E♭-C-G-C); 168; 202; 258 and 260 Vln II (E-C-A-C).

Problem. Gould PDF 36 (c): a group whose inner note lies closer to the beam than both outer notes is concave and takes a horizontal beam; PDF 38: in a chord the note nearest the beam counts. LilyPond makes a group horizontal only when an inner note passes the first note in the direction opposite to the beam's slope; in every other concave group it only reduces the slope. So at 27 the violins' F5-D5-B4-D5 (B4 is nearest the beam below) slopes down about 1/2 space, while 29's convex B4-D5-F5-D5 correctly slopes up: the page reverses Gould's distinction. c105 fixed this by hand only for the clarinet figures in pOne and recP (`\override Staff.Beam.damping = #+inf.0`); the rest of the score never got the rule.

Fix (score.ly, before `\layout`): a callback that sets the beam's damping to 100000 (flat) when the group, with the note nearest the beam per stem, begins and ends on the same staff position (PDF 36 (a)) or has an inner note strictly closer to the beam than both outer notes (PDF 36 (c)). It acts only on groups of three or more notes with all stems in one direction (no kneed or cross-staff beams), and manual `Beam.positions` overrides take precedence. It sits at Staff level so c105's `\override Staff.Beam.damping = #+inf.0` still wins where it is set.

```
#(define (gould-concave-damping beam)
  (let* ((heads (lambda (s) (let ((h (ly:grob-object s 'note-heads))) (if (ly:grob-array? h) (ly:grob-array->list h) '()))))
         (stems (filter (lambda (s) (pair? (heads s)))
                        (let ((a (ly:grob-object beam 'stems))) (if (ly:grob-array? a) (ly:grob-array->list a) '()))))
         (dir (ly:grob-property beam 'direction 0))
         (close (map (lambda (s) (apply max (map (lambda (h) (* dir (ly:grob-property h 'staff-position 0))) (heads s)))) stems)))
    (if (and (> (length close) 2) (not (= dir 0))
             (every (lambda (s) (= (ly:grob-property s 'direction 0) dir)) stems)
             (or (= (car close) (last close))
                 (let ((lo (max (car close) (last close))))
                   (any (lambda (p) (> p lo)) (cdr (drop-right close 1))))))
        100000 1)))
...
\layout {
  \context { \Staff \override Beam.damping = #gould-concave-damping }
```

(The scratch copy also has a logging branch keyed to `-dgould-concave-log`; it is silent in a normal build.) 120 groups meet the rule; most of them (repeated notes in the basses at 199-210 and 247-277, two-pitch figures) were already flat, and about fifteen change on the page. Each change checked at 250 dpi: the flat beams hang from or sit on a line (PDF 31). Parts: parts-head.ily copies score.ly up to `\layout`, so the definition reaches the parts, but parts-book.ly needs the same `\context { \Staff \override Beam.damping = #gould-concave-damping }` line in its layout for the parts to match. I did not build the parts.

### 2. Ledger-line groups left flat at the middle line (applied)

Bars: 90 Fl 1 and Vln I (B♭5-C6-D6-E♭6); 101 Vln I and 104 Fl 1 (B♭5-C6-D♭6-E♭6); 125 Vln I (C6-D6-E6-F♯6); 206 Vln II (B♭5-C6-D♭6-E♭6); 250 Fl 1, Vln I, Vln II (C6-D6-E6-D6); 253 Fl 1, Vln I, Vln II (G6-F6-E6); 265 and 267 Vln II (D6-C6-B5).

Problem. Gould PDF 35: when every note of a group lies beyond the first ledger line, "the beam takes only a slight slope": 1/4 space for a second, 1/2 space for any wider interval; PDF 36: the slope follows the outer interval; PDF 35: beams outside the stave should match the other angles. With down-stems reaching the middle line, LilyPond pins both ends to the middle line and the beam goes flat, so a rising scale prints like a repeated note. The score already applies this rule by hand at 199, 201, 206 (Vln I), 210, 222, 247-252 and 264-267 (Vln I) (c105, c195), but missed these groups. The worst cases are 206, where Vln I slopes and Vln II on the same figure is flat, and 265 and 267, where Vln I has `#'(0.25 . -0.25)` and Vln II, on the same D6-C6-B5 in the next staff, is flat. At 90 the Vln II beam rises a third below while Vln I and Fl 1 stay flat.

Fix, in the score's existing convention (beam centred 1/4 space above or below the middle line, so it sits on or hangs from it, PDF 34); `\offset` where the staff is shared, as c195 did at 222:
- clos.ily, closVlnOne and closFluteOne (90): `\once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25) Beam bes''8 c''' d''' es''')`
- dev.ily, devFluteOne (104) and devVlnOne (101): `\once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25) Beam bes''8 c''' des''' es'''`; devVlnOne (125): `b'' \once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25) Beam c''' d''' e''' fis'''`
- codaOne.ily, codaOneVlnTwo (206): `\once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25) Beam bes''8 c''' des''' es''')`, the same as Vln I there
- finale.ily, finaleVlnOne, finaleVlnTwo, finaleFluteOne: 250 `\once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0) Beam c'''8-. d'''-. e'''-. d'''-.` (outer interval a second: 1/4 space); 253 `\once \offset positions #'(0.25 . -0.25) Beam g'''8( f'''8 e'''8)`
- finale.ily, finaleVlnTwo 265 and 267: `\once \override Beam.positions = #'(0.25 . -0.25) d'''8( c'''8 b''8`, the same as Vln I

A comment in score.ly lists these bars. Checked at 250 dpi on pages 5, 14 and 15; pages 6 and 11 (125, 206) only by pixel difference at 100 dpi.

### 3. Cl 1's espressivo line 159-164 has flat beams against the same line in Vln II (applied)

Problem. c105's `\override Staff.Beam.damping = #+inf.0` covers the clarinet staff from 151 to 170. It was meant for the leggiero figures of 151-158 (its own comment names E♭-G-C-G, D-F-G-F, D-G-B-G, all concave). It also flattens Cl 1's espressivo line at 159-164, which doubles Vln II note for note: E♭5-D5-C5-B4 (159), F5-D5-B4-G4 (161-162), D5-B4-G4-B4 and so on. Here Gould PDF 36 (beam in the direction of the outer interval) and PDF 35 (matching angles) apply. On page 9 the violins' groups slope and the clarinet's are all flat, so the same line is engraved two ways in one system.

Fix (recP.ily): move `\revert Staff.Beam.damping` from the end of recPClOne and recPClTwo to the start of 159 (`es'8( g' d' g') r2 | \revert Staff.Beam.damping es''8(\mf...` and `c'8( es' b d') r2 | \revert Staff.Beam.damping es'1\mf`), with a c285 note under c105's comment. The concave groups in 159-164 (D-B-G-B, E♭-C-G-C, F-A♭-D♭-A♭ with stems up) stay horizontal through Finding 1, so Cl 1 now matches Vln II wherever the stem direction is the same. At 159.3 Cl 1 (stems up) C-E♭-G-E♭ is concave and stays flat while Vln II (stems down) is convex and slopes. Both follow PDF 36-37. I left pOne's override (11-26) as it is: every group there is a leggiero figure.

### 4. Repeated two-pitch figures stay horizontal (no change)

171-186 Vla (E♭-G-E♭-G, D-F-D-F) and 170 are horizontal. Gould PDF 37 allows either slope or horizontal for equal numbers of each note, and says horizontal "can look better in repeated figuration". 277 Cb C-C-C-G is horizontal because the odd note is farthest from the beam (PDF 37). No change.
