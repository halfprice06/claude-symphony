## Findings

### 1. Parts: rehearsal letters N, O and R are printed in the wrong system (applied)
Bars 199, 211, 247 (parts only). Adler PDF 299, item 4: "Chaos may result if a rehearsal number ... is omitted because it occurs while an instrument is resting. Make certain that every player has detailed information about what is happening every moment"; PDF 301: proofread the parts. In the v235 cor anglais part, N (199) stands under the staff of the 175 system, about 2.5 cm above the 184 system and left of its rest; O (211) stands under bar 184; R (247) sits in the 239 system over "poco a poco stringendo". In the tuba part O is about 3 cm right of the 211 barline. Cause: the score-only paddings `\once \override Score.RehearsalMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #2` (K, N, R) and `#9` (O), added in c185 and c225 to stack the score's letters above their tempo text, were not tagged, so they also act in the parts' compressed rests.
Fix (global.ily, lines for K 151, N 199, O 211, R 247): `\tag #'score \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #2 \mark \default` (and `#9` at O). The score is unchanged pixel for pixel (all 16 pages compared); in the parts N, O and R now stand at their barlines, and two of the three compressed-page warnings of the v235 parts build (cor anglais p. 1, Horn 1 p. 2) are gone.

### 2. Tempo marks over the timpani and percussion choir (applied)
Every tempo change (1, 49, 51, 81, 83, 149, 151, 199, 205, 211, 221, 223, 235, 243, 247, 271, 273). Adler PDF 294: "It is strongly urged that tempo markings be placed at the top of the score and repeated in the space separating each choir"; Ex. XVIII-1 (PDF 293) prints the tempo four times, over woodwinds, brass, timpani/percussion and strings. The score has it at the top, over the brass (c055) and over the strings (c045), but not over its percussion choir, the choir most dependent on the tempo and the one furthest from both the brass and the string marks (on page 13 the timpani sit about 12 cm below the brass mark, with the tuba's ledger-line notes in between).
Fix (score.ly, the timpani/percussion StaffGroup): `\new StaffGroup \with { \consists "Metronome_mark_engraver" } <<`. Tempo text only, no rehearsal letter, as in Adler's example. The marks fit in the existing gap above the timpani on every page (page 1: "Allegro con fuoco" above the four-line timpani label, clear of the tuba's A♭1); still 16 pages.

### 3. Barlines through the percussion choir (applied)
Every bar with timpani and cymbals/bass drum visible (pages 1, 13, 15, 16). Adler PDF 294: "Measure lines should be drawn only through the individual complete choirs"; in Ex. XVIII-1 the barlines run through timpani, xylophone and triangle/snare as one choir. v235 removes the Span_bar_engraver, so the timpani and the drum staff look like two unrelated staves although they share a bracket. Movement I removed the span bars because texts above its one-line staff cross the barline; the finale's drum staff carries no text in the score (its only texts are part-only cues), so that reason does not apply here.
Fix (score.ly): drop `\remove "Span_bar_engraver"` from that StaffGroup (same line as finding 2). Checked at 223, where the cymbal fff above and bass drum ff below the line stand clear of the joined barline. This differs from movement I's layout; the movement I thread may want the same change once its percussion texts are moved.

### 4. The timpani label gives the number of drums (applied)
Bar 1. Adler PDF 292: "Timpani: the number of drums should be given at the beginning of the score" (Ex. XVIII-1: "6 Timpani"). The label lists the tunings but not how many drums; the comment in intro.ily shows the plan is four drums (32", 29", 26", 23"), and the movement uses only G2, A♭2, C3 and E♭3 throughout.
Fix (intro.ily, introTimp): `\italic "4 timpani: G, A♭, C, E♭"` in place of `"Timpani: G, A♭, C, E♭"`.

### 5. Parts: "animando" (81) runs into "Tempo I" (83) (applied)
Bars 81-83 (parts only). Adler PDF 299 item 4 (the resting player must read each change) and PDF 301 (proofreading); Gould PDF 55 (characters at least half a space apart). In the parts that rest 81-82 and enter at or after 83 (Trumpets 2 and 3, bass clarinet, tuba, Oboe 2), the two-bar rest is narrow and "animando" ends against "Tempo I": in Trumpet 2 the gap is about 0.3 mm.
Fix (global.ily, bar 81): `\tag #'part \once \override Score.MetronomeMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #1 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "animando"`, so the word moves to its own tier instead of widening the bars. I first tried giving all parts' tempo texts horizontal room (extra-spacing-width, as the percussion part has); that added a page to eight parts and was dropped. With the chosen fix every part keeps its page count and page turns; the score is unchanged.
