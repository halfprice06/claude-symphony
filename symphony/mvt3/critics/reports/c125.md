## Findings

### 1. Missing "a2" where pairs enter in unison after rests: Tpt 1·2 at 271, Hn 1·2 at 273 (applied)

Problem: Tpt 1 and Tpt 2 rest 269-270 (c124) and come back together at 271 on `d'4( g'8 a'8 b'4 d''4)` ff. Hn 1 and Hn 2 rest 269-272 and come back together at 273 on `g'2-> fis'2`. Both pairs are engraved on one stem with no label, because the part-combiner was already in unison before the rests and does not print the label again. A conductor reading 271 or 273 cannot tell whether one player or both enter. On the same page the Ob 1·2, Cl 1·2 and Hn 3·4 entries at 269 and the Tpt 1·2 return at 275 do carry "a2", so the two entries that lack it are inconsistent with the rest of the close.

Evidence: Gould PDF 65: in double-stemmed writing, two parts with the same note value may share a notehead only "as long as they take two stems". A single stem with no label shows one part. On a part-combined staff the "a2" label does the job of the second stem.

Fix (finale.ily, applied): in `finaleTptOne` and `finaleTptTwo`, `\partCombineApart R1*2 | \partCombineAutomatic d'4(\ff ...`. In `finaleHnOne` and `finaleHnTwo`, `\partCombineApart R1*4 | \partCombineAutomatic g'2->\ff ...`. The score now prints "a2" at 271 (Tpt 1·2) and at 273 (Hn 1·2). No sounding change, and the parts are unchanged.

### 2. Upper strings at 273 do not state their dynamic next to the cellos' f (applied)

Problem: at T (273, Largamente) the strings change texture: Vln I, Vln II and Vla go divisi into measured tremolo, and the Vc enters on its tremolo C with `\f` (c119: Vc/Cb f under the divided upper strings). Vln I, Vln II and Vla have no marking at 273. Their last dynamic is ff at 265, eight bars, a rehearsal letter and a tempo change earlier. The only string dynamic printed at 273 is the cellos' f, so the bar reads as if the whole string group drops to f, which is the opposite of c119's plan. In the Vla part, 273 starts the last system with no dynamic in view.

Evidence: Gould PDF 54: spacing and symbols should let the eye read the music without contradiction. The strings are the one group where 273 shows an f in one staff and nothing in the staves above it. The c075 MOLA fix (restating dynamics after rests) was made for the same reason: a player or conductor should not have to search back for a level at a change of texture.

Fix (finale.ily, applied): `<g'' c'''>2:32->\ff^\markup \italic "div."` (Vln I), `<c'' g''>2:32->\ff^\markup \italic "div."` (Vln II), `<e' g'>2:32->\ff` (Vla). This is a restatement, not a new level: the crescendo 275-277 to fff at 278 is unchanged, and the Vc keeps its f.
