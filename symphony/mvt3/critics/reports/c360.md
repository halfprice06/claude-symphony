### 4. Ex. 2A's colour assignment: the bass clarinet never carries the motto in the finale (decided: not applied)

Sebesky says to give each line to the instrument that suits it and to spread colours across the whole score (PDF 11, 17–18). Movement I's motto starts in bass clarinet and cellos (I 1–4), and its tail returns in the bass clarinet at I 113–116 and II 32–34. In the finale the bass clarinet plays 53 notes, and every major-mode motto statement is in brass (224–228, 255–264) or in the head rhythm (265–272). I worked through, on paper and without building it, the one place where the bass clarinet could double the motto at movement I's own pitch: G2–E3–D3–C3–D♭3–C3 under Tbn 1·2 at 224–228. Against the bass C–C–B–A–F it makes no parallels. I decided against it for three reasons. Under f brass and fff timpani it would not be heard (Sebesky PDF 11, tonal balance). 223–234 is protected ("no more inner layers"). And mvt1.md asks the finale not to add another bass-clarinet motto tail at E♭3. Fix: none.
