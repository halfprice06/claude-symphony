## Findings

**1. Bars 95-96, Vln II and violas: a slur joins two notes of the same pitch and reads as a tie.** `closVlnTwo` has `g'1\mf\>( | g'1)` and `closVla` has `bes1\mf\>( | bes1)`. On the page the curve looks exactly like the cellos' and basses' tie in the same bars (crop of page 5). Adler says a slur means one bow (PDF 37). A re-struck note inside one bow is louré, which needs a dash on each note (PDF 42-43, Ex. II-30). As printed, players will hold the note, while the MIDI re-strikes it. The harmony holds G and B♭ under Vln I's E♭-D♭, so the hold is the right reading, and it matches the low strings. Fix: `g'1\mf\>~ | g'1` and `bes1\mf\>~ | bes1`. **Applied.**

**2. Bar 114, cellos and basses: the bowing does not prepare the pizzicato on 115 beat 1.** After the f stroke and rest at 113, the players take the dotted half of 114 as a down-bow. That leaves the bow at the tip with one quarter (0.36 s) to the pizzicato. Adler (PDF 54): "it is much easier to change from arco to pizzicato, if one figures out the bowing so that the players will have an up-bow stroke just before the pizzicato in order to arrive at the frog". The violas (two beats, c085) and Vln II (one quarter and a beat) have more time. A diminuendo on an up-bow goes against the habit Adler describes on PDF 38, but at p over 1.07 s that is easy, and this is the case where he says to mark a bowing. Fix: `d2.\p\>\upbow r4\!` (Vc) and `d,2.\p\>\upbow r4\!` (Cb) in `dev.ily`. **Applied.** (170→171 starts from a tied note and 50→51 from a seven-bar held note with *change bow at will*, so the bow cannot be planned there. Left as they are.)

**3. Bars 199, 211, 219, violas: the divisi marks do not follow the rule.** Since *unis.* at 139 the violas have played only single notes, so *unis.* at 199 cancels a divisi that is not in force. *div.* at 205 starts the chords, and they run without a break to 222. *div.* is printed again at 211 and at 219, and the 211-222 system shows the 219 mark a second time. Adler (PDF 30): mark *div.* where division starts and *unis.* where it ends. Fix: remove `^\markup \italic "unis."` at 199 (`codaOneVla`) and `^\markup \italic "div."` at 219 (`codaTwoVla`, `<c' dis'>1\p`). I keep the 211 mark as a courtesy, because it is the first bar after the page turn (page 12 starts at 209), after a double bar and a new key. The violas' *unis.* at 223 is unchanged. **Applied.**

**4. Bar 229, violas: *change bow at will* on notes that already change bow.** `arrivalVla` has `g2\cbaw bes2 | g1 | a1 | ...`: unslurred, untied half and whole notes at Maestoso. Adler (PDF 37): with no slur, every note takes a new bow. On PDF 38 he says to mark bowings only to counteract habit and "without overmarking the parts". The instruction is right where it sits over ties (141-146, 217-218, 235-238, 255-256), but here it tells the player nothing. Fix: `g2 bes2`. **Applied.** I kept it on the chorale's whole notes at 224 and 226, where ff whole notes of 2.1 s can take a hidden change of bow.

**5. Bar 124, violas: the slur runs through "arco, unis.".** The slur that starts on the D4 at 124.4 rises through the comma between "arco" and "unis." (page 7, top system). Neither Adler's examples (PDF 34, 39, 54-55, where *arco* and *pizz.* stand clear of slurs) nor Gould allow a collision. The `extra-offset 1.3` written for an earlier version no longer clears it, and 2.6 did not either (tested). Fix: replace `\once \override TextScript.extra-offset = #'(0 . 1.3)` with `\once \override TextScript.avoid-slur = #'outside \once \override TextScript.outside-staff-priority = #600`, so the text is stacked outside the slur. **Applied.** The text now stands clear above the slur.

**6. Measure numbers at 110, 147, 212, 259, 263 and 278 sit lower than the rest of the row.** Where a dynamic below the bass staff reaches further down (ff with an accent at 147, f at 212, fff at 263 and 278), the skyline pushes that number down. MOLA p. 4: "Placement of measure numbers should be the same throughout the work." I tested `BarNumber.staff-padding` 7.5 and 6.5 (from 5.5) in both places in score.ly. Both give 17 pages, so I reverted it. **Not applied.** It costs a page and the numbers can still be read. If the composer wants it, use a fixed offset, `\override BarNumber.outside-staff-priority = ##f \override BarNumber.Y-offset = #-9`, only in the systems that contain those bars, and check the page count.

No quotation, theme (F1, F2, the chorale top line) or protected passage is changed. All four applied changes are notation only. In the MIDI, only finding 1 changes anything (95-96 Vln II and Vla no longer re-strike), and that fits what is printed.
