## Findings

**1. 269–270: Haydn's device at a new place. Tested, not better, not applied.**
Problem examined: in the second C-major statement of F1, the flutes double the tune at C6 with Ob 1 and Vln I. Haydn (PDF 288) moves the flute in the second phrase to double an inner line two octaves higher. The inner line here is the head-rhythm figure in the violas, cellos, Bn 1 and Hn 3·4.
Test: in /tmp/critic-513/work/sections/finale.ily, Fl 1 and Fl 2 at 269–270 changed from `c'''2->( g''4. a''8 | bes''4-.) a''8( g''8 f''4-.) e''4-.` to `r8 g''8( e'''4.->) d'''8( c'''8 b''8 | c'''4->) d'''2.->`. The build had 0 warnings, 0 outer-voice parallels, par8 clean on 268–271 and 16 pages.
Why it is worse: the MIDI listing of 269–270 shows that the figure already sounds ff in Hn 3·4 (E4–D4–C4–B3) as well as the violas and Bn 1, so it does not need help to be heard. Moved to the flutes, the figure sits above the tune (E6 and D6 over G5–A5) at the coda's last F1 statement. It also makes a major second, C6 over B♭5, at the top on 270.1. Reverted. The scratch copy now matches v513.

**2. Heterophonic doubling (PDF 189, Ex. XV-34; PDF 264, 273–274). Not used in the score. Checked at four places and not applied.**
This is the one doubling device in the reading that the score does not use. I checked every unprotected restatement:
- 269–272 (Fl 1 in eighth-note neighbours around the tune, e.g. `c'''8 b'' c''' d''' g''4. a''8`): at ff in the top octave it gives seconds against Vln I and Ob 1, with no audible separate line.
- 181–183 (Fl 1 decorating the C.a./muted-trumpet peak, as in Ex. XV-34): the unison flute was placed there by c041 and c210 so the cor anglais is not overshadowed. An ornamented line at p would draw the ear from the pair.
- 67–76: the plan's build by addition (flute and oboe only from 77) would be lost.
- 187–190: c224 and c227 say "no further thickening there."
Adler offers heterophony as one option among doublings, not a rule, and none of these places needs it. I leave the score as it is.
