## Findings

### 1. Bars 97-98, Cl 1·2 staff: two whole rests float above Cl 2, with no "2." (applied)
Problem: c144 took Cl 1 out at 97-98, but wrote the rests as `r1 | r1`. The part combiner keeps the two voices apart, so Cl 1's whole rests print high over the staff above Cl 2's E4 (whole note tied to a dotted half). The staff does not say which clarinet holds the E. The oboe and bassoon staves in the same bars are labelled "1." and "2.".
Evidence: Adler PDF 291 point 1 (the score must be "as unproblematical as possible"); PDF 301 Ex. XVIII-10a (a shared staff labels the player who plays).
Fix (clos.ily): `closClOne = { ... | bes'1\mf\>~ | bes'1 | \partCombineSoloII R1\! | R1 \partCombineAutomatic | }`. The full-bar rests on their own did not do it (I tried that and the voices stayed apart); `\partCombineSoloII` is the same device Bn 1 uses at 107-109 in dev.ily. The staff now shows Cl 2 alone, labelled "2." with p. The Cl 1 part still shows its rests and the end of its hairpin.

### 2. Bars 61, 77, 105: soloists on shared staves carry no "1." (applied)
Problem: the part combiner prints "1." only when its state changes. When a part plays alone, rests with its partner, and then plays alone again, the label is not repeated, even after many bars or a page turn. Fl 1 at 61 (last label at 43, a page earlier); Fl 1 and Ob 1 at 77 (last labels at 43 and 47, 26 and 30 bars earlier, after two tempo changes and a key change); Fl 1 and Ob 1 at 105 (labelled at 101 on page 5, unlabelled after the turn to page 6). Each shows one unlabelled line on a "1·2" staff, which reads as a2. The recapitulation's parallel passage has its "1." (Fl 1 at 181). So the score is inconsistent with itself as well as unclear.
Evidence: Adler PDF 295 ("all the instruments must be clearly marked in all systems appearing on a page"); PDF 301 Ex. XVIII-10a; App. A PDF 312 ("1. (first only)").
Fix: score-only markups, as dev.ily already uses at 125-126. sOne.ily `f''4(\p\<-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1."` at 61; sTwo.ily the same on Fl 1's and Ob 1's `es'''2.(` / `es''2.(` at 77; dev.ily on Fl 1's `f'''8(\mf` and Ob 1's `f''8(\mf` at 105. No layout change.

### 3. Bar 58: Cl 2 and Bn 1 lack the breath that 178 has (applied)
Problem: c126 gave Cl 2 and Bn 1 breath marks at 178 (recapitulation). The exposition's 58 has none, though both players hold pp from 51 to 62 beat 3 (about 20 s at 2 = 72) with no rest. c141 and c144 rewrote 58 in both parts (Cl 2's moving quarters, Bn 1's D3-F3), so it is the natural place, and matches 178.
Evidence: Adler PDF 291 point 3 (every detail for every instrument) and PDF 301 (proofreading score and parts: parallel passages must agree).
Fix (sOne.ily): `bes4( aes4 f4 aes4) \breathe` in sOneClTwo and `d2 f2 \breathe` in sOneBsnOne. Cl 1's rest on 58.4 already gives it a breath. The comma sits beside that rest on the shared staff without colliding.

### 4. Ob 1 and Hn 1-2 parts: no cue before long-rest entries at 77 and 75 (applied, parts only)
Problem: Ob 1 enters at 77 (p <, the climax doubling) after 26 bars' rest (51-76), across the Poco meno mosso, a key change to six flats and back to three. Hn 1-2 enter at 75 (p pad) after 24 bars' rest across the same changes. Their parts show only numbered rests. The cycle-075 cues cover other such entries (harp 67, Tpt 1 167, etc.) but not these.
Evidence: Adler PDF 299 item 6 ("Cues are often necessary to facilitate re-entry after a long period of rest... the cue should be transposed to fit the part"); Ex. XVIII-7 (PDF 300).
Fix (sTwo.ily, tag 'part, removed from score and MIDI): `sTwoOboeOne = { R1*8 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring "vlnOne" #UP { R1*2^\markup \tiny "Vln. I" } } | ...` (Vln I B♭5-G5 | F5-E♭5-G5-B♭5); `sTwoHnOne`/`sTwoHnTwo = { R1*6 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring "vc" #DOWN { R1*2^\markup \tiny "Vc." } } | ...` (the cellos' counter-melody, which comes out at written F4-G4-F4 | E♭4-D4-C4-E♭4 in the F parts). I checked the built parts: the notes are correct, there are no warnings and the page turns are unchanged.

### 5. Bars 75-86, Hn 1-2: no breath since c144's pedal (applied)
Problem: since c144 the horns play from 75 to 86.2 without a rest: eight bars at 2 = 72 with a crescendo at 82, then the tied mf B♭ pedal 83-86 (about 18 s). Neither score nor parts show where to breathe. The obvious point is the barline into F (83), where the phrase and tempo change.
Evidence: Adler PDF 291 point 3; c046/c066's practice of marking breaths in long holds.
Fix (sTwo.ily): `es'1\< \breathe` (Hn 1) and `g1\< \breathe` (Hn 2) in bar 82. The hairpin still ends on the mf at 83.
