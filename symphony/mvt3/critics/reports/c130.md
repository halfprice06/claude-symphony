The finale recalls it in Tpt 1, Ob 1, Cl 1 and flutes (211–216), then the horns answer at movement II's own pitch, A♭3–D♭4–F4–G♭4 (217–218). The finale already uses that blend at 171–186 (muted Tpt 1 with the cor anglais, owed item 1's "repeat"). At 217 the dynamics fall to f and Hn 1 is marked espress., so this is where the cor anglais can be heard joining the line.
