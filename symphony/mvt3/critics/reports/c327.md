## Findings

### 1. 107-109: the lament bass is heard only as a bass, never as a line (applied)

Problem. The development's emotional centre is the chromatic lament F-E-E♭-D-D♭-C (107-109, i - V6 - v6 - IV6 - iv6 - V, p <) under the violins' 7-6 suspensions. It is the only fully chromatic tetrachord in the movement, but it sounds only at the bottom of the texture: Bn 2 F3, cellos F3, contrabassoon and basses F2. Above it the violas hold C4-C4-B♭3-A♭3-G3, the suspension line G5-F5-E5 is in Vln II with Ob 1 and Cl 1 (c213), and Vln I holds C6. Between C4 and G5 nothing moves, so the lament reads as a bass progression and not as the melody it is. Bn 1 and all four horns rest in these three bars (Bn 1 held A3 at 105-106; the combiner prints Bn 2 as solo).

Evidence. Adler, PDF 283 (Fidelio, Ex. XVII-22): "Study each doubling, for often a bass part is doubled several octaves higher". In the example (image, PDF 283) the lower voices are carried by divided violas and cellos in the singers' own register. PDF 288 (Haydn, Ex. XVII-24): in the second phrase the flute "doubles the tenor two octaves higher", and "devices like these add great variety". Bars 107-109 restate 99-106's material over a new bass, so this is the place for a new doubling. Adler's bassoon register (adler1 PDF 226, cited by c131) puts C4-B♭4 in the "thin, but intense" band. That is the right colour for a lament and keeps the line below the suspension voices.

Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-327/work/sections/dev.ily). Bn 1 doubles the lament two octaves above the contrabassoon and basses, F4-E4-E♭4-D4-D♭4-C4, p <, written apart from Bn 2 on the shared staff with the same p < as Bn 2 (equal dynamics):

```
devBsnOne = { ... | r4 a2.\p | a2 a2 | \partCombineApart f'2\p\< e'2 | es'2 d'2 | des'2 c'2 | e4\ff c'8( bes aes g f g) | ...
```

The previous bars were `\partCombineSoloII R1*3 \partCombineAutomatic |`. Bn 1 moves from its held A3 (106) up to F4 and from C4 (109) to E3 at 110, which is unchanged. Voice leading: the new line is an exact doubling of the bass, so its interval with every other part is the bass's interval, already checked by c092, c187 and c213. It is a sixth to a third under the violas' C4-G3 line, does not cross the suspension voices (G5-E5), and sounds no tone of resolution against a suspension: the doubled bass notes D and C at 108.3 and 109.3 are not the resolutions F and E. The only new lines in par8.py over 105-111 are Bn 1's octaves with the bass at 107.3-109.3, which is the doubling itself, as Bn 2's lines already were.

Protections answered. "Lament bass 107-110" and "lament spacing 107-109" (c053, c092, c132, c187, c212) are protected. The bass, the strings, the suspensions and their winds are unchanged. As with c213, which answered the same protection, only a doubling is added. c213's order of entries is kept: Bn 2 already played the lament from 107 with the strings, and the brass still enter only at 110. Hn 1·2 rest 107-109 as c239 left them.

### 2. 87-90: both halves of the bridge in one colour (considered, not applied)

Adler's first guideline (PDF 257-258, Ex. XVII-1 and XVII-2) and Mozart's colour alternation (PDF 267, Ex. XVII-10 item 2) suggest stating the scale figure at 87-88 in the strings and answering it at 89 in the winds. I decided against this. The bridge is a crescendo chain into the ff codetta at 91, and a crescendo needs instruments added, not exchanged: taking the violins out at 89 would thin the chain. c304 has just added Ob 1 at 89-90 for the same build. The 6/3 bridge is also protected (c147, c162, c187, c198, c212). No change.

### 3. 244: the accompaniment moves with the horn call (considered, not applied)

At 244 the strings and Bn 1 change chord on beat 2, with the call's C→B. This is the call's own rhythm, which PDF 259-260 warns against. But 243-246 is protected (c303), the chord change belongs to the cadential 6/4 → V4/3 harmony (c022, c082), and the violins' scale at 245.3 already moves while the call holds. No change.

### 4. 147-150: four bars of held ff dominant (checked, no change)

Every part holds the dominant in whole notes; only the trumpets restrike. This is a stop before the recapitulation, not an accompaniment, so ch. 17 does not apply. A moving inner line here would anticipate the stretto material that 139-146 has just finished, and c313 rejected a 6/4 neighbour. No change.
