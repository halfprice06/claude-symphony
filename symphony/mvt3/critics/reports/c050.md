## Findings

### 1. 274→275: the horns break off the 5–♯4–4–3 chain one note before its end (applied)
**Problem.** The finale's half of the cross-movement chain is G–F♯–F–E over C – F♯°7 – iv6/4 – I. The scherzo gave its statement (B4–A♯4–A4–G♯4, 357–360) to Hn 1 alone and then held the G♯. In v50 the finale's Hn 1·2 play G4–F♯4 (273) and F4 (274). At 275 they leap down to C4 to double the scherzo quotation, so the horn line never reaches E. This happened because c029's quotation (Hn 1·2 at C4) was written before c032 put the chain into Hn 1·2 ("273–275 … in Hn 1-2 a2", finale.ily comment). finale.md still says the chain is in Hn 1·2.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 125–126: in a phrase passed between parts, each part's last note coincides with the next part's first note. Here the horns' F has no continuation in the horns. Steinberg PDF 16: proper handling of parts. Rimsky PDF 128 (law of register): the statement a major third below the scherzo's Hn 1 belongs in the same or a lower instrument, and here that is the horns at G4. Losing the octave C4 does no harm. The quotation keeps C5 (Tpt 1·2, Cl 1) and C6 (Fl 1, plus Vln I after Finding 2), and Rimsky PDF 125 needs no third octave, because the middle register is full of held chord tones.
**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-050/work/sections/finale.ily):**
```
finaleHnOne … | f'1-> | e'1~\< | e'1 | c''4-> r8 d''8 c''4-> r4 | …
finaleHnTwo … | f'1-> | e'1~\< | e'1 | g'4-> r8 g'8 g'4-> r4 | …
```
(was `c'4->\< g8-. g8-. c'4-. e'4->~( | e'4 d'8 des'8 c'4-.) r4` in both). The held E4 is not in an octave of the quotation's D–D♭ (D5, D6), so c039's rule still holds.
**Quotation change, for the other threads:** the scherzo quotation at 275–276 no longer includes Hn 1·2.

### 2. 275–276: the scherzo's theme is quoted without its own instrument (applied)
**Problem.** The scherzo's theme A is a Vln I theme: p leggiero at 13, 105, 253 and 305 (themes-scherzo.md). In v50 its only full return in the finale (275–276) is in trumpets, clarinet, flute and horns, and the first violins hold a tremolo chord under it. The pitches survive, but the colour that marks it as the scherzo's theme does not.
**Evidence.** Rimsky's preface, PDF 20: tone colours are "inseparable" from an idea "from the hour of its birth". Steinberg repeats this point, PDF 16. Every other quotation in the finale keeps its source colour or makes a planned change: 199–204 keeps movement I's layout, 224 keeps Tbn 1 at movement I's pitch, and 143–145 keeps the scherzo's timpani. The violins can take the quotation at no cost. Vln I already holds C6 at 273–275, so by Rimsky's rule (PDF 125–126) the held C6 becomes the quotation's first note. The chain's E5 on the 275 downbeat is kept in the Vln I double stop and is also in Vln II and Ob 1·2.
**Fix (applied):**
```

```
(was `<e'' c'''>1:32\< | <e'' g''>1:32`). The quotation is now in Fl 1 + Vln I (C6) and Tpt 1·2 + Cl 1 (C5). Vln II, violas and cellos keep their tremolo.
**Quotation change, for the other threads:** add `%@ scherzoHeadC VlnOne 275` to coherence/themes-finale-quotes.ly and delete the `HnOne 275` and `HnTwo 275` lines. ### 3. coherence/finale.md does not describe the score as it is now (composer to edit; text given)
**Problem.** Rimsky's last preface (PDF 23) asks for a plain account of what is in the score. finale.md is the record the other three threads rely on, and it is wrong in six places:
- (a) The header says "Updated … for v41".
- (b) "Themes quoted from other movements" has no scherzo entries. The timpani's motive x at 143–145 appears only as a request in the scherzo's bar numbers, and theme A's head at 275–276 only inside the Ending paragraph. Item 2 says "211–218 is movement II (see below)", but there is no section below.
- (c) The Ending says G–F♯–F–E is in Hn 1·2. Before this cycle the horns stopped on F (Finding 1).
- (d) It says "the top descends G–F♯–F–E–D–D♭–C". The top voice is the held C6 (Fl 1, Vln I) at 273–275. The descending line runs at the E5 level: Ob 1·2 and Vln II E5 → Tpt 1·2 E5–D5–D♭5–C5.
- (e) It says the lower violas play C–A–A♭–G. They play E4–A3–A♭3–G3.
- (f) Cl 1 (c047) is missing from the E–E♭–F–E line.

Two more problems are outside finale.md. themes-mvt1.md (theme-p row) says movement I's tutti layout is "turned to major (199–210)". In the finale 199–210 is the C-minor tutti, and the major form is at 247–254. That is a relay item for the movement I record. Separately, the finale's plan and HISTORY call the eighth-note scale figure at 3–4, 43–50 and 101–106 "motive x". The scherzo's motive x (crotchet, two quavers, crotchet), which the finale quotes at 143–145, has the same name.

**Fix (text for finale.md; the composer edits it):**
- Header: "Updated for v51 (279 bars, 2/2, about 7:04; scherzo checked at v29, movement II at v201)."
- Add after item 12: "Scherzo (v29): 13. 143–145, timpani `g,4 g,8 g,8 g,4`, the retransition motive x (scherzo Timp 241, 249) a major third lower. 14. 275–276, theme A's head (scherzo Vln I 13–15) in C, turned to major, note values kept and rebarred: Fl 1 + Vln I C6, Tpt 1·2 + Cl 1 C5." Add "Movement II (v201): 15. 211–218, theme B's second phrase (II bSec 41–44, cor anglais) in doubled values an octave higher in Tpt 1 + Ob 1 + Cl 1, flutes an octave above, ending held on D♭." Change item 2's "(see below)" to "(item 15)".
- Ending sentence: "G–F♯–F–E in Hn 1·2 (holding E4 through 276), Tpt 3, Tbn 2, upper violas (ending on the thread pitch E4), Ob 1·2, Vln II and Vln I (lower note, to F5); inner lines C–A–A♭–G (Fl 2, Cl 2, Tbn 1; Tpt 2 to A♭), E–A–A♭–G (lower violas) and E–E♭–F–E (Hn 4, Bn 1, Cl 1); C held on top (Fl 1, Vln I C6; Tpt 1 C5; Hn 3 C5) 273–274. The chain's E5 (oboes, Vln II) passes to Tpt 1·2's E5 on beat 4 of 275, so the line runs G–F♯–F–E–D–D♭–C at the E5 level, under the top C6."
- In the plan and comments, call the finale's scale figure "the scale figure (3–4)", so that "motive x" means only the scherzo's motive.
