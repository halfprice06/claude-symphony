## Findings

### 1. Bar 47: Hn 4 hands its held A♭3 to Cl 2 with a cut, not a crossfade. Applied in scratch.
- **Bars:** 43–47 Hn 4; 47–50 Cl 2.
- **Problem:** Over the B♭ pedal, Hn 4 holds A♭3 pp, dolce, from 43. Cl 2 enters on the same A♭3 pp at 47.1. The horn then simply stops at 47.3. The pedal is otherwise flat and very quiet (pp, string harmonic, sul tasto), so the horn's release is the one event in the texture, and it falls on an unmarked beat of the bar. As written, a held tone changes colour in a step: the horn stops and the clarinet is left alone.
- **Evidence:** Casanelles PDF 149 describes the crossfade: the incoming dynamic layer starts at the level where the outgoing one ends, so the listener hears one continuous sound whose colour changes. On PDF 153 and 155 he says that overlapping two notes, so that the second attack is blurred, is the right legato for a sustained accompaniment under a solo line, as opposed to the lyrical swell. The overlap is already in the score (two beats). What is missing is the outgoing horn's fade. With it, the two beats become a crossfade from horn to clarinet in the bars just before the trumpet solo.
- **Fix (applied, sections/trans.ily):**
  ```
  transHnFour = { aes1\pp~^\markup \italic "dolce" | aes1~ | aes1~ | aes1~ | aes2\> r2\! | R1*3 | }
  ```
  No notes changed. Hn 3 rests in this section, so the shared Hn 3·4 staff has no conflicting dynamic. The MIDI is effectively unchanged.

### 2. 265–275: the basses' repeated eighths. Tested on paper and declined.
- **Reading:** PDF 150 and 152 (the machine-gun effect of identical repeated attacks).
- **Decision:** I did not add accents. The pulse already gets its accents from the timpani (c4-> r8 g,8) and from the upper parts. Players vary the attacks of unaccented repeated eighths themselves. Accents would turn the f support layer into a competing voice under the ff combination at 265–272, against principle 13 (PDF 290). c438 also kept these bars as they are.

### 3. 49–50: a morendo on the B♭ pedal before the trumpet solo. Declined.
- **Reading:** PDF 149–150 and 160 (an evolving held note).
- **Decision:** I did not mark a morendo. Fading the dominant pedal under the poco rit. would weaken the V of E♭ minor just before the solo. The pedal already stops on 50.3, which gives the solo a breath.

### 4. 278: accents on the fff final chord against a held level (compression, PDF 290–291). Checked; no change.
The rolls, the tremolo and the crash already hold the level after the attack. The accents mark the attack of the last chord, as the cadence at 263 does.
