### 1. 77.1-2: F2's climb to its B♭6 climax stands on a plain I6; it should be V6/5 of IV (applied)

**Problem.** In the E♭-major F2 statement the tune holds E♭6 at 77 and leaps to its peak B♭6 at 78. Under it the bass rises G→A♭ (77.1→77.3), but the chord over G is E♭/G (I6), so the semitone has no leading-tone function, and 76.3–77.2 is one E♭ chord across a barline at the point where the phrase should press forward. The first phrase (67-74) already has applied chords to ii (70.3) and V (73.4). The answering phrase has only the one to V at 80, and nothing before its climax.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 419 and 439 (point 1): any major or minor triad may be preceded by its applied V. The missing printed page 432 and Exercise 1b (PDF 439) give the specific use: the tonic turned into V7 of IV by adding ♭7. Ex. 25-21b (PDF 429) shows the 8-♭7 passing motion over I that introduces an applied 6/5 as "a rather frequent substitution". PDF 424 (Ex. 25-4): with the leading tone in the bass of an applied V6/5, the chromatic D♮→D♭ (bass D at 76.1 → inner D♭ at 77.1) is acceptable, and the passing E♭ chord at 76.3 softens it further (PDF 421, Ex. 25-6). PDF 419 and 439 (point 3): do not double the temporary leading tone or the 7th.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-412/work/sections/sTwo.ily).**
- Violas 77: `des'2(\mf c'4 f'4)` (was `bes2(\mf c'4 f'4)`). D♭4 is the 7th and falls to C4 in its own line on 77.3. The leap G3→D♭4 is a diminished fifth followed by a step inward (C4).
- Hn 2 77: `bes2\< aes2` (was `g2\< f2`). The horn's G3 doubled the temporary leading tone; it now moves B♭3→A♭3 at the unison with Bn 1, and from A♭3 it steps to G3 at 78.
- Harp RH 77 first half: `bes8 es'8 bes'8 es''8` (was `es'8 g'8 bes'8 es''8`), so the harp does not double the leading tone either. No pedal change.

At 77.1 the chord is now complete: G (Cb, Bn 2, harp) – B♭3 (Vla, Bn 1, Hn 2) – D♭4 (Vla) – E♭ (tune and its doublings). It resolves to A♭ at 77.3 (A♭–C–E♭, F in the harp: IV with added 6th, row 9), then vi7 at 78. Tune, bass, rhythm, dynamics, slurs and the protected climax at 78 are unchanged.
