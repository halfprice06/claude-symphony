## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 259-260, Hn 3·4: the crossed pair reads as a two-note chord (applied)
Problem: since c184, Hn 3 (half notes A♭3, up-stems) lies under Hn 4's whole note C4. At 400 dpi, the up-stem of each A♭ runs past the C4 semibreve, so bar 259 looks like a half-note chord A♭3–C4 followed by a lone A♭, and Hn 4's tie looks as if it starts on the A♭. Gould PDF 65 keeps the up-stem on the upper part even where the parts cross. PDF 66 says a semibreve must not appear joined to a stem. PDF 67 says overlapping parts must be laid out so that each part's progression is visible. At this point the crossing is not needed.
Fix (finale.ily): swap the two players' notes in 259-260 and keep each player's rhythm. Hn 3: `c'2->\< c'2 | c'2-> c'2 | es'2-> es'2 | g'2-> f'2`. Hn 4: `aes1\<~ | aes1 | aes1 | g1`. Hn 3 now holds C4 from 257 and rises a third to E♭4 at 261 (it was a leap of a fifth up from A♭3). Hn 4 moves down by step, A3 (257) – A♭3 (259-261) – G3 (262). The stave shows the same pitches as c184's version (A♭3 and C4, the A♭ still in a horn as well as Tbn 2), with no crossing and no stem through a semibreve. Outer voices are unchanged and the analysis still shows 0 parallels. This changes c184's horn assignment but not its pitch content.

### 2. 257-262, Cl 1·2: Cl 2's semibreves sit on Cl 1's up-stems (applied)
Problem: Cl 1's arpeggio starts at or below Cl 2's held chorale note (E5, D5, C5, B4). The semibreve was placed on the first eighth's stem, so each bar opened with what looks like an eighth-note chord. This is the case Gould PDF 66 forbids ("semibreves in particular should not appear to be joined to stems"), and PDF 67 says to put the lower or unstemmed part on the left with space between.
Fix (finale.ily, Cl 2): `\override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #-1.3` before 257's `e''1`, and `\revert NoteColumn.force-hshift` before 263's `c''1`. Checked at 400 dpi: each semibreve now stands clear, left of the up-stem. The A♭ flat at 259 stays at the height of its eighth. No pitches changed (255-264 is protected for its flute breaths and its pitches, and both are untouched).

### 3. Bar numbers at 212, 259, 263, 278 (open since c175; applied)
Problem: a dynamic at the barline (Cb f at 212, ff at 259, fff at 263 and 278) pushed that bar's number about 1.2 spaces below the row of the other numbers. MOLA PDF 4 asks for the same placement throughout. I first tried `outside-staff-priority = ##f` with a fixed Y-offset on those four numbers. That lined them up, but the Cb dynamics and hairpins were then pushed below the numbers, which was worse, so I withdrew it.
Fix (global.ily + codaOne.ily): `bnLow = \tag #'score { \override Staff.BarNumber.staff-padding = #6.8 }`, called at the first Cb note of 209 (the page turn), so the whole row on pages 12-16 sits 1.3 spaces lower and clears those dynamics. Checked: 209-222, 253-265 and 266-279 each have one straight row. The score is still 16 pages, because these pages have room at the bottom. 110 and 147, the other bars c175 listed, are on earlier pages and are left as they are.

### 4. Parts: tempo and rehearsal marks stacked at 235-247 (partly applied)
Problem: in every part that rests at Q (Fl 2, Ob 2, B.cl., Cbsn, Tbn 1, Tuba, percussion), the one-line text "Poco più mosso *molto cantabile* (𝅗𝅥 = 66)" is wider than the compressed rest 235-241/242. "poco a poco stringendo" (243) and "R Più allegro" (247) were therefore stacked above it in a second and a third tier. They sat close to the previous system and read as if they belonged to 226-230 (Fl 2 part, page 2). MOLA PDF 8 requires parts to be proofread for colliding marks, and Gould PDF 19 measures every symbol from the stave it belongs to.
Fix applied (global.ily): `\tempo \markup \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.4) \column { "Poco più mosso" \line { \medium \italic "molto cantabile" } } 2 = 66`. In the parts, Q and R/Più allegro now share one tier, and only "poco a poco stringendo" stands one tier higher. In the score the mark reads "Poco più mosso (𝅗𝅥 = 66)" with "molto cantabile" below it, at all three copies. Not applied: `\markLengthOn` across the parts. It removes the stacking but adds a page to the Cor anglais, Harp, Hn 3 and Hn 4 parts, and a `\once` version at 235 had no effect in 2.24. The parts build now reports "page compressed by 0.0 / 0.2 staff-spaces" for Hn 4 and Tpt 2 (the taller mark). Page counts of all 30 parts are unchanged and nothing collides.
