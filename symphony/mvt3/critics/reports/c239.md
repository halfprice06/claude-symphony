## Findings

**1. Bars 70: the violas' E♮4 comes before the movement's first E♮ (a regression since v229).**

- Problem: c232 made bar 70 a V4/3 of ii, with violas and cellos both on `e'2`.
- coherence/finale.md item 3 still says the violas' E♮4 at 97 is "the movement's first E♮", so the E♭/E♮ viola thread is at one pitch at 1, 97, 219–220 and 223. Since c232 the violas reach that pitch 27 bars early, and the inflection at 97 has lost its first-time effect.
- Evidence: Rimsky's economy rule (PDF 136) is that a colour held back gains fresh interest when it arrives. Rescoring a detail instead of repeating it (PDF 118) applies here too. The harmony at 70 needs the E♮ once, not in both string parts.
- Fix: in sections/sTwo.ily, sTwoVla bar 70, change `es'4( f'4 e'2)` to `es'4( f'4 g'2)`. The cellos keep `e'2`, so the V4/3 of ii is intact and the viola thread first turns to E♮ at 97 again.
- Applied. par8 is identical to v239.

**2. Bars 103–104: the held F4 in Hn 1·2 lies inside Hn 3·4's statement of the theme head.**

- Problem: at 103–104, Hn 3·4 play the theme head (G♭4–A♭4–F4–E♭4–D♭4) while Hn 1·2 hold F4 (`f'1~\p | f'1`). The held note is in the same family, the same timbre and the same octave as the moving line, so the line crosses it twice and grinds a second against it on E♭4 and G♭4.
- Evidence: Rimsky, PDF 132–133 (Ex 264–268): a florid part against a held fundamental "in the same register" is discordant; a different timbre makes it milder, and between woodwind and brass the basis lies an octave from the melody. The fanfare rule (PDF 115–116) gives the same advice: a brass phrase takes woodwind harmony.
- Fix: in sections/dev.ily:
  - devClOne: `c''4->) c'4-> | R1*2 | r4 f'8(\mf` → `c''4->) c'4-> | f''1~\p | f''1 | r4 f'8(\mf`
  - devHnOne: `R1*2 | r4 g'2.\p | aes'2 g'2 | f'1~\p | f'1 | r4 c''2. |` → `R1*2 | r4 g'2.\p | aes'2 g'2 | R1*2 | r4 c''2.\p |`
  - devHnTwo: `f'1~\p | f'1 | r4 a'2. |` → `R1*2 | r4 a'2.\p |`
  - The Cl 1 pedal F5 is now an octave above the horn theme, in another colour.
  - The first scratch build reported one false outer-voice octave at 104, because the Cb's staccato D♭2 at 104.4 left only Vln II's E♭5 as "bass". Fix in devCb bar 104: `es,4-.) des,4-. |` → `es,4-.) des,4 |`. The cellos keep their staccato. This makes the bass sound to the end of the theme.
- Applied. analysis.txt now shows 0 outer-voice parallels, the same as v239. The shared Cl 1·2 staff has Cl 2 resting at 103–104, so there is no dynamic clash.

**3. Bar 75: Hn 1·2 re-enter p after a long silence.**

- Problem: the horns return at 75 on `es'1\p` / `g1\p` under the sTwo theme.
- Evidence: Rimsky, PDF 136: brass re-entering after a rest should come in pp or ff, because p and f are weaker. PDF 127 asks for chords that alternate between groups to balance. With the strings at p the horn chord sits on top of the theme instead of beneath it, and the 67–82 balance (protected) is better kept with pp.
- Fix: in sections/sTwo.ily, change `es'1\p` to `es'1\pp` in sTwoHnOne and `g1\p` to `g1\pp` in sTwoHnTwo. The two parts are on a shared staff and have equal dynamics.
- Applied. No notes change, so this does not alter the protected balance pitches.

**4. Coda tempo (199–279) against Beethoven 5/iv.** Not applied.

- Observation: Beethoven drives the coda with one accelerating Presto and ends on many bars of hammered C major. This coda changes tempo about ten times and ends Largamente.
- Rimsky's economy chapter (PDF 136) supports holding back, so it does not argue against the plan.
- The tempo plan from 211 and the 273–279 ending are protected decisions (c039, c139). No change is justified.

**5. Augmentation together with the original (Ex 272–274, PDF 438–440) is not used.** Not applied.

- The reading's one technique the score does not use is to set motive x against its own augmentation, as in the Tsar Saltan bassoons.
- The natural place would be 143–145 (scherzo recall). That passage lies inside the protected 135–150, and its quotation matches the scherzo exactly, so adding a part there would change a quotation context.
- I tested no change and propose none for this cycle.

**6. 101–102 and 105–106: the horn and bass clarinet pads.** Not applied.

- The pads from c234 hold p under the string sf, which is correct sf-p scoring (PDF 129, Ex 253).
- They sit in the middle register and do not crowd the high wind scale.
- No change.
