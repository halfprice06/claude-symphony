### 4. The pivot-chord technique at 114: tested in the scratch build and declined
**Technique.** A&S PDF 443 and Ex. 26-2 (a pivot chord that belongs to both keys), and PDF 450 / Ex. 26-3 (the pivot after an applied chord). In v520, bar 113 cuts off the F-minor goal chord, and 114 enters directly on a soft D7 in the strings before the G-minor bassoon solo.

**Candidate.** On 114 beats 1–2, a pivot chord: C minor in first inversion (i6 of C, iv6 of g), with E♭ in the bass. It falls to D7 on beat 3 (Phrygian ♭6–5).

```
devVlnOne: fis''1\p\>              ->  g''2\p\> fis''2
devVla:    a2.\p\>\upbow r4\!      ->  g2\p\>\upbow a4 r4\!
devVc:     d2.\p\>\upbow r4\!      ->  es2\p\>\upbow d4 r4\!
devCb:     d,2.\p\>\upbow r4\!     ->  es,2\p\>\upbow d,4 r4\!
```

**Build.** 0 warnings, 16 pages, 0 outer-voice parallels. par8.py on 112–116 shows only the cellos doubling the basses at 114.3 and the existing 112.3 doublings.

**Verdict: not better.** I reverted it.
1. It sounds the home tonic, C minor, in the middle of the development. The movement's Ex. 26-8 plan keeps the tonic away until the leading-tone bass at 138–146 (A&S PDF 449).
2. Its E♭–D bass anticipates the development's own Phrygian approach to the same D at 121–122 (It6, c153), seven bars later.
3. A link already exists. Tpt 2's C5 at 113 is held as Vln II's C5 at 114, the seventh of D7, which falls to B♭4 at 115 (c173). So v520's 114 is a common-tone turn, which A&S PDF 458 accepts as a phrase modulation at the start of a new section.

v520 stays as it is.

### 5. Tonicized chords in the descending-thirds bass at 123–127: checked on paper and declined
**Technique.** A&S PDF 447, 452–453 (Ex. 26-6, 26-13; Points 6, 10). In G major the bass falls by thirds, G (123) – E (125) – C (126) – A (127), and none of these chords is tonicized. This is the one Unit 26 device v520 does not use.

**Why not.** Each applied chord would contradict a note of F2, the finale's own theme, which the cellos and violas carry here at G-major pitch:
- V6/5 of vi needs D♯ against the tune's D4 at 124.4.
- V7 of IV needs F♮ against its F♯4 at 125.4.
- V6/5 of ii would make the tune's D4 at 126.3 a seventh that rises to E4 at 127.

Changing F2 to fit would be a theme change for a passing colour. c320 declined the same device at 238 for a different reason. No change.
