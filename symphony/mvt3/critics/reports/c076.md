## Findings

### 1. Horn call 243–246: horns at the same dynamic as the trumpets (applied)
**Problem.** At 243 Tpt 1·2 and Hn 1·2 all enter `\f` with a crescendo from 244. Hn 2 carries the call at movement I's own pitch (E3–C4–B3, the quotation check `hornCallMajor HnTwo 243`) in the horn's weak low register, under two trumpets.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 41: "horns, in forte passages, are about one half as strong … the marks of expression in the horns should be one degree stronger than in the rest of the brass … two horns are needed to one trumpet". As written, two horns at f balance one trumpet at f, and there are two trumpets. The original pitch is the line most likely to be lost.
**Fix (sHead.ily).** Hn 1·2 `\ff` at 243, with no hairpin (they are already at the level of 247):
`sHeadHnOne = { R1*8 | e'2->(\ff c''2~ | c''4 b'2.~ | b'2 c''2~ | c''2.) r4 | }`
`sHeadHnTwo = { R1*8 | e2->(\ff c'2~ | c'4 b2.~ | b2 c'2~ | c'2.) g4 | }`
Both lines change together, so the part-combined staff keeps equal dynamics. The trumpets keep f with their crescendo.

### 2. Trumpets 1·2 have no rest from 243 to 268 (applied)
**Problem.** Tpt 1 enters at 243 on E5→C6 and holds C6/B5/C6 for 3½ bars. It then plays the S head 247–254, the chorale ff–fff 255–264 (a2 with Tpt 2) and F1 in major 265–268. Its only silences are a quarter rest at 246 and breath marks at 250 and 254. At 2 = 88 this stretch is about 35 s, roughly 4 s longer than at 100. The two bars that follow, 269–270, are the first full-bar rest.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 37: "Wood-wind players cannot manage extremely long sustained passages … care must be taken therefore to give them a little rest from time to time"; PDF 44: "The remarks on breathing … apply with equal force to the brass". Table C (PDF 43) marks the top of the trumpet compass as "little used", which is where 243–246 lies.
**Fix (finale.ily).** Tpt 1·2 stop after the first half of the second S-head phrase (252, on G, a sub-phrase end) and rest 253–254. Ob 1·2 keep the line at the trumpets' pitch (f''4. e''8 d''4 c''4 | b'2 c''2), and Cl 1·2, Hn 1·2 and violas keep it below. The trumpets then come back fresh for the protected chorale entry at 255:
`finaleTptOne`: `e''2.~( e''8 d''8 | c''4 a''2-- g''4) | R1*2 |` (in place of bars 251–254)
`finaleTptTwo`: `c''2.~( c''8 b'8 | a'4 f''2-- e''4) | R1*2 |`
This replaces the breath mark at 254 (cycles 46/48) with a two-bar rest for the same reason, which is breathing, and does not touch the chorale. The quotation record still passes 36/36, because `sHeadBrass` checks 247–250 only. For finale.md item 9: the S head at 251–254 is now in Tpt 1·2 for 251–252 only. The composer should update that line. No pitches of any theme change.

### 3. Vln I divisi not marked at 275 and 277 (applied)
**Problem.** At 273 Vln I is marked "div." and plays double notes. At 275 beat 2 it continues on a single line (the scherzo quotation g''8 g'' c''' e'''), at 277 it returns to double notes, and only 278 has "div. a 3". A player cannot tell whether the lower half joins the quotation or drops out, or when it splits again.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 25: "It is always as well to mark how the passage is to be divided in the score."
**Fix (finale.ily, finaleVlnOne).** `g''8-.^\markup \italic "unis."` on the first eighth of 275 and `<e'' c'''>4->^\markup \italic "div."` at 277. Checked in the scratch PDF: no collisions (page 16).

### 4. MIDI stringendo arrives one bar early (applied)
**Problem.** The stringendo steps in global.ily are `\mt 140 152 164 176` for 243–246, so bar 246 already runs at 2 = 88 and the printed "Più allegro 2 = 88" at R changes nothing. In the MP3 (which Dan listens to) the arrival at R has no tempo event, and the trumpets' last held C6 is already at full speed.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 41: brass excel at a gradual swell to the goal. The crescendo and the stringendo should reach their goal together at R. The cycle-39 note (Rimsky Ex. 209/272) already uses written-out graduation for 271–272.
**Fix (global.ily).** `\mt 140 s1 \mt 150 s1 \mt 160 s1 \mt 168 s1`, so the tempo reaches 176 (2 = 88) with the `\tempo` at 247. MIDI only; nothing printed changes.

### 5. Clarinet 2 on E6 at 265–268 (checked, no change)
Cl 1·2 a2 leap G5→E6 (written A5→F♯6) in F1 in major, ff. Table B (PDF 35) puts this in the "very high: bright, piercing" register, and PDF 36 warns only about octave leaps on the clarinet. A sixth slurred in a tutti ff is within professional second-clarinet practice, and the oboes, flutes and violins share the pitch. Left as is.

### 6. Vln II at A6 in 252 (checked, no change)
Vln I·II reach A6 by a fifth from D6 (dotted quarter, accented). PDF 26 gives A6 as the violins' practical limit and forbids skips only *above* it, so this is within the rule. The unison Vln II was accepted in cycle 49 and is not reopened.
