## Findings

### 1. 119–123: the two solo desks play their pp answers at the default MIDI loudness (applied)

**Problem.** The divided Vln I answers at 119–120 and 122–123 are written as `<< { soli } \\ { gli altri } >>`. Only the lower voice carries the pp. The `\\` construct makes the upper voice a new Voice with no dynamic, so the MIDI plays the soli G6 and D6 lines at velocity 90 while the section an octave below plays at 41. The audio render therefore has a loud high solo where the score prints pp dolce, over pizzicato strings at pp. The printed score is correct.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 128 says the answering phrase must be weaker than the phrase it answers. These lines answer a p bassoon, so they must not sound louder than it. Casanelles PDF 272 says the realisation, not the mark, decides what is heard. A visible second pp in the upper voice is not an option: the score moves Dynamic_engraver to the Staff, so two dynamics at the same moment conflict (I tried it and got 4 warnings).

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-300/work/sections/dev.ily).** The upper voice now continues the main voice, and the section goes into a new voice:

```
r4-\tweak stencil ##f \pp << { \voiceOne g'''8(^\markup \italic "dolce, 2 soli" f''' es''' d''' c''' a'' | fis''4) } \new Voice { \voiceTwo g''8(\pp_\markup \italic "gli altri" f'' es'' d'' c'' a' | fis'4) } >> \oneVoice r4 r2
```

Bar 122 gets the same change without the hidden pp, which is inherited. A c300 comment line sits above `devVlnOne`.

**Result.**
- In the MIDI, both voices now play at velocity 41.
- Every other track's notes and velocities are identical to v300 (I diffed them). The MIDI length is still 422.5 s.
- The page 7 image shows the same print: the pp and the "dolce, 2 soli" and "gli altri" markups are unchanged.
- No pitch changed. No quotation, F1, F2 or chorale top line changed.

### 4. Stop chord, E♭/E♮ thread and C-major arrival keep the cyclic rules (no change)

- The stop chord B–D–F–A♭ appears at 138 (B bass) and 222 (D bass) and resolves only at 222→223, with Tpt 1 B4→C5 and timpani G→C.

### 5. The Brahms-3 outer-voice E♭/E♮ cross relation stays unused (decision: do not add)

**Problem.** cyclic-notes "Still unused" lists one deliberate outer-voice E♭/E♮ cross relation in the chorale (A–S PDF 394, Brahms 3).

**Evidence.**
- Its only proper home is the chorales. 224–228 lies inside the protected arrival 223–234, and 259–263 is protected.
- The unprotected 255–258 has E♮ on top (G5, E6) over E2 → A1. An E♭ in the bass there would need ♭VI or iv in the bass. That would put A♭ in the bass before 261, and A–S PDF 593 says the half-step A♭→G into V is what makes the German sixth work. Taking that step early weakens the last cadence.
- The thread already makes its major/minor point in one voice.

**Decision.** Leave it unused and record it as declined for the finale.
