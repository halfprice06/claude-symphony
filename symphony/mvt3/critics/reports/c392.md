## Findings

### 1. 207-208: Vln II's run stays at 205's degree while every other voice of c302's sequence moves up a step (applied)

**Problem.** c302 made 205-208 a rising sequence: F1's head on A♭ over V7 (205-206), then on B♭ minor over ii7 (207-208), over the A♭ pedal. All the voices take part in the step up: the head, the held winds and brass, and Ob 2, Hn 3 and Tbn 1. The one exception is the moving inner line, Vln II's eighth-note scale. At 205-206 the violins run A♭4 up to an accented F6. At 207-208 Vln II plays the same A♭ scale from A♭3 (A♭-B♭-C-D♭-E♭-F-G♭-A♭ | B♭-C-D♭-E♭), then skips E♭5→G♭5 to the accent. This run is also identical, note for note, to the run at 209-210 over V7. As a result the only active line under the head says "V7" through the ii7 bars, and it is heard three times at one pitch level. On 208.2.5 its E♭5 also rubs a second under the head's passing F5 in the winds and Vln I.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 267, sec. 5: "Normally, as in the Bach prelude (Example 17-1), all the voices above a sequential bass are themselves sequential." Ex. 17-1 (PDF 263-264) and Ex. 17-2 (PDF 266-267) transpose every voice with the pattern. PDF 265-266: the second statement repeats the first on the next degree, with qualities adjusted to the key. The run transposed up a step stays in D♭ (B♭-C-D♭-E♭-F-G♭-A♭), so no accidental is needed. The 205-206 run ends on F6, the 207-208 run on G♭5: a step higher, which is exactly sequential. c302 had already chosen G♭5 for that last note (as a chord tone of E♭m7), so the accent does not change. The score's other two-statement sequences already work this way. At 99-106 every wind and string chord tone moves with the head (Bn 1 E3→A3, Hn 1·2 E4/G4→A4/C5, Vla/Vc chords F minor→B♭ minor). At 35-38 the violins' figure moves up a step with the head (E♭-D♭-C-B♭ → F-E♭-D♭-C).

**Fix (codaOne.ily, Vln II; applied).**
`aes8(\mf bes c' des') es'( f' ges' aes' | bes'8 c'' des'' es'') ges''4-> r4`
→
`bes8(\mf c' des' es') f'( ges' aes' bes' | c''8 des'' es'' f'') ges''4-> r4`

The run now starts on B♭3, the root of ii7, in unison with Bn 1's head note. It ends with a step into the accented G♭5. On 208.2.5 F5 doubles the head's passing tone instead of making a second with it, and 209-210 return to the A♭ run, so the line follows V7 - ii7 - V7. Rhythm, slur, mf, accent, bowing and every other part are unchanged. A comment (c392) was added in codaOne.ily.

**Protection.** HISTORY protects the 205-210 scoring (c176-c178), the violas 205-208, the horn unison 205-206, the A♭ pedal and the 209-210 A♭7 with the 210-211 suspension. This fix changes none of them. It changes the pitch level of one existing line on the same instrument, as c302 did with the head. c302 itself changed this run's last note. Not a quotation and not a theme, so no other thread needs to be told.
