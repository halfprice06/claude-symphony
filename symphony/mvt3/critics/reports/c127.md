## Findings

### 1. 257-262: Fl 2's arpeggio rises a fourth above the chorale's top line (applied)
- **Problem.** Since c117, Fl 2 doubles Vln II's broken chords an octave higher. Each half-bar figure peaks at A6 (257-258), A♭6 (259-261) and G6 (262), above the chorale's top note in Fl 1 and Vln I (E6, D6, C6, B5). A flute in its third octave is the most penetrating sound in the texture, so for six bars the highest audible line is the accompaniment, and the chorale's top octave (already thin, c119's open note) sits under it. The figure is also in the same instrument as the top line, which blurs both.
- **Evidence.** Adler PDF 274: "try to keep the accompanying forces registrally distant from the soloist whenever possible"; PDF 260 and Ex. XVII-3 (image): Tchaikovsky lays the string figuration under the solo, never above. PDF 264 and 267: Mozart keeps the soloist's own colour out of the accompaniment. PDF 279: Bizet puts a doubling above the voice only at its climax; here the climax is 263, not 257.
- **Answer to c117's reason** (the figure needs mixed colour and mass to be heard, Adler PDF 179). The figure keeps a wind colour: Cl 1 takes it at Vln II's pitch (A4-A5, clarion register, which carries), so it is a clarinet-plus-violin figure under the line instead of a flute figure over it. Haydn's re-assignment of a doubling to follow an inner line (PDF 288) is the model.
- **Fix.** Fl 2 joins Fl 1 on the top line and breathes after 260, between Fl 1's breaths after 258 and 261, so the top flute octave never stops (Brahms's flute dovetailing, Adler PDF 188, cited by c126). Flute staff split for the six bars so each player's breath shows:

### 2. 257-258: the violas re-strike the chorale's own pitch E4 (applied)
- **Problem.** The viola quarters hit E4 eight times a bar at 257-258, the same pitch as the chorale's lowest octave (Hn 1-2, Tbn 1, Bn 1 on held E4). A background figure on the line's pitch re-attacks a note that should sound as one sustained tone.
- **Evidence.** Adler PDF 274 (registral distance); PDF 288-289 (an accompaniment figure must not obscure the principal voice); the score's own rule from c074 (Hn 3 moved off the chorale's E4 at 257-258 for this reason).
- **Fix.** Vla `c'4 c' c' c' | c'4 c' c' c'` at 257-258 (was `e'4 ...`), with Hn 3 and Tpt 3 on C4, the chord's third that was thinly held. E4→C4 and C4→F4 make no parallels with the bass (A, F); analysis.txt shows 0.
