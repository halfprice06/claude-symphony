## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Page turns cut the two wind solos and leave one-bar orphans at section starts (applied)
Bars: 62/63, 115/116, 169/170, 197/198, 186/187, 87–90.
Problem: v55's page breaks are left to the free page breaker. The trumpet solo (51–66, the owed soft solo) turns the page at 62/63, in the middle of its last slurred phrase 59–66. The bassoon solo begins on the last bar of page 6 (115) and continues overleaf. Pages 10 and 11 each start with the last bar of the previous section (170 before L, 198 before the coda at 199), and page 10's second system starts at 186, one bar before the double bar and key change at 187.
Evidence: Adler PDF 299, item 5: players "have been known to leave out an entire passage" when a turn falls badly; the conductor's score has the same problem at a solo entry. PDF 291, item 2: the layout must make the structure "immediately discernable".
Fix (global.ily): `\break` at D 51; `\pageBreak` at E 67, before the bridge at 87 (`s1*4 \pageBreak s1*12` inside F), at H 115, at L 171, at 199 and at 211; `\break` at 187. Result, still 16 pages: p3 37–50 / 51–66 (the whole trumpet solo on one system), p4 67–76 / 77–86, p5 87–101 (bridge not split), p7 115–144 (bassoon solo complete), p9 159–170 (break at the recap's consequent, 158/159), p10 171–186 / 187–198, p11 199–210, p12 211–222, p15/16 break at 266/267 (between the two-bar F1 statements, was 265/266 inside one). I tried a break at 265 too; it makes 17 pages, so I left it out.

### 2. Bassoons at 101 and 105: two different dynamics at once on one staff (applied)
Bars: 101, 105.
Problem: Bn 2 ends its f phrase (with the cellos, which have sf) on beat 1 while Bn 1 enters p on the same beat with a held E3 (A3 at 105). The staff prints one dynamic, p, under Bn 2's quarter note, so Bn 2 reads its cadence note as p. Bn 2 also lacks the sf the cellos, basses and violas have.
Evidence: Adler PDF 294: two instruments on one staff that need different dynamics need two sets; Ex. XVIII-10a (PDF 301) prints Oboe 1's f above and Oboe 2's ff below. This score's rule allows only equal simultaneous dynamics on a shared staff, so the entries must not coincide.
Fix (dev.ily): `devBsnOne`: `r4 e2.\p` at 101 and `r4 a2.\p` at 105 (Hn and Cl 2 already hold the chord from the downbeat); `devBsnTwo`: `f,4-.\sf` at 101, `bes,4-.\sf` at 105. The staff now reads sf (beat 1) then p (beat 2).

### 3. Duplicate "1." over the flutes at 129 (applied)
Bars: 129.
Problem: Fl 1 shows "1." twice, stacked. Cycle 35 added a manual `^\markup \bold "1."`; since cycle 54 gave Fl 2 the chord at 110–113, the part-combiner prints its own "1." at 129 as well.
Evidence: Adler PDF 301 (proofreading); PDF 291, item 1 (clarity).
Fix (dev.ily, `devFluteOne`): `b''2(\mf\< g''2` without the manual markup. The Cl 1 manual "1." at 126 is still needed (the combiner prints nothing there) and stays.

### 4. "div. a3" collides with the barline at 278 (applied)
Bars: 278 (Vln II, Vla; Vln I tight).
Problem: 278 is the narrowest bar of the page; the text runs into the 278/279 barline and the stems of 279.
Evidence: Adler PDF 291, item 1; App. A PDF 312 gives the term as "div. a 3". Gould PDF 55: no characters may collide.
Fix (finale.ily, Vln I, Vln II, Vla at 278): `\once \textLengthOn <…>1->\fff~^\markup \italic "div. a 3"`. The bar widens to hold the text; page 16 still holds 267–279.

### 5. No rehearsal letter for 28 bars, 171–198 (applied; later letters move)
Bars: 187.
Problem: L (171) to the coda (199) is the longest gap in the movement and passes the C-major turn of the second theme at 187 (double bar, key change, violins arco). Every other gap is 20 bars or less.
Evidence: Adler PDF 291, item 3; PDF 299, item 4 and Ex. XVIII-5 (letters every 10 bars so a resting player can count).
Fix (global.ily): `\bar "||" \break \key c \major \mark \default` at 187. Letters after it move one place: 187 M, 199 N, 211 O, 223 P, 235 Q, 247 R, 255 S, 273 T. Comments in global.ily updated; anything else that cites letters (HISTORY: "R 255, S 273") should use bar numbers.

### 6. Tempo marks and letters only over the flutes and the strings (applied)
Bars: every tempo change and letter (e.g. 51, 83, 199, 211, 223, 247, 271–273).
Problem: The brass, between the woodwind and string blocks, has no tempo or letter in a 23-staff system.
Evidence: Adler PDF 294: "It is strongly urged that tempo markings be placed at the top of the score and repeated in the space separating each choir"; Ex. XVIII-1 (PDF 293) repeats "Allegretto" over woodwinds, brass, timpani and strings.
Fix (score.ly): the brass StaffGroup gets `\with { \consists "Mark_engraver" \consists "Metronome_mark_engraver" }`. I did not add a third copy over the timpani: it sits two staves above the strings' copy. Still 16 pages.

### 7. Five ledger lines for the violins' C7 at the arrival (applied, violins only)
Bars: 223.
Problem: Vln I's C7 tremolo stands on five ledger lines with the tremolo strokes through them, right under the "Maestoso" mark.
Evidence: Adler PDF 300, item 8a: use 8va "to avoid more than four ledger lines". App. A (PDF 303) writes the top of the flute and violin with 8va.
Fix (arrival.ily): `arrivalVlnOne = { \ottava #1 c''''1:32\fff \ottava #0 | …`. Sound unchanged; the protected C7 and tremolo stay. The flutes' C7/G6 at 223 stay in ledger lines, because both flutes play there and this score forbids `\ottava` on a shared staff when the other player sounds.

### 8. No breath between the bassoon solo's two phrases (applied)
Bars: 118/119.
Problem: The trumpet solo (54, 58, 62) and the cor anglais/muted trumpet (175, 179) mark their phrase ends with breath marks; the bassoon solo (115–122, same theme F2) does not mark the join between its two slurs at 118/119.
Evidence: Adler PDF 291, item 3: the score must give "every detail for every instrument", including tonguing and phrasing; consistent treatment of the same theme (cycle 46 added breath marks to the F2 solos).
Fix (dev.ily, `devBsnOne`): `es'2 d'2\!) \breathe | c'2.( es'4 |`.
