## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 220-226: the violins' canon is covered by the brass chorale (applied)
Problem: the brass stay ff from the arrival chord (219) through the chorale (220-224), and the canon in flutes, oboes, clarinets and violins enters at 222 marked f. By Rimsky's ratios (PDF 51) the chorale melody alone (Tpt 1 + Tbn 1 at ff) weighs four horns; the canon's upper octave (Fl 1 + Vln I) about one and a half, its lower octave (Ob 1, Cl 1, Vln II, g'-e'') about two, and that lower octave sits in the trumpets' own register. The canon, which the plan names as a main event of the arrival, is masked.
Fix: the arrival chord stays ff; the chorale itself is f from 220 in all brass and the instruments doubling it (bassoons, contrabassoon, violas, cellos, basses); the canon enters ff at 222 and returns to f at 227, where the brass re-enter f. In arrival.ily: `g'1\f` on Tpt 1 at 220 (and the same `\f` on the 220 note of Tpt 2, Hn 1-4, Tbn 1-2, B.tbn, Tuba, Bn 1-2, Cbn, Vla, Vc, Cb); `g''1\ff` (Fl 1) and `\ff` on the 222 note of Fl 2, Ob 1-2, Cl 1-2, Vln I-II; `a''1\f` (Fl 1) and `\f` on the 227 note of the same eight parts. Shared staves get equal dynamics. This changes no pitch of either protected chorale statement; the reason they are protected (the top line and the bass) stands.

### 2. 220 and 222: chorale voicing breaks register order and doubles the bass of V6 (applied)
Problem: at 220 Hn 1 holds c'' from the arrival chord above the chorale's first melody note g' (Tpt 1), so the first sound of the chorale has a horn on top (Rimsky PDF 90, rule 2). At 222 the chord is V6 over B, and Hn 3 doubles the B (Rimsky PDF 83: the bass of an inversion of the dominant is never doubled in an upper part); Hn 3 c'→b then runs in octaves with the bass c→b (par.py lists 12 pairs at 222; Rimsky PDF 83, consecutive octaves between parts).
Fix: arrival.ily, `arrivalHnOne = { c''1\ff | g'1\f | g'1 | g'1 | ...` (Hn 1 joins Tpt 1 in unison, the classic trumpet-horn blend) and `arrivalHnThree = { e'1\ff | g1\f | c'1 | d'1 | a1 | ...` (d' is the chord's fifth; I tried g first, but it made octaves with Tpt 2 c''→g', so d'). After the change par.py shows no pair at 219-229 that was not there before, and the 12 octave pairs are gone. Melody and bass untouched.

### 3. 243-250 and 256-260: woodwind pairs half idle in the final tutti (applied)
Problem: at 243-250 movement I's first-theme head is in Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1 and the upper strings while Fl 2, Ob 2 and Cl 2 rest; at 256-260 only Fl 1 plays and Ob 1-2, Cl 1-2, Fl 2 rest five bars before the last chord. Rimsky PDF 90 rule 1: wind instruments in a passage are doubled or not throughout; PDF 95: in pairs, mix the doubled timbres. With Rimsky's PDF 51 ratios the strings-and-winds theme (about 4.5 horn units) loses against the brass head (Tpt 1·2 + Hn 1·2, about 6), and the two heads are supposed to be heard together.
Fix (finale.ily): Fl 2 plays Fl 1's line in unison at 243-250 and 256-260; Ob 2 and Cl 2 play Ob 1/Cl 1's line in unison at 243-250; Ob 1 and Cl 1 play Vln II's line (`r8 g'8(\ff e''4.->) d''8( c''8 b'8 | c''4->) r4 r2 | ... | c''4-> r8 b'8 c''4-> r4 |`) at 256-260, Ob 2 and Cl 2 the same with 260 as `e'4-> r8 d'8 e'4-> r4`. The now redundant `\ff` on their 261 notes is removed. No page added.

### 4. 151-166: second flute in its weak octave and first oboe at F6 in the ff recapitulation (applied)
Problem: at 151-158 Fl 2 doubles the tune at the c'' octave and descends to D4 (bar 153) in a ff tutti, where the low flute cannot be heard (Rimsky PDF 90-91, rule 3 and the "low notes of the flute too weak" example; Adler PDF 186). At 159-166 Ob 1 leaves the tune's octave for C6-F6 (f''' at 162, es''' at 163), where Adler (PDF 198) says the oboe is thin and hard to control, and Rimsky's rule 3 example calls the high oboe "too piercing" against flutes. In the exposition (19-26) Ob 1 stays at the c'' octave.
Fix (recP.ily): Fl 2 bars 151-158 identical to Fl 1 (upper octave, unison pair, as Fl 2 already does at 159-166); Ob 1 bars 159-166 at the c'' octave, unison with Ob 2, except 162 `f''2-> es''4( d''4)` to keep the third below the tune. The flute's lowest note in the movement is now F4, the oboe's highest C6.

### 5. 123-130: sustained second violins sit above the harp solo (applied)
Problem: the harp's melody (d''-g'', ending b'-g'-a' at 129-130) is accompanied by Vln II holding b'/c'' pp, which lies above the melody at 127-130, and the violas holding g'/fis' just under it. A sustained bowed chord easily covers a plucked instrument (Rimsky PDF 51) and the order of register is broken at the cadence (PDF 90, rule 2). This is the harp's first melody in the symphony.
Fix (dev.ily): exchange the octaves of the two inner string parts: Vln II `g'1\pp^\markup \italic "arco" | fis'1 | g'1 | g'1 | e'1 | fis'1 | g'1\< | fis'1 |`, Vla `b1\pp^\markup \italic "arco" | c'1 | b1 | c'1 | c'1 | c'1 | b1\< | c'1 |`. Same pitch classes, everything now below the harp melody. The fifths G/C→E/A at 126-127 against the bass existed before in the violas and move to Vln II unchanged; I did not touch them.

### 6. 11-26: the horns overweight the lower octave of the first theme (applied)
Problem: F1 is in both violin sections at the upper octave and in Hn 1·2 plus violas an octave lower, all f. By Rimsky's PDF 51 ratios two forte horns equal both violin sections, and the violas add to the lower octave, so the theme's lower doubling is louder than the line itself. Rimsky's distribution principle (PDF 85, 96-97) keeps the weight in the melody's octave with lighter support below.
Fix (pOne.ily): `pOneHnOne = { c'2->(\mf ...` and `pOneHnTwo = { c'2->(\mf ...` (strings stay f). Horns 3·4 are already mf. The recapitulation (151) keeps its ff horns because there five woodwinds join the tune's octave.

### 7. Bass drum named on the percussion staff but never played (applied)
Problem: the staff reads "Cymbals / Bass drum (2 players)", and the brief lists the bass drum, but `bd` never occurs: a player sits through the movement. Rimsky PDF 53: indefinite percussion is rhythmic colour, used only at long intervals, which the cymbals already respect.
Fix: one stroke each at the two structural downbeats, with the cymbals: arrival.ily `arrivalPerc = \drummode { <cymc bd>1\ff | R1*11 | }`; finale.ily 261 `<cymc~ bd>1-> | cymc4-> r4 r2\fermata |`. In score.ly the drumStyleTable gives `(bassdrum default #f -2)` so the bass drum sits below the line and the cymbal cross on it; I checked the engraving on pages 13 and 16. The C-minor tutti at 199 stays without bass drum.
