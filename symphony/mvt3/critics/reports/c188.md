## Findings

### 1. 39-42 (also 28-34): the held B♭ chord is re-attacked at every barline during the staged diminuendo — applied
- **Problem.** In 39-42 every wind and brass part holds one pitch for four bars as separate whole notes: Fl 1 A♭5, Fl 2/Ob 1 D5, Ob 2 A♭4, Cl 1 F4, Cl 2 B♭3, Bn 1 D4, Bn 2 B♭2, Cbsn B♭1, Hn 1-2 F4/B♭3, Hn 3-4 F4/B♭3, Tbn 1-2 F3/D3, B.tbn B♭2. That gives four fresh tongued attacks from about 14 players inside the diminuendo to p. Above it only the violins' last F1 head moves, so these attacks are the loudest thing in the texture at the moment it should fade. The same happens at 28-34, where common tones in the pads (Fl 1 G5 29-31, Hn 1 F4 28-30, Cl 1, Cl 2, Ob 2, Fl 2, Ob 1) are re-tongued under the ff subject.
- **Evidence.** Adler PDF 309 and 321: notes under one slur (or a hold) take one breath, separate notes are tongued. Ex. X-15 and X-16 (PDF 306-307) hold the soft horn choir instead of re-striking it. c161 accepted the bass trombone "holding" B♭2 through this diminuendo, but the source re-strikes it. c175/c176 already tied the strings' held G4/B♭3 at 95-96 for the same reason. Bassoon 2, contrabassoon and basses already tie at 9-10 and in the transition (43-50).
- **Fix (pTwo.ily).** Tie every same-pitch barline repeat in the wind and brass pads. At 39-42 the chord is re-attacked once, at 39, where the diminuendo starts, then tied, e.g. `pTwoBassTbn … bes,1~\> | bes,1~ | bes,1~ | bes,2.\p r4`; `pTwoFluteOne … g''1~ | g''1~ | g''1 | f''2 aes''2~ | aes''1 … aes''1~\> | aes''1~ | aes''1\p`. Hn 1-2: `f'1~ | f'1 \breathe | f'1~\> | f'1~ | f'1~\p | f'1`, so they breathe after 38 (they play 27-42 with one quarter rest). Tbn 1 also ties E♭3 35→36.1. Strings unchanged (they change bow). No held chain is longer than 4 bars (11.4 s at 2 = 84, at p). Notes and dynamics unchanged.

### 2. 111-112: the winds and brass tongue the climax line and its 4-3 that the violins slur — applied
- **Problem.** At the development climax, Vln I plays `g'''4( aes'''2 f'''4) | f'''2~( f'''4 e'''4)`, with the F-E 4-3 suspension from c061 slurred. Fl 1-2 and Ob 1 double it note for note without slurs. Tpt 1 (`f''2~ f''4 e''4`), Hn 2 and Hn 4 (`f'2~ f'4 e'4`) carry the same suspension unslurred, so the resolution E is tongued at ff in six wind parts against a legato resolution in the strings. Ob 2 and Tpt 2 move D♭5-C5 under it, also tongued, and Tpt 2 re-strikes its D♭5 on 112.1.
- **Evidence.** Adler PDF 309 and 321: slurred notes are one breath, separate notes are tongued, so an unslurred doubling is heard as detached. Ex. X-12 (Don Juan, PDF 305) and Ex. X-43/X-44 (Bruckner 7, Mahler 2, PDF 317) slur held brass melody at ff and keep accents for the strong notes.

The accents on 113 and every dynamic stay as they are. Pitches unchanged, so the parallels check is unchanged.
