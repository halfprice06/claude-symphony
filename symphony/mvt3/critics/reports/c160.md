### 3. Open item "131-132 settled G" (open since c153): decided, keep the full cadence in G and close the item (no score change)
Bars: 131-134, 135-138.
Problem raised by c153: a settled G at 131-132 might slow the approach to the return.
Decision and evidence: from the cyclic side the cadence is needed. Sebesky PDF 11 says an entrance or event is effective only if something different has been established before it (the strings held back so the chorale's entry surprises). The stop chord B–D–F–A♭ at 138 is movement I's chord (I 26, 138), and it is struck sf > after the pp dim7 chain from 135. Its effect depends on the G-major close just before it (131-134, four bars, F2's half cadence with F1's head, then G major). Without that close the stop chord follows more unstable harmony and loses its contrast. On Sebesky's question "where does it sag?" (PDF 240): it does not sag here. The music runs 131-132 cadence → 135 pp dim7 → 138 stop chord sf > → stretto from 139 → ff 147, and the render's levels (-25 dB at 132, -32 at 135, rising to -18 at 147) show the same rise. The development key plan (G area 115-134) is protected, and this was decided once already (c153, form lens).
Fix: none. HISTORY.md can drop the repeated "Open: 131-132 settled G".
