## Findings

### 1. The thread's E4 reaches the tonic only in the wrong violas' octave (223). Applied.
**Bars:** 220–223, violas.
**Problem:** finale.md says the E♭/E♮ thread closes at 219–220, violas D♯4→E4, "at movement I's pitch". But at 220 the E4 is the 6th of the cadential 6/4 over G. It moves on to F4 at 221–222 (V7 and the stop chord), and at the arrival (223) the violas play G4–E5. So the violas never sound E4 on the C-major tonic. Movement I ends with its violas' E4 on a C-major chord (I 182, the movement's only E♮). The finale's arrival should answer it in the same instrument and octave.
**Evidence:** A&S PDF 483: after ♭II6, a 1̂ over a cadential 6/4 "functions as a passing tone, not a melodic goal. Only if V moves on to I does 1̂ appear as a goal." The same applies to 3̂: the E over 220's G bass is an appoggiatura, and its goal is the I chord at 223. Movement I record: mvt1.md, last-chord table (E4, violas, "the only E♮").
**Fix (applied):** arrival.ily, `arrivalVla = { <e' e''>1:32\fff | …` (was `<g' e''>`). The lower violas move D4→E4 by step (from the stop chord's D, its 3rd). The upper violas keep F4→E5. G stays in the cellos (G3), Tpt 2 and Hn 2. The violas' unison E4 at 224 now continues the same pitch. finale.md should add to quotation item 2 and to the scoring rules: "223: violas E4–E5 tremolo on the tonic, movement I's last viola pitch on C major."

### 2. Oboe 1 takes the theme's ♭2̂ upward, on the same staff degree (218→219). Applied.
**Bars:** 211–219, Ob 1.
**Problem:** Ob 1 doubles Tpt 1's theme B at the unison through 218 (c019) and ends on the held D♭5. At 219 Tpt 1, Fl 1 and Ob 2 take ♭2̂ down to C (the protected D♭–C–B–C line), but Ob 1 rises D♭5→D♯5 (a sounding whole step written on one staff degree) and then E5. The voice that carries the theme's last note therefore leaves the descending line where ♭2̂ resolves. On the shared oboe staff the D♯ and the C form a cluster (D♯5 over C5) at sub. p. The thread's D♯ does not need Ob 1: it is in the violas and Hn 3 (D♯4), which is where cycle 60 placed the thread's E4 approach.
**Evidence:** A&S PDF 491–492 and 499 point 3: "♭2̂ tends to move down", ♭2̂–1̂–♯7̂. PDF 496–497 (sections 18–19, Ex. 28-22/23): chromatic steps are written as minor 2nds on neighbouring staff degrees, never as an "augmented unison" on one line. Cycle 10's reason for Ob 1's D♯ spelling ("E flat (Tpt 1) becomes D sharp") dates from before cycle 19 put Ob 1 on theme B. Since then the note before it is D♭, not E♭.
**Fix (applied):** codaTwo.ily, `codaTwoOboeOne`, bar 219 `c''1\subP` (was `dis''1\subP`). Bars 220–222 are unchanged (E5, D5, D5). The oboes are a2 on C5 at 219 and a2 on E5 at 220, with equal dynamics. The top line D♭–C–B–C is unchanged.

### 3. finale.md's record of the last cadence and of the scherzo version is out of date. Record only.
**Bars:** 259–263; finale.md "Ending" and the header; finale.ily comment line 2.
**Problem:** finale.md (and the finale.ily header) call the last cadence "♭VI → German sixth → V7 → I". The score has iiø6/5 with A♭ (bass F, 259–260: Vln II A♭–D–F, Ob 2 C), then the German sixth on A♭ (261), V7 (262) and I (263). Cycle 22 recorded it correctly as I6–vi–iiø6/5–Ger6–V7–I. There is no separate ♭VI chord. finale.md also says it was checked against scherzo v48. It should say v59, which I checked, and in which nothing quoted has changed.
**Evidence:** A&S PDF 488–490 (section 9): in major the chromatic predominants come from mixture (♭6̂). Here that is the iiø6/5's A♭ at 259, which the Ger6 keeps as its bass. Labelling it ♭VI hides the mixture chord that the other movement threads would compare.
**Fix:** text only (no score change). finale.md "Ending": "Last cadence 259–263: iiø6/5 (mixture A♭) → German sixth (the bar-1 chord) → V7 → I". Header: "checked against … scherzo v59". finale.ily line 2: "iiø6/5–Ger6 at 259–261".
