## Findings (ranked)

### F1. 262–264, Tpt 1·2: the crossed minims collide on the movement's most important trumpet notes (applied)
- **Problem.** Since c035, Tpt 1 (B4 tied to B4, then C5 tied through 264) and Tpt 2 (D5, then E5) are written as tied minims with separate stems, to show that the parts cross. This follows Gould PDF 67. But the two note columns sit on top of each other. The heads overlap, and the ties run through the other part's heads and stems: in 263–264 there are eight minims and six ties in two narrow bars. This is Tpt 1's protected B→C, and on the page it cannot be read.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 66, "Overlapping parts": put the down-stemmed part on the left and separate the parts slightly so that the stems do not appear to include both notes. PDF 66 also says to keep noteheads separate. PDF 55: no characters should collide. PDF 51: when the notation gets this complicated, separate the parts.
- **Fix (finale.ily).** Tpt 1, at 262: `\override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.1 \tieDown b'2~ b'2 | c''2\fff~ c''2~ | c''2~ c''2 \revert NoteColumn.force-hshift \tieNeutral`. Tpt 2, at 262: `\override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0 \tieUp d''2~ d''2 | e''2\fff~ e''2~ | e''2~ e''2 \revert NoteColumn.force-hshift \tieNeutral`. Tpt 2 (stem down) now stands on the left and Tpt 1 (stem up) on the right. Tpt 1's ties bend below the staff part and Tpt 2's above it, so no tie crosses a notehead. The sound is unchanged. This refines c035; it does not undo it.

### F2. 111, Ob 1·2: a semibreve joined to a crotchet stem (applied)
- **Problem.** Ob 2 holds F5 as a semibreve while Ob 1 plays G5 as an up-stemmed crotchet on beat 1. LilyPond moves the lower part right, as PDF 62 asks. As a result, the F semibreve touches the G's stem and reads like one chord, G–F with a broken stem.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 66: semibreves in particular should not appear to be joined to stems, and some editions reverse the order so that the semibreve is never next to a stem.
- **Fix (dev.ily).** `devOboeTwo`: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0 f''1`. `devOboeOne` at 111: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.3 g''4 aes''2 f''4`.

### F3. 122, Vln I: an entry after three bars' rest with no dynamic (applied)
- **Problem.** The second answer to the bassoon (new in v65) enters after rests at 118–121 with no dynamic. The first answer at 117 has p and dolce. The player has to look back two systems' worth of bars to know the level, and the bassoon has a diminuendo at 122.
- **Evidence.** Gould Intro PDF 10: judge the level of information the performer requires, and do not make the player find their bearings. Restating the dynamic at every entry after rests is standard engraving practice. The excerpt's dynamics chapter is missing, so this point rests on the introduction.
- **Fix.** `r4 d'''8(\p c''' b'' a'' g'' fis'' | g''4) r4 r2`.

### F4. 151 and 171: the recapitulation has no double barline (applied)
- **Problem.** The score puts thin double barlines at 51, 67, 99, 199, 211, 223, 235 and 247, several of them with no key change. The biggest formal division, the recapitulation at 151, has none. Neither has its second theme at 171, whose exposition counterpart at 51 has one.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 53: thin double barlines mark divisions between sections. Gould Intro PDF 13: adopt a consistent approach.
- **Fix (global.ily).** `\bar "||" \mark \default % K 151` and `\bar "||" \pageBreak \mark \default % L 171`. No metre change is involved, so PDF 53's warning about metre changes does not apply.

### F5. 124, violas: "arco, unis." sits on the slur (applied)
- **Problem.** The text and the start of the slur (124 beat 4 to 126) touch. The comma of "arco," sits on the slur.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 55: at least ½ stave-space between characters, and no collisions.
- **Fix (dev.ily).** `r2. \once \override TextScript.extra-offset = #'(0 . 1.3) d'4(\p^\markup \italic "arco, unis."`.

### F6. 171, violas: a leftover "arco" (applied)
- **Problem.** The violas have been arco since 167 (recP ends with `c'1\f | c'1\> | d'1 | d'1\p`). The "arco" at 171 is left over from the old pizz. div. chords. It suggests that they had been pizzicato, and it sits on top of the real instruction, sul tasto.
- **Evidence.** Gould Intro PDF 10: give the level of information the performer requires, and nothing that sends them looking for a change that did not happen.
- **Fix (recS.ily).** `es8(\pp^\markup \italic "sul tasto" g es g)`. "ord., espress." at 187 still cancels it.

Not raised:

- The diminuendo hairpin from 114 that continues over the page turn into 115 (Vln II, Vla). The rule is in Gould's dynamics chapter, which the excerpt does not contain, and the hairpin is unambiguous.
