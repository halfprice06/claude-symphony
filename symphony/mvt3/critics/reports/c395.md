## What else I studied

The reading's remaining techniques are about horizontal spacing, which cannot be judged reliably by eye on a 37-stave A3 page. I therefore built a scratch copy with a logging callback (score only, /tmp/critic-395/helpers/log.ly, not in the work copy) that prints, for every notehead, stem and barline after line breaking, its bar, beat and horizontal position in staff-spaces. From that I measured the space each duration gets in every system, the distance from every bar's last stem to the next barline, and the stem direction of every chord against PDF 60-61. I then checked the worst places on 150-300 dpi renders of pages 1, 12, 14 and 15.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Stems 0.4 space from the barline in 72 bars (applied)

Problem: in close spacing LilyPond lets the last stem of a bar come within 0.38 space of the next barline, while leaving about 1.1 spaces after the barline. 72 bars are affected, most of them on the coda pages: 235-242, 245-246, 252-277, and also 3, 9, 13-36, 119-129, 153-170, 209-210. On page 15 the basses' last up-stem quaver of 257-262 almost touches each barline (rendered at 300 dpi), and the two parallel lines are hard to tell apart, which is the reason Gould gives.

Evidence: Gould PDF 57: "Stems must never come closer to a barline than one space, because the close parallel lines are difficult to read"; only an accidental or grace note may close to 1/2 space "in cramped conditions". PDF 56: "Allow a stave-space after a clef and on either side of a barline before a notational symbol."

Fix (applied, score only):
- global.ily, at the start of `global`: `\tag #'score \override Score.BarLine.extra-spacing-width = #'(-0.75 . 0)`. The barline counts 0.75 space wider on its left in the spacing, so the minimum stem-to-barline distance becomes 1.03 spaces. Parts and MIDI are not affected.
- Page 15 (253-265) is the tightest system. With 0.75 the free breaker split 253-279 into four systems (17-18 pages), and forcing one system made it 5 spaces too wide. Two changes keep it at one system: (a) at 253, `\tag #'score { \override Score.NonMusicalPaperColumn.line-break-permission = ##f \override Score.BarLine.extra-spacing-width = #'(-0.58 . 0) }`, restored to -0.75 at the page break before 266; (b) finale.ily, Cl 2 at 257-262: `force-hshift = #-1.0` instead of c185's `#-1.3`. The semibreve still stands left of Cl 1's first quaver, clear of its head and stem (300 dpi check). The 1.3 shift pushed every downbeat of 257-262 0.6 space further from the barline.
- Result: every stem on pages 1-14 and 16 stands at least 1.03 spaces from the next barline. On page 15 it is at least 0.86, against 0.38 before. The only bars below 1.0 are 252-264, which hold page 15 at 16 pages.

### 2. Bar 243 alone is spaced at four times the system's rate (applied)

Problem: c368 gave bar 243 a score-only `spacing-increment = #5.0` so that "mf cresc. molto" ended inside the bar. The two minims of 243 take 6.8 and 11.5 spaces, while the minim at 245 takes 3.1 and the dotted minim at 244 takes 5.2 on the same system. Read by spacing, 243 looks like two semibreves and 244 like a crotchet and a minim.

Evidence: Gould PDF 54: "Notes of equal duration require equal spacing for an entire system, even when the speed of the note-values changes", and each longer value should have "more space than its shorter neighbour (a), or at least as much (b)". PDF 57: added symbols should distort the rhythmic spacing as little as possible.

Fix (applied): global.ily, the spacing override at 243 is removed. `\crescMolto` now prints its words on two lines (`'span-text (markup #:override '(baseline-skip . 2.3) #:column ("cresc." "molto"))`), as `\cbaw` does since c035. In sHead.ily, Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc and Cb keep `\mf` at 243 and take `\crescMolto` on their first note of 244 (`<g' e''>4\crescMolto`, `<c' g'>4\crescMolto`, `g1\crescMolto`, `g,1\crescMolto`, `g,,1\crescMolto`). I tested the words at 243 beat 1 and beat 3: both times they ran into the 243|244 barline at the normal spacing. At 244 they fit inside the bar. The minims in 243 and 245 now take 3.3-3.5 spaces. The 4.8 for the second minim of 243 includes the space before the barline, and the dotted minim at 244 takes 5.6. This undoes c368's widening, not its text or its dashes to the ff at 247. The strings' crescendo now starts one bar later, which still fits c348's point that the push begins at 245. MIDI: the same notes; only the strings' velocities in 243-246 are slightly lower, and the ff at 247 is unchanged.

### 3. Bar 217: `\textLengthOn` gives the violas' first minim 12.3 spaces and the second 5.4 (applied)

Problem: c265 put `\once \textLengthOn` under "change bow at will" in Vln II, Vc and Cb at 217 so that the words did not cross the 217|218 barline. Text length acts only between the first column and the next one, so in the violas' portato minims the first takes 12.3 spaces, the second 5.4, and those of 218 take 3.6 each.

Evidence: Gould PDF 54 (equal durations, equal space; longer at least as much as shorter); PDF 57 (spacing should be affected as little as possible by added symbols).

Fix (applied): global.ily adds `cbawN = ^\markup \italic \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.3) \column { "change" "bow" "at will" }`, no wider than "change". In codaTwo.ily the three `\once \textLengthOn` are removed and `\cbawN` replaces `\cbaw` at 217 in Vln I, Vln II, Vc and Cb. The minims of 217 now take 4.1 and 5.7 spaces (the 5.7 includes the space before the barline), against 3.9 at 218. The words end before the barline (150 dpi check), and no notes changed.

### 4. No double bar at F (83) or T (273) (applied)

Problem: the score marks the start of a new section at a new tempo with a thin double barline at D, K, N, O, P, Q and R. F (83, the closing group at Tempo I after "animando") and T (273, Largamente, the close) are the only lettered tempo changes without one.

Evidence: Gould PDF 53: thin double barlines "mark divisions between sections in a piece", used neither at every change nor at random. Gould's introduction asks for one consistent approach (c065 cited it for 151 and 171).

Fix (applied): global.ily `\bar "||"` before `\lt \mmk "Tempo I" "84" \mark \default` at 83 and before `\lt \mmk "Largamente" "72"` at 273. In the parts, the letters already divide the rests there.

- 257-262: Cl 2's semibreves stand 0.64 space after the barline (0.74 in v395). Gould PDF 56 asks for one space, but the reversed order of PDF 66 needs the shift, and page 15 has no space left.
