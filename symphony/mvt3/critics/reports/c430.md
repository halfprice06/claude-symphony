### 3. Bars 273–279: cor anglais in the C-major close (tested, declined)
- **Candidate (Casanelles PDF 227–228, Man of Steel):**
  - The rule tested: a signature voice is granted in the last part of a climax.
  - The idea: the cor anglais, which is silent from 221 to the end, would join the 5–♯4–4–3 close and the last chord. Movement II's theme-B voice would then end the symphony on the thread pitch E4.
  - Scratch code: `finaleCorAnglais = { R1*26 | g'2->\ff fis'2 | f'1-> | e'1~\< | e'1 | e'4-> r4 e'4-> r4 | e'1->\fff~ | e'4 r4 r2\fermata | }`
- **Build result:** 0 warnings, 16 pages, 0 outer-voice parallels, and par8.py reports nothing at 272–279.
- **Why I declined it:**
  - It doubles Hn 1·2, Hn 2·4 and the upper violas at the unison inside a ff–fff tutti, so it adds no audible colour. PDF 239 asks that a signifying sound be perceived, not only written.
  - It also undoes the palette logic of PDF 219–220 and 245–248. Since c131 the cor anglais's last notes are 217–220, theme B's answer and its next notes A♭4–G4, the elegiac minor voice that the C-major coda leaves behind. Its silence from 221 is part of the arc.
- The candidate was removed. sections/ in the scratch copy is identical to v430.
