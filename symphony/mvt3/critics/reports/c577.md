## Findings

**1. 83-86: Ob 1 is in unison with Cl 1·2 and Vln II on the major head of F1 (tested and rejected).**
- Problem: the lower octave of the head (E♭5 down to F4) is three winds and Vln II at one pitch. Adler PDF 257 says a unison doubling "robs the timbral characteristics" of both instruments and gives only a stronger pitch. The score itself used Adler PDF 235 (an oboe in unison covers the clarinet and is hard to keep in tune with it) as c144's reason for taking Cl 1 out at 97-98.
- Tested in the scratch copy: `closOboeOne = { es''2->\f r2 | R1*3 | R1*2 | r2 f''8(\f\< ...`. Ob 1 reaches the goal of its 82 run on 83.1 and then rests until its canon entry at 89.3. That entry becomes a fresh colour in the Fl, Ob, Cl canon (PDF 253, 255).
- Build: 0 warnings, 0 parallels, 16 pages.
- Why I rejected it:
  - 83 is the closing group's first full major statement in the winds, f. Adler allows unison doubling in that near-tutti use (PDF 257, and Ex. VIII-39 at PDF 268).
  - Taking out the oboe removes the only double-reed edge from the lower octave. c164's reason still holds: that octave has to carry over the violas, cellos, Bn 1 and the horns' B♭ pedal.
  - Dropping the oboe after half a bar at the start of the theme reads as a broken entry, not as a diminuendo, which is the case Copland's cut is for (PDF 270).
- The scratch copy was reverted.

**2. 230-233: Fl 1·2 in unison on the top line of the plagal close (checked on paper, not applied).**
- Problem: A5-G5-A♭5-G5 is played by both flutes at the unison, and the same line is also in Tpt 1, Ob 2, the violas and Hn 3. This is the "overlapped" voicing of Ex. VIII-21d (PDF 256-257).
- Possible fix: `arrivalFluteTwo` bars 231-233 `c'''1 | c'''1 | c'''1`, a held C6 that encloses the line.
- Why not applied:
  - It puts a new top note over the bar-1 A♭→G motion. That motion is meant to sound on top here.
  - 223-234 is protected, and this proposal does not answer why.
  - Under f brass the unison adds only weight, which PDF 257 accepts in a tutti.

**3. 107-109: a hole from E4 to G5 in the p lament (checked, follows).**
- gaps.py reports a gap of 15-17 semitones between Bn 1's tenor doubling of the lament bass (F4-C4, c327) and the upper 6/3 voices (Vln I C6, Vln II, Ob 1 and Cl 1 G5-F5).
- This is a three-voice suspension chain plus a bass doubling, so the gap sits where the line structure puts it. PDF 257's spacing rule is about chords, not this.
- Filling it would double the 7-6 suspensions or the lament in a fourth layer. The passage is protected (99-109, c197; suspensions c022). No change.

**4. 265-268: the S head in five unison parts at E6-C6, with a C5-E6 gap below it (checked, follows).**
- Fl 1·2, Cl 1 and Vln I·II play the S head in unison over F1 in two octaves: Tpt 1·2 and Cl 2 at C5; Bn 1, Hn 1·2, violas and cellos at C4.
- This is a tutti, where PDF 257 accepts unison doubling. c509 tested Vln II an octave lower and declined it because it lands on the trumpets' C5 head. My check agrees. No change.

**6. 85: the lower octave of the tune passes under Hn 1's B♭4 pedal (checked, follows).**
