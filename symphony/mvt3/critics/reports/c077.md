## Findings

### 1. 247–254: the S head's upper octave has too few players, and at 253–254 one oboe carries it (applied)
**Bars:** 246 (pickup), 247–254.
**Problem:** In the combination at R the S head sounds in two octaves. The lower octave (E4, movement I's pitch) has Cl 1, Cl 2, Hn 1, Hn 2 and the violas, five parts in unison. The upper octave (E5) has Tpt 1 + Ob 1, with Tpt 2 + Ob 2 a third below. Since v77, Tpt 1·2 rest at 253–254, so the last two bars of the phrase (F5–E5–D5–C5 | B4–C5) on top are left to Ob 1, with Ob 2 a third below. This is the cadence of the theme, played ff under four parts on the P head (Fl 1·2, Vln I·II at G6), two trombones ff, horns ff and Tpt 3.
**Evidence:** Adler PDF 252 (VIII-18): a hand-over from one choir to another at the same pitch ("melting") is a sound device, so cycle 76's idea is right. But Adler PDF 255 says the melody "must come through" and not be covered. Adler PDF 267–268 (Dvořák *Carnival*, VIII-39), which is the standard tutti layout, has "the oboes, and clarinets filling out the upper-octave harmony by doubling the violas an octave higher". Here the clarinets stay at the violas' pitch. Adler PDF 257 says a unison doubling "robs the timbral characteristics from both and usually only results in a stronger pitch". The five-part E4 unison does not need a second clarinet. Adler PDF 274 says each gesture belongs in the instrument's best register. Cl 1 at E5–C6 is its strong clarion register; at E4, under horns, it adds almost nothing. With Cl 1 an octave higher, the trumpets' rest at 253–254 becomes a real melt: Ob 1 + Cl 1 hold the line at the trumpets' pitch, and the trumpets' return at 255 keeps its freshness (Adler PDF 253, 260).
**Fix (applied):** finale.ily, Cl 1 at 247–254 goes up an octave. It now plays exactly Ob 1's line, while Cl 2 stays on E4 with the violas and horns:
```
finaleClOne = { e''2.~(\ff e''8 d''8 | c''4 a''2-- g''4 | f''4. e''8 d''4 f''8 a''8 | c'''2 b''2) \breathe | e''2.~( e''8 d''8 | c''4 a''2-- g''4 | f''4. e''8 d''4 c''4 | b'2 c''2) \breathe | ...
```
sHead.ily, so that Cl 1 still enters with movement I's rising sixth (c030), now an octave higher: the pickup at 246 is `f'2. g'4\!` (was `g4`). The range is E5–C6 sounding (written up to D6). The shared staff now prints the two clarinets as two notes on one stem with equal ff; no "a2" at 247. Nothing else changes: the Tpt 1·2 rest at 253–254, the oboes and the 255 entry stay as they are.
**For the composer:** no theme is changed (the quotation record has no clarinet entry). Update the scoring prose in coherence/finale.md, item 9, from "Cl 1·2, Vla (movement I's pitch)" to "Cl 2, Vla (movement I's pitch); Cl 1 with Ob 1 an octave higher".

### 2. 243–246: Hn 1·2 ff under Tpt 1·2 f< (checked, no change)
