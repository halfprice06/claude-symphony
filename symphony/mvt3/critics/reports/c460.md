## Findings

### 1. 269–270 repeated F1's continuation with 266's harmony. 270 is now ii7 over the C pedal. Applied.

**Problem.**
- In the C-major close the continuation of F1's head, C–G–A–B♭–A–G–F–E, is heard twice: at 265–266 in the middle voices (Cl 2, Hn 1·2, Tpt 1·2, Bn 1, violas, cellos) and at 269–270 on top (Fl, Ob, Cl, Vln, in two octaves).
- Both times the only harmony under it is Tpt 3's repeated F4 and the C pedal, so 270 sounds exactly as 266 did.
- In v460 the four-bar harmonic pattern is I – (F over C) – I – V7 at 265–268, then I – (F over C) – I – IV6/4 at 269–272. The phrase that leads to the A6 peak at 272 starts with a bar that repeats 266 note for note in its harmony.
- At 270 beats 2–4 the tenor and alto are empty. Hn 3·4, Bn 1, the violas and the cellos end the motto-rhythm figure on C4 at 270.1 and rest.

**Evidence.**
- Sebesky PDF 19, Ex. 3/3A: "I usually try to avoid harmonizing a melody the same way twice". The two examples give one melody, bar for bar, different chords that "still arrive at the same resting place".
- Sebesky PDF 20: "Try to be aware of harmonic substitutions and use them whenever you feel the need for a little lift in a musical phrase … avoid unnecessary repetition which can lose the listener's interest."
- Sebesky PDF 11 (formal balance): vary what is already there rather than add something new. This change keeps the melody and only gives it a second harmonisation.
- The same principle has been applied once before in this score, at F1's consequent 20 (c257). It has not yet been applied to the coda.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-460/work/sections/finale.ily, with a c460 comment above `finaleBsnOne`):**

- From 270 beat 2 the bar sounds C pedal, A3 (Bn 1), D4 (violas), F4 (Tpt 3) and the tune A–G–F–E. That is ii7 over the pedal, with the tune's A as a chord tone, G and E as passing notes and F as a chord tone.

The C pedal, Tpt 3's line, the timpani and 266 are unchanged. This is not the tonicised IV that c373 and c420 declined, because there is no B♭ chord and no new leading tone.
