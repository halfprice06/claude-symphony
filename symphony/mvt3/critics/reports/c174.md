## Findings

### 1. 143–144: Hn 3–4 hold inside Hn 1–2's F1 head (applied)
- **Problem.** At 143 Hn 1–2 play F1's head a2, f, in G4–B3 (G4 D4. E♭4 | F4 E♭4 D4 C4 B3), an octave under Vln I. c117 added it so that "each theme must be heard at good strength". In the same two bars, Hn 3–4 hold F4/D4 in mid-crescendo (p at 139, < at 141, f at 145). Since c172, Hn 4 has D4 there. So the head sounds in unison with its own D4 and F4, makes seconds against its G4 and E♭4, and is crossed by its own C4–B3, all in the same colour. Tbn 1–2 (D4/B3, pp <) and the violas (D4) hold in the same octave too. That puts seven parts inside the six notes G4–B3, and the head is the one that gets covered.
- **Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 286–287: "The fewer the instruments in a frequency range, the clearer and more present they become ... sounds in the same frequency range mask each other." PDF 289: forte brass are the denser sound and come forward, so the held horns at about mf are the part that masks the head. Rimsky PDF 102: seconds go to instruments of different colour, the rule c119 and c129 already used to take seconds out of the horns.
- **Fix (retr.ily, applied).** `retrHnThree`: `d'1\< | d'1\mf | R1*2 | f'1\f |` (was `d'1\< | d'1 | f'1 | f'1 | f'1\f |`). `retrHnFour`: `g1\< | g1\mf | R1*2 | d'1\f |` (was `g1\< | g1 | d'1 | d'1 | d'1\f |`). Hn 3–4 reach mf at 142, rest two bars and re-enter f at 145, when everyone enters. The G–B–D–F harmony of 143–144 stays in Tbn 1–2/B.tbn (pp <), Vla D4, Cl 1 D5, Ob 1 F5, Bn 1 F3 and the B bass. The rest also gives Hn 3–4 the breath that c166 found missing (135–146 had one breath). This does not touch the protected stretto. No head entry, rhythm or timpani note changes. c172's reasons hold: A♭3→G3 at 139 still resolves the 9th in Hn 4, B is not doubled, there is no G3–F4 seventh in the pair, and no voice reaches C.

### 2. 119 and 122: the Vln I section answers the bassoon at the solo's own level (applied)
- **Problem.** The bassoon solo is p, with mp at its peak. Its accompaniment is pizz. pp. At 119 and 122 the whole Vln I section answers in G6–F♯5, marked p, the same level as the single bassoon below it. Only Vln II's pizz. G5 sounds anywhere near that register.
- **Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 286: a section sounds fuller than one player, which is the difference between the bassoon and the violins here. PDF 287: a part that is alone in its frequency range is "clear and present" without extra level. PDF 271–273, Fig. 6.1: Mahler marks his trumpets a level below the woodwinds to get the balance he wants, so the printed level stands for the result. Adler PDF 274: keep the accompaniment registrally distant from a woodwind soloist. The answer already is, so it does not need the solo's level.
- **Fix (dev.ily, `devVlnOne`, applied).** `r4 g'''8(\pp^\markup \italic "dolce" ...` at 119 and `r4 d'''8(\pp c''' ...` at 122 (were `\p`). The bassoon's notes and dynamics are unchanged (protected by c157/c171).

### 3. 123: the harp in G is pp in the cellos' and bassoon's octave (applied)
- **Problem.** The G-major passage is the harp's only stretch in the development. Its broken chords lie in G2–A3. That is the octave of the cellos' bass (E3–A2 from 125) and of Bn 1's dolce counter-line (E3–C4, p, 125–130), and it doubles the basses' arco G2 at 123–124. The harp is marked pp, below the cellos' p and Bn 1's p. Two sustained parts in its range mask a decaying pluck, so only its attacks get through. The passage that plan and HISTORY call "the harp in G" is not heard as harp.
- **Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 286–287 (masking in one range); PDF 275–276 (the attack transient is the short inharmonic part of a note; what follows it is masked); Adler PDF 113: the lower two octaves are "somber, dark". The register is fixed by c116's protection, so the level has to carry the colour.
- **Fix (dev.ily, `devHarpL`, applied).** `\stemUp g,8\p-\tag #'score ^\markup ...` (was `\pp`). It is now level with the cellos. The crescendo at 129 is unchanged. Notes, register and pedal diagram are untouched, so c116's protected broken-chord passage and pedals stay as they were.
