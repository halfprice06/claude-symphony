## Findings

### 1. 43-48: the three solo statements of the scale figure sit at one flat p (applied)

**Problem.** The transition passes the scale figure from Fl 1 (43-44) to Cl 1 (45-46) to Ob 1 (47-48) over the pp B♭ pedal. trans.ily describes this as "each time darker". All three statements are a bare p with no hairpin. The flute's statement lies in Adler's "clear and brilliant" band (F6-A♭5, Ex. VII-2, PDF 185). The clarinet's runs through the throat tones, "rather pale" (Ex. VII-56, PDF 210). The oboe's lies in the "warm, prominent" band (Ex. VII-32, PDF 198). With the same mark on all three, the line has no shape and the darkening is left to register alone.

**Evidence.** Adler's printed solos shape every figure. In Brahms 4/iv (Ex. VII-4, PDF 186) the flute is mp with `<` on each rising half-figure and `>` on the fall. In Tchaikovsky 6/i (Ex. VII-93, PDF 227) every two-note figure has its own hairpin and the levels change from figure to figure. In Ex. VII-49 (Tristan, PDF 206) the cor anglais phrase runs p < f dim. p. Each of the finale's statements falls an octave and climbs back: the flute's and clarinet's end on their top note, while the oboe's ends on two falling halves.

**Fix (applied, trans.ily).** The rising half of the first two statements swells into its top note. The last statement fades into the pp the oboe already holds at 49.
```
transFluteOne = { r4 f''8(\p es''8 d''8 c''8 bes'8 aes'8 | bes'8\< c''8 d''8 es''8 f''4)\! r4 | R1*6 | }
transClOne = { R1*2 | r4 bes'8(\p aes'8 ges'8 f'8 es'8 d'8 | es'8\< f'8 ges'8 aes'8 bes'4)\! r4 | ...
transOboeOne = { R1*4 | r4 ges''8(\p f''8 es''8 des''8 ces''8 bes'8 | aes'2\> ges'2) | aes'1\pp~ | aes'2. r4 | }
```
Nothing else in the passage changes: notes, slurs and the starting level of each statement stay as they were. Fl 2, Ob 2 and Cl 2 rest in these bars, so the shared staves get no unequal marks. None of the protected items changes: the violas at 46-49, the harp harmonics at 43-47 and c228's A♭3 hand-over from Bn 1 to Cl 2.

### 2. 236-237: the chromatic counter (Cl 1 + violas) is flat at p against the violins' swells (applied)

**Problem.** At 235-238 the violins carry movement I's second theme with a swell in every bar (c138, c238). Cl 1 and the violas play its chromatic counter `r4 c'' b' bes' | a'2 c''2~ | c''4 bes' a'2 | g'2 f'2` at p with no hairpin. c238 marked the tune because "a line held at one level sounds lifeless", and the same applies to the counter. The counter's one held note is the tied C5 from 236.3 into 237.

**Evidence.** Mendelssohn's Hebrides (Ex. VII-63, PDF 212) gives the second clarinet's independent line its own `< >` and its own word, separate from the first clarinet's. Brahms 1/iv (Ex. VII-9, PDF 188) swells the held note itself. Placing the counter's swell on its held note makes it rise with the violins' crescendo on 236. It then recedes on 237 while the violins climb to their C6 at 238, so the counter steps back as the tune peaks.

**Fix (applied, sHead.ily).**
```
sHeadClOne = { r4 c''4(\p b'4 bes'4 | a'2) c''2~(\< | c''4\> bes'4 a'2\! | g'2 f'2) | ...
sHeadVla = { r4 c''4(\p b'4 bes'4 | a'2) c''2~(\< | c''4\> bes'4 a'2)\! | g'2( f'2) | ...
```
Cl 2 rests at 235-238, so the shared clarinet staff gets no unequal marks. The notes and the 239-242 f (c250) do not change. This change touches only the marks of the S head, not the harmonic plan of 235-247 that is protected.
