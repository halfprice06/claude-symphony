## Findings

**1. Harp octave sign at 239–240 in the part: tested and declined.** Gould PDF 47 says a full score may use octave signs to save space, but "these octave signs should not be transferred to a playing part, except where absolutely necessary". It also says to use ledger lines rather than octave signs for isolated chords in performance material, and to keep a phrase in one octave. At present the harp part copies the score's 8 bassa for the two octave chords A1–A2 (239) and F1–F2 (240). In the part, those two bars are split by a line break at 239|240, so there are two 8 signs for two chords. They also hide the drop from G2 at 238 to A1, which looks like a step.

The test made the sign score-only in sHead.ily:
```
\tag #'score { \ottava #-1 \set Staff.ottavation = #"8" } <a,, a,>1\mf | r4 <f,, f,>2. \tag #'score { \ottava #0 } |
```
The full score is unchanged by this. In the part, the extra depth of F1 on four ledger lines pushed the last system (255–279) onto a third page. That adds a page turn just before the final glissando and the ff chord, and leaves one system alone on page 3. With the harp book's system padding cut from 0.5 to 0, the part fits on two pages again. But then the F1 ledger lines nearly touch the "T Largamente" mark, and the bar range "247–254" runs into it. Neither result is better than the present part. The present part also follows PDF 44, because the 8 is restated at the start of the system. Decision: keep v565 as it is.

**2. Ledger-line limits: no change.** The scan finds no woodwind or string note beyond five ledger lines (PDF 47) and no brass octave sign (PDF 47). The horns' four lines below the stave at 13, 21, 153 and 161 fall inside F1's phrase. A clef change there would break the phrase (PDF 47: change octave between phrases), and Gould says four lines are read at a glance (PDF 47). The cellos' three lines at 210 and 258 are kept as c405 left them (PDF 21–22: no clef change mid-slur).

**3. Rests on shared staves: no change.** Every situation in PDF 48–52 is handled by c205, c295, c385 and c475 (rows 17–25). The one remaining doubled-rest case, minim rests at 26.3, 113.3 and 204.3, prints as one symbol.

**4. Harp 8 bassa at 67–82: keep, in score and part.** This passage is 16 bars long, goes down to D1 (five ledger lines) and E♭1, and crosses a system break with the 8 restated at 77. That is the "more extensive use" Gould allows the harp (PDF 47), and the line ends right after the last notehead (PDF 44, c418).

No other finding. The score already meets every principle in this reading where it applies.
