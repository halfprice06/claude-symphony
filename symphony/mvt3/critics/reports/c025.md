## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bassoons 269–272: missing rest and a false "2." label — applied
- Problem: Bn 1 has `r8 g8( e'4.)…`, Bn 2 has `c4-> r4 r2`. The part combiner treats the first eighth as a Bn 2 solo: Bn 1's eighth rest is not printed, Bn 2's C3 is drawn with an up-stem in the upper voice, and "2." is printed at 271, so the player reads that only Bn 2 plays.
- Evidence: Gould PDF 50: "Two parts on a stave with different durations require their own sets of rests"; PDF 51: rests that are part of a beat align.
- Fix (finale.ily, `finaleBsnOne`): `\partCombineApart` before `r8\ff g8(` at 269 and `\partCombineAutomatic` before `f1->` at 273. Scratch build shows Bn 1's eighth rest, stems up, Bn 2 stems down with its own rests, no "2.".

### 2. Bassoons 107–109 and 114: Bn 1's whole-bar rests drawn against Bn 2's notes — applied
- Problem: Bn 1 rests (`R1*3`, `R1`) while Bn 2 plays the lament line and the held D. LilyPond prints Bn 1's semibreve rest high in the stave, beside Bn 2's half notes, and Bn 2's stems alternate direction. At 106 the same situation is shown correctly with "1.".
- Evidence: Gould PDF 50 (semibreve rests may not stray across the centre line; simultaneous rests separated); PDF 51 (when this gets complicated, simplify; parts share rests). A whole-bar solo is shown by the solo label, as this score does at 106 and 115.
- Fix (dev.ily, `devBsnOne`): `| a2 a2 | \partCombineSoloII R1*3 \partCombineAutomatic | e1\f |` and `| c2-> r2 | \partCombineSoloII R1 \partCombineAutomatic | d'2.(\p`. Scratch build prints "2." at 107 and 114, no rests, stems normal.

### 3. Bassoons 101: crossed parts and a rest on the dynamic — applied
- Problem: Bn 2 ends its phrase on `f4-.` (F3) while Bn 1 enters on `e1\p` (E3) below it. The stems cross (Bn 2 down above Bn 1 up), and Bn 2's crotchet rest is pushed below the stave onto Bn 1's "p".
- Evidence: Gould PDF 41 (overlapping parts), PDF 51 (a rest displaced by the other part moves further from the stave; separate or simplify when it gets complicated).
- Fix (dev.ily, `devBsnTwo` bar 3): `f4-.` → `f,4-.` (F2, with the double basses' `f,4-.\sf`). It matches the analogous bar 105, where Bn 2 ends on B♭2 below Bn 1's A3 and engraves cleanly. Scratch build: no crossing, rest clear, "p" clear. Outer-voice parallels still 0.

### 4. Harp left hand: four and five ledger lines in three phrases — applied
- Bars 67–82 (down to F1 at 71), 187–198 (D1 at 191, 5 ledger lines), 235–242 (D1 at 242).
- Evidence: Gould PDF 46 ("Use up to at least three ledger lines before transposing… and using an octave sign"); PDF 47 ("Keyboard and harp music may make more extensive use of octave signs"; keep a phrase intact at one octave and change between phrases); PDF 42 and 44 (italic 8 before the first note; corner right after the last notehead).
- Fix: one 8 bassa per phrase, written as `\ottava #-1 \set Staff.ottavation = #"8"` before the first note and `\ottava #0` after the last, in sTwo.ily (`sTwoHarpL`, 67–82), recS.ily (`recSHarpL`, 187–198) and sHead.ily (`sHeadHarpL`, 235–242). Pitches and MIDI are unchanged. In the scratch build the whole bass line sits in the stave, the line ends with a corner at 82, 198 and 242, and the 8 is repeated at the system break before 239.

### 5. Percussion: no dynamics at 263, 265 and 278 — applied
