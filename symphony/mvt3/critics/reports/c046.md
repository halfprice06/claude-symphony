## Findings

### 1. 243–255: no breath for Tpt 1·2 and Hn 1 between the held horn call, the S head and the chorale (applied)
Problem: Tpt 1 holds C6 from beat 3 of 245 through the whole of 246 under one slur, then re-enters ff on beat 1 of 247 with no rest. Then come two 4-bar ff slurs (247–250, 251–254, 4.8 s each at 2 = 100) with no gap between them or before the chorale entry at 255. Tpt 2 and Hn 1 have the same layout. Hn 2 is the only one of these players who has a gap (it gives the pickup G3 at 246).
Evidence: Adler PDF 292 (slurred phrases on one breath; frequent breathing room), PDF 309 and 321 (same rule for horn and trumpet), PDF 317 (C6 is the top of the safe range). With no gap, the player has to cut the C6 short at an unmarked point, or enter 247 without air.
Fix (applied): sHead.ily `sHeadTptOne … | c'''2.) r4 |`, `sHeadTptTwo … | c''2.) r4 |`, `sHeadHnOne … | c''2.) r4 |` (were whole notes). finale.ily: `\breathe` after `c'''2 b''2)` (250) and `b'2 c''2)` (254) in Tpt 1, after `e''2 d''2)` and `g'2 a'2)` in Tpt 2, and after `c''2 b'2)` and `b2 c'2)` in Hn 1·2. Quotation note: this shortens the last note of the horn-call quotation (movement I 13–14) at 246. themecheck ignores the last note's length, so the record still reads 35/35.

### 2. 247–279: trombones 1·2 play 33 bars without a rest into the fff close (applied)
Problem: Tbn 1·2 play every bar from 247 to 279 (about 43 s at f–fff). That covers the held chords, the chorale ff→fff, the F1 head at 265–268, the figure at 269–272 and the Largamente close. The trumpets get 271–272 off, Hn 3·4 get 265–268 and Hn 1·2 get 269–272. The trombones get nothing, and they lead the figure at 269–272 and hold the fff chord at 278.
Evidence: Adler PDF 292 (rests so the lips recover); adler2 PDF 6 (contrabass trombone "much too taxing", the same concern for endurance). Dvořák 9/iv gives its trombones a rest of about 10 bars inside the coda.
Fix (applied): finale.ily `finaleTbnOne` and `finaleTbnTwo`: 265–268 `R1*4` (was the F1 head at f, an octave under Hn 1·2/Tpt 1·2, doubling the cellos). They re-enter ff at 269 as before. The F1 head keeps C5 in Tpt 1·2, Hn 1·2 and violas and C4 in the cellos. This revises cycle 24 ("lower brass f at 265–268"), which only set their dynamic.

### 3. 199–228: horns play 30 bars without a rest, including held high notes (applied)
Problem: all four horns play every bar from 199 to 228 (about 50 s): the hammered tutti ff, the F2 figure, the A♭7 build with Hn 1 on written B♭5 for four bars (207–210), the D♭ episode, the crescendo 219–222, the fff arrival and the chorale. Their first rest is 229.
Evidence: Adler PDF 303 ("most taxing to play in the higher register all the time, so periodic rests are most welcome") and PDF 292.
Fix (applied): codaTwo.ily `codaTwoHnOne = { R1*4 | aes'1\f | …` and `codaTwoHnTwo = { R1*4 | c'1\f | …` (211–214 rest; they re-enter f at 215 after the trumpets' breath). The D♭, G♭ and A♭7 chords stay complete in Hn 3·4, the trombones, the bass and the woodwinds. Hn 1 now rests straight after its high held note.

### 4. 207–210: the low horn sits above the high horn (applied)
Problem: in the A♭7 build Hn 2 holds sounding C5 (written G5) for four bars with a crescendo, and Hn 3 holds G♭4 below it.
Evidence: Adler PDF 303 (I and III high, II and IV low; a third horn sits between 1 and 2) and PDF 301 Ex. X-2 (the cor basso range ends at written G5, so this sustained note is at the top of the low horn's range).
Fix (applied): codaOne.ily `codaOneHnTwo … | ges'1 | ges'1 | ges'1\< | ges'2. r4\! |` and `codaOneHnThree … | c''1 | c''1 | c''1\< | c''2. r4\! |`. The pitches and rhythm are the same; only the players are swapped, so the voicing now runs Hn 1 E♭5, Hn 3 C5, Hn 2 G♭4, Hn 4 E♭4. The all-pairs fifths check (c032/p5.py) gives the same output as v46.

### 5. 51–66 and 171–186: four 4-bar slurs back to back in the trumpet solo with nowhere marked to breathe (applied)
Problem: the solo (and the muted repeat with the cor anglais) is phrased 51–54, 55–58, 59–62, 63–66. Each slur ends on a held note and the next starts on the downbeat, so the breath has to be stolen from the last note at a point the player chooses.
Evidence: Adler PDF 292 and 321 (all slurred notes are one breath); cycle 28 marked the same thing for theme B at 214.
Fix (applied): `\breathe` after the slurs ending in 54, 58 and 62 (sOne.ily `sOneTptOne`), and in 174, 178 and 182 in recS.ily for both `recSTptOne` and `recSCorAnglais`. No notes, slurs or dynamics change, so the protected solo (soft, plain, undoubled at 51; muted with the cor anglais at 171) is the same music. F2 is the finale's own theme; only breath marks are added.
