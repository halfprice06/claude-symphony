## Findings

**F1 (applied): 263–264, Fl 2 drops out of the chorale's last C onto a weak E5.**
- **Problem.** From 257 to 262 both flutes play the chorale top line in unison with Fl 1, ending on B5 at 262. At the fff tonic of the movement's only root-position V–I (263), Fl 2 then leaps a fifth down from its leading tone to E5. It holds that note fff and diminishes through 264, while Fl 1 alone resolves to C6.
- **Evidence.** E5 lies in the flute's weak lower-middle register. Against Tpt 1·2 at C5 and the trombones at fff it cannot be heard, which goes against Adler PDF 159, rule 2. The note adds nothing: Vln II already has E5 (double stop, fff), and E4 sounds in Ob 2, Hn 3, Tpt 3 and the violas. Adler's models for the full tutti chord work the other way. In the Missa Solemnis (PDF 160, bar 21) the flute doubles the first violin at pitch on top. For Mahler 1 (PDF 164) Adler notes that "all the upper octaves are completely filled in". He also says a unison of like instruments "gives a peculiar weight" (PDF 165–166). The leap also breaks the "according to the book" voice leading of PDF 162, because the doubled leading tone in the top voice goes nowhere. Earlier critics found the chorale's top octave at 257–263 thin (c119, c127, c088). With C7 reserved for 223, a second flute on C6 is the one way left to strengthen it.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-404/work/sections/finale.ily, finaleFluteTwo, with a c404 comment).** Change `c'''1 | b''1 | e''1\fff~ | e''2.\> r4\! |` to `c'''1 | b''1 | c'''1\fff~ | c'''2.\> r4\! |`. The staff now prints "a2" on C6 at 263, matching Fl 1's dynamics and hairpin. Nothing else changes: the harmony, the top line, the cut at 264.1 and the flute breaths are as before. The 263–279 protection (c223, for the repeated IV6/4 over the tonic pedal) and the 259–263 cadence protection (c192, harmony) are both about harmony, and neither is touched. The rule that C7 sounds only at 223 still holds.

**F2 (considered, not applied): 262, the V7's seventh is weakly placed and the leading tone is in about ten parts.**
- **Problem.** At 262 the seventh F has only one held part on beats 1–2: Ob 2 on F4, its weakest register, at ff. B sounds in Fl 1·2, Ob 1, Cl 2, Tpt 1·2, Bn 1, Hn 1·2, Tbn 1 and Vln I.
- **Evidence.** Adler PDF 159 (rule 2) and PDF 162: in Schumann every pitch is doubled at least once and the sevenths resolve downward.
- **Decision: no change.** The B is the chorale melody doubled in three octaves, which rule 1 on PDF 159 allows. Hn 3 already takes the seventh on beat 3 (G4→F4→E4), so the bar moves from V to V7. Holding F in Hn 1 from beat 1 would cancel that 8–7, and adding a D makes fifths (c334, protected).

**F3 (not applied): 265–268, the motto in movement I's head rhythm has no brass.**
- **Problem.** Fl 1·2, Cl 1 and Vln I·II carry G–E–D–C–B–C at G5–E6, while Tpt 1·2, Hn 1·2, Bn 1, Vla and Vc play F1. By Adler's Meistersinger rule (PDF 178), each theme's colour should include a brass instrument.
- **Decision: no change.** No brass instrument can play G5–E6 at ff except the trumpets, and they carry F1. The motto gets its brass (Hn 3·4, then Tbn 1·2) at 269–272, where the rested horns enter fresh. The two elements already differ in rhythm and articulation (slurred against staccato), which PDF 176–178 asks for.

**F4 (not applied): 231–233, the timpani roll on C is f under the plagal close.**
- **Problem.** Bloch marks his rolls p and then crescendo because a loud roll covers the orchestra (PDF 184).
- **Decision: no change.** This roll is a pedal under brass that are also marked f, and it diminishes to p at 234. It lies more than two octaves below the Vln II portato pulse (F5), so it does not cover it. Bloch's case is a crescendo build, which this is not.

**F5 (not applied): 257–263, the chorale top line is in four parts at pitch (Tpt 1·2, Ob 1, Cl 2).**
- **Problem.** Adler PDF 165 says a sustained doubling at pitch loses colour.
- **Decision: no change.** Ob 1 with the trumpets is the affinity doubling Adler recommends (PDF 161, 172). The Cl 2 octave has been moved back and forth since c044, c088 and v110, so changing it again would be churn.
