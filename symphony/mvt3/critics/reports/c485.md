## Findings

### 1. Bars 68–69, bassoons: a crossing semibreve merged with the other part's note, and a semibreve against a stem (applied)

**Problem.** At 68 Bn 1 holds B♭2 as a semibreve under Bn 2's D3–E♭3 halves. The partcombiner sets the semibreve against Bn 2's down-stemmed D3, with the heads touching, so it reads as a two-note chord with a stem. Nothing shows that the B♭ belongs to Bn 1, the up-stem part. At 69 Bn 1's semibreve C3 stands left of Bn 2's dotted-half C3, whose down-stem touches the semibreve.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 65 says the upper part takes up-stems even when the parts overlap. PDF 66 says to keep noteheads separate, that stems should not touch, and that "semibreves in particular should not appear to be joined to stems". PDF 66–67 says to place a down-stemmed part to the left, slightly separated, and that "overlapping semibreves must be divided into stemmed notes in order to show part progression". c215 did this at 199–202 and 256, and c305 did the semibreve offset at 65 and 185; 68–69 were missed.

**Fix** (sections/sTwo.ily, score only; the part keeps `bes,1`; MIDI byte-identical):
```
sTwoBsnOne = { es1\p | \partCombineApart \tag #'score { \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.3 bes,2~ bes,2 \revert NoteColumn.force-hshift } \tag #'part { bes,1 } | \tag #'score { \once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0 } c1 \partCombineAutomatic | ...
sTwoBsnTwo = { es1\p | \tag #'score { \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0 } d2 es2 \tag #'score { \revert NoteColumn.force-hshift } | \tag #'score { \once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.9 } c2. bes,4 | ...
```
Result on page 4: at 68, Bn 2's down-stemmed D3 and E♭3 sit on the left and Bn 1's up-stemmed, tied B♭2 halves sit to their right, clear of them. At 69, Bn 2's dotted-half C3 sits clear to the right of the semibreve, with its dot beside it.

### 2. Bar-length bars: \cen out of date at 97 and 138, missing at 5 (applied)

**Problem.** c215 centred the bars that then held one bar-length note in every part. Later cycles added other durations to two of them: Cl 1's half rest and half note at 97 (dolce entry) and Tpt 1·2's quaver at 138. Those bars still carry `\cen`, which pushes the whole bar to the right. Bar 5 now holds only semibreves in every part (the second ff German sixth), but its chord stands against the barline. Bar 1 is centred because it opens the system.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 55: in such bars "the note is placed just left of the centre of the bar … When there are other durations in other parts, the single duration is placed at the beginning of the bar as normal." The script's list of pure bar-length bars is 5, 96, 135, 136, 137, 147, 219, 221, 223.

**Fix** (global.ily): bar 5, `s1*10` becomes `s1*4 \cen s1 s1*5`. Bar 97: `s1*9 \cen s1 \cen s1 s1` becomes `s1*9 \cen s1 s1 s1`. Bar 138: the third `\cen` after J is removed. Pages 1, 5 and 7 change. I tested a key-signature `space-alist.next-note` override for 135 and 219, which follow a key change. It changed nothing on the page, because the signature already places the note near the centre, so I left those bars alone. 223 opens a system, where the start-of-system distances on PDF 56 govern, so I left it too.
