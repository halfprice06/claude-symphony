## Findings

### 1. 43-50: the held seventh A♭3 is re-attacked twice; colour it by a hand-over instead (applied)

**Problem.** Over the transition's B♭ pedal (43-50, pp, poco rit. at 49-50) Bn 1 is the only wind holding the seventh of V7 of E♭, A♭3, for eight bars. It is written `aes1~ | aes1~ | aes1~ | aes1 | aes1~ | aes1 | aes1~ | aes2.`, so the same pitch is re-attacked at 47 and at 49. Both re-attacks fall where something else enters (Ob 1's figure at 47, Hn 1's pp D4 at 49), so a pp held note gets two audible tongued restarts in one colour at the quietest point of the exposition.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 190-191, "Coloring a Note", Ex. XV-37 (image): a held C, pp, passes Ob 1 → Fl 1 → Bn 1 → Cl 1 → cor anglais, each instrument entering under the previous one before it stops. PDF 190 (Ex. XV-36): the two sounds must overlap. PDF 192: at a change of colour "make certain that the seams don't show". The transition already changes colour phrase by phrase (figure Fl → Cl → Ob, "each time darker"); the held seventh is the one element that does not, and it has seams.

**Fix (applied in sections/trans.ily).**
```
transClOne = { R1*2 | r4 bes'8(\p aes'8 ges'8 f'8 es'8 d'8 | es'8 f'8 ges'8 aes'8 bes'4) r4 | \partCombineSoloII R1*4 \partCombineAutomatic | }
transClTwo = { R1*4 | aes1\pp~ | aes1~ | aes1~ | aes2. r4 | }
transBsnOne = { aes1\pp~ | aes1~ | aes1~ | aes1~ | aes2 r2 | R1*3 | }
```
Bn 1 holds A♭3 tied 43 to 47 beat 2 (about 6.5 s at pp). Cl 2 enters the same A♭3 pp in the chalumeau at 47, two beats under the bassoon, after Cl 1's figure has ended (46 beat 3), and holds it to 50 beat 3. The seventh then resolves to G♭3 at 51 in Cl 2's own part (Cl 2's first note at 51 is G♭3), while Bn 1, now resting 47.3-50, enters fresh at 51 on its own G♭3. No pitch, rhythm of the harmony, or dynamic changes; the violas 46-49 and the harp harmonics (protected by c206) are untouched. The clarinet staff prints "2." at 47 (checked on page 3). This also gives Bn 1 three bars' rest before its pp entry at 51.
