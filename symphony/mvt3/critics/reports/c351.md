## Findings

### 1. 258: F2's return in the coda has lost F2's climax rhythm. Give the peak its dotted figure (applied). This changes a statement of the finale's own theme F2.

**Bars:** 255–258 (letter S, Più allegro 2 = 88). Under the last chorale statement the cellos, violas and Hn 3·4 (Bn 1 from 257) play F2's third phrase in C major, ff espress.: G3.–E3 | D3 C3 E3 G3 | C4.–D4 | G4 F4 E4 D4 (c317, c321).

**Problem:** This phrase is the same as 75–78 in the E♭-major violin statement, a sixth lower, over the same harmony: the peak bar 78 is B♭6 over vi7 (bass C), and 258 is G4 over vi7 (bass A). At 78 every part that carries F2's peak plays it dotted: B♭6 dotted quarter, A♭ eighth, G, F (Vln I·II, Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1, Hn 1, Vc). This is the theme's climax figure and the only long-short figure in the major tune. c317 copied the rhythm of the minor trumpet solo's peak bar (62, plain quarters, protected as plain), not the major statement's. So at its last appearance, the one place where F2 is heard in the tonic major, F2 lacks its climax figure. The phrase's highest note, G4, is a quarter like the notes before and after it. A listener who knows 78 hears four even quarters inside a ff tutti and cannot tell this from an inner line.

**Evidence:** A&S PDF 50: long notes and high notes attract accent. At 78 the peak note is both, and at 258 it is only high. PDF 51: the simplest rhythms put the long note on the strong beat, as 78 does on 78.1 and 258 now does on 258.1. PDF 52: rhythmic groups "form an important element in the design of a composition". The dotted group belongs to F2's design, and a returning theme is recognised by such a group (c261's reason for F2's anticipation at 73/193). PDF 54 (Ex. 3-15): dissonances are normally short and unaccented. At 258.2 the passing F4 now sounds as a quarter on the beat against the chorale's held E6 and the arpeggiated A–C–E in Vln II and Cl 1. Dotted, it is an eighth on the off-beat. Beethoven 9/iv bar 96 (corpus) is the model for a plain tune whose one dotted figure is what the ear keeps.

**Fix (applied, sections/finale.ily):**
```
finaleVc, finaleVla:            g'4( f'4 e'4 d'4)   ->  g'4.( f'8 e'4 d'4)
finaleHnThree, finaleHnFour,
finaleBsnOne:                   g'4 f'4 e'4 d'4)    ->  g'4. f'8 e'4 d'4)
```
