## Findings

### 1. The bass trombone's return for the last chorale statement is a forte re-entry on the second theme's pitch (applied)

- **Bars:** 255–258 (bass trombone).
- **Problem:** The bass trombone rests 235–254 (20 bars, about 30 s) and comes back at 255, the first bar of the chorale's last statement, `e1\f`: E3, forte. That is the re-entry the reading calls weaker than pp or ff. E3 also doubles Tbn 2's E3 at the unison, and it is one of F2's own pitches at 255.4 (cellos, violas and Hn 3·4 play G3–E3, then D3–C3–E3–G3). c321 named exactly this ("F2's returning phrase ... inside ff held brass at its own pitches (... B.tbn E3)") and solved it by marking the held brass f, which kept the pitch where it was. From 257 the bass trombone in fact doubles the tuba's line an octave up (A2, F2, A♭2, G2, C3 over A1, F1, A♭1, G1, C2), so it is part of the bass, not of the held harmony.
- **Evidence:** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 136: "After a long rest the re-entry of the horns, trombones and tuba should coincide with some characteristic intensity of tone, either pp or ff; piano and forte re-entries are less successful", and "a group of instruments which has been silent for some time gains fresh interest upon its reappearance". PDF 132–133: a basis set an octave away from the line clashes less with it. par8.py on v393 flags 257.1 "Bass trombone E3→A2, bass E2→A1" as an octave with the bass that starts on the tied E3.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-393/work/sections/finale.ily):**
  `finaleBassTbn = { R1*8 | e,1\ff~ | e,1 | a,1~ | a,1 | f,1\<~ | f,1 \breathe | aes,1 | g,1 | c1\fff~ | …`
  (was `R1*8 | e1\f~ | e1 | a,1~\< | a,1 | f,1\ff\<~ | …`). The bass trombone enters ff on E2 with the tuba (ff) and the basses, keeps the tuba's upper octave at ff from 257, and crescendos with the others from 259 to fff at 263. Tbn 2 keeps the chord's E3; the held harmony (Hn 1·2, Tbn 1·2, Tpt 3) stays f < ff as c321 set it; pitch classes, the chorale top line and 259–263 are unchanged. This answers c321's reason rather than undoing it: the bass trombone leaves F2's register instead of being marked down inside it. Comment added above the variable.
