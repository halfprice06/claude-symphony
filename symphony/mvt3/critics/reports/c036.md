## Findings

### 1. Bassoons and contrabassoon have no rest from 211 to 254 (applied)
Bars: 211-234, 247-254. Problem: Bn 1, Bn 2 and Cbn play every bar of 211-234 (24 semibreves, ff to fff, Cbn down to B0), about 46 s; Bn 1·2 then go on through 235-254, so Bn 2 sounds 118 s of the 130 s from 195 to the end, its only rest being 255-256 (2.4 s). Evidence: Adler PDF 230: the contrabassoon's low notes take "considerable" breath, "it is advisable to provide quite a few periodic rests"; PDF 175 and 198 on double-reed breath control and mandatory rests. Where they can rest nothing is lost: at 231-234 the C pedal is in tuba, bass trombone, cellos and basses; at 247-254 Bn 2 doubles the bass trombone at the unison (c1 d1 f,1 g,1 …).
