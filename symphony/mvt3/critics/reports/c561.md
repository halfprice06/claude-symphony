## Findings

### 1. 257–258: F2's last climax bar has no wind in its upper octave (applied)

- **Problem.** At 255–258 F2 returns in C under the brass chorale (since c317/c371). The lower octave (G3–C4–G4) has violas, cellos, Hn 3·4 and, from 257, Bn 1. The upper octave (G4–G5) is Vln II alone, sul G. It shares its pitches with Tpt 1·2 ff on the chorale (G4, then E5) and with Tpt 3 and Hn 1·2 on E4/C4. So the octave that should lead is the weakest-doubled line, and it lies inside the brass register. In bar 258 the phrase reaches its peak (G5–F5–E5–D5), the rising fourth that critics since c141 have called F2's most memorable gesture. That bar should be heard.
- **Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 78: when only one octave of a soprano melody is doubled, the winds should go in octaves and the strings double the lower part. Ex. 90 (PDF 265, Sheherazade IV) shows this layout: clarinets a2 above, cellos + 2 horns below. PDF 76: a wind in unison with a string group "increases the resonance … and amplifies its tone", and the string still leads when the two are of equal power (one clarinet on Vln II). PDF 67: clarinet over bassoon is one of the natural octave pairs. PDF 74–75: the chorale top keeps its wind (Ob 1 with Tpt 1·2, the trumpet + oboe pairing; c044 put Ob 1 there).
- **Why not 255–256.** The woodwind rest at 255–256 and their fresh entry at 257 are protected (c033, c038, c486; c487 declined Bn 2 there for this reason). The cor anglais is silent after 220 on purpose (c430). The doubling therefore starts at 257, together with the other woodwinds. Rimsky approves doubling a single phrase (PDF 66–67), and this is the peak phrase.
- **Why Cl 2 and not Ob 2.** Ob 1 holds E5 through 257–258. With Ob 2 on F2, the F5 eighth and the D5s would make seconds inside the oboe pair. With Cl 2 the seconds fall between different colours. Cl 2 also returns from F2's last D5 to the chorale's D5 at 259 without a leap.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-561/work/sections/finale.ily, `finaleClTwo`):**
  ```

  ```
  It was `R1*2 | \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #-1.0 e''1\f\<~ | e''1 \breathe | d''1\ff\<~`. The dynamics match Cl 1 (f< at 257) on the shared staff. `\partCombineApart` keeps Cl 2's stems down under Cl 1's arpeggio; I checked it at 500 dpi and it reads clearly. The breath stays at the end of 258. The chorale's E5 continues in Tpt 1·2, Ob 1, Fl 1·2 (E6) and Vln I. F2's notes, the chorale top line, the harmony (vi at 257–258) and the woodwind rest at 255–256 are unchanged. This change is to scoring only; no theme pitch changes.
- **Checks.** Build: 0 warnings (build-warnings.txt empty), 16 pages, analysis.txt outer-voice parallels 0 (unchanged). par8.py on 255–260 gives the same output as v561: only the existing bass-octave doubling at 259.1 in the bass clarinet and Bn 2. c002/par.py lists new P8 pairs only between Cl 2 and Bn 1, violas, cellos and Hn 3·4 at 257.4 and 258.3. These are F2's own octave doubling, the same relation Vln II already had.

### 2. 83–86: three winds on one string group (declined on paper)

Rimsky PDF 76 says that "if several wind instruments play in unison with one group of strings, the latter will be overpowered". At 83–86 Vln II carries the closing theme's lower octave with Ob 1 and Cl 1·2 (Fl 1·2 on Vln I's octave). On paper this violates the rule. I keep it. The passage is f and the theme belongs to the winds as much as to the strings. The layout is protected (c147, c164, c166, c187, c254), and the clarinets were raised to this octave on purpose. Moving one clarinet down would put it in the violas' leggiero eighths, which c254 already treated as a crowded register when it set them mf.

### 3. 205–206: four horns with no wind above (no change)

Rimsky PDF 75 says that two horns doubled in the upper octave need three or four winds. At 205–206 the F1 head is in four horns a2 over Tbn 1·2 (Rimsky's horns-over-trombones octave, PDF 74, applied in c381). No wind is above it. The violins' rising scales hold the upper register, and the woodwinds take the head fresh at 207–208. A wind octave at 205 would use up that entry. Not changed.
