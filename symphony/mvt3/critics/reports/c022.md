## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 241–243: the 7th of II6/5 has no resolution, and resolving it would give parallel 5ths (applied)
**Problem.** c020 put II6/5 (F–A–C–D, bass F) at 242 and moved to III♯ (E–G♯–B) at 243. The 7th, C, is in Vln I, Vln II, Fl 1, Ob 1, Hn 3 and the harp. Every one of them leaves it by leap or rests: Vln I C6→E5/G♯4, Vln II C5→E5, Hn 3 C4→rest. No voice takes C to B.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 177: the 7th of II7 resolves "downward and by step" and does not enter by leap. Hn 3 leaps A→C into it at 242. PDF 178: when the resolution would go into the wrong voice, use II6 instead. The deeper problem is the bass. F falling to E under a 6/5 chord makes C→B parallel 5ths with the bass. PDF 191 rules out IV6/5 moving down to V for exactly this reason. PDF 183 (Ex. 12-10, Bach Chorale 108) gives the remedy: keep II with its root in the bass and bring in the 7th as a passing 8–7 above it.
**Fix.** At 242, put D in the bass, so that 241–242 is ii–ii7 with the melody's D–C as the passing 7th (Ex. 12-10). Then let C fall to B at 243 while the bass rises D→E (contrary motion, no 5ths). Bass motion stays by step, and III♯ still comes from a chord without G, which keeps c020's aim.
```
sHeadVc / sHeadBsnTwo:  d1 | f,1 | e,2\< g,2   ->  d1 | d,1 | e,2\< g,2
sHeadCb:                d,1 | f,,1 | e,,2\< g,,2 | g,,1  ->  d,1 | d,1 | e,2\< g,2 | g,,1
sHeadHarpL:             d,1 | f,,1 | R1*4   ->  d,1 | d,,1 | R1*4
sHeadHnThree:           a1 | c'1 | R1*4 |   ->  a1 | d'2 c'2 | b2 r2 | R1*3 |
sHeadOboeOne:           d''2 c''2) | R1*4 | ->  d''2 c''2) | b'2 r2 | R1*3 |
sHeadVlnTwo (243):      <e' e''>2\< <c' e''>2  ->  <e' b'>2\< <c' e''>2
```
This changes c020's 242 but keeps its I – (ii) – III♯ – cadential 6/4 – V4/3 – I. The comment in sHead.ily should now read "ii (241) – ii7 (242, passing 7th) – III♯".

### 2. Bars 78–79: VI – V6 – IV, with the leading tone in the bass falling a tritone (applied)
**Problem.** At the climax of F2 in E♭ (Vln I B♭6), the bass (Cb, Bsn 2, harp) runs C (vi7) – D (V6) – A♭ (IV). The leading tone D, in the bass, leaps down a tritone to A♭, and V moves back to IV.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 171 (Ex. 11-13, and Mozart K. 458 in 11-13e): VI may rise to V6, but V6 then goes on to I. PDF 172: roots must fall by 3rds or 5ths, and VI–IV is the good order. PDF 166: VI approaches V from above.
**Fix.** Hold C under the whole of 78. The inner D and B♭ at 78 beat 3 then work as neighbour and passing notes over vi7, and vi moves to IV down a third. The 77 IV→vi stays as an upper-third neighbour of IV (IV–vi–IV).
```
sTwoCb:     | c,2 d,2 | aes,,1 |  ->  | c,1 | aes,,1 |
sTwoBsnTwo: | c2 d2 | aes,1 |     ->  | c1 | aes,1 |
sTwoHarpL:  | c,2 d,2 | aes,,1 |  ->  | c,1 | aes,,1 |
```
This also removes the bass-against-Bsn 1 and bass-against-Hn 1·2 octaves at 78–79 that allpairs_par.py flagged.

### 3. Bars 57–58 and 177–178: the Phrygian iv6–V doubles the bass, giving parallel octaves (applied)
**Problem.** c002 introduced iv6 at 57 and 177. At both places Cl 2 and the violas double the bass note of iv6 and move with it to the root of V. At 57: Cl 2 C♭4→B♭3 and Vla C♭4→B♭3 against bass C♭3→B♭2. At 177: Cl 2 A♭3→G3 and Vla A♭3→G3 against A♭2→G2.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 169, Ex. 11-10: doubling the bass in IV6–V is marked "poor" (octaves). "The usual tone to double is the 3rd above the bass," and the typical line is 4–5 (PDF 169, Ex. 11-11).
**Fix.** Cl 2 takes 4–5, and the violas double the 3rd above the bass.
```
sOneClTwo (57):  ges2 ces'2  ->  ges2 aes2
sOneVla (57):    r4 <ges bes>4 r4 <aes ces'>4  ->  r4 <ges bes>4 r4 <aes es'>4
recSClTwo (177): es2 aes2    ->  es2 f2
recSVla (177):   r4 <es g>4 r4 <f aes>4  ->  r4 <es g>4 r4 <f c'>4
```

### 4. Bars 25–26, 165–166, 62–63, 182–183, 185–186: VI after V in the off-beat chords (applied)
**Problem.** The melody of each deceptive cadence is right: D–C or B–C on top, with the 3rd of VI doubled. The off-beat accompaniment is not. At 26: Hn 4's F, the 7th of V7, leaps up to A♭, and Bsn 1 goes G→A♭ in octaves with the bass's G→A♭. At 166: the same Hn 4 leap, plus Tpt 1 G4→A♭4 against the bass. At 63 the pizzicato violas go B♭→C♭, and at 183 G→A♭, each with the bass. At 186, Cl 1 holds the G of V over A♭, which turns VI into a major-7th chord, and then leaps to C.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 202–203, Ex. 13-12: in V(7)–VI the 3rd of VI is doubled "because the doubled 3rd allows 7 to move to 8 and prevents parallel 5ths or (in minor) an augmented 2nd". The 7th falls to the 5th of VI.

### 5. Bars 107–109: the lament's IV6 and iv6 never sound (applied)
**Problem.** The header in dev.ily says i – V6 – v6 – IV6 – iv6 – V over F–E–E♭–D–D♭–C. In fact Vln II holds A♭ for a bar and a half, then G for a bar. The sounding chords are E–A♭–C, then E♭–A♭–C (a 6/4), then D–G–B♭–C, then D♭–G–B♭, which has an augmented 4th over the bass and no F. So the Phrygian iv6–V at 109–110 never forms.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 168–169: iv6–V in minor needs the iv6 chord (B♭–D♭–F) with its 3rd above the bass (F) present. On this bass the right dissonances are 7–6 and 4–3 suspensions, not 4ths held across two bass notes.
**Fix.** Move Vln II half a bar earlier: A♭–G over F–E (V6), G over E♭ (v6), a double suspension 7–6 (Vln I) and 4–3 (Vln II) over D giving IV6, F over D♭ (iv6), and the 4–3 F–E over C. I checked that this makes no 5ths with the violas. It also removes the Bsn 2 / Vln II 5ths at 108 that allpairs_par.py flagged.

### 6. Bar 228: the canon puts B against the brass's IV (applied)
**Problem.** In 228 the brass chorale sits on IV (F–A–C), with C in Tpt 1, Hn 2 and Tbn 1. In the woodwind canon, Fl 2 and Ob 2 sound B (B5, B4) for a whole bar. Ob 2's B4 sits a semitone under Tpt 1's C5 at f/ff, unprepared, against the bass F.
**Evidence.** A&S PDF 196 (Ex. 13-3): D over IV is a 5–6 derivative, an apparent II6. With A under the canon's D, the bar is IV with an added 6th, which is consonant with the brass. PDF 177: dissonances are prepared or passing, and this B is neither.
**Fix.**
```
arrivalFluteTwo: | c'''1 | b''1 | a''2 bes''2  ->  | c'''1 | a''1 | a''2 bes''2
arrivalOboeTwo:  | c''1 | b'1 | a'2 bes'2      ->  | c''1 | a'1 | a'2 bes'2
```
