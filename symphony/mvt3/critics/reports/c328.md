## Findings

### 1. 42→43: the double basses break the dominant pedal for one beat and re-attack the same B♭1 at pp (applied)

Bars: 39–50. Problem: B♭ is the bass from 39 (V of E♭, half cadence) through 50, into the E♭-minor second theme at 51. At 42 the basses stop on beat 4 with the other strings and the bassoons, and re-enter at 43 on the same B♭1 one dynamic softer, under the violins' harmonics. The drop from p to pp is made by a silence and a new attack instead of under a sounding note. Flutes and Hn 2 hold through 42.4, so this is not a deliberate general pause; it is a seam in the one voice that does not change.

Evidence: Adler PDF 190 (Ex. XV-36): the soft level is reached by a sound that "is already there", and "it is essential that the two chords overlap"; PDF 192 (Ex. XV-40): where colour changes, "make certain that the seams don't show". Ex. XV-37 (PDF 191) passes a held C between instruments with ties, never with a gap.

Fix (pTwo.ily, pTwoCb last bar; trans.ily unchanged, its pp now ends the hairpin):
```
pTwoCb ... | bes,,1 | bes,,1 | bes,,1\p\>~ | }      % was: bes,,2.\p r4
transCb = { bes,,1\pp~\cbaw | ...                    % unchanged; the tie arrives from 42
```
The violins, violas, cellos and bassoons keep their breath at 42.4, so the section change at C is still heard; only the bass sustains, and the harmonics enter over a pedal that was never interrupted. The tie runs 42–50; "change bow at will" is already printed at 43. No pitch changes; key plan with the B♭ pedal into 51 (protected since c003) is kept and made continuous.

### 2. 121→122: Cl 1's D6 and the Vln I solo desks' D6 meet end to end, with no overlap (applied)

Bars: 121–122. Problem: c323's Cl 1 line ends `d'''4) r4 r2` at 122, and the two solo desks start on the same D6 on 122 beat 2. The pitch changes colour exactly at the beat, which is the "with seams" version of Adler's Ex. XV-40, and the clarinet's resolution is cut off when the violins begin.

Evidence: Adler PDF 191, Ex. XV-37 (a held pitch coloured by passing between instruments, overlapping); PDF 192, Ex. XV-40 ("very smooth: overlapping").

Fix (dev.ily, devClOne): `r4 d'''2(\pp cis'''4 | d'''4.) r8 r2` (was `d'''4) r4 r2`). The clarinet holds D6 under the violins' first eighth, at the unison, and stops as they move to C6. No new pitch, no new vertical interval; the bassoon solo and 123–130 are untouched.

### 3. 101 and 105: the sforzandi are all short strokes (not applied)

Strings and Bn 2 (at 105 also Hn 3·4 and Tbn 1) play staccato quarters marked sf; nothing long carries the attack (Adler PDF 189–190, Ex. XV-35 items 1–3). The usual remedy, a long sf > p in another group, does not fit here: on beat 2 the winds enter p on a different chord (V6 of F minor over E), so a held F-minor note would clash with the E in Bn 1 and bass clarinet, and 99–102 is protected ("the two ideas apart"). Kept.

### 4. 209–210: the woodwinds' fp < (not applied)

Adler PDF 190 would give the forte attack to another group and let the winds enter p. This was decided in c216 (fp < on the A♭7, Adler PDF 177–178) and c288 (sfp at 209 not applied), and 205–223's dynamic path is protected since c048. Kept.

### 5. 94→95 and 113→114: break-offs, not overlapped (not re-raised)

Adler's overlap (PDF 190) is the rule for a subito piano on one sound; both places break off and start something new, the case of Ex. XI-21 (PDF 38). c228 and c229 kept them on that ground and I agree.
