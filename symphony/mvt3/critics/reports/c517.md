## Findings

### 1. 212–214: the counter line under theme B is woodwind and horns only (applied)

Bars: 212–214 (codaTwo, Largamente, D♭ major).

Problem: under theme B (Tpt 1, Ob 1, Cl 1, flutes, Vln I, ff then f <), a counter line fills the theme's held notes: A♭–G♭–F–E♭ at 212 and A♭–B♭–C at 214, with B♭–G♭ between them at 213. Bn 1 plays it at A♭3–C4 and Hn 3·4 play it at 212 (an octave higher) and at 214 (at pitch). In these bars the cellos only double the bass (D♭3–D♭4 halves at 212, G♭3 at 213, A♭3 at 214), so the middle line has no string. It is also weak in register: a single bassoon at A♭3 under the ff tutti.

Evidence: Adler PDF 178 (on Ex. XV-26, Meistersinger 158–161): "The color of each theme is made up of a combination of at least one string, one woodwind, and one brass instrument." Adler's account of the second element there is "octave-lower cellos plus Horn I doubled at pitch". PDF 179: for a big sound, doubling "be of a mixed color". PDF 184: give each fragment to instruments that will be heard. The score already follows this rule at 239–242 and 247–254, but not here. The bass needs no cellos at 212–214. It is held by the basses (D♭2–D♭3, G♭2, A♭2), Bn 2, contrabassoon, bass trombone and tuba.

Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-517/work/sections/codaTwo.ily):
```
codaTwoVc = { des2\ff\> des'2 | aes4(\f ges4 f4 es4 | bes2 ges2) | aes4( bes4 c'2) | f1 | f,1 | ...
```
(was `des2\f des'2 | ges1 | aes1 |`). The cellos now double Bn 1 at pitch for 212–214, with the same slurs and the same f. They return to F3 at 215. Theme B, the basses, the dynamics path 205–223, Vln II's divisi at 212–214 and the violas' louré are unchanged.

Checks: par8.py on 210–216 lists only octave doublings of the bass itself, which also appear in v517's own run. Two fewer cello entries are flagged now (213.1 and 215.1), and no new fifths or octaves. analysis.txt still reports 0 outer-voice parallels. The build has 0 warnings and 16 pages, and the MIDI length is unchanged (422 s).
