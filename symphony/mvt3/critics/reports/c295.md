## Findings

### 1. 11-24, 62, 182, 201, 210, 222, 259, 268, 276: crotchet and quaver rests stand half a space off the stave grid (applied)

Problem. On shared staves, LilyPond's rest-collision code moves a voiced rest in half-space steps. Thirteen rests end up halfway between two correct positions:
- Bn 1·2, 11-24. Bn 1 plays off-beat staccato G3s (`r4 g4-. r4 g4-.`) over Bn 2's line. Its rests stand at 2.0, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0 and 4.5 spaces above the centre: five heights for one repeated figure. The half-space ones are at 11.1, 19.1, 24.1 and 24.3. At 300 dpi, bars 22-24 show the rests stepping irregularly above the stave.
- Single rests: Cl 2 62.4 (-5.5) and 182.4 (-6.5); Cl 1 210.3 (4.5), 259 (quaver, 3.5) and 268.2 (3.5); Fl 1 201.1 (quaver, 5.5), 222.3 (quaver, 4.5) and 276.4 (5.5).

Evidence. Gould PDF 49: "From the centre of the stave, rests move an exact number of stave-spaces up or down, so that they remain correctly positioned in relation to the stave-lines". The example beside that sentence shows crotchet and quaver rests stepping by whole spaces, against an "and not" version stepping by half-spaces. PDF 51: a rest pushed aside by the other part "moves further away from the stave". So the correction must move a rest away from the other part, never towards it. c205's table marked this rule "follows" because it had looked only at single staves. The measurements show the shared staves do not follow it.

Fix (applied, score.ly). This is a Staff-level callback that runs after the collision is resolved. If a crotchet or shorter rest stands a half-space off the grid, it moves the remaining half-space away from the centre. Minim and semibreve rests are excluded, because LilyPond already places them on a line or ledger line (PDF 48, 51; Hn 3·4 200 checked).

```lilypond
#(define (gould-rest-grid grob)
  (if (>= (ly:grob-property grob 'duration-log 2) 2)
      (let* ((par (ly:grob-parent grob Y))
             (y (ly:grob-relative-coordinate grob par Y))
             (ss (ly:grob-object grob 'staff-symbol))
             (y0 (if (ly:grob? ss)
                     (let ((c (ly:grob-common-refpoint grob ss Y)))
                       (- (ly:grob-relative-coordinate grob c Y) (ly:grob-relative-coordinate ss c Y)))
                     y))
             (frac (- (* 2 y0) (round (* 2 y0)))))
        (if (and (< (abs frac) 0.01) (odd? (inexact->exact (round (* 2 y0)))))
            (ly:grob-translate-axis! grob (if (> y0 0) 0.5 -0.5) Y)))))
...
  \context { \Staff \override Rest.after-line-breaking = #gould-rest-grid }  % c295
```

Result. I logged the positions again. All 13 rests now stand at whole-space heights: Bn 4.0/4.0/5.0/4.0, Cl -6.0/-7.0/5.0/4.0/4.0, Fl 6.0/5.0/6.0. No other rest moved, and no rest now stands at a half-space. At 200 dpi, 62, 222 and 276 touch nothing, and each rest sits at the level of its own part's notes. The three manual rest offsets from c205 (58, 170, 198) were already on whole spaces and are unchanged. This is the first time the score applies the whole-space rule. Earlier passes used only manual `extra-offset`s for single rests (PDF 50).

### 2. 210, Fl 1·2: Fl 1's beat-3 crotchet rest floats eight spaces above the centre (applied)

Problem. Fl 1 has G♭6 for a half note, then `r4 r4`. Fl 2 holds E♭6 for a dotted half. LilyPond puts Fl 1's beat-3 rest eight spaces above the centre. That is two spaces above Fl 1's own G♭6 and about five above the stave, far from both parts. Fl 2 has no notehead at that beat, so nothing collides.

Evidence. Gould PDF 51: "The space in which a rest centres, or its distance from the stave, should be on the same level as surrounding pitches"; when a note is on ledger lines, the rest goes just beyond the stave at that level. PDF 50: a rest moves out only as far as the other part requires.

Fix (applied, sections/codaOne.ily, score only, as c205 did at 58 and 170):
`ges'''1\fp\< | ges'''2 \tag #'score { \once \override Rest.extra-offset = #'(0 . -2) } r4 r4\! | }`
At 250 dpi the rest now stands level with the G♭6, clear of Fl 2's E♭6 and its dot.
