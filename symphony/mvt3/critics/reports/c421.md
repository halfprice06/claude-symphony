## Findings

**1. Bar 74, the S2 counter-melody (Vc, Vla, Hn 1): G3 sounds on the strong half against the bass's passing seventh. Applied.**

Problem: at 74 the bass goes from B♭ to A♭ on beat 3 (V → V4/2 → I6 at 75). The seventh A♭1 resolves to G at 75. The counter-melody plays `bes4 aes4 g4 f4`, so on 74.3 three instruments (cellos, violas and Hn 1, all on G3) play the seventh's resolution tone against it. The G3 also makes a minor ninth with the harp's A♭4. A&S PDF 352–353 (section 12, Ex. 21-12; section 13, Ex. 21-13b): sounding the tone of resolution before the dissonance resolves "produces an unclear sound and weakens the effect of the resolution". The recapitulated statement already avoids this: at 194 the counter-melody stands on B♭3, a chord tone of V4/2 (`\transpose c aes, { f4( e4 d2) }`).

The fix has two further gains. A&S PDF 363 (section 25): an anticipation falls on a weaker beat than the tone it anticipates. PDF 364 and Ex. 21-35: an anticipation can be tied to its resolution. So the G moves to beat 4 and is tied into 75. The counter-melody then answers, one bar later, the tune's own tied anticipation of F at 73.4→74. This is the rhythmic imitation between two voices that Ex. 21-21 shows. It is also the counter-melody's first syncopation, which "animate[s] and individualize[s]" the line (PDF 346, section 1). The tune, the bass, the harp and the dynamics are unchanged. The counter-melody is not one of the finale's exported themes (F1, F2 and the chorale top line are unchanged).

Fix (sections/sTwo.ily):

**2. Bar 242: Vln I's C6, the seventh of ii7, is left by leap. Tested, not applied.**

The second phrase of the S-head restatement ends `d'''2( c'''2)` over ii7 (D–F–A–C). The consonant D comes on the strong half and the seventh on the weak half, and Vln I then leaps to G♯5 at 243. I tested `d'''2( c'''4 b''4)` in Vln I, Vln II, Fl 1 and Ob 1 (scratch build /tmp/critic-421/test: 0 warnings, 16 pages, no new parallels), which resolves the seventh inside the line. I declined it.
- The seventh does resolve: B is in Vln II, Cl 1 and the violas at 243. That is a transferred resolution, which A&S PDF 360–361 (section 22, Ex. 21-28) accepts in orchestral textures.
- The two-minim ending with its diminuendo rhymes with 238's `c'''2 b''2`. Leaving the seventh open makes the E-major chord at 243 a turn rather than a continuation.
- The bar lies inside the protected S-head texture 235–242.
