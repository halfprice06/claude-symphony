## Findings

**1. Bars 17-18 and 23-24 (pOne, horn 3): mishandled II7. Applied.**
*Problem.* Horn 3 is the only voice that adds the 7th C4 to the supertonic chord, and it handles it badly both times. At 17.4 the chord over D is iiø7 (D-F-A♭-C). The C4 then rises to D4 at 18.2 over G, so the 7th is never resolved. At 23.4 the bass is F, so horn 3's C4 turns iiø6 into iiø6/5. The soprano (Fl, V1) moves D6→B5 into 24.1, which is 2→7.
*Evidence.* A&S p.177: the 7th of II7 must resolve down by step. A&S p.178, example 12-3c, rejects exactly II6/5 under a 2→7 soprano: the 7th has nowhere to go because the soprano takes the leading tone. The book's correction is plain II6. The recapitulation already does the right thing: at 157→158, trombone 1 moves C4→B3.
*Fix.* In `pOneHnThree` (pOne.ily line 25):
- 8th bar (bar 18): `r4 d'4-. r2` → `r4 b4-. r2`. Horn 3 now resolves the 7th C4→B3.
- 13th bar (bar 23): `r4 c'4-. r4 c'4-.` → `r4 c'4-. r4 d'4-.`. 23.4 is now iiø6 with D doubled, and the D is held as a common tone into 24.2.

I checked the MIDI output: 17.4 Hn34 F3/C4 → 18.2 G3/B3, and 23.4 Hn34 F3/D4. The horn rhythm and dynamics are unchanged. Hn 3/4 is part-combined, and its dynamics are unchanged.

**2. Bar 119 (dev, violin 2 pizzicato): root-position diminished ii° without a 7th in G minor. Applied.**
*Problem.* At 119, the bass A (Vc/Cb) carries A-C-E♭: a root-position diminished triad with nothing above it to justify it. The parallel exposition bar, 55, has the full iiø7. The A3–E♭5 tritone over the open bass sounds thin and unprepared before the V at 120.
*Evidence.* A&S p.164-165 says root-position II in minor is avoided because the 5th above the bass is diminished. A&S p.179 says the 7th makes root-position II usable in minor, and that in minor the complete II7 is the normal form.
*Fix.* In `devVlnTwo` (dev.ily), 119: `r4 <c'' es''>4 r4 <a' c''>4` → `r4 <es'' g''>4 r4 <es'' g''>4`. The chord is now A-C-E♭-G (iiø7). G5 is a common tone held from 118.4 (V2 `<bes' g''>`), and it resolves down to F♯5 at 120.2, as A&S p.177 requires. The viola keeps A4/C5, so the chord stays complete. The bassoon solo is untouched.

**3. Bars 148-150 (retr, new Tpt 1·2 strokes): checked, no change.**
The strokes use only V7 chord tones (B-D-F-G). The F5→G5 rise at 150 happens inside an unchanged V7, so it is a chord-tone change, not a resolution. They add no parallels; analysis.txt still reports 0. One weak point: at 150→151 no voice takes the 7th F to E♭ by step. Tbn 1 moves F4→C4, Tbn 2 moves D4→E♭3, and E♭ arrives only in Cl and Tbn 2. A&S p.177 asks for the 7th to fall by step, but 151-154 is i6/4 over the G pedal, so the dominant is still sounding. The F rises through the V's own pedal and the stepwise resolution is deferred, as A&S p.195-197 allows when the tonic chord prolongs the dominant. I leave it. The strokes are sound.

**4. Bar 205 (animato start), judged harmonically: follows, no change.**
After the Vsus at 204 (part of the protected movement-I quotation, with no B), the animato starts on A♭ (VI). A&S p.204 calls the deceptive VI the standard way into a varied continuation, so the tempo change belongs exactly here. The 3rd C of A♭ appears only in Bn 1 (C4), which is thin but correct (A&S p.202 asks for the 3rd doubled after V; with a Vsus and no leading tone, the augmented-2nd risk does not exist). I made no change, and global.ily's `\mt` step for 205 is correct as written.

**5. Bar 38.1 (pTwo): the 7th of ii7 in E♭ enters by leap. Not applied.**
The clarinet leaps to the 7th E♭4. A&S p.177 prefers a prepared or passing 7th. In this texture the strings sustain the chord and the clarinet doubles a line that is already heard, so the effect is mild. Optional fix: tie the clarinet over from the previous E♭. I did not apply it because it changes the clarinet's doubling of F2 material, which this lens should not touch for a minor point.

**6. Bars 16.3 and 17.3 (pOne): root-position ii° on the half-bar, with A♭ doubled across many instruments. Not applied.**
The 7th C arrives only on beat 4 (horn 3), so beat 3 is a bare diminished triad (A&S p.165, p.179). With Finding 1 applied, the 7th now resolves properly. Adding C on beat 3 would require changing violin and flute pitches in the F1 accompaniment, close to the protected F1 head. I leave it.

**7. Bar 78.1 (sTwo): spacing gap E♭4–B♭5 in the IV6 with the B♭6 appoggiatura. Not applied.**
The progression follows A&S p.169-170 (IV6 expanding IV). The gap of more than an octave between the upper voices is a spacing matter, outside this reading. I note it only.
