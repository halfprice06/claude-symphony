## Findings

### 1. 166: the cadence chord of the recapitulated F1 is marked at three levels (applied)

**Problem.** At 159-165 Vln II plays the counter-line f espress., Cl 1 doubles it mf, and Ob 2 and Cl 2 hold the harmony mf (c167). At 166 the counter-line has ended and these players strike the cadence chord with an accent, together with the tune's ff players. Vln II takes the tune's last note, C4, at the violas' pitch. Cl 1 and Ob 2 play chord tones, and Cl 2 resumes the broken-chord eighths that are f at 151. No new dynamic is written there, so the cadence chord is ff in the flutes, oboe 1, Vln I, violas and horns, f in Vln II, and mf in Cl 1·2 and Ob 2. At 167 every part is marked f again.

**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8: proofread the vertical lines of the score. Ex. 6 (PDF 11, Beethoven 9/iv from bar 851): every staff that shares an attack carries the same mark, and the editor adds [sf] in brackets where a woodwind staff lacked it. The level of 166 belongs to the cadence, not to the counter-line that ended on 165. At 151 the tune is ff and the accompaniment (clarinets, Hn 3-4, trombones) is one level lower, f (c164). The clarinets' mf at 166 is left over from the counter-line, not a choice about the cadence. Their 167 f then duplicates the restored level.

**Fix (applied, recP.ily).** Vln II rejoins the tune: `| c'2->\ff r2 | r4 es''8(\f d'' c'' bes')`. Cl 1·2 and Ob 2 return to the accompaniment level of 151, and the duplicate f at 167 goes: Cl 1 `| c'2->\f r2 | c''1 |` (was `c'2-> r2 | c''1\f`), Cl 2 `| g8(\f c' es' c') r2 | es'1 |` (was `g8( … ) r2 | es'1\f`), Ob 2 (`recPOboeTwoDiv`, used by both the score staff and the part) `es'2->\f r2`. Cl 1·2 share a staff, and their dynamics at 166 are equal. c167's f/mf for 159-165 is unchanged.

### 2. 103: the low strings and Bassoon 2 re-enter on F1's head with no dynamic (applied)

**Problem.** At 99 the violas, cellos, basses and Bn 2 play F1's head f. At 101 they play one sf stroke and rest for 1¾ bars. At 103 they enter again on the head in B♭ minor with no dynamic. Hn 3-4, Tbn 1·2 and the bass trombone enter on the same notes at the same moment marked f. The players' last mark is the sf of a single stroke, so the part does not say what level 103 is. In the score the brass staves say f and the string staves below them say nothing.

**Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10): the entry after a rest carries its dynamic (pp after the numbered rest). PDF 8: proofread each part as the player reads it, and the vertical lines of the score. c075 restated dynamics after rests in the parts as a rule for this score, and 103 is the only place in 1-198 where my script found an entry after a rest of more than a bar without a dynamic. The others are timpani strokes inside an unchanged f or ff, and part-only marks the script could not see.

**Fix (applied, dev.ily).** `bes'2->(\f` (Vla), `bes2->(\f` (Vc), `bes,2->(\f` (Cb), `bes2->(\f` (Bn 2; Bn 1 rests in 103-104, so the shared staff has one dynamic) at 103. The sound does not change: the MIDI level was already f. The page image (p. 6) shows f under each staff at 103, clear of the accent and slur.

### 3. 67 and 187: the second violins' part does not say "cantabile" (applied)

**Problem.** At 67 Vln I has "cantabile" and Vln II, playing the same tune an octave lower, has only "arco". At 187 Vln II plays in unison with Vln I and has only "unis. with Vln I". In the score the word is one staff above, but the Vln II part (checked in the scratch parts build, p. 2, bar 67) has no character word for F2. The violas and cellos have "espress." for their counter-melody, the cor anglais and trumpet have "dolce cantabile", and Hn 1 has "dolce". The second violins are the only players on the tune without its character.

**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 4: every expression instruction is part of the material. PDF 8: extracting parts from the score does not give error-free parts, so they must be read as the player reads them (c165 used this for the stray "arco" at 67 and 187).

**Fix (applied).** sTwo.ily, Vln II 67: `^\markup \italic "arco, cantabile"`. recS.ily, Vln II 187: `^\markup \italic \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.3) \column { "cantabile" "unis. with Vln I" }` (two lines, as \cbaw does, so the text stays inside bar 187; checked on p. 10).
