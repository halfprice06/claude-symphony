## Findings

### 1. 218→219: the theme-B echo's G♭ is the enharmonic pivot of the D♭ episode, but no voice carries it (applied)
- Bars: 209–210, 217–221, Hn 2.
- Problem: The D♭ episode is entered from A♭7 (209–210, V7 of D♭) and left through the same sonority respelled as the German sixth of C (219: A♭–C–E♭/D♯–F♯). Hn 2 holds G♭4, the seventh of A♭7, at 209–210, and since v109 ends the theme-B echo on G♭4 at 218. At 219 Hn 2 drops a diminished fifth to C4, and the German sixth's F♯4 appears in Tpt 2 by a leap of a diminished third from A♭4. So the pitch that turns V7 of D♭ into the augmented sixth of C is never held across the change.
- Evidence: A&S PDF 589 and 591–592, Ex. 32-10a: a V7 becomes a German sixth in a modulation down a half step (here D♭ → C), and the example ties the seventh across as its enharmonic (B♭ = A♯). PDF 588: this is a true enharmonic, heard even when not notated. PDF 586–587: a common tone sustained in an exposed voice is what makes a move between distant areas sound integrated. PDF 600–602: the plan should enlarge the work's motives. G♭/F♯→G is one: F2 opens on G♭ (51) and turns to G (67), theme B leans on G♭ (211–218), and the closing chain runs G–F♯–F–E (273–275).
- Fix (codaTwo.ily, Hn 2 only; Hn 1, the echo's notes and every protected item unchanged):
  `codaTwoHnTwo = { R1*4 | c'1\f | des'1 | aes2( des'2 | f'2. ges'4) \breathe | fis'1\subP | g'1 | d'1\< | d'1\mf\< | }`
  (was `… \breathe | c'1~\subP | c'1 | b1\< | d'1\mf\< |`).
  The echo's last note now splits. Hn 1 continues theme B to A♭4 (♭6), Hn 2 holds the G♭ as F♯4 (♯4), and both converge on G4 at 220. At 221 Hn 2 takes D4, so the V7 has its fifth in the brass (c104's point, met without touching Tpt 3's 6/4 resolution). The 6/4's C still falls to B in Tpt 1, Tpt 3, Hn 4, Bn 1 and the violins. Hn 2 moves D4→G4 at 223 as before. The F♯→G line now doubles Tpt 2 at the unison and Tbn 1 an octave lower, with no new parallels (p5.py output identical). E4 at 220 still comes only from D♯ (c060/c090).

### 2. 216–218: the held D♭ of the tune stays ff over the horns' f echo (applied)
- Bars: 216–218, Tpt 1·2, Fl 1·2, Ob 1·2, Cl 1·2.
- Problem: c108 thinned the held F/A♭ chord to f > mp so the echo is heard. But the tune's own held D♭ stays ff in two trumpets and six woodwinds until the sub. p at 219, and the horns answer at f under it. The echo of movement II at its own pitch, and now the G♭ pivot of Finding 1, are covered.
- Evidence: A&S PDF 585: rhythm, texture and dynamics decide how a change of key registers; the pivot has to be audible to work. Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 129 (cited by c108): an echo is softer than its statement, so the statement has to have finished, not still be ff above it.
- Fix: hairpin ff > f over 216, f at 217 in each of these parts, e.g. `codaTwoTptOne … es''2.( f''4 | des''1~\> | des''1~\f | des''1) \breathe | …`.
