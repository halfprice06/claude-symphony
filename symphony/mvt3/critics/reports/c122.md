## Findings

### 1. Bar 272: the F1 peak A–G–F has no harmony; the F stands bare over C (applied)

Problem. Cycle 121 gave 272 F1's consequent peak, `a'''2 g'''4 f'''4`, in octaves (Vln I/II, Fl, Ob, Cl, Tpt 1·2). Under it there is only the Cb C2 pedal, the motto's last C4 on beat 1, and Tpt 3 G4 on beats 1 and 3. On beats 2–4 the MIDI has only A or G or F plus C2. Beat 4 is a bare fourth/eleventh, F5/F6 over C2. Three things follow:

- The frame is the empty kind A&S warn about (PDF 269: 8–5 "might sound empty unless decorated").
- Tpt 3's G sits a ninth under the A, so beat 1 reads as C with an added sixth, not as a chord.
- The source bar 22 has the same peak harmonized as V9 → V4/3 → i. In 272 the peak arrives in the major without the harmony that gave it weight.

Cycle 121 called the F "the seventh", but no dominant sounds, and most of the F's leave by leap or rise into 273. A dominant would make that worse: seven octaves of unresolved sevenths.

Evidence. A&S PDF 368: over a tonic pedal I–IV–V7–I is the usual succession, and the upper parts resolve normally. PDF 267: voice-leading chords carry a repeated pattern. The close plan already has IV6/4 → iv6/4 → I over the pedal (cycle 23). I decided on IV6/4, not V7. As the root of IV, the F may leap to C in the top voice without a resolution obligation. The inner voices then resolve 4→3 and 6→5 into 273. The major IV at 272 also sets up the minor iv6/4 at 275.

Fix (finale.ily, bar 272; 273 unchanged):
