## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 207–208: Vln II's run and the violas' repeated chord stay f under the woodwind/Vln I F1 head (applied)
**Bars:** 207–208 (codaOne.ily).
**Problem.** At 207 the F1 head moves from the horns to Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1 and Vln I, at f. c104 lowered the horns and Tbn 1·2 to mf here so the head would come through. Two parts were left at f:
- Vln II's rising eighth-note run, A♭3–F5, which goes straight through Ob 1 and Cl 1's octave of the head (A♭4–E♭4–D♭4–C4).
- The violas' repeated E♭4/A♭4 chord, struck six times a bar in the same register.

So at 207–208 the two loudest moving parts in the middle register are background, and they share a level with the head's lower octave.
**Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 13–14 says one element is primary and the background must not divert the ear from it. PDF 211 (Ex. 279) says a background that runs through the tune's register in parallel distorts the focus. In Ex. 269 (PDF 205), the lead and the inner parts have separate printed levels. c157 fixed the same conflict at 205–206 by moving notes, and it kept the lower octave from 207 on purpose. My fix keeps that layout and changes only the level.
**Fix (applied).** In Vln II at 207, `aes8(\mf bes c' des') …`. In the violas at 207, `<es' aes'>4\mf r8 …`. Both crescendos from 209 (`aes8(\<`, `<es' ges'>1\<`) now start from mf and end at the ff of 211. The overall path (f at 205, crescendo from 209, ff at 211; protected since c048) does not change. No pitch changes.

### 2. 130: Hn 4 has no dynamic on its F1 head; Hn 3 on the same staff has mf < (applied)
**Bars:** 129–131 (dev.ily).
**Problem.** Hn 3 has `d'4\p\< … | d'1\mf\< | d'1\f`. Hn 4 has `b4\p\< … | d'2->( a4. b8 | c'4-.\f)`. The score prints Hn 3's mf at 130, but in the Hn 4 part one hairpin runs from p at 129 to f at 131. The player reaches the F1 head's accented D4 with no level, while Hn 2 and the violas, who play the same head, start it mf <. The brief requires equal simultaneous dynamics on shared staves.
**Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 214–215, Ex. 281: every voice of a section carries the same accents and hairpins.
**Fix (applied).** Hn 4 at 130: `d'2->(\mf\< a4. b8`. The score looks the same and the part is now correct.
