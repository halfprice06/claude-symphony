## Findings

### 1. 91–94: the ff codetta has no sustained tone; oboes and horns should hold the tonic, Mozart K. 201 style (applied)

**Problem.** The exposition's codetta 91–94 is the first tutti of the movement to bring in the full brass, timpani and both oboes in E♭. All 30 parts play the same detached strokes (`g4-> r8 g8 aes4-> r4`), with eighth and quarter rests between them. Nothing sounds through the rests. This holds through the ct°7 at 92 (c252), the displaced bar 93 (c248) and the cadence on 94. The closing group just before it has a held B♭ horn pedal at 83–86 (c144). The tutti that completes the phrase has no held tone at all.

**Evidence.**
- Adler PDF 238–240: in "forte tutti passages of the Classical and Romantic repertoire" the winds and horns "were used to strengthen the harmony, providing pedals which gave a strong, continuous, and solid quality to the music."
- PDF 240–241, Ex. VIII-8 and VIII-9: Mozart K. 201, first tutti, with "the traditional use of the pedal A in both the oboes and horns", shown against a version without it. Adler on that version: "Notice the lack of stability and grandeur without those sustained tones … how much the pedal added to the power of the cadence (especially the skip of an octave by the second horn which so dramatically emphasizes the dissonance)." The image (pg-241) shows the strings hammering repeated chords and the bass moving to V under the held A in both pairs.
- PDF 243, Ex. VIII-11 (*Figaro* mm. 43–45): "Chords built by flutes, oboes, and clarinets sustain and clarify the harmony … and lend weight to the tutti effect of the cadence," while the horns, trumpets, timpani and strings play the rhythm (pg-244).
- PDF 234: horns "are assigned pedal dominant or tonic notes similar to those given to the oboes." PDF 250–251: winds a2 work better in octaves (8'+4') than in unison.
- Score context: c144 applied the dominant half of this idea at 83–86. The tonic half, in the tutti where Mozart puts it, is missing.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-324/work/sections/clos.ily).** Ob 1·2 hold E♭6/E♭5 and Hn 1·2 hold E♭4/E♭3, ff, tied through 91–94.2. Hn 2 leaps E♭3→E♭4 onto the V on 93.4, as Mozart's second horn does. Everything else is unchanged:
