## Findings (ranked)

### 1. No cues in the long rests before exposed entries (applied)
Bars: Tpt 1 rests 1–50, then its p solo at 51; harp rests 1–66 and 83–234; cor anglais 151–170, then its solo at 171; bass clarinet 7–146; cymbals and bass drum 1–222, then the first crash, fff, at 223.
Problem: a player counting 50, 140 or 222 bars through tempo changes and fermata-like rits has nothing to check against before a solo or the arrival crash. MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest, the cues being transposed to the reading key… Cues must be audible and obvious"; Example 4 shows the "Viol. I" cue notes before the entry. score.ly already reads most players through `\removeWithTag #'part`, but nothing is tagged, and nine staves (Ob 1, Cl 1, B.cl, Bn 2, harp RH, Vln II, Vla, Vc, Cb) do not filter the tag, so a part-only cue there would print in the score.
Fix (scratch): every staff in score.ly reads its player through `\removeWithTag #'part`; after `\include "parts.ily"`: `\addQuote "oboeOne" { \removeWithTag #'part \oboeOne }`, and the same for tptOne, vlnOne, vlnTwo, bsnOne. Cues, e.g. trans.ily: `transTptOne = { R1*4 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring "oboeOne" #UP { R1*2^\markup \tiny "Ob. 1" } } | R1*2 | }`. Others: harp RH 63–66 from Tpt 1 (sOne.ily), C.a. 167–170 from Vln II (recP.ily; Vln II rather than Vln I keeps the transposed cue at B♭5, not B♭6), B.cl 145–146 from Bn 1's F1 head (retr.ily), harp RH 231–234 from Vln I (arrival.ily), percussion word cues "Tpt. 1: B, cresc." 221 and "Fl. 1: scale" 222 (codaTwo.ily; a DrumStaff takes no cue notes). In the scratch parts the cues come out in the reading key (the parts book sets `\transposition`). The score PDF and the MIDI are unchanged by this finding.

### 2. Trumpet 1 is told to put on and take off the mute only at the entry (applied)
Bars: 167–171, 187–199.
Problem: "con sord." stands on the first note of 171 and "senza sord." on the first note of 199. A player reading the part sees the instruction when the note is due. MOLA PDF 5 and Example 5 (PDF 10): a change is written in the rest where it is made ("to piccolo"), and the entry names what is played. Tpt 1 has four bars (about 5.7 s at 2 = 84) at 167–170 and twelve at 187–198, so both changes are possible, but only if announced there.
Fix (recP.ily, recS.ily): `r4 es'4-. r2 | R1*4^\markup \italic "metti sord." | }` and `c'1) | R1*12^\markup \italic "togli sord." | }`. "con sord." at 171 and "senza sord." at 199 stay. The 167 text also prints in the score over Tpt 1·2 (page 9); at 187 the trumpet staff is hidden, so only the part shows it.

### 3. Entries after rests without a dynamic (applied)
Bars and evidence: at 5 the strings drop to f at 3–4 and restate ff at 5 (Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc), but all winds, brass, timpani and Cb re-enter after two bars' rest with no mark; the column at 5 is half marked. At 103 Hn 3·4, Tbn 1·2 and B.tbn re-enter after three bars with no mark; at 105 Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1·2 re-enter after two bars with no mark; at 35 the contrabassoon re-enters after two bars with no mark while the bass trombone it doubles enters f. MOLA Example 3 (PDF 10): every entry after rests carries its dynamic; Example 6 (PDF 11): every staff of a tutti is marked. In a part the player has only his own line.
Fix: intro.ily `| R1*2 | c'''1->\ff |` (and the same in the 19 other wind, brass and Cb lines; `aes,,1->\ff\dnup` in Cb), timp. `aes,4->\ff`; dev.ily 103 `f'4->\f`, `des'4->\f`, `bes4->\f`, `bes,,4->\f`; 105 `r4 f'''8(\mf`, `r4 f''8(\mf` (Ob 1), Cl 2 `a'1\mf`, Cl 1 hidden in the score as at 101 (Finding 4); pTwo.ily Cbn `aes,,1\f`. Shared staves get equal dynamics (both players ff at 5, both f at 103). The levels are the ones already in force, so the MIDI is unchanged. I did not add marks to the single timpani strokes inside the f first theme (15, 18, 23, 31, 34, 158, 163, 254): they punctuate one phrase at one level, and a mark on each stroke would clutter the part.

### 4. Score-only marks inside the player variables (applied)
Bars: Cl 1 at 101 (and now 105) carries its mf as `-\tweak stencil ##f \mf`, so the Cl 1 part has no dynamic at its entry; Cl 1 126 and Bn 1 125 carry a typed "1." that means nothing in a single-player part; the harp pedal diagrams at 67, 123 and 235 are in the harp's variables.
Evidence: MOLA PDF 8 and Example 7: extracted parts lose or float "anchored" markings; PDF 5: "Harp pedaling should be left to the performer and not printed." Cycles 6, 45 and 68 kept the diagrams for the score; this keeps that decision and applies MOLA to the part.
Fix: dev.ily `c''8(-\tag #'score -\tweak stencil ##f \mf -\tag #'part \mf`; `-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1."`; Bn 1 `-\tag #'score ^\markup { \bold "1." \italic "dolce" } -\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "dolce"`; the three `\harp-pedal` markups prefixed `-\tag #'score`. The parts book reads players through `\removeWithTag #'score`. No change to the score.

### 5. The score's breaks and MIDI steps sit in `\global`, which every part reads (applied)
Problem: `\global` contains ten `\break`/`\pageBreak` commands placed for the A3 score and twenty `\mt` steps (`\set Score.tempoWholesPerMinute`). In a part built from it, the breaks force line and page breaks wherever the score turns (MOLA PDF 7: page turns belong in rests), and both the breaks and the `\set` steps interrupt multi-measure rests: in my first parts build Tpt 1's rest 115–134 printed as 20 single bars and the percussion's 222-bar rest as about 70 single bars, against MOLA PDF 5 (rests as numbered ranges) and Example 3–4.
Fix (global.ily): `\tag #'score \pageBreak`, `\tag #'score \break` at each break; `mt = #(define-music-function (bpm) (integer?) #{ \tag #'score { \set Score.tempoWholesPerMinute = #(ly:make-moment bpm 4) } #})`. The score keeps everything (it does not filter 'score), so its pages and MIDI are unchanged; the parts read `\removeWithTag #'score \global`. Now Tpt 1 prints "H 8 | 12", the percussion part fits one page.

### 6. The movement has no parts; add a parts book (applied in scratch)
Problem: MOLA's whole *Parts* section (PDF 5–8) presupposes engraved parts; v75 produces only the score. Building them exposed Findings 1–5, which a score proofread cannot show.
Fix: keep `parts-head.ily` (score.ly up to its `\layout`, without header and paper) and `parts-book.ly` in the version directory. Settings, each from the reading: A4 (PDF 8), `layout-set-staff-size 21` = 7.4 mm (PDF 7), header with instrument name and page number on every page (PDF 7), `skipBars = ##t` (PDF 5), `\transposition` plus `\transpose` for C.a., clarinets, horns, Cbn, Cb so music and cues read in the written key (PDF 5), Key_engraver removed for horns and timpani (PDF 5), `page-breaking = #ly:page-turn-breaking` with `Page_turn_engraver` and `minimumPageTurnLength` two bars for winds, brass, timpani, percussion and harp (PDF 7), the normal breaker for strings (the inside player turns), and `first-page-number = 2` for Bn 1, which has no two-bar rest before 95 (PDF 6, cover page). Build: `lilypond -o parts/ parts-book.ly` in the version directory gives 30 parts with no errors; the only messages are LilyPond's "page 1 has been compressed" (by 4 staff spaces) in the harp part. The composer may wire it into build.sh later; it does not affect the score build.
