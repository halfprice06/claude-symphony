## Findings

### 1. 199-210: the forced 12-bar system crushes the hammered tutti (applied)
- **Problem.** c151 put \noBreak on every bar from 199 to 210, so twelve full-tutti bars share one system. 199-204 (movement I's hammered tutti, the densest bars of the score: dotted heads in four octaves, two voices on every wind staff, accidentals, accents above and below) sit at their minimum widths. At 250 dpi in v155 the Cb eighths take about 20 px each in 200 and 203 but 28-30 px in 205-210. Inside 202 the first eighth takes about 70 px and the next ones about 25 px, so equal values are spaced unequally. Bar 204 is about 10 mm wide: its two accented crotchet chords and half rest get less room than the eighths of 203, and the B♮ chord sits against the barline. In Fl 1·2 at 201 the Fl 2 semibreve and Fl 1's G5 quaver touch.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 53-54: each longer value at least as much space as its shorter neighbour; equal durations spaced equally across a system. PDF 55: no characters closer than ½ space, nothing collides. PDF 57: stems never closer than one space to a barline. Introduction PDF 9: computer layout must be checked by eye, and poor spacing hinders the player. MOLA PDF 5: avoid over-crowding within each measure.
- **Answer to c151's reason (page cap).** Without the noBreaks, LilyPond put 206-210 on a page of their own (17 pages). The fix keeps 16 pages: it moves the page turn from 210|211 to 208|209, so page 11 holds 199-208 (10 bars) and page 12 holds 209-222 (14 bars). 209 starts the third two-bar unit of the F1-head build (205-206, 207-208, 209-210; c111), so the turn falls at a unit boundary. 211-222 are mostly minims and semibreves and take the two extra bars without crowding; the c045 hairpin minimum length at 219-222 still holds. A side gain: the crescendo of 209-210 and the violins' G♭6 suspended 210→211 (c151) are no longer split by a page turn.
- **Fix (global.ily, codaOne line):** in the \noBreak chain, change the break after 208 and remove the page break before 211:
```
\mt 176 s1 \tag #'score \noBreak s1 \tag #'score \pageBreak s1 \tag #'score \noBreak s1
\bar "||" \tag #'score \noBreak \key des \major \mark \default % O 211
```
(The first line replaces `\mt 176 s1 \tag #'score \noBreak s1 \tag #'score \noBreak s1 \tag #'score \noBreak s1`; the second replaces `\bar "||" \tag #'score \pageBreak \key des \major`.) Result: 199-204 take about 540 of 936 px of the system instead of 437, the Cb eighths are even through 199-203, and 204's crotchets and half rest have their proportional room. Score-only tags; parts unchanged. No note changed.

### 2. 130: unequal dynamics on the Hn 3·4 staff (applied)
- **Problem.** c154 gave Hn 4 the F1 head with Hn 2 (`d'2->(\mf\<` at 130), but Hn 3 on the same staff is still in its p< crescendo from 129 with no dynamic at 130. Because each staff prints one set of dynamics, the score shows only Hn 3's long p——f hairpin. Hn 4's mf disappears and the head's entry level is unreadable. The Hn 1·2 staff is correct (Hn 1 `fis'1\mf\<`, Hn 2 `\mf\<`).
- **Evidence.** Gould's introduction, PDF 11 and 13: consistent conventions so the familiar reads quickly. The brief's rule: shared staves need equal simultaneous dynamics. Gould PDF 65-66 (double-stemmed unisons): the D4 crotchet and D4 minim are correctly kept as separate heads, so only the dynamic is wrong.
- **Fix (dev.ily, devHnThree):** `d'4\p\< d'4 d'4 d'4 | d'4\mf\< d'4 d'4 d'4 | d'1\f |` (was `d'4 d'4 d'4 d'4` at 130). Both players now read p< at 129, mf< at 130 and f at 131, like Hn 1·2. The build prints one mf< under the staff at 130. Hn 3's line already reaches about mf there; the sound does not change.

### 3. 235-238: harp 8 bassa where no ledger line needs it (applied)
- **Problem.** The harp LH octave sign starts at 235 over C2, C2, D2, G2: at pitch these need at most two ledger lines. Only 239-242 (A1/A2, F1/F2, D2/D3) need the sign. The sign also runs across the page turn 238|239.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 46: use at least three ledger lines before an octave sign. PDF 47: ledger lines rather than octave signs for isolated notes (these are four held semibreves); change octave between phrases. 235-238 and 239-242 are the two phrases of the S head, and the page break (c023) falls between them. PDF 47 allows harps more octave signs, but not where no ledger line would otherwise be crowded.
- **Fix (sHead.ily, sHeadHarpL):** `sHeadHarpL = { c,1\p-\tag #'score ^\markup \small \harp-pedal #"---|----" | c,1 | d,1 | g,1 | \ottava #-1 \set Staff.ottavation = #"8" <a,, a,>1\f | <f,, f,>1 | <d, d>1 | <d, d>1 \ottava #0 | ...` The sign now begins with the second phrase at the top of page 14 and ends at 242 as before. Sounding pitches unchanged.
