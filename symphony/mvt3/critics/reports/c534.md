## Findings

The earlier passes over this chapter applied its main techniques: harmonic-series voicing of the pedals (43–50), sul tasto and back desks for the movement II recollection (95–98), the muted and distant returns (171, 178, 217), and the brass-density adjustments at 151–164, 164 and 205–208. On this pass I found no place where the reading clearly calls for a change. I tested two candidates in the scratch build and worked out others on paper. None was better, so the score is unchanged. The findings below are ranked by how close each came to an improvement.

### 1. Bar 252: Hn 3 holds A4 under the horns' S-head peak (tested in the build, declined)
- **Problem.** In the second statement of the S head (251–254), Hn 1·2 a2 ff play `c'4 a'2-- g'4` at 252. Hn 3 holds `a'1` f through the bar. The tune's tenuto peak A4 therefore enters on a pitch the same timbre is already holding, and the horns' line is covered from above on 252.1, where Hn 3's A4 is a sixth over the tune's C4. A is also the chord's third and is already in Hn 1·2, Tpt 1, Ob 1, Cl 1·2 and the violas, while the brass have F only in the tuba. In the first statement (248) Hn 3 holds D4, the bass's root, below the tune's A4.
- **Evidence.** PDF 286–287: "sounds in the same frequency range mask each other, thus diluting their contribution"; the fewer instruments in a range, the clearer each one is.
- **Tested fix.** finale.ily `finaleHnThree`: `e'2 g'2 | a'1 | f'1 | f'1` → `e'2 g'2 | f'1 | f'1 | f'1` (the root, as at 248, and a step from 251's G4).
- **Result.** The build is clean, but the all-pairs check (tools/critic-scripts/c002/par.py) reports five new parallel fifths on 252.1: Hn 3 G4→F4 against the S head's D5→C5 in Ob 1, Cl 1·2 and the flutes' D6→C6. This is the reason c139 gave for keeping A4 at 252 (finale.ily comment: "F4 there makes fifths with the D5->C5 top line, C5 makes fifths with the basses"). The reason still holds and every other note from G4 is a chord tone already covered, so A4 stays. Not applied.

### 2. Bars 83–86: Hn 1's held B♭4 inside the closing tune's lower octave (tested in the build, declined)
- **Problem.** Under the closing tune (Fl 1·2 and Vln I at E♭6; Ob 1, Cl 1·2 and Vln II at E♭5, f), Hn 1·2 hold B♭4/B♭3 mf for three and a half bars. The tune's lower octave passes through B♭4 three times and dips below it to A♭4–F4 at 84.3–85.1.
- **Evidence.** PDF 287 (brass overtones mask a woodwind line in their range; give each instrument its own range).
- **Tested fix.** clos.ily `closHnOne = { bes1~\mf | bes1~ | bes1~ | bes2 r2 | …` (Hn 1 with Hn 2 on B♭3, a2).
- **Result.** The build is clean (0 warnings, 16 pages, no new parallels in par8.py over 82–87 or in the all-pairs check). It is not better. Four instruments carry the lower octave at f against one horn at mf, in different timbres, and the upper octave is free. The B♭4/B♭3 octave gives the dominant pedal its ring above the bass. On B♭3 the unison would crowd the octave already held by Hn 2, Bn 1 (B♭3 at 84.3–85) and the violas' low eighths. Not applied.

### 3. Bars 187–190: Ob 1 in unison with both violin sections (checked, follows)
Ob 1, p "dolce", doubles Vln I·II (mf) at pitch for F2's first major phrase and drops out at 191. PDF 277–278 says such a doubling is heard as one note with added upper partials. That is what the passage needs: a reed tint on the violins' first major phrase after the cor anglais and muted trumpet of 171–186, not a second line. An oboe octave above would reach B♭6, which is out of range. No change.

### 4. Woodwind lines against brass in their register (MIDI scan, follows)
For every woodwind note that no other instrument doubles at pitch, I listed brass sounding within a fifth below at the same or higher MIDI level. The only exposed flute case is 89 (Fl 1's run two octaves above the horns' p < off-beats). The other hits are tutti chords, accompaniment figures under a line given elsewhere (the clarinets' broken chords at 11–25 and 151–166, where c264 already set the trombones to mf), or protected bars. This follows PDF 287's rule (no brass in a flute's register while the flute has a line).

### 5. Harmonic density and printed level (checked, follows)
At 243–246 the horns are ff under the trumpets' f on the horn call. This follows PDF 271–272 (Mahler's ppp gives the heard result) and PDF 289 (forte brass are denser and come forward), with two horns balancing one trumpet. At 205–208 the brass drop to mf/mp when the woodwinds take F1. At 247–254 and 265–272 the two heads are separated by register and by brass density (c484, c494, c524 checked the same bars). No change.

### 6. Low-register thirds (scan, considered at 84, declined)
The only thirds under C3 at f are passing quarters of the arpeggiated bass at 84.2 (F2 under Bn 2's held A♭2) and 86.2 (B♭2 over Bn 2's G2, the I6 bass of c143). PDF 274 puts the third no lower than the 5th partial. Doubling the bass quarters in Bn 2 at 84 would remove it, but it breaks Bn 2's slow line E♭3–A♭2–B♭2–D3–G2, which c504 set to avoid a low second at 85. A passing quarter does not justify that, so no change.
