## Findings

### 1. 74 and 194: the tune's held half-cadence note stays at full level while the counter-melody moves (applied)

Bars: 74 (Vln I·II), 75; 194 (Vln I·II, violas), 195.

Problem. F2's first half ends on its half cadence. The violins' F (exposition) or B♭ (A♭ recapitulation) is anticipated on 73.4/193.4 (c261) and held for five beats. Through that hold, the only motion is elsewhere:
- At 74, the violas' and cellos' counter B♭–A♭–F–G (Hn 1 doubling) swells and ties into 75 (c051, c458).
- At 194, the cellos' counter (with Bn 1) moves in quarters into 195.

The tune's held note had no marking at all. It stayed at mf (violas mp) over the counter's whole bar. The phrase then went straight on into the second half at the same level, so nothing marked where the first group ended.

Evidence.
- A&S PDF 54: a phrase is a group whose end coincides with a tonal goal. Here the goal is V at 74 and 194.
- PDF 56 (Ex. 3-19, image): an anticipation is an unaccented dissonance that is then held into its consonance.
- PDF 51: "the beginning of a note is heard as stronger than its continuation".
- PDF 52–53 (Ex. 3-11, image): groups overlap when one ends where the next begins. At 74 the counter's tie into 75 is that overlap, and the tune's group should end under it.
- Sebesky Ex. 281 note C (PDF 215–216), the passage c458 cited for the counter's swell, puts > on the sustained notes so that the moving part is heard. c458 shaped the counter but left the tune's held note unmarked.

Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-548/work):
- sections/sTwo.ily, Vln I `g''2( es''4 f''4~ | f''1\>) | bes''2(\mf c'''16 …`, and Vln II the same an octave lower (`f'1\>) | bes'2(\mf`).
- sections/recS.ily, Vln I and Vln II `\transpose c aes, { d''1\>) } | \transpose c aes, { g''2.(\mf es''4) }`, and violas `\transpose c aes, { d'1\>) } | \transpose c aes, { g'2.(\mp es'4`.

The second half restarts at the level it already had, so the dynamic plan, the 77–78 and 196–198 crescendos, and every pitch and slur stay the same. Hn 1 is left unmarked to avoid c458's part-combiner problem. This does not reverse c458's "194 not matched to 74": that point was about the counter's swell, and I added no swell.

Result: 0 build warnings (build-warnings.txt empty), 16 pages, and analysis.txt identical to v548's (outer-voice parallels 0). par8.py on 72–76 and on 192–196 gives the same output for v548 and the scratch build. Note velocities are unchanged (the held note's attack is still 73), so the change is in the parts and the score. The page images show the hairpins under the held note at 74 and 194 and the restated mf/mp at 75 and 195, with no collisions.
