### 3. Bar 242: Hn 3's held D4 sits on the counter-line's last two notes (applied)
- Problem: the chromatic counter (Cl 1 with the violas, movement I's counter continued) ends its second phrase at 242 by falling to C4 and rising to D4 (`c'2 d'2`). Hn 3 holds D4 through the bar. On beats 1–2 the counter's C4 rubs a major second under the horn in the same octave. On beats 3–4 its D4 merges into the horn's note, so the line's last step does not come through as a step. This is the bar before the horn call.
- Evidence: Adler PDF 274: "try to keep the accompanying forces registrally distant from the soloist," especially with a woodwind soloist. PDF 264: Mozart keeps the orchestral horns silent while the solo line sounds in their register. c527 made the same correction for Hn 3 at 139–140. Hn 3's D4 only doubles the bass D, which is also in the cellos, basses, Bn 2, harp and both violin parts (D6/D5). c247 put the concords in the horns at 239–242, and that holds at 239–241. The new reason applies only to 242, where the counter comes down into the horn's octave. It answers c247 and does not undo it.
- Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-590/work/sections/sHead.ily):
