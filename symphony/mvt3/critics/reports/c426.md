## Findings

### 1. 91-93: the bass trombone's short ff strokes drop to B♭1, the bottom of its chart. They now rise to B♭2 (applied)

**Problem.** In the E♭ codetta (91-94, ff), the bass trombone plays with the tuba in unison `es,4-> r8 es,8 bes,,4->` twice, then `r4 es,2-> bes,,4->`. Each dominant stroke is a quarter note at 2 = 84 (0.36 s) that falls a fifth from E♭2 to B♭1, right after an eighth. B♭1 is the lowest note on Adler's bass-trombone chart, for professionals and nonprofessionals alike (adler2 PDF 306, image). Adler says the very low brass notes speak slowly and are best used as slow bass notes (adler2 PDF 8; adler1 PDF 292). Here the note is a short accented stroke after a slide shift, the same case that c336 corrected in the tuba at 28-29. The 16-foot B♭1 is already in the tuba, contrabassoon and basses, so the bass trombone adds nothing there. Meanwhile the brass dominant has nothing between B♭1 and Tbn 2/Hn 4's F3, a gap of an octave and a fifth. The B♭2 root is only in the cellos and Bn 2.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-426/work/sections/clos.ily).**
```
closBassTbn = { R1*6 | ... | es,4->\ff r8 es,8 bes,4-> r4 | es,4-> r8 es,8 bes,4-> r4 | r4 es,2-> bes,4-> | es,2-> r2 | R1*4 | }
```
The bass trombone keeps its E♭2 with the tuba and rises a fourth to B♭2 on each dominant. This gives the brass chord its 8-foot root under the F3s, with the bass trombone in octaves with the tuba (B♭2 over B♭1). The cellos and Bn 2 already move at that octave. Rhythm, accents and dynamics are unchanged. par8.py over 89-95 prints the same 12 lines as v426 (cello and Bn 2 octaves with the bass, which were already there); nothing is new. The all-pairs check (c002/par.py) finds one new pair: a compound fifth between the bass trombone and the flutes from 92.3 (B♭2/F6) to 93.2 (E♭2/B♭5). Two quarter rests separate the notes and they are the V-I roots of a tutti, so I accept it. The analysis still shows 0 outer-voice parallels. I kept 103.1 (B♭1 f, a single stroke after rests, which is the slow case Adler allows). This change is new and undoes no earlier fix.

### 2. Score header: the bass C extension is also needed at 75-76 (applied)

**Problem.** Since c403 the basses play G1-F1-E♭1 | D1-E♭1 at 75-76, a passing V6/4 under the E♭-major F2. The analyser lists 9 notes below E1, the first at 75. The header still reads "C extension at 166 and 174-183". Adler marks the extension "Not found on every instrument" (adler2 PDF 302). A principal checking the header for these bars will miss the first passage, and players without the extension will take 75-76 up the octave (adler1 PDF 108), which breaks c403's stepwise line.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-426/work/score.ly).** In the subsubtitle: `(double basses: C extension at 75–76, 166 and 174–183)`. Page 1 sets it on one line with no collision. MIDI is unchanged.

### 3. Tpt 1 at 243-250: three C6s in eight bars (checked, no change)

Tpt 1 plays the horn call with C6 at 243 and 245 and then the S head with C6 at 250, with one quarter rest (246.4) between them. C6 is the top of Adler's professional chart and two semitones above the nonprofessional limit (adler2 PDF 305). The passage is still sound: Tpt 1 has rested 235-242 before it and rests 251-254 after it, the C6s are reached by step or from E5 inside slurred lines, the dynamic is f, and Ob 1 and Cl 1 double the S head at pitch. The horn call and the S head are protected (c156, c285, owed item 6). I tested no change here, because each option (swapping Tpt 1 and 2, or a lower octave at 250) either crosses the voices or gives a section player the C6.
