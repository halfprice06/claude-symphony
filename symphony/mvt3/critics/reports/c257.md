## Findings

### 1. 19-22: F1's consequent repeats the antecedent's harmony bar for bar; bar 20 gets a new, stepwise bass (applied)

**Problem.** The consequent (19-26) has the antecedent's tune an octave higher, and its first four bars also keep the antecedent's bass exactly: E♭-C | D-F | G-G-F-D | B-D at 11-14 and again at 19-22. The recapitulation's consequent repeats it a third time at 159-162. Sebesky's rule (PDF 19) is that he tries "to avoid harmonizing a melody the same way twice, whenever possible". His Ex. 3A shows how: the melody is unchanged, the bass falls mostly by step (A-G-F, then B-B♭-A), and the phrase ends on the same chord as Ex. 3. In this score the 18→19 joint cannot change, because 19 must open on i6 to avoid a root-position V-i in C. Bars 21-22 also cannot change, because they are the dominant goal (the "same resting place"). Bar 20 is free: it is not protected (the do-not-touch lists name 11-18, the cadence at 26 and 159-166), and its tune (B♭-A♭-G-F-E♭) falls through the space in which a descending tetrachord fits. A stepwise bass also takes the bass from the tertiary to the secondary focus level for one bar (PDF 14), which helps the texture lens: in the current version the cellos and basses only leap between chord roots (D3-F2) under the consequent's busiest point.

**Fix (applied).** In bar 20 the progression is now v6 (B♭-D-G) - iv6/5 (A♭-C-E♭-F) - iv7 (F-A♭-C-E♭) → V7 at 21. The bass from 19 now reads E♭3-C3-B♭2-A♭2-F2-G2. The tune's E♭ on 20.4 is now the seventh of iv7 and resolves to D at 21; that is the lift Sebesky's substitution rule asks for (PDF 20). pOne.ily, bar 20 only:
- `pOneVc`, `pOneBsnTwo`: `bes,2 aes,4 f,4` (was `d2 f,2`)
- `pOneCb`, `pOneCbsn`: `bes,,2 aes,,4 f,,4` (was `d,2 f,,2`)
- `pOneClOne`: `d'2( es'2)` (was `f'2( aes'2)`). F4 over the new B♭ would have made fifths with the bass (Cl G4 over C3 at 19.4 → F4 over B♭2). D4-E♭4-D4 is a neighbour line in contrary motion to the bass on 20.3, and its E♭ resolves with the tune's seventh.
- `pOneHnFour`: `r4 g4-. r4 f4-.` (was `r4 f4-. r4 f4-.`). G3 is held from 19 as the root of v6, so the off-beat F3 no longer makes fifths with the moving bass.
- Unchanged: the tune in all its parts, the violas `d'2( f'2)`, Hn 3 `r4 d'4-. r4 c'4-.`, Bn 1 `r4 bes4-. r4 aes4-.`, bars 21-26 and the deceptive cadence at 26. Bar 12 (antecedent) and 160 (recapitulation) keep D-F, so the recapitulation's consequent now restates the antecedent, not the exposition's consequent.

Checks: outer voices 20.1 B♭/B♭ approached in contrary motion, sixths on 20.2-20.3, seventh to fifth into 21. par8.py over 17-24 shows only the bass doublings and the 18.1 Cl 2 octave that is already in v257 (Cl 2 D4-G3 against the bass D-G), nothing new. This changes the harmony of F1, the finale's own theme, in one bar of its first consequent. The tune is unchanged and no quotation is affected. finale.md's F1 paragraph should say "20: v6-iv6/5-iv7 over B♭-A♭-F (since v258); 12 and 160 keep D-F".
