### 4. The harp's F♭ pedal at 277 and the rule "every F♭ resolves to E♭" (no score change; reword the record)
Bars: 277 (finaleHarpR/L).
Problem: since c256 the score's pedal diagram (`--v|-^--`) and the part's text "(B♯ = C, F♭ = E)" print an F♭ in the last bars that does not resolve to E♭. The symphony treats F♭→E♭ as a thread (movement II's six resolutions, the finale's one at 194), and BRIEF.md and cyclic-notes ("Conventions") state the rule without exception.
Decision: keep the F♭. On a pedal harp the F string can only be F♭, F♮ or F♯; F♮ rings a fourth and F♯ a tritone against the fff C chord, so F♭ (sounding E) is the only setting that keeps the glissando inside C major (Rimsky-Korsakov ch. I, PDF 46-47: chord glissandi obtained enharmonically). The rule concerns sounding notes in a voice; this is a string tuning, and what sounds is E, the chord's third. Sebesky PDF 11 (instrumentation, the colour saved for its moment) supports the glissando's single use at 277.
Fix: none in the score. finale.md wording as in Finding 1, item 9.
