## Findings

### 1. 276–277: the scherzo's closing motive x reaches the tonic only in the last bars (applied)

**Problem.** The finale quotes the scherzo's timpani motive x three times, always on G and always as an unresolved dominant figure: bar 9 (`g,4 g,8 g,8 g,4`, the scherzo's closing motive taken up in the first dominant preparation, c230), 143–145 (retransition) and 178 (pp near the rim). The scherzo's own last statements (Timp 363, 367, 371) are the closing form B–B–B–E, 5–5–5–1, ending on its tonic. The finale never gives that resolution. At 275–276 it quotes the scherzo's theme A head in C major (Tpt 1·2, Cl 1, Fl 1, Vln I), but in the gap after the quotation (276 beat 4) the timpani only continue the C roll. In the scherzo itself the timpani answer theme A's phrase ends in exactly that place, `r4 b,8 b,8 e,4` after the tune's `e''4 r4 r4` (scherzo bars 20 and 28; 260 and 268 in the reprise). So the last quotation of the scherzo is heard without the instrument and figure that frame theme A in its own movement.

**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 227: Zimmer leaves the drum ensemble out of the first stage of Man of Steel's climax and "rewards" the hero with it in the last stage, after he has committed to humanity; a sound tied to the story's idea is held back and given at the end. PDF 235 and 237–238 (Annihilation): the synthetic sound is kept for "the only time" at the end, "increasing the impact of the reveal". PDF 200: a reference works when the listener has the background; here the motive has been heard three times on G in this movement and at the very end of the scherzo. PDF 201 and 225: denotation comes from the sound's own source; the timpani are motive x's source in both movements. PDF 239: setting changes a figure's meaning; the scherzo's dying ppp figure becomes a tonic arrival inside the fff crescendo.

**Fix (applied, sections/finale.ily, finaleTimp, bars 275–277).**
```
was:  ... c1:32\<~ | c1:32 | c4-> r8 g,8 c4 r4 | ...
now:  ... c1:32\<~ | c2:32 g,4 g,8 g,8 | c4-> r8 g,8 c4 r4 | ...
```
The roll stops at 276 beat 3. The timpani play G–G–G on the G drum and land on C at 277.1. That is motiveXclose (b,4 b,8 b,8 e,4) a major third lower, 5–5–5–1, using the finale's own drums (G2 and C3, so the fifth falls as a rising fourth). Its last note is the first stroke of movement I's pulse at 277 (I 179–180), so the scherzo's figure leads straight into movement I's. The G strokes sit under the quotation's last C on 276.3 and in its rest on 276.4, the place the scherzo's timpani use. The crescendo from 275 to fff at 278 is unchanged. No other part changes; tuning is G, A♭, C, E♭ as before.
