## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 42 and 170: target `p` printed under a rest (applied)
Problem: at 42 Bn 1·2, Hn 3·4, Vla, Vc and Cb end the staged diminuendo with `bes,2. r4\p`; the `p` stands under the beat-4 rest, right beside the `pp` of 43 (page 3 shows "p pp" side by side in the low strings). At 170 Hn 1·2 do the same (`g'2. r4\p`), a beat after every other part reaches p on its note. Evidence: MOLA Example 3 (PDF 10) puts the target under the note the hairpin reaches; Example 7 (PDF 12) is the list of floating marks to avoid. A dynamic on a rest asks for nothing audible.
Fix (pTwo.ily, recP.ily): `bes,2.\p r4` (Bn 2, Vc), `d'2.\p r4` (Bn 1), `f'2.\p r4` (Hn 3), `bes2.\p r4` (Hn 4), `f2.\p r4` (Vla), `bes,,2.\p r4` (Cb); 170 Hn 1 `g'2.\p r4`, Hn 2 `d'2.\p r4`. The staging of cycle 8 is kept (Hn 1·2 p at 41, the rest at 42 beat 1).

### 2. 196–198: only the top line crescendos into the f at 198 (applied)
Problem: Vln I·II `\<` from 196 to f at 198, Fl and Ob p < f at 197–198, but Vla, Vc, Cb stay mf and Hn 1·2, Bn 1·2 stay p to the end of 198; the ff hammered tutti follows at 199. The column at 198 reads f / mf / p with no reason in the music (the bass A♭ is the chord that carries the E♭ to the coda). Evidence: MOLA Example 6 (PDF 11) marks every staff of a tutti; PDF 8 asks for vertical proofreading.
Fix (recS.ily): 197 Vla and Vc `g2(\< c'4 d'4) | c'1\f`, Cb `e,2\< f,2 | aes,,1\f`, Hn 1 `e'2\< f'2 | es'1\f`, Hn 2 `c'2\< a2 | aes1\f`, Bn 1 `e2\< f2 | es2.\f r4`, Bn 2 `c2\< c2 | aes,1\f` (shared staves get equal simultaneous dynamics).

### 3. 130: the violas' F1 head enters without a dynamic (applied)
Problem: the violas hold B3 pp from 127, `\<` at 129, then begin F1's head (`d'2->(`) at 130 with no mark, inside the general crescendo to f at 131. Every other entry of the hand-over (Vc p 123, Vla p 124, Cl 1 p 126, Vln mf 128, Fl mp 129) has its own level; this thematic entry (cycle 13) does not, so it starts at whatever the crescendo from pp has reached. Evidence: MOLA Example 3 (PDF 10): each new entry has its dynamic.
Fix (dev.ily): `b1\< | d'2->(\mf\< a4. b8 | c'4-.\f)`.

### 4. 116–118: the bassoon solo's hairpins have no targets (applied)
Problem: `a4 g4\< … | g'2.\! f'4\> | es'2 d'2\!)`: the swell to the peak G4 and back has no written level, so the player cannot tell whether the peak is mp or f against the pp pizzicato. Evidence: MOLA Example 3 (PDF 10), hairpins end on written dynamics.
Fix (dev.ily): `g'2.\mp f'4\> | es'2 d'2\p) \breathe`. mp puts the peak one level above the solo's p start and the pp accompaniment, and the line comes back to p before the second phrase at 119.

### 5. 229 and 139: redundant repeated dynamics (applied)
Problem: 229 violas `g2\f` while already f since 224; 139 Bn 1·2 and Hn 3·4 `p` one bar after their `sub. p` at 138 (the strings' p at 139 is a real target after `sf >` and stays). Evidence: MOLA PDF 8, proofreading; Example 7, marks that do not belong to an event.
Fix: arrival.ily `g2\cbaw bes2`; retr.ily `f1\subP | f1 |`, `b,1\subP | b,1 |`, `d'1\subP | d'1 |`, `aes1\subP | b1 |`.

No change proposed for the harp pedal diagrams (row 7): MOLA advises against them in parts, but this is a full score and the item belongs to the playability lens (cycle 6).
