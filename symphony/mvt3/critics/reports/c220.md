- **Problem:** A&S PDF 450 says 19th-century composers bring minor's I–tonicized III–V into major. In Ex. 26-9 (Brahms Op. 117/1) III is tonicized for three bars with its own IV–V–I. For lens 10 it is also the only place where the finale names the scherzo's key.
- **Evidence against expanding it:**

  - A&S PDF 456 §16 also supports reading 243 as a contrapuntal chord: the bass D–E–G passes to the dominant, as finale.md describes.
- **Decision:** keep 243 as it is.
