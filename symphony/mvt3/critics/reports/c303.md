## Findings

### 1. 231–234: the plagal close ends in a block release instead of a diminuendo by elimination (applied)

**Bars.** 230–234, the IV–I–iv–I close of the arrival stage, which leads into 235 (Q, Poco più mosso: movement I's second theme in the violins, p).

**Problem.** The brass (Tpt 1·2, Hn 1–4, Tbn 1·2, bass trombone, tuba), the timpani roll and the woodwinds (Fl, Ob, Cl 1·2) hold every chord to the end of 234. Together with the strings, they all play the same f > mf from 233 and all release together on 234 beat 3. Bar 235 then starts again, p, in the strings, clarinet, harp and Hn 3·4. This joint is where the coda passes from its first stage (brass chorale) to its second (strings). It is the one coda seam scored as a full stop of the whole orchestra followed by a quiet restart. Every other planned fade in the movement removes groups in order (39–42, 133–134, 167–170, 264), so this seam also breaks the movement's own practice.

**Evidence.**
- Rimsky PDF 130: "Diminuendo effects are accomplished by the elimination of the instruments in the reverse order (brass, wood-wind, strings)."
- PDF 127 (in the page image only): "the transition from a complex to a simple timbre, by the suppression of one of the groups, belongs essentially to the diminuendo."
- PDF 116: the scoring of a passage depends on whether it complements or contrasts with what comes after.

At 235 the strings take over, so 234 is a complement to what follows, not a contrast. The group that carries the next stage should be the last group sounding. The score already handles 264→265, the corresponding seam after the last chorale, this way: the brass stop on beat 1 and the strings carry on.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-303/work/sections/arrival.ily).** Only bar 234 changes; 230–233 and their hairpins are unchanged. Rhythms and dynamics at 234:
- Brass and timpani: a quarter note, p, on beat 1, so every voice still resolves (Tpt 1 A♭4→G4, Hn 3 A♭3→G3, Tbn 2 F3→E3).
- Woodwinds: a half note, mp.
- Strings: unchanged (dotted half, mf, Vln I·II pickup G4).
