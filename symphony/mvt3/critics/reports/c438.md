## Findings

**1. Bars 119 and 122, Vln I: "2 soli" puts two players in unison on an exposed pp line (applied in the scratch copy).**
- Problem: The upper voice of the divided answers to the bassoon solo is marked "dolce, 2 soli" and "2 soli". That means two violinists play the same G6–A5 | F♯5 and D6–F♯5 | G5 at pp, alone at the top of the texture. Casanelles (PDF 147–148) names exactly this case: two solo strings on one line are heard as "two distinct entities", because their pitch and vibrato do not coincide. His rule is one instrument or at least three.
- Evidence: c209 meant "two solo desks", which is four players, and cited Rimsky PDF 59 ("one or two desks"). The printed "2 soli" says two players. Rimsky's own "2 Soli" (PDF 214) are divided, not a unison, so they do not support a two-player unison.
- Fix:
  - In `dev.ily` `devVlnOne`, `^\markup \italic "dolce, 2 soli"` becomes `"dolce, 4 soli"`.
  - `^\markup \italic "2 soli"` becomes `"4 soli"`.
  - A c438 comment line is added before the c318 comment.
- Result: the line now has two desks, as c209 intended, and it meets the three-or-more rule. A single "solo" was also considered and not chosen. It would drop c209's stated desk count, and one violin a twelfth above a pp section is more exposed than the context needs.
- Notes, dynamics and MIDI are unchanged (score.midi is byte-identical to v438's). The text fits within the bars (checked on page 7).

**2. Bars 107–110: Ob 1 and Cl 1 in unison on the lament line, p < ff (checked, no change).**
Casanelles's two-instrument warning (PDF 147) is about layering like sounds. Oboe and clarinet are different colours, and the unison is a standard blended mix (Rimsky PDF 128–129). The crescendo to ff also brings in the full winds by 110, so the pair is not exposed for long. I kept it.

**3. Repeated-note figures 247–277 (checked, no change).**
The machine-gun principle (PDF 150) is already met. The accents fall on the first of each group, and the hairpins at 259–263 and 266–272 shape the repeats. c238 kept these and I see no new reason to change them.
