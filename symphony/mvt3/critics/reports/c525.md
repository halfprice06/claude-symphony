## Findings (ranked)

### 1. D and E collide in 8 parts; E stands over D's tempo text in 9 more (51–67) — applied
**Problem.** In every part that rests through the trumpet solo, "D Poco meno mosso (𝅗𝅥 = 72)" is longer than the compressed 16-bar rest 51–66. The next letter, E at 67, is then stacked on the text: in Ob 1, Bn 2, Hn 1, Tpt 2, Tpt 3, Tbn 1, Tbn 2 and tuba the E box sits on the tops of "meno mosso" (300 dpi crop of Hn 1: the box's lower edge runs through the x-height of "meno" and "mosso"). In Fl 2, Ob 2, C.A., B. cl., Cbsn, Hn 2–4 and timpani the E is clear of the text but stands above "mosso", so it reads as belonging to bar 51, not 67.
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 5 and Example 4 (PDF 10): each letter and tempo word over its own rest ("16 [D] 36", "Etwas bewegter" inside its rest). MOLA PDF 8 and Example 7 (PDF 12): extracted parts collide anchored marks; proofread them. Gould PDF 55. c435 fixed the same fault at P/Q in two parts by line breaks; this one is in 17 parts, so it is fixed at the source.
**Fix (applied, parts only).** global.ily: `\lt` now also calls a new `\ltRest`, which gives a multi-measure rest that starts at a lettered tempo change a minimum length equal to its letter-and-tempo text plus 10 staff spaces (the letter and the clef/key/time a line-start rest must also clear), and adds spanner rods so the length holds:
```
#(define ((c525-rest-fits-mark tm) grob)
   (let* ((t (grob-interpret-markup grob tm)) (w (interval-length (ly:stencil-extent t X))))
     (max 12 (+ w 10.0))))
#(define (c525-rest-rods grob) (ly:multi-measure-rest::set-spacing-rods grob) (ly:spanner::set-spacing-rods grob))
ltRest = #(define-music-function (tm) (markup?) (if (not (eq? (ly:parser-lookup 'isPartsBook) #t)) (make-music 'SequentialMusic 'elements '())
  #{ \tag #'part { \once \override Staff.MultiMeasureRest.minimum-length = #(c525-rest-fits-mark tm)
                   \once \override Staff.MultiMeasureRest.springs-and-rods = #c525-rest-rods } #}))
```
A rest that starts a line has its length measured from the line start, so in six parts (Ob 1, Bn 2, Cbsn, Tbn 1–2, tuba) the D text still overhung. I tested 12 and 14 spaces: 12 added a page to the bass trombone; 14 added pages to Tpt 2, Tpt 3 and the harp. Both were declined. Instead, `\partNoBreak` (a `\noBreak` only when `isPartsBook` is set, because the score keeps global's 'part tags) stands before D, so 51 never starts a line in a part. Result: no letter touches or stands over another letter's text at 51–67 in any part.

### 2. A (11) is set over "Allegro con fuoco" in parts that rest 1–10 — applied
**Problem.** In Tpt 1–3, the harp, B. cl., C.A., Bn 2, Cbsn, Tbn 1–2 and tuba, the 10-bar opening rest is shorter than "Allegro con fuoco (𝅗𝅥 = 84)", so A at 11 stood above "fuoco" (Tpt 2 page 1), reading as a letter on bar 3 or 4.
**Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 4 (PDF 10); PDF 5 (tempo marks in every part during rests; letters must match the score).
**Fix (applied).** global.ily, bar 1: `\ltRest \mmk "Allegro con fuoco" "84"` before `\tempo "Allegro con fuoco" 2 = 84`. The tempo text now ends inside 1–10, and A stands alone over 11–26.

### 3. Oboe 1 part prints the score's player label "1." at 187 — applied
**Problem.** recS.ily, `recSOboeOne` bar 187: `^\markup \column { \bold "1." \italic "dolce" }` is untagged, so the Oboe 1 part shows "1." above "dolce" (part page 2). It is the only player label left in any of the 30 parts (a text search of all parts finds no other "1.", "2.", "a2" or "Solo").
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 (extracted parts need proofreading). c075 and c205 made every other shared-staff label score-only; Bn 1 at 125 uses the pattern below.
**Fix (applied).** `g''2.(\p-\tag #'score ^\markup \column { \bold "1." \italic "dolce" } -\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "dolce" e''4)`. The score keeps "1." on the shared oboe staff.

### 4. Each part's first page names the instrument twice — applied
**Problem.** The running header (instrument centred, page number outside) is also printed on page 1, above the title block, which already names the instrument.
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 7: "Include instrument names as a header on each subsequent page." c345 judged this point "follows" but did not check page 1.
**Fix (applied).** parts-book.ly: `\unless \on-first-page \fromproperty #'header:instrument` in both oddHeaderMarkup and evenHeaderMarkup. The page number stays. Bassoon 1, which starts on page 2, is also handled because the test is on the first page, not on page 1.

### 5. Bass trombone hairpin at 149 after the relayout — applied
**Problem.** With the wider rests, 149 begins a line in the bass trombone part and LilyPond warned "crescendo too small" for `g,1~\sfp\<` (sfp < ff into 150).
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 and Example 7 (anchored marks moved by extraction); c315 used the same fix at bar 10.
**Fix (applied).** retr.ily, `retrBassTbn`: `\tag #'part { \once \override Staff.Hairpin.minimum-length = #6 \once \override Staff.Hairpin.springs-and-rods = #ly:spanner::set-spacing-rods }` before `g,1~\sfp\<`.

### 6. Harp part kept to two pages — applied
**Problem.** The wider rests moved the harp's last system (255–279) onto a third page.
**Fix (applied).** parts-book.ly: the harp book takes `\paper { system-system-spacing.padding = #0.5 }`, as Fl 2, Tpt 1, Tpt 3 and timpani do. It now has two pages again, and the turn is still in the rest after 122.
