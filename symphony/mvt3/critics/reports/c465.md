## Findings

### 1. Closely spaced groups slope by a full space (applied)
**Bars:** 211 groups of three or four notes. Examples: Vln I·II 17, 28–38, 157; harp RH 67–82 and 235–242; development 101–112, 119–122; Cl 1 and Vln 257–264; codaOne 205–210; intro 3–4 and 9–10.
**Problem:** These groups' stems stand 1.5–2.6 spaces apart, yet their beams slope 0.82–1.35 spaces. In the harp figuration at 67–82, groups at 0.65 alternate with groups at 1.0 inside one ostinato (bar 68 against 67).
**Evidence:** Gould PDF 34: "Notes spaced very close together horizontally (closer than three spaces) take only a slight angle (¼ or ½ space) regardless of the interval." Her "notation compressed" example shows a wide group with a ½-space beam. PDF 34: both ends attached to a line. PDF 35: "The stem that is going to be the shorter determines the starting point of the beam ... the other stem(s) must become longer". PDF 35: "matching angles look better."
**Fix (score.ly, applied):** a new callback `gould-close-slope` runs c375's `gould-max-slope` first and then applies the close-spacing rule. It acts on a group of two to four notes when all stems point one way, the beam is not broken or kneed, the stems average under 3 spaces apart and the slope is over 0.7. The end with the shorter stem stays where it is. The other end moves to the line-attached centre (n or n ± q, where q = (beam-thickness − line-thickness)/2) nearest a ½-space slope, at most 0.66. When the outer interval is a second, it moves to the nearest ¼ position instead. The moved end is accepted only if its stem gets longer. In the layout block, `\override Beam.positions = #gould-max-slope` becomes `#gould-close-slope`. Manual `Beam.positions` overrides do not call the callback, and c285's damping is unchanged.
Results: 194 groups changed, to 0.65 (123), 0.35 (27), 0.18 (41) and 0.5 (19). The 0.5 groups lie outside the stave, where no line attachment applies. No changed group has a stem shorter than before. One group at 222 is still steep; it is a manual override, see Finding 2.
**Exception:** in the harp LH arpeggio at 123–130 (up-stems, beam between the two harp staves), the longer stems overfilled page 7 by 0.1 space and triggered "compressing over-full page". That passage keeps c375's limit. To do this, dev.ily sets `\override Staff.Beam.details.gould-close-off = ##t` at 123 and reverts it at 131, and the callback checks the flag. With that, the build has 0 warnings and every page has the same bars and systems.
The sound is unchanged. parts-book.ly does not have the c285, c375 or c465 callbacks; the parts were not rebuilt.

### 2. Bar 222 Fl 1: the ledger-line glide slopes 1.18 against Vln I's 0.5 (applied)
**Bars:** 222 beat 4, Fl 1 G6–A♭6–B6 (shared staff, stems up). Vln I plays the same notes with a ½-space beam.
**Problem:** c195 added `\offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25)` here to give Gould's ½ slope. The offset now adds to the Staff callbacks' result (0.68), so the printed slope is 1.18 spaces, steeper than any other ledger-line group in the score, directly above Vln I's 0.5.
**Evidence:** Gould PDF 35, "notes on ledger lines": all wider intervals "take a slope of ½ stave-space". PDF 35, "beams outside the stave": "matching angles look better". PDF 35: the shortest stem outside the stave is 2½ spaces.
**Fix (codaTwo.ily, applied):** `r8 \tag #'score { \once \override Beam.positions = #'(9.25 . 9.75) } \tag #'part { \once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0.25) Beam } g'''8( aes''' b''')`. The stems are now 3.25, 3 and 2.75 spaces, and the slope is 0.5, matching Vln I. The part keeps c195's offset because it is engraved without the callbacks. This restores c195's accepted intent; it is not a reversal. Bars 216–223 are protected for their music only, and no note or mark changes.
