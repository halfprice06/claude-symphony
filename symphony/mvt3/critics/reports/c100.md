## Findings

### 1. 222→223: the stop chord's A♭5 does not resolve (applied)
- Bars: 222–223, Violins II upper desk.
- Problem: at 222 Vln II plays D5–A♭5, which is movement I's own stop-chord layout at its bar 138 (MIDI of mvt1 v221: Vln II D5/A♭5, Tpt 1 B4, timpani G2). In movement I that A♭5 is never resolved. At the arrival the finale resolves the other tones of that layout at pitch: Tpt 1 B4→C5 (movement I's last trumpet B4, finale.md item 3), violas F4→E4 (c081), Vln II D5→E5. The Vln II A♭5, the highest sustained note of 222 after the half-bar, leaps up to C6 instead.
- Evidence: A&S PDF 388, ♭6 "is strongly active in the direction of 5" and moves to it; PDF 503, the augmented-sixth family resolves by half step and gets its force from that. The resolution at pitch is the cyclic point of the arrival (owed item 2: "the trumpets' B→C resolves the B–D–F–A♭ stop chord"). G5 is otherwise only in Ob 2, which arrives by leap from D5.
- Fix (protected "arrival chord 223" item, re-raised because the change serves its stated reason, the stop chord's resolution at the arrival; one pitch in one divisi):
  `arrivalVlnTwo = { <e'' g''>1:32\fff | ...` (was `<e'' c'''>1:32\fff`).
  C6 still sounds in Ob 1 and Cl 2, and C7 in Fl 1 and Vln I, so the chord's spacing and top are unchanged. Applied in /tmp/critic-100/work/sections/arrival.ily with a comment.

### 2. coherence/finale.md and the brief are out of date after cycles 92–99 (text only)
- finale.md says "brass f on V7 of F" at 110–113. Since v99 (c098) the brass are ff there, and since v100 (c099) the bass trombone strikes and releases at 110 and 112. The other threads use this line for the rule "no full-brass C major before 223", so it should state why the ff brass at 110 is not a C-major triad (Tpt 3 on B♭).
- finale.md says "Cymbals and bass drum only at 223, 263, 278". The MIDI has the bass drum at 223 and 278 only; 263 is cymbal alone (c088 declined the drum there). The contrabassoon's sounding C1 at 263 and 278 (c099) is not recorded.
- The header still says "for v91 … scherzo v68" and the quotation heading says "Scherzo (v59)". The scherzo is at v78; its record is 41/41 against v78 and the quoted themes are unchanged.
- Key plan: "E major at 243 … closes the C – A♭ – E cycle". E major lasts half a bar under the horn call's E and goes straight to the cadential 6/4. A&S PDF 593–594 describes III♯ as a key area that needs elaborate preparation; this is a chord. It recalls the scherzo's key, and the cycle is carried by the movements' keys. The text should say so, so the scherzo thread does not count on an E-major area in the finale.
