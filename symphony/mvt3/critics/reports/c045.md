## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Tempo marks and rehearsal letters missing above the strings at 51 and 171 (applied)
**Bars:** 51–65 (D, "Poco meno mosso"), 171–185 (L, "Poco meno mosso").
**Problem:** `Mark_engraver` and `Metronome_mark_engraver` were consisted in the Violins I staff. When Vln I rests for a whole system, `RemoveEmptyStaves` hides the staff, and the letter and the tempo mark disappear from the string group. In those two systems the second violins, violas and basses start a new tempo with no mark over them. MOLA PDF 4: "All tempo indications should appear above the top staff and above the first violin line (or similarly positioned staff in the absence of strings) on each score page."
**Fix (score.ly):** move both engravers from the Vln I staff to the string StaffGroup, so they print over its top live staff (Vln I normally, Vln II at 51 and 171):
```
\new StaffGroup \with { \consists "Mark_engraver" \consists "Metronome_mark_engraver" } <<
  \new Staff = "Violins I" \with { instrumentName = "Violins I" ... }
```
Checked on pages 3 and 10: D and L, with their tempo marks, now stand over Vln II. On every other page they stay over Vln I.

### 2. 219–222 are over-crowded: the crescendo hairpins are about 1 mm long (applied)
**Bars:** 219–222 (sub. p, then `\<` to mp, `\<` to f, `\<` into the fff at 223), all wind, brass, timpani and string staves.
**Problem:** in v45 the four bars take about 2.4 cm at the end of page 12. The hairpin from mp in 221 is drawn about 1 mm long, the same size as a "<" glyph, on 25 staves. This is the crescendo that leads to the arrival. MOLA PDF 7: "avoid over-crowding to provide clarity within each measure"; Example 7 (PDF 12) shows dynamics squeezed against their neighbours as the error to avoid.
**Fix (global.ily, around 219–222):**
```
\bar "||" \key c \major
\override Staff.Hairpin.minimum-length = #13 \override Staff.Hairpin.springs-and-rods = #ly:spanner::set-spacing-rods
s1*2 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "allargando" \mt 124 s1 \mt 112 s1
\revert Staff.Hairpin.minimum-length \revert Staff.Hairpin.springs-and-rods
```
A Score-level override and a value of 7 had no visible effect, because the mp text takes up most of a short rod. With 13, the bars 219–222 fill half of the system and each hairpin is 1.5–2 cm long. Page 12 now starts at 209, and the total is still 16 pages.

### 3. No rehearsal letter for the last 25 bars (applied)
**Bars:** 255 (the chorale's last statement), 273 (Largamente).
**Problem:** letter Q at 247 is the last letter. The last chorale statement and the tempo change at 273 are the two landmarks of the close, and a conductor rehearsing the final page has only bar numbers to call. MOLA PDF 4: rehearsal letters should be used "in addition to measure numbers, their position corresponding to landmarks in the music." Earlier letters come every 8–20 bars and on every tempo change (D, F, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q).
**Fix (global.ily, finale section):**
```
\mark \default s1*10 s1*6 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "allargando" \mt 184 s1 \mt 164 s1 \mark \default \tempo "Largamente" 2 = 72 s1*6 s1
```
This gives R at 255 and S at 273. Earlier letters are unchanged. At 255, R sits clear of the "change bow at will" text (checked at 200 dpi).

### 4. Timpani rolls not tied across barlines, so they read as re-attacks (applied)
**Bars:** 151–152 (new in v45), 147–149, 220–222, 231–234, 235–238, 243–246.
**Problem:** consecutive bars of roll on one pitch are written `g,1:32 | g,1:32`, with no tie. Read literally, the player restarts the roll with a new stroke at every barline, which breaks the crescendos (220–222, 243–246) and the diminuendo (233–234) into bar-length pieces. Cycle 44 meant the G roll at 151–152 to be continuous ("so the new G pedal is heard as a pedal"). Adler part 2, PDF 80, Ex. XII-48: a longer roll is written as tied tremolo notes. MOLA PDF 5 asks for a separate, clearly prepared timpani part, and the part copies these notes directly.
**Fix:**
- recP: `g,1:32->\f~ | g,2.:32 r4`
- retr: `g,1:32\ff~ | g,1:32~ | g,1:32~ | g,2.:32 r4`
- codaTwo: `aes,1:32\subP | g,1:32\<~ | g,1:32\mp\<~ | g,1:32\f\< |`
- arrival: `c1:32\f~ | c1:32~ | c1:32\>~ | c2.:32\mf r4`
- sHead: `c1:32\pp~ | c1:32~ | c1:32~ | c1:32 | R1*4 | r2 g,2:32\p\<~ | g,1:32~ | g,1:32~ | g,1:32\f`

The accented re-attacks at 150→151 and 273, 274 are deliberate and stay untied. The MIDI is unchanged in effect.
