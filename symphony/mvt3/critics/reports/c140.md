### 2. The E♭/E♮ thread entry did not say where the E♭ is (applied in /tmp/critic-140/finale.md)
Bars: 1, 215–220.
Problem: finale.md says "E♭ → D♯ → E at 219–220" in the violas. The source has no E♭ in 216–218. The violas hold A♭4–C5 and then A♭4–D♭5, and since v137 Hn 3 rests 215–218. The E♭ the thread means is the violas' own E♭4 in bar 1 (`introVla <es' fis'>`). A reader in another thread could otherwise "fix" 218 by adding an E♭.

### 3. The cor anglais with Hn 1·2 at 217–218: kept as a mixed colour
Bars: 217–220.
Problem checked: Adler 2 PDF 188–189 lists the cor anglais's affinity doublings (oboe, bassoon, muted trumpet, viola, cello). The horn is not among them, and Adler says an at-pitch doubling changes the basic tone quality. Two horns at f on A♭3–G♭4 will carry more weight than the cor anglais.
Decision: no change. c130 wanted the theme's own instrument to be part of the answer, not to replace the horn echo. c108/c110 need the horn echo for Hn 2's G♭4 = F♯4 pivot at 219. The mixture is what Adler calls a change of basic quality, a new colour, which is the point. The cor anglais is alone on its own line at 219–220 (A♭4, G4), where the horns drop to sub. p. Taking the horns down to mf would put a horn echo under Tpt 1·2's held f D♭5/A♭4, which is the balance c110 removed.

### 4. Open item from c139, Tpt 3 A4 at 252: closed, kept
Bars: 251–253.
Problem: at 252, Tpt 3 (`a'4 r8 a'8 a'4 r8 a'8`) and Hn 3 (`a'1`) sound the horns' and violas' tenuto A4 of the S head at pitch. This is the at-pitch colouring Adler 2 PDF 189 warns about, for one bar.
Evidence that both alternatives fail: Tpt 3 F4 at 252 against Ob 1/Cl 1's D5 (251, last eighth) → C5 (252.1) gives G4–D5 → F4–C5, parallel fifths with the upper S head. Tpt 3 C5 at 252 against the bass C2 (251, second half) → F2 (252.1) gives G4/C2 → C5/F2, parallel fifths with the bass. Fifths in outer or prominent voices are worse than a one-bar unison (A&S Unit 5, which c002 applied to this passage).
Decision: keep A4. finale.md item 8 now records it and the reason.
