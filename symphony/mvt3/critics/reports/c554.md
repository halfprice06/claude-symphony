## Findings

### 1. Bar 93: the new ii6/5 puts its E♭–F second inside the trumpets and inside the horns (applied)

**Problem.** c552 turned the displaced chord on 93.2–3 into ii6/5 (A♭–C–E♭–F). To do this it moved Tpt 2 from G4 to F4 and Hn 4 from G3 to F3. As a result, the chord's one dissonance, E♭ against F, now sounds as a major second in two places, both ff and held for a half note:
- in the trumpets: Tpt 2 F4 over Tpt 3 E♭4;
- in the horns: Hn 4 F3 over Hn 2's held E♭3.

**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov, PDF 102–103: "Discords of the seventh or second are preferably entrusted to instruments of different tone colour". His diagram on PDF 103 keeps each family's chord consonant and splits the dissonant pair between trombones and horns. PDF 114 says balance matters most in held and rhythmic chords, and this is the codetta's held cadence chord. In v553 each family's own chord at 93 was a consonant triad. The seconds came only from c552's new chord tones.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-554/work/sections/clos.ily).**
- Tpt 3: `| r4 es'2-> d'4-> |` becomes `| r4 c'2-> d'4-> |`. Trumpets now play C5–F4–C4.
- Hn 4: `| r4 f2-> f4-> |` becomes `| r4 aes2-> f4-> |`. Horns now play E♭4–C4–A♭3–E♭3.

F stays in Tpt 2, Tbn 2 and Cl 2; E♭ stays in the horns, Bn 1, the flutes, the oboes and Vln I. The dissonance is therefore heard between trumpet and horn, as Rimsky-Korsakov lays it out.

What does not change: c552's ii6/5, its bass A♭, Tbn 1's prepared A♭ into V7, the top line E♭6–D6–E♭6, the rhythm and the dynamics.

Voice leading:
- Tpt 3: D4–C4–D4–E♭4, thirds over the bass B♭–A♭–B♭.
- Hn 4: F3–A♭3–F3–G3, in contrary motion to the bass at both joins.

A side effect: allpairs_par.py loses its only entries in 92–94, Tpt 3's octaves with Fl 1·2 and the violins on E♭→D at 93.2→93.4. It reports no new pairs anywhere.

**Build.** 0 warnings (build-warnings.txt empty). 16 pages; page 5 holds 87–101 as before. analysis.txt is identical to v554's apart from the density lines: outer-voice parallels 0. par8.py on 90–96 gives the same output as v554 (only the bass's own octave doublings).

### 2. Bars 152 and 156: Tbn 1 C4 over Tbn 2 D3 (checked, no change)
The trombones hold a minor seventh at mf over G. It is Tbn 1's prepared 4–3 suspension (C4 to B3 at 153), and the reading's rule says "preferably". Moving Tbn 2 off D3 would take the fifth of V out of the brass, and moving the C to another instrument would break the suspension's preparation in one voice. The chord is mf and inner. No change.
