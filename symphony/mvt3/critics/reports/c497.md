## Findings

### 1. 207–208: brass punctuation of the woodwind head (Hindemith's * device, PDF 42, 44). Tested, not applied
**Candidate.** At 207–208 the second statement of F1's head (B♭ minor) is in Fl 1, Cl 1, Bn 1 and Vln I, f. Hn 1–4 hold B♭4/E♭4/G♭4/A♭3 mf, and Tbn 1·2 hold D♭4/E♭3 mp. These are the brass that played the head at 205–206. Adler calls brass punctuating a theme presented by another group "a most common use of the brasses" (PDF 42). It would also take the held B♭4, G♭4, E♭4 and D♭4 out of the clarinet's octave of the head. The test was in `sections/codaOne.ily`:

```
codaOneHnOne:   r4 bes'4-.\mf r4 bes'4-. | r4 bes'4-. r4 bes'4-. |   (was bes'1\mf | bes'1)
codaOneHnTwo:   r4 es'4-.\mf r4 es'4-.   | r4 es'4-.  r4 es'4-.  |
codaOneHnThree: r4 ges'4-.\mf r4 ges'4-. | r4 ges'4-. r4 ges'4-. |
codaOneHnFour:  r4 aes4-.\mf r4 aes4-.   | r4 aes4-.  r4 aes4-.  |
codaOneTbnOne:  r4 des'4-.\mp r4 des'4-. | r4 des'4-. r4 des'4-. |   (D♭4 still resolves to C4 at 209)
codaOneTbnTwo:  r4 es4-.\mp r4 es4-.     | r4 es4-.   r4 es4-.   |
```

The build was clean: 0 warnings, 16 pages, and par8 at 205–210 matched v497.

**Why I declined it.**
- In Hindemith the brass strike in the gaps of the timpani's theme. F1's head at 207–208 has no gaps: a half note, a dotted quarter, then running quarters and eighths. The strokes on beats 2 and 4 fall under the held B♭ and F. That makes them accompaniment, not punctuation.
- The violas already hammer E♭4/A♭4 on 1, 2.5, 3 and 4.5 (c296, four changes in five cycles). Brass on 2 and 4 fills in the pulse, so 207–208 takes on the hammered texture of movement I's layout at 199–204, which the passage is meant to leave behind.
- The held mp chord is where the A♭7 build of 209–210 grows from (c178, c179). With strokes, the 209 crescendo starts from a fresh attack instead of from a chord already sounding.
- The masking it would cure is small: the head's top octave (Fl 1, Vln I B♭5) is clear of every held note.

Reverted. The protection on 205–210 stands.

**Why I declined it.**
