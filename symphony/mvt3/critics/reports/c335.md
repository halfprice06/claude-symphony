## Findings

### 1. Harp 8 bassa runs on through the rest at 82 (regression from c296). Applied.
**Bar:** 82, harp left hand.
**Problem:** c296 changed the harp's last note from `es,1` to `es,2 r2` so it would agree with "étouffez", but left `\ottava #0` after the rest. The dotted line now runs under the minim rest to the barline at 83. c295 had checked this corner when the note was still a semibreve, which is why it was not caught.
**Evidence:** Gould PDF 44: "Terminate the octave transposition with a corner … Place this immediately after the last notehead(s) … avoid terminating the line at the end of the duration". Her "incorrect" example shows exactly this. At 220 dpi the E♭ head is at x 350 and the corner at x 472, past the rest.
**Fix (sTwo.ily, `sTwoHarpL`):** `es,2 r2 \ottava #0 | }` becomes `<< es,2 { s8 \ottava #0 } >> r2 | }`. The corner now falls straight after the E♭ head (checked at 220 dpi). A spacer is used because LilyPond ends the bracket at the next column. The note itself, its MIDI and the part do not change.

### 2. Fl 1·2 at 229 beat 3: the flat of Fl 2's B♭5 reads as C♭6, and the heads touch. Applied.
**Bar:** 229.3, the c331 canon. Fl 1 plays C6 (crotchet) and Fl 2 holds B♭5 (minim) on the shared staff.
**Problem:** The parts are a tone apart and have different durations, so they are double-stemmed. LilyPond moves the lower minim to the right, as Gould says, but it prints the flat at the left of the whole column, in front of Fl 1's black C6. The score reads C♭6 over B♮5. The minim's head also overlaps the crotchet's head. Ob 1·2 and Cl 1·2 play the same rhythm a staff lower without the problem, because their second parts are far below.
**Evidence:** Gould PDF 63-64: "Place each notehead after its own accidental"; "an accidental is always best placed beside the notehead to which it applies"; if both accidentals go before both notes, "both notes must have accidentals, or the two pitches will appear to be the same". PDF 66: "Keep noteheads separate". PDF 66 also allows editions to "reverse the order of noteheads", as c305 did at 32, 65, 185, 214 and 251. In the scan, this is the only second between voices in the whole score that carries an accidental.
**Fix (arrival.ily, score only):** Fl 1 `d'''2 \tag #'score { \once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.2 } c'''4 des'''4 |`; Fl 2 `a''2 \tag #'score { \once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0 } bes''2 |`. The B♭5 minim now stands on the left with its flat directly in front of it, and the C6 sits to its right with a small gap. This matches the single-stem order for a second (PDF 61: lower note left). I tested 1.0 and 1.2 in a small file and chose 1.2. No pitch changes, no change to the canon (record line choraleCanon unchanged) and no change to the parts.
