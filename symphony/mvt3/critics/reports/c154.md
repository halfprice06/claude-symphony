### 1. 231-233: the violas' new portato quarters cannot be heard; give the pulse to Vln II as well (applied)
Problem. Cycle 153 restarted the violas' portato at 231-233 so the whole-note plagal close at 2 = 56 keeps a pulse until the harp's eighths at 235. All eleven brass are f, so are the woodwinds, and the timpani roll f on C3.
Evidence. Adler PDF 14: the brass "can easily overshadow the rest of the orchestra". PDF 15: above mf a single horn has half the weight of a trumpet or trombone, so the A3-C4 octave already holds Hn 2, Hn 3, Hn 4, Tbn 1 and Tbn 2 at f.
