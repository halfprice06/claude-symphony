## Findings (ranked)

### 1. K (strings), N and O stand beside their tempo text, not above it (applied)
Bars 151, 199, 211. MOLA PDF 4 asks that rehearsal letters be placed consistently at landmarks. In Example 6 (PDF 11) the tempo word sits in the same place over the top staff and over the violins. In this score D, F, P, Q, R and T put the box above the tempo text, as does K over the winds. At 151 the strings' K stood at the height of "Tempo I", to its left. At 199 N stood under and to the left of "Tempo I" in both groups, down at the notes' grace-note height. At 211 O stood at the barline, level with "Largamente". The cause is the same one c185 fixed at R: the box at the barline and a tempo text starting a little to its right do not overlap horizontally, so LilyPond does not stack them. A global override (tested) moved the whole layout to 17 pages, so the fix is per letter in global.ily:
```
\bar "||" \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #2 \mark \default % K 151
\bar "||" \tag #'score \pageBreak \key c \minor \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #2 \mark \default % N 199
\bar "||" \tag #'score \noBreak \key des \major \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #9 \mark \default % O 211
```
O needs 9 because its box sits left of the key change, further from the text. Checked at 200 dpi: all three now stand above their tempo text. 16 pages.

### 2. 243-246 violins: "poco a poco stringendo" runs into "unis.", and "unis." sits on the slur (applied)
MOLA PDF 8 and Example 7 (PDF 12) show this kind of collision. On one line the tempo text over Vln I ran from 243 to 245, and its last letters touched Vln I's "unis." at 245. Both violins' "unis." were also crossed by the slur of the run. Fix: put the tempo text on two lines, as c185 did for Q (global.ily):
```
\tempo \markup \medium \italic \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.4) \column { "poco a poco" "stringendo" } \mt 140
```
In sHead.ily, Vln I and Vln II get `r8 \once \override TextScript.avoid-slur = #'outside g''8(^\markup \italic "unis." ...` (g'8 for Vln II). Checked: the text ends over 244, and "unis." stands clear above the slur.

### 3. 197-198 oboe staff: Oboe 2's rests are printed on Oboe 1's hairpins (applied)
MOLA PDF 8 / Example 7 and Gould PDF 50. On the shared staff the part-combiner shows Ob 2's new whole-bar rests at 197-198 (they begin with Ob 1's re-entry). They are drawn below the staff, on the "p <" and "f <" hairpins. In recS.ily the score-tagged rests become spacers, so the combiner sets Ob 1 alone, as at 187-194:
```
recSOboeTwo = { R1*26 | \tag #'score { s1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring "oboeOne" #UP { R1*2^\markup \tiny "Ob. 1" } } | }
```
The Oboe 2 part keeps its rests and its Ob 1 cue. MIDI unchanged.

### 4. Harp part: first entry at 43 (p harmonics) after 42 bars with no cue (applied, part only)

