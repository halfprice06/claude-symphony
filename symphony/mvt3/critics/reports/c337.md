## Findings

### 1. 191–194: the oboe's doubling at pitch gives way to an answer in the bar where the tune holds (applied)

**Problem.** In F2's recapitulation in A♭, Ob 1 doubles the tune at pitch with Vln I and Vln II for all of 187–194 (c224), while violas and Hn 1 play the octave below. This is the movement's last full statement of F2. Its texture is the tune in five parts, the cellos' counter-melody with Bn 1, the clarinet background and the bass. Nothing answers the tune. In 194 the tune holds B♭4 for a whole bar, and nothing moves there except the clarinets' pp figure and the cellos' descent.

**Evidence.**
- Adler PDF 213, on Ex. XVI-10 mm. 25–32 (PDF 211): in Dvořák's answer to the strain, "the delay of the oboe entrance clarifies the tune much better, and makes the last four measures sound truly polyphonic". On the page, the oboes a2 enter at m. 27 with a figure in the tune's long notes. They do not join the tune.
- PDF 229 (Schoenberg on Op. 25): two like colours doubled at pitch "swallow each other up". At 191–193 one oboe in unison with two violin sections at mf adds little that can be heard.
- PDF 228 (Stravinsky): what precedes a solo entry should make that entry fresh.
- The score already delays the winds at 77 in the exposition, but there they only double the tune. An answer that is a polyphonic line is not used in any of F2's statements.
- c224's reason (a woodwind in the theme's colour, adler2 PDF 178) still holds, because Ob 1 keeps the doubling for the first phrase, 187–190.

**Fix (applied in recS.ily).** Ob 1 keeps 187–190 and its breath after 190. It rests 191–193. In 194, where the tune holds B♭4, it echoes the tune's bar 192 at the same pitch, mp espress.: E♭5–D♭5–C5–B♭4, with the first two notes as eighths after a quarter rest.

**Decisions.**
- *Beat 2 start.* The echo starts on beat 2 rather than on the downbeat. On 194.1 an E♭5 would sound for an eighth against Cl 1's F♭4, the minor ninth of V7 (c262), with the root above the ninth at close range.
- *No pickup.* An F5 pickup on 193.4 would make octaves with the bass F2→E♭2.
- *Voice leading.* Against the bass, the line is E♭/E♭ on beat 2, C5 passing over the D♭2 of the V4/2 on beat 3, and B♭ on beat 4. It ends in unison with the violins' held B♭4. There are no fifths or octaves with the cellos' D♭3–C3–B♭2 or the bass.
- *Unchanged.* The tune, the anticipation at 193.4 (Vln I·II, Vla, Hn 1), the harmony, all dynamics of the other parts, and 195–198 do not change. The exposition (67–82) has no echo, so the recapitulation now differs from it by an added line as well as by colour (PDF 213 on Bizet's repeat).

**Check.** The scratch build has 0 warnings and 16 pages. The outer-voice parallels in analysis.txt are 0, unchanged. par8.py on 190–196 reports only the existing Bn–bass octave doubling at 196.3, which is the same in v337. This finding touches no theme record line and changes no theme's notes. F2's notes and the finale's quotations are unchanged, so the other threads do not need to be told.

### 2. Schoenberg's passed countertheme (PDF 230–231): considered for 205–208 and 159–166, not applied

At 205–208 the horns hold an mp chord under the woodwinds' head. That chord supports the protected D♭–C–B–C path from 205 to 223. A moving horn line there would sit under four f woodwinds and the violins' scales, and would thicken a build that already has three layers (PDF 203 warns against too much colour). At 159–166 the Vln II and Cl 1 counter could pass to the violas, but the violas would then lie in the horns' F1 register (D3–A♭4) under an ff tune, where they would be covered. 151–158 is protected ("151–158 layout"). I decided to make no change.

### 3. Brahms's contrary-motion clarinets (PDF 205, 208): considered for 11–18, not applied

Cl 1 and Cl 2 break the chords in parallel thirds and sixths, between the tune's C4 and C5 octaves. In contrary motion Cl 2 would either meet Cl 1 in unison on each beat or drop to E♭3–G3. There it would blur the bass under the horns' tune. The figure stays as it is.

**Do not touch:** the 187–190 oboe doubling and the 194 echo as now set, F2's anticipation at 193.4, the cellos' and Bn 1's counter-melody at 187–198, and every protected passage listed in HISTORY.md.
