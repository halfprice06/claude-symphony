## Findings

### 1. Harp LH 239–242: the 8 bassa line ends after 240, so 241–242 are written at pitch (applied)

- **Problem.** Since c155 one 8 bassa line has covered all four bars of the harp's left hand at 239–242: `<a,, a,>1 | r4 <f,, f,>2. | <d, d>1 | <d, d>1`. That suits 239–240, where A1 and F1 would sit on three and four ledger lines. But at 241–242 the octave D2–D3 is printed under the sign as D3–D4. The upper D4 then needs a ledger line above the bass stave, and the lower D3 sits on the middle line. At pitch the same chord lies on the stave: D3 on the middle line and D2 just under it. So the octave sign produces an extra ledger line where it should save one.
- **Evidence.** Gould PDF 46: "Use up to at least three ledger lines before transposing pitches up or down an octave and using an octave sign." Gould PDF 47: players "can easily read at least four ledger lines at a glance", and isolated chords should use ledger lines rather than octave signs. Gould PDF 47 ("Using octave signs"): keep a figure at one octave and change octave between phrases. The barline 240|241 is where the harmony changes and where the LH moves from the low register into the stave. This does not reverse c155: the 8 bassa stays where it saves ledger lines (239–240) and goes only where it adds one.
- **Fix (in sections/sHead.ily, `sHeadHarpL`).** Change `| r4 <f,, f,>2. | <d, d>1-\tag #'score _\markup \small "G♯" | <d, d>1 \ottava #0 |` to `| r4 <f,, f,>2. \ottava #0 | <d, d>1-\tag #'score _\markup \small "G♯" | <d, d>1 |`, with a c515 comment. The line now ends with a corner after the F1 at 240 (Gould PDF 44). The G♯ pedal mark stays under 241. No loco is needed, because the pitch gap is large (PDF 48).
- **Result.** Applied in /tmp/critic-515/work. Build: 0 warnings (build-warnings.txt is empty), 16 pages, no system moved. score.midi is byte-identical to v515's, so analysis.txt's outer-voice parallels and par8.py over 239–243 are unchanged (only the existing cello and bassoon octave doublings of the bass are listed).
