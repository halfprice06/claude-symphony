## Findings

**1. Bar 75, beat 3: harmonise the turn's 6 with a VIIø7 neighbour chord over the tonic pedal. Applied.**

Problem: c271 gave F2's E♭-major repeat a turn: `bes''2( c'''16 bes'' aes'' bes'' g''4)`. That puts 5-6-5 (B♭-C-B♭) in the soprano over the tonic bass E♭, but the pad does not move with it. On beat 3 the violas' and cellos' passing A♭3 sounds a semitone above Hn 2's held G3 and a tone below Bn 1's held B♭3. The beat is a cluster, E♭-G-A♭-B♭-C, and the C has no chord. The score has used VIIø7 only inside V (248, 253), never in the job A&S give it first.

Evidence:
- PDF 381, sec. 14 and Ex. 22-14 (Schubert D. 845): in major, VIIø7 "typically occurs as a neighboring chord to I ... frequently with a soprano progression (5) 6 5".
- PDF 377, sec. 7: harmonising 6 in 5-6-5 is "a particularly characteristic and important function".
- PDF 379, Ex. 22-10: the leading-tone seventh over a tonic pedal.
- PDF 382, Ex. 22-15b: with the 7th in the soprano, double the 3rd of I.

Fix (sTwo.ily, bar 75 = 9th bar of the section):
- `sTwoHnOne`: `es'1\pp` → `es'2\pp d'4 es'4`
- `sTwoHnTwo`: `g1\pp` → `g2\pp f4 g4`
- `sTwoBsnOne`: `bes1~ |` (bar 75) → `bes2 f4 bes4~ |`
- `sTwoHarpR` bar 75: `es'8 g'8 bes'8 es''8 es'8 g'8 bes'8 es''8` → `es'8 g'8 bes'8 es''8 f'8 d'8 g'8 bes'8`

Beat 3 is now D (Hn 1, harp), F (Hn 2, Bn 1, harp), A♭ (violas, cellos) and C (the violins' turn) over the E♭ pedal. Every voice returns by step to I on beat 4: D→E♭, F→G, C→B♭, A♭→B♭. The 7th C is only in the soprano, and the I chord doubles its 3rd G (Hn 2 G3, violins G5). F2, the bass, the counter-melody, the dynamics and the horns' pp are unchanged. The horns keep consonances on the strong beats (c148) and move only by neighbour notes. My first version had Bn 1 on C4 and the harp on F4-A♭4. allpairs_par.py showed that this made octaves with the violins' B♭-C and with the violas' A♭-B♭, so I moved Bn 1 to F3 and the harp to F4-D4. The final version adds no line to allpairs_par.py's output.

**2. Bars 178→179: a d5 moves to a P5 in similar motion (Bn 1, Cl 2). Applied.**

Problem: in the C-minor F2, Bn 1 holds B2 through 178. On beat 4 Cl 2 plays F3 above it (a d5). At 179 both rise, to C3 and G3 (a P5). The leading tone is right, but the 7th rises and the pair makes the hidden fifths A&S mark "avoid". In the exposition at 58 Bn 1 plays D3-F3 (c145), so the pair does not form. The recapitulation had not transposed that layout.

Evidence: PDF 375, sec. 4 and Ex. 22-3c ("if the upper dissonance forms a diminished 5th, the similar motion that follows creates an obtrusive set of hidden 5ths; avoid this voice leading"). PDF 377 box. scan.py (d5/A4 → P5 in similar motion, all pairs of parts) flagged 179.1. It no longer does.

Fix (recS.ily, `recSBsnOne`, 8th bar): `b,1 \breathe` → `b,2 d2 \breathe`. On beat 4 Bn 1 D3 and Cl 2 F3 now go to C3 and G3 in contrary motion. As at 58.3-4, the weak half bar has three chord tones; the exposition omits the root there and this bar omits the third. The cor anglais's held D4, the pizzicato motive x in the cellos and timpani, and the pp level are unchanged. The protection of 171-186 covers the solo, its doubling and the pp accompaniment, not this one inner note. c263 changed the same clarinet and bassoon pad at 172 and 180.

**3. VIIø4/3 → I6 (Ex. 22-16) at the 74→75 and 194→195 joints. Not applied (decided).**

Both joints pass through V4/2 → I6 (bass B♭-A♭-G and E♭-D♭-C). That is the place for VIIø4/3. A&S say it leads to I6 "usually with 6 in the soprano" (PDF 382). Here the soprano holds F, the anticipated 2 (c261), and in that disposition V4/2 is the correct chord. 194 is also inside the protected 187-198. No change.

**4. The other upper-voice tritones that move to a fifth. Not applied (decided).**

At 13.4, 21.4, 23.4, 153.4 and 173.3 the interval is an A4 that moves to a P5 inside one harmony (an arpeggiation of V or of ii). A&S allow an A4 to resolve irregularly (PDF 375). At 165.1 (Ob 2 F4 / Cl 2 B3 → D4 / G3), 192.3 (the tune's D♭5 over Bn 1's G3) and 147-149, a d5 moves to a P5 while V7 goes to V or to V9. The rule on PDF 375 concerns the resolution to I. All three passages are protected (151-166, 187-198, 147-150). No change.

**5. ♭VI in major (Ex. 23-5, 23-6) is still the one mixture device the score never uses. Not applied (decided).**

The only places where it could serve are 89→90 (a deceptive V7→C♭ at the closing group's cadence, which would need added bars and would break the protected 6/3 bridge 87-90) and the C-major coda (protected, and it would anticipate the A♭ of 259-261). The score already has ♭6 in every major area except the protected G-major hand-over. I confirm c222's decision and record it again so a later critic does not search for it.
