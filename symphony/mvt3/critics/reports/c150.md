### 5. Imitation and echo by register (Rimsky PDF 128–129): the cyclic answers follow the rule; no change
Bars: 211–220, 226–230, 51/67.
Evidence: theme B in the trumpets (ff, an octave above movement II) is answered an octave lower, at movement II's pitch, by Hn 1·2 with the cor anglais at f, while the low strings go f > mp (v139). That is imitation in octaves with less resonance, which Rimsky says "creates an effect resembling an echo". The answering instruments are lower in range, as the law of register asks, and they are brass and double reed after brass and double reed, so the colours are related. Ex. 252 (Sadko, PDF 417) has the same order: an oboe solo, then the trumpets' answer off stage, where the distance makes it weaker. The canon at 226 goes an octave higher and is given to higher instruments (violins, Fl/Ob/Cl). F2 changes character from the minor solo to the major statement, and each is scored in its own way (trumpet over pizzicato; violins with the pad). Nothing to change.
