## Findings

### 1. The parts are A4 with 7.4 mm staves; MOLA prefers paper larger than A4 and a 7.5 mm staff, and the other three movements use B4 (applied, parts only)

Problem: MOLA PDF 8: "While A4 parts are considered the minimum, paper larger than A4 is preferred and recommended among librarians", with JIS B4 "the most common size". PDF 7: 7.5 mm is "the most readable staff size for all instruments", and 7.0 mm is less readable for strings because two players share a stand. PDF 7 also limits flute, violin and tuba parts to 8–10 staves a page (up to twelve for others, if not crowded). The finale's 30 parts are A4 at 21 pt (7.4 mm) with no system limit. Movements I, II and III print their parts on B4, so a player's folder for the symphony would hold one movement on smaller paper. Violin II also kept a page turn inside the music at 61|62 (pizz. pp, no rest on either side; c255 kept it because bars 1–66 did not fit page 1).

Answer to c255's reason (the reflow would undo the turns): I rebuilt all 30 parts on B4 and measured every turn against per-player MIDI. Every turn is now in a rest: Bsn 2 186|187 (16 bars before), Cb, Vc, Vla, Vln I and Vln II 66|67 (the half-bar rest before E, as c255 designed), Vla, Vln I and Vln II 186|187, Cbsn 256|257 (2 bars), Cl 1 130|131 (2), Cl 2 146|147 (8), Fl 1 122|123 (9.5), Fl 2 198|199 (32.5), harp 122|123 (40), Hn 1, 2, 4 and Ob 1 186|187 (17–20), Hn 3 and timpani 218|219 (4), Ob 2 268|269 (4.25), Tbn 1·2 and tuba 246|247 (12.75), Tpt 1 254|255 (4). Two manual breaks needed changing: on B4 Violin I's bars 67–186 fit one page, so c165's break at 234 became a turn inside the music (234|235); it moves to 126, between facing pages. The bass clarinet now fits on one page, so its start on page 2 and the forced break before K are dropped. Violin II's page 1 now holds 1–66, so its first turn joins the other strings at 66 and the 61|62 turn is gone. The set goes from 88 pages to 65; no part has more turns than before.

Fix, in parts-book.ly:

```
\paper { page-breaking = #ly:page-turn-breaking paper-width = 257\mm paper-height = 364\mm max-systems-per-page = 12 top-margin = 20\mm ... }
\layout { #(layout-set-staff-size 21.26) ... }        % 7.5 mm
part-fluteOne, part-fluteTwo, part-vlnOne, part-vlnTwo, part-tuba:  \paper { max-systems-per-page = 10 ... }
part-vlnOne:  ... \vlnOne } { s1*66 \pageBreak s1*60 \pageBreak s1*60 \pageBreak } >>     (was s1*66, s1*120, s1*48)
part-vlnTwo:  ... \vlnTwo } { s1*66 \pageBreak s1*120 \pageBreak } >>                    (was s1*61, s1*125)
part-bassCl:  \paper { page-breaking = #ly:optimal-breaking }   and no { s1*150 \pageBreak }
```

The reflow left Ob 1's bar-10 crescendo too short to print (the build's only new warning). It gets the part-only minimum length that c315 gave Fl 1, Fl 2 and Ob 2 (sections/intro.ily, introOboeOne):
`R1*3 | \tag #'part { \once \override Staff.Hairpin.minimum-length = #6 \once \override Staff.Hairpin.springs-and-rods = #ly:spanner::set-spacing-rods } aes''2\mf\< f''4->\f r4 |`

Pages viewed after the change: Horn 2 (both), cor anglais, bass clarinet, Violin II 1 and 4, Violoncello 2 (12 systems, not crowded), Flute 1 p. 1, Contrabassoon p. 1, timpani (both). Violin II has a fifth page with one system (269–279), facing page 4, so it costs no turn; an eleventh system on page 4 would not fit at 7.5 mm.

### 2. Six exposed entries after 15–46 bars of rest have no cue (applied, parts only)

Bars and parts:
- Fl 1 61.4 (p <, doubling the trumpet solo's peak, after 17 bars, in the Poco meno mosso) and the same entry in the recapitulation at 181.4 (15 bars).
- Contrabassoon 87 (f, the descending bass G–F–E♭–D–C–B♭ four bars after F, after 46 bars through C, D, E, the animando and Tempo I).
- Horn 2 195 (p, open, the F3–F♭3–E♭3 line with the violas, after 25 bars, mid-phrase).
- Cor anglais 217 (f espress., with Hn 1·2, after 30 bars through M, N, the più animato and O).
- Bass clarinet 257 (f <, two bars after S, after 33 bars).

Problem: MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest… Cues must be audible and obvious to the musician reading the part", and Example 4 (PDF 10) prints them in the bars just before the entry. The set already cues 43 entries (c075–c255), but these six were missed. None of them falls on a rehearsal letter's downbeat, and all six come in the middle of a phrase.

Fix (part-only, tag 'part; the score's music and MIDI are unchanged), each preceded by a c345 comment:


### 3. The score does not list its instrumentation (applied, score page 1 only)

Problem: MOLA PDF 6: the score's opening matter gives "a list of the full instrumentation", the "keys of all transposing instruments, and a list of all percussion instruments", and "how many percussionists will be required". The title block gives the duration and the octave transposition of the contrabassoon and basses, but not the forces, the timpani count or the two percussion players (the latter only in a staff name). A front page would break the 16-page cap, so the list goes into the title block.

Fix (score.ly header, subsubtitle, after "Duration: about 7 minutes"):

```
\small "Instrumentation: 2 flutes, 2 oboes, cor anglais in F, 2 clarinets in B♭, bass clarinet in B♭, 2 bassoons, contrabassoon — 4 horns in F, 3 trumpets in C, 2 trombones, bass trombone, tuba —"
\small "4 timpani (G, A♭, C, E♭), percussion (2 players: crash cymbals, bass drum), harp — strings"
```

Result: 16 pages; only page 1 differs (systems slightly closer, no compression warning); pages 2–16 pixel-identical to v345 at 40 dpi.
