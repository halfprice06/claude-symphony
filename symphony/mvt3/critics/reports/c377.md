## Findings

### 1. 87–90: the closing bridge doubles where the reading teaches a dialogue and imitation (applied)
**Problem.** The closing bridge carries the scale figure twice over the 6/3 chain: B♭5/B♭4 at 87–88, F6/F5 at 89–90. Both times the scoring is the same mixture: Vln I·II in octaves, with Fl 1 on Vln I and, since c304, Ob 1 on Vln II at 89–90. The ff tutti at 91 then arrives as more of the same sound. Adler gives three reasons to do it differently. In the Eroica (PDF 253–255, Ex. VIII-20), Beethoven passes a short gesture from strings to winds at different pitch levels and then "sums it up with a cadential tutti phrase." In Schumann 1/iii (PDF 253), choirs used "first separately and then together" produce completion. In Mozart 38 (PDF 271–272, Ex. VIII-42), two wind groups imitate each other, which works because each wind colour is distinct. The score already has this kind of exchange at 99–106 (strings' head, then the winds' scale figure). But two woodwinds are never in canon anywhere, and the passage into the codetta is the clearest spot for it.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-377/work/sections/clos.ily).**
- 87–88: only Vln I·II play the figure. Fl 1 rests (`R1*2`).
- 89–90: only the winds play it. Fl 1 leads at F6, f <: `r4 f'''8(\f\< es''' d''' c''') bes''( aes'' | bes''8 c''' d''' es''') f'''4-> r4\!`. One beat later, Ob 1 answers at F5 and Cl 1 an octave lower at F4, as a canon at the octave:
  - Ob 1: `r2 f''8(\f\< es'' d'' c'' | bes'8 aes' bes' c'' d'' es'') f''4->\! |`
  - Cl 1 (89–90 were rests): `r2 f'8(\f\< es' d' c' | bes8 aes bes c' d' es') f'4->\! |`

  The two lines move in sixths and then tenths, and they meet on F5 on 90 beat 4, where the flute drops its octave.
- Vln I·II rest at 89–90 (`R1*2`) and re-enter ff on the down-bow at 91. Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 127 supports this: a group that enters fresh adds weight to the arrival.

The bass, the violas' and bassoons' 6/3 chain, the horns' p < repeated chords, the timpani and every note from 91 on are unchanged. F1, F2 and the chorale are not touched, and no quotation is involved. This replaces c304's oboe doubling of Vln II at 89–90; the reading's reason is given above. The winds are marked f rather than mf because they now carry the figure alone after the f strings.
