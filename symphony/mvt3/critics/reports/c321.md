## Findings

### 1. 255–258: F2's return in the coda is buried inside held brass at its own pitches. Applied.

**Problem.** At 255–258, under the last chorale statement, the cellos and Hn 3·4 (Bn 1 from 257) play F2's third phrase in C major: G3–E3 | D3 C3 E3 G3 | C4–D4 | G4 F4 E4 D4. They are marked f, "espress.". Around them the held brass are ff on the line's own pitches:
- Tbn 1·2 hold G3/E3 (255–256), then E4/C3.
- Hn 1·2 hold G3, then G3/B3, then E4.
- The bass trombone holds E3.
- Tpt 3 strikes C4 and E4 in half notes.

The line's first two notes, G3 and E3, are the same pitches the trombones are holding, two dynamic levels louder. Meanwhile the violas play sixteen repeated quarter notes (C5, then E4) that add nothing.

**Evidence.**
- Sebesky PDF 219: "if a passage contains notes which lie in a low, weak … register, the arranger should not have four trumpets blaring in unison at the same time". Here the lead is in the cellos' and low horns' weak middle register, under five ff brass parts holding the same notes.
- PDF 208 (Ex. 273): to make a moving line tell, Sebesky puts it in unison cellos "to give the line more emphasis" and says "we are deliberately sacrificing overall ensemble balance in this passage in favor of melodic motion". Ex. 274, where the cellos only hold the chord, is "much less interesting".
- PDF 211 (Ex. 279): a background that moves inside the lead's register "distorts the aural focus".
- PDF 213: give every part, the inner ones too, "as meaningful … a melodic line as possible". The violas' repeated notes fail this test.
- PDF 224: a recalled tune must be recognisable or it should not be there.
- The models do the same thing. When Dvořák brings the Largo's theme back in 9/iv, or Tchaikovsky brings the motto into 5/iv's coda, the recalled line is in strings and horns at the top of their dynamic, never under held brass in its own octave.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-321/work/sections/finale.ily).** Three changes:
- The violas leave their repeated notes and join the cellos at the unison, ff "espress.". The line is now Vc + Vla + Hn 3·4 a2 (+ Bn 1 from 257).
- The line is ff (was f).
- The held brass harmony enters f and grows with the woodwinds' re-entry curve (f at 257, ff at 259). This keeps c033/c038's crescendo by addition, now in the brass as well.

The static-bar scan finds no free hold that would take one. Adding a trill elsewhere would put colour where Sebesky says to pass it up.
