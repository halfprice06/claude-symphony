## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 149: the timpani hold a ff roll through the brass's sfp < (applied)
- Bars: 147–150 (retr.ily, `retrTimp`).
- Problem: since c226 the horns, Tpt 3, trombones and tuba re-attack at 149 sfp < ff into 150 under the allargando. The timpani roll on G2 is tied ff from 147 to 150, so it covers the dip that makes the brass re-attack audible; the one instrument that sounds the G pedal loudest ignores the effect.
- Evidence: Rimsky PDF 48: "In tremolando they can execute the most gradual crescendo, diminuendo, the sfp and morendo"; PDF 41: in the brass "the sf > p effect being excellent". The score has no timpani sfp anywhere, so the technique is new to the movement. c174 protected "timpani 139–150" before c226 added the brass sfp; this keeps every timpani note and rhythm and only lets the roll join the later brass dynamic.
- Fix: `retrTimp` ending `g,1:32\ff~ | g,1:32 | g,1:32\sfp\<~ | g,2.:32\ff r4 |` (was `g,1:32\ff~ | g,1:32~ | g,1:32~ | g,2.:32 r4 |`): the tie 148→149 is cut so the roll re-strikes sfp with the brass.

### 2. 207–208: horns mp in the same chord as trombones mp (applied)
- Bars: 207–208 (codaOne.ily, Hn 1–4).
- Problem: under the woodwinds' f restatement of F1's head in B♭ minor, the four horns (A♭3–G♭4–E♭4–B♭4) and Tbn 1·2 (D♭4, E♭3) hold the pad, all mp, while the bass trombone and tuba are f. Marked alike, the horns sound weaker than the trombones in the same chord.
- Evidence: Rimsky PDF 41: horns are about half as strong as trumpets and trombones; "the marks of expression in the horns should be one degree stronger than in the rest of the brass". The per-player MIDI scan found 207–209 to be the only unprotected bars where soft horns sound at the trombones' level (234 is c304's single quarter). Vln II and violas are already mf there.
- Fix: Hn 1–4 `\mf` instead of `\mp` on 207's whole notes (`bes'1\mf`, `es'1\mf`, `ges'1\mf`, `aes1\mf`); both shared horn staves change together. The crescendo from 209 stays.

### 3. 132 and 278: solid harp chords without "non arpegg." (applied)
- Bars: 132 (dev.ily, `devHarpL`, the ff G chord G2–D3–B3 / D4–G4–B4) and 278 (finale.ily, `finaleHarpL`, C2–G2–C3 on the fff downbeat).
- Problem: both chords are meant to strike with the tutti on the beat (c297: "one solid ff G chord"; 278 lands with the cymbal and bass drum), but the part says nothing, and a harpist rolls a chord by default.
- Evidence: Rimsky PDF 46: "The chords must always be broken (arpeggiato); should the composer wish otherwise he should notify it (non arpeggiato)."
- Fix: `<g, d b>4\ff->^\markup \small \italic "non arpegg." \laissezVibrer` at 132 and `<c, g, c>4\ff_\markup \small \italic "non arpegg."\laissezVibrer` at 278. Text only; MIDI identical.
