## Findings

### 1. Bar numbers: one placement for the whole movement (applied)

Bars: 1–208 (pages 1–11), especially 149–150 on page 8.

Problem: MOLA PDF 4 says that "placement of measure numbers should be the same throughout the work". The score numbers every bar below the double basses. Up to bar 208 the row is 5.5 staff spaces below the basses (score.ly, the Double basses `\with`). From 209 on, c185's `\bnLow` (global.ily, called in codaOneCb at 209) sets it to 6.8 spaces. c185 did this so that the dynamics at the barlines of 212, 259, 263 and 278 would no longer push single numbers below the row. So pages 12–16 have one row height and pages 1–11 another. On page 8 the same fault c185 fixed later in the score is still there: the basses' and cellos' `sfp < ff` at 149–150 pushes "149" and "150" below the row of 144–157. The bounding boxes put them 1.1 pt below the others, and the page image shows the step. Page 9 has a smaller step at 170 (0.7 pt).

Evidence: MOLA PDF 4 (one placement for measure numbers throughout the work; if every bar is numbered, below the system). Ex. 6 (PDF 11) and Ex. 3 (PDF 10) set every number at one height. Gould PDF 19 (measure every symbol from the stave) is the rule c185 applied to pages 12–16 only.

Fix, applied in /tmp/critic-585/work:
- score.ly, Double basses staff: `\override BarNumber.staff-padding = #6.8` (was `#5.5`), with a c585 comment. Every page now uses c185's distance. `\bnLow` at 209 now sets the value the row already has, so it changes nothing. I left it in place so that codaOne.ily stays untouched.
- The lower row made pages 2 and 7 over-full: LilyPond warned "compressing over-full page" by 2.3 and 1.7 spaces. I removed both warnings with fixed staff distances that hold each staff where LilyPond's own compressed layout put it, so nothing moves visibly:
  - global.ily, page 2 (c251's fixed distances, bars 14 and 27), each value about 1% smaller:
    `(12.04 10.67 11.76 8.61 8.61 11.04 9.06 10.74 8.64 8.64 8.61 8.61)` and
    `(9.81 13.00 8.88 14.58 8.61 13.00 12.38 11.11 8.78 8.61 11.28 14.99 10.53 9.57 10.74 9.98)`.
  - global.ily, page 7 (new, score-only), at H (115):
    `\tag #'score \overrideProperty Score.NonMusicalPaperColumn.line-break-system-details #'((alignment-distances . (13.48 11.22 8.13 11.22 14.85 11.70 12.00 14.34 9.30)))`;
    at the fixed break before 128:
    `... (alignment-distances . (10.80 13.20 11.15 9.06 10.12 13.48 12.42 8.20 7.38 7.89 9.26 13.34 13.07 11.49 12.93 12.35 12.31)))`.

Result: every system's numbers now share one height. The largest deviation on all 16 pages is under 0.6 pt, where before it was 1.1 pt at 149–150 and 0.7 pt at 170. The distance below the basses is now the same on every page. Pages 12–16 are pixel-identical to v585. Pages 1–11 change only in the row's height and in small system shifts where LilyPond had spare vertical room (pages 3, 4, 10). Page breaks, system breaks and horizontal spacing are unchanged. I checked pages 2, 7, 8 and 10 at 200 dpi. On page 2, system 1's row stands to the left of system 2's letter B and clear of it, as before. On page 7, no stave or mark touches another. The build has 0 warnings, 16 pages, and the MIDI is byte-identical.

### 2. Harp pedal diagrams against MOLA PDF 5 (not applied)

MOLA PDF 5 says harp pedalling should be left to the performer. The score prints diagrams at 43, 67, 123, 187 and 235, and texts at 115 and 247. c495 and c568 kept them. The settings at 43 (low D string), 241–243 (G♯) and 277 (the enharmonic glissando tuning) are requirements of the passage, not fingering-like choices, and movements I and II follow the same convention. I have no new reason that answers this, so I keep them.

### 3. Rehearsal letter at 123 (not applied)

The harp and string entry in G major at 123 is a landmark without a letter. c495 declined a letter there because H (115) and J (135) frame it and an extra letter would shift every later letter in the parts. Nothing has changed since that decision, so I keep it.

### 4. Title-page revision stamp (not applied)

MOLA PDF 6 asks for a print or revision date. The header reads "Full score in C — draft v585". c399 defined this stamp, and the release check (cycle 599) turns it into the version and date. A date now would be overwritten at that check, so I made no change.
