## Findings

### 1. 273–279: the closing chain and the scherzo quotation go by at Allegro molto with no change of tempo (applied)
Problem: 273–276 is the movement's most important cyclic event after the arrival. G–F♯–F–E over C – F♯°7 – iv6/4 – I completes the chain of closes from movements II and III, the scherzo's head follows at 275–276, then the V–I echo (277) and the fff chord (278). At 2 = 100 the seven bars last 8.4 s, the same speed as the F1 statements before them, and nothing in the score marks the change from thematic statements to the closing chords. The texture changes completely at 273 (string tremolo, held wind and brass chords, the Cb eighths alone keep the pulse). Every level from 255 is ff or fff, so dynamics cannot set the passage off (reading 12, PDF 290–291: a compressed range leaves only presence, no contrast). Held chords and tremolo need time to sound in the hall (reading 10, PDF 158).
Evidence: Dvořák 7/iv changes subito to "Molto maestoso" for exactly this texture (tremolo strings, held chords, the major close). Dvořák 9/iv, this movement's own model for recalled themes, slows to "Meno mosso e maestoso" for its recall (328–331) before the last tutti.
Tempo audit (the focus question): every other change is marked and prepared. 49–50 poco rit. → 51 Poco meno mosso; 81–82 animando → 83 Tempo I; 149–150 allargando → 151 Tempo I; 169–170 poco rit. → 171; 197–198 poco rit. → 199 Tempo I poco a poco più animato (\mt 172, 176 at 203, 207); 211 Largamente comes after a beat-4 rest in every part at 210 (a caesura, so a subito change is fine); 221–222 allargando → 223 Maestoso; 235 Poco più mosso comes after the plagal close; 243–246 poco a poco stringendo → 247 Allegro molto. 273 was the only change of character with no marking.
Fix (applied, global.ily):
```
s1*10 s1*8 \tempo "Largamente" 2 = 72 s1*6 s1
```
I decided on Largamente at 2 = 72 rather than a new Maestoso, because 223 already has "Maestoso" at 2 = 56; 72 is the tempo of the theme-B recall at 211, so the score adds no new tempo. The change is subito, as in Dvořák 7/iv: 272's F1 run lands on C at 273, which prepares it. The \tempo carries its own MIDI value, so no separate \mt is needed. The movement lasts 3 s longer (421 → 424 s).
Quotation note for the composer and the other threads: the scherzo's theme A head at 275–276 keeps its pitches and note values (record check 35/35 unchanged), but it now sounds at quarter = 144 instead of 200 (the scherzo's own speed is quarter = 264), roughly an augmentation by 1.8. finale.md should say "275–276 at Largamente" so the scherzo thread knows.

### 2. 273: Trumpets 1·2 re-enter with no dynamic (applied)
Problem: Tpt 1·2 rest at 271–272 (c026) and come back at 273 on the held C5/A4–A♭4, with no dynamic. The other re-entries in the same bar have one (Hn 1·2, B.cl, Cbn, B.tbn ff). For a brass player the dynamic is the colour (reading 3, PDF 148), and after a rest the last printed ff is two bars and a system behind.
Fix (applied, finale.ily; both parts on the shared staff carry the same mark):
```
finaleTptOne: ... R1*2 | \once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.2 c''1->\ff | ...
finaleTptTwo: ... R1*2 | \once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0 c''2->\ff a'2 | ...
```

### 3. 115–122: the bassoon solo (theme F2) has no phrasing dynamics (applied)
Problem: the solo is marked "p solo, espress." and nothing more. Its first phrase climbs to the peak G4 (117) and falls back. Its last note, A3 at 122, is the point where the melody passes to the cellos at 123. Buhler, quoted in reading 6 (PDF 155), describes the lyrical legato players use as a swell into the note of arrival. Reading 7 (PDF 152) says the score has to write the shape down. The trumpet solo at 51 has its single hairpin at its climax (60–62), and that climax is not part of the bassoon's eight bars. So the development's statement currently has no shape at all.
Fix (applied, dev.ily devBsnOne): `a4 g4\< bes4 d'4 | g'2.\! f'4\> | es'2 d'2\!)` for 116–118, and `a1\>) | R1*2\!` for 122, a diminuendo into the cellos' p entry. Bn 2 rests here, so the shared staff has no conflicting dynamics.

### 4. 123–130: the harp sits at the melody's level and does not join the crescendo (applied)
Problem: the harp's eighth-note arpeggios (left hand, 8va bassa, G1–A3) start p at 123, the same level as the cello melody (p, in the same register) and the violas (p, 124). From 129 every other part has a hairpin into f at 131 (Fl 1, Vln I mf<; Vln II, Vla, Vc, Cb, Hn 1, Hn 3·4, Bn 1 <), but the harp stays p to 130. Reading 11 (PDF 290): an accompaniment's level has to be set below the line, and the layer that keeps going has to join the overall shape. The pizzicato accompaniment of 115–122 is pp under the p solo; the harp should match it.
Fix (applied, dev.ily devHarpL): `\stemUp g,8\pp^\markup ...` at 123; `g,8\< b,8 d8 g8 ...` at 129; `\stemNeutral R1\! R1*3` at 131.

### 5. 257–263: the woodwind crescendo is one 7-bar hairpin from mf, while the rest of the orchestra starts its crescendo at 259 (applied)
Problem: the upper and low reeds re-enter mf at 257 (cycles 33, 34, 36) under ff strings and brass, with a single hairpin to fff at 263. Strings, brass and the violas' quarters start their crescendo at 259. With one long hairpin, nothing tells the woodwinds where to be at 259, so they either stay too soft for two bars or arrive at ff too early. Reading 7 (PDF 152) says a staged dynamic has to be written as stages.
Fix (applied, finale.ily, all ten woodwind parts; shared staves get the same mark): `\f\<` on the 259 note, e.g. Fl 1 `e'''1\mf\<~ | e'''1 | d'''1\f\<~ | ...`; Bn 2 `a,1\mf\<~ | a,1 | f,1\f\<~ | ...`. The mf re-entry at 257 (c034, c036) is unchanged.

### 6. 134 and 210: hairpins end on dynamics printed under rests (applied)
Problem: at 133–134 Vln I·II, Vla, Vc, Cb and Hn 3·4 have `x1\>~ | x2. r4\p`. A p under a rest has no meaning, and the next bar (135) starts pp, so two dynamics stand a beat apart. At 210 the timpani has `aes,2.:32 r4\ff`, which then repeats the ff of 211. Reading 7 (PDF 152): a target dynamic tells the player where the line arrives, which only works if it sits on a note.
Fix (applied): `x2. r4\!` in the seven parts at 134 (dev.ily), and `aes,2.:32 r4\!` at 210 (codaOne.ily). The hairpins now end at the rest and their targets are the pp of 135 and the ff of 211. No sounding change.
