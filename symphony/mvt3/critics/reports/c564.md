## Findings

### 1. Bar 35: A♭ timpani roll ff > f under the held low-brass chord (applied in scratch)

**Bars.** 35 (pTwo bar 9), where F1's head arrives in A♭ in Vln I·II and Fl 1.

**Problem.** Tbn 1·2, the bass trombone and the contrabassoon re-enter here for the first time since bar 6 and hold the A♭ chord ff > f for the whole bar (c208), over the basses' held A♭1. The timpani give one f quarter stroke and rest. So the drum is a degree softer than the brass it doubles, and it is gone after one beat while the brass hold. It is the only unprotected held loud low-brass chord in the movement where a drum of the bass's pitch is available and the timpani do not roll (`held.py`).

**Evidence.** In Rimsky-Korsakov's appendix every held loud chord that uses timpani has them roll on the bass (chords 2–5, 8–12, 17–20, PDF 505–507); none has a single stroke under a held chord. Ex. 209 (PDF 375) shapes the roll with the held chord ("dimin."). The score already does this at 5 (c294), 39–40 (c326) and 167–168 (c474); 35 was left with c208's stroke, which c208 kept without a reason. The A♭ drum is free: its next use is the stroke on 36.3, and the retuning to B♭ in the rest at 37 keeps its 2.5 bars.

**Fix (applied).** sections/pTwo.ily, `pTwoTimp`, bar 35:

```
aes,4->\f r4 r2 |        % was
aes,2.:32->~\ff\> aes,4:32\f |   % now, the shape of the Cbsn's aes,,2.~\ff\> aes,,4\f
```

Bar 36 (E♭ and A♭ strokes, no new mark, as c208 wanted), the muta at 37 and the B♭ roll at 39–40 are unchanged. A one-line c564 comment sits above `pTwoTimp`. The printed roll lines up with the trombones' and contrabassoon's hairpins on page 2; the page layout is unchanged.

### 2. Bar 166: chord 8's strike-and-hold, and the fifth of the i6 (checked, no change)

At 166.1 the closing i6 of the recapitulated F1 has its G only in Cl 2 (G3, soft), and the whole orchestra strikes and stops. Rimsky's chord 8 (PDF 506) would let wind and brass hold after the string strike, and principle 11 would complete each group's chord. I decided against both. The bar is the phrase end of F1 in two parts (C over E♭; PDF 122, tutti in two parts), the cut-off before the 167 link is the phrase ending (c474), and 151–166 layout and the E♭ bass and timpani at 166 are protected.
