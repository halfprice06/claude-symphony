## Findings

### 1. Bar 129: the horns carry the moving parts of the I6–IV6–iv6 chain (applied in scratch)

**Problem.** At 129 the harmony changes three times in one bar: I6 over B, then IV6 over E, then iv6 over E♭, and then V7 at 130. Hn 3·4's repeated quarters carry the motion. Hn 3 leaps D4→G4 onto Hn 1's held G4, which makes a unison. Hn 4 goes G3–G3–E3–C4, a sixth-leap up, and then falls a third to A3 at 130. Hn 3 also leaps G4→D4 at 130. The woodwind are nearly still: Fl 1 B5–G5 and Bn 1 B3–C4 in halves. This is the reverse of the reading.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 108: "In a chain of consecutive chords it is advisable to entrust the stationary parts to the brass, the moving parts to the wood-wind". The same page says concords go to the horns. PDF 112 says "correct progression of parts" decides when the melodic position keeps changing.

**What earlier changes wanted, and how the fix keeps it.** c033 wanted a quarter-note pulse in the horns for the crescendo (Beethoven 6/i), so the pulse stays. c372 and the iv6 change wanted IV6 and iv6 complete. Both are still complete: C4 is in Hn 3 (unison with Bn 1) and G3 is in Hn 4. c129, c157 and c159 settled 130–131, which is unchanged.

**Fix (applied).** In /tmp/critic-547/work/sections/dev.ily:
```
devHnThree … | d'4\p\< d'4 c'4 c'4 | d'1\mf\< | …    (was d'4 d'4 g'4 g'4)
devHnFour  … | g4\p\< g4 g4 g4 | a4\mf\< a4 a4 a4 | … (was g4 g4 e4 c'4)
```
The horns now hold chord tones on the pulse. They move only by step into 130: Hn 3 D–C–D as a neighbour, Hn 4 G3→A3. Hn 3 no longer doubles Hn 1's G4. The motion of the chain is in the bass (E–E♭–D), in Bn 1 (B3–C4) and in Fl 1. The resulting voicings are 129.3 E3–G3–C4–G4–G5 and 129.4 E♭3–G3–C4–G4–G5. A comment with the reason is at the top of devHnThree.

**Build.** Build of /tmp/critic-547/work is clean: build-warnings.txt is empty, 16 pages, and analysis.txt shows 0 outer-voice parallels. par8.py on 128–131 reports only the cellos' octave doubling of the basses, the same output as v547. I checked the engraving at 129 on page 7: the shared Hn 3·4 staff prints the two voices cleanly.

### 2. Bar 80.3–4: horns inside the bassoons (checked, not changed)

**Problem.** At 80.3–4 Hn 2 F3 and Hn 1 A♭3–G3 sit between Bn 2 B♭2 and Bn 1 B♭3, at p. PDF 108 calls horns inside bassoons "not to be recommended".

**Why no change.** Here Hn 1 is doubling the tune with Cl 1 and the cellos, so it is not a four-part pad. Bn 1's B♭3→A♭3 (80.3→81) approaches the held seventh by step. Moving it to F3 would replace that with a leap, and c147 accepted this crossed pad. I tested the alternative on paper only and declined it: it is not better.

### 3. Bars 101–102 and 105–106: horn neighbour chord over a held bassoon (kept)

**Problem.** Read literally, PDF 108's chain rule would put the neighbour motion (G–A♭–G, B♭–C–B♭) in the woodwind and hold the horns.

**Why no change.** c247 considered this and kept it, and c519 has just revoiced Hn 2 here. The motion is one neighbour chord over a pedal, not a chain of harmonies. I have no new reason to reopen it, so I do not.
