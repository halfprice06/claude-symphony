## Findings

### 1. Parts: rehearsal letters and tempo words at different heights (D, F, K, N, O, P, Q, R, T) — applied
**Problem.** At the nine letters that coincide with a tempo change, each part prints the boxed letter and the tempo text as two separate grobs. The letter is centred on the barline. The tempo mark is aligned on the first note or rest. When the two overlap horizontally, the letter is stacked above the tempo; when they do not, the letter drops to staff height beside or below the tempo. The result changes from letter to letter in one part. In Trumpet 1, D is above "Poco meno mosso" while K sits a tier below "Tempo I". In the percussion part, D, K, N and R are at staff height left of their tempo words, while E, L, O and P are a tier higher. In Vln I, N is below "Tempo I" and O is above "Largamente".
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 5: tempo changes and rehearsal letters in every part. Example 4 (PDF 10) prints "[E] Tempo I": the boxed letter at the barline and the tempo word after it, on one baseline. Adler2 PDF 299 Ex. XVIII-5 sets the letters on one row at the barlines.
**Fix (parts only; the score and the MIDI are unchanged).** In global.ily:
```lilypond
#(define (c315-letter-centre grob) ...)          ; centres the letter, not the whole line, on the barline
#(define ((c315-letter-with-tempo tm) grob)
   (ly:stencil-combine-at-edge (ly:text-interface::print grob) X RIGHT
     (grob-interpret-markup grob (markup #:fontsize -2 tm)) 0.8))
mmk = #(define-scheme-function (txt n) (markup? string?)
  #{ \markup \line { \bold #txt \normal-text \concat { "(" \fontsize #-1 \general-align #Y #DOWN \note {2} #UP #(string-append " = " n ")") } } #})
lt = #(define-music-function (tm) (markup?)
  (if (not (eq? (ly:parser-lookup 'isPartsBook) #t)) (make-music 'SequentialMusic 'elements '())
  #{ \tag #'part { \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.stencil = #(c315-letter-with-tempo tm)
                   \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.self-alignment-X = #c315-letter-centre
                   \once \omit Score.MetronomeMark } #}))
```
Each letter that has a tempo gets `\lt \mmk "Tempo I" "84"` (or the matching text) placed just before its `\mark \default`. Q gets a `\column` with "molto cantabile". In parts-head.ily, `isPartsBook = ##t` goes before `\include "global.ily"`.

The flag is needed because score.ly reads `\global` without `\removeWithTag #'part`, so a plain `\tag #'part` would also act in the score. My first test did that: it removed the timpani-level tempo marks from the score. With the flag, the 16 score pages are pixel-identical to a fresh v315 build. If parts-head.ily is regenerated, the `isPartsBook` line must be kept.

**Layout follow-ups**, all in parts-book.ly. In the percussion part, RehearsalMark takes horizontal space with 2 spaces after it, as its tempo marks do (c195), so "R Più allegro" stays inside the right margin and "P Maestoso (𝅗𝅥 = 56)" no longer touches the cymbals' fff at 223. Percussion and timpani system padding is 0.5, and cor anglais padding is 0.2. Every part keeps its v315 page count.

### 2. Flute 2 part: page 1 compressed, and its turn depended on the breaker — applied
**Problem.** The v315 parts build warned "compressing over-full page by 0.1 staff-spaces" on Flute 2's page 1. After Finding 1, the free page-turn breaker put the part on three pages with a turn at 249|250, inside the hammered ff music.
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 7: a turn falls preferably at the end of a multi-bar rest. PDF 8 and Ex. 7: proofread the extracted parts.
**Fix.** `\book { \bookOutputName "part-fluteTwo" \paper { system-system-spacing.padding = #0.5 page-breaking = #ly:optimal-breaking } …` and a `{ s1*198 \pageBreak }` voice in its score. The part has two pages with the turn in the rest 187–198, as in v315, and no compression.

### 3. Parts: the title is not the programme title — applied
**Problem.** Every part is headed "Symphony No. 1 — IV. Finale". The score reads "Symphony No. 1 in C minor".
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 6: the full title as it would appear in a formal concert programme, on the score, and at minimum the title on the front of each part.
**Fix.** `title = "Symphony No. 1 in C minor — IV. Finale"` in all 30 books of parts-book.ly (and in the unused mkparts.py). It is still one line, and no page count changed.

### 4. Flute 1, Flute 2, Oboe 2 parts: the crescendo at bar 10 vanishes — applied
**Problem.** In these three parts, bar 10 follows the rest 7–9 and is set narrow. The `\mf\<` … `\f` inside the bar was too short to draw, and the parts build reported "crescendo too small" three times. The parts printed "mf f" with no hairpin, so the crescendo of the dominant preparation into A was missing from three wind parts. The score is not affected.
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 and Example 7 (PDF 12): marks placed in the score do not survive extraction and must be proofread in the parts.
**Fix.** In intro.ily, before the hairpin note in `introFluteOne`, `introFluteTwo` and `introOboeTwo`: `\tag #'part { \once \override Staff.Hairpin.minimum-length = #6 \once \override Staff.Hairpin.springs-and-rods = #ly:spanner::set-spacing-rods }`. I first put this in `\global`, but it split every part's multi-bar rest 1–10 into 1–9 + 10, so it now sits in the three instruments only. The parts build has 0 warnings.

### 5. Harp part, page 1: three systems spread over a whole page — not applied
**Problem.** The harp's page 1 (bars 1–66; the turn falls in the rest before E) has three systems with gaps of about 15 staff spaces.
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 7: an even amount of music per system, so the eye travels at a steady speed.
**Decision.** I tested `ragged-bottom = ##t`. It sent the part from 4 to 6 pages, because the page-turn breaker then fills pages differently. Two extra pages and two extra turns cost more than white space, so the v315 layout stays.

### 6. Comments say "parts only" for `\tag #'part` material in `\global`, but the score reads it too — documentation, no change
**Problem.** The comments at c235 (81), c255 (197, 221) and in my Finding 1 assumed that `\tag #'part` in global.ily reaches only the parts. score.ly uses `\global` without `\removeWithTag #'part`, so those MetronomeMark paddings also act in the score. They are harmless there and the score has always been built with them. A future part-only change to `\global` should use the `isPartsBook` flag from Finding 1, or score.ly should read `\removeWithTag #'part \global`. I did not change score.ly, because that would move the score's tempo texts at 81, 197 and 221.
