## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 135-139: every voice of the diminished-seventh chain falls, and the cellos only double the basses (applied)

**Problem.** The retransition opens with four bars of diminished sevenths, pp < into the sf stop chord at 138. Every line moves down by semitone in parallel: Vln I A♭5-G5-G♭5, Vln II F5-E5-E♭5, both viola lines, Cl 1·2, Bn 1·2, Hn 3·4 (137-138), and the bass D-D♭-C-B, which the cellos double at the octave. The crescendo is carried by dynamics and by added groups (c308), not by any line. The chain is the only build in the movement with no rising voice. The other builds all have one: 9-10 and 89-90 (violin runs), 129-131, 167-169 (Bn 1 E♭-F-F♯-G since c362), 219-222 (D♯→E) and 244-246.

**Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 147, on Ex. 198: "The gradual rise of the ensemble up to the high climactic cadence ... is helped considerably by the constant linear movement—at least one voice is always moving upward." Rule 7, PDF 140: the lowest cello does not need to double the bass; doing it "out of habit" grounds the sound. PDF 145-146 (Ex. 195-196, images): the second cello adds a chord tone "for temporary richness", and the extra note is dropped "as soon as this imbalance is resolved" (economy). PDF 149 (Ex. 200, image): the bottom cello gets "melodic motion", and the cellos cross so that neither has an awkward line. PDF 159 (Ex. 207, image): a divided cello line carries "important melodic passing" notes. The score has no divided-cello line that moves; c267's division at 43-50 holds a fifth.

**Fix (applied).** In each chord one tone lies a whole step above a tone of the previous chord: D (B°7/D) → E (C♯°7) → G♭ (C°7) → A♭ (B°7). So half the cellos can leave the bass at 136 and rise against it, a wedge that opens out of the unison. At 139 they take the stop chord's A♭3 down to G3 with Hn 4, the 9-8 that c172 placed there, and join the others for the F1 head at 140. retr.ily:

```
retrVc = { d1\pp\<\upbow | <des e>1^\markup \italic "div." | <c ges>1 | <b, aes>1\sf\> | <b, g>1\p | g2->(^\markup \italic "unis." d4. es8 | ...
```

(was `d1 | des1 | c1 | b,1 | b,1 | g2->( ...`; a c367 comment is added above "RETRANSITION").
