# Cycle 4, critic C (holistic comparison), on the c03 render

Lens: listen through the data as a conductor would; compare c03 with the c00 port baseline (five cycles back does not exist yet), with Movement I's latest finished render on the same tools (symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/cycles/c44) and with the reference recordings. Dan's standing complaint is that the renders sound wet and mushy; the question is whether c03 fixed it and what mush is left. Scripts ran on the c03 stems and master against `cycles/c03/rendered_build/parts.json` (its tempo map for every render; c00-c02 differ only at qn 880-888).

## Verdict on "wet" (measured)

c03 fixed the wet part. Master: fermata RT(-5..-25 dB) 1.31 s, late RT 1.98 s, drop 0.3 s after the cut-off 18.5 dB (recordings 1.1-1.6 s and 13-17 dB; Movement I c44 1.29 s). Stem phrase ends (notes followed by at least 0.4 s of rest, no other note of the track sounding; drop 0.15 s after the note-off, median), c00 -> c03: strings on Sus MAX 5.5 -> 12.5 dB (time to -20 dB 0.42 -> 0.24 s), brass Sus MAX 7.0 -> 10.1 dB (0.43 -> 0.27 s), winds Sus MAX 9.7 -> 13.3 dB (0.33 -> 0.23 s), wind legato rows 8.9-10.3 -> 13.4-16.6 dB. The loud-tutti tone colour is also on target now: spectral centroid of the loudest 10% of frames 1019 Hz (c00) -> 878 Hz (c03), against 870 / 818 Hz in Brahms 1/i and 4/i (c01 critic C) and 879 Hz on Movement I c44. The thicker tutti since c00 is the c01 loud_eq doing its job, not mush; leave it.

The mush that is left is in the time domain: notes that end on the string legato and tremolo rows still ring as long as they did in c00, the samples' own release trails still carry a lot of recorded room, the strings arrive late against the winds and brass in tutti attacks, and the re-bows on held string notes became holes when the release cap went in. As a single number: the master's envelope modulation at 2-16 Hz (3 s windows, louder half of the piece) rose only 0.1-0.5 dB from c00 to c03 and is still 1.3-2.2 dB under the Dvořák 9/i recording in all three bands, 150-500 Hz, 0.5-2 kHz and 2-6 kHz (different piece, so this is inferred, not a target).

## Findings, ranked by what a listener would hear

### 1. String phrase ends on the legato and tremolo rows still ring for 0.35-0.45 s: the release cap missed about 80% of the string phrase ends (measured)

Where: every string track. Most phrase ends, among them the slurred tune ends at qn 192, 452, 676, 1060 and 1068 (Violins II), 676 and 1060 (Violins I), 452 (Violas) and 1076 (Violoncellos); the chorale tremolos from bar 223 (qn 888-936); the closing tremolos at 273-279.

What is wrong: c03 capped only the Sus MAX and Sus Marc LITE sustain groups (mix.json `sus_release_cap_ms` rows). In parts.json, of the string notes that are followed by at least 0.3 s of rest, 475 end on Leg Slur MAX and 322 on Leg BC MAX, against 192 on Sus MAX. So about 81% of the string phrase ends kept the patches' release. Read in the patch files, the legato-row sustain groups ship 394-931 ms on the 2nd Violins and Violas, 333-931 ms on the Celli and 316-931 ms on the Basses; the Tremolo groups ship 131-700 ms on the 2nd Violins, 524 ms on the Violas and Celli and 630 ms on the Basses. The patches' own fast slur groups use 133 ms. `build_reaper.cap_release` also skips any group with a region on a row that is not listed, so sustain groups shared by Sus MAX and a legato row were not capped either.

Evidence (c03, drop at 0.15 s / time to -20 dB, medians): Violins II Leg Slur MAX 2.4 dB / 0.41 s (n 6; c02 5.4), Violins I Leg Slur 5.5 dB, Double basses Leg Slur 4.0 dB / 1.3 s, Violoncellos Leg BC 7.3 dB / 0.45 s (c02 7.2); Tremolo: Violins II 4.2, Violas 3.9, Basses 3.3, Celli -0.4 dB (c02 2.5 / 5.2 / 5.3 / -2.6). On the same tracks, Sus MAX ends moved by 5-10 dB (Violins I 6.3 -> 16.5, Violins II 4.5 -> 10.0, Basses 7.2 -> 15.0, Celli 4.4 -> 10.6). Strings over all: Leg Slur MAX 4.9 dB in c00, 4.0 dB in c03; Tremolo 4.5 -> 4.0 dB. The long legato release also keeps the old pitch under the next note in running legato lines (inferred from Movement I c44: the old pitch 70 ms into the next viola eighth fell from -3.6 to -6.2 dB with the Sus cap). That is the "smear" a listener hears inside slurred string passages.

Fix: `build/mix.json`, on Violins I, Violins II, Violas, Violoncellos and Double basses, set `sus_release_cap_ms` to `{"ms": 150, "dyn": ["pp","p","mf","f","ff"], "rows": ["Sus MAX","Sus Marc LITE","Leg Slur MAX","Leg BC MAX","Leg Port MAX","Tremolo"]}`. `cap_release` already leaves the "TLeg" transition groups and the "RT" release trails alone, and listing every legato row together also catches the shared groups. This is an Opus state change, so all five string tracks need a full rebuild (the c03 horn level rows are still missing in REAPER, see CARRYOVER.md, so the horns need a rebuild or a `--midi-only` write as well). Checks on the c04 render: phrase-end drops of at least 10 dB at 0.15 s on the legato and tremolo rows, and no ending falling more than 10 dB in its first 0.05 s; legato join dips and pcX (`actor_scripts/m03.py dips|pcx`) no more than 1 dB / 10 ms worse than c03 on the common joins. If joins dip, use 237 ms on the legato rows only.

### 2. The a2 horns, flutes, trumpets and trombones are one player pushed 3 dB, not two players (measured scoring and patches; gain inferred, Movement I measured)

Where: Horns 1-2 a2 for 96 s, including the F1 tune at bars 11-26 and 148-166 and the Più allegro at 247-268 (Horn 2 has 192 notes dropped); Horns 3-4 for 44 s (27-34, 145-150, 255-258, 269-272); Trumpets 1-2 at 255-276; Trombones 1-2 at 205-206, 224-228 and 247-254.

What is wrong: manual p.10-11. A unison pair has two players' beating and a wider, rounder tone. The mockup drops one copy and raises the other by 10·log10(2) = +3 dB on the solo KS Master, so the horn tune in F1 and the a2 chorale lines sound like one horn played louder. Movement I replaced exactly this with a "Horns a2" track on the 2FH KS Master (c13; config in symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/build/score_config.py lines 620-633: `TRACKS` entry, `TRACK_WINDOWS` with the a2 windows, Horn 1's rules copied into the window). Its measured lessons: 2FH plays +2.4..+3.9 dB over the boosted solo horn at f/ff, so set its level per dynamic from its first render (Movement I LEARNINGS c13). It is 5-15 dB brighter above 1.6 kHz, which a static 1.25 kHz -3 dB shelf plus a 1.6 kHz -8 dB shelf switched on at CC1 108 and above corrected (c17). It cost 43 -> 45 GB (c13).

Fix: a "Horns a2" track on `/Volumes/MAC DRIVE/EW Hollywood Brass Diamond/Hollywood Brass Opus Instruments/2 French Horns/06 Keyswitch/2FH KS Master.oib` for the Hn 1/2 a2 windows listed above (build `_A2W` from mockup-events.tsv: spans where every Hn1 and Hn2 onset shares pitch and time). Copy Movement I's lines 620-633 and its c17 shelves (mix.json "Horns a2" eq/loud_eq), with `sus_release_cap_ms` as Horn 1. c03 settled at 44 GB, so render it in the second pass (`build_reaper.py --pass2`, `PASS2_TRACKS`), as Movement I does. If a second pass is set up, the trumpet a2 span at 255-276 on 2TP KS Master is the next candidate (25 s at the climax). Measure: the a2 windows' level against c03's Horn 1 in 0.5 s windows per dynamic, with per-window roles to within ±1 dB; the 2-5 kHz front of the a2 accents under +3 dB over the body.

### 3. The patches' release trails still add 8-12 dB of recorded room to each string, horn and trombone note-off; port Movement I's `rt_gain_db` (patch values measured; effect to test)

Where: every Hollywood Strings section and the 1FH and 1TB tracks; heard most in the loud detached passages (B, K, N, R) and at every string cut-off.

What is wrong: p.222: the room is heard "as long as the samples are playing (including the release trail samples)". With the hall halved and shortened, the recorded release trails (`releaseTrail: true` groups) are the largest thing left after a note-off. Their `SourceProcessor` gains on the rows in use, read from the patches: 2nd Violins +7.7..+12.0 dB, Violas +4.6..+12.0, Celli up to +12.0 on the legato rows (-1.6..+5.8 on Sus MAX), Violins I Tremolo +12 (volume 3.98), Horn 1 +2.7..+10.1, Trombone 1 Leg Slur MAX +3.9..+12.0. The Trumpets (-4.7..-0.5), Flutes (-0.5) and Basses (-0.4) are already at or under 0 dB. The trail length is not set by its AHDSR (Movement I c22), so the cap in finding 1 does not touch these. Movement I c45 critic C measured that the trails set each family's early tail (0.15-0.25 s after phrase ends: horns -9.8, strings -13.5, winds -15.4, Trumpets 1-3 -16.0 dB re body) and the old pitch inside string sections (-10 dB 0.25 s after the note-off). It added a `rt_gain_db` mix key (symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/tools/build_reaper.py, `rt_gain`, line 267, applied at line 328) and is testing -6 dB in its pass 2. The result is not in yet.

Fix: copy `rt_gain` and its call into this folder's `tools/build_reaper.py` (generic, off by default). When Movement I's c45 test passes, set `"rt_gain_db": {"db": -6}` on Violins I, Violins II, Violas, Violoncellos and Horns 1-4 (and "Horns a2"), and `{"db": -3}` on Trombones 1-2 and the bass trombone, in the same full rebuild as finding 1. If the result is not in when c04 builds, put one alias test track in the pass-2 session ("Vln II rt test", -6 dB) and judge it there. Measure: stem level 0.15-0.25 s after phrase ends (target: strings and horns within 2 dB of the winds' -15 dB), the old pitch 0.1-0.25 s after a note-off in the detached Violins II / Violas eighths, and the body level of sustained notes within 0.5 dB (the trails must not have been carrying the sustain). A further lever, inferred and untested: the strings still use the factory Main-heavy mix (Close 0.66 / Main 1.0, `groups.string.mics` null). Shifting it toward Close would also dry the sound (p.222), but it changes balance everywhere, so leave it until 1 and 3 are measured.

### 4. The strings arrive late against the winds and brass at tutti attacks (measured with a coarse estimator; cause measured by the c03 actor)

Where: the 186 onsets where 12 or more wind, brass or string tracks start a note together; worst at qn 106, 610, 626, 646 and 81 (bars 27, 153, 157, 162, 21).

What is wrong: a late, soft string front under a punctual brass attack is heard as a smeared attack: the tutti seems to start twice. Per stem, the time of the steepest 5 ms rise within -0.12..+0.25 s of the score onset: strings minus winds and brass, median +27 ms, p75 +84 ms, p90 +130 ms. Spread within each family: strings IQR 145 ms, brass 50, winds 65. The estimator is crude: notes that continue under a legato join have no clear rise, so read these as an order of magnitude. The c03 actor's pcX numbers point to the same cause: the 16 Violins I and 15 Violins II new joins after slurred eighths sit at a median +85 / +128 ms (the lead is capped at 0.75 of the eighth), entries at 600 are +51..+72 ms, Violas 1048 +255 ms, Basses 900 +170 ms, and the attack probe flags the Violins I join spread (84 ms against 75).

Fix: c03's deferred C #4, the per-onset string legato leads from pcX, refit on the c03 render into `build/leg_leads.json` for the joins more than 40 ms late. Raise the 0.75-of-the-eighth cap on `SHORT_SUS_DETACH_JOIN_LEG` / `SHORT_SUS_JOIN` leads to 0.9 where the predecessor is a slurred eighth at 168 qpm or slower. Add Violas 1048 and Basses 900 to `entry_leads.json`. Target: pcX median of the new joins within ±30 ms, Violins I rSD at most 75 ms, and the tutti strings-minus-winds/brass median within ±15 ms.

### 5. The re-bows on held string pedals turned into 8-16 dB holes after the release cap, and nobody refitted them (measured)

Where: Violoncellos bars 43-49 (re-bows qn 172.6, 178.95, 185.3, 192.3, p-pp under the motive x solos), Violins I 188.8, Double basses 170.9, 560.0 and 577.4 (bars 43-48, 141-145), and the chorale Violoncellos 920.0, 924.6 and 929.2 (bars 231-233).

What is wrong: `build/rebow_fills.json` is empty, so the automatic re-bows (abutting Sus MAX pieces 0.03 qn apart) have no level shapes. With the 150 ms cap, the old piece now dies before the new soft Sus MAX attack arrives. Stem dips (reference: the mean of the levels before and after, 10 ms frames), c02 -> c03: Violoncellos 172.6 / 178.95 / 185.3 / 192.3 5.6 / 3.1 / 0.5 / 7.5 -> 15.1 / 15.6 / 14.0 / 9.0 dB, each 60-80 ms more than 6 dB down; Violins I 188.8 4.0 -> 13.8; Basses 170.9 1.1 -> 11.9, 560.0 6.6 -> 10.1, 577.4 -0.8 -> 8.5; chorale celli 920.0 / 924.6 / 929.2 6.9 / 4.3 / 2.8 -> 10.4 / 8.0 / 7.5. Most of the bar 43-49 holes are masked on the master (0.1-2.4 dB), but 192.3 reaches 7.8 dB for 30 ms on the master (c02 5.8). Movement I c44 measured the same effect (re-bow dips 1.7-4.4 -> 5.4-7.7 dB after its cap) and its refit brought them to 3.1-5.4 dB.

Fix: run `tools/fit_rebows.py 3 cycles/c03/rendered_build/parts.json --write` now (the fit reads the c03 stems; no new render needed), then score_to_parts.py. Check the clamped re-bows in its summary. If the 60-80 ms holes at p stay over 6 dB, raise the re-bow attack for those pieces with `REBOW_VEL_BY_CC` at CC 46 52 -> 66 rather than a deeper fill. Finding 1 does not change the re-bows (they are Sus MAX), but refit again on c04 if the legato-row cap changes the neighbouring notes. Target: every listed re-bow under 6 dB on its stem and under 3 dB on the master.
