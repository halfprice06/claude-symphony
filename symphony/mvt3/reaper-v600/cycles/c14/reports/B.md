### 2. First theme, first statement (bar 11): the head note is 6-10 dB over the rest of its phrase (measured; one part is a drifted note)

Where: Violins I, Violins II, Violas, qn 40-44 (bar 11); also Violins II at qn 72-75.5 (bar 19) and 600-603.5 (bar 151).

What is wrong: the theme's accented C falls to its second note, the G, by 6.5 dB on Violins I, 5.7 on Violins II and 5.9 on Violas (A-weighted averages of each note's slot), 4.8 dB on the master. Evidence for the causes. (a) The envelope: `vol_db` on the three tracks rises from +3.1 dB at 40.0 to +5.4 at 40.2 and returns to 0 at 41.68; 50 ms levels of the Violins I stem from 0.1 s before qn 40: -46 -43 -38 -31 -32 -35 -35 -35 -30 -28 -30 -35 -35 -39 -42 -42 -44 -44. Targets: head-to-second-note fall of 3-4 dB on each string stem at bar 11 and on Violins II at bars 19 and 151 (A-weighted slot averages; now 5.7-9.5), master fall at bar 11 of 3.5 dB or less (now 4.8). Do not touch the recapitulation's Violins I and Violas (3.3 / 3.4 dB).
