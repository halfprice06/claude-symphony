# Critic C, cycle 1 (blend and space), on the c00 render

Lens: one room. Reverb, seating, mics, tone matching between sample sets, low end against references. Cycle 0 is the port baseline, so these are the problems a listener would notice most, ranked by audible gain. Scripts measured the c00 stems and master (sum of stems matches the master); references are the stored metrics of Brahms 1/i and 4/i (Musopen, lossless, the closest genre: fast loud movements, Brahms 1 also C minor with contrabassoon) and Brahms 1/ii; the Dvořák MP3 only where noted.

## Findings

### 1. The loud tutti is thin and top-heavy: Movement I's loud-tutti body EQ and bass-octave rows were dropped in the port (measured)

Where: every ff/fff tutti; bars with 12 or more staves at ff/fff (from `mockup-events.tsv`): 1-2, 5-6, 27-32, 35, 91-94, 110-113, 147-148, 150-166, 199-204, 211, 223, 248-254, 259-279 (qn 0-8, 16-24, 104-128, 136-140, 360-376, 436-452, 584-592, 596-664, 792-816, 840-844, 888-892, 988-1016, 1032-1116). All tracks below 300 Hz plus Violins I/II.

What is wrong: at the climaxes the master is made of violins (and flutes) with little 100-250 Hz body, which is the opposite of how an orchestra's spectrum moves with level. Movement I solved exactly this in c16-c25 with `loud_eq` (wet-automated ReaEQs) and +3 dB bass-octave role rows (KIT §14); the port removed both because they were score-timed (PORT.md), and nothing replaced them.

Evidence (master, 8192-point frames): spectral centroid of the loudest 10% of frames 1007 Hz against 870 / 818 Hz in Brahms 1/i and 4/i (Movement I after its fix: 853). Loud-minus-soft spectral shape at 157 Hz -5.0 dB against +2.8 / +4.2 dB in the references (at 79 Hz -7.7 against -4.7 / -0.9; at 2.5 kHz +3.3 against +6.3 / +4.1). Loudest 15% of frames: 100-250 Hz share -11.4 dB, 20-100 Hz -13.7 dB, 500-2000 Hz -2.0 dB; Brahms 1/i and 4/i over their whole length: -7.4 / -7.1, -8.9 / -8.8, -3.7 / -4.2. In the loud frames Violins I alone make 41% of the master's 2-5 kHz and 31% of 6-20 kHz; Violoncellos make 72% of 20-100 Hz (their G2/F2 fundamentals at 87-98 Hz), Double basses only 20%. At the bar-223 fff chord (qn 888.3-891.8) 2-5 kHz holds 25% of the master's energy (-6.1 dB re total) and the trumpets' C-major chord sits 13.7 dB (A-weighted) under the master. A plain stem simulation (zero-phase FFT magnitude of the EQs below, +3 dB roles, both inside the spans above, summed in STFT frames) gives: loud-10% centroid 1010 -> 857 Hz, 157 Hz loud-minus-soft -5.0 -> -1.0 dB, loud-15% 100-250 Hz share -11.3 -> -7.7 dB, 2-6 kHz -9.7 -> -11.1 dB, loudest frame +0.3 dB (measured simulation, not a render).

Fix (`build/mix.json` `loud_eq`, `build/roles.json`): port Movement I's c42 `loud_eq` bands with Finale points (Wet 0 -> 1 over the 2 qn before each span start, 1 -> 0 over the 2 qn after its end; for the span at qn 0 start at 1 from the pre-roll): Violoncellos bell 150 Hz +6 dB 1 oct; Double basses bell 160 Hz +6 dB 1 oct plus bell2 98 Hz -4 dB 0.5 oct; Bassoon 2 bell 140 Hz +3 dB 1 oct; Contrabassoon, Bass trombone, Tuba bell 140 Hz +4 dB 1 oct; Timpani bell 140 Hz +3 dB 1 oct; Violins I and II high shelf 2.5 kHz -2.5 dB. Add role rows of +3 dB on Double basses, Contrabassoon, Bassoon 2, Tuba, Bass trombone and +2 dB on Bass clarinet over the same spans, starting 0.5 qn before each ff downbeat (not a bar early: KIT §14 c23) and opening over 4 qn at the end (KIT §13). Leave Timpani out of the roles (Dan's roll rule). Targets on the render: loud-10% centroid 820-900 Hz, 157 Hz loud-minus-soft at 0 dB or above, delivery limiter still at most about 3 dB, the climax order (223, 271-279) unchanged.

### 2. The bass octave is uneven and the tonic C2 is thinned by Movement I's pitch bells (measured)

Where: Double basses throughout (their most-played pitch is C2, 175 qn; then G1 110, A♭1 89, B♭1 89, D2 84, B1 65, E♭2 58, F2 54 qn), and the C-major arrivals (qn 888, 1048, 1108-1112) where basses, tuba and bassoon 2 hold C2 and the contrabassoon C1.

What is wrong: the basses' static bells in `mix.json` (`mute_eq` 61.7 Hz -6 dB and 73.4 Hz -5 dB at 0.25 oct, `eq` bell2 87.3 Hz -10 dB at 0.15 oct) were fitted on Movement I's hot B1, D2 and F2. Their skirts take about 6 dB off C2 (65.4 Hz) and C♯2, so in this C-minor/C-major piece the tonic is weaker than the notes the bells were meant to tame. The tuba's 70 Hz low shelf -4 dB and 84 Hz bell -5 dB (Movement I's E2/F2 fix) also land on its C2 (85 qn).

Evidence: fundamental minus harmonics 2-4 on sustained Double basses notes (onset +0.12 s, up to 1.5 s): C2 -8.7 dB, D2 -6.5, E♭2 -10.4, B1 -4.4, E2 -3.4, F2 -1.2 (still the hottest after a -10 dB bell), G1 -20.3, A♭1 -20.8, B♭1 -14.3. Removing the current EQ's computed response gives the raw sample values B1 +5.2, C2 -0.2, D2 +2.6, E2 +5.2, F2 +11.8, so C2 is not a hot pitch and loses 6.0 dB to bells aimed at its neighbours. Tuba C2 fundamental -18.5 dB under its harmonics. Master third-octave bands re total: 62.5 Hz -30.1, 78.7 Hz -25.6 dB against -12.3 / -12.7 and -16.8 / -13.2 in Brahms 1/i and 4/i (the references' 62.5 Hz band may hold mains hum, so read 78.7 Hz as the safer number: 9-12 dB low).

Fix (`build/mix.json`, Double basses): narrow and re-centre the bells: `mute_eq` 61.7 Hz -5 dB at 0.12 oct and 73.4 Hz -4 dB at 0.12 oct, `eq` bell2 87.3 Hz -12 dB at 0.15 oct. Computed on the measured per-pitch values this gives B1 -2.7, C2 -5.1, C♯2 -3.3, D2 -4.9, E2 -2.6, F2 -2.1 dB (C2 +3.6 dB, every pitch from B1 to F2 within ±1.5 dB of -3.5). Tuba: drop the 70 Hz low shelf (keep the 84 Hz bell, narrowed to 0.2 oct). Check on the render that each sustained bass pitch from B1 to F2 sits within ±3 dB of the median fundamental-minus-harmonics, and that the loud-frame 20-100 Hz share stays between -14.5 and -11 dB.

### 3. The woodwinds sound nearer than the strings and brass; one doubled line plays at two depths; Clarinet 2 wanders (measured; fix partly inferred)

Where: all woodwinds, most exposed in the solos (Oboe 1 "solo, espress." qn 376, Bassoon 1 solo qn 456, the motive x of Flute 1 / Clarinet 1 / Oboe 1 qn 168-200) and the cor anglais doubling Trumpet 1 con sordino (qn 680-731).

What is wrong: with Close 0.5 / Main 0.8 the woodwind KS Masters stay Close-dominated (factory Main 11-40 dB under Close, KIT §12), so the winds keep the least room of any section and sit in front of the strings. The cor anglais has no send override (0.35, against 0.5 for flutes and oboes) and is the driest track, while the muted trumpet it doubles sits at the trumpets' 0.65 send. Clarinet 2 has no `width` entry, so it keeps the wind group's 0.5 (Opus pans -0.70..+0.30) while Clarinet 1 has 0.05.

Evidence: early-tail drop (body 0.4-0.1 s before a note end minus the level 0.15-0.25 s after it, notes of 1 qn or more followed by 0.8 s of rest), median per stem: Cor anglais 16.1 dB, Clarinet 1 14.8, Bassoons 14.4-14.6, Oboes 13.6-14.1, Clarinet 2 13.4, Flutes 11.6-11.9; Violins I/II 10.1-10.4, Violas 12.6, Celli/Basses 9.1-9.2, Horns 9.5-10.5, Trumpets 10.7-12.4, Trombones 10.8 (a larger drop means drier). In the doubling, Trumpet 1's phrase ends drop 8.2-8.9 dB and the cor anglais's 15.8 dB. Per-pitch left-right offset (400-1000 Hz) SD: Clarinet 2 4.6 dB against Clarinet 1 2.2 (Movement I c25 measured the same narrowing effect: 4.2-6.0 -> 2.4-3.7 dB).

Fix: (a) `build/mix.json` `tracks` "Clarinet 2": add `"width": 0.05` (as Clarinet 1); "Cor anglais": add `"send": 0.5` (as the oboes). Both are Opus state changes, so the two tracks get a full rebuild. (b) MANUAL-GAPS #4 (manual p.138): one A/B in this render on Oboe 1, Clarinet 1 and Cor anglais with `"mics": {"Mid": 0.7, "Main": 0.8}` (Close unloaded; a swap, about neutral in memory), gains re-matched to the old K-weighted level per dynamic, then the soft_trim/dyn_trim fits checked. Target: woodwind early-tail drops within 2 dB of the strings' 10-12.6 dB, and the solos' 2-5 kHz presence over the strings no lower than now. If it works, extend it to the whole wind group in c02.

### 4. At the fff chords the flutes are the loudest instruments in the orchestra (measured)

Where: bar 223 (qn 888-892; Flute 1 C7, Flute 2 G6, fff), bars 278-279 (qn 1108-1113; Flute 1 G6, Flute 2 E6, fff). Flute 1, Flute 2.

What is wrong: one sample set sticks out of the tutti. A flute at fff in its top register adds brilliance in a full orchestra, but a listener should hear the brass C-major chord and the violins as the body, not the flute over the trumpets. `mix.json` gives the flutes `dyn_trim_db` f -4, ff -1 and no fff entry, so fff plays the top layer at full gain.

Evidence (A-weighted levels re the master over each bar): bar 278 Flute 1 -7.7 dB (the loudest stem; Violins I -8.1, Trumpet 3 -11.5, trombones -12.6); bar 279 Flute 1 -7.9 (loudest; Trumpet 3 -9.6, Trumpet 1 -12.3); bar 223 Flute 2 -7.3 (second after the violins; Flute 1 -13.1, Trumpet 3 -13.7). Flute 1 is also among the three loudest stems at bars 222 and 278-279 in `metrics.md`.

Fix: `build/mix.json` Flute 1 and Flute 2 `dyn_trim_db` add `"fff": -5` (the flutes' fff spans are only qn 888-900, 1048-1056.5 and 1108-end, so nothing else moves; at 1048-1056 Flute 2 is already 11 dB A under the master, so check that span does not drop out); if the critics B/C of c02 still find Flute 1 the top stem at 278-279, add a `note_trims.json` entry of -2 dB on the qn 1108 note. Target: at 223 and 278-279 each flute at least 3 dB A under the loudest violin section and no louder than the loudest trumpet.

### 5. The con sordino trumpet is muffled rather than muted, so its colour disappears into the cor anglais (measured; fix inferred)

Where: Trumpet 1 qn 680-731 (bars 171-183, "con sord., dolce cantabile", unison with the cor anglais; `ART_SPANS` "Mute Sus").

What is wrong: every layer of the 1TP mute rows runs through a fixed "Bandpass Filter" 90 Hz-3 kHz (read in `1TP KS Master.oib` and `1TP Mute Sus.oib`; no open 1TP patch has one). A straight mute makes the trumpet thin and nasal with strong energy at 1.5-5 kHz; with the top cut at 3 kHz the line sounds like a distant, dull horn. The printed doubling (two colours on one melody) becomes one darker cor anglais.

Evidence (per sustained note, onset +0.1 s; 24 muted notes): 5-10 kHz minus 0.5-2 kHz -66.5 dB against -48.7 dB for the same track's open p solo at qn 200-264 (18 dB darker) and -43.3 dB for the cor anglais; 2-5 kHz minus 0.5-2 kHz -21.5 against -18.5 (open p) and -17.7 (cor anglais). Level 3.3 dB A under the cor anglais (median over the unison notes). Its early tail is 7 dB wetter than the cor anglais (finding 3).

Fix: give `build_reaper.py`'s Opus state edit (the mechanism that sets the harp's `env_release_ms`) a per-row option, e.g. `mix.json` "Trumpet 1": `"bandpass": {"rows": ["Mute Sus"], "low_hz": 250, "high_hz": 8000}`, which rewrites the Bandpass `low`/`high` on the Mute Sus layers only (a full rebuild of Trumpet 1). Fallback without a state edit: a switched REAPER EQ on Trumpet 1 (a `loud_eq` spec with points 679.5 -> 1, 731.5 -> 0): high shelf 2.5 kHz +6 dB and a bell 400 Hz -3 dB 1 oct. Target: muted 2-5 kHz minus 0.5-2 kHz at or above the cor anglais's (-17.7 dB), 5-10 kHz within 10 dB of the open p trumpet, level kept 2-4 dB A under the cor anglais.

### 6. The timpani play everything on hard sticks, but the score asks for soft sticks at 178 and medium sticks for the rolls of 219-246 (measured; score read)

Where: Timpani qn 708-711 (bar 178, pp "near the rim", "soft sticks" printed at 171) and qn 872-984 (bars 219-246: the sub. p roll into the fff arrival, the chorale's rolls f -> p at 230-233 and the p -> f rolls at 243-246; "medium sticks" printed in the rest at 215, "hard sticks" again at 255; `sections/codaTwo.ily`, `recS.ily`). `build/score_config.py` puts the whole part on `Timpani Hrd KS` (the port read only the bar-1 "hard sticks").

What is wrong: hard-stick samples at p give a bright, rattling roll under the soft brass chorale where the composer wanted a rounder one, which is also the kind of roll Dan hears as covering the texture.

Evidence: 1-8 kHz share of the timpani stem in the medium-stick rolls -15.5..-17.1 dB, the same as in the hard-stick f rolls at 260-262 and 273-275 (-14.7..-17.1); Movement I's felt rolls measured -38 (ppp) to -22 (f) on the same kind of share (Movement I LEARNINGS c12). The bar-219 sub. p roll sits only 11.0 dB under the master; 178's pp figure 18 dB under.

Fix: the library has felt but no medium sticks (manual p.123-126; `Timpani Flt KS.oib` has the same rows and keys as Hrd KS). Best: a felt timpani track for qn 707.5-711.5 and 871.5-985 in the pass-2 session (`build_reaper.py --pass2`, `TRACK_WINDOWS` excluding those spans from the main Timpani track), with Movement I's felt `TIMP_CC1_MAP` / `TIMP_ROLL_TRIM_DB` (they were fitted on felt). Cheaper interim: a switched EQ on Timpani (`loud_eq` points 707.5 -> 1, 711.5 -> 0, 871.5 -> 1, 985 -> 0): high shelf 1.5 kHz -6 dB. Target: 1-8 kHz share of those rolls 5-8 dB under the hard-stick rolls at the same dynamic, and the rolls at least 3 dB under the quietest sustained section after their first beat (BRIEF item 5).

Lower priority (not ranked): the Violins I "4 soli" at qn 473-511 play on the full section with -3 dB (`SOLI_AS_META`) and keep the section's full image (stem correlation 0.38, side/mid -2.2 dB, the same as its tutti passages); a `side_shelf_switched` side cut over 472.5-511 or the DIV A patch in pass 2 would make it sound like a smaller group. Hall length (above) is close enough to the reference for now. No loop pulse found on the master in the chorale (qn 892-936) or the closing chords (1088-1112): 100-300 Hz band and centroid autocorrelation at 3.95 s -0.05/-0.20 and 0.06/-0.08, as low as a control passage.

Do not touch: the shared true-stereo hall (one IR, fx 0.19, wet 2.0, same in every instance) and the seats as set in Opus (Violins I -7.9 dB R-L, celli +3.5, basses +4.9, horns left, low brass right; master correlation 0.57, 250 Hz-4 kHz 0.57, close to the references' 0.47-0.55); the side-only low and 170 Hz shelves on the bass tracks (master correlation 0.56 at 120-250 Hz, references 0.54-0.60); the crash cymbals and bass drum (crash adds 5-12 dB at 5-16 kHz at qn 888, 1048, 1108; bass drum adds 9-10 dB at 30-80 Hz at 888 and 1108; both recorded with Close 0.3 / Main 1.0 and send 0.45); the sfp at qn 592 done in CC11/CC1 inside Opus (the hall rings on).
