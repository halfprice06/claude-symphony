## Cyclic rules applied in v01 (do not undo without answering the reason)
- No root-position V–i or V–I cadence in C before the last cadence at 254–255. Earlier cadences in C are inverted, deceptive (26, 186) or plagal (227–230).
- Trumpets first enter at 51 with the soft solo; tacet 1–50.
- String tremolo and the violins' C7 only at the arrival (219 onward).
- The stop chord B–D–F–A♭ (138, 218) resolves to C only at 218→219, trumpets B→C coupled with the timpani.
- The E♭/E♮ thread closes in the violas (D♯→E at 215–216).
- No tam-tam. Hn 3·4 "senza sord." at their first entry (they end movement II muted). The first timpani label gives the retuning.
- Each owed item from cyclic-notes sounds once: soft trumpet melody (51), arrival (219), chorale (219–230 and 251–255), S head in C major (231), brass combination of heads (243), hammered tutti (199), horn call in the trumpets (239–242), E♭/E♮ thread (215–216).
