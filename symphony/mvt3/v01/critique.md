# v01 composer's notes

First draft, written from cyclic-notes.md "Start here for movement III" and movement-3-plan.md. 262 bars, 2/2, about 6:40. Build clean: 0 warnings, 0 outer-voice parallels, 16 pages.

Known weaknesses, for critics to check:
- Many sections were generated from short patterns, so the accompaniment figures repeat for long stretches (clarinet broken chords in P1, hammered eighths in the finale). The inner voices need more independent lines.
- The development (99–134) is a chain of episodes (call and response, lament bass, bassoon solo, harp solo); its build to the retransition may be too even.
- The coda has four stages after the recapitulation (199–262, 64 bars, about a quarter of the movement). Check that the Maestoso arrival, the Poco più mosso second-theme head and the Allegro molto do not stop and start too often.
- The first theme F1 has not been tested against strong models yet; its second bar repeats the falling step pattern of the first.
- Trumpet 1 reaches C6 at 239 and in the finale (analyser limit B♭5); I accepted this for C trumpet.
- Dynamics and articulation were set section by section and have not been checked across joins.
- Percussion is used only at the arrival and the finale.
