# v221 — critic 201 (parts layout: page turns at the attacca) — parts only, score unchanged

Report: critics/reports/c201.md

Asked for by the movement II thread. Players who hold a note into movement II bar 1 (Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc, Hn 3), or who enter there or soon after (Hn 4, Cb, timpani), had a page turn inside the held note or just before the entry. Each of these parts now ends movement I on an even, left-hand page, facing movement II's page 1. Every earlier turn falls in a rest (parts-gen.py only):
- Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc: 2 pages, no cover page. Turns in rests at 84–93, 103–106, 103–106 and 107–109. The viola part uses slightly closer spacing.
- Cb: cover page, music on pages 2–4, turn in the rest at 171–174.
- Hn 3, Hn 4, timpani: cover page, music on page 2, no turn.
Sources: MOLA PDF 5–7; Gould PDF 9–10; Finale Smart Page Turns manual; NJ Symphony copy of MOLA p. 5.

Score: unchanged music, 18 pages. Subtitle reads "v221" (promote.sh had re-added "draft"; removed by hand). Parts rc=0, 30 PDFs. The audio is unchanged from v220 (mvt1/v220/score-r04.mp3).
