## Progress since v2
- The horn's E♭→C→B at bars 13–14 now plants the second theme's opening before it is heard; when the cellos begin at bar 47 the interval is already familiar.
- The second theme's cadence breathes: F5 is reached a second time over D♭ (bar 66) and the line descends in quarters to A♭. The recapitulation gives the violins an apotheosis: the expected A minor is replaced by A♭ major at fff with E♭6 on top.
- The development wedge is now built from the second theme's long-note-and-sigh, rising A♭–B♭–C–D♭–D into the dominant pedal.

## Problems
1. **Closing section (68–75)** is eight bars of A♭ pedal with the motto stated twice identically. It neither sums up the exposition nor prepares the development.
2. **The off-beat-eighths texture** survives in the development (bars 96–101 and the dominant pedal at 108–112). The climax needs a new rhythmic level, not the same pulse.
3. **Fugato countersubject** is a plain descending scale moving in parallel with the subject. A countersubject should move when the subject holds and hold when it moves.
4. **The motto is stated literally too often** (introduction, closing ×2, dominant pedal, coda ×2). In the coda it should be transformed, not just repeated.
5. Development D1 harmony is static tremolo under the dialogue (lower priority).
