## Change from v14 (review point 2): the second theme's continuation
The four bars after the theme's first peak (bars 52–55) used to slide down by semitones: g'–g♭'–f'–f♭'–e♭'–d♭'–c'. They now read:

`a♭'4. g'8 f'4 f''4~ | f''4 e♭''8 d♭'' c''4. b♭'8 | a♭'4 g' b♭' d♭' | c'1`

over A♭7/C, D♭, B♭ minor, E♭7, A♭.
- The phrase now has a second high point: an octave leap f'→f'' on the last beat of bar 52, tied over the barline. The theme's first half rose by a sixth (a♭→f', e♭'→c''); this rise of an octave is wider still, so the phrase grows instead of sagging.
- It then descends by step through B♭ minor, without the chromatic slide, and ends on the third of A♭ (c'), not the root. The phrase sounds open, so the violins' A' entry continues it.
- Bar 54 (a♭'–g'–b♭'–d♭' over E♭7) puts a suspension and a turn where the old version had a scale.
- The recapitulation (bars 139–143, C major) uses the motto's minor sixth (a'→f'') in place of the octave, over F, D minor 7, G7, C, so the recapitulation varies the theme instead of transposing it. Horn, violas and cellos carry it, and the bass now moves through D instead of A♭.
