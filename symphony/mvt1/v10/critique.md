## Changes from v9 (review points 3, 4 and 8)
- **D1 (bars 77–84)**
  - Each answer now enters two beats after its question, so the dialogue overlaps instead of alternating in one-bar blocks.
  - The units shrink from a full bar (A♭m, Em) to half bars (C minor, bars 80–81, low then high).
  - The tutti statement arrives at bar 82 and the B°7 strokes at 83, one bar earlier than before.
  - Bar 84 is a held G (cellos, basses, horns, timpani) dying to p, so the fugato's G-minor subject enters out of its own dominant.
  - The colours still change with each entry:
    - A♭ minor: bassoon and cellos, answered by flute and clarinet.
    - E minor: horns and cellos, answered by oboe and first violins.
    - C minor: trombones, horns and bassoon.
  - Trumpets hold notes instead of playing off-beat eighths.
- **Wedge (bars 103–108)**
  - Three one-bar units, then two half-bar units, then quarter notes.
  - The bass descends chromatically F–E–E♭–D–D♭–C, giving Fm, C7/E, Cm/E♭, G7/D, D♭ (Neapolitan) and D7/C, so the approach to the G pedal is driven by harmony, not only by the rising top line.
- **Development climax (bar 114)** is now a diminished seventh (B–D–F–A♭) over the G pedal, with cymbal and tam-tam. The A♭ major chord is kept for the recapitulation's ♭VI apotheosis (bar 152), which no longer repeats the same sonority.
- **Retransition (bars 121–122)**: the harmonic-minor scale is gone. The motto enters in stretto over the timpani crescendo: clarinet on beat 1 of 121, violas on beat 3, second violins and bassoon in 122. The second violins stop on d'' so the recapitulation's first-theme head, which itself begins g–e♭'–d'–d♭'–c', completes the motto.
