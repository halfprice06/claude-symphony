### 1. coherence/mvt1.md stops at v441; the bar-122 climax record no longer matches the score (record only)

**Bars:** 57, 121–123, 165.

**What is wrong.** mvt1.md says "Last update: v441 (cycle 440)". The changes since then:
- c444 (v445): the cellos tremolo A♭3 at 122 and G3 at 123 with the upper strings (`aes1:32\fff | g1:32\>`); before, they held plain minims. The double basses keep the G1 pedal untremoloed.
- c445 (v446): no 8va in Vln I at 121–123 (same pitches); rest positions at 102, 126.4, 134.4, 62.4.
- c446 (v447): harp étouffez at 57.1 and 165.3.

None changes a quoted pitch. But the climax-hierarchy item 2 describes 122 as "A♭6 in Vln I (tremolo) and Fl 1 over the G pedal". Since v445 bar 122 is a full-string fff tremolo (Vln I A♭6, Vln II D5/A♭5 non div., violas B3/D4, cellos A♭3) over the basses' G1. The finale thread relies on "string tremolo only from 223" for its own movement, and on movement I's "string tremolo with the violins' C7" reserve. A finale critic who reads only mvt1.md would not know that movement I's second climax now has the full-string tremolo colour. I checked it against IV 223 as a sound (PDF 204) and it stays distinct: V♭9 over a G pedal, not C major; top A♭6, not C7; tam-tam, not clashed cymbals and bass drum; no trumpet B→C; and the tremolo collapses at 123–126. By the Man of Steel rule (PDF 227) the colour appears earlier in a form that differs from the reward. So the score is right and only the record is behind.

**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 221–222: a team keeps one score coherent through shared, versioned material, so that it "feels seamlessly composed by a single person". PDF 200: a quotation, or a reserve, depends on what the other party knows about the source.

**Fix.** Do not change the score. In mvt1.md:
- Change "Last update: v441 (cycle 440)" to "v451 (cycle 450)".
- In "Climax hierarchy", item 2, after "(A♭ as the minor ninth of V)", add: "Since v445 the cellos tremolo A♭3–G3 with the upper strings at 122–123 (double basses hold G1 untremoloed), so 122 is a full-string fff tremolo; it differs from IV 223 in harmony (V♭9 over G), top note (A♭6), percussion (tam-tam) and the absence of the trumpets' B→C."

### 2. Considered and rejected: planting the finale's first theme F1 in movement I

**Bars considered:** 77–116 (development), 171–174 (coda).

**Question.** The lens allows movement I to foreshadow the later movements' themes. It already seeds the scherzo (P's second bar = motive x; 78–79 G5–F♯5–F5–E5 = theme A bar 2; 106 unresolved D♯5) and the finale's harmony (12 = IV 1; 37 the first E♭-minor sound; 75 A♭7 → D♭). It has no seed of the finale's melodies. I scanned v450 full.midi, all parts, top note per onset, for F1's head shape (C–G–A♭–B♭–A♭–G–F–E♭, intervals −5 +1 +2 and +1 +2 −2 −1) and F2's (B♭–G♭–F–E♭, −4 −1 −2). Neither occurs.

**Why I reject it.** Casanelles's Man of Steel case (PDF 227) is about a colour that "modestly appears" early so that its full statement is a reward. It does not argue for previewing the reward's tune. In the corpus models of this plan, Dvořák 9 and Brahms 1, the finale's main theme is new; the cyclic links run backwards from the finale, as here (finale.md, quotations 1–12). F1 and F2 are what the finale exports, and their freshness is part of the finale's arrival. A seed would also have to go into an inner part of a protected passage (77–130 scoring, c439; coda harmony 171–182, c400), where a listener would not hear it. That fails the brief's test of a real improvement.

**Applied in scratch copy:** no.

### 3. Considered and kept: the forte string tremolo at 167–170 before the stop chord

**Bars:** 167–170.

**Question.** Vln I and Vln II play divided tremolo f–ff in C minor over the C pedal, into the stop chord at 170 (fermata). The finale's arrival does the reverse: stop chord at 222, then all-strings tremolo on C major at 223. Does 167–170 spend the finale's sound?

**Why I keep it.** Under PDF 239, the sound of a device takes its meaning from its spectrum and context. Here the tremolo is minor, under the brass P head, and ends on the unresolved chord with Tpt 1·2's B4. The finale's tremolo comes after the chord and on the resolution. Movement I asks the question, and the finale answers it in the same sound. This is the "journey" of PDF 246, and the passage is protected (167–170 in c389; the 170 stop chord in c374).

**Applied in scratch copy:** no.
