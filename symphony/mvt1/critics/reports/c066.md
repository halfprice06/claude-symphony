# Critic 066 — lens 6, playability: wind and brass fatigue (v85)

## What I studied
I checked the changes made since cycle 56 (cycles 57–65) for range, breathing and dynamics on shared staves. Then I measured every wind and brass player's load over the whole movement. The per-player MIDI comes from `all2.ly`, rebuilt in /tmp/critic-066/work, with v85's tempo map applied (I estimated the rits). The corpus measurements come from the MusicXML of Tchaikovsky 6/i (`reference-corpus/musicxml-converted/xml/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.musicxml`, shared staves split by voice, by chord position and by "I."/"a 2" marks) and Dvořák 9/i (`Dvorak_Sym9_mvt1.musicxml`, separate parts, exposition repeat not unfolded). The scripts are in /tmp/critic-066/scripts.

For each player I measured:
- **play%**: the share of the movement's time spent sounding;
- **run1beat**: the longest stretch with no rest of one beat or more;
- **nogap**: the longest stretch with no rest at all;
- **high%**: the share of playing time in the high register (horn above written G5; trumpet above C6; oboe above A5; trombone above F4).

Web sources:
- Rimsky-Korsakov, *Principles of Orchestration*, ch. I: wood-wind players "cannot manage extremely long sustained passages … care must be taken therefore to give them a little rest from time to time", and "the remarks on breathing … apply with equal force to the brass" (https://www.gutenberg.org/files/33900/33900-h/33900-h.htm).
- orchestrationonline.com, "Woodwinds: note durations and breathing": larger instruments use more air and loud dynamics cost more breath; long phrases need "generous periods of rests" afterwards (https://orchestrationonline.com/woodwinds-note-durations-and-breathing/).
- VI-Control threads on long brass sustains: players stagger their breaths, and bass instruments need breath sooner (https://vi-control.net/community/threads/long-brass-sustains-and-how-to-break-them-up.80158/).

I found no free online text of Adler.

## Overall picture
High-register load is close to zero for every brass player: Hn1 0.3%, trumpets and trombones 0%. Tpt1 plays 20% of the time, against 18% in Tchaikovsky and 23% in Dvořák. Flutes and oboes are at or below the models. Players clearly above the models:

|---|---|---|---|
| Tbn 2 | 29% / 28.2 s (103–116) | 17% / 23.9 s | 19% / 9.3 s |
| Tbn 1, B.tbn | 31% / 24.4 s, 22.5 s | 20%, 26% | 19% |
| Bsn 2 | 58.6% / 28.1 s (97–111) | 46% | 44% / 21.6 s |

Tchaikovsky's horns hold up to 49 s with no rest at 312–324, so the horns are not overloaded overall. The problem is where they breathe: in the passages below, nobody has a written breath before an ff entry.

## Findings (all applied in /tmp/critic-066/work)

**1. Tbn 2, 103–116: 14 bars with no rest, into the fff climax.** This is the longest unbroken brass stretch in the score, longer than any tenor-trombone stretch in either model. Tbn 1 and B.tbn already rest at 103–104 (cycle 26), but Tbn 2's C there only doubles Hn 3 and Tpt 3. Its 105 entry already carries a restart `\mf\cresc`.
Fix (`devTbnTwo`): `R1*19 | c1\mp\cresc | c1 |` → `R1*19 | R1*2 |`. Tbn 2 now enters at 105 with Tbn 1, so the shared staff has the same `\mf\cresc` on both parts. The new longest stretch, 24.4 s, matches Tchaikovsky's climax.

**2. Horns 1–3, 103–118: no breath before the augmented-motto entry at 109.** Hn 3 plays 16 bars with no rest (33 s). Hn 1·2 play 13 bars (26.5 s) at mp, then ff, then fff. Cl 1·2 and Bsn 1 already breathe on beat 4 of 108.
Fix (`dev.ily`): Hn 1 108 `aes'2 a'4 r4` (as Cl 1); Hn 2 108 `fis'2. r4`; Hn 3 108 `d'2. r4`, and a staggered Hn 3 breath at 112 `g'2. r4` (Hn 4 already rests at the end of 111 and 115). Tpt 3 still holds D at 108, and Hn 4 holds G at 112. The motto notes from 109 are unchanged.

**3. Hn 4, 116–126: 11 bars of G with no rest (22.4 s), ending in the ff recapitulation.** Cycle 36 suggested a rest at 122 and it was not applied; that rest would cut the crescendo into 123. Bar 118 is a better place: 119 is already a fresh attack (`g1\pp\<`), and Bsn 2, B.cl, Cbsn and timpani all hold G there.
Fix (`devHnFour`): `g1\p | g1\pp\<~` → `g2.\p r4 | g1\pp\<~`. The swell from 119 to 122 and the timpani are untouched.

**4. Bsn 2's overall load (58.6% against 44–46%): redistribute the 148–151 tonic pedal.** Bsn 1 rests from 139 to 151, while Bsn 2 plays almost every bar from 139 to 162, including the fff climax. The timbre is the same, so giving the four bars to Bsn 1 changes no sound.
Fix (`recTwo.ily`):
- `recTwoBsnOne`: `R1*13 |` → `R1*9 | c1\mp | c1 | d2 f,2 | g,2. r4 |`
- `recTwoBsnTwo`: bars 148–151 → `R1*3 | R1 |`
- Staggered breaths in the climax: Bsn 1 157 `d2 g,4 r4`, with Bsn 2 holding G; Bsn 2 158 `a,2.\ff\> r4`, with Bsn 1 and the strings continuing.

Bsn 2's share drops to 56%. The rest of its load is the 139–147 pedal, which is protected.

**5. Development block 97–108: Bsn 2, Cbsn and both clarinets play 12–14 bars with no rest.** Bsn 2 plays 28 s and Cbsn 26 s, ff then mf-cresc, and the contrabassoon uses the most air of the section. Cl 1 and Cl 2 play eighth-note arpeggios in unison for 6 bars and go straight into the wedge pad (22.9 s). Every one of these lines is doubled:
- Bsn 2 doubles B.cl and B.tbn exactly;
- the two clarinets double each other;
- Cl 2's G at 104 is also in Hn 2 and Tpt 2.

Fix (`dev.ily`):
- Bsn 2 at 100: `c2. r4` (inside the `\transpose c cis` block);
- Cbsn at 102: `f,,2. r4`;
- Cl 1 drops the last eighth of 102 (`… a' r }`), while Cl 2 completes the figure;
- Cl 2 at 104: `g'2. r4`.

This touches the protected B-minor block 97–102 only by adding rests in doubling parts. The melody, the brass counter and the harmony are unchanged.

**6. Bsn 2, intro 9–14: 6 Lento bars with no rest (27.6 s, pp up to f and back to pp).** At ♩=50 this is the longest unbroken bassoon stretch in the score. Bsn 2 doubles the Cb exactly, and B.tbn holds A♭ at 12.
Fix (`introBsnTwo`): `aes,1\f |` → `aes,2.\f r4 |`. The German sixth at 12–13 is unchanged.

**7. Hn 2, 148–160: 13 bars with no rest (27.9 s), including the protected tune doubling at 154–157.** Hn 1 rests at 149–152.
Fix (`recTwoHnTwo`): 153 `c'1` → `c'2. r4`, a breath before the ff entry of the tune. The notes at 154–157 are unchanged. The new longest stretch is 15.5 s.

**Checked and fine (no action):**
