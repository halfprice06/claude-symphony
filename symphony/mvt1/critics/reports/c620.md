### 7. Where the music still falls short in this lens (candidate rejected; no change)

The weak point for the cyclic design is the same one c580 and c610 named: the horn call has no clear statement after the development. c580 rejected 144–146, so I examined the only other gap, 165.3–167.1. There the C-major second group dissolves and the violas turn E♮→E♭ at 166.

Hn 1 would need E♭3–C4–B3 on 166.3, 166.4 and 167.1. The MIDI shows three problems:
- Hn 1 is already busy. It plays E4→E♭4 at 166.1–3 with the violas, which is the E♮/E♭ thread at its pitch, and it takes the P pickup at 167.1.5.
- The call's B3 would go straight to the P head, so it could not stay unresolved. The call is left unresolved in movement I and in III 3–6; only the finale resolves it.
- A third horn colour would sit on the protected E♮→E♭ turn (c093, c110).

So the change would spend what the finale needs and crowd a protected join. I reject it.

Nothing else in the reading meets the brief's bar. The arc sets up the later movements as Adler PDF 20 asks:
- The motto, horn call and S head are each first given to one colour.
- The colours the finale needs are saved.
- The three A♭ climaxes point to movement II's key.
- The coda hands movement II its first chord.
