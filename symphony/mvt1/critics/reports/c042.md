# Critic 042: harmony and voice leading (lens 2), version v61

## What I studied
I built a one-track-per-instrument MIDI from v61 (`all.ly`, recompiled at v61) and used two scripts (in /tmp/critic-042/scripts): `son.py` lists every sounding pitch per instrument at each half-beat in a bar range, and `clash.py` lists every minor 2nd, major 7th or minor 9th between two sounding notes where at least one is attacked on that beat. I also re-ran the earlier critics' all-voice parallels checker (`tools/critic-scripts/vl2.py`). I checked every bar named in the task (19–22, 33, 39–40, 56–57, 63, 103–108, 135, 162) beat by beat, then went through the whole score.

Corpus: Tchaikovsky 6/i (`musicxml-converted/xml/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.musicxml`, read at sounding pitch with music21) and its MCTL harmony annotations (`annotations/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1/form/harmony.csv`). Web: Wikipedia, "Augmented sixth chord" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_sixth_chord); Rubin, "Augmented Sixth Chords" (https://www.d.umn.edu/~jrubin1/JHR%20Theory%20Augmented%206.htm); SFCM's Aldwell & Schachter supplements (https://sfcm.edu/study/majors/academics/music-theory-and-musicianship/sfcm-theory/online-materials/harmony-supplements/aldwell-schachter-chapter-26); Wikipedia, "False relation" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_relation).

**Checked and fine:** the viola counterline and Vln II syncopations at 19–22 (Neapolitan 6 to V7 at 21, with Db going down to B by a diminished third, which is standard); the re-voicing at 56 and 63; the ff chords at 33 and 135 themselves; 162 (the G7 resolves correctly, then E becomes Eb on beat 3).

## Findings (ranked)

**1. Bars 104 and 107, viola eighths (cycle 37): the neighbour note ignores the harmony.**
The pattern `c' c' b( c')` runs through 103–108 unchanged. At 104 the chord is C7/E, and Bb4/Bb5 are held in Vln I, Vln II, Ob, Cl1, Hn1 and Tpt1. The viola's B natural forms a diminished octave against them on beats 2 and 4, twice in the bar, during the crescendo. At 107 the chord is Db major, and the viola's lower neighbour G natural sounds a tritone above the Db bass and a minor 9th below the Ab held in five upper parts.
Evidence: Aldwell & Schachter (SFCM supplement): "the best way to avoid a cross-relation is to keep the chromatic motion exclusively in one voice." Tchaikovsky 6/i, MCTL measure 93: when the clarinets bring in D♯3 (vii°7/V over the tonic pedal), the D natural stays only in the bass pedal. Horns 3·4, which had held D on beats 1–2, move to A on beat 3 when the D♯ enters. Tchaikovsky allows a chromatic neighbour against a pedal, but not against the same note held in an upper part.
Fix (dev.ily, devVla): 104 `c'8 c'8 bes8( c'8) c'8 c'8 bes8( c'8) |`; 107 `aes8 aes8 bes8( aes8) aes8 aes8 bes8( aes8) |` (the upper neighbour, a whole tone from both Ab and C). I tried Gb at 107 first, but it formed a minor 2nd with the F held in four parts, so I rejected it. Rhythm and slurs are unchanged. **Applied.**

**2. Bar 39, beat 2: the new melody (cycle 41) clashes with the viola's chromatic tail.**
The new figure puts F5 (Fl1, Ob1, Vln I) on 39.2, where the viola imitation sounds Gb4 (`aes'8) g'8( ges'8 f'8)`). The result is a major 7th / diminished octave between two attacked notes on the beat, and F is the note the viola's Gb is moving to. Before cycle 41 the melody had Bb on that beat, so there was no clash.
Fix (trans.ily, transVla bar 39): `aes'8) g'16( ges'16 f'4) f'2:32`. This keeps the chromatic G–Gb–F (the motto's descent), so Gb now sounds only against the melody's Eb5 (a major 6th), and the viola reaches F together with the melody. **Applied.**

**3. Bars 168–169: parallel fifths between the two solo lines (cor anglais motto against the cello theme).**
CA D5 / Vc G4 at 168.4.5, then C5 / F4 at 169.1. These are consecutive fifths between the two most exposed voices, at p, with nothing else moving. This passage is protected ("cello theme vs CA motto 167–170", cycles 29 and 40) for its cyclic content. The fix keeps every pitch of both lines.
Fix (coda.ily, codaCorAnglais): `es''2.~ es''8 d''8~ | d''4 c''4 des''4 c''4~ | c''2) r2`. The CA holds D over the barline, so 169.1 forms a 6–5 over iv (sounding as iiø6/5), and the fifths become oblique motion. The iv–N6–i6/4–V plan of 168–170 is unchanged. **Applied.**

**4. Bars 12–13: German sixth to V with Mozart fifths (viola Eb4–D4 against bass Ab2–G2).**
Wikipedia and Rubin both name this as the known fault of Ger6→V. The standard remedies are a cadential 6/4 or holding the Eb over the barline. The protection ("Ger6 into V 12–13", cycles 12 and 22) covers the progression, and this fix keeps it.
Fix (intro.ily, introVla bar 13): `es'2\p( d'2) |`. The Eb becomes a 6–5 over G, and it also sounds together with Hn1's motto Eb3 on beat 2. **Applied.**

**5. Bars 37–38: Hn3 in parallel fifths with Bsn2 and the bass.**
Hn3 Bb3→C4 over Eb→F (Bsn2 Eb3→F3, Cb). These are exposed fifths in the horn pad at mf crescendo.
Fix (trans.ily, transHnThree bar 37): `des'1\p\<`. This makes Ebm7, and the 7th Db resolves down to C at 38. Hn4 keeps its Gb, and the dynamics on the shared staff stay equal. **Applied.**

**6. Bar 64, beat 4: Db against D natural in the bass (Cl1 and Hn2 counterline).**
The chord on beats 3–4 is D–F–Ab–C, with the D in Vc and Cb. The counterline's passing `des'8` sounds an augmented unison / minor 9th against the bass, and a minor 2nd against the viola's re-attacked C4. This is the same kind of clash cycle 37 fixed at 56, 57 and 63.
Fix (tTwo.ily, tTwoClOne and tTwoHnTwo bar 64): `c'2 r8 c'8( d'8 es'8 |`. **Applied.**

**7. Bars 56–57: parallel fifths between two horns.**
Hn2 (with Cl1) G4→Ab4 against Hn3 C4→Db4. I first tried a retardation (`c'4( des'2.)`), but the harp and cello arpeggio sounds Db on 57.1.5 against it, so I rejected that.
Fix (tTwo.ily, tTwoHnThree bar 57): `aes1` (unison with Hn4; Db is already in Vla, Vc and harp). **Applied.**

**8. Bars 134–135: fifths into the ff eighth chord.**
Tpt2 D4→G4 and Ob2 D5→G4 move in parallel fifths with the bass G→C, which is in ff tutti brass. At 33 there is a rest before the chord, so the problem does not arise there.
Fix (recOne.ily): recOneTptTwo `es'8->\ff`; recOneOboeTwo `c''8->\ff` (unison with Ob1). **Applied.**

Not raised as a finding: bar 60 (Cl1/Hn2 F4 as the major 7th of Gb, with the viola's Gb4 a semitone below on an off-beat) is the one remaining sharp inner clash. The F does resolve to Eb, so I left it. On harmonic rhythm: the second theme 47–55 changes chord once per bar, against about 1.9 per bar in Tchaikovsky 6/i's second theme (MCTL harmony.csv). The passage is protected, and 60–63 already speeds up, so no action.
