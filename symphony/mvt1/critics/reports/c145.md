## Findings (ranked)

**F1. Bars 73–76: rhythmic spacing is uneven within the 73–84 system. Applied.**
Problem: the flute motto in bars 73–76 is spaced much tighter than the same note values in 77–84. Measured at 220 dpi on v164 page 7: in bar 74 the dotted half `aes''2.` gets 39 px and the eighths get 21 px each. In bar 77 the clarinet's eighth `es''8` gets ~35 px, and a quarter gets ~90 px. A dotted half at 74 is therefore narrower than a quarter at 77.
Evidence: Gould PDF 54: "Notes of equal duration require equal spacing for an entire system", and a longer value needs "more space than its shorter neighbour … or at least as much". PDF 55: spacing must give "an illusion of evenness".
Fix (global.ily, around the 73–76 block):
```
\break
\newSpacingSection \override Score.SpacingSpanner.common-shortest-duration = #(ly:make-moment 1/16)
s1*4
\bar "||" \key c \minor \mark \default \newSpacingSection \revert Score.SpacingSpanner.common-shortest-duration
```
After the fix, bar 74 has 55 px for the dotted half and ~28 px per eighth, against ~30 px per eighth at 77. Only page 7 changes. No break moved, and the page count stays 17. I tested two other remedies: `uniform-stretching` barely helped, and moving the break to 81 pushed the score to 18 pages.

**F2. Unison black note against a semibreve: the semibreve looks attached to the stem (70 Tpt 1·2, 140 Ob 1·2, 158 Tpt 1·2). Applied.**
Problem: Tpt 1 `aes'4` / Tpt 2 `aes'1` (70), Ob 1 `des''8` / Ob 2 `des''1` (140) and Tpt 1 `g'4` / Tpt 2 `g'1` (158) print the semibreve right against the up-stem of the black note. The pair reads as one stemmed chord, so the player of part 2 could take it for a quarter or an eighth.
Evidence: Gould PDF 66: "Keep noteheads separate; stems should not touch. Semibreves in particular should not appear to be joined to stems." Same page: "Some editions reverse the order of noteheads so that the semibreve is never adjacent to a stem."
Fix: clos.ily `closTptTwo`: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.7 aes'1`. recTwo.ily `recTwoTptTwo`: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.7 g'1\ff`. At 140 the beamed eighths leave no room on the right, so I used the reverse order. recOne.ily `recOneOboeTwo`: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #-1.45 des''1\ff`, which puts the semibreve to the left with a small gap and the eighth group unchanged.

**F3. Heads a second apart touch (126 Cl 1·2; 134 Fl 1·2 and Ob 1·2). Applied.**
Problem: at 126, Cl 1's `d''1` sits against the down-stem of Cl 2's `c''4`. At 134, Fl 1's `f''4` (tied) is displaced right but its notehead overlaps Fl 2's `g''1`. Oboe 1 `f'4` against Ob 2 `g'1` is the same.
Evidence: Gould PDF 62: when "a tone or semitone separates the two parts … the lower part is displaced to the right". PDF 66: noteheads are kept separate. PDF 55: "no characters should collide".
Fix: dev.ily `devClTwo` bar 126: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.7 c'4-\tweak X-offset #-2 \p( b2.)`. recOne.ily `recOneFluteOne` and `recOneOboeOne` bar 134: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.7 f''4` and `… #1.7 f'4`.

**F4. Crossing parts print as one "box" (70 Ob 1·2) and unison stems join (158 Ob 1·2). Applied.**
Problem: at 70, Ob 1 `aes'4` (up-stem) lies a fourth below Ob 2 `des''2` (down-stem), vertically aligned. The two stems frame both heads, so it reads as one chord. At 158, Ob 1 `g'4` and Ob 2 `g'2` are offset, but their stems meet in one continuous vertical line.
Evidence: Gould PDF 66–67, "Overlapping parts": "Place a down-stemmed part to the left … Slightly separate the parts so that the stems do not appear to include both notes". The "and not" example there shows exactly the aligned layout at 70. PDF 66: "stems should not touch".
Fix: clos.ily `closOboeOne` bar 70: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0.9 aes'4`. recTwo.ily `recTwoOboeTwo` bar 158: `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #1.5 g'2\ff`. Checked at 300 dpi: Ob 2 is left, Ob 1 is right, with a visible gap.

**F5. Bars 117–120 flutes: crossing parts with the duration dot crowded. Not applied (open).**
Problem: Fl 2 holds `f''1 | g''1 | f''2. r4 | f''1` while Fl 1 starts each bar below it (`r8 b'8 aes''4.` …). The notation follows PDF 65 (upper part up-stems even when overlapping). At 119, however, Fl 2's dot on `f''2.` sits almost against Fl 1's `d''8`.
Evidence: Gould PDF 67: a dot is placed "close to its notehead … usually a half stave-space's distance". PDF 55: at least ½ space between characters.
Suggested fix to test: in `devFluteOne` bar 119, `\once \override NoteColumn.force-hshift = #0.6` on `d''8`. Alternatively, write Fl 2's 119 as `f''2 ~ f''4 r4`, which removes the dot and keeps the sound. I did not apply either because I could not check them against the 8-bar system without a third rebuild. The scoring of 117–122 is protected, and neither change alters a note.

**F6. Bars 176, 180–182: whole-note bars are not centred. Not applied, cosmetic.**
Gould PDF 55 says a bar holding one note and nothing shorter should place it just left of centre. LilyPond starts it at the beginning of the bar. The bars are wide, so Gould's own exception ("in widely spaced bars, the note can be placed closer to the barline") covers them. No change.
