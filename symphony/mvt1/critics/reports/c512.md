## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 176→177, coda chorale: Vln II and Tbn 2 move G→F in parallel octaves (applied)

**What is wrong.** In the pp chorale the real voices at 176–177 are: bass E♭→D (Cb, Vc, B.cl, B.tbn), Tbn 2 G3→F3, violas C4→A♭3, Tbn 1 E♭4→D4, Vln II G4→F4, Cl 1 C5 held. Tbn 2 and Vln II go G→F together an octave apart. v512 vl.txt lists it:
```
bar 177 beat 1.00 P8 tbnTwo:G3->F3 vlnTwo:G4->F4
```
They are two independent voices, not a doubling: before 176 Tbn 2 has E♭3 under Vln II's G4, and after 177 they part (178: Tbn 2 G3, Vln II E♭4). So for one move the texture loses a voice: A&S PDF 83 (Ex. 5-18), "one part duplicates the pitch and motion of the other in a different register; this provides some contrast but not enough to give the feeling of two individual voices". PDF 84 excuses octave doubling only where a part doubles another line "for variety of sound or for emphasis", which is not the case here. The texture is soft, slow (crotchet = 72, rit.) and has six real parts, the case in which A&S treat voice independence most strictly (PDF 85: doublings acceptable in orchestral textures, but where the number of voices stays the same "such doublings should be strictly avoided"; PDF 87, point 1: the fewer the voices, the stronger the effect).

c422 wrote that the chorale 175–182 "follows Unit 5 note for note"; this line was in its vl.txt but not discussed. c309 kept Tbn 2's F3→G3 at 177→178 (a different motion); c247 put Vln II on G4 at 175–176 so that Cl 1's E♭4 enters on a pitch of its own. Neither reason is touched by the fix.

**Fix** (coda.ily, codaVlnTwo, bar 177; only Vln II changes):
```
% v512
g'1\pp^"unis."~ | g'1 | f'1 | es'2 f'2 |
% c512
g'1\pp^"unis."~ | g'1 | aes'2 f'2 | es'2 f'2 |
```
What it does:
- 176→177: Vln II G4 rises a step to A♭4 while Tbn 2 falls G3→F3 (contrary motion, PDF 82: the most independent motion). The octaves are gone.
- 177.1–2 is the same iiø7 (D–F–A♭–C): A♭ is now in the violas (A♭3) and Vln II (A♭4), F in Tbn 2 alone. The 7th C (Cl 1) is still not doubled (PDF 76).
- 177.3: Vln II skips A♭4→F4 together with Cl 1's C5→B4. The skip lies inside vii°7 (B–D–F–A♭), the kind of skip within a dominant-type chord PDF 82 allows. From 177.3 to the end of the bar the sonority is exactly as before.
- 177.4–178 are unchanged: F4 falls to E♭4 in tenths with the bass D→C (c192), the doubled 7th at 177.4 (c472) is as before, and Vln I's G4 enters on a pitch no other part holds.
- The A♭ in Vln II also places the motto's ♭6 (A♭) at the top of the inner strings one beat before Cl 1's B4, so the A♭–B diminished seventh that colours the last motto is heard in a high register rather than only in the violas' A♭3.

**Applied in the scratch copy:** yes (/tmp/critic-512/work/sections/coda.ily, with a c512 comment).
