Sources:
- Berklee, "Writing for the Harp" (https://www.berklee.edu/node/2392666): the left foot works D, C and B and the right foot works E, F, G and A. A beat of rest gives the harpist time to set up a pedal change.
- Harp Column forum, "string noise when using pedals" (https://harpcolumn.com/forums/topic/string-noise-when-using-pedals): changing a pedal under a sounding string buzzes or bends the pitch, so the strings concerned must be damped first.
- Timpani ranges: Yamaha TP-8300R lineup (https://usa.yamaha.com/products/musical_instruments/percussion/timpani/tp-8300r/lineup.html) and freepercussionlessons.com/timpani-ranges.
- LilyPond 2.24 harp notation (https://lilypond.org/doc/v2.24/Documentation/notation/harp).
- Adler's breathing guideline for winds: a breath every 4–8 bars of moderate tempo.

The reference corpus has no harp part. I used it only to confirm that a muta needs bars of rest (Dvořák 7, `mutopia/DvorakA_O70`, timpani retunings during rests).

## Findings (ranked)

**1. Harp 59–60: the G♭ pedal change is marked on the beat where G♭ sounds. (Applied.)**
In bar 60, `<ges,, ges,>` (LH) and `ges''` (RH) are plucked on beat 1, and the "G♭" mark is attached to that same beat. A pedal has to be set before its string is plucked, so the change as written is impossible. The previous G♮ is the RH g'' on beat 3½ of 59, so the only window is beat 4 of 59. The LH already rests there.
Fix, applied in tTwo.ily: `<es, es>2\< << r2 { s4 s4_\markup \small "G♭" } >> |` in bar 59, and the mark removed from bar 60. If the composer also wants to avoid the short slide of the ringing g'' to G♭, the RH of 59 can drop g'': `r2 es''8( bes'' des''' es''')`. I did not apply that, because the strings hold the G.

**2. Harp 159: the A♮→A♭ change comes under ringing A strings at f. (Applied.)**
The A♮ strings are plucked at f in 158 (LH `<a,, a,>`) and 159 (RH a''). The A♭ change is on beat 3 of 159, two beats after the a''. The low A strings ring for many seconds, so the pedal would bend them audibly (Harp Column thread above). Both hands rest on beats 3–4, so damping costs nothing.
Fix, applied in recTwo.ily: `<a'' c''' f'''>2\arpeggio r2^\markup \small \italic "étouffez"` in bar 159. The "A♭" mark stays on the LH rest. The 151 change does not need this: the a'' from 150 was plucked six beats earlier in the top octave and has decayed.

**3. Harp 50–51 and 62–63: two more pedal marks are too late, or fall on ringing strings. (Applied; no notes changed in protected 47–55.)**
- 51: "G♭" sits on beat 1, but the RH G♭ is the second eighth, 0.27 s later at ♩=112. Moved to beat 4 of 50: `<< es,,1 { s2. s4_\markup \small "G♭" } >>`.
- 62: "F♮" sits on beat 1, two beats after the F♭s of 61 (`<fes, fes>`, fes') were plucked, so they are still sounding. F♮ is not needed until 64. Moved to the LH rest on beat 3 of 63: `es,8( bes, des g) r2_\markup \small "F♮"`. This also keeps the G♮ (63, beat 1) and the F♮ two beats apart on the right foot.
I checked every other harp note against the pedals in force, including F♭ = E at 56 and 61 and C♭/E♭ spellings, and found no other conflict. The largest hand spans are an octave, in RH chords such as 152 `<f'' bes'' f'''>` and in the LH octaves. The broken chords in 56–68 stay within an octave per hand. All notes lie between E♭1 and F6.

**4. Bassoon 1 33–40: eight bars without a breath. (Applied.)**
The solo starts an eighth after the bar-32 tutti and continues through whole notes to f at 40. That is about 15 s at ♩=126 with no rest, and Bsn 1 has played almost continuously since 19. The cellos double 35–40 and Bsn 2 enters at 37, so a breath at the end of 36 is covered.
Fix, applied in trans.ily: `g2 es2 \breathe |`.

**5. Clarinet 1 109–116: about 16 s of fff with no rest. (Applied.)**
Cl 1 rests only on beat 4 of 108, then plays the triplets of 109–113, f'' at 114 (fff) and d'' at 115. Cl 2 got breaths in cycle 16, but Cl 1 did not. In 109–113 Cl 1 plays the same triplets as Cl 2 and the violas, so dropping one triplet costs nothing audible.
Fix, applied in dev.ily, bar 112: `\repeat unfold 3 { \tuplet 3/2 { bes8 des' f' } } r4`. The part-combiner prints "2." for that beat and "a2" at 113, which is correct.
