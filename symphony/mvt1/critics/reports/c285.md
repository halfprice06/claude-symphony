## Findings

### 1. Cello tenor clef falls inside the phrase at 97–98 (applied)

**Bars:** Vc 97–98 (dev.ily, the B-minor second theme; c231 gave the cellos this line).

**What is wrong:** The cellos hold B2 for three beats in bass clef, then take the theme's pickup F♯3 on beat 4. The tenor clef is printed after that pickup, at the start of 98. So the first two notes of the phrase, F♯3 slurred up to D4, are read in two different clefs. The leap of a sixth that starts the theme is spread across a clef change.

**Evidence:** Gould PDF 22, "between phrases": "In a passage of continuous playing, place a new clef between phrases, wherever this occurs in the bar. This placing overrules the above considerations. It enables the reader to see the pitch contour of the phrase intact." Her example shows the clef before a slurred pickup, not after it. The score already follows this rule for the same theme at 46 and 146, where c115 put the tenor clef before the cello pickup ("clef before the pickup, between phrases (Gould PDF 22)"). Bar 97 was written later (c231) and did not follow that model. The clef is also between beats (beat 3→4), as PDF 22 asks, and it does not cut a tie (PDF 23).

**Fix:** in dev.ily, cello line of 97–98:

```
\transpose c cis { bes,2.\ff \clef tenor f4( | des'2.~\< des'8 c'8\>) | ...
```

(was `bes,2.\ff f4( | \clef tenor des'2.~ ...`). F♯3 sits on the second line in tenor clef, so it needs no ledger line.

**Applied in /tmp/critic-285/work/sections/dev.ily** (with a comment line). Score page 9 now shows the tenor clef between the B2 and the pickup. The Violoncello part shows the same, with the line still breaking after 98.

### Considered and kept (no change)

- **8va in Vln I at 31, 71–72 and 121–123 but not at 140 or 160.** Gould PDF 47 allows octave signs in a full score "as necessary, to save space between staves". The A♭6 at 31 has four ledger lines and there is room on page 4, so the sign is not strictly necessary, and the three A♭ peaks look different on the page. c245 kept this deliberately, all signs are already out of the playing part, and writing 31 and 72 at pitch would change the vertical spacing of pages 4 and 7 against the fixed 17-page layout. Keep.
- **Two spacing sections in the 73–84 system** (PDF 54, equal values equally spaced across a system). c145 made 73–76 its own spacing section so that the quavers of 74 and 77 come out the same width. That is the aim of PDF 54, and the section is protected. Keep.
- **Breath marks above the Hn 3·4 staff at 64, 128→129 and 134** sit at the top of the staff while the note is low. They are legible, and the rule for breath marks is outside this reading. Keep.
- **Dotted minim rests at 170 in the percussion and harp** (`r2.` before the fermata crotchet rest). Gould's chapter 6 (grouping rests by the metre) covers this, outside the assigned range, and PDF 52 (rests on a single line) is followed. Not raised.
