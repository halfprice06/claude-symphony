# Critic 16 (lens 6: playability), v35

## What I studied
I compiled v35 with one MIDI track per player and no part-combining (/tmp/critic-016/build/m.ly). From that MIDI I measured each part's range, total playing time, longest stretch without a rest and its length in seconds (using the tempo map), and slur lengths in seconds for the strings. I read every section file. I checked harp pedalling note by note at 47–68 and 148–178, and timpani tuning and retuning across the whole movement. For the corpus I ran the same measurements with music21 on `musicxml-converted/xml/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1` and `Dvorak_Sym9_mvt1`:
- In Tchaikovsky 6/i, Corni I·II play 51% of the movement and their longest run with no rest of two quarters or more is 102 quarters.
- In Dvořák 9/i, Corni I·II play 27% and their longest run is 56 quarters.

Web sources:
- Bow time per dynamic (about 1–3 s per stroke at ff, 10–15 s at pp): https://timbreandorchestration.org/isfee/extreme-orchestration/bowed-strings/right-hand-techniques and https://www.robin-hoffmann.com/?p=4043
- Principal-horn endurance, and resting the first horn before exposed passages: https://hornmatters.com/?p=25256 and https://www.hornmatters.com/2024/11/fundamentals-12-endurance-ease-of-production-at-all-levels-of-fatigue-in-all-ranges/
- Harp pedal changes on ringing strings, which buzz worst on the low wound strings: https://www.instructables.com/Virtual-harp and https://www.thehermesexperiment.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/SONG-VCP-Writing-for-Harp-Handout.pdf

**Testing.** I tested all the fixes below together in a scratch copy at /tmp/critic-016/test/v. It builds with 0 warnings, and the parallels report shows only the known bar-96 doubling.

**Cycle 6 judged.** Its changes worked. The double basses return to arco at 90. The dominant pedal 109–118 is now staggered across bass clarinet, bassoon 2, contrabassoon and tuba. Clarinet 1 now has breaths at 108 and 116. The f'' in bars 52–54 and 144–145 is now with clarinet 1. The violin bowings at 55–68 are split. The timpani tunings are clear.

**Checked, no change needed:**
- **Timpani** (G, A♭, C, E♭): all retunings have 5–10 bars, and the A♭ at 82 uses a drum already tuned.
- **Cor anglais at 167–170:** its top note, concert e♭'' (written b♭''), is in its singing register.
- **Trombones at 171–175:** pp, G3–E♭4, playable.
- **Horns 1·2 on the tune at 154–157:** written d'–e♭'', easy.
- **Trumpets:** only 22% playing time, highest note written b♭'', no endurance concern.
- **Divided-string chords:** all playable as double stops.
- **Harp pedal names at 51–66 and 148–162:** all correct.

## Findings

### 1. Horn 1 carries too much: 56% of the movement, including 65 s at 138–166 with only one bar's rest
- **Problem:** Cycle 14 gave horn 1 the clarinet's counterline at 56–68 and 148–151. Horn 1 already plays the second theme at 47–55 and 139–147. Horn 2 is silent from 47 to 68 and from 139 to 151.
  - In the recapitulation, horn 1 plays the tune (139–147), then the counterline, then the held notes at 152–153, then the fff tune at 154–157, then 158–162, then the ff coda at 163–166, with a single bar's rest at 145.
  - Horn 1 plays 221 s of 427 s, and horn 2 plays 138 s.
- **Evidence:**
  - Tchaikovsky's Corni I·II together play 51% of 6/i, shared between two players. Dvořák's play 27% of 9/i.
  - Hornmatters: the principal's endurance is finite, and "even a very short rest before exposed passages can be of great help".
- **Fix:** horn 2 plays the doublings. The horn colour cycle 14 asked for stays, so this does not undo that fix.
```
tTwoHnOne bars 56–68:   R1*13 |
tTwoHnTwo = {
  R1*9 |
  es'2\p^\markup { \bold "2." \italic "espr." } r8 e'8( f'8 g'8 | aes'2) f'2 | f'2 r8 f'8( es'8 des'8 |
  des'2) es'2 | f'2 r8 f'8( es'8 des'8 | fes'2) ces'2 | c'4 es'8( f'8 g'4 aes'4~ |
  aes'4 g'8 f'8) g'2 | c'2 r8 c'8( des'8 es'8 | f'2) aes'2 | des'4 f'8( g'8 aes'4 bes'4~ |
  bes'2 g'4 des'4) | c'1\p |
}
recTwoHnOne bars 147–153:  e'1\p | R1*4 | f'1\mf\< | f'1 |
recTwoHnTwo bars 139–152:  R1*9 | g'2\mp^\markup \bold "2." r8 g'8( a'8 b'8 | c''2) a'2 | a'2 r8 a'8( g'8 f'8 | f'2) b'2 | d'1\mf\< |
```
- **Result in the test build:** horn 1 drops to 182 s and horn 2 rises to 177 s. Horn 1's longest stretch without a 3 s rest falls from 65 s to 37 s, and horn 1 has 4 bars' rest before the ♭VI climax.

### 2. Violins hold one slur for six bars at ff in the B-minor block (97–102)
- **Problem:** In `devVlnOne` and `devVlnTwo` one slur runs from bar 97 beat 4 to the end of bar 102. That is about 10 s at ff in one bow.
- **Evidence:** Timbre & Orchestration gives 1–3 s per stroke at ff. Cycle 6 split the slurs at 55–68 for this reason; this passage was missed.
- **Fix:** slur by bow, inside the existing `\transpose c cis`.
```
devVlnOne: \transpose c cis { r2. f'4(\ff | des''2.~ des''8 c''8) |
  bes'4( ges''2 f''4) | es''4.( des''8 c''4 es''8 ges''8) | bes''2( a''2) | f''4( des'''2 c'''8 g''8) } |
devVlnTwo: \transpose c cis { r1 | des'2.~(\ff des'8 c'8) |
  bes4( ges'2 f'4) | es'4.( des'8 c'4 es'8 ges'8) | bes'2( a'2) | f'4( des''2 c''8 g'8) } |
```

### 3. Cellos hold one slur for five bars in the coda (171–175, about 18 s), and the bass clarinet has the same line with no breath
- **Problem:** `codaVc` and `codaBassCl` both have `g,1\pp( | es1 | d1 | c2 des2 | c1)`. The trombones already split this line after bar 172.
- **Evidence:** about 18 s at pp is longer than the 10–15 s limit for a pp stroke (Timbre & Orchestration). The bass clarinet is the costliest clarinet in air.
- **Fix** (both parts), matching the trombones:
```
g,1\pp( | es1) | d1( | c2 des2 | c1) |
```

### 4. Woodwinds play the recapitulation climax and the development with no rest at all
- **Oboe 1** plays 148–162: 15 bars, 36 s, through the fff, with no rest.
- **Flute 1** plays 150–162: 31 s.
- **Clarinet 2** plays 97–118: 42 s at ff–fff with no rest. Cycle 6 gave clarinet 1 breaths but not clarinet 2.
- **Bassoon 1** plays 103–118: 31 s.
- **Fix:** nothing is lost from the texture, because flute 2 and violin II carry oboe 1's pitches at 152–153, and oboe 1 and violin I carry flute 1's pitches at 150–151.
```
recTwoOboeOne bars 151–154:  c'''2 b''2\! | R1*2 | g'4\ff e''2.~ |
recTwoFluteOne bars 139–152: R1*13 | d'''2.~\mf\< d'''8 cis'''8 |
devClTwo bar 108: fis'2.\ff r4 |    bar 115: b'2. r4 |
devBsnOne bar 108: fis2. r4 |       bar 116: g2.\> r4 |
```
- The clarinet 2 and bassoon 1 breaths fall at different points from clarinet 1's breaths at 108 and 116, so the sound never empties.

### 5. Horn 1 sounds e'' (written b'') in the recapitulation's lyrical solo (bar 143)
- **Problem:** The recapitulation transposes the exposition's c'' at bar 51 up a third. Cycle 6 removed the f'' at 144 but left this note. It is held for a half note under a crescendo, after four bars of solo.
- **Evidence:** horns in Tchaikovsky 6/i never sound above c'', and in Dvořák 9/i never above c♯''.
- **Fix:** the horn joins the cello octave for these two bars. Violas keep the upper octave.
```
recTwoHnOne bars 143–144:  g4( e'2) d'4 | c'4.( b8 a2) |
```

### 6. Horn 4 plays 97–134 (38 bars, about 72 s) with only quarter-note rests
- **Composer's reason for rejecting this in cycle 6:** the development breaths made it unnecessary.
- **Answer:** those quarter rests give the player air but no rest for the lips. The run is 152 quarters long, against 102 for Tchaikovsky's Corni III·IV pair and 56 for Dvořák's horns.
- **Fix:** at 127–129 horn 4 only doubles the cello, double bass and bass trombone quarter-note line at mp.
```
recOneHnFour bars 127–130:  R1*3 | b,2.->\mf r4 |
```

### 7. Harp: D♮ to D♭ in 1½ beats on a ringing low wound string (bars 64–65)
- **Problem:** d, (D2) sounds on beat 3 of bar 64. Its pedal must go to D♭ for the downbeat of 65 while that string is still vibrating, which makes the buzz the sources warn about. The cellos and double basses already have the D.
- **Fix** (`tTwoHarpL`): no D♮ is needed, so the D♮ and D♭ marks go.
```
bar 64: f,8(_\markup \small "F♮" c f aes) aes,8( c f aes) |
bar 65: des,8( aes, des f aes2) |
```

### 8. Bass clarinet goes down to concert C2 (bars 92–93)
- **Problem:** concert C2 is written D3, which is below the low E♭ of the older standard instrument.
- **Fix:** add `^\markup \small "(low C instr.)"` at bar 92. If an older instrument must be allowed for, write `c2` an octave up instead.

## Do not touch
The horn and cello second theme over the harp (47–55), the timpani plan and its retunings, the trumpet writing, and the trombone and cor anglais lines at 167–175 are idiomatic and well paced.
