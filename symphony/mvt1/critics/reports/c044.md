## Findings

### 1. Bars 58–61 and 148–151: one oboe added to Vln I + Vln II in unison (applied)
**What is wrong.** In the violins' restatement of the second theme, Ob 1 plays the tune in unison with Vln I, which Vln II also doubles. This happens at 58–61 in the exposition and at 148–151 in the recapitulation.

**Evidence.**
- Rimsky-Korsakov (Chapter I) names this exact combination as one in which the oboe is wasted: "the addition of one wind instrument to all or part of the strings in unison, only thickens the resonance of the latter, the wood-wind timbre being lost in the process: Vns I + Vns II + 1 Ob."
- Tchaikovsky 6/i 130–137: the violins and violas carry the second theme in three octaves with no wind doubling. Woodwinds and horns play repeated-chord harmony, and Fl 1 joins only at the peak (134).
- Dvořák 9/i 158–170: Vln I + II alone take the theme over from the solo flute (149–156).

**Fix.**
- `tTwoOboeOne = { R1*22 | }`. Fl 1 still enters at 60 as the colour entry for the climb.
- `recTwoOboeOne` opens `R1*15 | g'4\ff( e''2.~ |`. Ob 1 now rests from 139 and enters fresh at the 154 climax. The climax notes are unchanged.

The harmony is still covered by Hn 3·4, the viola syncopations and the harp. Ob 1 has 45–46 before the exposition rest and the 137–138 solo before the recapitulation rest, so neither leaves a fragment.

### 2. Bars 19–22: Ob 1 doubles the first theme's consequent phrase (applied)
**What is wrong.** Vln I states 15–18 alone. Ob 1 then joins it in unison for 19–22, including the d♭''' peak at 20, which is high and strained on the oboe. This covers the viola espressivo counterline (cycle 37) in the oboe's most penetrating octave. It also spends the oboe colour before the hemiola at 23, where the woodwinds should arrive as a group.

**Evidence.**
- Tchaikovsky 6/i 30–39: Vln I carries the first theme with no wind doubling.
- Rimsky-Korsakov (Chapter II): "the strings will predominate provided that the two instruments are of equal power, e.g. when violins are coupled with an oboe". The oboe adds nothing that a ff violin section on the A string lacks.
- Cycle 38 left the levels alone but did not argue that the doubling was needed.

**Fix.** In `tOneOboeOne`, replace everything before bar 23 with `R1*8 |`, and make bar 23 `es''4(\mf\< d''4 ...`. The dynamic matches Ob 2 (`g'4\mf\<`) on the shared staff.

The 15–22 sentence is strings, bassoon and Hn 3·4 only, and 23 becomes a woodwind colour entry. No melody notes change.

### 3. Bars 35–38: Ob 1 doubles Vln I's imitative entries; the clarinets have a one-bar fragment at 36 (applied)
**What is wrong.**
- The transition imitation is a dialogue: Vla at 34, Vln I at 35, Vla offset by half a bar at 37–38. Ob 1 in unison with Vln I turns it into wind-plus-string against string, and it covers the viola answers.
- Cl 1·2 hold a single bar (g'/e♭' at 36) between rests, the only unprotected one-bar wind fragment in the score.

**Fix.**
- `transOboeOne`: `c''8->\ff r8 r4 r2 | R1*5 | \partCombineSoloI d''8\mf\<(^\markup \bold "1." es''8 f''8 bes''8) d'''2-> | f'8( ... fis'') | \partCombineAutomatic g''1\ff~ |`. Ob 1 now enters at 39 with Fl 1 for the climb.
- `transClOne`: `es''8->\ff r8 r4 r2 | R1 | bes'1\p( | g'1) | R1*4 |`
- `transClTwo`: `c''8->\ff r8 r4 r2 | R1 | f'1\p( | es'1) | R1*4 |`

Bar 35 is B♭7 (bass B♭, viola d', cello a♭), so the clarinets now sustain V–I into 36 and replace the oboe with an independent harmonic role. I first tried a♭'→g' in Cl 1, but `vl2.py` flagged an octave with the cello/bassoon 7th resolution (A♭→G), so I used b♭'→g'. After the change there is no new `vl2.py` line, and the fragment check finds no new one-bar wind entries anywhere.

### 4. Bars 103–106: Ob 1, Tpt 1 and the flutes all on the wedge line; Ob 1 should rest with the flutes (applied)
**What is wrong.** Cycle 34 rested the flutes at 103–106 so that they enter fresh at 107. Ob 1 still doubles Vln I an octave below throughout (with Tpt 1 mp), so the f entry at 107 is only half an event.

**Fix.**
- `devOboeOne` bars 27–31: `R1*4 | des''4\f c''4 es''4 des''4 |`.
- `devOboeTwo`: `\partCombineSoloII aes'1\mf\cresc | bes'1 | c''1 | c''2 b'2 | \partCombineAutomatic aes'1\f |`. The f at 107 is equal for both oboes, and the "2." label is printed at 103.

Flutes and Ob 1 now enter together at 107. The restart at 103 is lighter, which suits the cycle 8 "mf/mp restart".

### 6. Bars 98–102: Vln I in four doublings across three octaves (not applied, protected)

**Fix (if the composer chooses).** If this passage is ever reopened, drop Ob 1 at 98–102 and keep the cor anglais colour (cycle 4). Not applied: it is protected by cycles 29 and 31.
