## Findings

### 1. Three text items collide with the barline or run past the end of the system (applied)

**Bars and what is wrong:**

- **55.4, Vln I and Vln II** (tTwo.ily). "molto cantabile" is left-aligned at the pickup E♭4, the last beat of the system before the break at 55/56 (c329 set this). The text runs to x = 807.88 pt, the end barline of the system. In Vln II it sits in the space between the two violin staves, so its last letter runs into the span barline. In Vln I it ends flush with the barline.
- **72.3, Vc and Cb** (clos.ily). "change bow at will" on the tied A♭ at 72.3, the last half-bar before the break at 72/73, ends at 807.94 pt. In both staves it sits between staves and the final "l" touches the span barline (checked at 250 dpi).
- **84, Timpani** (dev.ily). "muta A♭ in B♮", centred over a whole-bar rest in a narrow last bar, ends at 810.5 pt, past the end of the system.

**Evidence:** Gould PDF 55 (Spacing symbols): "Where space is limited, the distance between characters should not be less than ½ stave-space and no characters should collide." PDF 56 (Mid-system): "Allow a stave-space after a clef and on either side of a barline before a notational symbol … Where a barline comes before the end of the stave, allow a stave-space at the end of the stave." Gould's introduction (PDF 9) gives the reason: a part or score that makes the player stop to read a marking costs rehearsal time. These three are the only places in the score where text reaches the right edge (the bounding-box scan lists nothing else except key signatures, page numbers and the "attacca" end mark, which is meant to sit there).

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-375/work):**

- tTwo.ily, Vln I and Vln II at 55–56: "molto cantabile" moves from the 55.4 pickup to the 56 downbeat (`c''2.~\<^\markup \italic "molto cantabile"`). Vln II's "unis." stays on the pickup and is centred on it (`^\tweak self-alignment-X #CENTER ^\markup "unis."`); it now ends at 797.4 pt, 3 spaces clear of the barline. The `\once \override TextScript.self-alignment-X = #LEFT` lines from c329 are removed. At 56 the text stands at the start of the new system above the continuing "sul D" line, so c329's aim (the two texts do not read as one marking) is kept.
- clos.ily, Vc and Cb: "change bow at will" moves from the tied note at 72.3 to its continuation at 73 (`aes1~^\markup \small \italic "change bow at will"`, and `aes,,1~` in the basses), the first bar of the next system. The note is the same held A♭ (72.3–76), and the bow change it describes happens in 73–76. c306's protection of the marking on the long bass tie is kept.
- dev.ily, timpani at 84: `R1^\tweak self-alignment-X #RIGHT ^\markup \small "muta A♭ in B♮"`. The text now ends at the centre of bar 84 (788.3 pt), still on the bar right after the last stroke at 83, as c296 asks for retuning marks.

I tried three other forms first and rejected them. Right-aligning "change bow at will" to 72.3 pushed it back across the 71|72 barline. Right-aligning "molto cantabile" to 55.4 put "unis." above "tutti, div." over the beat-1 chord in the Vln II part. A `\once \override` before the timpani rest at 84 split the multi-bar rest in the timpani part (parts-lib merge-mmrs!), which moved the cue at 113 and made the part 3 pages. The tweak form has none of these effects.

No pitch, rhythm, dynamic or bar changes. Each changed line has a c375 comment.

### Considered and kept (no change)

- **"sul D" at 55.4** ends at 806.2 pt, 1.7 pt (just under ½ space) from the system's end barline. It is the start of a text spanner, it does not touch the barline, and moving it off the pickup would misplace the string instruction. Keep.
- **Hn 3 "pp" under C4 at 110** sits about 0.45 space below the notehead (measured at 600 dpi), at the limit of PDF 55's ½ space but not touching. LilyPond default placement; not worth a manual offset.
