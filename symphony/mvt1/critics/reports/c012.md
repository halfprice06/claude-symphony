# Critic 012: v31, lens 2 (harmony and voice leading)

## What I studied
I read BRIEF, HISTORY, LENSES, c002, v31 `critique.md`, `analysis.txt` and the section files. `score.midi` is part-combined, so I compiled a separate MIDI with one staff per instrument variable, 30 tracks in the order critic 2's scripts expect. I ran `vl2.py` and `clash.py` on it. I also ran a variant of `vl2.py` with the parallel-fifth "doubling run" filter switched off. That filter hides any run of three or more consecutive fifths, and that is how bar 96 has escaped every check since v21. I checked every hit in the changed passages by hand against a per-instrument note dump.

Corpus: Tchaikovsky 6/i, `musicxml-converted/xml/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.musicxml`. At bars 171–176 the fugato entries are staggered (violas 171, violin II 175, cellos/basses 174–175). At bars 190–195, at the development climax, the descending scale is doubled only in octaves (oboes/bassoons, trumpets in octaves) over held thirds in horns and trombones. No line is doubled at the fifth.

Web:
- Rimsky-Korsakov, *Principles of Orchestration*, ch. III, "Consecutive octaves and fifths" (https://gutenberg.org/files/33900/33900-h/33900-h.htm). It says consecutive octaves between upper parts are not permissible. It tolerates fifths only when they come from octave-duplication of parts that move in sixths.
- Harmony and Musicianship, "Deceptive resolutions" (https://pressbooks.pub/harmonyandmusicianshipwithsolfege/chapter/deceptive-resolutions-of-secondary-dominants), and Ithaca College, "Deceptively-resolving dominants" (https://musictech.ithaca.edu/MusicTech/ICTheory/OnlineText/TheoryII/Unit2/DecRes/DecRes.html). Both say that in V7–VI the 7th falls to the fifth of VI and the leading tone rises to the doubled third.

All fixes below were applied together in a scratch build (/tmp/critic-012/mvt1/v31/). It builds with 0 warnings, and the outer-voice check still shows only the known bar-96 false positive. The all-voice check shows no new parallel fifths. The only new parallel octaves are the intended doublings at 124 beat 4 and 169 beat 3.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 96: five consecutive parallel fifths in the fugato stretto
**Problem.** The top line (Fl 1, Ob 1, Vln I) plays F–D♭–C–C♭–B♭. The line under it (Cl 1, Vln II, Vla) plays B♭–G♭–F–F♭–E♭, the subject a fifth lower, starting on the same eighth. The result is five consecutive parallel fifths at f in the two highest string parts. This has been in the score since v21. The shipped scripts never flagged it because `vl2.py` drops fifth runs as "doublings".

**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov (ch. III) allows fifths only as a by-product of doubling parts that move in sixths. Tchaikovsky 6/i 190–195 doubles his stretto scale in octaves, and at 171–176 his fugal entries never start together.

**Fix (dev section bar 20).** Put the lower line a third below, so that both lines descend chromatically in parallel minor thirds. Hold the last note so that it does not form fifths with the brass D♭→C.
- `devClOne`, `devVlnTwo`: `r8 des''8 bes''4.-> a''8 aes''4 |`
- `devVla`: `r8 des'8 bes'4.-> a'8 aes'4 |`

### 2. Bars 123–124: the counterline's suspensions sound against their own resolution notes in Trumpet 1
**Problem.** The counterline E♭–D♭–C–B (Fl 1, Ob 1, Vln I/II) suspends across each beat. Trumpet 1 already holds the resolution note in the same octave as Ob 1 and Vln II:
- 123 beat 4 and 124 beat 1: D♭5 against trumpet C5, a minor 2nd at ff.
- 124 beat 3: C5 against trumpet B4, another minor 2nd. Horn 3 also sounds B3 here.

c002 fixed this same kind of fault at bar 124 (oboe 2, horn 1). The c007 counterline brought it back in the trumpet.

**Fix (recOne bars 1–2).**
- `recOneTptOne`: `g'1\ff | g'2. b'4 |` (Trumpet 1 doubles Trumpet 2. B on beat 4 moves with the counterline and avoids octaves with the bass G→A♭ into 125.)
- `recOneHnThree`: `c'1\ff | c'2. b4 |`

Beat 3 becomes G7sus4 over the melody's A♭ appoggiatura, and the whole chord resolves together on beat 4.

### 3. Bars 155–156: the new Hn 1·2 / Cl 1 counterline leaves the dominant 7th unresolved at the climax
**Problem.** At 155 beats 3–4 the only voice holding the chordal 7th F is the counterline (Hn 1·2, Cl 1, F4). The melody's F6 was a passing note. At the deceptive arrival on ♭VI (156) this F leaps up to C5. As a result the 7th never resolves, and the fff ♭VI chord has no E♭ in any instrument.

**Evidence.** Both pressbooks and Ithaca: in V7–VI the 7th falls to the fifth of VI, and the leading tone rises to the doubled third. The score already does the second part correctly (B→C in Hn 3, Tbn 1, Tpt 3).

**Fix (recTwo bar 18).** In `recTwoHnOne`, `recTwoHnTwo` and in `recTwoCounter` (which Cl 1 plays), replace `c''2\fff r8 c''8( bes'8 aes'8 |` with:

`es'4\fff c''4 r8 c''8( bes'8 aes'8 |`

F→E♭ resolves the 7th and supplies the fifth of ♭VI. The rising sixth E♭→C answers the motto's opening leap. The climax shape (154–157 is on the do-not-touch list) is unchanged.

### 4. Bar 42: c002's own fix created parallel fifths
**Problem.** Hn 1·2 `c'2 bes2` moves C→B♭ on beat 3, while the melody (Fl, Ob 1, Cl 1, Vln I/II, Tpt 2) moves G→F in four octaves. The result is C/G → B♭/F, fourteen voice-pairs of parallel fifths at ff dim.

**Fix (trans bar 10).** `transHnOne`, `transHnTwo`: `c'4 bes2.)\>`.

The 13th still falls to B♭ (c002's intent), but a beat before the melody moves. This also shortens the C4/D♭4 rub against Hn 3 and Tpt 3 to one beat.

### 5. Inner brass and horn fifths in exposed places: 66, 70, 132
- **Bar 66.** Hn 4 moves A♭3→F3 while the bass moves D♭→B♭: parallel fifths. I tried keeping A♭ as the prepared 7th of ii7, but that doubles the melody's A♭→G in octaves (66–67), and B♭ doubles its A♭→B♭. Fix: `tTwoHnFour` bar 20 `r1` (the horn rests, then re-enters on G3 at 67).
- **Bar 70 (codetta).** Tpt 2 moves A♭4→F4 on beat 3 against the bass D♭→B♭: parallel fifths. Fix: `closTptTwo` bar 2 `aes'1`. It becomes the 7th of B♭m7 and resolves to the G already written in bar 71. This touches no melody or texture of the protected codetta.
- **Bar 132.** Tpt 1 (C5→A♭4) moves against Tpt 3 (F4→D♭4) as a root-position triad shifted down a third: parallel fifths inside the trumpet section, ff. Fix: `recOneTptThree` bar 10 `f'1\ff` (common tone held; D♭ stays in Tbn 1 and Hn 3). Bar 133's `des'2 b2` follows by step.

### 6. Bar 169: cor anglais C4 against viola D♭4 in the coda's Neapolitan
**Problem.** Bar 169 is the Neapolitan sixth (D♭/F) of the iv–N6–i6/4–V cadence (168–170). The violas hold D♭4 for the whole bar. The cor anglais motto plays C4 against it on beats 1–2, a minor 2nd in the same octave, pp, between two similar reed and viola colours. The c010 change from horn to cor anglais exposed this. c002 objected to the same spacing at 42 and 65.

**Fix (coda bar 7).** `codaVla`: `c'2 des'2 |`

Beats 1–2 stay on F minor, and the Neapolitan arrives with the cor anglais's own D♭ on beat 3. The motto is unchanged, and so is the viola's c007 line E♭–D at 170.

### 7. Bar 64: basses and harp contradict the cellos' D
**Problem.** On beats 3–4 the cellos descend to D2 (D–A♭–C–F, the viiø7/V of the expanded cadence c002 praised). The double basses (`f,1`, sounding F2) and the harp LH (F2–A♭2–C3–F3) stay on F. The cellos therefore sound below the basses, and the real bass of the chord is unsupported.

**Fix (tTwo bar 18).**
- `tTwoCb`: `f,2 d,2 |`
- `tTwoHarpL`: `f,8( c f aes) d,8( aes, c f) |`

This changes the doubling only. The harmony is the same.

### 8. Bars 67–68 and 161–162: the cello arpeggio's held top note doubles the melody's 2–1 at the cadence
**Problem.** At both second-theme cadences, which c011 rebuilt, the cello arpeggio ends on a held half note an octave or two below the melody's penultimate note: B♭3 at 67 under B♭4, and D4 at 161 under D5. The cellos then drop to the bass on the melody's final note. The result is octaves between melody and bass at a perfect cadence. Suggested fix (not tested):
- `tTwoVc` bar 21: `es,8( bes, des g des'2)`, so the 7th D♭ falls to the C of bar 68's arpeggio.
- recTwo Vc bar 23: end on `b2` in place of the top D.
