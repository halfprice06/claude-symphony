## Sources

- MOLA, *Guidelines for Music Preparation* (`pdfs/`, PDF 7 = printed p. 5): right-hand pages odd, left-hand pages even; "allow each player ample time to turn the page. Consider the tempo … and the playing technique"; a multi-measure rest is preferred at the end of a page, otherwise the turn must still fall in rest. PDF 6: a cover page is "only essential when pagination requires the music to begin on page two in order to accommodate page turns". PDF 5: "Tacet until…" is not acceptable inside a movement, so a tacet or turn page is ruled out mid-movement.
- Gould, *Behind Bars* excerpt (PDF 9 = p. xi): "ill-judged page-turns … can hinder a good performance". PDF 10 (p. xii): tacet only for a whole movement. PDF 8 (contents): ch. 18 "Part Preparation" (p. 555) is not in the excerpt.
- Web: Finale *Smart Page Turns* plug-in manual, https://usermanuals.finalemusic.com/Finale2012Mac/Content/Finale/Smart_Page_Turns.htm. It adjusts breaks "to avoid page turns during active portions of the music", treats first page right or left as a setting, and optionally adds blank pages for the best turn solution: the same right/left-page logic used here.
- Web: MOLA *Music Preparation Guidelines for Orchestral Music* (older edition hosted by NJ Symphony), p. 5: "The page layout should allow comfortable page turns." https://njsymphony.org/assets/doc/Music-Preparation-Guidelines-for-Orchestral-Music-1-b9203af5a3.pdf

## Fix (applied in scratch, `parts-gen.py`)

Every affected part now ends mvt I on an even, left-hand page. Mvt II p. 1 faces it, and each earlier turn falls in a multi-measure rest. All pages below were viewed as images (pdftoppm).

| Part | New layout | Turn in mvt I |
|---|---|---|
| Vln I | no cover, 2 pp. | 10-bar rest 84-93 at the end of p. 1 (about 19 s) |
| Vln II | no cover, 2 pp. | 4-bar rest 103-106 at the top of p. 2 (about 7.6 s) |
| Vla | no cover, 2 pp., spacing-increment 1.45 (part only) | 4-bar rest 103-106 at the top of p. 2. This is the viola's last rest of 2 or more bars. Without the tighter spacing, 173-182 spilled onto a 3rd page. |
| Vc | no cover, 2 pp. | 3-bar rest 107-109 at the end of p. 1 (about 5.7 s), its only rest of 2 or more bars |
| Cb | cover + music on pp. 2-4, forced `\pageTurn` after bar 174 | pp. 2-3 form a spread with no turn; the turn is in the 4-bar Più lento rest 171-174 at the end of p. 3 (about 13 s). P. 4 holds 175-182. The alternative 2-page layout turns in a 2-bar rest at crotchet 126 (about 4 s), which is too tight. |
| Hn 3, Hn 4, Timp | cover + music on p. 2 | none (cover page per MOLA PDF 6) |
| Harp | unchanged (cover + 2 pp.) | not affected |

The strings no longer need c185's cover: each has a rest long enough for the one turn. The cover moves to the 1-page parts (Hn 3, Hn 4, Timp) and the Cb.
