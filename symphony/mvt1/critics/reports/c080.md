## Findings

**1. Cut the violins' literal restatement of the head, old 156–159 (applied).**
- **Problem:** at J the head is played in C major by Hn 1, Va and Vc at 147–150. The violins then repeat the same four bars at the same pitch at 156–159, and the head comes back a third time at the climax (162). That is three heads in 16 bars. The exposition did the same at 47/56/62, so the recap adds nothing new here. It also uses up, in movement I, the violins singing the major head at mf, which is the strongest thing movement III could bring back.
- **Evidence:** Tchaikovsky 6/i drops one of its two exposition statements (89 and 130) and gives one statement at 305 that grows into the climax. Our recap now has the same shape: one statement (147–155), a two-bar build, then the climax. Dvořák 9/i keeps both statements (374/382), so the corpus is split on whether to compress. What decides it here is the cyclic purpose. Movement I already uses its main material heavily (cyclic notes, critic 40), so III needs something movement I has held back. The recap first group is already half length (protected, cycle ~215), so this cut keeps the recap proportionate: 20 bars against the exposition's 22.

**2. Cyclic advice: what the recap now withholds, and where it goes (applied to cyclic-notes.md as an appended note).**
- **Withheld:**

In movement I, the major falling head is now heard only at pp in Hn/Va/Vc (147–150). At the climax (158) it appears only in its rising form.
  - (b) The chromatic counter under it, `r4 c''4( b'4 bes'4 | a'2) c''2~( | c''4 bes'4 a'2 | g'2 f'2)`, with the C tonic pedal (`c1 | c1 | d2 f,2 | g,1` in the bass).
- **Use:** movement III, after the brass chorale has arrived in C major. The violins sing (a) with (b) over the tonic pedal, mf–f, molto cantabile, the lyrical statement the first movement's recap left out. Do not use it in movement II, which should keep to the minor/anacrusis form and the motto's tail (existing notes). Movement III can then present three things movement I held back:
  - the brass forte falling head (critic 40);
  - this violin statement with its counter;
  - the fast brass combination of both heads (critic 70).
