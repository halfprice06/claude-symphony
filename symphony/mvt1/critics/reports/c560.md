### 2. The coda's trombone chorale and its timpani-without-trumpets pulse set up the finale correctly (verified; keep)

**Bars:** 175–182.

Adler distinguishes the homogeneous consort (musica sociata) from the heterogeneous one (musica fracta) (adler1 PDF 21), and notes that the timpani "invariably played together with the trumpets" (same page). Movement I's coda uses both points for the cycle. The chorale at 175–179 is a homogeneous consort: three trombones pp dolce, with B.cl, cellos and basses only doubling the bass trombone. The finale's chorale in C major (IV 224–228, 255–264) is the mixed brass choir (trumpets and horns with an independent bass, f–ff), which is on the "Do not reuse" list. So the finale's chorale differs in consort as well as in mode and dynamic, and it will sound like an arrival, not like a louder repeat of 175.

The pairing measurement on full.midi shows that every loud timpani stroke in movement I comes with trumpets. The timpani play without them only in soft rolls (1, 11–13, 40, 62–63, 76, 80–81, 97, 117, 144, 166, 174) and in the coda pulse `c4 r8 g,8 c4 r4` at 179–180 and the roll at 181. The trumpets' last note is the unresolved B4 at 170. The finale brings the pair back together where that B resolves (Tpt 1 B4→C5 with timpani G→C at IV 222→223). The plan is consistent; c460 found the same, and the v560 MIDI is identical.

**Fix:** none.

### 3. Where the movement could still fall short in this lens: three candidates considered (no change)

I looked for a place where a listener would miss something the later movements need, using the reading's rules on marking a line's high point and choosing an instrument for a register.

- **The brass motto's apex at 118.** Adler's Ex. XV-32 (adler2 PDF 187) marks the high note of a line by adding instruments on that note. At 118.1 the motto's E♭4 apex is in Hn 1·2, Tbn 1·2 and Bsn 1 at pitch with B.tbn an octave lower (snapshot), with no trumpet or added octave above. Adding Tpt 1·2 on the E♭ (or doubling the line at E♭5 in the trumpets, the three-octave power spread of PDF 189) would mark it. I recommend against it. The trumpets' first entry in this stretch at 122 is what makes the fff arrival on A♭ over G new in colour as well as loudness. A trumpet statement of the motto at ff would also come close to the finale's reserved "falling motto in full brass at forte" (mvt1.md, "Do not reuse"); in minor it is not the same thing, but it would make the finale's major brass motto less new. 117–122 is protected (HISTORY.md cycle 86: motto pitches and ties 117–124; cycle 104: 117–122), and this reason does not answer that protection.
- **The coda cor anglais 171–174.** Adler's register rule (adler2 PDF 188, Ex. XV-33) says the same tune a fifth higher needs a different instrument. The coda C.a. sounds G4–E♭5, a fourth above its intro statement (D4–B♭4, 5–8); c551 noted the higher register. It stays where the Berlioz solo itself goes at its peak, and the cyclic rhyme with 5–8 and the duet with the solo cello (answered by II's solo violin and Ob 1 at 15–22) are protected and quoted. An at-pitch viola doubling (Adler's affinity list, PDF 188) would thicken a pp duet that needs to stay two voices. Keep.
- **Foreshadowing the finale's F1/F2 or the scherzo's main theme more openly.** Not reopened. c260, c330, c460 and c510 rejected it with the Tchaikovsky 4 / Brahms 1 precedent (the finale's themes arrive new; the cyclic material is the motto and the stop chord), and the scherzo already has its seeds at pitch (78–79 G♯5–G5, quoted by scherzo v471; P bar 2 rhythm; Hn 1 D♯5 at 106). Adler's list of works (adler2 PDF 195) names Tchaikovsky 4's finale for the return of first-movement material, not for previews of finale tunes.

None of these is a real improvement. The movement's arc in this lens holds: A♭ reached at 82, 122 and 160 with rising strength and never resolved to C major at forte; the stop chord left in silence at 170; the trumpets silent after their unresolved B at 170 while the timpani keep the pulse; a homogeneous pp minor chorale; a ppp C-major chord whose violas' E♮ passes into movement II. Every finale reserve holds in the v560 MIDI (identical to v551, which c550 checked: Vln I and flutes top at A♭6, Vln II at C6, tuba at F2, Tpt 1·2 end on B4 at 170).

**Fix:** none.
