# Critic 058, lens 8 (expression), on v77

## What I studied
I read every section file, global.ily, score.ly and HISTORY.md, and looked at PDF page 15 of my rebuilt copy. To check the slurs, I wrote a LilyPond probe, `/tmp/critic-058/probe/probe.ly`. It puts each player on a staff of their own and logs every note, slur start and end, tie and dynamic, with bar and beat, to `events.tsv`. Three scripts read that file, all in `/tmp/critic-058/scripts/`:
- `slurs.py doubling` compares slur patterns, bar by bar, between parts that play the same rhythm and pitch classes.
- `slurs.py recap` matches bars by rhythm and interval content, transposition-invariant, and compares the exposition with the development, recap and coda.
- `slurlen.py` converts every slur to seconds, using the tempo map and the current dynamic, for breath and bow checks.

`corpus_slurs.py` runs the same doubling comparison on the MusicXML corpus. In doubled bars, the masters agree as follows:

| Pair of parts | Tchaikovsky 6/i (same / differ) | Dvořák 9/i | Beethoven 9/i |
|---|---|---|---|
| String with string | 222 / 11 | 69 / 8 | 254 / 76 |
| Wind with wind | 62 / 27 | 165 / 0 | 397 / 14 |
| String with wind | 77 / 102 | 4 / 30 | 297 / 94 |

So string sections slur alike, wind pairs slur alike, and winds and strings often differ on purpose. Two examples:
- **Dvořák 9/i bars 47–50** (`Dvorak_Sym9_mvt1.musicxml`): the oboes slur each bar, and Violin I plays the same eighths with separate bows.
- **Tchaikovsky 6/i bars 305–312:** Flute 1, Violin I and Violin II have identical slurs. At 309–311 an accented appoggiatura is slurred to its resolution (`D#6->( C#6)`).

Web sources:
- Gould, *Behind Bars*, as quoted at https://www.notat.io/viewtopic.php?p=12173 (search excerpt; the page itself returned 403). In string music a slur always means bowing. In winds, keep the slur for legato and leave out phrasing marks.
- Robin Hoffmann, https://www.robin-hoffmann.com/?p=4043. A full violin bow lasts about 12 s at pp, 3 s at mf and 1 s at f.
- https://wiki.youngcomposers.com/Bowing_technique. Players split long slurs because of their length.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Recap 160–161: the slur stops on the suspension and starts again on the resolution (applied)
**Problem.** In Vln I/II the slur stops on the suspension and starts again on the resolution: `d'''2\mp( c'''2) | b''4\>( …`.
- The c''' on beat 3 of 160 is the 4–3 suspension over G (cycles 23, 48). Its resolution b'' is re-bowed and starts a new phrase.
- The diminuendo starts on the resolution, not on the dissonance.
- In the exposition at 66–67, the line is one slur, `bes''2\>( aes''2 | g''4 …)`, and the 4–3 resolves inside it.

**Evidence.** Tchaikovsky 6/i 310 slurs the dissonance to its resolution.

**Fix.**
- Vln I: `d'''2\mp( c'''2\> | b''4 a''8 g''8 f''4 d''4) |`
- Vln II: `d''2\mp( c''2\> | b'4 a'8 g'8 f'4 d'4) |`

The trough levels from cycle 53 (mp at 160, p at 162) are unchanged.

### 2. Recap 131: the head figure has no slurs (applied)
**Problem.** At 131, Fl1, Ob1, Cl1, Vln I and Vln II play `r8 aes''8\< f'''4.-> es'''8 d'''8 c'''8` with no slurs. The same bar in the exposition (29) is slurred: `r8 aes''8( f'''4.->) es'''8( d'''8 c'''8)`. Cycle 28's rule slurs the head everywhere except 82, 85–96 and 109–112, so 131 breaks the rule.

**Fix.** In all five parts: `r8 aes''8\<( f'''4.->) es'''8( d'''8 c'''8) |`, with octaves adjusted. In Vln I, the `\ottava` goes after the opening `(`.

### 3. Recap 157: the string sections slur the turn differently (applied)
**Problem.** Vln I/II slur the turn `d'''4( b''8 c'''8)`. Vla and Vc carry the tune an octave or two lower (cycle 4) and have no slur, so the two halves of the string body bow it differently. Hn1·2 and Tpt1 also have none.

**Evidence.** In the corpus, string sections agree in 95% of doubled bars (Tchaikovsky 222/11).

**Fix.** `f'2 d'4( b8 c'8)` in recTwoSync (Vla), recTwoArp (Vc), Hn1 and Hn2. Tpt1 gets `f''2 d''4( b'8 c''8)`. Flutes and oboes keep their whole-bar slur, because in the corpus winds often take a longer slur than strings.

### 4. Exposition 64–65: an 8-beat slur at f in the violins (applied)
**Problem.** `aes''2.~( aes''8 g''8 | f''4. es''8 des''4 f''8 aes''8)` puts 4.3 s at f in one bow. The recap version (158–159) is already bowed by the bar.

**Evidence.** Hoffmann gives about 1 s per full bow at f. Gould says string slurs are bowings.

**Fix.**
- Vln I: `aes''2.~( aes''8 g''8) | f''4.( es''8 des''4 f''8 aes''8) |`
- Vln II: the same an octave lower.

The 66–67 slur stays, because a diminuendo is easy on a long bow. Exposition and recap now bow alike: split at 64|65 and 158|159, one slur over 66–67 and 160–161.

### 5. Wedge 105–106: the new 4–3 suspensions are tongued at the resolution (applied)
**Problem.** Cycle 57 tied G over the barline and resolved it to F on beat 2. In winds and brass, `g'1~ | g'4 f'2.` re-tongues the F, which accents the resolution.

**Fix.** Slur from the tied note to the resolution in Cl 2, Hn 2, Hn 4, Tpt 2, Tbn 1 and Bsn 1: `g'1~( | g'4 f'2.)`, with octaves adjusted. This takes 4 s of breath during a crescendo, which is fine. The Vc is left alone: a new bow on the F under the crescendo is normal.

### 6. Fugato free voices 92–95: the suspensions are not slurred to their resolutions (applied)
**Problem.**
- Vc 92: `es'4~ | es'4 des'4`. The 4–3 over B♭ has no slur, while the 7–6 at 93 has one.
- Vla 94–95: `f'1~ | f'4\< ges'4` has no slur.

**Fix.** Each slur starts on the first tied note:
- Vc: `c'2. es'4~( | es'4 des'4) c'2~( | c'4 bes4) g2`
- Vla: `f'1~( | f'4\< ges'4) bes2`

The notes, the entries and the fugato staccatos are unchanged.

### 7. Bar 118 and coda 167: the delayed leading tone and the neighbour note are unslurred (applied)
**Problem.** At 118, `c'4\p b2.` in Cl 2, Hn 3, Vla and Vc (`<g c'>4 <g b>2.`) means a new bow or new tongue on the resolution, at p. At 167 the viola's neighbour `c'2\pp b2 | c'1` would be re-bowed twice.

**Fix.**
- Cl 2 and Hn 3: `c'4\p( b2.)`
- Vla: `c'4\p( b2.)~ | b1~ | b2. r4`
- Vc: `<g c'>4\p( <g b>2.)`
- Coda Vla: `c'2\pp( b2 | c'1)`, which is 6.7 s at pp, well within one bow.

### 8. Differences between doubling parts that are correct and need no change
- **97–102:** the winds take two-bar slurs (cycle 28) and the strings slur by the bar. Vln I and II agree with each other, and Fl1, Ob1 and CA agree with each other.
- **154–156:** the winds slur and the strings and brass play separate notes. Dvořák 9/i 47–50 does the same.
- **123–126:** the trombones are unslurred, as decided in cycle 8.
- **60–63:** the Tpt1 counterline has the same slurs as Cl1, and its p< to mp sits below Cl1's mf, which is correct for a soft trumpet.
- **33–34:** the bassoon solo uses the head's slurs.
- **Cycle 49 staccatos:** they are consistent in all fugato entries (85, 88, 91, 94).
- **Harp 148–162:** unchanged.
- **CA 5–8:** a 15.6 s slur at p, protected and playable.
