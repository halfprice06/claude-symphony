The chapter is about film and television scoring. What carries over to a concert symphony is its teaching on: holding a colour back for one event, tying a sound to one meaning, transforming a device by changing its sound, the referential level a quotation depends on, pulsation used in place of percussion, and shared versioned records that keep a team's score coherent.

### 3. Two links not yet in the seeds list (record only)

**Bars:** 27, 30, 131 and 179–180; 13.

**What is wrong.**
- The timpani in the hammered tutti play `c4 r8 c8 c4 r8 c8` ff at 27, 30 and 131. The coda pulse `c4 r8 g,8 c4 r4` pp at 179–180 is the same figure, with its quaver moved to G and the last quaver dropped. So the figure that IV 229–230 quotes, and that runs through II 84–85 and III 9 and 363–371, is already heard three times at ff in movement I before its pp form at the end. This is what gives the finale's quotation its referential base (PDF 200). mvt1.md records the pulse only as motive x with its first quaver silent (c310). If a later critic thins the timpani at 27–32 or 131, the link is lost without anyone noticing.

### Checked and kept

- **Bar 158, full brass ff on the cadential 6/4 of C** (Tpt 1 E5; horns E4, G4, C5; trombones C4, G3, G2; tuba G1). The question is whether this spends the sound of IV 223, which Annihilation's rule would reserve (PDF 235–238), now that PDF 204 asks us to judge the sound, not the chord label. I keep it. The 6/4 stands over the G pedal and goes to V7 at 159 and ♭VI at 160. It has no stop chord before it and no trumpet B→C. The finale's arrival has root position, Tpt 1 B4→C5, the violins' C7 tremolo, clashed cymbals and bass drum. Those are five distinguishing features, and by PDF 238 the reveal stays "clearly distinct". This passage is also protected (c283, c337).
- **Bar 122, high violin tremolo fff on A♭6**, against the finale's tremolo arrival on C7. The pitch is a major third lower, the chord is a dominant minor ninth over G, and the tremolo collapses at 123–126. This follows the Man of Steel pattern (PDF 227): the colour appears early in a form that differs from the reward. The finale's own rule reserves "string tremolo with the violins' C7", not string tremolo in general.
