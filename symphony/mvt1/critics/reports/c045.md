## Findings

### 1. Bar numbers are not on one line and touch dynamics (whole score; worst at 19, 40, 41, 43, 56, 68, 90, 91, 104–108, 122, 166, 177)
**Problem.** Since cycle 35 each number is placed on its own, so it jumps up and down with the hairpins. Many numbers sit level with a dynamic and touch it ("40f", "43pp", "90mf", "166ff"). The "cresc." dashes run through 104–108, and at 177 the number touches the start of the hairpin.

**Evidence.** Hand-engraved scores keep bar numbers in one fixed position (Dvořák 7 conductor PDF, p. 12). Gould treats bar numbers as a layout element (ch. 16).

**Fix (score.ly, Double basses `\with`).** After `outside-staff-priority = #1000`, add:
`\override BarNumber.staff-padding = #5 \override BarNumber.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #0.5`

**Result.** All numbers now sit on one line below the dynamics, with no collisions on any page. The page count is unchanged.

### 2. Bar 111, bassoons: a false "1." (applied)
**Problem.** Bsn 2 rests on beat 4 (`g,2. r4`). The part-combiner hides that rest and prints "1." over Bsn 1's d'. Bsn 2 then comes back at 112 with no label, so the "1." stays in force wrongly.

**Evidence.** Dvořák 7/i Fagotti, lines 53–54 and 59 (above), handle the same situation. Gould's rule is numerals or rests, not a solo label for a one-beat rest.

**Fix (dev.ily, devBsnOne).** `es'2. \partCombineApart d'4 | \partCombineAutomatic des'2. c'4~ |`

### 3. Bars 127–129, Horns 3·4: the Hn 4 whole rest collides with Hn 3's beat-3 notes (applied)
**Problem.** Hn 4's R1 (from the cycle-16 rests) is centred in each bar and prints on top of Hn 3's third quarter note.

**Fix (recOne.ily, recOneHnThree).** `\partCombineSoloI c'4->\mp\< … aes4 | \partCombineAutomatic d'2.-> r4 |`

**Result.** "1." prints at 127, and the rests disappear.

### 4. Bar 41, Horns 1·2: "a2" missing (applied)
**Problem.** Both horns play `r2. es4(\ff | c'4 bes2.)`. Because the bar starts with a rest, the combiner prints the unison without a label.

**Fix (trans.ily, transHnOne).** `\partCombineUnisono r2. es4(\ff | c'4 bes2.)\> | \partCombineAutomatic r2 …`. This is the same device the coda uses at 163.

### 5. Bar 122, Bassoons: "1." missing (applied)
**Problem.** Bsn 1 enters alone after three bars in which both players rest. No label prints, even with `\partCombineSoloI`.

**Fix (dev.ily, last bar of devBsnOne).** `\partCombineSoloI g,4(\f\<^\markup \bold "1." es2 d4) |`. This is the same workaround as Fl/Ob at 39.

### 6. Bar 42, Clarinets: two hairpins in one bar (applied)
**Problem.** Cl 1 starts its diminuendo on beat 3, as the flutes and oboes do. Cl 2 starts on beat 1 (`bes1\>`). Because dynamics are engraved per staff, both hairpins print. Shared staves need equal simultaneous dynamics.

**Fix (trans.ily, transClTwo).** `\after 2 \> bes1`

### 7. Bars 84–85, timpani and cello (applied)
**Problem (timpani).** The muta text "muta C in B" was squeezed under the pp at the right edge of 84. "B" is also ambiguous, since German usage reads B as B♭, and the score writes "B♭" at 34.

**Fix (timpani, dev.ily).** Change to `<< g,1:32 { s2.\> s4\pp } >> | R1^\markup \small "muta C in B♮" |`, so the text sits on the rest bar 85 where the player retunes. At 103, change the text to "muta B♮ in C".

**Problem (cello).** `g,1\>` ran over the system break into the mf at 85, which reads as a diminuendo into mf.

**Fix (cello, dev.ily).** `r8\! d8\mf`

### 8. Bar 92, bass clarinet: "(low C instr.)" runs past the barline at the right margin (applied)
**Fix (dev.ily).** `bes,2^\markup \small "(low C instr.)" c,2`

**Layout note.** Moving the muta text changed LilyPond's line breaking (pages 4–9 reflowed, and page 4 became half empty). I restored the v64 systems with an explicit break before 85 in global.ily: `\mark \default s1*8 \break s1*12`. The development already starts its fugato there.
