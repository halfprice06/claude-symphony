## Findings

### 1. Cb 117 and 179: "change bow at will" is set larger than the same instruction everywhere else (applied)

**What is wrong.** The instruction "change bow at will" appears twelve times in the score. Ten are `\markup \small \italic` (Vc and Cb 3, Vc 13, Cb 41, Vc and Cb 73, Vln II, Vla and Vc 179, Vln I 181). The two double-bass marks at 117 (dev.ily, the G1 pedal) and 179 (coda.ily) are `\markup \italic` without `\small`, so they print at the normal text size. On page 18 the Cb mark at 179 is visibly larger than the identical marks in Vln II, Vla and Vc directly above it in the same bar; on page 11 the Cb mark at 117 is larger than its counterparts at 41 and 73. The size difference suggests a different kind of instruction where there is none. The source history (HISTORY line 584, "change bow at will Cb 117, 179") shows both were added in one earlier cycle before the others were standardised to `\small`.

**Evidence.** Gould introduction, PDF 13: "Effective communication results from establishing a convention and adopting a consistent approach." PDF 11: "what is familiar is of most help to the musician. The familiar can be read and understood quickly." The score's own convention is clear: every technical text of one kind has one size (all "sul G/D/A" spanners `\italic \small`, all timpani "muta" texts `\small`, all "étouffez" `\small \italic`), and these two marks are the only exceptions I found.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-575/work).**
- sections/dev.ily, 117: `g,,1\ff~^\markup \small \italic "change bow at will"`
- sections/coda.ily, 179: `c,1~^\markup \small \italic "change bow at will"`

Each with a one-line `% c575` comment. The parts take the change through parts.ily. No layout change: the same 17 music pages, the same system breaks, 0 warnings.

### Considered and kept

- **Vc/Cb "change bow at will" at 73** runs across the 73/74 barline because bar 73 is narrow. It hits no note, dynamic or other symbol, and c375 moved it here from 72.3 to clear the system end. Moving it to 74 would put it after the bow change it describes. Keep.
- **Vc 46.4-55**: tenor clef for 47-50, treble from 51. Bass clef for 47-50 would need three ledger lines for A-flat4 at 50 (allowed by PDF 21) but would still need treble at 51 for F5, so there would be the same number of changes; tenor is the cellist's normal clef for this register. Both changes fall between phrases (PDF 22). Keep.
- **Vln I 181**: "change bow at will" sits above the "sul D" dashed line. PDF 43 puts a long extension line outside short-term information only for octave lines; a string line is a different case, and the text is clear of it. Keep.
- **Hn 3·4 at 145**, two *pp*: both needed (c485). Keep.
- **Cb part, page 4 holds only 175-182**: this is what makes the page turn fall in the 171-174 rest (Gould PDF 9-10 on page turns; protected). Keep.

### Beyond the rules

I looked for a place where the notation stops the music from reading as clearly as a master's score: a phrase cut by a clef change, a climax hidden by an octave sign, a soft ending cluttered by text.
