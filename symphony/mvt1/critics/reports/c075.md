# Critic 075 — lens 5, engraving and notation (v94)

## What I studied
I rendered all 17 pages of v94/score.pdf at 100 dpi and zoomed to 200–500 dpi on bars 102–111, 60–63, 84, 117–128, 143–146, 162–186 and the title. I read global.ily, score.ly, dev.ily, recOne.ily, tTwo.ily and coda.ily. Corpus: Dvořák 7 (Mutopia, `reference-corpus/pdf/DvorakA_O70__DvorakSYMPH7_conductor.pdf`, p. 6, and its source `DvorakSYMPH7M1_Staves.ly`, which sets `soloText = "1."` and uses partCombine in the same way as this score). Web: Gould's *Behind Bars* has no searchable text online. For the condensing convention I used Steinberg's Dorico manual, "Player labels" (https://steinberg.help/dorico/v4/en/dorico/topics/page_formatting/page_formatting_condensing_player_labels_c.html, summarised in search results because the page now redirects to a PDF): a label is shown at the start of a phrase whose condensing differs from the previous one, and at the start of a system. Dorico's condensing follows Gould. A search on divisi rests turned up nothing citable (notat.io and Steinberg forum threads only).

## Findings (ranked)

**1. Bar 145, Oboes: "1." printed twice, and Ob 2's whole-bar rest overprints Ob 1's entry.** The code adds `\bold "1."` by hand, and partCombine adds its own "1." on top of that, so the score shows "1. dolce" above a second "1.". Ob 2's `R1*3` (144–146) is a multi-measure rest. The part-combiner keeps it and centres it in the bar, so it lands on the stem of Ob 1's g' (beat 3), and Ob 1's own r2 does not print. Fix (applied), recOne.ily: Ob 1 `R1*2 | \partCombineSoloI r2 g'4(\p^\markup \italic "dolce" e''4~ | e''2\> d''2)\! \partCombineAutomatic |`; Ob 2 `... | R1 | r1 | R1 |`. Result: "1." once, then the half rest on beat 1, then g'.

**2. Bar 110, Horns 3·4: Hn 4's whole-bar rest is printed next to Hn 3's pp C, and there is no "3." label.** This has the same cause as finding 1: Hn 4's `R1` stays visible, and the C has no player label after three bars of rest. Fix (applied), dev.ily devHnThree: `\clef treble \partCombineSoloI c'1\pp\< | \partCombineAutomatic`. It now prints "3." over a single C. The treble clef at 110 is right because 111–116 continue in treble. General note for the composer: on a shared staff, a multi-measure rest `R` in one player against notes and an `r` rest in the other shows the silent player's rest. Hn 3·4 at 127–130 shows the same effect, but there the rest sits clear above the note, so it is readable.

**3. Bars 107–111, Violins II div.: the lower half has no rest at 109, and "div." is repeated at 111.** At 109 the lower voice is a spacer `s1`, so the staff shows one F with nothing for the lower desks. A player cannot tell whether the lower desks double the F or rest. The MIDI has them silent. On a divided single staff each half needs its own rest when silent. At 111, Vln II are still divided from 107, so the second "div." is redundant: a label marks a change, not a continuation (Dorico/Gould rule above). Fix (applied): lower voice `\once \override Rest.staff-position = #-8 r1` at 109 (the default position collided with the F notehead); `^"div."` removed at 111. The sounding result is unchanged.

**4. Bar 106, Clarinet 2: the accent is printed below the note, on the open end of the diminuendo hairpin.** Ob 1 has the same notes at 106 with the accent above. Because Cl 2 is the second part-combined voice, its scripts go down, so ">" and ">——" merge into one sign. Fix (applied): `g''4.^>\> fis''8` in devClTwo.

**5. Bars 60–62, Violas: the crescendo hairpin runs through the rest into bar 62 and stops at the sub. p on beat 2.** Every other part gets sub. p on the downbeat of 62. The viola hairpin passes over the beat-4 rest of 61 and the beat-1 rest of 62, so the violas appear to crescendo past the moment the others drop. Fix (applied), tTwo.ily: `des''4) r4\! | r4 <c' es'>2\subP`. The hairpin now stops at the barline, and sub. p stays on the first note.

**6. Bar 175, Cor anglais: "espr." touches the stem of the metronome note in "Più lento (♩ = 72)".** Fix (applied), coda.ily: `g'4(\p_\markup \italic "espr."`. This places espr. under the p, the same placement cycle 35 chose for Fl 73.

**7. Bass clarinet low C requirement (92, 105–106).** The low-C instrument is needed for sounding C2 at 92 (written D3) and for B♭1/B1 at 105–106 (written C3/C♯3). The only instruction is a small "(low C instr.)" at 92, 13 bars and a page before 105. A player has to choose the instrument before the concert, so the requirement belongs on the first system or an instrumentation page. A mid-movement text is a reminder, not the instruction. Fix (not applied, because it changes the page-1 margin and should be checked against the 17-page layout): in score.ly, `instrumentName = \markup \center-column { "Bass clarinet" \small "(low C)" }`; keep the small text at 92.

**8. Title page and front matter (page 1).** The header has the title, the movement and "Full score in C — draft v94". The version number is correct. The page lacks a composer line and the transposition notes that a C score needs: double basses and contrabassoon sound an octave lower; bass clarinet to low C. Fix (not applied; it is Dan's call): `composer = "…"` and `subsubtitle = "Full score in C (Cb. and Cbn. sound an octave lower) — draft v94"`. Remove the draft label only for the final version.
