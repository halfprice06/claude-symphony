### 4. The scherzo's theme-A cell is heard twice at 78–79, not once (records; keep)

### 5. No hint of the finale's F1 or F2 should be added (keep)

The lens allows foreshadowing. I searched every part of the v380 MIDI for F1's head intervals (C5–G4–A♭4–B♭4–A♭4, any transposition) and F2's (B♭–G♭–F–E♭–G♭–B♭): neither occurs. c260 and c330 rejected adding them. Sebesky's rule is to vary a motive already used before bringing in a new one (PDF 11). Planting F1 in movement I would bring a new idea into the movement that owns the motto. The finale already derives its themes from movement I's material (its Ger6 at I 12, its E♭-minor colour at I 37, the B♭ pedal at I 37–44). The scherzo is already foreshadowed (78–79, P's second-bar rhythm, Hn 1 D♯5 at 106, the horn call). One more relation I found and do not recommend recording: at 39 Vln I, then Fl 1 and Ob 1, play D5–E♭5–F5–B♭5, scale degrees 3–4–5–1 over the B♭ pedal, the degrees of the trio tune's head (G♯–A–B–E). The figure is in quick eighths inside a run, so it is not heard as a theme.

### 6. The soft, slow span across the attacca does not sag (keep)
