## Findings

### 1. Bar 161, beats 1–2: the diminished seventh over D doubles its bass in four upper parts and has its leading tone in one trombone (applied)

What is wrong. Bar 161 follows the fff ♭VI at 160. On beats 1–2 the harmony is B–D–F–A♭ over the D bass (vii°6/5, going to V7 over G on beat 3). From full.midi:

```
161.1  D2 Cbn Tu Cb | D3 Bsn2 B.tbn | F3 Tbn2 | B3 Tbn1 | D4 Bsn1 Tpt3 Va | F4 Hn1·2 Tpt2 Va Vc | A♭4 Hn3·4 | D5 Cl2 | F5 Ob2 Tpt1 Vln II | A♭5 Ob1 Cl1 | F6 Fl1·2 Vln I
```

The bass D sounds in four upper parts (Bsn 1, Tpt 3 and the violas on D4, Cl 2 on D5). B, the root of the chord and the leading tone of C, is in Tbn 1 alone. The string section, the only group playing on every quaver, sounds only D and F: Vln I F6, Vln II F5, Va D4/F4, Vc F4, Cb D2. The violas are the one harmonic part of the strings here, and their double stop doubles both the tune's F and the bass.

Evidence. Rimsky PDF 83: "The bass of an inversion of the dominant chord should never be doubled in any of the upper parts", and PDF 84: "This applies also to other chords of the seventh and diminished seventh". His Bad/Good pairs on PDF 83–84 (viewed as images) differ only in an upper note on the bass's pitch class. PDF 85: "It is necessary to make sure that the harmonic notes are not lacking in the upper parts." PDF 87: "the resonance of different harmonic parts must be equally balanced", and PDF 88 says that when a sustained or repeated string chord needs filling, the violas' double stops should do it. Scan 1 shows that 161 is the one chord of this kind outside protected or quoted bars (finding 2). The vl.txt check also lists the doubling: `bar 161 beat 1.00 P8 clTwo:C5->D5 vla.lo:C4->D4`. Cl 2 and the violas' lower note move in octaves C→D into the doubled bass, which goes against PDF 83 ("consecutive octaves between the upper parts are not permissible").

Fix. The violas hold B3/F4 for the whole of 161, as they already do on beats 3–4. Their lower note now steps C4→B3 from 160 (the leading tone, with Tbn 1 at pitch) instead of rising C4→D4 onto the bass. The upper note F4 is unchanged. In first position B3 is on the G string and F4 on the D string, the same double stop the violas already play on 161.3–4, so the bar becomes one repeated chord.

```
% recTwoSync (violas), bar 161, was:
  <d' f'>8[ <d' f'> <d' f'> <d' f'>] <b f'>[ <b f'> <b f'> <b f'>] |
% now:
  <b f'>8[ <b f'> <b f'> <b f'>] <b f'>[ <b f'> <b f'> <b f'>] |
```

Applied in /tmp/critic-604/work/sections/recTwo.ily, with a c604 comment line above the bar. The chord now has three upper D's (Bsn 1, Tpt 3, Cl 2). Each of these was placed for its own line: c169 made Tpt 3 fall E♭4–D4–C4, Cl 2's D5 comes after c154/c408, and Bsn 1 is in octaves on the bass (c334). B now sounds in a brass colour and a string colour, and the strings now hold three of the chord's four notes. The violas' B3 is a unison with Tbn 1, the same unison they already make on 161.3. That is a doubling at pitch of strings with brass, not the trumpet-and-trombone unison that c169/c414 removed. The protected items in these bars are unchanged: the viola double stops (c426), 158 C4/E4 (c514), 159 B3/E4–F4 (c522), the tune's octave layout 158–161, the counter, the brass and the tutti layout 158–162 (c489). Only one viola note on two beats changes. The change undoes no earlier fix.

### 3. The three A♭ climaxes, weighed by PDF 51 (keep)

At 160 the tune's octaves weigh, in horn units at ff, A♭4 about 5 (Hn 1·2, Tpt 2, Vc), A♭5 about 3½ (Ob 2, Tpt 1, Vln II) and A♭6 about 2 (Fl 1·2, Vln I). This is the order PDF 96 describes: "the two extreme parts are the thinnest and weakest in tone, the intermediate parts the fullest and strongest". At 82 the head lies in three octaves (E♭6 Fl 1·2 + Vln I; E♭5 Ob 1, Cl 1·2, Tpt 1, Vln II; E♭4 Bsn 1, Va), with the brass held f in the middle. At 122 the V♭9 is spread from G1 to A♭6 in the order of the harmonic series (PDF 85), with seconds only in the tenor (c404) and every chord note in at least two colours. Each climax has its own colour and its own top (E♭6, A♭6 over the pedal, A♭6 over ♭VI), and the ladder is protected (c484, c574). Keep.
