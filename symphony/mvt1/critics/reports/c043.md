## Findings

### 1. Development 77–122: do not lengthen it. Its length and internal layout match the models.
**Bars.** 77–122.

**Assessment.** The development is not too short.
- **Length.** At 0.74 of the exposition it sits at the median of the corpus. It is longer than Dvořák 7 (0.32) and Dvořák 9 (0.61), and shorter than Beethoven 9 (0.89). Only the Eroica is far longer.
- **Share of the movement.** 46/178 bars is 26%. That equals Beethoven 9 (141/547 = 26%) and exceeds Dvořák 9 (96/452 = 21%) and Dvořák 7 (47/314 = 15%).
- **Internal layout.** It has every function Caplin's model asks for, in Dvořák 9's proportions. I measured those from the xml dynamics:

| Function | Dvořák 9/i | Share | v62 | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opening (ff) | 181–187 | 7% | 77–84 (motto dialogue, A♭ crash) | 17% |
| Quiet trough | 188–205 (p/pp/ppp) | 19% | 85–96 (fugato from a solo cello: core 1, a sequential model descending by fifths G–C–F–B♭) | 26% |
| Build and ff core | 206–260 (build 206–216, ff 217–260) | 57% | 97–114 (core 2: B-minor model 97–102, fragmentation over the chromatic bass 103–108, layered V pedal 109–114) | 39% |
| Release and rebuild | 261–276 | 17% | 115–122 | 17% |

Open Music Theory, summarising Caplin, expects "multiple cores (usually only two)" followed by a retransition. v62 has exactly that.

**Effect of adding bars.**
- An inserted sequence (for example the B-minor model repeated a step higher between 100 and 101) would push the ratio past Beethoven 9's.
- It would delay the fff at 114, which already has three ff steps before it (82, 97, 108).
- It would enter material that cycles 21, 29, 31 and 33 protected.

**Fix.** None. Do not add bars to 77–122.

### 2. Recapitulation first group 123–138: its compression is in the normal range. Keep it.
**Bars.** 123–138.

**Assessment.** The recapitulated P-zone (12 bars of theme plus the 4-bar link 135–138) is half of the exposition's P+TR (15–46). That is Beethoven 9's ratio exactly (301–338 against 1–79 gives 0.48). Dvořák 7 compresses harder (196–213 against 1–72 gives 0.25).

Each strand of the exposition's first group is accounted for:
- 123–126 = 15–18, with the tutti of 27–28 folded in;
- 127–130 = the hemiola 23–26;
- 131–134 = 19–22, rising to A♭6;
- 135 = the cycle-33 elision.

Nothing essential is missing. Lengthening it, for example by restoring 27–32, would add a third ff block in a row: the development's 97–116 plateau, then 123–134, then a restored tutti. That is the opposite of what the movement needs.

**Fix.** None.

### 3. Do not add slow music anywhere. The slow frame is already heavier than in the models.
**Bars.** 1–14 and 167–178.

**Assessment.** The introduction and the slow coda take 26 of 178 bars (15%), but at ♩ = 50–72 they last about 120 s of 414 s, or 29%.
- In bars, the models' slow frames are smaller: Tchaikovsky 6/i has 38/354 bars (11%) and Dvořák 9/i has 23/452 (5%).
- The models give more of their duration to fast music. Dvořák 9's exposition repeat alone adds about 157 fast bars.

Both slow passages are protected, and I do not ask for cuts. This follows Dan's guidance that length is not the constraint, but balance is.

**Rule for future revisions.**
- If the movement ever needs more weight, add Allegro bars, not Lento ones.
- No new rit./allargando words in 97–162.
- No slower metronome mark at 167 or 175.

**Fix.** None.

### 4. Bars 109–122: a long dominant lock, protected, but do not extend it
**Bars.** 109–122.

**Assessment.** The G pedal takes 14 of the development's 46 bars (30%). That is more than in Dvořák 9, where the retransition 261–276 runs over a C♯ diminished chord and B♭ and locks on B7 only at 273–276 (4 bars, per my bass extraction). Beethoven 9 enters its recapitulation at 301 with no long dominant pedal at all.

It works here because of the layering (109–113), the fff at 114 and the measured accelerando (119–122), as critic 23 found. I do not ask for a change.

**Rule.** Any future expansion of the development must not add dominant bars. If more weight were ever needed, the only place where length would cost nothing structurally is before 97, in non-dominant harmony.

**Fix.** None.

### 5. dev.ily header comment names the wrong key (documentation; applied)
**Bars.** dev.ily line 3 (bar 97).

**Problem.** The comment says "D3 97–108 (12): second theme in B♭ minor". Since cycle 2 the music has been in B minor (global.ily `\key b \minor` at 97, `\transpose c cis`). A future reviser reading the header could reason from the wrong key.

**Fix (applied).** Line 3 now reads:

`% D2 85–96 (12): fugato.  D3 97–108 (12): second theme in B minor (fugato ends in B♭ minor; shifted up a semitone at 97), then the wedge.`
