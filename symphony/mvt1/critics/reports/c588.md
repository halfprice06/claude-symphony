## Findings

No change. Five places were probed because they changed after the last pass of this reading (c488) or because the reading's rules bear directly on them. Each holds.

**1. Bars 125–126, Hn 1 recall of the horn call (c581).** Hn 1 enters mp espr. on E♭3 at 125.2; from the C4 it is doubled by Hn 3 (held C4, f > from 124), Cl 2 and violas. MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11) requires that parts on one line agree in stress and in the level they reach. All four reach p on the B3 at 126; Hn 1's > p and the others' written p at 126 agree. Hn 3's louder held C4 in 125 is a decaying pedal, not the line, which is the case Ex. 6's horns show (no [sf] on the held note). The Hn 1 part prints "mp espr. > p" without "Solo" (correct, as the note is doubled; 13 keeps "Solo"), and "sostenuto" and "a tempo" sit clear of the notes (Ex. 7). Keep.

**2. Bars 164–166, Cb part after c586.** The slur and tie G2–G2–G1 cross the system break at 164–165 without crossing the "rit." text; levels mp > at 162–163, decrescendo 165, p < at 166 with the down-/up-bow pair; nothing floats or collides (MOLA PDF 8, Ex. 7). Keep.

**3. Bar 108, viola swell (c518).** hp.py flags the > at 108.4 as leading to a higher level (mp cresc. at 111). The swell peaks on the C5 suspension and falls back to the phrase's p, then 109–110 continue at p with a < on 110. MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10) writes the same kind of shape, "pp < > mp", within one phrase; a swell returns to the prevailing level unless a new one is written. The mp at 111 is the start of the wedge, a new phrase after the viola's clef change. Keep.

**4. Bars 156–160, tempo.** "poco animando" at 156 has no word at 158 (K), and global.ily keeps accelerating (115, 118, 120 at 158–159) before "largamente" (100) at 160. MOLA PDF 4–5 and Ex. 4 treat a word as standing until the next one, so print and MIDI agree, as at 62–65 in the exposition. Keep.

**5. Bars 98 and 142, bow marks against wind doublings.** The scan lists Vln II's up-bow at 98 and the cellos' down-bow at 142 as articulation differences with the winds. They are string bowings, printed for an effect (MOLA PDF 5: bowings only for a specific effect): the 98 up-bow lets Vln II bow with Vc and Vln I (c538), and the 142 down-bow is the fresh B (c505). The stress marks themselves agree. Keep.

**Beyond compliance.** I looked for a place where the marking could make the music land harder, and tested one candidate in thought against the reading: a character word ("espr." or "appassionato") on Vln I's mf pickup at 155.4–156, where the violins take the S tune into the climax. I rejected it. The passage already carries "poco animando", mf with a crescendo through 157 to ff at 158, and the tutti entry; the reading's Ex. 6 shows a Presto tutti whose character is carried by level and stress alone, and c339 kept movement I without a major 'Grandioso' tutti as the finale's reserve (HISTORY.md, cycle 339). The rest of the expressive plan (sf at 20, sub. p at 62 and 84, the S swells, tenutos at 158 and 160 in every part on the tune, the staggered brass exits 162–163, morendo violas at 181) was weighed by c378, c458, c468, c478, c488 and c578, and nothing in MOLA bears against it.
