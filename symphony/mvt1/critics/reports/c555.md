## Findings

No change. I found no place where the score or the parts break a rule from this reading. The changes made after v466 were all carried into the parts correctly: the viola part prints the treble clef as a cautionary clef at the end of the line before 107 and returns to the alto clef at 111 (MOLA PDF 5, Ex. 3); the bow marks at 98, 110 and 131 appear in the Vln II and Cb parts; the harp part has mp at 47; and the c546 system break before 113 changes only the score.

I also asked where, inside this lens, the page could serve the music better. I looked at the three passages that carry the most weight and at one layout question.

1. **The horn call 41–42 (score p. 5; Hn 3 part).** Both horn staves have "a2", ff and "molto espr." under the pickup, and the diminuendo ends at p on 43. "molto espr." runs a little past the barline into 42 in the score. That is text extending from its note, not a collision with a note or a dynamic, and moving it would separate it from the pickup it describes. The call reads as one four-horn gesture. No change.
2. **The climax 158–162 (score p. 16).** Every part with the tune or the counterline has fff at 160, the held low brass stay at ff under it, "largamente" is over the top staff and Vln I, and the brass diminuendo at 162 ends at mp in every staff. The harp chord at 162 has f between its staves and its pedal change (A♮) under the bass staff, in the score only. A conductor can read every level at a glance (MOLA PDF 8, vertical proofreading). No change.
3. **The coda 171–182 (score p. 18; Vln I, Hn 3, timpani, C.a., harp and Cb parts).** Più lento, rit. and Tempo I appear in every part, also in rests (MOLA PDF 5, Ex. 4). The Vln I part has the Cl. 1 cue at 175–176 before its pp entry at 177, and its last line holds "unis.", "sul D", "rit.", "Tempo I", "change bow at will" and "attacca" without a collision. At 400 dpi the barline before 179 is a single barline with the half note's stem about one space after it, not a double bar. The Hn 3 part has the timpani cue at 179–180 before its ppp entry. The harp part has ppp and "étouffez" on its final chord. The tied parts end on even pages, so nobody turns into movement II.
4. **Double-bass part, page 4.** It holds one line (175–182). That follows from the cover page and the turn in 171–174 (parts-gen.py, c201); without the cover, the only turn would fall in the two-bar rest at 88–89, about 4 seconds at crotchet = 126, which MOLA PDF 7 asks us to avoid ("consider the tempo"). The single last line is the price of a safe turn. No change.

I found no engraving change that would make these pages clearer or make a climax or transition land harder on the page.

## Checked and kept

- **Left margin of the parts, 15.2 mm against MOLA's 20 mm (PDF 7).** The script found that the only thing printed inside the left 20 mm is the bar number at the start of each line. The staves begin at 20 mm, and the top, right and bottom margins hold (19.8–20.3 mm, ≥ 22.9 mm). MOLA's own Ex. 3 and Ex. 4 (PDF 10) print the line-start bar number ("7", "65") at or in front of the clef in the same way. Moving every number over the clef would change every system's skyline and puts at risk the page plan the attacca depends on (c201, c275, c393). No change.
- **"Entry without a dynamic" lines in the parts audit** (e.g. Fl 1 62, C.a. 67, Tpt 1 67, harp 154, Hn 3 179). I read the C.a., harp, Hn 3 and Vln I cases in the PDFs: they are cue notes, not real entries, as c495 found. Every real entry after a long rest that I read has its own dynamic (C.a. 69 f, 147 p, 171 p; harp 47 mp, 156 mf; Hn 3 181 ppp; Vln I 155 mf, 177 pp).
- **Multi-bar rests broken at 103 and 111 in the parts without key signatures.** Kept for the reason c465 gave: they are the section joins the conductor beats, and they match the score's double bars.
- **Score margins 15/12 mm.** MOLA PDF 7's 20 mm margin is in the paragraph on parts; for the score it sets only the staff size (4 mm minimum) and continuous family barlines, and both hold.
- **Composer name** on the cover and the part fronts (MOLA PDF 6). Closed at c200; I do not reopen it.
- **"draft v555" in the score subtitle** (MOLA PDF 6). This is the cycle stamp; c420 and c520 replace it with the version and date at each release check, and the next one is at cycle 620.
