# Critic 8 (lens: expression) on v27

## What I studied
I read every section file of v27 and checked pages of the PDF. In the corpus I used Tchaikovsky 6/i (`musicxml-converted/ly/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.ly`): the opening bassoon, bars 1–6; the tempo words around the second theme, bars 84–100; violin I at 89–100; and a count of all its expressive text. I also used Dvořák 9/i (Mutopia `DvorakA_O95/Sym9/Sym9-lys/brass.notesA.ily`, `woods.notesA.ily`, `percussion.notes*.ily`). The two scores differ sharply in expressive text. Tchaikovsky 6/i has 58 "cresc.", 29 "dim.", 10 "cantabile", 9 "ritenuto", 5 "incalzando", 4 "morendo" and 4 "largamente, forte possibile". v27 has 3 "espr." and 1 "dolce", and no tempo modification inside any section. Web sources:
- Rimsky-Korsakov, *Principles of Orchestration*, on comparative resonance and dynamic grading in the brass: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/33900/33900-h/33900-h.htm
- Barenboim on Mahler's separate dynamics for each instrument: https://danielbarenboim.com/mahler-interview-for-universal-edition/

## Findings (ranked)

**1. Dev 103–108 (dev bars 27–32): crescendo from ff to ff. Recap 131 (recOne bar 9) has the same problem.**
Most parts sound ff from 97 or earlier: Fl 1, Ob 1, Vln I/II, Bsn, Cbsn, B.cl, Hn 1·2, Tbn 1, B.tbn, Vc and Cb. These parts begin the 6-bar `\<` at 103 with no new starting level and arrive at ff at 109. Tpt and Tuba start at f. The wedge, the main build of the development, therefore cannot be played as written. At 131 the same thing happens over one bar: `r8 aes''8\ff\<` goes to `des'''8\ff`, and the Timp has `aes,1:32\ff\<` → `\ff`. Dvořák 9/i shows the usual practice: before a long cresc. the level drops first ("cresc. poco a poco" from p, strings.notesA.ily line 466).
Fix: in the dev bar 27 note, replace `\<` (or `\f\<`) as follows.
- Woodwinds and strings: `\mf\cresc`. For example, Fl 1 and Vln I: `aes''2.~\mf\cresc aes''8 g''8`; Ob 2: `aes'1\mf\cresc`; Cl 2: `f'1\mf\cresc`; Vln II: `<f' aes'>1:32\mf\cresc^"div."`; Vla: `c'1:32\mf\cresc`; Vc: `f1\mf\cresc`; Cb: `f,1\mf\cresc`. Do the same for B.cl, Bsn 1·2 and Cbsn.
- All brass: `\mp\cresc`. For example, Tpt 1: `aes'2.~\mp\cresc aes'8 g'8`; Tbn 2: `c1\mp\cresc`; Tuba: `f,1\mp\cresc`.
Pairs stay equal. The existing `\ff` at 109 (108 in Cl and Tpt) ends the cresc. At recOne bar 9, change `\ff` to `\f\<` in every part that has it, and keep the `\ff` in bar 10. Finding 6 gives the exceptions for the low brass.

**2. No tempo modification inside any section.**
Every change of pace is a new block tempo: 47, 69, 73, 139, 163, 167, 175. Tchaikovsky 6/i prepares its second theme with "ritardando molto" (84–86) and inflects it with incalzando at 93, ritenuto at 95–96, come prima at 97 and ritenuto at 99–100. Dvořák 9/iv writes "poco a poco ritardando" and "a tempo" (percussion.notesF.ily lines 64–67). Fix in `global.ily`, using Dvořák's syntax:
- trans (33–46): `s1*12 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "poco rit." s1*2`
- tTwo (47–68): `s1*19 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "rit." s1*3`. The "a tempo" at 69 then has a meaning.
- dev last block (109–122): `s1*4 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "allargando" s1*2 \tempo "a tempo" s1*8`. This gives allargando at 113–114 and a tempo at 115.
- recOne (123–138): `s1*14 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "poco rit." s1*2`
- recTwo second block (154–162): `s1*7 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "rit." s1*2`
- coda first block (163–166): `s1*3 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "allargando" s1`. The block before Tempo I (167–174): `s1*6 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "rit." s1*2`

**3. Second theme, cello and horn, 46–52 (trans 14; tTwo 1–6): broken phrasing, a pickup at the wrong level, and no swell.**
The Vc pickup `es4\p` (bar 46) leads into a theme marked mf. The slurs break the line at the barline and before es′ and b♭′, so the c′–b♭–a♭ descent has no slur. The violins' restatement at 55–57 has one slur over the whole phrase, so the two statements disagree. Tchaikovsky 6/i 89–100 marks each half-bar of his second theme with `\< \>` and tenutos on the peaks.

- transVc bar 14: `f2 g4 es4(\mf`
- tTwoVc bars 1–6:

  Bar 7 is unchanged and closes the slur.
- transHnOne bar 14: `r2. es4(\mp`
- tTwoHnOne bars 1–6:
`c'2.~\<^"dolce" c'8 bes8\> | aes4\! f'2--\< es'4\!) | des'4.( c'8 bes4 des'8\< f'8 | aes'2\> g'2\!) | es'4( c''2--\< bes'4\!) | aes'4.(\> g'8 f'2\!) |`
- Recap 138: the horn, violas and cellos are all mf. That breaks your own rule that the horn sits one level below the cello. recOneHnOne bar 16: `r2. g4(\mp^"dolce"`.

**5. Motto statements, 1–8 (intro 1–8): no dynamic shape.**
The motto's minor sixth and its chromatic fall are written at a flat pp, and the cor anglais answer at a flat p. Tchaikovsky 6/i bars 1–6 (bassoon) marks each cell `\< \>`, with terraced levels pp → p → mp and an sf on the peak.
Fix:
- introVc and introCb bars 1–4: `r2. g,4(\pp\< | es2.\>~ es8\! d8) | c2(\< des4\> c4~ | c1\!) |` (Cb an octave down)
- introBassCl: the same hairpins inside its single slur.
- introCorAnglais bars 5–8: `r2. d'4(\p\<^"espr." | bes'2.\>~ bes'8\! a'8 | g'2\< aes'4\> g'4~ | g'1\!) |`

**6. Brass balance at 12, 131–134 and 163–165.**
Rimsky-Korsakov: in forte one horn equals two woodwinds, and the "other brass" are marked one degree softer than the horns. Critic 4 applied this at 27–32 (trombones and tuba f). It was not applied elsewhere.
- At 12 three trombones play f against a melody carried only by Vln I and Fl 1. introTbnOne `c'2.\mf c'4`, introTbnTwo `es2.\mf es4`, introBassTbn `aes,1\mf`.
- At 131 (recOne 9): in Tbn 1·2, B.tbn, Tuba, Tpt 3 and Hn 3·4, write `\f` at bar 9 with no hairpin, and delete their `\ff` at bar 10.
- At 163 (coda 1): codaTbnOne `es'1\f`, codaTbnTwo `g1\f`, codaBassTbn `c1\f`, codaTuba `c,1\f`. The `\ff` at bar 4 stays.

**7. Expressive text is almost absent at the two climaxes and at the end.**
Tchaikovsky marks his climaxes "largamente, forte possibile" and his endings "morendo".
Fix:
- dev bar 21 (97): add `^"largamente"` to the first note of Vln I, Vln II, Fl 1, Ob 1 and the cor anglais.
- recTwoMelodyB bar 5 (154): `c'''4\fff^"largamente"`.
- The end:
  - codaVlnOne bars 15–16: `c''1~\> | c''1\ppp\fermata |`
  - codaVla bars 15–16: `es'1(\>^"morendo" | e'1\ppp\fermata) |`
- Closing 73: closFluteOne `r2. c''4(\p^"espr."`.

**8. Minor: the build warning "decrescendo too small" (dev.ily line 123).**
It comes from Hn 3·4 at 84, `s2.\p\> s4\pp`. Write `g'1\p\>` there and put `\pp` on the next note, or drop the hairpin.

None of these findings undoes an earlier fix. Finding 6 extends cycle 4's balance at 27–32 to its parallels.
