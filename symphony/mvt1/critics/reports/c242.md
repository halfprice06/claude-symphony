## Findings

### 1. Trumpet 1 at 132–134: two augmented seconds, B4–A♭4–B4 (applied)

Bars 132–134 (recapitulation, hammered tutti over the low P head). Tpt 1 plays G4 | G4 B4 | A♭4 (whole note) | B4 in the timpani rhythm. Bar 133 is the A♭ chord D–F–A♭ over the A♭ bass, a Phrygian approach to the V at 134 (bass ♭6̂–5̂). Tpt 1 leaps down B4→A♭4 into it and back up A♭4→B4 out of it. These are two melodic augmented seconds in the highest brass part at f–ff. A&S teach that the IV6–V approach in minor is voiced to avoid exactly this: Ex. 11-10d and e (PDF 169) mark the A♭–B augmented second "poor", and PDF 165–166 (Ex. 11-5) say the same for VI–V. No other trumpet passage in the movement has an augmented second (the scan found none at 27–32 or 167–170).

Fix: Tpt 1 holds B4 at 133. B is the leading tone common to the V of 132, the vii°4/2 of 133 (B–D–F–A♭ over A♭) and the V of 134, so the trumpet line becomes G4 | G4 B4 | B4 | B4: a held common tone, which is the smoothest connection the reading allows. A♭ is still in the bass (cbsn, tuba, B.tbn, basses, timpani), in Fl/Ob/Vln I·II and Cl 2, so the chord loses nothing; the brass chord at 133 now has the leading tone (Tpt 1 B4, Tpt 2 F4, Tpt 3 D4) and resolves to B4/G4/D4 at 134 by common tone and step.

recOne.ily, `recOneTptOne`: `g'4 r4 g'4 b'4 | aes'1 | b'4 …` → `g'4 r4 g'4 b'4 | b'1 | b'4 …`

HISTORY protects "notes of 131–134" (cycle 84) for the balance of the low theme under f brass, and cycle 174 accepted the trumpets' timpani rhythm. The theme, the brass dynamics and the rhythm are unchanged; only one held trumpet pitch changes, so the stated reason is not touched.

### 2. Bass A♭1→B1 into the stop chord at 25→26 (applied)

Bars 25–27 (exposition, end of the hemiola). The double basses play C2 | F1 F1 A♭1 | B1 (stop chord, ff) | C2, and Bsn 2 doubles F2–A♭2. With the root-position vii°7 at 26 (chosen by cycle 152) the bass line is ♭6̂–♮7̂–8̂ in C minor, A♭–B–C. This is A&S Ex. 11-14b (PDF 171), the bass line 6̂–7̂–8̂ in minor with VI before V6/5, marked "wrong" for the augmented second in the bass; Points for Review 8 (PDF 172) allows the rising bass only with IV6 from melodic minor (A♮). The A♭1 is also a fourth bass note inside the hemiola's fourth group (25.2–25.4), whose harmony is otherwise iv throughout. The recapitulation (137→138) does not have the problem: its bass goes A♭1→D2 into the 6/5 stop chord.

Fix: the bass takes D at 25.4. The harmony of beat 4 becomes iiø7 in root position (D–F–A♭–C: Hn 3 A♭3, Vla/Cl 2/Hn 2 C4, F in many parts), so 25–26 is iv – iiø7 – vii°7, roots falling F–D–B, the order A&S give as correct (PDF 172: "VI–IV–II–V is good"). A&S PDF 179 (Ex. 12-4b) note that in minor the 7th makes root-position ii° sound well, and the 7th C is prepared as a common tone from 25.2 (PDF 177). Bsn 2 takes D3 with the basses and repeats it in the stop chord, where it already had D3.

tOne.ily, `tOneCb`: `c,4 f,,4-> f,,4 aes,,4 | b,,2.->` → `c,4 f,,4-> f,,4 d,4 | b,,2.->`
tOne.ily, `tOneBsnTwo`: `c4 f,2-> aes,4 | d2.->` → `c4 f,2-> d4 | d2.->`

One point of dissonance treatment: the 7th C4 (violas, Cl 2, Hn 2) does not step to B in its own part; the B arrives at C4's register in Cl 1 (F4→B4) and the upper violas (B4). In a ff tutti that is the "rather free or complex texture" A&S exempt on PDF 177, and the alternative, iv6 with the augmented second in the bass, breaks the stricter rule. The stop chord at 26 (B root, every part) is unchanged, so this does not undo cycle 152. Hn 3's A♭3 is now the only A♭ on 25.4 and moves to D4 by tritone as before.

### 3. Checked and kept as written

- **161→162, top line B5→A5 at the second deceptive cadence (V7–vi in C major).** A&S PDF 203 say the deceptive cadence is strongest with 7̂–8̂ or 2̂–1̂ on top. Here the tune falls 7̂–6̂ while Tpt 1 (B4→C5) and Hn 2 (B3→C4) take the leading tone up, and the 3rd C of vi is doubled (Tpt 1, Tpt 3, Tbn 1, Hn 2), so Ex. 13-12's doubling rule is met. It should stay. Putting B5→C6 in the violins at fff would anticipate the finale's B→C arrival, which coherence/mvt1.md reserves ("Movement I never confirms C major at forte"), and 158–166 is protected as climax harmony.

It should stay.
