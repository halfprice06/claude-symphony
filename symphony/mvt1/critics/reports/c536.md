## Findings

No finding meets the bar for a change. Every principle in the reading that applies to this score is followed, bar by bar. The harp writing has been reviewed against this chapter three times before (c246, c336, c446), and each pass closed every gap it found. Nothing in the harp has changed since c446 except the entry level at 47 (p to mp, c519), which fits PDF 114: the 47-55 figures lie mainly in the upper-middle and top register, where the harp is light.

What I checked this pass beyond the earlier reports:

1. **The A♮ to A♭ change at 163.3 (new check).** No earlier report followed the LH A1/A2 struck at 162 (f). Low wound strings ring longest (Adler PDF 113-114), so this octave is still sounding at 163.3, where the A pedal moves for the A♭5 of 164. The étouffez at 163.3 has no hand named. Under the convention c446 set ("étouffez" = both hands, "(m.d.)"/"(m.g.)" = one hand), it damps the LH as well, so the A strings are stopped at the same instant as the mark. Adler PDF 113 says the harpist moves the pedal "just a split second before playing the note", and the A♭ is first needed at 164.1, a full two beats later at crotchet 100. The order is damp, then change, which is correct. No change. The part (page 3, bar 163) prints the étouffez above the RH and "A♭" under the LH at the same beat, which shows the order clearly enough.

2. **G♮ at 53.3 after the G♭4 of 51.** The G♭4 is struck on the second quaver of 51 and the change is marked 2.5 bars later (about 5.5 s at crotchet 112). G4 lies in the "rich and warm" middle register (PDF 113-114), which sustains longer than the top, but at mp it has largely decayed by then. PDF 113 also says the experienced harpist makes the change later than marked, and G♮ is first needed on the fourth quaver of 54. The mark at 53.3 gives the harpist warning; the harpist will move the pedal on 54.1 or later. No change.

3. **Harp part.** I rebuilt the parts in the scratch copy: 0 warnings, harp part 3 pages (cover plus two). The page turn falls in the 147-153 rest. Every étouffez, the 8va at 56-59, and both pedal marks at 161 and 163 print clearly. The part has no pedal diagrams, which follows MOLA PDF 5. Adler PDF 112 calls the opening setting "a help", not a rule. The harp is not one of the five parts tied into movement II.

### Rejected candidates (the master's-level question)

I also asked where, within this lens, the harp could add colour or memorability that a master would have used. I considered three changes and rejected all three.

- **Harp harmonics at 181-182** (Adler PDF 116: bell-like, best C3-A5, need light accompaniment; the ppp coda is that texture). Written C4/G4 would sound C5/G5 over the final chord. Rejected: the last chord at 182 is quoted by movement II (I 182 = II 1) and protected ("182 last chord", "delayed harp ... entries"). The low C2-G2-C3 chord is also the "somber, dark" bottom register PDF 113 describes, which suits a ppp attacca better than a bright overtone. Adding a bell colour at the last moment would also draw attention from Vln I's last motto.
- **Bisbigliando under the flute motto at 73-76** (Adler PDF 118, Ex. IV-16: a "rustling or whispering" tremolo at a very soft level). Rejected: harp tacets 69-155 are protected (c246, line 629 do-not-touch). Holding the harp back makes its return at 156 heard as an event, and 73-76 is movement II's opening colour (mvt1.md seed list), which should stay plain.
- **Moving the 156-165 RH chords down an octave (F5-F6 to F4-F5)** so they sit in the middle register, which carries better (Adler PDF 114: "ff at the top is like mf in the middle"; Rimsky PDF 47: "never a very powerful instrument"). Rejected: the voicings 147-166 are protected (c246, c446). The upper register keeps the harp clear of the violins' tune and the viola fills at E4-C5, so the move would blur the texture more than it would add weight. The weakness the move would address, harp f under the ff tutti at 162, was closed at c197: the later chords of the chain (163-166) come through as the brass fade to mp and drop out, so the harp is heard where its light top register can carry. I have no new reason to reopen it.
