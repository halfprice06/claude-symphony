## Findings

### 1. Bassoon 1 plays 19–26 for about 15 s, mf into a crescendo to ff, with no breath, while the horns beside it breathe (applied)

Bars 19–26 (first theme, Allegro appassionato, crotchet = 126). Bassoon 1 doubles the cellos' bass line: F3 A♭2 | D♭3 | F3 G3 | G3 C3 | C3 (crescendo, accents) B2 | B2 C3 | C3 F3 | F3 ff on the stop chord at 26. It has no rest from 19.1 to 26.3. full.midi gives 14.8 s. The event list has no `\breathe` and no slur in the span. The crescendo starts at 23 and ends on the ff stroke at 26, so the most air is needed at the end of the longest unbroken stretch.

Horns 3 and 4 play the same bars and have breathed at the end of 22 since cycle 356, "at the repeated note". Bassoon 1 has the same repeated note there: C3 on 22.3 and again on 23.1. A breath costs no note. The cellos play the same pitches, so the bassoon's short gap is covered.

Evidence: Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 37: wind players "cannot manage extremely long sustained passages, as they are compelled to take breath; care must be taken therefore to give them a little rest from time to time". PDF 44 says this applies to the brass too, and that is why the horns already breathe here. PDF 41 says brass can swell from pp to ff; the woodwinds, PDF 31–32, "lack the vitality and power" of the other groups, so a bassoon crescendo to ff at the end of 15 s needs a fresh breath more than a horn's does. The same breath sits on Hn 3·4 at 22 (c356), Bsn 1 at 36 and Hn 3·4 at 39, so the first group and the transition now breathe in one place across the section.

Fix (tOne.ily, tOneBsnOne, line 48):
`g2 c2 \breathe | c2.\<-> b,4->~ | b,2 c2->~ | c4 f2.-> |` (was `g2 c2 | c2.\<-> ...`), with a comment line for c496. No pitch, rhythm or dynamic changes. It also prints in the Bassoon 1 part (checked: page 1, end of bar 22).
