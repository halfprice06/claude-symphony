## Findings (ranked)

**1. Part-combine labels missing or misplaced: 56, 69, 78. Applied.**
- **56, Bsn 1·2:** Bsn 2 plays alone (56–68) with no "2.". `tTwoBsnOne` is not defined, so the combiner sees one 22-bar rest and never switches to solo. Fix in tTwo.ily: `aes,1\pp^\markup \bold "2."`.
- **69, Hn 1·2:** the horns are in unison, but the combiner kept them apart for the first beat. Hn 2's hairpin from 68 is still running there. The result was an eighth note with stems both ways, two slurs, accents above and below, an "f" touching the stems, and "a2" printed late on beat 2. Fix in clos.ily: begin `closHnOne` with `\partCombineUnisono r8 es8\f( c'4.->)` and end bar 72 with `\partCombineAutomatic`. The staff now shows one voice, with "a2" on the downbeat.
- **78, Hn 1·2:** the horns are in unison after rests, but no label prints. The combiner's state is still "a2" from 69–72, so the staff reads as an unlabelled line. Fix in dev.ily, Hn 2 (the markup prints from that voice): `r8 b8\mf(^\markup \bold "a2" g'4->~`.

Gould asks for a player label on a shared staff at every entry where the reader cannot tell who plays. The LilyPond manual names this limitation of `\partCombine`.

**2. Duplicate rests on shared staves where both players rest together (e.g. 32 Fl, Ob, Cl). Applied.**
In `\partCombineApart` passages each voice printed its own half rest, one above the other. Gould: on a shared staff, rests that fall at the same time and have the same value are printed once. Fix in score.ly: add `\consists "Merge_rests_engraver"` to the Staff context. The layout did not change and it is still 17 pages.

**3. "espr." for the bassoon solo printed above the staff, next to the clarinets' ff: 33 and 77. Applied.**
`^\markup \italic "espr."` placed the word between "1. Solo" and the clarinet staff, so at 33 it read as "ff espr." for the clarinets. Gould places expression words with the dynamic. The flute at 72 and the cor anglais at 171 already use "p espr." below the staff. Fix in trans.ily (33) and dev.ily (77): `\mf_\markup \italic "espr."`.

**4. Final bar 182: no fermata over the resting staves. Applied.**
Only the sounding parts carry the fermata. In 13 staves it was missing over the whole-bar rest (Fl, Ob, CA, Cl, B.cl, Bsn, Cbn, Hn 1·2, Tpt, Tbn, B.tbn, Tuba, percussion), and also in the harp RH. Gould: a fermata appears in every part, resting parts included. Dvořák 7/ii (above) does this. Fix in coda.ily: the last `R1*N` of each resting part becomes `R1*(N-1) | R1\fermata`. Add `codaHarpR = { R1*15 | R1\fermata | }`. Shared staves print one fermata, and the MIDI and audio length are unchanged (436.5 s).
