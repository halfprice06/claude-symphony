# Critic 17 (lens 7: texture and counterpoint) on v36

## What I studied

I built a MIDI with one track per instrument and ran `vl2.py` (parallels between all voices) and `clash.py` (sustained seconds and sevenths) on v36 and on a scratch copy with my fixes. From the corpus (music21 dumps of the MusicXML) I used:

- Dvořák 9/i bars 32–47 (Vln II and Vc use two different broken-chord shapes at 36–38) and bars 91–102 (the drone accompaniment changes when the tune repeats at 99).

## Findings

### 1. The recap second theme copies the exposition accompaniment (148–151)
`recTwoSync` (Vla `r4 <e' g'>2 <e' g'>4`) and `recTwoArp` (Vc `c8( g c' e' g'2)`) are the exposition's 57–60 transposed, with the harp LH doubling the cello's arpeggios, as it does in the exposition. The counter is transposed too.

Evidence: Tchaikovsky 6/i. At 90–94 the second theme sits over held horn chords. At its return (306–310), violas and cellos play rising eighth-note chromatic lines (D♯3 E3 E♯3 F♯3 G♯3 A♯3 B3 C♯4 | D4 D♯4 E4 …), and the clarinets and bassoons add a moving counterline. The recapitulation is given a new texture rather than copied.

Fix: in `recTwoSync` bars 1–4 (148–151), the viola fills the beats where the cello holds, with falling or rising eighth notes taken from the theme's falling figure. The figure moves against the counter's eighths, and the bar-150 tail falls in sixths below the counter, so the viola does not move in octaves with the tune.
```
r4 g'4~\mp g'8 f'8( e'8 c'8) | r4 a'4~ a'8 g'8( f'8 c'8) | r4 d'4~ d'8 c'8( b8 a8) | r4 b4~ b8 d'8( f'8 g'8) |
```
Bars 152 onwards are unchanged.

### 2. Fugato, fourth entry: back to two voices plus bass (94–95)
At 94 the countersubject is in Vln II (an octave up), Vla and Cl 1. The Vc, which was the free voice at 91–93, drops to `bes2 bes2 | es2 des2` in unison with Bsn 1. So the entry that should be the climax has subject, countersubject and bass only, which is critic 7's original complaint again.

There is a second problem at 93. The Vla `des''2 c''2` runs in octaves with Cb, Bsn 2 and B.cl `des,2 c,2`. The checker hides this as a doubling run, but the two lines move des–c together in octaves.

Evidence: Tchaikovsky 6/i 171–187, as critic 7 cited it: voices accumulate up to the last entry.

Fix (`devVla`, section bars 17–19 = 93–95): `f'2 e'2 | f'1 | ges'2 bes2 |`
- The viola becomes the third voice: a chromatic e'–f'–g♭' line (the motto's semitone), sustained in the same way as the Vc at 91–92.
- Cl 1 and Vln II keep the countersubject.
- I first tried `ges'2 f'2`. `clash.py` showed that f' against the oboe's g♭' made a sustained minor 2nd, so I changed the last note to bes. In 95 the bes is part of the E♭m7 over F.

### 3. Transition: the imitation stops at 37 (37–39)
At 33–36 the head enters every bar: Vc/Bsn at 33, Vla at 34, Ob/Vln I at 35. From 37 to 40 only Ob/Vln I carry the sequence, over four bars of unchanging tremolo (Vln II `aes'1:32` ×3) and whole notes in Vc and Bsn 1.

Evidence: Tchaikovsky 6/i 171–187 tightens the imitation into stretto as the passage builds. Here it does the opposite and thins out to one voice.

Fix (`transVla`, section bars 5–7): the viola answers each sequence head two beats later, an octave lower.
```
ges'2:32\p\< r8 bes8( ges'4~ | ges'8 f'8 fes'8 es'8) r8 c'8( aes'4~ | aes'8 g'8 ges'8 f'8) f'2:32 | <d' f'>1:32\f |
```
The g♭' on the downbeat of 38 is a ♭2–1 appoggiatura over F minor, the motto's own D♭–C step one level up.

### 4. Three instruments play the same arpeggio for six bars (97–102)
Cl 1, Cl 2 and Vla play the same `des' f' bes' f'` shape for 48 eighths in unison. This finding does not touch the protected Hn/Bsn/Tbn counter.

Evidence: Dvořák 9/i 36–38 gives Vln II and Vc two different broken-chord shapes at the same time.

Fix (`devVla`, section bars 21–26, inside the existing `\transpose c cis`): the viola plays the shape upside down, and drops a register at 98.
```
bes'8\f f' des' f' bes' f' des' f' | f' des' bes des' f' des' bes des' | bes' ges' es' ges' bes' ges' es' ges' |
ges' es' c' es' ges' es' c' es' | a' es' c' es' a' es' c' es' | bes' f' des' f' a' es' c' es'
```
This also removes the existing fifths at 99 (Vla f♯→e against the tune's D♭→B) and the octaves with Hn 3 at 102. I tried `des''` at 98 first; it made octaves with the tune, so it is avoided.

### 5. Repeated double stops at the recap climax (131–134)
- **The viola figure:** the Vla repeats `<c' f'>8` ×8, `<des' f'>8` ×8 and `<d' g'>8` ×8 under a single line doubled in five instruments at the octave. This is the fourth use of the same repeated-chord motor (27–31 Cb; 69–71 Vla; 123–126 Cb/Vla; 163–165 Vc/Cb).
- **Octaves:** the v36 viola's c'→d♭' at 131–132 also runs in octaves with the tune.

Evidence: Tchaikovsky 6/i 29–34 keeps the sixteenth-note motor but builds it from the theme's turn (B B B B B A♯ B C♯ E D C♯ B), and passes it from cellos to violas.

Fix (`recOneVla`, section bars 9–12): keep the eighths and take the theme's turn (`b8( c'8)` from bar 16).
```
c'8\f\< c'8 b8 c'8 f'8 f'8 e'8 f'8 | f'8\ff f'8 es'8 f'8 aes'8 aes'8 g'8 aes'8 | aes'8 aes'8 g'8 aes'8 f'8 f'8 e'8 f'8 | g'8 g'8 fis'8 g'8 d'8 d'8 cis'8 d'8 |
```
- In 134 the order is g' then d' so that the viola does not move f'→d' in unison with Hn 2 and Tpt 2.
- The Cb keeps its pedal eighths.

### 6. Intro: the inner voices hold still under the violin's sixths (10–11)
At 9–11, Vln II, Vla and Vc hold one whole note per bar while Vln I leaps through three rising sixths.

Evidence: Tchaikovsky 6/i 4–5 and 10–11. The violas answer the bassoon's head instead of padding.

Fix (`introVlnTwo` bars 10–11): `f'2. c'4( | aes'2) f'2 |`. The second violins echo the motto's rising sixth (c'–a♭') one bar after Vln I, then move f'→f♯' into the German sixth. The protected E♭/E♮ pivot, the violin leaps and the Ger6→V are untouched.
