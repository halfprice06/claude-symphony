## Findings

**1. No front matter, and no composer on the title (title page; MOLA PDF 6).** MOLA asks for "a page or pages at the beginning of the work" that give the movement titles, "an approximate duration … for each movement", "a list of the full instrumentation", the "keys of all transposing instruments, and a list of all percussion instruments", and "how many percussionists will be required". It also says "the title of the work and the name of the composer should be printed prominently". v174 has none of this. The percussion forces appear only as "(2 players)" in a staff name, and nothing outside the timpani's first-bar text gives the timpani tuning. Fix (applied): a one-page `\bookpart` before the score with the duration (c. 7½ minutes, from the MIDI rendering at 436 s), the instrumentation with keys (clarinets and bass clarinet in B♭ with low C, horns in F, trumpets in C), timpani (four drums: G, A♭, C/B♭/B♮, E♭), percussion (2 players: 1 crash cymbals and tam-tam, 2 suspended cymbal), harp, strings, the sounding-pitch note and "attacca". The global `\paper` now has `first-page-number = 0` and the score's bookpart keeps `page-count = 17`, so the front page is unnumbered and the music still occupies pages 1–17 with the same breaks. The PDF has 18 sheets, and the extra one is the front page. Not applied: add `composer = "…"` to `\header`. The composer must supply the name; I did not guess it.

**2. Transposing instruments named without their key (Horns, Trumpets, Bass clarinet; staff names; MOLA Ex. 1–2 PDF 9, PDF 6).** MOLA's model score names "Horns 1, 2 in F", "Trumpets 1, 2 in C" and "Bass Clarinet in B♭". Our clarinets already say "in B♭". The horns, trumpets and bass clarinet did not, so a reader of this C score could not tell what the parts will be written in. Fix (applied, score.ly): `instrumentName = \markup \center-column { "Horns 1·2" "in F" }` (and for Horns 3·4), `{ "Trumpets 1·2" "in C" }`, `{ "Trumpet 3" "in C" }`, `{ "Bass clarinet" "in B♭" \small "(low C)" }`. "in C" for the trumpets follows MOLA's example. If the composer wants B♭ trumpets, change the word here and on the front page. The page-1 system did not reflow.

**3. Hn 1 half rest floats above the stave at 102 (dev.ily, devHnOne; Gould PDF 51; MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7).** This is the open item from cycle 145. Hn 2's beamed eighths (r8 f8 a8 c'8, transposed) push Hn 1's rest a full space above the top line, and LilyPond drew it without a ledger line, so it floats. Gould: "Minim and semibreve rests are placed on a ledger line. The ledger line moves far enough away from the stave to be outside the other part." Fix (applied): `c'2 a2 | bes2 a''2\rest } |`. The pitched rest sits on the A5 ledger line, and LilyPond now prints the ledgered half-rest glyph. I first tried `\tweak staff-position` and `\override Rest.staff-position`; rest collision overrode both. Bar 43 (also in the cycle-145 item): checked at 300 dpi, and no change is needed there. The rest sits on the top stave line above Hn 2's G4, which is a stave line rather than a ledger line, so Gould's rule is met. I close both parts of the item.

**4. Fl 2's dot reads as Fl 1's at 119 (dev.ily, devFluteTwo; Gould PDF 67).** Fl 2's `f''2.` is in the lower voice, so its dot dropped into the E5 space. That put it right next to Fl 1's D5 eighth on the "and" of beat 1, where it looked like Fl 1's dot. Gould: "When notes are on a line, place the dot in the space above." Fix (applied): `\tweak Dots.direction #UP f''2. r4`. The dot now sits in the G5 space, clear of Fl 1. This closes the cycle-145 item.

**5. Timpani carry the key signature and the key changes (score.ly; MOLA PDF 5, Ex. 6 PDF 11).** MOLA: "Traditionally, horns, tuned percussion, and timpani use any required accidentals rather than a key signature." In Ex. 6 (Beethoven 9) the timpani staff has no key signature while the rest of the score is in D. Ours printed three flats, four flats at 47, naturals at 77, sharps at 97, and so on, for a player who only needs the pitches of four drums. Fix (applied): `\new Staff = "Timpani" \with { \remove "Key_engraver" … }`. The accidentals now appear on the notes (B♭ at 34 and 40, E♭ at 41–43, A♭ and E♭ elsewhere), and the key-change cancellations disappear from that staff. The muta texts and the bar-1 tuning label are unchanged.

**6. For the parts stage (cycle 185), not for now: horns, harp pedalling (MOLA PDF 5).** (a) MOLA's tradition of no key signature also covers horns. In a C score with horns at concert pitch, keeping the key signature is normal modern practice, and Ex. 6 is a transposed score. I recommend no key signature only in the horn parts, written in F. (b) "Harp pedaling should be left to the performer and not printed." The diagrams at 47/155 and the letters at 51–66 are protected (cycles 106, 136) and help anyone checking the score for playability. Cycle 106 already marked them "score-only", and MOLA agrees with that: keep them out of the harp part. No score change.
