## Findings (ranked)

**1. Bars 139–141, Vln I: the 8va starts inside the slurred pickup.**
Problem: `r8 g''8\f\<( \ottava #1 f'''4.->)` prints the pickup G at pitch and the F under the 8va. On the page the slurred minor-seventh leap G5–F6 looks like a falling second, G5–F5. The same gesture at 29 (`r8 g''8( f'''4.->)`) is written at pitch, and so is the flute line at 139–141 on the shared staff. So the exposition and the recapitulation, and the flute and the violins, look different for identical music.
Evidence: Gould ch. 1, "Using octave signs", PDF 47 (p. 33): "A written change of octave tends to obscure the contour of a line ... keep a phrase intact at the chosen octave ... an octave change in the wrong place obscures the relationship between the intervals". On the same page: "Music for woodwind and strings can use up to five ledger lines before moving into an octave transposition." The highest note is a♭''' (four ledger lines). In Dvořák 7/iv, DvorakSYMPH7M4_musique-Violino_I.ly lines 717–719 (`d r \ottava #1`), the 8va starts after a rest, so the whole phrase sits under it.
Fix (recOne.ily, recOneVlnOne line 121): delete both octave commands:
`r8 g''8\f\<( f'''4.->) es'''8( d'''8 c'''8 | des'''8\ff es'''8 f'''8 g'''8) aes'''2->~ | aes'''4. g'''8 f'''4 d'''4 | c'''2-> b''2-> |`
Applied. Page 13 has room for the ledger lines and nothing collides.

**2. Bar 154, Hn 1: entry after a rest bar with no dynamic.**
Problem: `R1 | c''4(\> b'4 d''4 f'4)`. The horn comes in after a full bar's rest with only a diminuendo, so the player has no starting level. The last printed horn dynamic is the mp of the pickup at 146.
Evidence: Adler ch. 18, adler2 PDF 294 (p. 607), says dynamics go below each staff and need separate sets only when two players share a staff. Hn 2 rests at 147–155, so a dynamic here causes no conflict on the shared staff. Every other re-entry in the score has a dynamic (I scanned all variables with a script).
Fix (recTwo.ily, recTwoHnOne line 76): `R1 | c''4(\mp\> b'4 d''4 f'4) |`. Applied.

**3. Pages 15–16: 9 bars, then 4 bars of similar density.**
Problem: page 15 holds 158–166 and page 16 holds only 167–170. The quarter notes in the climax are spaced about 7 mm apart, while the double-bass eighths at 167–169 are about 9 mm apart: the shorter notes get more room than the longer notes on the page before.
Evidence: Gould, "Rhythmic spacing", PDF 54 (p. 40): "For a passage of like rhythms ... aim to place a similar number of bars on adjacent systems". PDF 55 (p. 41): "each system should contain a comparable number of symbols".
Fix (global.ily): move the break from 167 to 163. The K page break stays.
`\mt 120 s1*2 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "largamente" \mt 100 s1*3 \break s1*2 \tempo \markup \medium \italic "rit." \mt 98 s2 \mt 92 s2 \mt 87 s2 \mt 81 s2 \bar "||" \key c \minor \mark \default` (and remove the old `\break` before L).
Applied. The result is 158–162 (5 bars, all staves) and 163–170 (8 bars, with harp, without cymbals). Still 17 pages. Side effect: the protected E♮→E♭ crescendo 166→167 now runs inside one system, so its system-start stub before the ff at 167 is gone. The notes and dynamics are unchanged.

**4. Bar 55, Vln I/II: "molto cantabile" runs past the final barline.**
Problem: the text is left-aligned on the last-beat pickup of the system, so in Vln II it sticks out into the right margin.
Evidence: Gould, "Spacing symbols", PDF 55: "no characters should collide". Gould on octave lines, PDF 44: nothing goes beyond the last barline of a system.
Fix (tTwo.ily lines 75 and 83): `\once \override TextScript.self-alignment-X = #RIGHT es'4(\p^\markup \italic "molto cantabile"`. Applied. The text now ends at the note.

**5. Bar 180, Hn 3·4: the "3." label is in small plain type.**
Problem: `^\markup \small "3."` differs from every other player label (bold "1.", "2.", "3.", "4." from part-combine and the manual `\bold` labels at 47, 64 and 146).
Fix (coda.ily line 29): `c'1\ppp~^\markup \bold "3."`. Applied.

**6. Verified with no change needed.**
(a) Every ottava ends right after its last notehead (31, 40–43, 71–72, Fl 98–100, 121–123), as Gould PDF 44 asks. No ottava sits on a shared staff where the other player sounds.
(b) Rests on two-part staves (43 Hn, 161–164 Bn/Hn) follow Gould PDF 50–51: upper-part rests above, lower below, whole and half rests outside the other part.
(c) Tempo marks are above the top staff and above Vln I on every page, and every bar is numbered below the system, as MOLA PDF 4 requires. Adler PDF 294 would also repeat tempo marks between the choirs, but MOLA, the newer standard, does not ask for that, and the space is not available.
(d) Rehearsal letters are 14–20 bars apart. Dvořák 7/i has letters every 11–26 bars (A 25, B 42 ... N 266, from the comments in DvorakSYMPH7M1_musique-Violino_I.ly), and it also leaves a double bar (bar 149) without a letter. So no letters are needed at the unlettered double bars at 103 and 111.
(e) Bar 40, Vln I: the 8va starts on beat 3 of the run. Gould allows a change "between beats" when that cannot be avoided, and cycle 35 chose one bracket for 40–43. I leave it.
