## Findings

On this third pass the printed score follows every principle in the reading. c228 and c328 already fixed what the chapter found in the print (tam-tam and cymbal fades, the brass swells at 40 and 121, the clarinet and bass-clarinet fades). I found one fault. It is not in the print but in how the print's own dynamics reach the MIDI and score.mp3.

### F1. Four of the score's dynamic marks do not reach the MIDI: Cb fp at 33, "sub. p" at 62 and 84, Vln I "pp espr." at 177 (trans, tTwo, dev, coda). Applied.

**What is wrong.** The score prints these marks with its own dynamic scripts: `\subP` ("sub. p", global.ily) and `\ppEspr` ("pp espr.", coda.ily). Both are markups. The Cb at 33 is marked `\fp`. LilyPond's MIDI volume table knows none of the three: `(default-dynamic-absolute-volume "fp")` returns #f, and a markup is not looked up at all. So each part keeps its previous level in full.midi, score.midi and score.mp3:
- **177–182, Vln I:** the last motto statement over the chorale, printed "pp espr." and closing ppp into movement II, plays at velocity 90 (between mf and f; the violas under it are at 62, pp). It stays there until the diminuendo of 181. This is the movement's quietest page, and the rendering makes its top line the loudest.
- **62–63, eleven parts** (Cl 1, Hn 2, Bsn 2, Hn 3·4, harp, Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc, Cb): the printed subito piano under "poco animando" plays at velocity 90, the level the crescendo of 60–61 reached.
- **84, Vc and Cb:** the held G after the ff strokes plays at velocity 90 instead of p.
- **33–36, Cb:** the fp plays at the earlier ff level for four bars under the bassoon solo and the cellos' p entry at 35.

**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 129: sf-p and p-sf effects come by "the natural dynamic process ... which depends upon the player" or by orchestration. Here the orchestration is right: the fp at 33 is his method (a), a string attack against a soft line (Ex. 253, PDF 418, where the Vc/Cb sf drops under the soft wind entry), and the sub. p at 62 and 84 are the "natural" kind. But the only player the composer can check is the MIDI, and it plays none of them. Velocities from full.midi (v428 → scratch): Vln I 177 and 179 90 → 62; Cl 1, Vln I, Bsn 2 and Cb at 62 90 → 69; Vc 84 90 → 69; Cb 34 90 → 69. The rest of the file is unchanged. The doubling scan no longer flags the Cb against the cellos at 35–36 or Vln I against the violas at 177.

**Fix** (global.ily only; no printed mark, note or layout changes). After the `mt` definition:
```
#(define (expr-volume d)
  (let* ((s (if (string? d) d (markup->string d)))
         (ws (reverse (string-split s #\space)))
         (known '("ppp" "pp" "p" "mp" "mf" "f" "ff" "fff" "sf"))
         (hit (find (lambda (w) (member w known)) ws)))
    (cond ((string=? s "fp") (default-dynamic-absolute-volume "p"))
          (hit (default-dynamic-absolute-volume hit))
          (else (default-dynamic-absolute-volume s)))))
```
and as the first line of `global = {`:
```
  \set Score.dynamicAbsoluteVolumeFunction = #expr-volume
```
Standard dynamics pass through unchanged, because `default-dynamic-absolute-volume` is called on the same string. A markup is read for its dynamic word, so "sub. p" gives p and "pp espr." gives pp. fp gives its sustained level, p: a MIDI note has one velocity, and the four bars after the attack matter more than an attack already covered by the tutti stroke. Any later `make-dynamic-script` markup the composer adds is handled the same way.

### Checked and kept

- **Unterminated swell hairpins in the MIDI.** A `\<` that ends at `\!` with no following dynamic makes LilyPond's MIDI rise far beyond a swell. Examples: the B.cl's pp motto swell at 3 reaches velocity 101, and the Hn 1/Vc mf swells at 103 reach 114. The print is correct. Fixing this would need hidden MIDI-only dynamics at dozens of swells, and it would change nothing a player reads. Leave it.
- **Slurs of the runs into 33, 41 and 69 end on the last 16th, not on the arrival note** (R-K PDF 130 says a glide is "as a rule" slurred to its main note). R-K means small unmeasured grace notes. These runs are measured, and each arrival is accented and needs a new bow. Protected by c108. Keep.
- **Brass entries at f or mf after rests** (row 25): each keeps its stated balance reason. PDF 133 asks for the basis to be quieter than the melody, so a level below the tune does not conflict with PDF 136.
- **Tempo words.** Every printed word (poco rit. 44 and 145, poco animando 62 and 156, rit. 66, 165 and 177, allargando 121, sostenuto 123, a tempo 127, largamente 160, Più lento 171, Tempo I 179) has its `\mt` steps in global.ily. The fermata at 170 is MIDI crotchet = 40. No change.
