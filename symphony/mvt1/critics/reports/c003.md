# Critic 3 (lens 3: form, pacing and proportion), version v22

## What I studied
I timed every v22 section from `global.ily` (436 s total, matching `analysis.txt`). I built a tension curve from `score.midi` (parts sounding × velocity) and read the score for bars 105–135 and 152–180 (PDF pp. 10, 11, 14, 15).

For the corpus I wrote a MusicXML profiler (scripts in /tmp/critic-003/scripts) and ran it on Tchaikovsky 6/i, Dvořák 9/i, Beethoven 9/i and Mozart 41/i. It gives per-bar time from the MuseScore tempo marks, the number of parts sounding and the current dynamic, plus every ff/fff bar and the registral peaks. I placed section boundaries from the key changes and rehearsal marks in `Dvorak_Sym9_mvt1.musicxml` and from the tempo words in `Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.musicxml`, and checked them against the MCTL form CSV (`annotations/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1/form/`).

For Brahms 1/i I used P. Saelzer's bar-number outline (https://fmmc.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Brahms-1.1-Pablos-Sketch.pdf). Web searches did not turn up a bar-numbered analysis of Dvořák 7/i (e.g. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphony_No._7_(Dvo%C5%99%C3%A1k)), so I left it out.

## Findings

### 1. The planned main climax (bar 156) is the weakest peak of the second half (bars 114–166)
**What is wrong.** The plan makes the C-major second theme "the emotional high point". By three measures it ranks below the earlier peaks.
- **Orchestral mass** (average voices, `analysis.txt`): 105–108 is 27.2, 129–132 is 25.8, 113–116 is 25.0 and 69–72 is 25.0, but 153–156 is only 23.8.
- **Length of the loud zone:** the dev climax is ff/fff for 7 bars (109–115, 13 s) and the recap first theme for 12 bars (123–134, 23 s). The ♭VI climax gets 4 bars (154–157, 9.6 s), and the brass are already at mf/mp by bar 157.
- **Repeated marking:** fff appears at 114, 132, 156 and 166, four times in 111 s.

**Evidence.**
- Dvořák 9/i marks fff only twice (bars 59 and 400).
- Tchaikovsky 6/i places its last fff at the recap second-theme climax (313). Bars 313–316 stay ff/fff and the music holds f or louder through 324, about 45 s, before the coda.
- Brahms 1/i holds its development climax for 22 bars (321–342).

**Fix.** This keeps the bar count.
- Hold the brass through the deceptive cadence at 158 (A minor, bass `a,,1`) and move the strings' diminuendo to 159:
  - `recTwoTptOne = { R1*15 | g'4\ff e''2.~ | e''4 f''4 g''2 | c''4\fff aes''2~ aes''8 g''8 | f''2 d''4 b'8 c''8 | c''1\ff\> | R1*4\! | }`
  - `recTwoTbnOne`: bar 157 `b1\ff`, bar 158 `c'1\>`.
  - `recTwoBassTbn`: 157 `d2 g,2`, 158 `a,1\>`.
  - `recTwoMelodyB`: `a''2.~ a''8 gis''8 | g''2\> f''4 e''8 f''8 |` (take the `\>` off bar 158).
- Downgrade bar 132 (recOne bar 10) from `\fff` to `\ff` in every part, and take the flute's b''' at 130 down to b'', so that only 156 carries fff and a♭''' together after the development.
- This tempers cycle 1's recap peak but does not undo it: the a♭''' at 156 stays.

### 2. The retransition goes from p to ff in 2 bars (121–123)
**What is wrong.** After the collapse (116–118) the music sits pp in 119–120 (clarinet T1 head). The crescendo starts only at 121 (mf), and the full tutti arrives at 123. The development's climax and the recapitulation therefore read as two separate blows rather than one arc.

**Evidence.**
- Dvořák 9/i, after its ff at 251–261, holds a pp chord (261–269), then lets the dominant arpeggio wander pp (270–276), and recapitulates quietly at 277: 16 bars from climax to return.
- Brahms 1/i builds from bar 293 to 321 (28 bars) and keeps the tension into the recapitulation (343).

v22 does neither.

**Fix.** Take the Brahms option with 4 bars, keeping the bar count:
- start the crescendo at 119: `devClOne` 119 `r8 g8\pp\< es'4.-> …`;
- Hn 3·4 `g1\pp\<` at 119;
- timpani `g,1:32\pp\<` at 119, not at 122;
- Vc/Cb `\<` at 119;
- marked mf at 121, f at 122.

Alternatively, take the Dvořák option: start the recap at f (bar 123 `\f`) and reach ff only at 131. This also serves finding 1.

### 3. The coda's slow tail is static and states the augmented motto twice in a row at the same pitch
**What is wrong.** From 171 to 180 no part moves faster than half notes apart from timpani rolls. That is 42 s (13.3 s Più lento plus 28.8 s Lento). In that span:
- bass clarinet, Vc and Cb play `g,1 es1 d1 c2 des2 c1` (171–175);
- the clarinet plays a variant (172–176);
- VlnI play the same contour in near-augmentation two octaves higher (175–180).

The violin entry is heard as a repeat, not an answer. The coda as a whole is 63 s, 14% of the movement.

**Evidence.** Tchaikovsky 6/i coda (335–354): the brass chorale is repeated, but over a quarter-note pizzicato B-major scale that runs in VlnI/VlnII/basses through bar 350. Only the last 3–4 bars are static. Coda shares in the corpus are 9–10% (Tchaikovsky, Dvořák, Brahms).

**Fix.** Overlap the violins with the bass statement (stretto at 2 bars) and cut 2 bars, giving a 16-bar coda of about 53 s:
- VlnI 171–178: `R1*2 | r2. g'4(\pp | es''1~ | es''2. d''4 | c''2 des''2 | c''1~ | c''1\fermata) |`
- Cb 171–178: `g,,1\pp | es,1 | d,1 | c,2 des,2 | c,1~ | c,1~ | c,1~ | c,1\fermata |`
- Move the Lento to the new bar 175.
- Give the C pedal a pulse: Cb pizz. `c,4 r4 c,4 r4` in 175–176, or timpani `c4\pp r4 c4 r4`.

Inner voices at 173–174 need re-voicing so the violins' e♭'' sits over d and d♭. The augmentation from v11 is kept, so this does not undo it.

### 4. Severe, outside my lens: violin II has an unplayable F♯3 at bar 97
**What is wrong.** `analysis.txt` flags it ("Violins II … F#3, bar 97"). It is a side effect of transposing D3 up a semitone in v22 (`devVlnTwo` inside `\transpose c cis`: `r2. f4(`), and the composer's check did not mention it.

**Fix.** Drop the upbeat in VlnII: `r1 | des'2.~\ff des'8 c'8 | …`. VlnI and oboe 1 already play the same upbeat an octave higher (`r2. f'4(`). Alternatively, raise that one note an octave (`f'4`).

### 5. The first two fugato entries are in the same instrument (85, 88)
**What is wrong.** The cellos state the subject at 85 and again at 88, with only the basses added (1.6 voices on average). Entries then come every 3 bars, rising by fourths (G, C, F, B♭). The development's main crescendo gains no new colour for 6 bars (11 s).

**Fix.** Give the second entry (88) to violas plus bassoon 1 at the same pitch. The cellos then take the countersubject the basses now play (`c4. d8 es8( f8) g4 | es4 d8( c8) f4. es8 |`, an octave up).

### 6. Stale bar numbers in section headers (minor)
The header comments in `recOne.ily`, `recTwo.ily` and `coda.ily` give 123–134, 135–158 and 159–172. The real ranges are 123–138, 139–162 and 163–180. Correct them.

## Do not touch
These are at a master's level and should stay:
- the development's single accumulation from 85 to 108;
- the G pedal with the augmented trombone motto (109–113);
- the overall section proportions, which sit inside the corpus range on every axis measured.
