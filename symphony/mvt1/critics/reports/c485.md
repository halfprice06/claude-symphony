## Findings

### 1. Violas 111–116: the last quaver of every bar sits 0.4–0.6 space from the barline (applied, partly)

**What is wrong.** In the wedge 111–116 the violas play `c'8 c'8 b8( c'8)` patterns in quavers, beamed in fours with up-stems (correct for C4/B3 in the alto clef, PDF 38). The system 111–120 is dense, because 117–120 hold triplet semiquavers in the clarinets and violas, so the quaver width in 111–116 is small and the last quaver's up-stem (on the right of its notehead) ends up beside the barline. The probe measured the stem centre at 0.38 (111, 112), 0.45 (113, 114), 0.61 (115) and 0.54 (116) stave-spaces from the following barline. At 300 dpi the stem and barline read as a pair of parallel lines in every bar of the passage. No other stem in the score is nearer than 0.98 space.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 57 (Spacing symbols, mid-system): "Stems must never come closer to a barline than one space, because the close parallel lines are difficult to read." PDF 56: "Allow a stave-space ... on either side of a barline before a notational symbol." PDF 55: "The barline may go where the position of the next beat would be. It usually goes slightly earlier" – here it comes at about a third of a quaver's width. The intro (PDF 9) gives the reason: the violists are reading a fast repeated-note figure under a crescendo; a stem that merges with the barline costs a glance per bar. c345 fixed the same fault at 68.4 (sixteenth run into 69) with the same mechanism, so this follows the score's existing practice.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-485/work/global.ily).** Before each of the barlines 112–117:

```
s1 \tag #'score { \once \override Score.BarLine.extra-spacing-width = #'(-0.4 . 0) } ... (six times)
```

This moves the six stems to 0.68 space. I tested -0.5 through -1.0 as well: from -0.5 on (which would give the full one space at -0.8), LilyPond prints "too few pages: 17 (should have at least 18)" twice under `page-count = 17`, although the printed layout is unchanged (I compared every text item's y-position on page 11 between -0.4 and -0.8: identical within 0.01 pt; with `page-count` removed, -0.8 gives the same 17 music pages and no warning). So the full fix is blocked only by LilyPond's page-count estimate, not by space on the page. I kept -0.4 to keep the build at 0 warnings, with a margin. Tagged score-only, so the parts are untouched (the viola part has its own spacing; I did not find the fault there).

### 2. Clarinet 2 at 118.1: triplet group starts 0.7 space after the barline (applied)

**What is wrong.** Cl 2's first triplet semiquaver at 118 (down-stemmed lower voice on the Cl 1·2 stave) has its stem 0.93 space from the left edge of the barline, about 0.74 from its right edge, with the notehead nearly touching it. Gould PDF 56 asks for a stave-space after a barline before a symbol, and PDF 57 for one space between any stem and a barline.

**Fix (applied).** `s1 \tag #'score { \once \override Score.BarLine.extra-spacing-width = #'(0 . 0.4) } s1 s1*2` at 117–120 in global.ily. The stem is now 1.17 from the barline's left edge (about one space clear). This change alone builds clean.

### 3. The 17-page layout has almost no vertical margin under `page-count` (observation, no change)

The page-count warning in Finding 1 is triggered by a change of 0.0001 staff-space in the computed height of the 111–120 system. Any later change that adds a little height or tightens that system can produce the same two warnings even when the printed pages do not change. If it appears, the test is to build once with `page-count = ##f`: if that gives the same 17 music pages (break bars 1, 23, 31, 39, 56, 64, 73, 94, 103, 111, 121, 131, 139, 147, 158, 163, 171), the warning is LilyPond's estimate and not a real overflow. I recommend no change to the paper settings now; this is a note for the composer and later critics.
