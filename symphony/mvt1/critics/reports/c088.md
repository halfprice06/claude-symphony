# Critic 088 — lens 8 (expression): articulation and phrasing across recurrences (v107)

## What I studied
I tabulated every statement of the motto, the first-theme head (15, 27, 33–36, 79–82, 85–96 fugato, 103–110, 117–119, 131–134, 139, 143, 167–169), the transition answers (35–40), the second-theme head (46–58, 69–71, 73–76, 97–102, 103–110, 146–155, 158–161, 175–178) and the codetta (69–72). For each I listed slurs, accents, tenutos, staccatos, words and hairpins in every doubling part. Corpus: Tchaikovsky 6/i, second theme at bars 89–93, 130–134 and 305–309 (`musicxml-converted/xml/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.musicxml`, read with music21). Dvořák 9/i: horn theme at bars 24–25, 59–60 and 277–278 (`mutopia/DvorakA_O95/Sym9/Sym9-lys/brass.notesA.ily`, `hornIIImA`), and the motif macros `motifWninetyone` and `motifWthreesixteen` (`woods.notesA.ily` lines 15–27, bars 91 and 316).

Findings from the corpus:
- In Tchaikovsky every statement keeps the same slurs, the same <> pair in each bar and the tenuto on the peak note (A5 at 93, A6 at 134, F♯6 at 309). This holds in Vln I, Vln II, Va, Vc and both flutes. Only the word changes ("teneramente, molto cantabile" → "senza sordini" → "con dolcezza") and the dynamic level.
- Dvořák varies articulation between statements on purpose. His tutti at 59 adds staccato; his recapitulation at 277 drops the accent; at 316 the recap macro uses staccato where bar 91 has slurs. Within one statement, though, every doubling part shares the same macro, so the doublings are identical.

Web sources:
- Rimsky-Korsakov, *Principles of Orchestration* (https://www.gutenberg.org/files/33900/33900-h/33900-h.htm). He covers legato and staccato capability in winds and graded dynamics between doubling groups. He says nothing on slurs in doublings.
- Gould's rule that string slurs mean bowings and wind slurs mean legato. I found it only quoted second-hand in a search result. The source thread (https://www.notat.io/viewtopic.php?p=12173) returned 403.
- Adler-based course notes (https://personal.stevens.edu/~abrick/orchestration1/files/HMU397_appendix1.html).

## Findings (ranked)

**1. The second-theme head loses its tenuto in the development (99, 102, 104, 108).** Every other statement marks the leap note with a tenuto: 48, 51, 57, 70, 148, 151 and 176 (cycle 28 policy: "tenuto on leap note"). The development statements do not:
- B-minor statement: 99 `bes'4( ges''2 f''4)` and 102 `f''4( des'''2 …`, in Vln I, Vln II, Fl 1, Ob 1 and CA;
- 104: Hn 1 and Bsn 1 `d'4 bes'2 a'4`;
- 108: Fl 1 and Va `e'4 c''2 b'4`.

Tchaikovsky 6/i keeps the peak tenuto in all three statements, at p, mf/ff and p. Fix (dev.ily): add `--` to the leap note in each of those parts. For example `bes'4( ges''2-- f''4)` and `f''4( des'''2-- c'''8 g''8)` inside the `\transpose c cis` block, `d'4 bes'2-- a'4` and `e'4 c''2-- b'4`. No notes change (protected 97–110 notes untouched). **Applied.**

**2. The codetta's first-theme head (69–71) is unmarked in Vln II, Cl 1 and Hn 1·2.** `r8 es'8 c''4.-> bes'8 aes'8 g'8 | aes'8 bes'8 c''8 des''8 f''4.->` has no slurs. Under the cycle-28 rule the head is slurred everywhere except the listed marcato places (82, 85–96, 117–120), and 69 is not on that list. Bars 69–70 have the same shape as 19–20, which is slurred `aes''4.->) g''8( f''8 es''8 | des''8 es''8 f''8 aes''8)`. Fix (clos.ily, all four parts): `r8 es'8\f( c''4.->) bes'8( aes'8 g'8 | aes'8 bes'8 c''8 des''8) f''4.-> es''8 | des''8( c''8 bes'8 aes'8) g'4.-> bes'8`. Only articulation changes; the codetta notes are protected. **Applied.**

**3. In the recap the rising eighths into the A♭6 peak (140) carry no articulation.** At 139 the slur closes at the end of the bar. That leaves `des'''8 es'''8 f'''8 g'''8` in 140 bare in Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1, Vln I and Vln II. Bar 140 does what bar 20 does (four rising eighths into a held peak), and bar 20 is slurred. Fix (recOne.ily, five parts): `es'''8( d'''8 c'''8 | des'''8\ff es'''8 f'''8 g'''8) aes'''2->~`. **Applied.**

**4. In the recap pickup (146) the doubling parts carry different words.** Hn 1 has "dolce" but Va and Vc have "espr.". The exposition marks Hn 1 and Vc "dolce" at 47. Tchaikovsky changes the word between statements, but never between the doubling parts of one statement. Fix (recOne.ily): `r4 g4(\mf^\markup \italic "dolce"` (Va) and `r2. g,4(\mf^\markup \italic "dolce"` (Vc). The mp/mf difference stays (horn balance, rejected in v18 and cycle 8). **Applied.**

**5. In the transition (37–38) Ob 1 is slurred differently from the viola it doubles.** The viola, and Cl 1 in the next answer (38–39), break the slur after the tie: `ges'8) f'8( fes'8 es'8)`. That is the head's own articulation (`es''4.->) d''8( des''8 c''8`). Ob 1 slurs straight through. Fix (trans.ily): `r8 bes'8\mf( ges''4->~ | ges''8) f''8( fes''8 es''8) r2`. **Applied.**

**6. In the coda (169–170) the violas slur into the accented fermata chord while the brass they double re-attack it.** Hn 1·2 and Tpt 1·2 have `es''8( d''8 c''8) | b'2.->`, but Va has `es'8( d'8 c'8 | <d' f'>2.->)`. This also differs from the protected cycle-33 tutti chord, which has a separate attack in every part. Fix (coda.ily): `r8 aes8( f'4.->) es'8( d'8 c'8) | <d' f'>2.-> r4\fermata`. **Applied.**

**7. In the transition (36) the head's second bar has no accent in Vln I.** `bes'4) a'8( bes'8) g'4. f'8` lacks the accent that bar 2 of the head carries everywhere else: 16, 28, 92 (fugato), 104, 132 and 168, as `aes'4.-> g'8`. The only other exception, 144, is under a diminuendo and stays. Fix (trans.ily): `g'4.-> f'8`. **Applied.**

**8. Deliberate variations to record (no change).** These should be noted in critique.md so later critics do not "fix" them:
- Strings use bowing slurs where the winds slur the phrase: violins 70 `aes''4) f'''2--( es'''4)` against the wind slur over 69–70; Vln I 62–63 against Fl 1; 97–102 one-bar string bows against two-bar wind slurs (cycles 16 and 28).
- Trombones are unslurred on the recap head 131–134 (cycle 8).
- Brass and strings use separate attacks under slurred winds at 158–161 (protected).
- Fl 1 is marked mp against Va p at 107–110 (low flute, balance).
- Fl 1 has the head with no tenuto at 73 (the motto variant has no leap).
- The fugato is marcato with staccato tails (cycle 49).
- Hairpin swells appear at 47–50 but there is one long crescendo at 55–61 (protected).
- Cb has no slurs at 175–179 while Vc, B.cl and trombones slur in pairs. These are separate bass bows on long pp notes. The composer may add `g,,1\pp( | es,1) | d,1( | c,2 des,2 | c,1)~` if he wants the parts to match visibly. Not applied.
