## Findings

Ranked by how much each stands between this score and a master's work. All three are applied in the scratch copy /tmp/critic-308/work.

### 1. Bass trombone and tuba ff under the low P theme at 131 (recOne)

**What is wrong.** At the recapitulation the P theme is in the tenor: Bsn 1·2 and cellos ff, Tbn 1·2 f (graded down by c144 so the trombones do not dominate the unison), Cl a2 ff. Under it the bass trombone holds C3 and the tuba C2, both **ff**, for whole bars (131–134). These are the only held brass at ff in the passage. Hn 3·4 and the trumpets were brought to f by c264 and c174 for the same reason. At the two parallel tuttis, 27 and 167, the bass trombone and tuba are f. At 131 the theme is low, so the bass needs more care than at 27, not less. As written, the bass trombone, in the same family and timbre as the trombones carrying the theme, is one level louder than them and a fifth below their first note.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 329, Ex. X-69: the trombone's middle register is "very strong". The f trombones already carry, so a held bass trombone at ff in the same colour outweighs the line. PDF 316: brass "tremendous carrying power tends to unbalance the normal brass section". PDF 317 and 325 (Bruckner, Mahler, Wagner examples): the strong mark goes to the line that leads, not to held notes. The score's own grading at 27 and 167 (protected "graded brass 27–32, 131, 167") puts B.tbn and tuba f, and c144's note at recOne.ily line 104 says the theme should be "a blend, not a trombone solo".

**Fix (applied).** recOne.ily:
```
recOneBassTbn = {  c1\f | ...     % was c1\ff
recOneTuba    = {  c,1\f | ...    % was c,1\ff
```
The arrival keeps its weight from the timpani ff pulse, the contrabassoon and the double basses' ff quavers, which are unchanged. No notes change. This follows the protected grading at 131; it does not undo c144, c174 or c264, it finishes them.

### 2. Trumpet 2's held A♭4 ff at 82 (dev)

**What is wrong.** c304 set all held brass at 82 to f under the ff head (Tpt 1, Fl, Ob, Cl, violins). It left Tpt 2's held A♭4 at **ff**, and its note says why: Tpt 2 "shares Tpt 1's staff". So the dynamic comes from the engraving, not from the music. The result is that the loudest held tone in the chord is a trumpet in its clear, carrying middle register. It also sits a semitone above the G4 on which Tpt 1 starts the head, in the same colour and at the same level. Hn 1 already holds the A♭4 at f.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 316: the trumpet's carrying power unbalances the brass. PDF 318, Ex. X-46: the middle register is "clear, bright and most articulate", the register that carries most. PDF 313: in tuttis the trumpet's held notes are a secondary, supporting role, which means they are marked under the line, as c304 did for every other held brass part. The brief's rule that shared staves need equal simultaneous dynamics rules out printing f for Tpt 2 under Tpt 1's ff.

**Fix (applied).** dev.ily, `devTptTwo`: Tpt 2 rests at 82 and comes in on the ff strokes at 83. The ff is part-only, because the score already shows Tpt 1's ff on the shared staff:
```
devTptTwo = {
  R1*6 | aes'4->-\tag #'part \ff r4 aes'4-> r4 | R1*27 |   % was R1*5 | aes'1\ff | aes'4-> r4 aes'4-> r4 |
```
score.ly: `<< \global \pc \tptOne \removeWithTag #'part \tptTwo >>`, and Tpt 2 is added to the list of tagged variables in the comment. The A♭4 stays in Hn 1 (f), and A♭ is also in Tbn 2, B.tbn, tuba, Cb and the woodwinds. Tpt 2 had rested since 72, so it now rests 73–82. This answers c304's do-not-touch line ("brass layout at 82"): its stated aim was held brass f under the ff head, and this was the one exception. The climax order 82 < 122 < 160 is kept, and 82 becomes slightly lighter. No new parallels: the vl.txt diff is empty.

### 3. The horn call at 13–14 has no expressive word and no "Solo" (intro)

**What is wrong.** Hn 1's call E♭3–C4–B3 is the movement's only undoubled horn melody (mvt1.md), and the S theme grows from it. Every other solo entry in the movement has a word: C.a. "espr." at 5, Bsn 1 "Solo espr." at 33, B.cl "Solo espr." at 77, Hn 1 "dolce" at 47 and 146, Hn 2 "espr." at 56, solo cello "Solo espr." at 171, Cl 1 "espr." at 175. The call has only "mp", and the shared staff prints "1.", which reads like a section entry.

**Evidence.** Every solo-horn example in the reading carries a character word with its dynamic: Brahms 1/iv "f sempre e passionato" (PDF 304, X-8), Beethoven 6 "p dolce" (PDF 305, X-9), Ravel "expressif" (X-11), Strauss "ff molto espr." (X-12), Tchaikovsky 5/ii "Solo … dolce con molto espr." (PDF 309, X-24). Adler labels these "Solo" (X-19, X-24).

**Fix (applied).** intro.ily, the same mechanism c138 used for Bsn 1 at 33:
```
introHnOne = { R1*10 | f'1\p\< | es'2.\f es'4 | \set Staff.soloText = "1. Solo" r4 es4(\mp-\tag #'part ^\markup \bold "Solo"_\markup \italic "espr." c'2~ | c'4\> b2)\p r4 \set Staff.soloText = "1." | }
```
The score shows "1. Solo" above and "mp espr." below. The Horn 1 part shows "Solo" and "espr." (checked in partsout/parts-12-horn-1.pdf). Notes, slur and dynamics are unchanged, so c221's do-not-touch line on the call (its notes) is kept.

## Considered and kept (no change)

- **No muted or stopped brass** (PDF 307–308). Adler's uses are single stopped notes, soft passages and sf>p. The places where they might go are the collapse at 124–126 (protected, c239/c289) and Hn 1's unresolved D♯5 at 106 (a scherzo seed). Neither needs a new colour. Brahms, Dvořák and Elgar do not use them in comparable first movements, and c234/c269 kept them out. Keep.
- **Motto 117–121 in horns and trombones, ff, unslurred** (PDF 309, 321: separate notes are tongued). A tongued, tied brass motto at ff is the brass idiom for a theme of this kind; slurs or tenutos add nothing the ties do not already give. Keep (protected c289).
- **Tpt 1·2 ff on the strokes at 32 while Tpt 3 and the trombones stay f.** This is inside the protected dynamics of 27–32, which the finale quotes at pitch. The strokes go straight to tutti ff at 33. Keep.
- **Trombones mf under horns f at 12** (PDF 329: "very strong" middle register). Correct grading. Keep (protected).
- **Trumpet pp cresc. at 113** (PDF 319: middle register takes every dynamic) and **Tpt 3 B3 only at fff at 122** (low notes safer loud). Keep.
- **Hn 1 diminuendo into its rest at 162** (open since c865, protected exit order): Hn 2 on the same staff shows p at 164. Keep.
