This is the fourth pass; the earlier ones were c255, c345 and c455. I read every text page (PDF 53-57, 60-68; 58-59 are the chapter title and contents) in full and rendered every page of the range at 80 dpi as images. I read all 17 music pages of score.pdf at 90 dpi and zoomed to 200-250 dpi on pages 10, 11, 12, 13 and 15.

This time I measured instead of only looking. I wrote a LilyPond probe (/tmp/critic-545/probe/probe.ily). It logs the horizontal extent of every printed stem, notehead, accidental and barline, in staff spaces, by system, staff and bar. A script (/tmp/critic-545/scripts/gaps.py) then lists every stem nearer than one space to a barline, every notehead nearer than one space after a barline, and every accidental nearer than half a space. It also gives the distance between consecutive noteheads. The probe covers 14,557 items in v545.

## Findings

Both findings are layout only. No pitch, rhythm, dynamic or articulation changes, and full.midi and vl.txt are identical to v545's.

### 1. Bars 111-120: the system is packed below Gould's minimum. Move the system break from 111 to 113 (applied)

**What is wrong.** The system 111-120 holds ten bars: six bars of straight quavers in the wedge (111-116) and four bars of triplets against straight quavers (117-120). Columns at 0, 1/3, 1/2 and 2/3 of a beat need room, so the four triplet bars take 26-28 spaces each and the six quaver bars get about 17.5. The probe shows the result in v545:

- Viola quavers in 111-116 stand 1.6 spaces apart. That leaves 0.3 space between noteheads that are 1.3 spaces wide. The triplet quavers of 117-120 on the same system stand 2.2-2.5 spaces apart. At 90 dpi page 11 shows 111-116 squeezed and 117-120 open.
- The last up-stem in each bar sits 0.60 space from the barline: violas 111-116 and Tpt 1·2 113-114. Even c485's six `extra-spacing-width = #'(-0.4 . 0)` overrides leave the stems there. c485 reported the violas; the trumpets were not reported.
- In 117-120 the noteheads are also packed, 0.3-0.45 space apart (for example, 118 Cl 1·2 and violas).

The system is at LilyPond's rod minimum from end to end. That is why c485 found that any further barline padding gives the "too few pages" warning. No more space can be found inside this system.

**Evidence from the reading.** Gould PDF 54: "Each longer value ... should have more space than its shorter neighbour (a), or at least as much (b)". The "not acceptable" example on that page shows exactly this pattern: shorter values with more space than longer ones. Also PDF 54: "Notes of equal duration require equal spacing for an entire system". PDF 55: "Where space is limited, the distance between characters should not be less than 1/2 stave-space and no characters should collide". PDF 57: "Stems must never come closer to a barline than one space". PDF 54 also gives the remedy: "aim to place a similar number of bars on adjacent systems ... Where the density of the notation increases, the uniform space for a note-value may need to be noticeably different from that of an adjacent system". The page before has room to spare. The 103-110 system (page 10) holds eight bars of mostly long notes and rests on eleven staves.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-545/work/global.ily).** Take the `\break` away after 110 and put one after 112. Remove c485's six -0.4 barline overrides, which are no longer needed:

```
  \bar "||" \key a \minor
  s1*8
  \bar "||" \key c \minor
  s1 s1 \break s1 s1 s1 s1
```

**Result (probe on the rebuilt score).**
- Viola quavers 2.6 spaces apart in 111-112 (page 10) and 2.3 in 113-116. Triplet quavers in 117-120 are 2.2-2.5. The quaver now gets at least as much space as the triplet quaver on the same system.
- Noteheads at least 1 space apart.
- No stem in 103-120 is nearer than 0.96 space to a barline, without any override. Cl 2's 118 group (c485) is 0.96 from the barline.
- Systems 103-112 / 113-120 / 121-130 hold 10/8/10 bars.
- The double barline and C-minor key signature at 111 now fall mid-system with the space PDF 56 asks for (checked at 200 dpi). The viola alto clef sits before the double bar at the end of 110, as before.
- Page 10 now also shows the Contrabassoon, Tuba and Violins I staves, which rest in 103-110. That costs nothing in reading.

**Earlier decisions.** c325 and c335 protect "page breaks (same last bar on every page)". c485 kept 111-120 to keep 0 warnings, and c099 set the break before 121. I keep 121: 122 is not touched. The page-break protection was a stability check, not a musical reason. Here the reading requires a change, because the 111-120 system cannot meet PDF 54, 55 and 57 at any spacing setting. The change still gives 18 PDF pages, 0 warnings and 17 music pages. Fifteen page ends are unchanged; page 10 now ends at 112 (was 110) and page 11 still ends at 120. New page ends: 14, 30, 38, 46, 63, 72, 84, 102, 112, 120, 130, 138, 146, 157, 162, 170, 182. The parts do not use the score's breaks (`\partClean`), so they are unchanged.

### 2. Bar 123, Trombones 1·2: the semibreve second sits 0.58 space after the barline (applied)

**What is wrong.** At 123 Tbn 1 holds G3 and Tbn 2 F3 as semibreves on one stave. A second takes the lower head left of the line (Gould PDF 61, correct here), so that F3 head pushes left to 0.58 space from the barline. This is the only notehead in the score nearer than 0.86 space to a preceding barline.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 56 (mid-system): "Allow a stave-space ... on either side of a barline before a notational symbol". PDF 57 allows less only for an accidental or grace note in cramped conditions, not a notehead.

**Fix (applied).** global.ily, before the "sostenuto" mark at 123: `\tag #'score { \once \override Score.BarLine.extra-spacing-width = #'(0 . 0.5) }`. The head is now 0.98 space from the barline. The build is clean, and the 121-130 system and its page are unchanged. Score only.

### No further change

I also looked within this lens for places where the page holds the music back. The 111-120 system was the clearest case. In v545 the wedge, the crescendo that leads into the dominant pedal at G, was printed in a cramped block next to wide triplet bars, and a conductor reading it saw the passage get smaller just as it builds. Now the wedge reads at the same pace as the bars it leads to. Other items I checked and kept:
- 147-157 against 158-162: protected, and 158-162 carry the most text in the score.
- Fl 1·2 117-120: protected since c345.
- Stems at 0.90-0.95 space at Vln II 32, the 82 strokes and Vc 130: within 0.1 space of the rule, which is LilyPond's default note-to-barline spacing; I did not change them.
