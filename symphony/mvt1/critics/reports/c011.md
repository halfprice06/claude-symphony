## Findings

### 1. Bars 64–68 and 159–161: the main tune closes with its plainest material
**Problem.** After the climax at 62–63, the violins end the theme with two identical units that fall by step:
- `aes''2.~ aes''8 g''8 | g''4 f''4 es''4 des''4`
- `f''2.~ f''8 es''8 | es''4 des''4 c''4 bes'4 | aes'1`

Bars 65 and 67 are four even quarters, the most neutral rhythm in the theme. The recapitulation repeats the pattern at 159–162: `g''2 f''4 e''8 f''8 | a''2.~ a''8 g''8 | g''4 f''4 e''4 d''4 | c''1`.

The ending is the last thing a listener carries away, and here it has none of the theme's own cells. Those cells are the syncopated sixth (48), the rising arpeggio with its 4–3 (49–50), and the cadential turn (53–54). The descending fill after the 62 leap is correct in principle (gap-fill, post-skip reversal). What it lacks is identity.

**Evidence.**
- Tchaikovsky 6/i: the cadence bars 95–96 restate the opening figure of 89–90 exactly (F♯5–E5–D5 | A4–F♯4–D4–F♯4–B4.–A4), and 99–100 do so again.
- Dvořák 9/ii: bars 15–16 restate 7–8 literally. The last phrase (17–18) brings the tune's highest note and its dotted cell into the cadence.

In both, the cadence rhymes with the theme's own head. Huron's account of the pleasure of a predicted return supports the same choice.

**Fix (exposition).** Bars 65–66 rhyme with 49–50 (dotted quarter, eighth, rising arpeggio, then two halves). Bar 67 rhymes with 53. The line stays below the d♭''' of 63. ### 2. Bars 102–103: the B-minor statement of T2 breaks off, and the line jumps
**Problem.** The development's T2 in B minor (97–102) ends with `f''4 des'''2 c'''4`, which sounds as F♯–D–C♯ inside `\transpose c cis`. The next bar starts the dominant build on a♭'' over F. So the melody drops from C♯6 to A♭5 just as the bass slides from F♯ to F. This is the open issue logged in cycle 9, heard from the melodic side: nothing joins the two bars.

**Evidence.** In Tchaikovsky 6/i, the chromatic shifts in the second-theme area (142–146) are carried by a held or repeated top note. The same principle is a common-tone pivot, and the melody already owns the right pitch: G♯ is the 9th over F♯ and is enharmonically the A♭ of bar 103.

**Fix.** On beat 4 of 102, split the c♯''' into c♯'''–g♯'', so the line lands on the 103 pitch. Section bar 26 (movement 102) is written inside `\transpose c cis`:
- `devFluteOne`: `f''4( des'''2 c'''8 g''8) }`
- `devOboeOne`: `f''4 des'''2 c'''8 g''8) }`
- `devVlnOne`: `f''4 des'''2 c'''8 g''8) }`
- `devVlnTwo` (it reaches a♭' at 103): `f'4 des''2 c''8 g'8) }`
- `devCorAnglais`: `f'4 des''2 c''8 g'8) }`

Those are all the doublings. No slur is added. The existing phrase slur still closes on the new last eighth. The F♯→F bass slip remains a harmonic question for the harmony lens. This change does not undo any earlier fix.
