# Critic 055 — lens 5, engraving and notation (v74)

## What I studied
I rendered all 17 pages of v74/score.pdf at 100 dpi and cropped the dense areas at 200–220 dpi: bars 11–14, 19–21, 26–33, 69–71, 85–99, 109–113, 136–138 and 148–162. I compared the labelling of shared staves and tuplets with hand-engraved Mutopia sources in the corpus. Dvořák 7, `mutopia/DvorakA_O70/.../Common/Declarations.ly`, defines `oneSolo = "1. Solo"` (line 329) and a `tupletOff` macro (lines 375–376). Its movement-I staves set `soloText = "1."` per pair (`DvorakSYMPH7M1_Staves.ly`). Dvořák 9, `mutopia/DvorakA_O95/Sym9/Sym9-lys/Mvt1_Adagio.ly` lines 56–64, uses "Solo III"/"Solo IV" on the horn 3·4 staff, so each player is named by their own number. Beethoven, Egmont, `defs.ily` line 53 (`hideTupletNumber`, used in `viola.ly` line 96), hides the numbers of running triplets. Tchaikovsky 6/i (`musicxml-converted/ly/Tchaikovsky_Sym6_mvt1.ly` line 1838) prints "I." with "solo". The conventions from Gould's *Behind Bars* below are cited from memory: player numbers on shared staves, tuplet numerals dropped once a pattern is established, pedal changes written in rests ahead of the note, and hairpins not run through rests. My web searches found no online copy of the relevant pages. The search results (https://handbook.musescore.org/idiomatic-notation/harp, https://www.steinberg.help/dorico/v3/en/dorico/topics/notation_reference/notation_reference_harp_pedaling/notation_reference_harp_pedaling_c.html, https://notat.io/viewtopic.php?p=1628) confirm only general harp-pedal and tuplet practice, not page numbers.

## Findings (ranked)

**1. Harp pedal changes printed under the note that needs them (56–68, 148–162). Applied.**
Most text pedal marks sit under the first note that uses the new pitch, even when the harpist has rests just before. The worst case is bar 160: "A♭" is printed under beat 1, but A♮ sounds on beat 1 of 159 and A♭ on beat 1 of 160. The only time to change is the rest on beats 3–4 of 159, and nothing marks it there. The same applies at 158: "B♮ A♮" is under the first note, after four empty bars (154–157). At 152–153, "B♭" and "A♭" are under the notes, but no B or A sounds in 151. In the exposition, "F♭" is at 61 beat 1 (the beat-3 rest of 60 is free) and "F♮" is at 64 (no F sounds in 62–63). Fix (no notes changed):
- recTwo harpL: `<g,, g,>2 r2_\markup \small "B♭ A♭" | <bes,, bes,>2\< r2 | <f,, f,>2\f r2 | R1_\markup \small "B♮ A♮" | R1*3 | <a,, a,>2\f r2 | <f,, f,>2 r2_\markup \small "A♭" | <d, d>2 <g,, g,>2 |`
- tTwo harpL: `<ges,, ges,>2_\markup \small "G♭" <es, es>2_\markup \small "F♭" | <des, des>2 <fes, fes>2 | es,8(\mf_\markup \small "F♮" aes, c es) r2 | … | <f,, f,>2 r2 |`

**2. Horns 3·4 are labelled "1." and "2." (13, 73, 127, 161). Applied.**
The global `soloText "1." / soloIIText "2."` also applies to the Hn 3·4 staff, so Hn 4's pp pedal at 73 reads "2." and Hn 3's solos at 13, 127 and 161 read "1.". A player or conductor reads "2." as horn 2, who is on another staff. Dvořák 9 (above) names horns 3 and 4 by their own numbers. Fix in score.ly, on the Horns 3·4 staff: `\with { … soloText = "3." soloIIText = "4." … }`.

**3. Hn 2 re-enters at 64 without a label. Applied.**
After cycle 54 gave 60–63 to Tpt 1, the Hn 1·2 staff is empty for four bars and Hn 2 re-enters at 64 (mp). The part-combiner does not restate "2.", because its state did not change, and the last "2." was printed at 56 on the previous system. Fix in tTwo `tTwoHnTwo`: `c'2\mp^\markup \bold "2."`. The Tpt 1 counterline at 60–63 already shows "1." correctly, and the flute and oboe rests at 60–61, 77–78, 152–153 and after 157 engrave cleanly.

**4. Tuplet number on every triplet, 109–113 (Cl 1·2, Vla). Applied.**
The clarinets and violas show twenty "3"s each over five bars of continuous eighth-note triplets. Egmont (`hideTupletNumber`) and Dvořák 7 (`tupletOff`) hide them once the pattern is clear. Fix in dev.ily: keep bar 109 as is, put `\omit Staff.TupletNumber` before bar 110 and `\undo \omit Staff.TupletNumber` after bar 113, in clOne, clTwo and vla. The Staff prefix is needed on the part-combined clarinet staff. Bar 121 (cello, one bar) is left alone.

**5. Bassoon "solo, espr." stacked above "1." (33, 77). Applied.**
On the brief's question: "solo" is the right word as long as the player number is also given. Dvořák 7 uses "1. Solo" and Tchaikovsky 6/i uses "I." + "solo". The problem is the layout: the combiner's "1." and the italic "solo, espr." print as two separate lines. Fix in trans.ily (33) and dev.ily (77): `\set Staff.soloText = "1. Solo" r8 g8\mf^\markup \italic "espr."( …) | \set Staff.soloText = "1." c'4) …`. The page now shows "1. Solo" in bold with "espr." above it.

**6. Vln II hairpin runs through a whole bar of rest (95–97). Applied.**
The crescendo begun at 95 continues across bar 97, which is entirely rest in Vln II, and ends at the ff on 98. Fix in dev.ily `devVlnTwo`: `\transpose c cis { r1\! | …` (ends the hairpin at the 97 barline). The Vln I and flute hairpins that lead over beats 1–3 of 97 into the pickup ff are standard and left alone.

**7. Bar numbers pushed sideways or downward by the double-bass dynamics. Not applied.**
At 26, 41–42, 109, 116, 118, 121–122, 127, 132, 137, 158 and 166, the centred number below the Cb staff is moved left or down by a Cb dynamic, so these numbers sit off their barlines. Suggested fix (untested, and it may change the page count): in score.ly, give the Cb BarNumber `staff-padding = #7` instead of 5, so all numbers clear the dynamics on one baseline. Keep them under the Cb staff (protected).

**8. "étouffez" at 160 is ambiguous. Not applied.**
The word sits over the right-hand rest on beat 3, while the left hand strikes a G octave on that same beat. It is not clear which strings are damped. Fix: `r2^\markup \small \italic "étouffez (m.d.)"`, or move it to the LH rest at 161 beat 3 if the intention is to damp everything before the 162 chord.
