## Findings

### 1. There were no parts. parts.ily only assembles the per-player variables (applied)
parts.ily is a generated list of single-player variables; no file engraved a part. I added a separate build target next to score.ly, built from those single-player variables (not the part-combined staves), so each player has his own dynamics (MOLA PDF 7: one wind player per part):
- `parts.ly`: one `\book` per player, 30 PDFs (`partsout/parts-NN-<instrument>.pdf`); generated by `parts-gen.py` (edit the table there, not parts.ly).
- `parts-lib.ily`: removes score-only material from a player's music (system/page breaks, `\tag #'score` material, part-combine labels "1."/"2."/"a2", rest/note-column overrides placed for the other player, pitched rests), moves dynamics hung on rests to the next note, joins neighbouring full-bar rests, drops `\mt` MIDI steps, inserts cues, and has an optional proofreading log (`PARTS_AUDIT=1`).
- `parts-clefs.ily`: clef plans for the horn 4 and bass clarinet parts.
- `build-parts.sh`: `bash build-parts.sh <version-dir>` after `tools/build.sh <version-dir> score.ly` (build.sh regenerates parts.ily). Build is 0 warnings, about 30 s.
Paper B4, staff 7.2 mm, 20 mm margins, page-turn-aware breaking (MOLA PDF 7–8).

### 2. Transposed parts printed the concert key signature (applied)
The key signatures are in `\global`; the first extraction transposed the notes but not `\global`, so Cl 1 read three flats at the opening instead of one (MOLA PDF 5: transposing parts in the transposed key). Fix in parts-gen.py: a part that transposes its notes transposes `\partGlobal` by the same interval (`\transpose bes c' \partGlobal …`). Clarinets now read D minor / B♭ major / C♯ minor / B minor / D major; C.a. G minor etc. Horns, trumpets and timpani have no key signature (Key_engraver removed), per MOLA PDF 5 and the c165 agreement with movement II; the score keeps concert pitch with signatures.

### 3. Multi-measure rests broken into single bars (applied)
Hn 4 at 171–174 printed four separate whole-bar rests and 107–110 printed "3" + one bar, because `R1 | R1` keeps bar-check events between the rests and `R1*3\! | R1` carries a hairpin end. MOLA Ex. 3–4 (PDF 10) show one numbered rest, split only at tempo changes and rehearsal letters. Fix in parts-lib.ily: bar checks are dropped when joining rests (the score build still checks them), and a rest that only ends a hairpin may be joined to the rests after it, keeping the `\!`. Hn 4 now shows "6" at 171–176 and "4" at 107–110.

### 4. Tempo words stacked over short rests (applied)
In Hn 4, "poco animando" (156) sat above "Poco meno mosso" over the 9-bar rest, and "rit." (177) floated above the "Più lento" mark: the anchored-marking collisions MOLA warns about (PDF 8, Ex. 7 PDF 12). Fix: the parts layout gives MetronomeMark and RehearsalMark horizontal extent (the `\markLengthOn` overrides, `extra-spacing-width` / `extra-spacing-height`). Each word now stands over its own bar. One side effect: Hn 4 now fits on one page.

### 5. Horn bass clef: modern notation and return point (applied)
Only Hn 4 needs bass clef (concert D3/A♭2 at 11–12 and the low line 23–25). The part writes these in new notation, a fifth above sounding in both clefs (Adler PDF 304), with the warning note Adler suggests: "bass clef in new notation: sounds a 5th lower". The earlier attempt went back to treble at 28. I moved it to 26: the ff C4 and f D4 at 26–27 sit at the bottom of the treble stave instead of above the bass stave. The score's bass clefs on the Hn 3·4 staff are dropped in the parts. Hn 1–3 stay in treble (two ledger lines at most).

### 6. Harp pedal marks: kept in the score, left out of the harp part (applied)
MOLA PDF 5: "Harp pedaling should be left to the performer and not printed." The diagrams (`\pedAflat`, `\pedNat`, `\pedBflatAflat`) and the small pedal letters ("G♭", "F♮", "B♮ A♮" …) at 47–50, 54–57, 154–156 stay in the score, where they show the conductor that the changes work. In the harp part they are redefined as `\markup \null` or filtered out (`partCleanHarp`). This follows c155/c165 ("harp pedal marks out of the harp part"). If the composer wants them kept in the part, delete the three `ped… = \markup \null` lines in parts-gen.py.

### 7. Page turns and cover pages (applied)
Wind and brass parts take 1–2 pages. In each 2-page part the page break is placed by `ly:page-turn-breaking` at a rest of at least two bars: Fl 1 and Hn 1 turn in the 4-bar rest at 127–130, and Ob 1 turns into the 9-bar rest at 147. Strings and harp have three pages: a cover (title, movement, instrument), with the music on pages 2–3 as one opening, so there is no turn at all. This is the case MOLA PDF 6 gives for a cover page. The cover no longer repeats the title block above itself (`bookTitleMarkup = ##f` in those books).
