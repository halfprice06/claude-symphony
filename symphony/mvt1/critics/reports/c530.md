## Findings

This is the fourth pass of this reading under this lens. c230, c330 and c430 recorded the structural links: the key cycle as the motto's sixth, the common-tone join into movement II, the 77–82 Appassionata plan, the development stations that spell the stop chord, and the ♭VI at 160. I did not repeat those. I looked for places where the arc falls short of a master's, not only for rule breaks. I found none that clears the bar without spending a finale reserve or reopening a protected passage. The result is no score change. Findings 1–3 give the evidence. Finding 4 lists what I considered and rejected.

### 1. Nothing another movement relies on has changed since the v521 release check. Verified; no change.

- Note diff of full.midi, v521 against v530: one change only. The violas at 159.1–159.1.5 play E4 (was F4), from c522. Bar 159 is the cadential 6/4 → V7 inside the protected climax. No other movement quotes it, and it touches no reserve: no C-major triad, no trumpet B→C, no new register.
- `themecheck.py themes-mvt1.ly <work> --beats 4`, run from a scratch copy with `python3 -B`: 35/35 unchanged.
- The reserves (A♭6 ceiling, no C7, the tuba's top note F2, the only four-horn unison at 41–42, no trumpet B→C onto C major, no forte root-position C major in the full brass) were checked on the MIDI at c520. The one note changed since then cannot affect them.
- The scratch build of v530 unchanged gives 0 warnings, 18 pages, and only "bar 96: octaves F5->Bb2 over F2->Bb1". vl.txt is identical to v530/vl.txt.

Fix: none.

### 2. Development 73–116: a long run of minor keys, judged against A&S PDF 596. Keep.

A&S PDF 596 notes that composers avoid "an unrelieved succession of minor chords, the sonority of the minor triad creating more tension than that of the major". From the A♭-minor turn at 73 to the wedge at 116, every key area in movement I is minor: A♭ minor, E minor, C minor, the fugato's D–G–C–F minor ending in B♭ minor, then B minor, D minor, E minor, B♭ minor and F minor. That is 44 bars, about 85 s at crotchet = 126. I checked whether this is a flaw in the arc. On the MIDI the run is broken by major sounds at four points. 78.3 is E major for one beat, with G♯5 on top. 82 is A♭ major ff, a whole bar of the P head over the A♭ bass (pitch classes A♭ C E♭ on both halves of the bar). 110 is a soft C-major triad acting as V of F minor. 115 is the one-bar ff root-position D♭ chord. The dominant pedal 117–130 then brings V major.

The book's own model for this plan is the Appassionata (Ex. 32-15 and 32-19, PDF 596 and 600). Its exposition ends in A♭ minor and its development moves through minor keys by major thirds before A♭ major returns as V7 of D♭. Movement I follows the same shape, and its A♭ major at 82 is the place where Beethoven's A♭ major returns. More major keys in the development would spend what the later movements need: II's A♭ major, the scherzo's E-major trio, and the finale's C major, which mvt1.md says movement I must never confirm at forte. The dark run is the start of the arc, not a weakness in it.

Fix: none.

### 3. After the deceptive ♭VI at 160 the motto tail is enlarged only in the inner voices. Keep, and protect Tpt 3 at 160–162.

K. 542 (Ex. 32-21 and 32-22, images, PDF 601–602) is the closest model in the reading to bar 160. A deceptive ♭VI stops the second theme's cadence, and the passage that follows has a "prominent chromatic line" B–B♭–A–G♯ that enlarges the movement's opening motive. In v530 the bars after the fff ♭VI work as follows (MIDI):

- The top line (Fl 1·2, Vln I) falls A♭6 (160.2) – G6 (160.4.5) – F6 (161.1) – D6 – B5 – C6. This is ♭6–5 over the vii°7 of V and V7: the P head's figure, enlarged and heard.
- Tpt 3 has C4 (158) – B3 (159) – E♭4 (160) – D4 (161) – C4 (162), one note per bar. The violas move E4 (158–159.1) – F4 – E♭4 (160) – D4/F4 (161). So the motto tail E♭–D–C is there in whole bars, as K. 542 enlarges its motive, but only in inner voices under fff.

I asked whether to bring that line forward, for example by doubling it in Hn 3·4 at 160–162. I rejected this for two reasons. First, the motto tail is E♭–D–D♭–C, and its D♭ would need ♭II after ♭VI in C. The "Do not reuse" list in mvt1.md reserves "♭VI moving to ♭II in C" for the finale. Without the D♭, a louder E♭–D–C is a plain scale step and would not point to the motto. Second, the climax harmony 158–166 is protected (c430, c443), and the top line already carries the audible enlargement, ♭6–5. The inner E♭4–D4–C4 in Tpt 3 is the right weight for a trace that the finale completes, since its arrival is G–E–D–C–D♭–C in the chorale (IV 224–228).

Fix: none. Add Tpt 3 C4–B3–E♭4–D4–C4 at 158–162 to the do-not-touch list, so that a later voicing change does not lose the inner motto trace (c414 gave Tpt 3 its fresh E♭4 at 160 for a different reason).

### 4. Considered and rejected

a. **Prepare the finale's D♯4→E4 the way Haydn prepares E♭ through D♯ (PDF 597).** Rejected. Movement I leaves its D♯s open on purpose (Hn 1 D♯5 at 106, Vln II D♯4 at 108, both recorded seeds). The scherzo's horns already resolve D♯4→E4 many times (scherzo.md: Hn 2 at 104–127, 196–197, 216–217). Adding the resolution in movement I would take away the one thing the thread leaves for the finale's violas at IV 219–220.

b. **Make the coda's E→E♭ at 166→167 abrupt (subito ff, no crescendo), the Hammerklavier kind of inflection (PDF 584–585).** Rejected. The reading lets rhythm and dynamics decide how abrupt an inflection sounds (PDF 585). A falling inflection (E→E♭) lowers intensity by the book's own measure. The crescendo from 166.3 into the f/ff at 167 is protected (c418 horns p< into 167; c443 162–166 release; c458 Cb crescendo at 169). An abrupt C-major-to-C-minor shock would also come close to the finale's chaos-to-light turn (the PDF 597 footnote), which belongs at IV 222→223.

c. **Turn the deceptive ♭VI at 160 to A♭ minor (K. 542's ♭VI → ♭VI♭, PDF 601).** Rejected for the reason c430 gave, which still holds. A♭ minor is already used where the reading's Appassionata model puts it (73–77), and movement II needs A♭ major to arrive unspent.

d. **Hint at the finale's F1 or F2.** Rejected (c330, c430 reasons). The reading asks for "organic connection" (PDF 602), and the finale's themes already grow from I 12 (the Ger6 = IV 1) and I 37 (the first E♭-minor sound).
