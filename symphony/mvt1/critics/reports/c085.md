## Findings (ranked)

**1. Page turns fall inside phrases: 101|102, 128|129, 137|138, 64|65. Applied.**
- The B-minor block 97–102 leaves its last bar, which carries the turn into A♭ (cycle 11), alone at the top of p. 9. The key change at 103 is mid-system.
- The a tempo bars 127–130 (cello measured accelerando) split 2+2 across pp. 11–12. The recapitulation (H, 131) then starts mid-system.
- The hemiola 135–138 loses its last bar to the page turn after 137.
- The poco animando arrival splits after 64, inside the second-theme ending 64–68.

Gould's score-layout chapter asks for system and page breaks at phrase or section boundaries, rehearsal letters and key changes where possible. Dvořák 7's Mutopia score starts systems at its letters. Fix in global.ily:
- `s1*6 \break` before `\bar "||" \key a \minor` (103)
- `\break \mark \default` at H (131)
- `s1*9 \break s1*3` (break before 94)
- `s1*8 \break` before `\key c \minor` (111)
- `s1*2 \break s1*2` after G (119)
- `s1*8 \break s1*6` after H (139)
- `\mt 118 s1 \break s1*2` (64)

The new pages are 47–63, 64–76, 85–102, 103–110, 119–130, 131–138 and 139–146. It still makes 17 pages. The extra breaks at 94, 111, 119 and 139 are needed: without them LilyPond wanted 18 pages. Bars 73–76 on p. 6 were already tight and are a little tighter now, but they stay legible.

**2. Short names leave out player numbers on some shared staves (every system after the first). Applied.**
Systems after the first print "Fl.", "Ob.", "Cl.", "Bn." and "Tbn.", but "Hn. 1·2", "Tpt. 1·2" and "Tpt. 3". Gould asks for one abbreviation scheme throughout. Dvořák 7 (above) numbers every pair. Fix in score.ly: `shortInstrumentName = "Fl. 1·2"`, and the same for `"Ob. 1·2"`, `"Cl. 1·2"`, `"Bn. 1·2"` and `"Tbn. 1·2"`. The layout does not change.

**3. Harp, 156: crescendo hairpin on one plucked chord followed by a rest. Applied.**
`<bes,, bes,>2\mf\< r2 | <f,, f,>2\f` puts a hairpin over a decaying half note and a half rest. Gould's harp section says dynamic change on harp happens between attacks, not during one attack. The f at 157 already shows the growth. Fix in recTwo.ily: `<bes,, bes,>2\mf r2 | <f,, f,>2\f r2`.

**4. Clarinets, 103: no "a2" in cycle 84's new unison. Applied.**
Cl 1 and Cl 2 were already in unison at 97–102. The part-combiner therefore prints nothing at 103, and the only "a2" is on the previous page. Next to it, the oboe staff reads "2.", so a conductor sees "Ob 2 alone" and an unlabelled clarinet line at a key change and new section. Restating a2 after a section change is standard (Dvořák 7 defines its own `\Atwo` markup for this). Fix in dev.ily, Cl 1 at 103: `r8^\markup \bold "a2" a'8(\f f''4.->) ...`.

**5. Violoncellos, 62: plain p among sub. p. Applied.**
Cycle 68 set sub. p in all parts at 62. Vc plays forte arpeggios through 61, but `tTwoVc` has `es,4\p^"pizz."`. Fix in tTwo.ily: `es,4\subP^"pizz." r4 es4 r4 |`. The MIDI level does not change (\subP maps to p).

**6. Clarinets, 126: the p touches the Cl 2 quarter-note stem. Applied.**
The Cl 1 whole note d' and the Cl 2 quarter c' are a second apart, so the second head is displaced. The p sits between the heads. Fix in dev.ily: `d'1-\tweak X-offset #-2 \p` (Cl 1) and `c'4-\tweak X-offset #-2 \p( b2.)` (Cl 2).

**7. P. 16 holds only 167–170, and the exposition climax 27–32 is split 30|31 across a page turn. Not applied.**
The four-bar brass head (L) fills a whole A3 page. That is defensible, because the page turn then falls at the fermata and the Più lento (M). Keeping 27–32 on one page would need either 18 pages or a 10-bar tutti system on p. 2, so I left both alone. If page count is ever freed, put a `\break` before 27.

**8. No other faults found** in the hairpin, ottava or slur checks on the 17 pages. The bar-number baseline, the tam-tam/cymbal labels and the harp pedal letters are consistent.
