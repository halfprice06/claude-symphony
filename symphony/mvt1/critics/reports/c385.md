## Findings

### 1. Horns 3 and 4, bars 179–180: the "Timp." cue prints the timpani roll of 168–169, not the pulse that is played (applied)
**What is wrong.** Before their ppp entry at 181, Hn 3 and Hn 4 have a "Timp." cue at 179–180 (added at c295). At those bars the timpani play the first group's pulse, `c4 r8 g,8 c4 r4` twice. In both parts the cue now shows two tied whole notes with tremolo strokes. That is the roll at 168–169, eleven bars earlier. The cause is in the quote, not the cue list. Since v366 (c365, "medium sticks" shown in the score) tOneTimp holds `\tag #'part { R1^"medium sticks" | R1*10 } \tag #'score { R1*10 | R1^"medium sticks" }`. `\addQuote "cueTimpHigh" { \transpose c c' \timp }` quotes the variable whole, with both alternatives one after the other, so the quoted timpani line is 193 bars long instead of 182 (I measured `ly:music-length`: timp 193, perc 192, hnOne 183, corAnglais 186¾; every other quoted variable is 182). MOLA PDF 5 says cues "must be audible and obvious to the musician reading the part". A cue that shows a roll while the player hears soft separate strokes misleads the horn player at the quietest entry of the movement, inside the Tempo I change. MOLA PDF 8 is the general rule: "simply extracting parts from the score in notation software will not lead to error-free parts". No earlier critic saw this because the cue was correct when c295 added it.
**Fix (parts-gen.py):**
```
\addQuote "cueTimp" { \removeWithTag #'score \timp }
\addQuote "cueTimpHigh" { \transpose c c' \removeWithTag #'score \timp }
\addQuote "cueHnOne" { \removeWithTag #'score \hnOne }   % not used now; same fault
```
The Hn 3 and Hn 4 parts now show G4 crotchet, rest, D4 quaver, G4 crotchet, rest (the pulse at written pitch an octave up, as c295 intended) in both bars. Page counts are unchanged.

### 2. Bars 66–68: "rit." and "Tempo dell'Allegro" stand together over one rest in eight parts (applied)
**What is wrong.** c368 moved Tempo dell'Allegro from 69 to beat 3 of 68. The parts split multi-bar rests only where a tempo word falls on a barline, so every part that rests from 66 to 68 prints one rest, "3 (66–68)". Both "rit." and "Tempo dell'Allegro (♩ = 126)" hang over its first bar. This happens in Hn 1, Tpt 3, Tbn 1, Tbn 2, B.tbn, tuba, timpani and percussion. A player counting the rest reads a new tempo at 66, two bars early, and cannot see that the rit. runs to the middle of 68. MOLA PDF 5: "Tempo and meter changes must be indicated on all parts, even during periods of extended rest". Ex. 4 (PDF 10) shows how: the viola part breaks its rest at each change ("9 | C … Etwas bewegter 16 | D 36 …"), so every tempo word stands over its own bar. The parts that play in 68 (Fl 1, Hn 2, Vln I) are correct already: "rit. 2 (66–67)", then 68 with the tempo word over beat 3.
**Fix (parts-lib.ily, parts-gen.py):** a `\partSplitAt` helper that reuses `insert-cue` to cut a given bar out of a multi-bar rest. Bars inside a cue or outside any rest are left alone.
```
partSplitAt = #(define-music-function (bars m) (list? ly:music?)
  (for-each (lambda (b) (insert-cue m 0 (bar-moment b) 1 (lambda (len) (mmr-of len)))) bars)
  m)
```
parts-gen.py puts `\partSplitAt #'(68)` in front of every part's cue list and in front of `\partClean \perc`. All eight parts now print "rit. 2 (66–67)" and a one-bar rest at 68 with "Tempo dell'Allegro" over it. The parts with a cue at 67–68 (Ob 1, Ob 2, C.a., Cl 2, B.cl, Bsn 1, Cbsn, Tpt 1, Tpt 2) are unchanged.

### 3. Five exposed entries after 9–12 bars' rest have no cue, although their section partners have one (applied in four parts)
**What is wrong.** MOLA PDF 5 expects "logical cues … during long periods of rest". c265, c295, c355 and c365 cued most such entries. They skipped entries on a letter or a tempo change, and I keep that rule. These five are on neither, and in three of them the neighbouring player in the same section already has a cue for the same entry:
- **Hn 3 and Hn 4, 56**: pp pad entry in the Poco meno mosso after "9 (47–55)". Hn 2, entering in the same bar, has a Vc cue at 54–55.
- **Tbn 1, 113** and **B.tbn, 114**: pp entries in the wedge after 10 and 11 bars. Tbn 2 (Vc cue 111–112) and the tuba (Vc cue 109–110) have cues for the same chord.
- **B.cl, 91**: f after "12 (79–90)", twelve bars at crotchet = 126 (about 23 s), the longest uncued Allegro rest in the wind parts, with no letter between E (77) and F (97).
- **B.cl, 139**: f after the 127–138 rest (split at H), entering straight after the stop chord at 138.

**Fix (parts-gen.py cue lists):** Hn 3 `VC+("DOWN",54,2)`; Tbn 1 `VC+("UP",111,2)`; B.tbn `VC+("UP",112,2)`; B.cl `VA+("UP",89,2)` (the violas' fugato entry, written A4–F♯5 in the B.cl part) and `VC+("UP",137,2)` (the cello hemiola and the stop chord). Every part keeps its page count. Hn 4 does not get the cue: with a two-bar cue, and also with a one-bar cue at 55, its part grows to three pages. That gives an odd last page and breaks the c201 attacca plan (Hn 4 relieves Hn 3 at movement II bar 1). Hn 4 enters with Hn 3, its neighbour on the stand-row, and the 47–55 rest begins at letter C, so that part stays as it is.

### 4. "Lento (♩ = 50)" runs into the "14" of the first rest in seven parts (applied)
**What is wrong.** In Fl 2, Cbsn, Tpt 1, 2, 3, tuba and percussion, the first bar is a 14-bar rest. Its number sits at the height of the tempo mark, and the mark's closing parenthesis touches it, so the line reads "Lento (♩ = 50)14". MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12) name this kind of collision between extracted marks. The parts with shorter opening rests (Fl 1 "10", Ob 1 "11") stack the mark above the number. Those parts are clear.
**Fix (parts-gen.py \layout, Score context):** `\override MetronomeMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #0.8`. The skyline now keeps a little horizontal space around each tempo mark, so the mark sits above the number in all seven parts. No part changes its page count or page turns. The one-page parts reflow slightly, and I checked the percussion, tuba, trombone and timpani pages after the reflow.
