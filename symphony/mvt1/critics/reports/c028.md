# Critic 28 (cycle 28, lens 8: expression) on v47

## What I studied
I read every section file of v47 and rendered pages 7, 9, 13 and 15 at 70 dpi. Corpus: Tchaikovsky 6/i (MusicXML, dumped with music21). Bars 19–22: the violas' and cellos' first theme slurs the pickup into an accented long note, and every note carries a slur or a staccato. Bars 89–94: the second theme is p, horns and bassoons are pp with <mp> swells, and the f peak at 93 has a tenuto on the leap note. Bar 170: the fugato carries a word ("feroce"). Bar 267: "pesante" on the fff brass. Dvořák 9/i (Mutopia): flute solo at bar 149 is p over strings at ppp. Its recurring head `b8.-> d16-. d8-. a-.` has the same articulation every time it appears, and the violins take over the flute's slurs unchanged. Web sources:
- Rimsky-Korsakov, *Principles of Orchestration*, "Crescendo and diminuendo": brass make "crescendo chords into the most brilliant sforzando climaxes", and a diminuendo removes brass first (https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/33900/pg33900.txt).
- Gould, *Behind Bars*, as quoted in forums: a slur in string parts is a bowing, and a slur in wind parts is legato or phrasing (https://www.notat.io/viewtopic.php?p=12173, https://forums.steinberg.net/t/slurs-and-legato/885206).

I tested all fixes below together in a scratch copy, /tmp/critic-028/work/v47, using the script /tmp/critic-028/apply.py. tools/build.sh gives rc 0 and 0 warnings, and the page count stays at 17.

### 2. No crescendo into the fff climax (155)
Everything is ff at 154. At 156 everything jumps to fff and "largamente" begins. In 155 the only swell is the suspended cymbal's pp<. Rimsky-Korsakov says the brass in particular should build into such a climax. As written, the step to fff reads as subito, and the cymbal swells alone. Fix, recTwo k17: start `\<` on the downbeat of 155 in every sounding part, ending at the existing fff. Examples: `recTwoMelodyB` `e'''4\< f'''4 g'''2 |`, the same in `recTwoVlnTwo`, `recTwoFluteOne`, `recTwoFluteTwo` (both flutes, because the staff is shared), `recTwoOboeOne`, `recTwoHnOne`, `recTwoHnTwo` and `recTwoTptOne`. Also `recTwoSync`/`recTwoArp` `e'4\< f'4 g'2 |`, `recTwoCounter`/`recTwoOboeTwo`/`recTwoHnThree` `b'2\< f'2 |`, `recTwoClTwo` `d'1\<`. Low parts take the hairpin on the second whole note: `recTwoHnFour` `g1\<`, `recTwoTptTwo` `g'1\<`, `recTwoTptThree` `b1\<`, `recTwoTbnOne` `b1\<`, `recTwoTbnTwo` `g1\<`, `recTwoBassTbn` `g,1\<`, `recTwoTuba`/`recTwoCbsn`/`recTwoCb` `g,,1\<`, `recTwoBsnOne`/`recTwoBsnTwo` `g,1\<`, `recTwoTimp` `g,1:32\<`. Shared staves get the same hairpin from both players.
