## Findings

### 1. 182: "attacca" appears in only one of 30 parts (applied)

**Problem.** The movement's cyclic close depends on movement II starting with no pause:
- five parts are tied across (Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc, Hn 3);
- Hn 4 relieves Hn 3 on II bar 1 beat 2;
- the timpani keep G–A♭–C–E♭;
- the viola's E♮ falls to E♭ at II bar 2.

In the score, "attacca" is a \textEndMark inside Vln I's coda line. Extracted parts take it only from that variable. pdftotext on v219/partsout: Violin I = 1, every other part = 0. The Horn 3, Viola and Cello players, who must hold the tie into the next movement, are never told to. Horn 4, who must enter in movement II's first bar, is not told either.

**Evidence.**
- MOLA PDF 5: instructions that govern the performance appear "on all parts, even during periods of extended rest".
- MOLA PDF 7: players need time to change pages, and "attacca" is what tells them there is none.
- Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 21, axiom II: parts must work at sight.
- A&S PDF 445–446: the join is a common-tone link, so a pause would break the modulation itself.
- Corpus and web as above.

**Fix (applied).**
- `coda.ily`, Vln I bar 182: `\tag #'score \textEndMark \markup \italic "attacca"`. The score is unchanged, and the part no longer prints it twice.
- `parts-gen.py`:
  ```
  partGlobalPlain = \partGlobalOf \global
  partGlobal = << \partGlobalOf \global { \skip 1*182 \textEndMark \markup \italic "attacca" } >>
  ```
  The harp's LH staff uses `\partGlobalPlain`, so the harp prints it once.
- Result: "attacca" in all 30 parts, once each. Part page counts are unchanged.

### 2. Front page: the timpani tunings are wrong since v196 (applied)

**Problem.** The front page says "Timpani (4 drums: G, A♭, C/B♭/B♮, E♭)". Since cycle 176 the retunes are on the A♭ drum: "muta A♭ in B♭" at 34 and "muta A♭ in B♮" at 84, each returned to A♭. The comment in trans.ily says "The C drum now stays at C all movement." A timpanist setting up from the front page would assign B♭ and B♮ to the wrong drum.

**Evidence.**
- MOLA PDF 6: the front matter lists percussion "including required note ranges".
- Adler App. A, adler2 PDF 307 (c176): B♭2 and B2 lie at or below the bottom of the C drum.

**Fix (applied, score.ly).** `\line { "Timpani (4 drums: G, A♭/B♭/B♮, C, E♭)" }`

### 3. Title block: "draft v219" on the release copy (applied)

**Problem.** Cycle 39 added a draft number for tracking during the series. Cycle 200 ends the series, and the promoted version is the release. "Draft" on the cover says the score is not final.

**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 6: the title appears "as it would appear in a formal concert program", and "it is helpful to add a catalog number, print date, or revision date".

**Fix (applied, score.ly).** `"Full score in C — v220, 12 October 2026"`. The composer sets the number to the promoted version. The composer name stays unset (c198 decision).
