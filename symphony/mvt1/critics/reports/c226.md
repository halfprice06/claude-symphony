## Findings

### 1. Violins I, bars 11–13: two six-second bows at Lento, one under a crescendo to f (applied)

What is wrong. At Lento, crotchet = 50, one bar lasts 4.8 s. Bar 11 slurs `b'4( aes''4~ | aes''2.)` under the crescendo that arrives at f on the downbeat of 12: five beats, 6.0 s, on one bow. Bar 12 then ties `g''4~ | g''1\>`, another five beats (6.0 s) starting at f. Sixteen violins are the top line of the introduction's climax here, doubled by Fl 1.

Evidence. Adler PDF 39: "Even if the dynamic is soft, special caution must be taken in slow passages not to overload the bow." PDF 39–40, Ex. II-23 (Liszt, *Les Préludes*), which I viewed as an image: a long slur under a crescendo at a slow tempo "is impossible to perform as the composer has marked while still adhering to the crescendo marks, unless it is broken up into several bows" (Ex. II-24). PDF 38, Ex. II-20 (Beethoven, *Coriolan*): Beethoven marks ⊓ and V over one long note so that the following attack is solid. PDF 38 also sets the direction: crescendo falls naturally on an up-bow and diminuendo on a down-bow.

Fix, applied. The slur ends on the crotchet A♭5; bow changes are marked on the tied continuations. The resulting bowing is: 9 up (anacrusis, PDF 38), 10.4 down, 11.3 up with the crescendo, 12 down at f, 12.4 up, 13 down with the diminuendo. No note, tie or dynamic changes.

```
f''2) b'4( aes''4)~ | aes''2.\f\downbow g''4~ | g''1\>\downbow | R1\! |
```
(intro.ily, `introVlnOne`). The bars 9–13 notes are protected; they are untouched.

### 2. Cellos, bars 63–64: one beat to return from pizz. to arco at poco animando (applied)

What is wrong. The cellos play pizz. at 62–63 (`es,4 r4 es4 r4 | es,4 r4 e,4 r4`) and arco at the downbeat of 64, mf, beginning the semiquaver-arpeggio figure. Between the last pizz. (63, beat 3) and the arco there is one crotchet at poco animando (crotchet = 115–118): about 0.5 s.

Evidence. Adler PDF 54: "some short time must be allowed for the player to prepare himself to pluck and then again to resume the natural way of playing ... These are rare and should be avoided if at all possible. (This is especially true of the return from pizzicato to arco, which is a bit more awkward ... because the bow must be rearranged into playing position.)" The score already does the other half of Adler's advice: at 61 the last group is marked up-bow, so the hand is at the frog for the pizz. at 62 (PDF 54).

Fix, applied. The pizz. E2 on beat 3 of 63 is cut. The same E♮2 is held arco by the double basses (`es,2\< e,2`) and Bassoon 2 has E3, so the chromatic bass E♭–E♮–F (protected in cycle 67) still sounds in full. The cellos get three beats (about 1.5 s) to take the bow.

```
es,4\subP^"pizz." r4 es4 r4 | es,4 r4 r2 |
```
(tTwo.ily, `tTwoVc`). The all-pairs check gives no new parallels.

### 3. Violins II, bar 73: "unis." a half-bar late (applied)

What is wrong. `<d' f'>2(\pp^"div." es'2) | es'1^"unis."`: the divided D4/F4 resolves on beat 3 to a single E♭4, but "unis." appears only at 74. On beat 3 the player reads a single note while "div." is still in force.

Evidence. Adler PDF 30: "When divisi is no longer in order and the part returns to single notes, the word unisoni (or more commonly ... unis.) should appear on the part." Ex. II-12 (PDF 31, viewed) shows the grouping written at the point where the voices join.

Fix, applied. `<d' f'>2(\pp^"div." es'2)^"unis." | es'1 |` (clos.ily). Both halves of the section move to E♭4 on beat 3 (D→E♭ and F→E♭ of the common-tone diminished seventh, as c172 intended).

### Checked and kept

- **Vc/Cb/B.cl 1–3 (motto).** Slurs of five beats (6.0 s) at pp with small swells. Adler PDF 39 warns about slow bows, but at pp a 6 s bow is normal for cellos and basses, and the up-bow anacrusis carries the swell's crescendo (PDF 38). Scoring of 1–4 is protected. No change.
- **Vc 175–176.** `g,1\pp( | es1)` is 6.7 s at crotchet = 72, pp. The bow is shorter (PDF 39), but at pp this is still inside one bow, and the slur keeps the chorale's G–E♭ joined (chorale slurs protected, c188). If a performance has trouble, the remedy in Adler's own terms is Ex. II-25: stagger the bow among the players. No change.
- **Vln I 177–179.** `g'4( es''1)~ es''2. d''4( c''2 des''2)` at pp: the longest bow is 6 s at pp, and 180 already carries "change bow at will". No change.
- **Bar 33 triple-stops** (Vla, Vc) are ff, short, with two open strings each: Adler's best case (PDF 29).
- **Bar 61→62 up-bow before pizz.** follows PDF 54 exactly.
