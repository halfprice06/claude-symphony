## Measurement first: is the bass too root-position?
- **Root position:** by beats, v121 is 66% root position. The MCTL annotations give Beethoven 9/i 62% and Tchaikovsky 6/i 73% (by duration).
- **Harmonic rhythm:** the average chord lasts 2.4 beats in v121, against 2.6 in both models.
- **The tuttis critic 99 named:**
  - 27–32 stands over a chromatic, mostly inverted bass, C–D–E♭–E♮–F–D–G: i, vii°4/3, iv4/2, vii°7/iv, iv, iiø6/5, V.
  - 131–134 and 167–170 stand over pedals.

So critic 99's "root-position bass" holds for one passage only: the codetta 69–72, which is 94% root position with diatonic chords only. The development's route (103–117) and the climaxes (122, 158–166) are protected. I checked them and found nothing to fix there.

The more useful result of the check was a set of unflagged parallel fifths in exposed, unprotected bars: 46, 47, 52, 69, 70, 72 and 24. Rimsky-Korsakov, ch. III (PDF 81): "No perfection in resonance can accrue from faulty progression of parts."

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 69–72, codetta: a textbook chain over root position, with fifths in the violas. APPLIED.
- **Problem:**
  - The bass runs A♭–F | D♭–B♭ | E♭ | A♭ (I–vi | IV–ii | V7 | I): falling thirds, all diatonic, in half notes in every part.
  - The broken-chord violas move in fifths with the bass at 69 beat 3 (E♭4/A♭1 → C4/F1) and at 70 beat 3 (A♭4/D♭2 → F4/B♭1).
  - Violas and cellos also make fifths into the cadence, 71→72 (B♭3/E♭3 → E♭4/A♭3).
- **Evidence:**
  - Tchaikovsky 6/i closing group, bars 134–141, has an applied diminished seventh with its leading tone in the bass on the last beat of every second bar: G♯1 at 136 beat 4 → A1 at 137, and again at 140→141. MCTL marks vii°7/V, vii°7/ii, ii4/2, ♯iv° and ♭VI6 in 16 bars (onsets 540–600). The v121 codetta has no chromatic chord.
  - A&S Ex. 12-1 (PDF 176) shows the Schubert *Ständchen* moving I–(VI)–II6/5–V–I. PDF 177: "By far the most important position is II6/5, an indispensable chord."
  - A&S PDF 420 (Ex. 25-4): "In progressions containing applied V6/5 … the active character of the leading tone in the bass makes up for any slight harshness". The cross relation must stay between an inner voice and the bass. Here E♭ is in the inner voices, E♮ in the bass, and the top voice holds C.
  - A&S PDF 419: "the temporary leading tone is not doubled".
  - Rimsky PDF 83: "The bass of an inversion of the dominant chord should never be doubled in any of the upper parts."
- **Fix (clos.ily):** the new progression is I–V6/5 of vi | vi–ii6/5 | V7 | I, with E♮ only in the bass. The melody, the counter (Cl 1, Hn 1·2, Vln II) and bar 72 are unchanged.
  - Bass (BCl, Bsn 2, BTbn and Vc at their own octave; Cbsn, Tuba and Cb an octave lower): `aes,2 e,2 | f,2 des2 | es1`
  - Fl 2: `es''2 g''2`
  - Ob 2: `c''2 c''2 | c''2 bes'2`
  - Cl 2: `aes'2 g'2`
  - Bsn 1: `c'2 c'2`
  - Hn 3: `aes2 g2`
  - Hn 4: `es2 c2`
  - Tpt 2: `aes'2 g'2`
  - Tbn 1: `es'2 c'2 | c'2 des'2`
  - Tbn 2: `c'2 c'2 | c'2 bes2`
  - Timp 69: `aes,4 r8 aes,8 r2`
  - Vla: `c'8( aes8 c'8 es'8) g8( bes8 g8 bes8) | c'8( aes8 c'8 f'8) f'8( des'8 bes8 des'8) | g8( bes8 des'8 es'8) g'8( es'8 bes8 des'8) |`. Each half-bar still starts on a third or fifth, which is cycle 90's rule. The 7th, D♭, now resolves to C at 72.
- **Result:** the bass is A♭–E♮–F–D♭–E♭–A♭. The E♮–F also echoes the movement's E♭/E♮ thread (29–31, 63–64). None of the parts listed above is protected. The broken-chord figure accepted in cycle 90 is kept.

### 2. 51–52, second theme: parallel fifths between the melody and the violas. APPLIED.
- **Problem:** Hn 1 and Vc go B♭4→A♭4 while the viola top goes E♭4→D♭4, at p, under a solo line.
- **Fix (tTwo.ily):** Vla 52 `<aes f'>1` (was `<aes des'>1`).
- **Protections:** this keeps 47–55 "plain" and "neutral" (cycles 67, 82, 91). The bass, the harmony (IV) and the texture are unchanged; only one note of a sustained chord moves, in contrary motion.

### 3. 45–47, end of the transition: fifths and an unresolved seventh. APPLIED.
- **Problem:**
  - Vla C4→B♭3 against the Ob 1 solo G4→F4 (45→46) makes fifths.
  - Vla B♭3→E♭4 against the bass E♭→A♭ (46→47) makes fifths.
  - The 7th of the V7 at 46 (D♭, Vln II) leaps to A♭ and never resolves.
- **Evidence:** A&S PDF 177: the dissonance's "resolution is exactly the same — downward and by step".
- **Fix (trans.ily):** Vla `c'2 es'2 | bes2 des'2 |`. The viola's D♭ resolves to C at 47, and both fifths are gone.

### 4. 33–36: a falling-fifth chain over root-position whole notes. APPLIED.
- **Problem:** the Cb plays C | F | B♭ | E♭ under the bassoon's solo head, the most mechanical bass in the exposition.
- **Evidence:** A&S, Unit 17 §7 (PDF 269), Ex. 17-4, Handel's Musette, is in C minor and harmonizes the same series as I–IV6–VII–III6, with bass C–A♭–B♭–G. In A&S's words, the alternation "tends to enhance one of the stepwise bass lines".
- **Fix (trans.ily):** Cb 34 `aes,,1` (iv6).
- **Why I stop at 34:** I did not continue to III6 at 36. A G in the bass there would double the leading tone of A♭ (Vc/Bsn 1 have G), so 36 keeps E♭.

### 5. 24–25, first-theme hemiola: Hn 4 moves in fifths with the top line. APPLIED.
- **Problem:** Hn 4 G2→C3 under Fl/Ob/Vln I D5→G5 at 24 beat 3.
- **Fix (tOne.ily):** Hn 4 `c2.\mp\<-> g,4->~ | g,2 g,2->~ | g,4 f,2.->`, with the accents kept.
- **Side effect:** at 25 beat 2, Hn 4 G→F now doubles the inner G→F line two octaves lower. That is duplication in Rimsky's sense (PDF 82–83), not a fault.

### 6. 97–99: figuration fifths in a protected block. NOT APPLIED.
- **Problem:** in the B-minor block, the clarinets' last arpeggio eighth F♯4 → E4 moves in fifths with the melody C♯ → B into 99.
- **Why not applied:** 97–102 is protected and the fifths are hidden in the arpeggio. If the composer reopens it, the fix is Cl 98, last eighth `d'` (concert, after `\transpose c cis`: write `des'`).

### 7. Development and climaxes. NO CHANGE.
- **Development:** the route (Ab pedal/E, C, V; B minor; D–E–F–G, wedge, Fr6 over C, G pedal) already uses mixture, Neapolitan, Ger6/Fr6 and applied diminished sevenths. I found nothing a master would add that does not break cycle 72/73's protections.
