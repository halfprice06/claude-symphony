## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 166: the brass vii°7 has no A♭ and four leading tones, reached by augmented seconds
**What is wrong.** Cycle 29 put Hn 1·2 on the tune, so B now sounds in Tpt 1·2 (B4), Hn 1·2, Hn 4 and Tbn 2 (B3). The brass chord is B B B B D D F with no seventh (A♭). Hn 4 and Tbn 2 reach their B3 by an augmented second (A♭3→B3) at ff. Four B3s sit a major seventh over B.tbn/B.cl/Bsn 2 C3: the densest low-register rub in the score, under a fermata.

The rhyming bars do not do this. At 26 the brass carry A♭ twice (Hn 1, Tbn 1) and B only twice (Tpt 1, Hn 4 an octave lower).

**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov ch. III: "In a chain of consecutive chords it is advisable to entrust the stationary parts to the brass." A♭ is the common tone from 165.

**Fix (coda bar 4).** Hold the A♭.
- `codaHnFour`: `aes2.->\ff r4\fermata`
- `codaTbnTwo`: `aes2.->\ff r4\fermata`

The shared staves keep equal ff: Hn 3 d' with Hn 4 aes, and Tbn 1 d' with Tbn 2 aes. The vii°7 rhyme with 26/130 is kept and becomes complete in the brass. The tune is untouched.

### 2. Bar 28: Tpt 1 re-strikes G4 on beat 3 against the melody's A♭4
**What is wrong.** Cycle 29's timpani rhythm (`g'4 r4 g'4 r4`) re-attacks G4 on beat 3 exactly when Ob 1, Cl 1 and Vln II arrive on A♭4. This is a minor second in the melody's own octave, with trumpet at f.

In v42 the G was held, so the dissonance was a tie. Now it is a fresh brass attack on the melody's ♭9. A ♭9 needs the root a ninth below it, not a second: Fl 2's A♭5 over Tpt 1's G4 on beat 1 is the correct spacing.

**Fix (tOne bar 14).** `tOneTptOne`: `g'4 r4 b'4 r4`

The B4 is a chord tone that leads to Tpt 1's C5 at 29 and keeps the rhythm. It removes the beat-3 G/A♭ entries from clash.py. If the composer dislikes the doubled leading tone, use `g'4 r4 r2` instead (Tpt 2·3 keep beat 3).

### 3. Second-theme harmony: little more variety is needed, but the restatements' ii–V can move
**Judgement.** Critic 22's description is now too strong. Inside the internal phrases the groups already contain:
- V6/5 of IV at 51 and 143 (C–E♭–G♭–A♭; C7/E);
- an augmented I at 56;
- IV over the tonic pedal at 57 and 149;
- the C–B–B♭ slide at 148;
- V7→♭III at 59–60 and 151–152, a real deceptive move;
- Dø7 at 64;
- V–vi at 157–158;
- the beat-3 dominants at 66 and 160.

What is still plain is the ii→V approach: root-position ii for a whole bar, then V (49–50, 53–54, 58–59, 141–142, 145–146, 150–151).

**Evidence.**
- Tchaikovsky 6/i keeps its first statement over chromatic neighbour chords and changes chord every half bar (vii°7/ii–ii, vii°7/V–I6/4–V7: MCTL bars 93–96). His reprise reharmonizes one bar (133.1 ii4/2 where the first statement had vii°7 at 92.1).
- In 2nd-B, 11 of 25 chords are inverted (vi6, V6, V6/4 of vi).
- Dvořák 9/i bars 149–154 harmonize the flute tune I–vi–iii–V(over A)–I: one chord per bar, but the bass moves G–E–B–A–D–G rather than by root fifths.

The first statements (47–55 and 139–147) are protected and already contain the V6/5 of IV. I recommend changing only the restatements: make ii → ii6 on beat 3. This gives the later hearings a moving bass (scale degrees 2→4→5) and half-bar harmonic rhythm into V, and needs no melody note changed. The melody's beats 3–4 are B♭–D♭–F (58) and D–F–A (150), all in the new chord.
- **tTwo bar 12 (58):** `tTwoCb`: `bes,,2 des,2`; `tTwoBsnTwo`: `bes,2 des2`
- **recTwo bar 12 (150):** `recTwoCb`: `d,2 f,2`; `recTwoBsnTwo`: `d2 f2`. The cello arpeggio already ends on F3 there, and the counter is on A4.

Hn 3 (D♭4) and the Vla dyads already double the new bass note, scale degree 4, which is the textbook doubling for ii6. In the test, the only octaves this adds are bass-line doublings. I do not recommend inversions or chromatic chords inside 47–55 or 139–147.

### 4. Bar 151: viola holds the resolution B3 under the melody's C6 suspension
**What is wrong.** `recTwoSync` bar 4 (`r4 b4~ b8 …`) sounds B3 for 1½ beats from beat 2, while Ob 1 and the violins suspend C6 until beat 3. Cycles 2, 12 and 22 removed this fault at 14, 28, 101 and 123–124. The exposition model (59, Vla `<bes des'>` then `<des' g'>` on beat 4) avoids it. This viola fill predates cycle 23 but was not caught.

**Fix (`recTwoSync` bar 4, used by `recTwoVla` at 151).** `r4 g4~ g8 b8( d'8 f'8) |`

This moves stepwise from A3 at 150 and reaches B only after the resolution. Tested: clean.
