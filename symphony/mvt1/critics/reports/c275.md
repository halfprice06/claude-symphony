## Findings

Both findings are in the parts. On the score pages I found nothing that goes against the reading. Earlier engraving cycles (c165, c185, c225–c265) have already applied this chapter to the score.

### 1. Oboe 1 has no cue before its forte entry at 69 (applied)

**Where.** Oboe 1 part, 44–68 rest, entry at 69.

**Problem.** Cycle 274 moved the 45–46 link from Ob 1 to Cl 1, so Ob 1 now rests from beat 2 of 44 to 68. That is about 25 bars, through a tempo change, poco animando and rit. Ob 1 then enters f on the S head at Tempo dell'Allegro (D), together with Tpt 1 and the flutes. Ob 2 and Cl 2 already carry a "Cl. 1" cue at 67–68 for their entries. Ob 1, who plays the tune at 69, has none.

**Evidence.**
- Adler PDF 299, item 6: "Cues are often necessary to facilitate re-entry after a long period of rest. Both the instrument playing that particular cue and the place where the cue begins should be clearly indicated. The notes in the cued parts should be smaller."
- Ex. XVIII-7 (PDF 300) shows such a cue, labelled "Vn. II", in small notes over the rest.
- Adler PDF 299, item 4: "every player has detailed information about what is happening every moment".

**Fix (parts-gen.py, table P).**
```
("03-oboe-1", "Oboe 1", "oboe", r"\partClean \oboeOne", None, "", "", [CL+("UP",67,2)]),
```
The part now shows a 47–61 rest, a 62–65 rest, 66 rit., then the "Cl. 1" cue at 67–68 in small notes (B♭4–G4–D♭4 | C4), then the f entry. Ob 1 stays at 2 pages, and its turn is still in 147–155. The score is unchanged.

### 2. A multi-bar rest is split at 73 with nothing printed there (applied)

**Where.** The Cor anglais, Trumpet 3, Percussion and Harp parts print the 69–76 rest as two rests, "4 (69–72)" and "4 (73–76)". At 73 there is no rehearsal letter, tempo word, key change or barline type.

**Problem.** The cause is the score's spacing section at 73–76 in global.ily (`\newSpacingSection` and the SpacingSpanner override from c145). In the parts these cut the global's skips and split the rest. A player counting 4 + 4 bars cannot tell why the rest is split, and may suspect a missing mark.

**Evidence.**
- Adler PDF 299, Ex. XVIII-5 and XVIII-6: a rest is broken only where a rehearsal number ("9" then "7" across [70]) or a change of metre falls. Otherwise it stays one numbered rest.
- MOLA PDF 10, Ex. 3–4, which parts-lib.ily already cites for joining rests.

**Fix (parts-lib.ily).** partGlobalOf now drops the score-only spacing commands, as it already drops the `\mt` MIDI steps:
```
#(define (spacing-section? m)
   (or (eq? (ly:music-property m 'name) 'SpacingSectionEvent)
       (and (eq? (ly:music-property m 'name) 'EventChord)
            (let ((es (ly:music-property m 'elements)))
              (and (pair? es) (every (lambda (x) (eq? (ly:music-property x 'name) 'SpacingSectionEvent)) es))))
       (and (eq? (ly:music-property m 'name) 'ContextSpeccedMusic)
            (let ((e (ly:music-property m 'element)))
              (and (ly:music? e) (memq (ly:music-property e 'name) '(OverrideProperty RevertProperty))
                   (eq? (ly:music-property e 'symbol) 'SpacingSpanner))))))
#(define (part-global-drop? m) (or (mt-step? m) (spacing-section? m)))
partGlobalOf = #(define-music-function (m) (ly:music?)
  (merge-skips! (part-clean! (ly:music-deep-copy m) part-global-drop?)))
```
The four parts now print one rest, "8 (69–76)". The splits at E (77) and at the tempo words are unchanged.

**Side effect, handled.** The viola part plays through 73–76. With the spacing section gone, its spacing changed and the part grew to 3 pages, an odd count that breaks the c201 attacca plan. So the viola part's own spacing goes from 1.45 to 1.42 (LAYX in parts-gen.py, with a comment). The viola is back to 2 pages, and its one turn is still in the 103–106 rest at the top of page 2. The other parts keep their page counts.
