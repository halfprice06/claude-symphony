## Findings

### 1. Nine exposed entries after long rests have no cue (applied in the parts)
MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest, the cues being transposed to the reading key of the instrument. Cues must be audible and obvious." Ex. 4 (PDF 10) shows a "Viol. I" cue before the entry. I listed every entry after seven or more bars of rest from full.midi and compared the list with the cues already in parts-gen.py and the part-tagged cues in the section files. Tutti entries on a downbeat with the whole orchestra (26, 69, 158) need no cue. These exposed entries had none:

| Part | Entry | Rest before | Cue added (bars) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harp | 47, p arpeggios at C | 46 bars | Ob. 1, 45–46 |
| Vln I | 55 beat 4, p pickup alone | 12 bars | Vc., 53–54 |
| Hn 2 | 56, "2." espr. p tune | 11 bars | Vc., 54–55 |
| Bsn 2 | 60, "2." p | 17 bars | Vc., 58–59 |
| Timp. | 62, pp roll sub. p | 18 bars | Vc., 60–61 |
| Fl 1 | 64, "1." mf with the violins | 22 bars | Vln. I, 62–63 |
| Fl 2 | 68, p pickup into D | 25 bars | Vln. I, 66–67 |
| Ob 2, Cl 2 | 103, f in D minor | 19 bars | Vln. I, 101–102 |
| Hn 2 | 156, p poco animando | 13 bars | Vla., 154–155 |

Fix (parts-gen.py, table P): `[VI+("DOWN",62,2)]` for Fl 1; `VI+("DOWN",66,2)` for Fl 2; `VI+("DOWN",101,2)` for Ob 2 and Cl 2; `[VC+("UP",58,2)]` for Bsn 2; `[VC+("DOWN",54,2), VA+("DOWN",154,2)]` for Hn 2; `[VC+("UP",60,2)]` for timpani; `[VC+("DOWN",53,2)]` for Vln I; harp RH `("cueObOne" "Ob. 1" ,UP 45 2)`. The Hn 2 cue at 54 failed at first ("partCues: no rest for cue"): the part merges bars 45–55 into one rest that carries the `\!` ending the 44 diminuendo, and `insert-cue` refused any rest with articulations. In parts-lib.ily the cue may now go into the later bars of such a rest; the leading part keeps the articulations. Cues are transposed for the horn and clarinet by `instrumentTransposition`. No part changed its page count or page breaks (checked bar by bar for all 30 parts). Applied.

### 2. Multi-measure rests in the parts show the count but not the bars (applied in the parts)
MOLA PDF 5: "Numbering each measure should be avoided, except in the case of multi-measure rests, where measure number ranges are helpful (e.g., '27–117')." Ex. 3 (PDF 10) prints "9 – 11" under the 3-bar rest. Every part has long rests that cross tempo changes and rehearsal letters (harp 77–96, C.a. 81 bars before 91, brass 47–61); a range lets the player check his count against the conductor's bar numbers. Fix: a stencil callback `mmr-with-range` in parts-lib.ily, switched on in the parts' `\layout` (`\override MultiMeasureRest.stencil = #mmr-with-range`). It reads the first bar from the rest's left paper column (`rhythmic-location`), so the range always matches the score, and prints it small under the rest for rests of two bars or more. The harp's lower staff keeps the plain rest, so the range is not printed twice. Checked in Tbn 1 (1–11, 13–14, 15–25, 34–39 … 171–174), harp and Cb: correct numbers, no collisions, page counts and breaks unchanged. Applied.

### 3. Score: odd page numbers fall on left-hand pages (applied)
MOLA PDF 7: "Parts and scores should be produced double-sided … Right-hand pages must be odd-numbered in the upper right corner and left-hand pages must be even-numbered in the upper left corner." The front-matter page (c155) is the first right-hand page, but it is numbered 0, so music page 1, with its number at top right, prints on the back of it, a left-hand page; every later page has the same fault. Fix in score.ly: `first-page-number = 1` (was 0). The front page is page 1 with no printed number (LilyPond default), and the music runs on pages 2–18, odd numbers on the right. Nothing else moves: the same bars are on every page (checked page by page), still 17 pages of music and 18 PDF pages, and the K page break and `page-count = 17` are untouched. This answers c155's reason for page 0 (keeping music pages 1–17): no file or coherence record cites score page numbers. Applied.

### 4. Abbreviated staff names drop the instrument key (applied)
MOLA Ex. 2 (PDF 9) keeps the key in every abbreviated name of a transposing instrument: "Cl. 1, 2 (B♭)", "Bass Cl. (B♭)", "Hn. 1, 2 in F", "Hn. 3, 4 in F", "Tpt. 1, 2 in C", "Tpt. 3 in C". The full names on page 1 have the keys (c155), but from page 2 on the score shows only "Cl. 1·2", "B.cl.", "Hn. 1·2", "Tpt. 3". The conductor gives pitches to players who read in B♭ and F, so the key belongs on every page. Fix in score.ly: `shortInstrumentName = \markup \center-column { "Hn. 1·2" "in F" }` and the same for Hn. 3·4 (in F), Tpt. 1·2 and Tpt. 3 (in C), Cl. 1·2 and B.cl. ("(B♭)"). Two lines keep the margin narrow. Bars per page unchanged. Applied.

### 5. Parts: top margin 15 mm (applied)
MOLA PDF 7: "Leave a 20 mm (0.75-inch) margin surrounding the image area of the music." parts-gen.py has left, right and bottom margins of 20 mm and a top margin of 15 mm. Fix: `top-margin = 20\mm`. Page counts are unchanged in all 30 parts. Two parts reflow: Fl 1 now turns after bar 102 into the 6-bar rest 103–108 (about 11 s at crotchet 126; it was the 4-bar rest 127–130), and Fl 2 and Hn 2 shift one line break on their single page. Every part that crosses into movement II (Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc, Cb, Hn 3, Hn 4, timpani) still ends on an even page with the same breaks. Applied.

### 6. String parts at 7.2 mm rather than 7.5 mm (not applied)
MOLA PDF 7: 7.5 mm is "the most readable staff size for all instruments"; 7.0 mm "may be readable for winds … it is less so for strings because they share a stand." All parts are at 7.2 mm. I tested 7.5 mm for the five string parts (`#(layout-set-staff-size 21.34)` in their `\layout`). Vln I, Vln II and Vc stay on two pages, but the viola needs three: page-turn breaking puts only the 5-bar opening rest on page 1 and ends the music on page 3, a right-hand page. That breaks the c201 page plan for the attacca (the viola's E4 is tied into movement II, so its last page must be a left-hand page facing movement II's first page). A cover page for the viola would give four pages with a turn inside its few short rests. 7.2 mm is inside MOLA's acceptable band (not under 7.0 mm), so I recommend it stays as it is. Mixing 7.5 mm violins with a 7.2 mm viola would serve no purpose either.
