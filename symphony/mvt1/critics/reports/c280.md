### 2. Two of the changes link movement I more closely to movement II; add them to the seeds list (records)

Sebesky's rule of formal balance is to grow the whole from material already heard (PDF 11), and Ex. 2A shows a melody passed from one instrument to another over the same background (PDF 18). Two of the recent changes now foreshadow movement II.

(a) Bars 45–47. Since v275, Cl 1 ends the link on E♭4 (beat 3 of 46), and Hn 1 takes E♭3 on beat 4 as the pickup to the A♭-major S head. Movement II enters its theme A the same way. In II 3–6 the winds play alone, Cl 2 holds E♭3 to beat 2 of II 6, and Hn 1 enters on beat 3 with E♭3–F3 into A♭ major (mvt2/latest intro.ily: `introClTwo … es2 r4`, `introHnOne … es8( f8`). Both A♭ melodies are therefore handed from a clarinet's E♭ to the first horn's E♭3. Movement I's handoff is an octave apart and movement II's is at the unison. This answers c274's choice of the clarinet with a reason that comes from the symphony as a whole.

(b) Bars 59–62. Since v274 the bass descends by whole bars G♭2 (60) – F♭2 (61) – E♭2 (62, cadential 6/4). The harp doubles it, with G♭1 at 60. Bar 61 is iv6 of A♭ with F♭ in the bass (harp A♭–D♭–F♭; cellos F♭–D♭–F♭–A♭). Movement II's climax has the same bass, G♭1–F♭1 at its 62 (Cbsn `ges,,2 fes,,2`, Cb the same), and reaches E♭2 at 65 after the A-minor quotation. Its iv with F♭ comes at II 5 (over A♭) and II 88 (root position). mvt2.md says the F♭→E♭ semitone is "why the F♭/E–E♭ thread is protected". Movement I already had F♭→E♭ at 61→62. The whole-bar G♭ now gives II's full bass line G♭–F♭–E♭, at p<, in the same key, a movement early.

Fix (records, not applied; I may not write under /mnt/project-files). Add to "Seeds of later movements" in coherence/mvt1.md:
- "Bars 45–47: Cl 1 ends the link into S on E♭4 (beat 3 of 46) and Hn 1 takes E♭3 on beat 4 as the pickup to the A♭ S head (since v275). Movement II enters theme A the same way: Cl 2 E♭3 to beat 2 of II 6, Hn 1 E♭3–F3 on beat 3."
- "Bars 60–62: the bass falls G♭–F♭–E♭ by whole bars (Cb G♭2–F♭2–E♭2, harp G♭1 at 60; since v274), with iv6 of A♭ over F♭ at 61, into the cadential 6/4 at 62. This is the bass of movement II's climax (G♭1–F♭1 at II 62, E♭2 at II 65) and its F♭→E♭ thread (II 5, 21, 78, 88)."

### 4. The cycle-67 protection of the violas' E♭–E♮–F at 56–57 is answered, so c277 stands (no change)

HISTORY (cycle 67) protects "viola E♭–E♮–F 56–57". c277 made the violas rest at 56–59 and did not mention that protection. For this lens the question is whether the E♭/E♮ thread loses an appearance. It does not. The same pitches E♭4 (56, beats 1–2), E♮4–F4–G4 (56, beat 4), F4 (57) are in Cl 1 and Hn 2's counterline, and they are its first notes (tTwoClOne, tTwoHnTwo, bar 56). The cello arpeggio has E4 at the top of 56. The thread record in mvt1.md names only the violas at 12, 166 and 179–182 and Vln II at 8–9. The finale and movement II answer those bars, not 56. Sebesky's Ex. 1 (PDF 12–13) makes the case for the cut: the line was a doubling in the lower-middle register, and taking it out leaves the moving parts clear.

Fix: none in the score. HISTORY should record the cycle-67 item as closed: "c067 viola E♭–E♮–F 56–57: the line is kept at pitch in Cl 1 + Hn 2; violas rest 56–59 (c277)."

### 6. Movement I's peaks leave room for the later movements (keep)

Sebesky warns against using everything in the first chorus, because it "leaves no room for development or surprise" (PDF 11). He asks whether a piece "build[s] logically, creating a satisfying whole" (PDF 240). Across the four movements the violins' highest notes now rise in steps: A♭6 in I (four times), B♭6 in II (76, 79), B6 in III (once, 336), C7 in IV (once, 223). The loudest marks go the same way: I has fff only at 122 and 160, II has no fff, III has one fff with no brass at fff, and IV has fff at 223, 263 and 278. Movement I's percussion is one suspended-cymbal stroke and the tam-tam twice, and the finale's crash and bass drum are new. The damping at 164 and 182 makes the movement I colours shorter and does not add to them. Keep movement I's A♭6 ceiling and the two fff marks. Add to the seeds paragraph: "Violin peaks rise by movement: A♭6 (I) – B♭6 (II) – B6 (III) – C7 (IV); keep A♭6 as movement I's ceiling."
