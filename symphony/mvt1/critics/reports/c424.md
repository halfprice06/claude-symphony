## Findings

### 1. Bars 117–121: the held upper line is in the second flute alone, in its weak middle register, under the ff brass motto, and it crosses the first flute (applied)

What is wrong. At 117–121 the brass play the second form of the motto ff (Hn 1·2 + Tbn 1·2 + Bsn 1 on G3–E♭4–D4–D♭4–C4, B.tbn an octave lower), Hn 3·4 hold the G3/G4 pedal, Cl 2 and the violas play triplets around B3–F4, and the first-theme head is thrown in detached attacks by Fl 1 + Vln I (upper octave) and Ob 1 + Cl 1 + Vln II (lower octave). Between the head's attacks, on beats 1 and 3, the only held note above G4 is Fl 2: F5 (117), G5 (118), F5 (119), F5 (120), G5 (121). In the dump at 117.1, 118.1, 119.1, 119.3, 120.1 and 120.3, F5 or G5 sounds in Fl 2 and nowhere else. That line carries the seventh of V (F) and the fifth of the 6/4 (G). Fl 1 starts each half-bar below it (B4, E♭5, D5, F5 against F5 and G5), so the pair is crossed for four bars; c345 already found that the crossing cannot be engraved cleanly (Gould PDF 67) and left it open.

Evidence. PDF 51: in forte "Horn = 2 Clarinets = 2 Oboes = 2 Flutes", and a trombone equals two horns, so the held line weighs ½ horn against about 6½ in the motto. PDF 90, rule 3, gives this very case as "to be avoided": "The second flute will sound too weak". PDF 91 (b): "one tone quality should be devoted to the stationary and another to the moving parts"; here the flutes are both. PDF 90 rule 2: normal order of register unless crossing is deliberate. PDF 84: upper and inner pedals are effective in the orchestra, but only if the pedal can be heard. Meanwhile Ob 2 rests from 117 to 121.

Fix. The held line goes to Ob 1, in a colour that carries at F5–G5 and differs from the moving flutes. Ob 2 takes the head's lower octave that Ob 1 played, and Fl 2 joins Fl 1 on the head (a2, as at 115–116). Ob 1 breathes on 119.4 and 120.4, where Ob 2's head has the same F5, and rejoins Ob 2 for the climb E♭5–F5–G5 at 121 (a2) into its A♭5 at 122, as before. Both pairs are now in normal order. Dynamics are ff in both oboes, as the shared staff requires. The brass motto, the head, the triplets, the pedal and every level are unchanged. This also closes c345's open item "Fl 117–120 split notation".

Only the lowest octave shares its register. Correcting the count does not change c404's conclusion.
