## Findings

### 1. Bars 15–22: the bass is marked at the tune's level, then drops where the phrase grows (applied)
**What is wrong.** At the Allegro the P theme is in Vln I alone at f. Vln II and violas are mf. The cellos (half notes) and the double basses (the quaver pulse) are f, the same level as the tune. At 19 Vc and Cb drop to mf, exactly where the continuation starts its crescendo to the sf D♭6 at 20 and the violas' espr. counterline swells mf<f. The marked shape of the bass is therefore f for the four-bar basic idea and mf for its intensification, the opposite of the phrase. c038 set Vc/Cb mf at 19 so the viola counterline could be heard, but left 15–18 at f; c184 checked only that the melody is on top.

**Evidence.** Sebesky's focus chart (PDF 13–14) puts the soloist on the primary level and the rhythm section (bass) on the tertiary level, and warns that the ear must not be "diverted or confused" by the background. In Ex. 2A (PDF 18) the solo is marked one level above the accompanying strings. Ex. 1 (PDF 12) gives all background parts one level. In v278 Vln II and violas already follow this (mf); only the bass does not.

**Fix (tOne.ily).** `tOneVc`: `c2\mf d2 | …` at 15 (was `\f`), and the `\mf` at 19 removed (now redundant). `tOneCb`: `c,4\mf r8 c,8 …` at 15 (was `\f`), the `\mf` at 19 removed. Bsn 1 still enters mf at 19, so the bass gains weight at 19 instead of losing it. The crescendo from 23 to ff at 26 is unchanged. Applied.

### 2. Bar 177: the last statement of the motto has no expressive word (applied)
**What is wrong.** From 175 every part is pp: the trombone chorale (marked "dolce"), Cl 1, Vln II, violas, Vc, Cb and the tam-tam. Vln I enters at 177 with the last motto, the line the movement ends on and that movement II continues sul D. It carries "unis." and "sul D" but no character word, so on the page it is one more pp part. c258 kept the pp level, and the coda's dynamics are protected (c231), so the level should stay.

**Evidence.** Sebesky Ex. 2A (PDF 18) marks each solo entry with a dynamic and a word ("SWEETLY", "PRETTY", "EASY"). The focus principle (PDF 13–14) asks the arranger to direct the ear to the primary element; here the only means that leaves the level alone is the word. Every other solo in this movement already has one: C.a. 5 and 171 "espr.", solo Vc 171 "espr.", Tbn 175 "dolce".

**Fix (coda.ily).** A combined dynamic `ppEspr = #(make-dynamic-script (markup #:line (#:dynamic "pp" #:hspace 0.5 #:normal-text #:italic "espr.")))`, and `g'4(\ppEspr^"unis."\startTextSpan` at 177 (was `\pp`). It prints "pp espr." on one line under the G4. No notes, levels or timing change. Applied.

### 3. Bar 103: bass clarinet f on the same D2 as Bsn 2 at mf, above the S head (applied)
**What is wrong.** At 103–106 the P head is in Ob 2 and Cl a2 at f; the S head is in Hn 1, Bsn 1 and Vc at mf. The bass D2–G2–A2–B♭1… is held by Bsn 2 at mf (c168: "mf with Bsn 1") and by the bass clarinet at f, in unison, with Cb pizz. f. The sustained bass clarinet in its strong low register is marked above the S head it supports, and above the bassoon it doubles.

**Evidence.** Sebesky Ex. 1 (PDF 12): parts that sound together as one background carry one dynamic. Focus (PDF 13–14): the bass is tertiary. The pizzicato basses can stay f: a pizzicato note decays at once (c259 counts the pizz. bass as what keeps the wind tutti clear).

**Fix (dev.ily).** `devBassCl` at 103: `d,1\mf` (was `\f`). The diminuendo at 106 is unchanged. Applied.

### 4. Bar 142: Ob 2 doubles the accented C5–B4 without the accents (applied)
**What is wrong.** At 142 Fl 1, Ob 1 and Vln I·II play `c2-> b2->` (C–B, the leading tone into 143). Ob 2 plays the same C5–B4, in unison with Ob 1, as `c''2 b'2` with no accents. The score staff shows the accents from Ob 1, but the Ob 2 part does not.

**Evidence.** Sebesky Ex. 281 (PDF 214–215): every part of a voiced section carries the same accents and hairpins. My scripted check of all doublings of four or more notes found no other mismatch of this kind; 72 (Vc/Cb hold pp under the winds' accents) is intended.

**Fix (recOne.ily).** `recOneOboeTwo`, 142: `c''2-> b'2->`. Applied.
