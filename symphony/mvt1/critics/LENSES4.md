# Critic lenses, series 4 (cycles 421-620; cycle N uses lens ((N-1) mod 10) + 1)
1. Melody and memorability: the motto, the first theme P, the second theme S, the horn call, their contour, rhythm, climaxes and development. Would a listener hum them afterwards? Compare with the great tunes of Tchaikovsky, Dvořák, Brahms, Rachmaninoff and Elgar. Themes are not locked: a better version of a theme is welcome, but report every change to a theme that a later movement quotes (see coherence/finale.md and coherence/mvt2.md).
2. Harmony and voice leading: progressions, pedals, inner-voice motion, dissonance treatment, chromatic and Neapolitan colour, the modulation plan.
3. Form, pacing and proportion: section lengths, the tension curve, transitions, the logic of the development, recapitulation changes, the coda. Compare with corpus first movements.
4. Orchestration: balance, doubling, register, colour, use of each section, the three A♭ climaxes. Sources: Rimsky-Korsakov and Adler.
5. Engraving and notation (Gould's *Behind Bars*, MOLA): dynamics, hairpins, slurs, beaming, ottavas, part-combining labels, rehearsal and tempo marks, system and page layout, and the instrumental parts. Inspect many pages of the PDF.
6. Playability, instrument by instrument: ranges, breathing, bowing, harp pedalling, timpani tuning and retuning, brass endurance, divisi, page turns in the parts.
7. Texture and counterpoint: inner-voice interest, accompaniment figures, counter-melodies, the fugato, imitation, the bass line.
8. Expression: dynamics, articulation, phrasing slurs, tempo changes, rubato indications and expressive text.
9. Holistic comparison with one specific master first movement, the one named in your task (see MASTERS.md). Would a conductor believe a master wrote this? Also compare with the version 10 cycles earlier and flag anything that got worse.
10. Cyclic design and emotional arc across four movements: movement I (C minor) is followed by II Adagio (A♭), III Scherzo (E) and IV Finale (C minor to C major). Read /home/user/claude_composes/symphony/coherence/*.md and themes-*.ly. Judge how movement I's introduction, motto, themes, climaxes and coda set up the later movements, whether it foreshadows material the later movements develop (the scherzo's and finale's themes may be hinted at here), and whether it keeps back what the finale needs (the "Do not reuse" list in coherence/mvt1.md). Movement I may change freely; changes that touch something another movement quotes or answers must be listed in your report under "Cross-movement effects".
