## Assessment of the new theme
I cannot hear it, so this is inferred from the score. The new antecedent has a clear contour: a held note, a leap, a stepwise descent, then a half cadence on a whole note. That is the shape of the long Romantic tunes in the corpus (Dvořák 9/ii, Tchaikovsky 6/i second theme). The risk is that its bar-3 figure, a dotted quarter then quarters and eighths, is generic. Later loops should check whether bar 3 needs a more characteristic rhythm, perhaps a triplet.
