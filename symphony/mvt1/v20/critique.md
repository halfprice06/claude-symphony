# Critique of v20 (final in this round)

Method: every page rendered at 60 dpi and read, build log, `analysis.txt`. 180 bars, about 7:16.

## Changes from v19
- Engraving: the repeated-eighth passages written as `\repeat unfold` (violas at 69, 131 and 163; cellos and basses at 132 and 163) printed a dynamic on every note. The dynamic is now on the first note only.
- No musical changes. This version is the v19 music with clean engraving.

## Check results
No build warnings. 0 exposed parallels, apart from the analyser's known false positive at bars 96–97.

## Where the movement stands
Form (bar numbers):
- Introduction 1–14 (Lento): motto in bass clarinet, cor anglais, violins; German sixth.
- First theme 15–32: peaks on a held d♭''' (Neapolitan) and on a♭''' in the tutti.
- Transition 33–46.
- Second theme 47–68 (A♭).
- Codetta 69–72 (forte) and closing 73–76.
- Development 77–122.
- Recapitulation 123–162: the first theme returns with its peak, and the second theme comes back in C major.
- Coda 163–180: forte C minor, then the two themes combined, the motto in augmentation, and an ending on C minor with E♭ turning to E♮ in the violas.

Weaknesses I know of and have not fixed:
1. The off-beat viola chords in the second theme still run for long stretches.
2. The A' climax keeps one chromatic descent.
3. The G pedal at 109–122 is 14 bars long.
4. Brass mostly sustain chords.
5. The first theme's rhythmic cell is used throughout the movement. This is intended, but it is the main risk of monotony.
6. All of this was judged from the score. I have not heard the rendering, and a human listener will find balance and pacing problems that reading cannot show.
