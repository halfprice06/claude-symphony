# v80 — cycle 60 (critic 60, lens: cyclic design — the D♭ question)

Report: critics/reports/c060.md

Decision: the D♭ does not become a key in the recapitulation. Reasons: the Eroica's excursion (402–416) happens in a soft, still-arriving recapitulation, while this one arrives ff at 123 and is half-length; Tchaikovsky 5/i's F♯ minor is a transposition plan, not a problem-note return; shortened recapitulations in the corpus (Beethoven 9/i, Dvořák 9/i) do not detour; added bars would undo the protections of 123–134 and weaken the A♭ climax at 156 (it would sound as D♭'s dominant). The D♭ is left as a question for the finale, as in Tchaikovsky 5.
