## What I considered and did not do
The reviewer suggested a canon in the low strings one bar behind the theme in the coda. I wrote it out and found the theme's bar 2 against its own bar 1 gives parallel fifths (D♭–A♭ to C–G). A canon at half a bar would avoid that, but the opening is only four bars and the pedal already gives it weight, so I left it out.
