# Manual reading plan for critic cycles 36-41

Dan (2026-11-06): use the library manual in depth. Page numbers are PDF pages. Where the 2021 text and the 2025 manuals disagree, the 2025 manuals and the installed patch files win; `guides/eastwest-hollywood-orchestra/DIGEST.md` section 11 lists the known conflicts, and `loop/MANUAL-GAPS.md` lists what the manual offers that the mockup does not use.
