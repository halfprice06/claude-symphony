- **Introduction, bars 5–8:** the cor anglais now answers the bass clarinet's opening motto (d'–b♭'–a'–a♭'–g'). The clarinet is saved for bar 11. The first three entries are now three different colours: bass clarinet with low strings, then cor anglais, then first violins.

## Problems I see now
1. The oboe's descent (b♭'–a♭'–g'–f'–e♭') is a plain scale. It is short, and it hands over to the theme, but it is the least distinctive line on the page.
2. The I6 in bar 45 gives away the tonic two bars before the theme. Masters do this (Brahms often lands on I6 before a theme), but it weakens the arrival a little.
3. D4's triplets (bars 109–113) are still uniform.
