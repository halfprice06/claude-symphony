# Critique of v18

Method: source, build log, `analysis.txt`.

## Changes from v17 (review points 7 and 8)
- **Fugato (bars 85–87).** The contrabasses no longer double the cellos' subject at the same pitch around D2, which would have been muddy. The cellos play the subject alone an octave higher (D3–B♭3), and the basses play pizzicato on the main beats (D, G, E♭, G, B♭), then return arco for the countersubject in bar 88.
- **Second theme in D3 (bars 97–102).** Five instruments had the tune in unison or octaves. The second violins now play it an octave below the firsts, and the second flute is silent. The tune is now in three octaves (flute 1; first violins and oboe; second violins), each with a clear role.
- **Timpani tunings.** The part needed six pitches in the development, including F to B♭ between consecutive bars. Removed the F roll at bar 96, the D roll at bar 108, the F stroke at bar 31 and the D stroke in the recapitulation (now G). The whole movement now uses four drums: G, A♭, B♭/C (one pedal drum, retuned during rests of at least seven bars) and E♭.

## Not changed, with reasons
- **Brass pads in bars 27–32 and 123–126.** The reviewer wanted the motto rhythm in the trumpets. Under a melody that already uses that rhythm in the violins and woodwinds, the trumpets would double it and make the texture heavier. Sustained brass under a string tune is normal in Tchaikovsky's tuttis, so I left it.
- **Horn mp and cellos mf on the same line at bar 47.** These differ on purpose: a horn in that register sounds louder than a cello at the same marking. Composers routinely mark doublings differently for balance.

## Check results
No build warnings. The analyser reports one octave at bars 96–97 (F5→B♭2 over F2→B♭1). The "top voice" at bar 97 is the bass itself, because everything else rests on that downbeat. It is the same line in two registers, not a parallel between independent voices.
