# Cycle 12, critic B: balance and levels (render under review: c11)

Method. Levels are K-weighted mean-square levels in dB, both channels summed, from an STFT (Hann 4096, hop 480) of the dry stems and the master in `cycles/c11/render/`, placed on the score with the tempo map of `build/parts.json`. A "note level" is the mean over the first 0.25 s of the note (the whole note if it is shorter). Comparisons are the c08 stems (the last earlier cycle that kept stems) and the c10 master. "Measured" marks a number taken from the audio or the build data. "Inferred" marks a judgement about cause or audibility.

Fix. `build/mix.json`, per track, after ONE_TIME_c12 item 11's width make-up (trumpets +2.2 to +2.8 dB, trombones +1.0 to +1.9 dB): add +2.0 dB to `gain_db` of "Trumpet 1" and "Trumpet 2" and +3.0 dB to `gain_db` of "Trombone 1", "Trombone 2" and "Bass trombone".
