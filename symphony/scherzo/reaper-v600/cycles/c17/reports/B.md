# Cycle 17, critic B: balance and levels (on the c16 render)

Positions are score quarter notes (qn); bar b starts at qn 3(b-1). Levels are mean-square dB of the stem (mean of the channels' powers) unless "A-weighted" is stated. "Note level" is the window from 30 ms to 300 ms after the score onset. The envelope is the one `tools/build_reaper.py vol_points` writes from `build/parts.json` and `build/mix.json` (source cut before `ENV_LUA`, guard on; "without the guard" means `ENV_NO_GUARD=1`). Every number was measured on `cycles/c16/render/` against `cycles/c15/render/` and `cycles/c14/render/` unless marked inferred. No proposal below uses the track volume envelope for a note, a figure or a bar.
