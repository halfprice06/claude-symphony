# Cycle 03, critic C: tempo, timing and transitions (Lens C3), on the c02 render

Material: `cycles/c02/render/` stems and master, `cycles/c02/build_render/parts.json` (tempo map and moved onsets), `build/mockup-events.tsv`, `tools/score_to_parts.py`, `build/score_config.py`. Perceived attack ("pc") is `tools/pcx.py attack_pcx` (mean of the half-amplitude and body - 10 dB crossings on the note's harmonic band, previous pitch excluded), in ms from the score onset; for pizzicato and harp I also used the broadband and 1-6 kHz envelopes (time to peak - 6 dB). Same-pitch repeats cannot be read by the band method and are left out (celli and basses in the hemiolas). Times convert on the render's own tempo map.

## Findings, ranked by what a listener would gain

### 1. The long note after a slurred eighth arrives 0.1-0.26 s late in the strings: the second idea's hemiola and the trio tune limp at every pickup (measured)

Where: the second idea, bars 45-49 (qn 134, 144, 146), 121-125 (362, 372, 374), 277-281 (842); trio 169-184 celli (512, 524) and 197-200 (Violins I 584, 591, 597; Violas 579, 585, 596); trio return 209-224 (Violins I and II 632, 644, 656); coda 1061.5. Tracks Violins I, Violins II, Violas, Violoncellos.

What: the pattern is dotted quarter, slurred eighth, long note (the hemiola's tied half, or the trio tune's tied downbeat), all under one slur. The eighth is played on Sus MAX (the `short_sus` rule: a slurred string note under 0.25 s on the tempo map; at quarter 264 every slurred eighth, 0.114 s, and even a slurred quarter, 0.227 s, qualifies). The long note after it then sits on Leg Slur MAX with no legato partner on its own row, so it plays a Sus-to-Leg transition (Movement I c05 measured the same thing at +0.205 s, comment at `STR_SHORT_AFTER_SUS` in score_to_parts.py). Its lead is 0 or at most 0.054 s (the 0.45-of-the-eighth cap).

Evidence (measured): of 45 such string notes, 31 arrive more than 0.1 s late; medians Violins I +176 ms (n 14), Violins II +171 (13), Violas +124 (7), celli +92 (11). At qn 134 (bar 45 beat 3, the syncopated half of the hemiola) Violins I +216, Violins II +169, while Flute 1 +16, Oboe 1 +11, Clarinet 1 +24 play the same rhythm: a 0.15-0.2 s flam, more than an eighth at quarter 264. At 632 and 644 (bar 211 and 215, ff espress.) Violins I +175/+151, Violins II +212/+208 against Clarinet 1 +26/+26 doubling the tune. Celli 512 and 524 (trio tune, bars 171 and 175) +216/+220. Examples from parts.json: Violins I 133.5 Sus MAX ends 133.99, 134 Leg Slur MAX starts 134.000 (v98); Violins II 631.5 Sus MAX, 632 Leg Slur MAX at 632.000.

Fix: keep the eighth on the legato row when the next note of its slur is long, so the pair is an ordinary legato join with legato leads. `short_into_long()` in score_to_parts.py (around line 2023) already does this for a slur's first note (`SHORT_SUS_TO_LONG = 1.0`); add a generic switch, e.g. `SHORT_SUS_TO_LONG_ANY = False` by default, that skips its "first note of the slur" test, and set it True in score_config.py. If the long note then still lands late because `LEG_CAP_FRAC` 0.75 caps its lead at 0.085 s (264) / 0.10 s (228), move eighth and target together with `GROUP_LEAD_AT` (e.g. Violins I `(133.5, 134.0, 0.10)`), which exists for exactly this ("a fast run into a legato target"). Fallback if the legato eighth smears: make the long note a fresh Sus MAX bow (`DOWNBOW_STROKE_AT` plus an `ABUT_LEAD` entry, see finding 2); abutting Sus MAX strokes measured +23..+50 ms here at lead 0. Measure the listed onsets with pcx on the next render; target within 0.04 s of the winds.

### 2. The f hemiola strokes ("ritmo di quattro battute", bars 101-104 and 301-304) and the summit's accented halves (bars 336-338) trail the winds by about 0.1 s (measured)

### 4. The G.P. (bar 340) is filled by a ringing timpani chord: the printed staccato stroke is played long (measured)
