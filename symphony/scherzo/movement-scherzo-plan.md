# Scherzo plan (movement III of four): Allegro vivace in E minor, trio in E major

Written 2026-10-12 for draft v01, after Dan's decision to make the symphony a four-movement work (I C minor – II A♭ – III Scherzo E – IV Finale C minor → C major). Sources: cyclic-notes.md, sections "Four movements (Dan, 2026-10-12): plan for the scherzo" and "Start here for movement III" (that section now means the finale), mvt1/v221, mvt2 (latest), mvt3/v26 (the finale, paused).

Models: Dvořák 9/iii (Molto vivace, E minor, with a gentler trio), Beethoven 9/ii (scherzo with motivic timpani), Bruckner and Brahms scherzi (hemiola), Eroica iii and Dvořák 8/iii (soft trio).

## Key decision
E minor for the scherzo, E major for the trio and the coda. Reasons: the symphony stays in minor until the finale's C-major arrival; the trio and the coda bring E major, so the movement begins and ends on G♯ (= A♭, movement II's tonic and the pivot into the finale); no C major anywhere (C major belongs to the finale). The F-major episode (♭II, Neapolitan) answers movement I's D♭ (♭II of C).

## Cyclic rules for the scherzo (cyclic-notes "Four movements"; do not undo without answering the reason)
- No C-major key and no C-major triad anywhere (C major belongs to the finale's arrival). A minor (iv) and D7/A7 etc. are fine.
- Trumpets only in tutti rhythm or fanfare figures (motive x): no trumpet melody, so the finale's soft undoubled trumpet solo keeps its effect.
- Not in the scherzo: string tremolo, the violins' C7, the trumpets' B→C, the stop chord B–D–F–A♭ resolving to C, the falling major motto in full brass, the brass chorale, the violins' C-major second theme, the combined heads, the hammered tutti in major, a tuba melody, a high horn unison, a full low-brass unison bass, cymbals (the finale's first crash is still its arrival).
- Violin peak B6 at most (352), so the finale's C7 is the symphony's top.
- Hn 3·4 "senza sord." at their first entry (they end movement II muted). The timpani's first label gives the retuning made in the pause (E, G, B, E).
- The movement begins and ends on G♯ in the horn (movement II's A♭; the finale can take it back as A♭). The E♭/E♮ thread is not closed here: the finale's violas still close it (D♯→E); the scherzo spells D♯ as E's leading tone throughout.
- Quote the motto, do not develop it (development belongs to I and IV): the chromatic tail (14–15 and every theme statement; 373–376), the horn call (4–7).

## Orchestra
As the finale without percussion: 2 flutes, 2 oboes, cor anglais, 2 clarinets, bass clarinet, 2 bassoons, contrabassoon, 4 horns, 3 trumpets (Tpt 3 tacet in v01), 2 trombones, bass trombone, tuba, timpani, harp, strings. No cymbals or bass drum.
