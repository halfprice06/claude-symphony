## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Shared staves give no player label at four solo entries after rests (applied)
- **Bars:** Ob 125, Ob 197, Bsn 141, Bsn 193.
- **Problem:** At each of these bars one player enters alone after 4–48 bars in which the staff was empty, and the staff shows one line with no "1.". The part-combiner prints its solo label only when the state changes, and it carries the solo state through the rests. At the same bars the flutes print "1." (125, 197). The reprise twin of 125 (Ob, 281) does print "1.", and Bsn gets its automatic "1." only at 201, eight bars after it entered. A conductor cannot tell from the staff whether one or two players are playing.
- **Evidence:** MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11): shared staves name the players. Adler PDF 294: an instrument "is labeled every time it enters". The composer already does this by hand at Fl 197 (`^\markup \bold "1."`), and parts-lib's combine-label? removes such labels from the parts.
- **Fix:** sC.ily `c''2->(\p\<^\markup \bold "1." a'4~` (125); tB.ily tBOboeOne `e''4(\f^\markup \bold "1." fis''8` (197); link.ily `b,2.\pp^\markup \bold "1."` (141); tB.ily tBBsnOne `\partCombineSoloI a2.\p\<^\markup \bold "1."` (193). Checked at 150 dpi; the parts are unchanged.

### 2. Bar 271: Vln II and violas start a crescendo with no dynamic after two bars' rest (applied)
- **Problem:** At 271 Vln I, cellos and basses print p under the crescendo to f at 273. Vln II and violas, back from rests at 269–270, print only the hairpin. They are still at their pp from 253, but in the score and in their parts the starting level is not given. The parts audit lists both parts ("hairpin only").
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 8: proofread the vertical lines. MOLA Ex. 3–4: a dynamic at each entry after rests.
- **Fix:** dA.ily `r4 g'4\pp\< g'4` (Vln II) and `r4 b4-.\pp\< b4-.` (Vla). I decided on pp rather than p: it keeps the accompaniment one level under Vln I (c099), and the MIDI stays byte-identical.

### 3. Four cues begin with two empty bars (applied, parts)
- **Problem:** The Fl 2, Ob 1 and Ob 2 parts cue "Vln. I" from 269 for four bars, but Vln I rests at 269–270, so the first two cue bars are labelled rests (Ob 2 part, line 241; Ob 1 part, line 237). What sounds there is the Bsn 1 scherzando call. The bass clarinet cues "Vc." from 135, where the cellos rest at 134–136 and the timpani play alone (c077). These cue bars are neither audible nor obvious.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5: "Cues must be audible and obvious to the musician reading the part"; Ex. 4 cues only sounding notes.
- **Fix (parts-cues.json):** "02", "03", "04": `["cueVlnOne","Vln. I","UP",271,2]` (Vln I's tail, on the stave, just before the a2 entry at 273). "08": `["cueVc","Vc.","UP",137,2]` (the cellos' last motive x before the pp entry at 139). A Bn. 1 cue at 269 would lie three to five ledger lines below the treble stave, so I did not use it.

### 4. Horns 1 and 2 cue the cellos four ledger lines below the stave (applied, parts)
- **Bars:** Hn 1 and Hn 2 parts, 193–196.
- **Problem:** The cue before the horns' mf entry at 197 is the cello bass, which in horn transposition sits on the fourth ledger line below the treble stave (the audit's only horn hits, 193–194). In the same bars Vln II plays the tune p< from F♯4 to C♯5, which is written C♯5–G♯5 for horn in F, inside the stave.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5 (audible, obvious, transposed); Adler PDF 300 item 7: choose the cued line "depending on where they lie in the register"; Adler PDF 299 item 6.
- **Fix:** "12", "13": `["cueVlnTwo","Vln. II","UP",193,4]` (the quote cueVlnTwo already exists in parts.ly). Checked in the Horn 1 part.

### 5. The harp part still prints the opening pedal setting (applied, parts)
- **Problem:** The pipeline removes every harp pedal diagram from the part (73, 141, 188, 191, 235, 333) under MOLA PDF 5, but "set pedals in the pause: D C B♭ / E F G A" at bar 1 survives. It is a `\small \line` markup, which pedal-letter-markup? does not recognise.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5: "Harp pedaling should be left to the performer and not printed."
- **Fix (parts-lib.ily):** a new predicate pedal-setting-markup? (a \small \line whose italic item begins "set pedals"), ORed into partCleanHarp. The score keeps the setting (c116).

### 6. Parts have a 15 mm top margin (applied, parts)
- **Problem:** parts-gen.py sets `top-margin = 15\mm`; the left, right and bottom margins are 20 mm.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 7: "Leave a 20 mm (0.75-inch) margin surrounding the image area of the music."
- **Fix:** `top-margin = 20\mm`. Every part keeps its page count. One page turn moves: Oboe 1 now turns at 288/289, into the 8-bar rest at S, instead of 196/197 (also an 8-bar rest). Page 1 holds 12 systems, the MOLA maximum, and is not crowded.

- Vln I and Fl 1 parts use 12 systems per page: allowed "as long as symbols are not crowded", and they are not.
