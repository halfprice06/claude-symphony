## Findings

The reading gives one verdict throughout: the key plan, the pivots, the confirming cadences and the returns of V already do what Unit 26 teaches (rows 1–18). I looked for a bar-level breach and found none. The cases I tested and rejected:

- G major has no IV in its cadence (51–55). This is forced by the C-major rule; applied V of V is the "form of II" that row 3 allows.
- The coda's IV area comes before I (341–348). Row 12 calls this safe late in a piece, and 353–356 confirms E with ii, IV and V.
- The reprise has no leading tone in the bass before 253. Row 10 asks for it when the V is minor; here the V is major and D♯ sounds in Vln I at 250 and 252.

So I make no musical change. The one finding is a cyclic mismatch in the shared records.

### 1. The scherzo's shared state file names v114, and the finale's branch still carries the v87 copies (coherence/scherzo.md; no bars)

Problem. symphony/coherence/scherzo.md line 4 reads "Current version: symphony/scherzo/v114". The current version is v120, and the file's facts are true of v120 (I checked each one against the v120 sections). The finale thread reads the coherence folder from its own branch. There, coherence/scherzo.md still says "Current version: symphony/scherzo/v87" (dated 2026-10-13), and themes-scherzo.ly/.md are also the v87 copies. Finale.md (v141) lists this as still open: "the copies of scherzo.md, themes-scherzo.ly and themes-scherzo.md in symphony/coherence/ are still from v87 ... please put the v114 ones there."

Evidence. The brief's coherence rule says changes to the ending, themes, keys or tunings "must be named so the composer can notify the other threads". The rule only works if the other thread can read the current file. Nothing the finale quotes has changed between v87 and v120 (record 41/41), so no notes are wrong today. But the finale checks its quotations against a file 33 versions old, and the next theme change would reach it only by message. A&S PDF 448 (row 9) holds a tonal plan to account for every key area; the joins C – A♭ – E – C (row 13) can be checked only if each movement's current ending and opening are on record where the other movements look.
