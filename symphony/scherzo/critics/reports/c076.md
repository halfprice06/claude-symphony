## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Horn dynamics one degree above the trumpets and trombones (101–105, 301–305) — applied
**Problem.** At 101 and 301 all the brass enter pp<, horns included. At 105 and 305 all the brass are f, horns included, while the woodwind, strings and cor anglais are ff.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 41: "the marks of expression in the horns should be one degree stronger than in the rest of the brass; if the trumpets and trombones play pp, the horns should be marked p". In forte, "two horns are needed to one trumpet". At 105–120 two trumpets at f sit over Hn 1–3 crotchets and Hn 4's tenor countermelody (c007), and the countermelody is one horn under a ff tutti, also at f. With the marks as they are, the countermelody is covered.

**Fix.**
- Hn 1–4 `\pp\<` → `\p\<` at 101 (sB) and 301 (dB).
- Hn 1–4 `\f` → `\ff` at 105 (sC) and 305 (dC): `b'4->\ff`, `e'4->\ff`, `g'4->\ff`, `g2.(\ff`.

Both shared horn staves keep equal dynamics. The c049 point (brass re-entries soft at 101/301) still holds.

### 2. Vln I "sul A" goes past the A string's limit (169–172) — applied
**Problem.** c031 marked "sul A" from 169 to 172. That takes the A string up to E6 at 170, D♯6 and C♯6 at 171: 7th position, a 12th above the open string.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 26: a high note on one of the three lower strings is limited "to the one in the fourth position, either the octave note or the ninth of the open string", which is A5/B5 on the A string. c031's reason was the warm colour of the A string for soft high notes (Adler PDF 75). That colour stays for the opening G♯5–A5–B5, which lie within Rimsky's limit.

**Fix.** In tAVlnOne:
- 170: `b''2 e'''4^\markup \italic "nat.")`
- 173: remove `^\markup \italic "nat."`

The string crossing is a rising fourth (B5 → E6) inside the slur. This changes part of c031.

### 3. Fl 1 has no rest from 37 to 55 (bar 44) — applied
**Problem.** Fl 1 plays 19 bars without a rest (about 13 s, p< to f): the G-major counterstatement, then the second idea. The only breath is a stolen quaver at 48/49. c016 gave Vln I a rest on beat 3 of 44; the flute kept its D5.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 37: "Wood-wind players cannot manage extremely long sustained passages ... care must be taken therefore to give them a little rest from time to time". A flute entering on D6 at f needs a full breath.

**Fix.** sAFluteOne bar 44: `a''4-. fis''-. r4 |` (was `d''-.`). Ob 1 keeps its D4. After this, 45–55 lasts about 7.5 s.

### 4. Harp off-beat chords carry no "non arpegg." (153 and 341) — applied
**Problem.** The trio and coda accompaniment chords on beats 2–3 are unmarked. Only the chords at 149, 151, 236 and 361 carry the arpeggio sign.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 46: "The chords must always be broken (arpeggiato); should the composer wish otherwise he should notify it (non arpeggiato)". Crotchets 0.26 s apart blur if rolled.

**Fix.** tAHarpR 153 and codaHarpR 341: `<gis b e'>4^\markup \small \italic "non arpegg."`. The mark also prints in the harp part.

### 5. The final harmonic chord is three notes in one hand (375) — applied
**Problem.** LH `<e gis b>4-\flageolet`, and the RH rests.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 47: "Chords in harmonics can only consist of three notes written close together, two for the left hand and one for the right", and harmonics only softly. Adler allows three in one hand. I chose Rimsky's split because the right hand is free and two-plus-one is the more secure way to place the last soft sound.

**Fix.** codaHarpL: `<e gis>4-\flageolet\p^\markup \small \italic "harm." r4 r4`. codaHarpR: `b4-\flageolet r4 r4` at 375. The sound is unchanged (E4–G♯4–B4).

### 6. The coda's harp pedal change is printed at the chord (333/341) — applied
**Problem.** After 104 bars' rest, "C♯ D♮" stood under the first chord at 341, the moment it is needed. At 73 and 141 the full setting is printed in the rests (c026).

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 46–47: the harp is diatonic and its chromatic notes depend on the pedals. The conductor's score should show the setting before the entry, as it does elsewhere. The part omits pedalling by design (c075, MOLA).

**Fix.** codaHarpL bar 333 (same system as 341):

```
R2.*8 | \once \override MultiMeasureRestText.self-alignment-X = #LEFT \once \override MultiMeasureRestText.parent-alignment-X = #LEFT R2._\markup \small "Ped.: D C♯ B / E F♯ G♯ A" | R2.*7 |
```

Then remove `_\markup \small "C♯ D♮"` at 341.
