## Findings

### 1. Coda 329-332: the sustained wind bass of the descent E-D-C-B changes timbre with no common note, and the contrabassoon enters mp (applied)

**Bars:** 329-332, bass clarinet and contrabassoon.

**Problem:** These four bars are the bass of the coda's last crescendo. Over the basses' detached crotchets (E-D-C-B), the dotted-minim wind bass starts in the bass clarinet (E2 at 329, D2 at 330), which then stops. The contrabassoon takes C2 at 331 and B1 at 332. This hand-over is needed because the bass clarinet cannot reach C2 or B1 (c016). But the two parts never sound together. Rimsky-Korsakov's rule for passing a line to another timbre (PDF 125-126) is that "the last note of each part is made to coincide with the first note of the following one", starting "on one or two notes common to both parts". As it stands, the sustained bass changes colour on the bar line at 331, in the middle of a four-note descent. The descent is the structural motion of the coda's first stage: ♮VII at 330, the Neapolitan 6/4 at 331, V at 332.

The contrabassoon also enters marked mp at 331. c050 put the woodwinds in at p at 329 and recorded "Cbsn p at 331", so the text and the record no longer agree. The reading prefers a p entry for two reasons. A group added during a crescendo should enter p or pp and grow faster (PDF 127, "Amplification"). Entries at mp are "colourless and common-place" (PDF 136).

**Evidence:** coda.ily, `codaBassCl = { R2.*4 | e,2.\p\< | d,2.\mp | R2. | ...` and `codaCbsn = { R2.*6 | c,2.\mp\< | b,,2.\f | ...`. The page 11 image shows the bass clarinet's last note and the contrabassoon's first note in successive bars.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-213/work/sections/coda.ily):** the contrabassoon enters one bar earlier, on the bass clarinet's D2 and at p. Its crescendo to f at 332 is unchanged.

```
codaCbsn = { R2.*5 | d,2.\p\< | c,2. | b,,2.\f | e,,2.\ff | ...   % was R2.*6 | c,2.\mp\< | b,,2.\f |
```

The contrabassoon doubles the basses' D2, the bass of the ♮VII chord (c083), so no new pitch is added and no outer-voice motion changes. The bass clarinet part is not changed. I added a c213 comment above the c050 comment.
