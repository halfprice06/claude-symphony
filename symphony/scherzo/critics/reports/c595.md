## Findings (ranked)

### 1. In the ff tutti, Cl 1 sits out while theme A's lower octave has only two reeds (applied; audible)

**Bars:** 106–120 and 306–320 (Cl 1). The downbeat of 105/305 does not change.

**Problem:** In both tutti statements of theme A, the theme is in the violins, Ob 1 and the flutes (upper octave). Its lower octave (E4–B3, the "e'4-. b8-. b-." line) is played only by the cor anglais and Cl 2. c067 keeps strings off that octave. Against ff strings, Hn 3·4's tenor line, the brass and the timpani, two reeds in their middle register carry the octave that gives theme A its body. Cl 1 meanwhile plays G5 on the downbeat of 105/305 (the seventh's resolution, c093) and then rests 15 bars until 121 (or until the close at 321). Ob 2 does the same, but the lower octave lies in the oboe's coarse bottom fifth, so Ob 2 is not the right instrument for it.

**Evidence:** Adler PDF 297, Ex. XVIII-4a. Brahms 1/i mm. 85–87 is the one full tutti that ch. 18 prints. Flutes, oboes, clarinets and bassoons are each marked "zu 2" on the melody's octaves at ff, with the violins. No woodwind player sits out the main theme of a tutti. The same chapter asks that the score give "every detail for every instrument" (PDF 291, item 3). A clarinet resting through the loudest statement of the main theme, with no reason given in the source, is the kind of gap a conductor would query. Dvořák 9/iii (corpus), the movement's model, also doubles the woodwind pairs in its tutti statements of the scherzo theme.

**Fix (applied):** Cl 1 joins Cl 2 in unison from 106 to 120 and from 306 to 320. The part-combined staff then prints "a2".
- sC.ily `sCClOne`: `\partCombineApart g''4-. r4 r4 | \partCombineSoloII R2.*15 | \partCombineAutomatic g'2(\subP …` becomes `\partCombineApart g''4-. r4 r4 | \partCombineAutomatic g'2->( fis'8 f'8 | e'4-.) b'8-. b'-. g'4-. | fis'4-. dis'-. b-. | e'4-. b8-. b-. e'4-. | a'2->( gis'8 g'8 | fis'4-\sysLabel "a2"-.) dis'8-. dis'-. b4-. | e'4-. r4 r4 | a'4-. g'-. fis'-. | e'4-. dis'8-. dis'-. e'4-. | fis'4-.( g'8 a'8) b'4-. | c''2->( b'8 fis'8) | e'4-. b8-. b-. e'4-. | g'2->( fis'8 f'8 | e'4-.) dis'8-. dis'-. fis'4-. | e'4-. r4 r4 | g'2(\subP …`. That is Cl 2's own line, note for note.
- sC.ily `sCClTwo`: `\partCombineSoloII` at 106 becomes `\partCombineAutomatic`. Its `\sysLabel "2."` at 111 is removed (the "a2" now comes from Cl 1).
- dC.ily `dCClOne` / `dCClTwo`: the same change for 306–320. `\sysLabel "a2"` goes on Cl 1 at 312 and 315 (in place of Cl 2's "2.").

The seventh's resolution on Cl 1 at 105/305 (c093), the tenor countermelody, Ob 1 and the flutes are unchanged. No string is added to the lower octave, so c067 holds. Cl 1 keeps two beats' rest before its sub. p entry at 121 (as Cl 2 does) and before its p chord at 321. The dynamic is the ff both clarinets already carry from 104/304. No line goes silent, so no cue changes. Cl 1 sounds at 165–168 as before, and no cue in parts-cues.json points at Cl 1 in 106–120 or 306–320. In the Clarinet 1 part, the page turn moves to 208/209, after 8 bars' rest. It is still two pages.

### 2. "senza sord., dolce" at 73 sits close under Hn 1·2's pp (not changed)

At 73 the Hn 3·4 instruction is correctly above its own staff (PDF 294). Hn 1·2's diminuendo target pp hangs just above it, so it can read as one Hn 1·2 marking. I tested `-\tweak staff-padding #0.2` on the markup. It did not move: Hn 3's stem holds it at that height. I reverted the tweak. There is no clean fix short of moving Hn 1·2's pp, which marks where their diminuendo ends. The horns at 73 cannot misread it, because Hn 1·2 are not muted. Left as it is.

### Considered, not changed
- **Ob 2 on the lower octave at 106–120/306–320.** The line goes down to B3 at ff, the oboe's coarse bottom register (App. A, PDF 304; Adler ch. 7). Cl 1 is the better second reed. Not built.
- **Hn 1·2's held ff chord at 72/300 at f** (applying the Brahms level to brass). It is a sustained chord inside the tutti, not punctuation. Several earlier cycles kept "horns ff→pp at 72" (HISTORY), and bar 72 is on the do-not-touch list. Not built.
- **"rit." at 370 in the Clarinet 1 and 2 parts.** It prints above the 370–375 multi-rest, but at the height of the "W" box, so in Clarinet 1 it reads as belonging to the line above (under bar 346). This was already so in v595. The viola and timpani parts place it correctly. A part-only `\tag #'part` padding override in global.ily would fix it. I list it for the composer's parts pass; the score is unaffected.
- **B6 at 336 without 8va** (PDF 300, item 8a). Kept, as c055–c505 decided.
- **Hn 1 at 41 without a cue** (34 bars' rest). Under the set's 40-bar rule, and c505 kept it. Hn 2's cue at 37 covers the same entry.
