## What else I studied

**Bruckner 8/ii**, from my knowledge of the score (1890 version; I claim no bar numbers). Allegro moderato in C minor and 3/4, one beat to the bar. The main figure is a short motive in the violas and cellos under a soft violin tremolo. Bruckner builds it up by sequence and added layers to full-brass ff blocks, and he separates the blocks with general pauses. The trio, Langsam in A♭ major, is a slower, softer world: horns, warm strings, and the harps that the symphony saves for the trio and the Adagio. The scherzo returns da capo.

## Holistic comparison with Bruckner 8/ii

Would a conductor believe a master wrote this? A conductor would believe a master in the Dvořák–Brahms line wrote it. Nobody would take it for Bruckner, and the brief does not ask for that. What the two scherzi share:
- one short rhythmic cell saturating the movement: motive x here, Bruckner's "Michel" figure there;
- the main figure first heard in the lower strings in the development's fugato (57: Vc, then Vla, Vln II, stretto);
- a long build over a dominant pedal: 89–104, with the timpani on B and the "ritmo di tre/quattro battute" grouping;
- a general pause before the last section (340);
- a trio that slows the pulse, softens the dynamics and brings in the harp (153).

Bruckner's signature sounds would breach this movement's cyclic rules: the string tremolo under the theme, the brass-choir climaxes, and full-brass unisons on the theme. All three are kept for the finale (BRIEF.md, cyclic rules), so their absence is a decision, not a weakness.

Bruckner gets weight from long terraces. This movement gets the same effect in a smaller frame through its graded entries:
- 13 → 29 → 37 → 41;
- the coda's stretto 325–333, dovetailed from the bottom up (Adler PDF 213, Ex. 217);
- one fff, at 336.

Bruckner's trio is much slower than its scherzo. c129 rejected a slower trio here for reasons of proportion (the dance pulse of Dvořák 9/iii and the Eroica), and I see nothing in Bruckner 8/ii that answers that reason, so I do not re-raise it. I propose no structural change.
