## Findings

### 1. The rendered harp harmonics at 375 sound an octave low and louder than the held G♯. The scherzo's last chord is misrepresented in every mockup. (Applied.)

**Bars 375, Harp RH and LH. MIDI only.**

**Problem.** The last chord is printed as harmonics: LH E3–G♯3 and RH B3, each with a circle. They sound E4–G♯4–B4. coherence/scherzo.md and finale.md both describe it that way, and the finale ties its opening E♭4 to that E4. LilyPond's `-\flageolet` is only a sign, so score.midi plays the written pitches. In the v130 MIDI the harp's last notes are 52, 56 and 59 (E3, G♯3, B3) at velocity 53. The held G♯ is lower: Hn 1 G♯4 at velocity 37 and Vln I G♯5 at 31. So the mockup's last chord is a plucked E-major triad an octave too low, and it is the loudest thing in the bar. The real chord is soft bell tones at the horn's pitch. c116 left this open ("harmonics at 375 an octave low in the MIDI render only").

**Evidence.**
- Casanelles PDF 210–212 (rows 13–14): the mockup is how the score is heard and judged, and the MIDI is the score's temporal and pitch equivalent. Dan listens to the renders (r07 is built from score.midi), so this is the version of the link into the finale that he hears.
- PDF 204–205 (row 15): a sound that does not match its real model reads as unreal.
- Adler I PDF 116: harmonics sound an octave above the written note and are "very soft".
- This is the last sonority before the finale, and the cyclic notes build a same-pitch link on it.

**Fix (applied).** A one-chord MIDI transposition. The print is unchanged: page images of pages 1, 5, 9, 12 and 13 are pixel-identical to v130's. In sections/coda.ily, above codaHarpR:

```
harmOct = #(define-music-function (m) (ly:music?) #{ \set PianoStaff.midiMaximumVolume = #0.6 \transposition c'' #m \transposition c' #})
```

and at 375:

```
codaHarpR … | \harmOct { b4-\flageolet } r4 r4 | R2. | }
codaHarpL … | \harmOct { <e gis>4-\flageolet\p^\markup \small \italic "harm." } r4 r4 | R2. | }
```

The rebuilt MIDI plays 64, 68 and 71 (E4, G♯4, B4) at velocity 32, under Hn 1's 37. The \set sits inside the function because lyparse.py, and so partlengths.py, read a bare `\set PianoStaff…` as notes. The harp part (parts.ly) puts both staves in a PianoStaff, so the same code works there. I also added a comment line, c130, above c099's in coda.ily.

### Points tested and rejected
- **Reserve the soft held top violin note.** Movements I, II and III all end on a soft held Vln I note (I C5, II C5, III G♯5), against row 1. I keep it. The finale's bar 1 continues in Vln I at the same pitch, so the violin is part of the link, and HISTORY protects it for that reason.
