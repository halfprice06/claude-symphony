## Findings

None of the 27 principles is violated in a way that a change would improve. Every °7 is approached by neighbour, common tone or passing tone. Every °7 resolves its 7th down and its leading tone up, or moves into another °7 by semitone (87→88). Every ♭6 in E major comes from I or from ♮6 and reaches 5 in its own voice. Every chromatic step that involves the bass happens inside one voice. The two second-pass faults c132 left (282→283 and the voicing at 126) are gone in v222. I tested and rejected the points below, ranked by how close they came.

### Considered, no change

1. **358→359: Vln II's G4, the 7th of A♯°7, leaps to E4 instead of falling to F♯** (A&S PDF 375 §3, PDF 377 box). Over the E pedal this chord is the motto tail's common-tone °7 (Ex. 22-10 role, PDF 379). It moves to iv, not to I, so the 7th has no F♯ in the next chord to fall to. A step to F♯4 would add a 9th, F♯, to the A-minor chord under Hn 1's A4, against the motto's A at pp. c043/c070 protect Vln II G4–E4 so that the violas do not cover the motto's A4. I keep it.

2. **87→88: Hn 3's C4, the 7th of D♯°7, rises to C♯4.** By §3 the 7th falls. This is a chromatic succession of °7s (A&S PDF 561, c072): all four voices move by semitone (D♯→E, F♯→E, A→A♯, C→C♯). Making the 7th fall to B3 would put B3 against the bass A♯2 and break the rising °7 → °7 motion, which c072 and c132 kept. I keep it.

3. **252→253: Cl 1 leaves the V7's 7th (A4) on beat 1 of 252, and the reprise starts at 253.** Vln II takes G4 on beat 2 of 253, in the same register, a transferred resolution (A&S PDF 376, Ex. 22-5). With poco a poco accelerando and only the timpani and Vln I's falling arpeggio in 252, a held A4 would blur the reprise downbeat. c184 already set the clarinets here. I keep it.

4. **188→189: harp B♯3 (III♯, G♯ major) and the basses' B1 on the next downbeat.** This is a ♯5–♮5 cross relation split between two parts. But the harp itself moves B♯3 → B4 (the chromatic step stays in one instrument, Ex. 23-12b), and A&S lists V as a destination of III♯ (PDF 395, review 5b). c170 and c206 settled the harp here. I keep it.

5. **Mixture in the trio's third statement (209–224).** Ex. 23-8 (PDF 392–393) shows mode change used to vary a repetition. The trio's third statement repeats the first one's harmony in E major, with the mixture only at 202, 235 and 359. Adding ♭6 to the third statement would have to change T's phrase-2 harmony (c212 kept it) or the ii7–ii6/5 cadence (c202 protected it). The codetta's iv at 235 already does what Ex. 23-8 describes, one phrase later. I keep it.
