## Findings

### 1. 205–208: Hn 3·4 hold a chord under Hn 1's motto-tail preview (applied)

**Problem.** The scherzo quotes movement I's motto three times in the horn: the call (3–6), the tail's preview (205–208, Hn 1 since c324) and the tail (357–360). At 3–6 (c090) and 357–360 (c060) the quoting horn is the only horn sounding, as in movement I (I 13–14). At 205–208 Hn 3·4 hold D♯4 and B3/F♯3 under Hn 1's G♯4–A4–B4–A♯4–A4, the only quotation played against other horns. c374 removed the sevenths inside the horns but kept the chord, although it noted that "in the anticipation the same line sounds against its own colour, and in the statement it does not."

**Evidence.**
- Adler PDF 264 (Ex. XVII-9): Mozart "never uses the orchestral horns while the solo horn is playing", only "in the introduction and during interludes, so as not to interfere with the solo sound". Here 201–204 is the interlude and 205–208 the solo.
- PDF 273: instruments blend "with acoustically sympathetic orchestral combinations"; a horn line over a horn chord is the extreme case.
- PDF 257: dialogue fixes the solo timbre in the listener's mind; the preview should prepare the single horn of 357–360 (c324's reason). This is a stronger reason than c374's, which asked only for consonant horn notes.

**Fix (tB.ily, applied).**
