### 2. The ♭6→5 link at both joins: keep Cl 2's G4 at bar 7 (no score change; add it to the do-not-touch list)

**Bars.**
- Movement II 89–92: Vln II A♭4.
- Scherzo 1–2: Hn 1 G♯4. Scherzo 7: Cl 2 G4 (with Cl 1 E5 and Bsn 1 B3).
- Scherzo 360–376: Hn 1 G♯4. Finale IV 1–2: Hn 1 A♭4 → G4.

**What it does.** In C, the symphony's key, A♭ is ♭6. A&S PDF 388 says ♭6 "tends to move to 5". The symphony makes this resolution twice, in the same octave.
- First join: movement II's last A♭4 becomes the scherzo's G♯4 and falls to G4 in Cl 2 at bar 7, the move from E major to E minor.
- Second join: the scherzo's last G♯4 becomes the finale's A♭4 and falls to G4 at IV 2.

Movement II's ♭6 of A♭ (F♭, its 87–89) is also the scherzo's tonic E. The scherzo's last E4 (the harp harmonic at 375) falls by semitone to the finale's E♭4. This is ♭6–5 of A♭ completed across the movement.

The do-not-touch list protects the G♯ frame and "no E4 at 7–8" (c040). It does not protect Cl 2's G4, which is the 5 of the first resolution. An octave change there (G3 or G5) would leave the first ♭6 resolved in another register.

**Fix.** HISTORY.md do-not-touch: "Cl 2 G4 at 7 (movement II's A♭4, held as G♯4 in Hn 1 at 1–2, resolves ♭6→5 of C at this pitch; the finale does the same at IV 1–2; A&S PDF 388, 395)."

### Considered and decided

1. **87–89: Hn 3 C4–C♯4–B3.** This looks like the ♭6–♮6–5 line that Ex. 23-2h (PDF 388) calls poor. I keep it.
   - The C4 is held from 85 as the 5 of F major and is diatonic in E minor. It is not a mixture ♭6 in a major key, which is the case the rule describes.
   - The line rides the chromatic °7 succession over the bass A–A♯–B (A&S Unit 31; c072, c222).
   - The alternatives are worse. A♯3 at 88 would double the applied leading tone with the bass clarinet's A♯2 and make octaves into 89. G3 would double the 7th with Bsn 1.
2. **♭VI (PDF 390), the unit's "most important" mixture chord, is absent.** Here it would be C major, which belongs to the finale. Holding it back is what makes the finale's C-major arrival new. For the finale's rework: its C – A♭ – E – C key plan already treats the scherzo's key as III♯ of C reached without ♮5–♯5 (PDF 395, points 5–6). Nothing to send.
