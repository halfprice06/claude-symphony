## Findings

### 1. Bar 314: a root-position augmented III in the full tutti at ff (applied)

- **Bars.** 313–315, theme A bar 10 in the reprise tutti (dC). Part 1's statement is at 113–115.
- **Problem.**
  - c058 gave the reprise a rising bass F♯–G–A (iiø7 – passing i6 – iv) in place of part 1's A–G♯–A. c143 then changed part 1's bar 114 to V6 of iv and moved the violas there to B3/E4. Bar 314 kept the violas' old B3/G4.
  - On beat 2 of 314 the tutti melody takes the lower neighbour D♯ (Fl, Ob, C.a., Cl, Vln I and II, at ff). Under it the bass G (Bsn 2, Cbsn, Cb, Hn 4), the violas' G4 and B3 and the cellos' B3 sound with no E anywhere: the sonority is G–B–D♯ in root position.
  - Part 1's bar 114 (G♯ bass, violas E4) and the quiet statements at 22 and 262 (G♯ bass) do not have it.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 240, §9: "the augmented form of III … does not play an important role in pieces written in minor. Most of the apparent instances of III as an augmented triad are in 6/3 position and express dominant rather than mediant harmony."
  - Points for Review 7 (PDF 245): "III in minor with raised 7̂ (III as augmented triad) should be avoided."
  - The score's other G–B–D♯ (98 b3) is the book's acceptable kind: a 6/3 over the dominant pedal B. The half-beat scan found 314 is the only root-position case in the movement.
  - Bar 314 is the tenth bar of the last full statement of theme A, so the sound falls in the movement's most exposed tutti.
- **Fix (applied).** In dC.ily, `dCVla`, the tenth bar of the line (314):
  ```
  r4 <b g'>4-. <b g'>4-.   →   r4 <b e'>4-. <b e'>4-.
  ```
  - This is part 1's bar 114 note for note (sCVla bar 10).
  - Beat 2 now sounds E–G–B with the melody's D♯ as a neighbour against the held E: i6 over the passing G, which is what c143 said the bar means.
  - The bass line (c058), the horns and every other part are unchanged.
- **Voice-leading check.** The violas' upper note goes A4–E4–A4 (was A4–G4–A4).
  - Against the melody it makes a 6th, then a 7th onto the neighbour, then an octave.
  - Against the bass G it makes a 6th.
  - There are no new parallels; analysis.txt still lists the same 7 known outer-voice parallels (69, 326–328).
  - In the MIDI the staccato viola chord ends before the second D♯ quaver, so that half-beat still sounds G–B–D♯. The same happens at 114, and the harmonic event on the beat is now i6.
- **Undo check.** This does not undo c058 (bass F♯–G–A kept), c143, c287 (violas' beat-3 B3 at 306/318 untouched) or c022.

## Decisions on points tested and kept (my decisions; composer, keep them)

1. **Reprise without a major III (253–304).**
   - Row 6 praises major III's contrast, and the recorded reason for leaving it out is "the reprise's job is to stay home" (c003, c083, c173, c263).
   - The reading gives a stronger reason than that one. The reprise does go through III (G at 289), and it is minor because the fugato subject carries the motto tail on its own degrees (B♭–A–A♭–G = 3̂–2̂–♭2̂–1̂). A G-major III would need B–A♯–A–G, which is no longer the motto's tail.
   - Ex. 15-1 also shows that the consequent may retrace III; the score does that, in minor.
   - Kept, with this reason added.
2. **The link 133–152 stays on E.**
   - I tested a III (G major) at 143–144 before the G→G♯ turn, on Dvořák's model (row "What else", 219–231) and on PDF 231's point that I→III "sounds … like an expansion of I".
   - Dvořák uses III as the route to a trio in VI. Here the trio is in I major, and the event of the link is Hn 1's G→G♯ (145). A G-major chord just before it would sound the G as a root and blur the mode change into a chromatic-mediant step.
   - Kept, as c253, c293, c313 and c323 decided.
3. **Coda 329–332.**
   - Ex. 15-15 asks for 4̂ to prepare the seventh of V7. Vln I has A5 on top at 330, and Bsn 1 holds A3 through 330–332 and resolves it to G3 at 333.
   - Moving the seventh into Vln II (A4 at 332) would leave it unresolved, because Vln II leaps to E5 at 333.
   - Kept.
4. **214 beat 1** (A♯–C♯–E for one beat). The root F♯ enters with T's held peak on beat 2, and c263 showed that Bsn 1 F♯4 here makes fifths. Kept.
5. **Proportions and length.** The three blocks are 97/88/93 s, the reprise is half of part 1, and the coda is 44 s. Kept.
