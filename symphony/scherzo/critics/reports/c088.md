## Findings

All five findings concern a single gap that Adler PDF 309 names directly. In this score, wherever the horns hold harmony on repeated dotted minims, the notes carry no slur and no articulation. A horn player reads an unslurred note as a fresh, normally tongued attack, so every bar starts with a "tu" accent, at one bar per 0.68 s (scherzo) or 0.79 s (trio). Elsewhere the score marks this distinction carefully. The trio's first horn pad (153–168, Hn 3·4) is tied in four-bar phrases, the woodwinds and strings carry phrase slurs everywhere (c069, c078), and the horn solos at 341 and 357 are slurred. The unmarked pads are therefore an inconsistency in the score, not a deliberate pulse. Masters write these pads as portato (tenuto under a slur), which Adler calls soft or legato tonguing, or tie them. Portato keeps the one-to-a-bar pulse and asks for a soft attack. I chose portato over ties so that the existing harmonic rhythm and pulse stay exactly as they are. No notes, rhythms, dynamics or hairpins change.
