### 1. Join from movement II (v215) into bars 1–7: keep.

- **Check.** II ends with A♭4 in Vln II, under C5, ppp, with a fermata and no attacca. III opens with Hn 1 G♯4 alone, pp, with a fermata at 2. Pizz. E and timpani E come at 3. The timpani label at 1 matches II's muta text pitch for pitch. Hn 3·4 end II after "via sord." (91) and enter III marked "senza sord." at 73.
- **Evidence.** This is the *Widmung* common-tone modulation (A♭ → E with A♭/G♯ held; A&S PDF 586–587). The tone is exposed as the only sound, as PDF 587 asks. The E♭4 of II's last chord (Cl 2) comes back as the horn call's held D♯4 at 5–6. That is Haydn's preparation of a remote key by its enharmonic tone (PDF 597–598, Ex. 32-17), and it is left unresolved for the finale.
- **Decision.** No change.

### 2. Join from bars 361–376 into finale v209 bar 1: keep.

- **Check.** The last sound is G♯4 (Hn 1) and G♯5 (Vln I) alone. The harp harmonics E4–G♯4–B4 and pizz. E2/E1 sound at 375 under them. The finale's bar 1 has A♭4 in Hn 1 and A♭5 in Vln I, ff, as A♭–C–E♭–F♯. The muta label at 376 ("low E in G, G in A♭, B in C, high E in E♭") equals the finale's bar-1 label. The scherzo ends with soft sticks; the finale asks for hard sticks.
- **Evidence.** The ending thins to a bare octave on the common tone, after which the new key enters ff. That is the Schubert 9/iv recapitulation (A&S PDF 587, Ex. 32-4a), done across a pause. In C the scherzo's E is III♯, and A&S say III♯ moves to V through a predominant (PDF 594, Ex. 32-12). The finale's German sixth on A♭ does that at IV 1–2, and it takes the scherzo's G♯ as its bass, the ♭VI → augmented sixth → V route of PDF 593. Between the harp's E4 at 375 and the finale's E♭4 there is a falling inflection, which PDF 585 allows to be abrupt when the pitch pair is the piece's motive.
- **Decision.** No change.

### 6. Note for the finale's rework (no scherzo change).

The reading's last example (K. 542, PDF 601–602) uses this scherzo's own motive, B–A♯–A–G♯ in E major. Mozart enlarges it into a key plan by mixture: B–B♭–A–G♯ through G major → G minor. The finale already enlarges the tail as a line at IV 273–276 (G–F♯–F–E–D–D♭–C). The scherzo's part of the chain is complete as it stands: it states the tail on E's degrees and on B, and it keeps the stop chord, C major and the trumpets' B→C out. So I decided against adding a further statement of the tail to the scherzo; it would be development, which the BRIEF reserves for movements I and IV.
