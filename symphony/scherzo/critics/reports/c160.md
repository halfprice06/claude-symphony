## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 73: Horn 4's part has no "senza sord." (and no "dolce") at its first entry (applied)
**Problem.** A binding cyclic rule (BRIEF.md; plan, "Cyclic rules") says Hn 3·4 are marked "senza sord." at their first entry, because both players end movement II muted. Movement II marks the mute in both players' own parts. Its Hn 4 has "con sord." at 85 as a part-only tag (mvt2 c046, Adler part 1 PDF 295) and "via sord." at 91. In the scherzo the reminder is attached only to `sBHnThree`. The score prints it once on the shared staff, which is correct. But the extracted part parts-15-horn-4.pdf has neither "senza sord." nor "dolce" at 73. `pdftotext` finds the words in Horn 3's part and not in Horn 4's. coherence/scherzo.md says "Hn 3·4 marked 'senza sord.' at their first entry". That is true of the score and false of Horn 4's part.
**Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 241: "Learn to trust the judgment of your ear instead of your eye. Music does not exist on paper." What sounds is what each player reads. Here the part, not the score, is what reaches the player. PDF 240: compare the intention (the rule and the coherence file) with the result (the part).
**Fix (applied, sB.ily).** In `sBHnFour`, bar 73:
`f2.--(\pp -\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "senza sord., dolce" | f2.-- | …`
The score removes `\tag #'part` material (score.ly `\removeWithTag #'part \hnFour`), so the score still prints the words once, from Hn 3. I checked this: one occurrence in score.pdf. I rebuilt the parts with build-parts.sh (0 warnings). Horn 4's part now shows "senza sord., dolce" at 73, as Horn 3's does.

### 2. Bars 4–8: the bassoon sits inside the horn call's register; movement I puts a low dominant pedal under its call (applied)
**Problem.** Bars 3–6 quote movement I's horn call (I 13–14: Hn 1 E♭3–C4–B3, 3–1–7, one slur). In movement I the only sustained sound under the call is Bsn 2's G2, the dominant, below the call's lowest note. The horn line therefore stands alone above a pedal. In the scherzo, Bsn 1 holds B3 from bar 4. That note lies inside the call's own range (G♯3–E4): a fourth under the horn's E4 at 4 and a third under its D♯4 at 5–6. c150 brought the bassoon down to pp, and in the MIDI it plays at velocity 25 under the horn's 44. The level is right, but the register is not. Horn and bassoon in the same octave fuse into a dyad (E/B, then D♯/B), so the quotation sounds like the top of a two-part chord, not movement I's single line over a bass. The pizzicato bass speaks only on beats 1 of 3, 5 and 7, so for most of 4–6 the bassoon's B3 is the lowest sound and sits right against the horn.
**Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 12–13, Ex. 1 (seen on PDF 12): the cut line was the one in the lower-middle register, next to the primary element. Removing it made the remaining motion clearer and let the primary element "dominate a little more noticeably". PDF 13 (focus): the background must not divert the ear from the primary element. PDF 241–242 (the past carried into the new sound): the quotation should bring the source's sound with it, not only its notes. Movement I v221, intro.ily: `introBsnTwo = { … | g,1\p~\> | g,2.\pp r4 | }` under `introHnOne … r4 es4(\mp c'2~ | c'4\> b2)\p`.
**Fix (applied, intro.ily).**
```
introBsnOne = { R2.*6 | b2.~\pp | b2\> r4\! | R2.*4 | }
introBsnTwo = { R2.*3 | b,2.~\pp^\markup \bold "2." | b,2.~ | b,2.~ | b,2.~ | b,2\> r4\! | R2.*4 | }
```
Bsn 2 holds the dominant B2 under the call (4–6), as movement I's Bsn 2 holds G2. Bsn 1 adds B3 at 7, entering with the clarinets' E5/G4 when the chord turns to E minor. So the E-minor chord at 7–8 keeps its close voicing (B3–G4–E5 over B2 and the pizzicato E). c090's points still hold. The call is a single horn line (do-not-touch: Hn 1 alone on 3–6). Pitch classes and the harmony are unchanged, and there is still no E4 at 7–8 (c040). The `"2."` label is a combine label, which parts-lib.ily strips from the extracted part.
