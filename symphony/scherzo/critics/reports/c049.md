## What else I studied

I studied Dvořák, Symphony No. 7, iii in the local corpus: reference-corpus/pdf/DvorakA_O70__DvorakSYMPH7_conductor.pdf, PDF 94–146 (printed pp. 92–144), and the source DvorakSYMPH7M3_*.ly. The movement is in 6/4. The cellos carry the theme (p poco espr.) against syncopated upper strings, and almost every bar has an fp or fz cross-accent (bars 1–12, p. 92–93). The coda (bars 225–255, p. 139–144) moves from "poco a poco ritard." with pp low strings, through "In Tempo", to strings and timpani alone. The winds are silent in the last bars.

Verdict: a conductor would accept the plan of this score as a master's, including the keys, the timpani motive, the hemiola cadences, the trio, and a coda that recalls the trio and dies on G♯. The places that betray a less experienced hand are local. In the reprise the flute's heterophony makes false relations against the violins. The last cadence loses its bass. The trio's forte climax is shaped only in the melody. Brass re-entries are marked at levels Rimsky-Korsakov calls weak.

Against v39, the large shape is unchanged and most changes from c039–c048 are improvements. Two things got worse:
- **Bar 354 lost its bass.** v39 had the bass on B at 353 and 354 and E at 355. c043's expansion of the cadential 6/4 left 354 with nothing below the violas' G♯3 (Finding 2).
- **197.** Hn 1·2 now re-enter mf after a 52-bar rest, which PDF 136 calls a colourless re-entry. v39 had f. c047 lowered it because Hn 1 doubles Cl 1's tune at the unison. That balance reason holds, so I leave 197 as it is.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Reprise heterophony: false relations and an appoggiatura sounding against its own resolution (253–268) — applied
**Problem.**
- At 254 and 266, Fl 1's F6 crotchet on beat 3 sounds against Vln I's F♯5 quaver, a diminished octave. At 258 the same happens with G6 against G♯5.
- At 264 the flute plays C6–B5–A♯5–B5 *at the violins' pitch* while Vln I holds the C6 appoggiatura over V7. The resolution note B5 and the appoggiatura C6 sound together in one octave, at the top of the theme's climb (c039's swell).

**Evidence.**
- Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 132: "Melodic design comprising notes foreign to the harmony, passing or grace notes, embellishments etc., does not permit that a florid outline should proceed at the same time with another one, reduced to essential notes". He adds that this produces "false relation" and is milder only an octave away.
- PDF 134: the composer "who relies entirely on the difference between two timbres will often find himself using the most painful discords".
- The trio's heterophony (169–184) has only passing seconds and no false relation, so it is tolerable. The reprise has both faults.

**Fix (dA.ily, Fl 1).** The turn stays on beats 1–2; the chromatic tail moves with the violins, two octaves (or one) above.
- 254 and 266: `g'''8( a''' g'''4 fis'''8 f''')`
- 258: `a'''8( gis''' a'''4 gis'''8 g''')`
- 264: `c'''2(\> b''8 fis''8)`, in unison with Vln I for this one bar.

### 2. The last cadential 6/4 has no bass in its second bar (354) — applied
**Problem.** Since c043 the cadential 6/4 lasts two bars (353–354), but Vc and Cb pluck B only at 353 and 355. In 354 the lowest note is the violas' G♯3, so the bar sounds as I6, not as the 6/4 over B.

**Evidence.**
- Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 168: when the real bass is left out, the upper parts produce chords of the sixth and 6/4 effects. In Ex. 304 (p. 493) and Ex. 311 (p. 501) he places the bass where the harmony needs it.
- v39 had a bass note in every bar of this cadence.

**Fix (coda.ily).** Bar 354: Vc `b,4 r4 r4`, Cb `b,,4 r4 r4`, still pizz. The arco change at 358 keeps its time.

### 3. The forte return of the trio tune peaks without the orchestra (217–221) — applied
**Problem.** At 219 the tune reaches A6 at f, with a swell in the melody parts only (c038). Under it the horns and bassoons stay p and the violas and basses stay mf, so the trio's emotional peak has no support.

**Evidence.**
- Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 123: lyrical climaxes "should be supported melodically and harmonically by the orchestra… The culminating point… often coincides with the entry… of the trombones or other brass instruments, or by a rush of strings".
- Ex. 280 (p. 447) puts "poco più f" in every accompanying part at the singer's piena voce. Ex. 281 (p. 449) brings in the brass f at the Allargando.
- The soft peak at 177–179 is correctly left unsupported (PDF 124).

**Fix (tC.ily).**
- Hn 1·2 and Bsn 1·2: `\<` at 217, `\mf\>` at 219, `\p` at 221.
- Violas: `\<` at 217, `\f\>` at 219, `\mf` at 221.
- Cb: `\<` at 217, `\f\>` at 219, `\mf` at 221.
- The shared staves carry identical dynamics. No notes change.

### 4. Bassoons double the horns in unison under the oboe's solo (225–228) — applied
**Problem.** Ob 1 plays T's head p over a pp sustained E-major chord in which Bsn 1 doubles Hn 3 on B3 and Bsn 2 doubles Hn 4 on E3. The one exception is 227, where Bsn 1 has C♯4, which the harp also plays. Cl 2, the violas and Vln II also sustain, so six held parts sit under one solo.

**Evidence.**
- Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 121: "Doubling of wood-wind and horns… to form one harmonic part is likewise to be avoided" under a solo. "Incessant four-part harmony is to be deprecated"; fewer parts and pedals are better.
- On the same page he approves bassoon and horn sustaining *different* notes, which is what c047 relied on. These are unison doublings.

**Fix (tC.ily).** Bsn 1 and Bsn 2 rest at 225–228 (`R2.*4`). The horns keep the musette drone as at 153. Bsn 1 enters fresh with the tune's head at 233.

### 5. Brass re-entries at p after long rests (101, 301, 201) — applied
**Problem.**
- At 101 and 301, Tpt 1·2 and Hn 1–4 re-enter p< into the hemiola build, while the trombones beside them enter pp<. The trumpets have rested 100 bars at 101.
- At 201, Hn 3·4 re-enter p after 32 bars.

**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 136: "After a long rest the re-entry of the horns, trombones and tuba should coincide with some characteristic intensity of tone, either pp or ff; piano and forte re-entries are less successful". PDF 130: a prolonged crescendo is built by adding instruments, and the late group crescendos faster.

**Fix.**
- sB.ily and dB.ily: `\pp\<` for Hn 1, 2, 3, 4 and Tpt 1, 2 at 101 and 301 (the target stays ff at 104/304).
- tB.ily: Hn 3·4 `\pp` at 201.
