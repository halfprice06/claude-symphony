### Considered and kept

**1. The exit from the trio, 225–252 (28 soft bars, 22.5 s, bass on E from 225 to 242).**
- *The case against it.* This is the one stretch where the movement stops moving forward. Its four-bar units are I–I–IV–I three times, then four bars of held E major under a poco rit. Dvořák returns from his trio in about 9 bars.
- *Why I keep it.* By Unit 16 §9 (PDF 256–257, Ex. 16-13) and Unit 19 §4 (PDF 309, Ex. 19-7), the codetta is a tonic prolonged by IV neighbours. Its third unit already turns IV into iv (235), so the mixture that arrives at 241 is prepared. The retransition is an expanded 6/4 over V (row 26) with a stepwise bass E–A–C–B (PDF 256).
- *Why Dvořák is not the model here.* His return is fast because his scherzo comes back loud. This score's da capo comes back pp/mp, on the Beethoven and Eroica model. A soft return needs a longer approach.
- *Cost of a cut.* It would renumber 237–376, which the finale cites.
- *Decision.* I agree with c103, c113 and c123 and keep it.

**2. Coda stage 2 starts on IV (341) after the G.P.** E minor at 339 becomes, in hindsight, the upper 5th of A. E is then heard as V of A (343, 348) and only becomes I at 349–356 (Unit 16 §4, PDF 253). So the movement's last root-position tonic is held back for 16 bars after the G.P. That is the reading's own device for making a late tonic a goal. Kept.

**3. Thirty-six bars of slow coda after eleven fast ones** (31.2 s against 11.2 s). The slow stage is the cyclic frame: the trio tune, the motto tail and the G♯ pivot, all protected. Within it, the cadence 353–356 is expanded (row 26) and the tonic arrives only at 356. Held E then fills 356–376, with Hn 1's G♯ on top for the last eight bars. That tonic follows the reading's own goal-directed design. Kept.

**4. Direct V–i at the three reprise points (104→105, 252→253, 304→305).** Ex. 16-3 (PDF 252) gives III between V and I as an option before a reprise, not a requirement. Here the movement's energy runs through the V pedals and the hemiolas, and a divider would weaken the arrivals. Kept.
