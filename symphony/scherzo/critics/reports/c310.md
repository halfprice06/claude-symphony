### 1. The finale record hides three MOVED lines behind "38/38 unchanged" (shared tool, coherence/lyparse.py)

- **Problem.** `themecheck.py themes-finale-quotes.ly mvt3/v288 --beats 4` prints "38/38 unchanged" but also "MOVED scherzoHeadC FluteOne / VlnOne: expected at 275, found at [276]" and "MOVED choraleLast VlnOne: expected at 255, found at [256]". The music is right: finale Fl 1 and Vln I play C6–G5–G5–C6–E6 | E6–D6–D♭6–C6 at 275–276 as recorded. The cause is the finale's v286 beam edits, `\once \offset positions #'(-0.25 . 0) Beam`: lyparse does not strip `\offset`, reads "s" in "positions" as a spacer and "eam" as a note, and adds a beat. A second fault: `\tag #'score { … } \tag #'part { … }` (finale Hn 1·2 at 255–256) is counted twice, so Hn 1·2's G4–F♯4–F4–E4 at 273–275 reads two bars late. That line is the finale's half of the motto chain answering 357–360 (A&S PDF 601–602), and a future check would misplace it.
- **Evidence.** Points for Review 5 (PDF 603): the large-scale links hold only through the motivic design, which these records track. The scherzo's c290 and c300 checks quoted "38/38" from this output.
- **Fix (tested).** In coherence/lyparse.py, `strip()`, add after the `\cueDuring` line:
```python
    while True:
        m = re.search(r"\\tag\s*#'part\s*\{", text)
        if not m:
            break
        i, depth = m.end(), 1
        while depth:
            depth += {'{': 1, '}': -1}.get(text[i], 0); i += 1
        text = text[:m.start()] + ' ' + text[i:]
```
and after the `\override` line:
```python
    text = re.sub(r"\\offset\s+[\w.-]+\s+#'?\([^)]*\)\s+[\w.]+", ' ', text)
```
(the checkout copy also lacks the `\set|\unset` line that /mnt has; take it too). With the patched copy (/tmp/critic-310/tools/lyparse.py, diff in lyparse.patch): finale record 38/38 with no MOVED line, scherzo record 51/51, movement I record 35/35, and Hn 1·2 read G4 F♯4 | F4 | E4 at 273–275. The file belongs to all threads; the composer should apply it and tell the finale thread.
