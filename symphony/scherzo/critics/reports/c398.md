## Findings (ranked)

### 1. T's bars 3–4 suspension has no swell into the tie, unlike T's other two tied notes (applied)

**Bars.** 155–156 (Cl 1), 171–172 (cellos), 199–200 (Cl 1·2, Vln II, Vla), 211–212 (Cl 1, Vln I, Vln II), coda 343–344 (Hn 3, in A).

**Problem.** c331 made T's bar 3 take the V7's seventh A on beat 3 and tie it into the tonic bar as a 4-3 (Ex. 21-23a). c331 itself argued that T now has one tied-note cell heard three times, at bars 3–4, 10–11 and 13–14. The score shapes the second and third ties with a `<` into the tied note and a `>` out of it: 161–162 (c348) and 165–166 (c098). The first tie has only the `>` from the downbeat of bar 4. That tie is the movement's most-heard suspension, in every statement of the trio tune. Its dissonance gets no more weight than its resolution, and the cell does not sound the same way the three times it comes.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 348, sec. 4: "dissonant suspensions receive something of an accent and their resolutions are softer". Sec. 3: the resolution is "a relaxation, a decrease in intensity", which needs a higher level before it. Beethoven's `<>` before the suspended V7 in Ex. 21-37 (PDF 365) is the same shape, and the PDF 357–358 model is this exact progression.

**Fix (applied).** I used the same pattern as c098's cadences, with a hidden peak one level above the phrase and c338's existing resolution targets kept:
- tA.ily Cl 1: `dis''4. fis'8 a'4\<~ | a'4-\tweak stencil ##f \mp\> gis'2-\tweak stencil ##f \pp)`
- tA.ily cellos: `a4\<~ | a4-\tweak stencil ##f \mf\> gis2…`
- tB.ily Cl 1, Vln II, Vla: `a'4\<~ | a'4-\tweak stencil ##f \ff\> gis'2)`. Cl 2 shares the staff, so it gets the same hairpin on its held C♯: `<< cis'2. { s2 s4\< } >> | bis2.-\tweak stencil ##f \ff\>`.
- tC.ily Cl 1 (peak f), Vln I and Vln II (peak ff): the same pattern.
- coda.ily Hn 3: `d'4\<~ | d'4-\tweak stencil ##f \mp\> cis'2)`.

The `<` is a swell on the held A (one beat), as at 165. The MIDI attack velocities are unchanged.

### 2. Hn 1's 9-8 at 216 is now shaded; c198/c298's reason for leaving it no longer holds (applied)

**Bars.** 216 (Hn 1 C♯5 tied from 215, resolving to B4 on beat 2 over B; Hn 2's 7th E4 resolves to D♯4 on the downbeat).

**Problem.** This is the one dissonant suspension in the trio with no mark. c198 and c298 both agreed it needed one. Both rejected it for one reason only: in their layout a horn hairpin pushed the score to 15 pages.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 348 (as above); PDF 347, Ex. 21-2, double suspensions. The layout reason lapsed with c395's system breaks. With exactly c298's hairpins (`cis''2.~ | cis''4\> b'2)`, `dis'2.--\>)`), /tmp/critic-398/work216 builds at 13 pages with 0 warnings.

**Fix (applied).** tC.ily:
- Hn 1: `cis''2.~ | cis''4\> b'2) \breathe | gis'2.--(-\tweak stencil ##f \pp\<`
- Hn 2: `dis'2.--\>) \breathe | e'2.--(-\tweak stencil ##f \pp\<`

The hidden pp at 217 gives the MIDI diminuendo an end. It also starts the existing crescendo to mf at 219 from pp instead of p, which nothing on the page shows. Both horns of the shared staff have the same dynamics, and 217–223 still print as chords (checked at 200 dpi). The whole build stays at 13 pages; page 8 now starts at 212 instead of 213.

### 3. The hemiola 4-3s at 55, 131 and 287 stay unmarked (no change)

In the hemiola's minims the dissonance falls on a weak part of the shifted metre (PDF 362–363, Ex. 21-31). The tenuto on the third minim marks the hemiola, not the resolution. c298's reasoning holds, and the passage is protected (c002, c085).

### 4. The tone of resolution against the new suspensions (no change)

At 156, 172, 212 and 344, no other voice sounds the resolution before beat 2. c331 cleared the harp and violas, and c054 has Hn 2 enter on beat 2 at 212. At 200 the bass G♯ is the 9-8 case that PDF 352 allows. All follow PDF 352 and Ex. 21-12.

### 5. Pedal points and the suspended dominant (no change)

Every dominant pedal leads into its tonic with a built crescendo:
- 89–104 into 105 (timpani motive x, then the hemiola build);
- 201–208 into 209 (pp roll `<` to the f return; motto tail `<>`);
- 245–252 into 253 (Bass Cl B2 pp from c396, cresc. to p at 252, then the pp leggiero reprise).

The coda holds V for three bars (336–338) before the E minor ff at 339, the entire-chord suspension of Ex. 21-37. The movement has no 7-6 chain, so Mozart's "cresc. → p" pattern (Ex. 21-17) has no place here, and I add none.
