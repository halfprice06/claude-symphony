### Checked, no change
1. **Fl 1 37-56.** Twenty bars with one crotchet rest (44, beat 3): theme A in unison with Fl 2 at 37-44 (p< to f), then the second idea's melody 45-56 (mf<f). Adler asks for rests after sustained flute passages and shows Brahms giving the second flute the held notes (PDF 188). Here the second idea is in three slurs (45-48, 49-52, 53-55), each 2.7 s or less at 2.=88, and the flutist breathes at the slur breaks by shortening the last quaver of 48 and 52; Fl 1 then rests 57-100. A Brahms-style dovetail would split the melody's swell at 46 and 50 between two players for no gain at this speed. Kept.
2. **Contrabassoon 209-224.** Sixteen bars at mp-mf with one breath mark at 216 (two slurs of 6.3 s each at 2.=76). Adler warns that the low register needs much breath and periodic rests (PDF 230). The notes here are E2-C♯3 except B1 at 211 and 221-222, mostly above the lowest octave, where the air demand is lower, the dynamics are soft, and the part then rests 225-280. Kept.
3. **Cbsn A2-B1, slurred ninth down at 220-221** (mp, tenuto). Adler says some downward bassoon skips are difficult (PDF 226) and the contrabassoon speaks slowly at the bottom (PDF 230). B1 is not in the lowest fifth, the note is held a whole bar, and the double basses have the same B1 (c184), so a late speech is covered. Kept.
4. **Both flutes on A6 ff at 110 and 310**, reached by a leap of a fourth from E6 and held a minim with an accent. Adler's caution is for pitches above A6 (PDF 186). A6 at ff in a full tutti is secure for professionals; the unison helps the sound and the doubling Vln I covers small intonation differences. Kept.
5. **Fl 1 A6 at p in the reprise heterophony 254-266.** Already reasoned by c036 and c069 (Adler PDF 174, 186): p, not pp, and Vln I mp below. Kept.
6. **Bsn 1 101-120** (twenty bars f-ff). The breath marks at 104 (c246) and 116 (c086) split it into 4 + 12 + 4 bars, the longest 8.2 s with slur breaks at 109/110 and 112/113 inside it. Kept.
7. **Bass clarinet 73-89 and 139-148**: held pp chalumeau notes, runs of 5.2-5.9 s with a crotchet rest at 80, 89 and 144. Adler's warmest register (PDF 217). Kept.
