## Findings

### 1. Vln II 169–184: short end stems under horizontal beams, and one bar flipped against the passage (applied)

**Bars.** Vln II 171, 173–175, 177, 179, 180 (tA.ily, `tAVlnTwo`).

**Problem.** c407 gave Vln II a slurred quaver arpeggio from the second quaver of each bar, so each bar beams five quavers as one group. These are the only five-note groups in the score, and they were added after c375's scan. Most are arches (B4–E5–B5–E5–B4 and similar), so the end notes lie nearest the beam. In v465:
- At 173–175, 177 and 179 the end stems are 2.74 spaces on notes in a space, and at 180 they are 2.74 on line notes. The inner stems in the same groups are 3.7–5.2. LilyPond shortens beamed stems, and neither c105's flat-beam branch nor c375's wrapper covers a single-voice group whose stems do not all point away from the centre.
- At 177 and 179 the result is a beam straddling the bottom stave-line. In the bars on either side the beam hangs below the stave (/tmp/critic-465/pages/vln2-7.png), so the beam line jumps up into the stave twice in eight bars.
- At 171 (F♯4–A4–D♯5–B4–A4) the majority rule gives up-stems. This is the only bar of the sixteen with stems up, so its beam and its slur change side (slur below), while the other fifteen bars keep the slur above.

**Reading.** PDF 32: "The stem length (including the width of the outer beam) should be as close to 3½ spaces (normal stem length) as possible", with 3¼ for a note on a line (PDF 33). A horizontal beam has no angle to excuse a shorter stem (PDF 35 allows 3 only for angled beams). PDF 39, visual considerations: "When groups within a passage together roughly span the width of the stave, visual considerations may suggest maintaining the same stem direction for the whole passage … this allows all slurs … to remain next to their noteheads." The passage spans F♯4–B5, the whole stave.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-465/work).**
- beams.ily: c375's minimum length now also applies to horizontal groups of three or more notes, whatever their stem direction. Sloped groups and pairs are unchanged. The one changed line in `gould-ledger-beam-positions`:
  ```scheme
  (if (or (and (any negative? ps) (or sloped (< (length stems) 3))) (<= deficit 0.15))   ; c465: horizontal groups of 3+ also
  ```
  A comment block above it gives the reason.
- tA.ily, bar 171 of `tAVlnTwo`: `\stemDown r8 fis'8( a'8 dis''8 b'8 a'8) \stemNeutral`, with a comment citing PDF 39.

**Result (probe logs compared, all 444 beams).** Exactly seven beams moved, all in Vln II, and no other beam changed:
- 173–175: −3.0 to −3.76, end stems 3.5.
- 177 and 179: −2.0 to −2.76, now below the stave like their neighbours, end stems 3.5.
- 180: −3.5 to −4.01, line notes at 3.25.
- 171: stems down with the slur above, beam −4.24→−3.76, rising with the outer interval F♯4→A4 (PDF 36), shorter stem 2.98 (PDF 35).

Compare /tmp/critic-465/pages/vln2-7.png (v465) with vln2-7new.png (scratch). Print only: the MIDI and analysis.txt are identical to v465's.
