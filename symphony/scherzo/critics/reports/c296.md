## Checked and kept (decided)

- **Pedal timing at 188–190.** The script confirms the c206 fix. B♯3 is last struck on 188 beat 3. Bar 189 beat 1 is free in both hands. B♮ is first needed on 189 beat 2. A♯ comes at 192, two bars after its mark at 190. Left foot (B) and right foot (A) never move together. Kept.
- **Low bass notes 81–84 (D2, B♭1, G1, A1, a dotted minim each).** The wound strings ring (Adler PDF 112–113). At 84 the struck A1 sounds against G1 and B♭1 if they are not damped. c206 kept this, with the reason that damping the previous bass string while placing the next is ordinary harp practice when the harmony changes, and here the hand has the whole bar to do it. I accept that reason. A printed mark on every bar would be clutter that Adler does not ask for (he asks only for marks the player cannot infer). Kept.
- **Close-position chords on beats 2–3 (153–168, 185–192, 225–236, 341–348).** Adler prefers wide three-note chords (PDF 114). Spreading them upward would put the top note in the solo's register, which c004, c028 and c100 ruled out. Spreading downward would put them among the wound strings under the pizz. basses (PDF 113). The chords sit in the "rich and warm" middle two octaves. Kept, as c026, c116 and c206 decided.
- **Repeated chords at 2. = 76 (crotchet = 228).** The same three strings are struck on beats 2 and 3, about 0.26 s apart, and beat 1 is free to replace the fingers. This is within normal technique, and the reading gives no limit on it. Kept.
- **Harmonics at 375 in unison with Hn 1's G♯4.** Adler asks for light accompaniment (PDF 116). Everything else sounding is ppp or pp, and the unison G♯4 only colours the horn. This is part of the protected closing page (357–376). Kept.
- **E major for the trio, a sharp key with short strings (PDF 114).** Adler adds that "a good harpist can make any key sound well". The key is fixed by the form and the cyclic plan. Kept.
