## Findings

### 1. Bars 329–332: Ob 2 joins Cl 1 on the motto's chromatic tail (applied)

**Problem.** At 329–332 the woodwinds carry the movement's last in-tempo statement of the motto tail at theme A's own pitch: Cl 1 plays G5–F♯5–F5–E5–D♯5 in dotted minims, p<, with the cor anglais an octave lower (c517). The strings' motive-x stretto crescendos from mf at the same time. Vln I plays G5–E5–E5–G5 | A5–F♯5–F♯5–A5, and Vln II plays E5–B4–B4–E5 | F♯5–D5–D5–F♯5 | F5–A4–A4–A♯4. Both violin sections sit in the clarinet's octave, and they play one level louder. c517 already noted that "Cl 1 G5 lay on Vln I's and Vln II's own staccato pitches". The cor anglais it added helps the lower octave, but the line at its cyclic pitch is still one clarinet inside two violin sections.

**Evidence from the reading.**
- PDF 274: keep the accompaniment "registrally distant from the soloist whenever possible … especially when dealing with woodwind solo instruments". Rhythmic contrast "lessens" this need but does not remove it. Here the register cannot move. G5–F♯5–F5–E5 is theme A bar 2 at pitch, which movement I (I 79) and the finale (IV 273–275) quote. The violins' build cannot move either, because Vln I rises in tenths over the bass E–D–C–B (c032, c033).
- PDF 257, guideline 4: "distinct color differentiation to feature soloist". PDF 257: all instruments except the piano "blend into the orchestra rather naturally". A clarinet in its clarino register blends with violins. An oboe does not.
- PDF 264 (Ex. XVII-9): a doubling in a contrasting colour does not weaken a line. Ob 2 + Cl 1 in unison over the cor anglais an octave lower gives the oboe-family octave that Rimsky-Korsakov calls always good (PDF 68, Ex. 60, c561's table).

**Cyclic reason.** This bar group is the scherzo's model for two later quotations. The finale's IV 272 recalls the F at 331 as IV6/4, and IV 273–275 then plays G5–F♯5–F5–E5 in Ob 1 + Ob 2 (with Vln II, Hn 1, violas), as I checked in v595. Movement I's I 79 gives the same notes to Ob 1 + Vln I. With the change, the scherzo's own last statement has oboe colour at the same pitch, so the listener can hear the link across movements I, III and IV by colour as well as by pitch.

**Fix (applied).** sections/coda.ily:
```
old: codaOboeTwo = { R2.*8 | e''4-.\ff b'8-. b'-. e''4-. | …
new: codaOboeTwo = { R2.*4 | g''2.\p\< | fis''2. | f''2 e''4 | dis''2. | e''4-.\ff b'8-. b'-. e''4-. | …
old: codaOboeOne = { R2.*8 | g''2.\ff | …
new: codaOboeOne = { R2.*4 | \partCombineSoloII R2.*4 | \partCombineAutomatic g''2.\ff | …
```
The rhythm, p< and lack of slur are those of Cl 1 (c050, c517). D♯5 resolves by step to Ob 2's own E5 at 333 (theme A's head). I chose Ob 2 rather than Ob 1. Ob 1 holds G5 at 333 (s125's layout), and D♯5→G5 would be a diminished fourth. Ob 1 also keeps its fresh ff entry at 333. Without the `\partCombineSoloII` the shared staff printed no player, so I added it and now "2." prints.

**Parts.** Ob 2 rested 306–332 and now enters at 329 after 22 bars, so I added a cue (c435) in parts-cues.json "04": `["cueVlnTwo","Vln. II","UP",327,2]`. Vln II sounds at 327–328 (E5 ×4 | G5 F♯5 F5). The cue prints correctly in the rebuilt Oboe 2 part. No existing cue points at the oboes, so no other cue changes.

**Does it undo an earlier fix?** It answers c517's and c393's build order, "flutes and oboes held back to 333 (PDF 172)". That order is about holding back the high register. Ob 2 at G5–D♯5 adds no new register: it sits at Cl 1's pitch, below Vln I. The flutes still enter first at 333, and Ob 1 still enters there on its own G5. c302 and c279 listed 329–332 and the coda stretto as do-not-touch with no stated reason. I leave their notes, harmony, dynamics and order unchanged. The F on beats 1–2 of 331 (IV 272) is unchanged.

**Why a listener hears it.** At 329–332 the crescendo is led by the violins' staccato quavers. The tail's held notes are the only long notes above the bass. With an oboe on them, the chromatic descent G–F♯–F–E–D♯ comes through the quavers instead of merging with them.

### 3. Bars 357–360: Vln II la metà a third under Hn 1's motto tail (considered, not applied)

Row 14 applies to the close as well. Vln II plays G♯4–G4–E4–E4 in the horn's dotted-minim rhythm a third under B4–A♯4–A4. I worked out a swap. The violas would take G♯3–G3–E3–E3, and Vln II would hold the violas' E4–E4–E4–B3. That opens the A♯°7 to E3–G3–C♯4–E4–A♯4. I did not build it, for two reasons. First, PDF 264 (Mozart) says a doubling in a vastly different colour leaves the solo intact. A half section of violins at pp under a horn at p< is that case, and c490 already applied PDF 274 here by halving the section. Second, c050's G♯→G in one voice, in the violins, repeats Vln I's G♯5→G5 at 1–7. That is the change of mode the movement opens with (c470). Moving it to the violas weakens that link more than it frees the horn.

### 4. Bars 153–160: harp off-beats a third under T's head (checked, kept)

The harp's off-beat E4/D♯4/F♯4 lies a third or sixth under Cl 1's G♯4–A4–B4 (T's head, which movement I relies on). PDF 274's own exception applies: plucked off-beats against a legato tune are a strong rhythmic contrast. PDF 278 (Verdi) asks for "very simple figures" here. This is the trio's reference sound (c090), which the coda's 341–348 recalls, and c527 kept it. Kept.

### 5. Bars 1–12 and 361–376: the frame (checked, follows)

The horn call is a single horn line over a bassoon pedal below its lowest note and a heterophonic violin G♯5 (PDF 264, 274). Its timpani answers set up "an atmosphere of expectation" (PDF 269). The close is the same frame: Hn 1 and Vln I on G♯, the timpani's motive x joining the phrases (PDF 273), harmonics a cappella. These are the movement's best cyclic bars, and nothing in this reading argues for a change.

### 6. Ob 1 instead of Ob 2 at 329 (considered, rejected)

Ob 1 would match the finale's IV 273 label. But it would leap D♯5→G5 (a diminished fourth) into its held G5 at 333, or it would need s125's oboe layout reversed, which brings back the noteheads that cross. The colour, not the player, makes the link. Ob 2 does this and resolves by step.
