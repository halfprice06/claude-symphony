## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Every phrase is four bars long; the retransition should change gear as Beethoven's does (89–100). Applied.
**Problem.** From bar 13 to bar 104 every group is 4 or 8 bars. That includes the fugato entries (57, 61, 65), the F-major episode and the retransition, where the head enters at 89, 93 and 97. Beethoven 9/ii, which the plan names as the model for motivic timpani, gets its tension in the development from "Ritmo di tre battute" (bar 177): the timpani strike on the first bar of each three-bar group, and four-bar groups return at bar 234. A conductor would notice that this scherzo never changes its bar grouping.
**Evidence.** In v19 the timpani play x at 89, 91, 93, 95 (two-bar), the head enters at 89/93/97 (four-bar), and the hemiola follows at 101. Rimsky's examples of a rhythm in the timpani that marks phrase groups: No. 70 (PDF 242–243) and No. 78 (PDF 251).
**Fix (applied, sB.ily, global.ily).**
- Head entries in three-bar groups: Vla 89–91 (unchanged); Vln II 92–94 `e''4-.\pp b'8-. b'-. e''4-. | a''2->( gis''8 g''8 | fis''4-.) r4 r4`; Vln I 95–97 `e''4-.\p b'8-. b'-. e''4-. | g''2->( fis''8 f''8 | e''4-.) r4 r4`.
- Vln I 98–100: `e''4-.\< b'8-. b'-. g''4-. | fis''4-. dis''-. b'-. | a''4-. fis''-. dis''-.`.
- Vln II 95–100 continue the B7 arpeggios. Vla crotchets on B from 92.
- Timpani x on each group downbeat (89, 92, 95), then every bar 98–100, roll at 101 as before.
- Marks: `\tempo \markup { "a tempo" \medium \italic "(ritmo di tre battute)" } \mt 264` at 89, and `\tempo \markup \medium \italic "ritmo di quattro battute" \mt 264` at 101, the hemiola. Tempo is unchanged; each new tempo word has its `\mt`.

The hemiola at 101–104 now completes a 3+3+3+3+4 span. Bar count is unchanged.

### 2. Hn 3·4 in bare fifths and fourths for the whole F-major episode (73–88). Applied.
**Problem.**
- Hn 3 C4 / Hn 4 F3 (73–74, 77, 80), D4/G3 (75–76) and C4/G3 (79) are open fifths in a same-colour pair, under a soft harp melody.
- In 85–88 the horns move to thirds, so the colour changes in mid-phrase.
- Rimsky gives soft horn pairs thirds and sixths (PDF 73–74; No. 76, PDF 247; No. 120, PDF 292–293). PDF 90 rule 4: concords to the same colour, discords to different colours. Bare horn fifths at pp also read as a horn call, which is not this passage's character.

**Fix (applied, sB.ily).**
- Hn 3 at 73–84: `a2. a2. bes2. bes2. c'2. d'2. bes2. a2. d'2. d'2. bes2. c'2.` (73 keeps `\pp` and "senza sord.").
- Hn 4 A3 at 77 and 84.
- Bsn 1 takes the chord fifth: D4 at 75, 76, 83; C4 at 79.

Every horn pair is now a third or sixth: F major A/F, G minor B♭/G, D minor D/F, C7/E B♭/G. The harmony is unchanged and no new C-major triad appears (cmajor.py: none).

### 3. The seventh of V7 doubled in the brass and left by leap (104, 308). Applied.
**Problem.** On the ff B7 at 104 the seventh A is in Hn 1 (A4) and Tpt 2 (A4), as well as in Fl 1, Cl 2, Vln I, Ob 1, Bsn 1 and Tbn 1. At 105 Hn 1 leaps A4→E5 and Tpt 2 falls A4→E4, so the strongest instruments carry the dissonance without resolving it.
- Hn 1 A4 over Hn 3 B3 is a seventh in one colour.
- Tpt 1 D♯5 over Tpt 2 A4 is a tritone in one colour.
- Rimsky PDF 102: sevenths and seconds go to different colours. PDF 81 and 83–84: resonance depends on correct part-writing, and dissonant chord members should not be doubled.

**Fix (applied, sB.ily and dB.ily).** At 104 and 308: Hn 1 `fis'2.\ff`, Hn 2 `dis'2.\ff`, Tpt 2 `fis'2.\ff`. The brass now holds B–D♯–F♯. The woodwinds, Vln I and Tbn 1 keep the A. Hn 1·2 and Tpt 1·2 keep equal simultaneous dynamics.

### 4. The ff tutti theme has a hollow middle and single-note string off-beats (105–120, 309–324). Applied.
**Problem.** In 105–120 the off-beats (beats 2–3) are Ob 2, Cl 1 (G4/A4) and violas on one note (B3, C4 or D♯4). Since v10, Vln II plays the tune, so the string section's harmony at ff is one viola note.
- Rimsky PDF 87: short forte string chords take double stops, supported by the winds. No. 97–99 (PDF 274–277) show off-beat double and triple stops in a tutti.
- PDF 86 and 96: no empty middle in forte.

**Fix (applied, sC.ily and dC.ily).**
- Viola off-beats as double stops: `r4 <b g'>4-. <b g'>4-.` (bars 1–3, 5, 8, 10, 13, 14, 16 of the section), `<fis' a'>` at 108/312, `<c' a'>` at 110, 113, 115 (and 314, 317, 319).
- Bars with D♯4 stay single.
- All are playable in 1st or 3rd position on adjacent strings.
- The G4/A4 doubles Ob 2 and Cl 1 in unison, which Rimsky PDF 83 allows ("notes in unison resulting from correct duplication need not be avoided").

### 5. Comparison with v09: nothing structural got worse; one texture got thicker, and that is acceptable. Not applied.
I diffed every section file. Changes since v09 that touch quality:
- the rising sixth in the horn call (11);
- T's third phrase to A5;
- the A-major first half of the coda T (349–356);
- Vln II octave doublings at 45, 121, 169, 209 and 357;
- Bsn 1 inner line 209–224.

The only measurable thickening is the trio return 209–224: 11.1 voices on average against 9.1 in v09, from the Vln II octave doubling and the divided violas. Rimsky PDF 76 warns that several winds in unison with one string group overpower it. Here Cl 1 doubles Vln II at the same pitch, one wind against one string group, so the strings still lead. This is forte by design. I keep it.

The v09 → v19 changes at the B6 peak (344), the general pause (348) and the close (365–384) touch neither the notes nor the register. The coda horn T (349) moved from sounding G♯4–E5 to C♯4–A4. That is written G♯4–E5 for horn in F, the middle register Rimsky recommends for soft horn phrases (PDF 72), so it is not a loss.

### 6. Beethoven comparison: where the score already matches a master's standard.
- **Timpani.** As in Beethoven 9/ii, the timpani state the rhythmic motive alone (9–12) and answer the theme (20, 28).
- **Fugato.** Strings enter one by one at pp (57–72) and build to an ff stretto, as Beethoven's opening fugato builds through the strings.
- **Phrase lengths.** The hemiola cadences (53–55, 129–131) give the irregular phrase-length moments that Beethoven gets from his three-bar groups. With finding 1, the development now has both.

No change is needed here.
