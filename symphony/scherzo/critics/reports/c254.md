### Considered, no change (my decisions)

- **Inverted string spacing (PDF 150–151, V-26).** `space.py` finds no string chord with a gap wider than an octave in its upper half and a narrow gap below. The wide upper gaps it lists (20, 28, 48–51, 65–70, 169–184, 197–199) are melody-over-bass textures, not chords: an octave-doubled tune over a bass with the harmony in winds or violas, which is principle 32's octave doubling, not a spacing fault. The full chords at 72 (A1–A2–E3 | E4–A4 | E5–C♯6 | E6, winds filling A3–A4) and 339 (E1–E2–B2 | G3–B3–E4–G4–B4–E5 | G5 | E6) are wider at the bottom, as the book asks. No change.
- **A6 at 50 before the B6 peak at 336 (PDF 129).** Vln I reaches G6/A6 at 46 and 50 in the second idea, again at 178–179 (mp), 218–219 (ff) and 350–351 (pp), and B6 only at 336. Haydn's lesson is about the octave transposition used once, at the end. Here the extreme (B6, the only fff, full brass, violins in four octaves) is still reserved for 336, and A6 at 50 belongs to the second idea's recorded contour. c074 and c166 kept it for the same reason. No change.
- **Viola off-beats on the cellos' tenor pitch at 106, 113–116 and 306, 313–316 (PDF 135).** `uni2.py` shows the violas' lower note (B3 or C4) attacked on beats 2 and 3 while the cellos hold the same pitch in the tenor countermelody. This is like colour in like register, the case PDF 135 warns about. But the countermelody is doubled by Bsn 1 and Hn 3·4 at ff, the violas' upper note carries the off-beat chord, and the tutti 105–120/305–320 is on the do-not-touch list (c214, c224). Moving the violas' lower note would also reopen the fifths c207 found at 112–113. The pitch repeated inside the held note reinforces the tenor line and does not cover it. No change.
- **Viola pizz. off-beats on the cellos' sustained pitch at 253, 256, 262–263, 266 (PDF 135).** The cellos' arco sostenuto line holds B3 (or C4, F♯3) while the violas pluck the same pitch on beats 2 and 3. Plucked against bowed is a change of colour, which is the remedy the book gives, and the reprise is recoloured on purpose (principle 17). No change.
- **Cello and bass pizzicato on every downbeat 225–240 (PDF 154, V-29: thinning for the diminuendo).** Vc E3 and Cb E2 pluck the tonic (A at the plagal bars) under the codetta for sixteen bars, with the harp's left hand. Tchaikovsky drops the bass out at the tonic to score the diminuendo. Here the thinning is already done by the harp, which goes from three to two to one attack per bar (c153), by the solo winds handing T's head down Ob 1 → Cl 1 → Bsn 1, and by the poco rit. The pizzicato E is the pedal that holds the codetta on the tonic, and the timpani roll takes it over at 237. Taking the cellos out at 233 would leave Bsn 1's T head in the same register as Hn 4's E3 with no plucked bass under it. No change.
- **Theme A's dips at 16 and 32 without a cello doubling (PDF 141, V-19).** Shostakovich's layout (tune in Vln I, off-beats in Vln II and violas, downbeats in Vc and Cb) is the layout of 13–28. In V-19 the cellos join when the tune goes low; here the dip lasts one bar on a half cadence, and the cellos would have to leave the bass B. c207 decided this; the closes 137–138 and 321–324 already give the low head to the cellos. No change.
- **Double bass use (PDF 130–131, Ex. V-6).** Adler's "+Bass / −Bass" plan matches the score: the basses are out for the fugato subjects (57–64), at the sub. p second idea (121–124, 277–280), and for the strings-free F episode; under the soft trio statements they only pluck downbeats with the harp, and they return for the dominant pedal 89–104, the tutti and every cadence. Cb plays no double stop anywhere; at 339 it has one E1 (PDF 154). No change.
- **Four colourings of T (PDF 133–135, 152–154).** Cl 1 over harp at G♯4 (153), Vln I sul A with Vln II an octave below and divisi violas in a pale register (169), the forte return with Fl 1 held back until 217 (209), and the coda's hand-over Hn 3 → Ob 1 + Bsn 1 → Vln I/II + Cl 1 (341–356). This is the chapter's Brahms lesson (save the brighter colour) and its Tchaikovsky lesson (move the melody's doubling from part to part) together. No change.
