## Findings

### 1. The shared checker coherence/themecheck.py ignores "abs", so the scherzo's at-pitch links are never checked at pitch by the other threads (tool and messages; tested)

- **Problem.** Since c260, themes-scherzo.ly marks seven lines "abs": the horn call (1), theme A bar 2 at G5 (14, 106, 254, 306), the closing motive x (363, 367, 371), and the last G♯ in Hn 1 (368) and Vln I (369). Only kit/themecheck_abs.py reads that flag, and only kit/promote.sh runs it. coherence/themecheck.py is the checker named in every critic task and in the other threads' records. Its `%@` pattern drops the word "abs". Its `shape()` compares intervals modulo 12, so a one-note line such as the last G♯ is checked for presence only.
- **Evidence (tested).** In a scratch copy (/tmp/critic-350/abstest) I changed Vln I's last G♯5 at 369 to A5. coherence/themecheck.py printed "theme record: 52/52 unchanged". kit/themecheck_abs.py printed "CHANGED lastGsharpVln VlnOne 369 … record: G#5/3, version: A5/3". The finale's bar 1 (Hn 1 A♭4, Vln I A♭5), its IV 243 (Vln I G♯5→G5), movement I's bar 79 and IV 273–275 all depend on exact pitch in a named instrument. Rimsky PDF 20: the colour is "inseparable" from the work, so the link is the pitch in that instrument, not the contour. A check that cannot see a transposition does not protect it.
- **Fix (tool, composer).** Copy kit/themecheck_abs.py over symphony/coherence/themecheck.py. Its import path already points at symphony/coherence, so no edit is needed. Lines without "abs" behave exactly as before: finale 39/39, movement I 35/35, scherzo 52/52, all tested with the abs version. Then merge lyparse-mvt2.patch into coherence/lyparse.py, which leaves all four records unchanged.
- **Message (finale, movement I and movement II threads):** "coherence/themecheck.py now honours 'abs' on %@ lines (exact pitch). Before this, a record line marked abs, or a one-note line, passed whatever the pitch. Records that protect a pitch in a named instrument should add 'abs': for example finale.md's IV 1 A♭4/A♭5 and IV 243, and movement II's lastAflat/lastC (finding 3)."

## Weighed and kept

- **5–7, the horn call's D♯4 left unresolved when the clarinets enter (c040).** Rimsky PDF 126–127 asks for a regular progression of parts across a change of group. The note on PDF 127 allows an exception where a strong contrast is meant. Here the reason is stronger still: the finale resolves this note at IV 219–220. Kept.
- **369 and 375, additions inside the closing diminuendo.** PDF 127 says a diminuendo is made by dropping groups. The Vln I G♯5 and the harp chord at 375 are the notes the finale's bar 1 takes up in the same instruments (PDF 125–126). The cyclic reason is the stronger one. Kept (c050, c150, c250).
- **172–184, Fl 1 answers after c349.** They follow the law of register (PDF 128): higher imitation, higher instrument. They are not echoes: the rhythm is new, and at 176 and 183 so is the pitch level. They double no note of T. Kept.
