## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 51-55: in the render, the tune's lower octave (Vln II) fades to pp through the G-major cadence. Applied.
**Problem.** Vln II plays the second idea an octave under Vln I (c009). The swell at 49-52 (`\<` at 49, `\>` at 50, `\!` at 52) is the same as in Vln I. In LilyPond's MIDI, though, a decrescendo closed by a bare `\!` runs to the next printed dynamic in that part. For Vln II the next one is the p of the fugato at 65. The render therefore plays Vln II at velocity 53 for 52-55, while Vln I is at 83 and the print says f for both. For Vln I the next printed dynamic is f, so it is unaffected. The cadence of the second idea (52-55, the hemiola 53-55) loses its lower octave in every render Dan hears.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 159, first consideration: "The melody notes should be more prominent than the harmony notes". The render puts Vln II's tune below the violas (62) and the bass (75). PDF 164 (Brahms 3/i): the tune of the first and second violins is not overshadowed.
**Fix (sA.ily, sAVlnTwo, bar 52).** `fis''4\! e''4. d''8)` becomes `fis''4-\tweak stencil ##f \f e''4. d''8)`. This is the hidden target the score already uses at 104 and 304 (`-\tweak stencil ##f \ff`), and nothing is printed. After the change, Vln II plays 83 at 52-55, the same as Vln I.

### 2. Bars 263-268: in the render, the flute's heterophony ends its swell at mf and plays over the principal voice. Applied.
**Problem.** c069 marked Vln I mp and Fl 1 p, so that the theme stays in front of the flute's decoration an octave above (E6-A6). Both parts swell at 263-264 and close with a bare `\!` at 265. In the MIDI, the flute's hairpins run toward its next printed dynamic, the f at 273, so it plays 265-268 at 58-62 (mf). Vln I's run toward its own next dynamic, the p at 271, so it drops to 53-57. In the render the subsidiary voice, in the flute's most piercing octave, is louder than the theme for four bars. The print is right; the render reverses it.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 184 (Schoenberg): "principal gestures were to be played louder than the subordinate ones", and a subsidiary idea "in any kind of piercing register" is pp. PDF 162-163: a high flute at piano "always sounds louder than it should".
**Fix (dA.ily, bar 265).** dAFluteOne: `e'''4-.\! b''8-.` becomes `e'''4-.-\tweak stencil ##f \p b''8-.`. dAVlnOne: `e''4-.\! b'8-.` becomes `e''4-.-\tweak stencil ##f \mp b'8-.`. Nothing is printed. After the change the flute is at 37-41 and Vln I at 63-67 for 265-268, the levels c069 wrote.

### 3. Bars 353-356: in the render, the top of the trio tune's last cadence drops below the accompaniment. Applied.
**Problem.** In the coda's last statement of T, Vln I's swell (349-352) closes with `\!` at 353, and c098's softer resolution at 355 closes with `\!` on the D♯. Vln I's next printed dynamic is the ppp at 369, so in the MIDI both hairpins run toward ppp. The top line of the movement's last 4-3 cadence (the cadential 6/4 at 353-354, V7 at 355, E at 356) plays at 31-35. Below it, Vln II plays at 41-45, the violas at 37 and the pizzicato basses at 50. The print says pp for all of them, with the tune on top.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 159 (melody more prominent than harmony). PDF 164: "the violins ... are able to play softly on any note high or low". The violins can keep the tune on top at pp; only the MIDI loses it.
**Fix (coda.ily, codaVlnOne).** At 353, `gis''4\!( b'' e'''` becomes `gis''4-\tweak stencil ##f \pp( b'' e'''`. At 355, `dis'''\!` becomes `dis'''-\tweak stencil ##f \pp`. Nothing is printed. After the change, Vln I plays 41 at 353-356, the level of Vln II. The closing page's notes and printed marks are unchanged (do-not-touch 357-376 is not affected; these bars are 353-356).

### 4. Bars 45-56: the violas play their inner line at f, a level above the rest of the harmony. Applied.
**Problem.** In the G-major second idea, the harmony layer is Cl 1·2 mf and Hn 1·2 mf. c084 lowered the horns to mf so that they do not cover the tune's lower octave. The violas carry their f from the off-beats at 41 into this layer (`g'2( g'4~` at 45 has no dynamic). They are a full section, legato, in G4-A4, directly under Vln II's and Ob 1's lower octave, D5-A5. They are marked at the level of the tune (Vln I and II f) and a level above the instruments they share the harmony with.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 159 (melody notes more prominent than harmony notes). PDF 184: the subordinate parts are marked softer than the principal voice. PDF 179: the background is the sustained, softer layer. This is the same reasoning c084 applied to the horns.
**Fix (sA.ily, sAVla, bar 45).** `g'2( g'4~` becomes `g'2(\mf g'4~`. The mf is printed under the viola staff at 45. The hemiola cadence 53-55 keeps c085's louré bowing. The reprises at 121 and 277 already start sub. p in all parts and are unchanged.
