## Findings

1. **Trio codetta 225–236: three identical bass groups. Applied.** Bars: 229–232. The codetta repeats one 4-bar group three times, passing T's head from Ob 1 (225) to Cl 1 (229) and Bsn 1 (233). Each group has the same pizz. bass, E–E–A–E (I–I–IV–I), on the root. The only harmonic change is iv in the third group. A&S PDF 107 (Ex. 7-2) gives the remedy for exactly this case: moving the bass between root and 3rd "creates a melodic activity in the bass that makes it possible to continue the same harmony without monotony". PDF 108 (Ex. 7-4a) names parallel 10ths between the outer voices as the most important such voice leading. PDF 110 (Ex. 7-8b) allows the bass to reach I6 by a falling sixth. PDF 108 §4 allows any tone of I6 to be doubled. c133 named the codetta as the trio's one static place, and c153 answered that by thinning the harp. This answers it by the reading's own means, without a cut and without renumbering. The fix (tC.ily, bar 230 only):
   - `tCVc` bar 230: `e4 r4 r4` → `gis,4 r4 r4`
   - `tCCb` bar 230: `e,4 r4 r4` → `gis,,4 r4 r4`
   - `tCHarpL` bar 230: `e,4 r4 r4` → `gis,,4 r4 r4`

   The second group becomes I–I6–IV–I, with the bass E2–G♯1–A1–E2. Its G♯–A runs in compound 10ths under Cl 1's B4–C♯5. The first group stays on the root and the third keeps its iv, so the three groups are root, I6 and minor colour instead of three times the same thing. Hn 4's E3 pedal, the violas' G♯3 and the IV IV iv plan (c173) are unchanged. No C-major triad. The harp pedal is already G♯. Applied in /tmp/critic-343/work.

2. **Theme A's PACs every eight bars (20, 28, 112, 120, 260, 268, 312, 320). Kept.** PDF 115 forbids a cadential effect in the *middle* of a phrase. Every one of these V–i arrivals ends an 8-bar phrase (13–20 HC/PAC, 21–28 iv–V–PAC), which is the sample melody's own plan (PDF 117). I tried the reading's remedy, I6 at 312, to run 305–320 as one span. It creates the V7–I6 hidden octaves on G that PDF 109 (Ex. 7-7) forbids: tenor A3→G3 against bass B→G. The V4/2 route (PDF 117, Ex. 8-8) would double the 7th with the tenor countermelody's A3. Kept.

3. **Twenty bars on E before the trio (133–152). Kept.** PDF 102 explains why a held chord loses accent. PDF 112 requires root position when a chord carries a whole section, and the bass E is in root position here. The span's melodic content is the cellos' motive x (133–139) and Hn 1's G→G♯–A–G♯ (141–149). Dvořák's equivalent passage is longer. c153, c193, c243, c253 and c313 protected it, and the reading gives no stronger reason against it.

4. **Develop theme A by inversion over the expanded V (Kuhnau, PDF 94)? Considered and rejected.** Kuhnau expands his V with his opening melody inverted, and Dan wants the themes to develop. At 89–100 or 245–252, inverting the head would either invert bar 2, the motto's chromatic tail, which the cyclic rules say to quote and not develop, or invert only motive x's fourth (E–A–A–E), which is too small to read as development. The retransition works because the head returns in its own shape before the reprise. That anticipation is the expanded V's formal job (PDF 95, Ex. 6-4).

5. **The link's E major is confirmed by mode change, not by its V (141–152). Kept.** PDF 93 says only V confirms a tonic, and Ex. 8-3a / 8-11 offer the textbook V6/5 neighbour. At 147 beat 3 it would sit under Hn 1's A, with bass clarinet D♯2 and Bsn 1 F♯4. I did not apply it. A D♯→E resolution in the bass under the pivot G♯ would answer the horn call's D♯, which is left unresolved at 5–6 because the finale owns the D♯→E resolution (c030, cyclic-notes). The trio's first V7–I comes at 155–156.

6. **F-major episode without a V (73–88). Kept**, for c253's reason: V of F is the banned C-major triad, and the episode is a ♭II parenthesis inside the approach to the B pedal (88→89).

7. **Proportions and length. Kept.** The trio is 0.80 of the first scherzo, the reprise 0.56 and the coda 0.51 (Dvořák 9/iii: shorter trio and coda). 4:38 is the shortest of the four movements. A reprise cut (c333) or a codetta cut would renumber 241–376, which movement I and the finale cite, and the reading gives no reason that outweighs that.
