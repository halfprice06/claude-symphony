## Findings

### 1. The parts break Gould's beam rules that the score follows (applied)

**Bars and parts.** Flute 1 part 253–267 (all ledger-line groups, e.g. 261–263). Theme A's A5–F♯5 pair at 24, 116, 264 and 316 in the parts of Vln I, Vln II, Fl 1, Fl 2, Ob 1, cor anglais and Cl 2. The trio call's turn F♯5–E5–D♯5–E5 in the Oboe 1 part at 185 and 197.

**Problem.** The parts are built from parts-gen.py's template, which never includes `gould-ledger-beam-positions`, so the parts keep LilyPond's default beams. The probe of the v195 parts found:
- Fl 1 255, 259, 261, 262, 263: beams flat on the middle line (position 0.0) with stems of 4.2–6.7 spaces. The convex groups at 259 (A6 down to E6) and 263 (rising to A6 and back) are horizontal instead of sloped ¼ or ½.
- Theme A beat 2 at 24/116/264/316: slope 0.62 over notes 2.0–2.9 spaces apart (positions −0.19 → −0.81) in seven parts.
- Ob 1 185 and 197: the concave turn slopes 0.19 (−1.81 → −2.00).

**Reading.** Gould PDF 35: "When all the notes of a beam fall outside the first ledger line, the beam takes only a slight slope ... Intervals of a second take a slope of ¼ stave-space; all wider intervals take a slope of ½ stave-space". The example on that page puts the beam inside the stave just under the top line, not on the middle line. PDF 33: one clear stave-line between the innermost beam and the first ledger line, with stems near 3½ spaces (PDF 32). PDF 34: "Notes spaced very close together horizontally (closer than three spaces) take only a slight angle (¼ or ½ space) regardless of the interval." PDF 36 (c): the beam is horizontal when an inner note is closer to the beam than either outer note. PDF 37: a convex group takes a sloping beam. These are the rules c015 and c105 applied to the score (HISTORY, cycles 15 and 105).

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-195/work).**
- New file `beams.ily`, holding the c015/c105 block moved unchanged from score.ly (the comments, `c105-near-ys`, `c105-repeated?`, `gould-ledger-beam-positions`), under a c195 header comment.
- score.ly: the block is replaced by
  ```
  % c015/c105 beam callback (Gould PDF 31-37): see beams.ily (c195: shared with the parts)
  \include "beams.ily"
  ```
  The score's `\override Beam.positions = #gould-ledger-beam-positions` in the Voice context stays.
- parts-gen.py template: after `\include "stems.ily"` add `\include "beams.ily"`, and the Voice context becomes
  `\context { \Voice \consists #middleLineStemEngraver \remove "Dynamic_engraver" \remove "Dynamic_align_engraver" \override Beam.positions = #gould-ledger-beam-positions }`.

**Result.** I re-ran the probe on the rebuilt parts. Fl 1 261 is at (1.24, 1.24), 262 at (1.24, 1.0) and 263 at (1.24, 1.0): the beams hang under the top line with a ¼ slope. I checked this at 250 dpi (/tmp/critic-195/cmp261.png, old above, new below). Theme A beat 2 is now at 0.52 in every part, and the Ob 1 turn at 185/197 is horizontal. The only remaining flags are stems of 2.93 spaces on angled pairs, the same as in the score (row 12). The score is unaffected: pages 1, 9 and 13 are pixel-identical to v195, and the text is identical. All 29 part PDFs keep their page counts, so no page turn moved.

No other finding. On this second pass the score follows every principle in PDF 31–41 that applies to it. The callback, stems.ily and the default 3/4 beaming leave no group to correct.

### Checked and kept (decided, no change)

1. **Angled stems of 2.92 spaces inside the stave** at 23, 76, 115, 263, 315 and 354 (A5–G5 type pairs). Gould PDF 35 allows 3. The 0.08-space shortfall comes from LilyPond's sit position (0.18 instead of 0.24). Forcing 3.0 would put an end in mid-space and break PDF 34, which matters more. Keep.
2. **Fl 1 / Vln I 38 and harp RH 74, B5–A5 sloped 0.18.** A5 is on the first ledger line, not beyond it, so PDF 35's ¼ rule does not strictly apply, and 0.18 is the attached position nearest ¼. Keep.
