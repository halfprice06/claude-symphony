## What else I studied

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The second idea's playing word is missing on the staves that join or double the tune at 45, 125 and 281 (applied)

Problem: the second idea is marked "espress." where it first appears in each statement: Vln I/II at 45 and 121, and Fl 1/Ob 1 at 277 (c358). The instruments that double it or take it over were not marked:
- 45: Fl 1 and Ob 1 double Vln I/II (D6/D5) at mf with no word. Vln II also has no dynamic at 45, though every other playing staff at C restates one (Vln I f, Vla mf, Vc/Cb f).
- 125: Fl 1 and Ob 1 join the violins' second phrase at pp with no word.
- 281: Vln I, Vln II and Vc take the second phrase from Fl 1/Ob 1 with no word. The cellos leave the bass line for the tune at this point.

The parts show the gap. The Fl 1 part has the tune three times: at 45 (mf, no word), at 125 (pp, no word) and at 277 (sub. p espress.). In the Vln I, Vln II and Vc parts, 281 is the only statement of the tune without the word.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 8: give equal attention to the vertical and the horizontal lines, and look at the music through the player's eyes. Ex. 6 (PDF 11): marks are restated in every staff that plays the line.

Fix (applied):
- sA `sAFluteOne` `d'''2->(\mf^\markup \italic "espress."\< b''4~`; `sAOboeOne` `d''2->(\mf^\markup \italic "espress."\< b'4~`; `sAVlnTwo` `d''2->(\f^\markup \italic "espress."\<\upbow`.
- sC `sCFluteOne` `c'''2->(\pp^\markup \italic "espress."\< a''4~`; `sCOboeOne` adds `^\markup \italic "espress."` after its "1.".
- dA `dAVlnOne` `r8 d'''8(\p\upbow^\markup \italic "espress."`; `dAVlnTwo` likewise on `d''8`; `dAVc` `c'2->\<(^\markup \italic "espress." a4~`.

Only the print changes: the MIDI is the same, and no level changes except Vln II at 45, which was already f from 41.

### 2. Playing words on inner staves are struck through by the group's span bar (applied)

Problem: where a text on a staff inside a group overhangs a bar line, the span bar runs through it. My scan found 12 places, and I checked each at 200 dpi:
- "espress." at 45 (Vln II), 121 (Vln II) and 277 (Ob 1, through the full stop);
- "senza sord., dolce" at 73 (Hn 3·4, between "sord.," and "dolce");
- "non arpegg." at 81, 185 and 225 (harp);
- "non div." at 105, 300 and 339;
- "arco, espress." at 253 (Vc);
- "scherz." at 269 (Bsn).

c268 fixed the same fault at 376 for one text. The rest remain.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 7 (Ex. 6): bar lines are continuous within each family, so the span bars must stay. PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12): anchored markings must not collide. The text has to give way, as c268 decided.

Fix (applied, score.ly, after the Staff context line):
`\context { \Staff \override TextScript.whiteout = #1 \override TextScript.whiteout-style = #'box \override TextScript.layer = #3 }`

Spacing is unchanged. A pixel comparison against v368 shows changes only at those bar lines and at the texts added in finding 1. A rescan finds no struck text. The parts are not affected, because they have no span bars. I first tried `'outline`, but it left the line visible between letters.

### 3. Bar 176: Fl 1's second answer has no level after a three-bar multi-rest (applied)

Problem: Fl 1 answers the cellos' trio tune at 172 (p dolce), 176 and 183–184. c358 added p at 183 after its six-bar rest. At 176 the part shows the multi-rest "173–175" followed by the entry with no dynamic.

Evidence: MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10) shows this case: a three-bar multi-rest "9–11", then an entry marked pp.

Fix (applied, tA `tAFluteOne`): `r4 dis'''8(\p e'''8 fis'''4)`.
