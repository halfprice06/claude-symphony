## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 202–203: the top voice's ♭6 does not reach 5. Applied.

**Problem.** At 201–202 the trio's middle turns A major → A minor (iv, mixture) before the B pedal at 203. The chromatic line C♯4–C4–B3 is correct in Bsn 1 and Hn 4. But the top voice of the texture at 202 is Vln I C5 (flutes, oboes and Cl 1 rest), and it stops on that ♭6. The next top note is D♯4, at 203, in other instruments. The most exposed ♭6 in the trio is left in the air, and only an inner voice an octave lower resolves it.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 388: "♭6 is strongly active in the direction of 5. Once introduced, it tends to move to 5." PDF 395, Points for Review 3. Ex. 23-2a–e (PDF 388) resolve ♭6 in the voice that carries it. The trio's other ♭6 (C5 in the harp at 235) and the coda's ♭6 (violas C4 at 359) both resolve in their own register, so 202 is the exception.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-070/work/sections/tB.ily).** `tBVlnOne`, bar 203:
`… a'4--\p r4 r4 | c''4-- r4 r4 | R2. | R2. | …` → `… a'4--\p r4 r4 | c''4-- r4 r4 | b'4-- r4 r4 | R2. | …` (one R2. fewer after it, so the part keeps its length).

Vln I C5→B4 moves in contrary motion to the bass A→B, so the outer voices have no parallels. It doubles the C–B of Bsn 1 and Hn 4 an octave higher. That octave doubling is of a line and is not an independent parallel. I gave Vln II nothing at 203: its E4→F♯4 would make fifths with the bass A1→B1, and D♯4 would be a fourth leading tone. The analyser output did not change (7 parallels, the same bars).

### 2. Movement I's and movement II's records still describe an older scherzo. Documents only.

**Problem.** Three coherence files that the scherzo thread does not own disagree with the score and with scherzo.md and finale.md:
- themes-mvt1.md, row mvtOneHornCall: "Scherzo Hn 1 3–7 (G♯–E–D♯–E)".
- themes-mvt1.ly, line 56: "scherzo bars 3–7 Hn 1 G#–E–D#–E (3–1–7–1 in E major)". Since v31 the call is G♯3–E4–D♯4 with D♯4 held through 5–6 and no resolution. Since v41 bar 7 has no E4. The finale's Hn 3 D♯4→E4 at IV 219–220 depends on this.
- themes-mvt1.md, row mvtOneMottoTail, and themes-mvt1.ly line 129 give the scherzo's motto tail as "Hn 1 365–368". mvt2.md line 185 does the same ("The scherzo answers it with B–A♯–A–G♯ in E (365–368)"). Since v24 the tail is at 357–360.

A later thread that checks its quotations against these records could "restore" a resolution the scherzo deliberately withholds, or look for the tail at the wrong bars.

**Evidence.** BRIEF, "Coherence across movements": report any mismatch as a finding. In mixture terms the unresolved D♯ matters to the arc. D♯ is E♭, the ♭3 of C, the pitch of the symphony's E♭/E cross relation. A&S PDF 394–395 (Ex. 23-13, Brahms 3/i): a deliberate major/minor cross relation can be "an important feature" of a whole work, as long as it is kept consistent.

**Fix.** The composer sends the movement I and movement II owners (through the coordinator) these replacements:
- "Scherzo Hn 1 3–6 (G♯3–E4–D♯4, the 7 held and unresolved, as in movement I; no E4 at 7–8)".
- "Hn 1 357–360 (B–A♯–A–G♯)".

No scherzo notes change.

### 3. 358–359: Vln II's ♭3 leaps to 1. Considered; no change.

**Problem.** In the plagal close, Vln II goes G♯4–G4–E4 (357–359). A&S's model for ♭3 in major is the chromatic passing line ♮3–♭3–2 (Ex. 23-4, PDF 389). Here the ♭3, which is also the seventh of A♯°7, leaps down a third.

**Decision.** No change, for three reasons:
- The harmony E – A♯°7 – iv6/4 – E is on the do-not-touch list, and the finale and movement II rhyme with it.
- The only stepwise continuations are worse. A4 makes a unison with Hn 1's A4, which c042 removed for good reason. F♯4 turns 359 into iiø4/2 and changes the protected harmony.
- The ♭3 G does reach the tonic's region, and the ♮6–♭6–5 line (violas C♯–C–B) carries the mixture.

I record it here so later critics do not reopen it.

### 4. The III→IV join repeats the I→II join at the thread pitch. Documents only.

**Problem.** The scherzo's last chord (375) has exactly one E4: the harp's harmonic (written E3, sounding E4). The finale's bar 1 has E♭4 in the violas (div., with F♯4), Vln II, Cl 2 and Hn 2. So the join moves E4 → E♭4 at the thread pitch, in the same register as I→II (violas E4 at I 182 → E♭4 at II 2). scherzo.md and finale.md mention only the cellos' E2 → E♭3. A later cycle could revoice the harp chord and lose this without knowing it was there.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 387: one degree in two forms (E and E♭ as 3 of C) for juxtaposition. PDF 394–395 (Brahms 3/i): the cross relation as a structural motto.

**Decision.** No notes change. I tested the idea of giving the E4 to the violas as a pizzicato at 375 and rejected it, for three reasons: the movements are separated by a pause, the harp already sounds the pitch, and a viola note would thicken the chord just before the lone G♯. The fix is a sentence for scherzo.md (Ending) and for the finale thread: "375: the harp harmonic E4 is the scherzo's last E4; the finale's E♭4 (violas, Vln II, Cl 2, Hn 2) answers it at the thread pitch, as movement II's violas answer movement I's E4."
