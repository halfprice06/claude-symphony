## Findings

### 1. Bars 193–196: the strings pass the call's turn among themselves while Cl 1 holds; let the clarinet answer it a bar later (applied)

**Problem.** From 185 the trio's middle is built on one figure: the oboe's call opens with a turn, crotchet and four quavers (E–F♯–E–D♯–E, c021). The cellos answer it at 189. At 193–196 the violas carry the turn alone, on A at 193 (A–B–A–G♯–A) and on B at 195 (B–C♯–B–A–B), with a held bar after each. Above them Vln I and II rise in octaves to the forte at 197, where Ob 1 and Fl 1 bring the call back. In the bars where the violas hold (194, 196), Cl 1 also holds: C♯5 through 194, A4 through 196. So in the four bars of crescendo the turn sounds twice, in one inner string voice, then two bars of nothing but held notes. The call comes back at 197 without having been passed round.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 253–254 (Eroica, Ex. VIII-20): a little gesture passes from instrument to instrument across winds and strings, and then a cadential tutti sums it up. The tutti at 197 is ready, but the gesture before it stays in one string section. PDF 271–272 (Mozart 38/iii, Ex. VIII-42): oboe and bassoons imitate each other a bar apart over the strings. Adler's reason: because each woodwind has its own colour, wind counterpoint, "especially rhythmical", works well. The rendered example shows exactly this layout: one instrument plays the figure while the other holds, then they swap. Bars 193–196 already have the holds in the right places for this. Only the second voice is missing. The score already uses wind imitation in the trio: Fl 1 echoes T's head under the cellos at 172, 176 and 183. The middle section, the one built on imitation (call, answer), has none in the winds.

**Fix (applied, sections/tB.ily, tBClOne):**

```
old: ais'2.(\pp | a'2\p\< cis''4~) | cis''2. | b'2. | a'2. | gis'2(\f ...
new: ais'2.(\pp | a'2\p\< cis''4~) | cis''4( dis''8 cis''8 b'8 cis''8 | b'2.) | b'4( cis''8 b'8 a'8 b'8) | gis'2(\f ...
```

Cl 1 now answers each viola turn a bar later in the call's rhythm. At 194 it turns on C♯5 (C♯–D♯–C♯–B–C♯) over the F♯-minor bar. At 196 it plays the violas' 195 turn exactly, an octave up (B4–C♯–B–A–B), over the V7. The listener hears the turn in violas, clarinet, violas, clarinet, a bar apart, rising under the violins into the forte call at 197. The harmony, the sequence c302 protects, the violin line, the dynamics (p< to f), the slur break and Cl 2's breath at 196 (c396) are all unchanged. The new notes are neighbour and passing notes on weak quavers. The seventh A (c032) now sounds on beat 3 of 196, together with Vln II and Hn 1 (c172), and falls to G♯ at 197 in those voices. Cl 1 goes B4→G♯4 into T. On beat 1 of 196 the chord is V with the suspended sixth G♯ (Vln I/II, Hn 1, Bsn 1) over B. Cl 1's B4 is the root, so it no longer holds A4 against G♯4 in the same register. That also removes a semitone rub of the kind c039 and c129 removed elsewhere. The only new rubs are quaver neighbours against the violins' chord tones on beat 3 (194 B4 against Vln II C♯5; 196 A4 then B4 against Vln II A4), as in Mozart's example. Cl 1 doubles Vln II's C♯5→B4 at 194–195 as it did before, so no new parallels appear (analysis.txt unchanged). Written for clarinet in A the turns lie at E5–F♯5 and D5–E5, in the clarion.

**Why I kept it.** c302's do-not-touch for 185–196 covers the diatonic sequence, and its notes and bass are untouched. No other rule or protection covers Cl 1's inner line at 194 and 196. Nothing is silenced or moved, so parts-cues.json needs no change.

### 2. Bars 225–236: the codetta's solos are a relay, not an overlapping imitation (checked, kept)

Ob 1 (225), Cl 1 (229) and Bsn 1 (233) each state T's head in four bars, one after another. Mozart's model (PDF 271–272) would have each solo hold while the next one enters. But c423 protects "nothing held above the codetta's wind solos". Holding the oboe over Cl 1's entry would put a stronger colour over the clarinet. Adler's own warning about enclosure applies (PDF 256: lack of carrying power in some registers). The third statement is already changed (C♮ in Bsn 1 at 235, PDF 255). No change.

### 3. Bars 29–34: Fl 1 and Ob 1 in unison on the call (checked, kept)
