## Findings

### 1. Bar 7 resolves the horn call's D♯4 to E4 at pitch, and closes the E♭/E thread three movements early (applied)

**Bars:** 5–7, Cl 1·2.

**Problem.** The horn call holds D♯4 in Hn 1 through 5–6 and stops on beat 2 of bar 6. Four quavers later, on the downbeat of 7, Cl 2 enters pp on E4 (`introClTwo = { R2.*6 | e'2.~\pp | …`), under Cl 1's G4. At pp in a three-voice texture, the ear hears D♯4 → E4 in the same octave: the call is resolved, one beat late, by another instrument.

Three other movements depend on this D♯4 staying open:
- finale.md (v61): "Hn 3's D♯4→E4 also resolves the scherzo horn call's unresolved D♯4 (scherzo 5–6) at its own pitch" (IV 219–220).
- scherzo.md lists the held D♯4 under "What the finale relies on".
- The intro's own comment says the call is "left on its 7th over V, unresolved".

There is a second link. Movement II's last chord has Clarinet 2 on E♭4 "morendo" (mvt2.md, last-chord table). The first note Cl 2 plays in the scherzo is E4. So across the pause, the same player moves E♭4 → E4: this is the E♭/E thread that the brief says "is not closed here: the finale's violas still close it", at the thread's own pitch E4 (movement I's violas E♭4→E4 at I 179–182, the finale's violas at IV 219–220).

**Evidence.**
- Adler ch. 15 (adler2 PDF 188): the same line an octave higher or lower is a different thing, because register changes its character. Here, the octave of the E decides whether the D♯ resolves.
- Adler ch. 1 (adler1 PDF 22): once it matters which instrument plays a note, the assignment is the composer's responsibility. The choice of Cl 2 on E4 is not neutral here.
- Movement I does what the scherzo's comment says. After the call's B3 (I 14), bar 15 has no C4 in the horn's register (Vla E♭4, Vln II G4). C sounds only in the bass, so the 7th meets its tonic only in another octave.
- Reduced to a piano reduction (Adler, adler1 PDF 23), v40's 5–7 reads D♯4–E4 in one voice.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-040/work/sections/intro.ily).** I swapped the clarinets: Cl 1 takes E5 and Cl 2 takes G4. The chord is still E minor: E5, G4, B3 (Bsn 1, Hn 2) over pizz. E. The G♮ that turns the key to minor now sits at the pivot's own pitch (G♯4 at 1–2 → G4 at 7). The D♯4 is left without an E4 at pitch, and E sounds only in the bass and an octave higher, as in movement I.
```
introClOne = { R2.*6 | e''2.~\pp | e''2 r4 | R2.*4 | }
introClTwo = { R2.*6 | g'2.~\pp | g'2 r4 | R2.*4 | }
```
- E5 is written G5 on the A clarinet, in the clarion, and is secure at pp.
- The written B♭4 that c036 kept for Cl 1 at 7–8 ("throat B♭") moves to Cl 2. The sound is the same G4.
- Both parts have the same rhythm and the same pp, so the shared staff engraves as one chord (checked on page 1).
- No theme statement changes (themecheck 41/41).

**What I left alone.** The coda also has D♯4→E4 in Hn 3 at 332→333 and 338→339. These are inner leading tones in a ff/fff tutti, doubled by Tpt 2 and the violas, so I left them. They are not the exposed call.

**Bar 7 changed, so the opening changed.** The finale and movement II threads should be told: "scherzo bar 7: Cl 1 E5, Cl 2 G4 (was G4/E4); the horn call's D♯4 and movement II's Cl 2 E♭4 are no longer answered by E4 at pitch."

### 3. The finale should not quote the trio tune T; the scherzo keeps it as its own (decision, no change)

Of the scherzo's exported themes, the finale now uses theme A's head, motive x, the motto close, the held D♯4 and the last G♯. It does not use T.

I decided this is right, and that the scherzo should not reshape T for the finale. T's character is tied to its colour and register: dolce clarinet, sul A violins, horn in A major in the coda. Adler (adler2 PDF 188) says a tune's character is fixed by the instrument and range it was given, and that moving it changes it. The finale's E-major moment (IV 243) is half a bar inside a C-major arpeggiation, and there T could only appear as a fragment in brass at ff.

The models agree. Tchaikovsky 4, which Adler lists for study (adler2 PDF 195), brings back only the fate motto in its finale, not the scherzo's tunes.

No scherzo change, and no request to the finale.
