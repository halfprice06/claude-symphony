## Findings

### 1. The records give the closing timpani motive x as ppp throughout; the score has pp, pp, ppp (records only; no score change)

**Bars:** 363, 367, 371 (timpani).

**Problem.** The score prints `b,4\pp b,8 b,8 e,4` at 363, the same at 367 with no new mark (so pp), and ppp "near the rim" at 371. Four records describe all three statements as ppp:
- the HISTORY do-not-touch line added for the third series ("Closing timpani motive x at 363–371 (ppp B–B–B–E)");
- scherzo.md's "What the finale relies on" ("closing timpani motive x (363–371, ppp B–B–B–E)");
- finale.md's link paragraph ("the timpani play motive x ppp on B–B–B–E at 363, 367 and 371");
- the c230 comment in the finale's intro.ily ("ppp, B-B-B-E at its 363, 367, 371").

scherzo.md's own Ending section has it right: "pp at 363 and 367, ppp at 371".

**Evidence.** Adler ch. 1 (adler1 PDF 22) makes every note, timbre and nuance part of the composition. A link that another movement relies on has to be recorded as it is printed. The dynamics are correct as printed and should not be changed to match the records. The step from pp to ppp follows the fade of the whole last page, as XV-37 (adler2 PDF 191) passes a held note on at a steady soft level: Hn 1 and Fl 1 are pp at 361–364, Vln I is ppp at 369, and Hn 1 reaches ppp at 375. The ppp "near the rim" at 371 is the softest stroke (adler2 PDF 80, c236). A ppp at 363 would put the first stroke below the pp horn and flute it supports.

**Fix (decided).** Keep the score. At promotion:
- correct the HISTORY do-not-touch line to "Closing timpani motive x at 363, 367, 371 (B–B–B–E; pp, pp, ppp near the rim)";
- correct scherzo.md's relies-on line the same way;
- tell the finale thread so it can correct finale.md and its intro.ily comment. Its own quotation at IV 9 is mf< and does not depend on the level.

### 2. Bar 9 of both outer frames now has the timpani's motive x; record it as a link (records only)

**Bars:** scherzo 9–12 (timpani alone: B–B–B–G | E | B–B–B–E), finale 9 (G–G–G–G, mf<).

**Problem.** Since finale v231 the finale's first motive x falls at its bar 9, the same bar number as the scherzo's first timpani motive x. The scherzo's first statement (9–12) and its last (363–371) now frame the finale's first, all on the same drum set retuned in the pauses (B → C, E → G). scherzo.md records 9–12 only as the link back to movement II's timpani (c090), and 363–371 as the link forward. The opening statement has no protection for the forward link.

**Evidence.** Adler ch. 1 (adler1 PDF 21): the timpani's identity as a choir instrument comes from the figures it is given. Ch. 15 (adler2 PDF 188): a figure keeps its character only in its own register and instrument. The scherzo gives motive x to the timpani alone at both ends of the movement, and the finale takes it over in the same instrument. The finale thread built IV 9 from 363–371 (its c230). If a later critic altered 9–12, that change would weaken a correspondence the finale now relies on, and nothing in the records would warn them.

**Fix (decided).** Add to scherzo.md's Opening bullet for bars 9–12: "the finale (v231) also places its first motive x at IV 9, in the timpani, so the scherzo's first and last timpani statements frame it; tell the finale before changing 9–12." Make no score change.

## Points tested and rejected

- **Movement II and the scherzo both end with harp harmonics and soft timpani** (Adler adler1 PDF 19–20: colour gives each piece its personality; Monteverdi's one-mood, one-combination rule). The likeness is real, but it is narrow:
  - II's harmonics are its theme head, A♭–C–E♭ arpeggiated (83), and its timpani answer the head in mirror (84–85).
  - The scherzo's harmonics are one E-major chord on the last pulse (375), and its timpani play the movement's own motive x.
  - II holds a full A♭ chord; the scherzo holds one pitch in two octaves.
  - The harmonic E4 at 375 is protected (c220), because the finale answers it with E♭4 at IV 1.
  
  Keep.
- **Hn 1 re-attacks G♯4 at 368 while Hn 3 holds it** (XV-40, seams). The re-attack is pp under a held unison at pp, which is the dovetail XV-37 asks for. Hn 3 leaves at 369 just as Vln I enters. Keep (c006, c226).
- **G♯5 is absent at 365–368.** c140 rejected adding it, and its reason stands: the bars mirror bars 1–2, horn G♯4 alone, before Vln I colours the note from above (XV-37). Keep.
- **A finale quotation of the trio tune** (row 10, register decides character). This was rejected by c130, c140 and c230, and Adler PDF 188 supports them: T in C-major ff would be a different tune. Keep.
- **Coupling trumpets with the timpani at 363–371** (adler1 PDF 21). The trumpets are excluded here by the cyclic rule (tutti rhythm only), and the solo timpani is the point of the frame. Keep.
