## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Hn 3·4, 341–344 and 365: Hn 4's rests run under Hn 3's solo, a rest collides with a note, the "3." is missing at 341 and doubled at 365 (applied)
Problem: at 341 Hn 3 has T in A major alone. The shared staff prints it as two voices: Hn 3 with up-stems and Hn 4's whole-bar and minim rests on the middle line under it. At 343 Hn 3's quaver B3 sits on Hn 4's rest. There is no "3." at 341. At 365, where Hn 3 comes back after 20 bars, the staff prints "3." twice, one above the other (the combiner's label and c325's manual one). In v326 the combiner printed "3." at 341 and a clean solo line. c331's tie d'4~ | d'4 and the hairpin ended on the rest at 345 (`R2.\!`) made the combiner keep the two parts apart.
Evidence: Gould PDF 50–51: separate rests only where two parts with different durations sound together; otherwise parts share a rest, and no rest may be displaced onto the other part's notes. PDF 55: nothing collides. PDF 13: one convention, applied consistently (here the c135 rule: a label at a solo entry after rests).
Fix, sections/coda.ily (the score's own convention, as dC 18–27 and sB 56–57):
```
codaHnThree: ... | R2. | \partCombineSoloI cis'2(\p^\markup \italic "dolce" d'4 | e'2 a'4 | gis'4. b8 d'4~ | d'4\> cis'2) | \partCombineAutomatic R2.\! | ...
codaHnFour:  ... | b2--\> r4\! | R2. | \partCombineSoloI R2.*4 | \partCombineAutomatic R2.*32 | }
```
Result: "3." at 341, no rests under the solo, one "3." at 365. Notes, tie, slur and hairpin unchanged.

### 2. Page 8, bars 210–236: labels in the wrong bars, a duplicate, two entries unlabelled, rests under Cl 1's solo (applied)
Problem: the systems now start at 213 and 233. c325's labels were written for systems starting at 214 and 234, so they now print in the second bar of a system: Cl 1 "1." at 214 (with no label on the system's first bar), Bsn "1." at 234 (a second "1." one bar after the combiner's "1." at 233), Hn 3·4 "4." at 234 in the middle of a slurred phrase (the system's first bar, 233, is unlabelled). Hn 4's entry at 229 after four bars' rest has no label. Cl 2's whole-bar rests are printed under Cl 1 at 213–216: c331's hairpin, which now ends on Cl 1's A at 213, keeps the combiner from switching to solo there, so Cl 1 also lost its "1." at 213.
Evidence: Gould PDF 13 (consistency), PDF 50–51 (shared rests; separate rests only for parts with different rhythms), PDF 55 (a duplicate label is a needless symbol).
Fix, sections/tC.ily:

Result: "1." over Cl 1 at 213 with no rests under 213–216, one "1." on the bassoons at 233, "4." at 229 and at 233. I tried \partCombineSoloI from 210 to clear Cl 2's rests at 210–212 too; the slur that starts at 209 cannot cross the switch ("cannot end slur"), so 210–212 stay as in v326.

### 3. global.ily, c235 system-label filter: it has never filtered anything (applied)
Problem: c235's `c235-first-in-system` is meant to print a restated label only when its note starts a system. It compares `(ly:grob-property col 'rank 0)` of the note's column with the system's left column. `rank` is not a grob property, so both values are always 0 (my probe printed "0 0" for all 18 labels) and every label prints wherever it is placed. This is why finding 2 happened silently: any later change that moves a system break leaves labels in mid-system.
Evidence: Gould PDF 9: "A computer programme will apply notation principles, but the operator must decide if these are the most apt solutions, and a trained eye is required to refine the settings and evaluate" the result.
Fix, global.ily, inside `c235-first-in-system` (test by musical moment):
```
(if (not (and (ly:grob? col) (ly:grob? left)
              (let ((wc (ly:grob-property col 'when #f)) (wl (ly:grob-property left 'when #f)))
                (and (ly:moment? wc) (ly:moment? wl) (not (ly:moment<? wc wl))
                     (ly:moment<? (ly:moment-sub wc wl) (ly:make-moment 3/4))))))
    (ly:grob-set-property! grob 'stencil #f))
```
A label now prints only if its note falls in the first bar of its system. With the v335 sources the new test drops exactly the three stale labels of finding 2 and keeps the other 15. With finding 2's sources it drops nothing.
