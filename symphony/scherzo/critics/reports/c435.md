## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Four string and wind entries in the trio's middle section lost their cues (applied, parts only)
- **Bars:** Vln I 193 (rest 133–192, 60 bars), Cl 2 193 (137–192, 56), Bsn 1 193 (149–192, 44), Vla 177 (133–176, 44).
- **Problem:** c427 silenced the cor anglais at 189–192 so the cellos answer the oboe alone, and removed the "C.a." cues at 189 from parts 07, 09, 26 and 28 instead of moving them to the cellos (c427's own report said the cue must now come from "Vc."). Four players now count 44–60 bars across the link's poco rit., the key change and the Trio tempo, and enter at 193 on the crescendo into the trio's forte with nothing printed before it. c431 moved the violas' entry to 177 (they take over the cellos' T from Hn 3), also uncued.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest … Cues must be audible and obvious." Ex. 4 (PDF 10) puts the cue just before the entry, after the tempo change. MOLA PDF 8: extracted parts are not error-free; a score change has to be proofread in the parts. The set's rule (c075, c255, c345) is a cue at every entry after 40 or more bars' rest.
- **Fix (applied):** parts-cues.json: `"07":[["cueVc","Vc.","UP",189,4]]`, `"09":[["cueVc","Vc.","UP",189,4]]`, `"26":[["cueVc","Vc.","UP",189,4]]` (the cellos' answer dis'4 e'8 dis' cis' dis' | b2 fis4 | gis4. a8 b4 | fis2., at pitch: three ledger lines below the violin stave at most), `"28":[["cueVc","Vc.","UP",173,4]]` (a2 b4 | cis'4 fis'2 | e'4. dis'8 ais4~ | ais4 b2, the line the violas join in unison at 177, in the alto clef). Checked in the PDFs.

### 2. Trumpets: a stale cue at 297 and no cue for the coda entry at 333 (applied, parts only)
- **Bars:** Tpt 1·2, 297–333.
- **Problem:** c428 took the trumpets out of 289–324. Their parts still print a "Vln. I" cue at 297–300, which now leads into 32 more bars of rest (301–304, 305–324, 325–332). The real entry is the ff motive x at 333, after 213 bars' rest (120–332) and after the Più mosso at 325, with no cue. c428 reported "Parts cues: none refer to the trumpets"; it checked cues whose source is a trumpet, not the trumpet parts' own cues.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5 and Ex. 4 (PDF 10), as in F1; a cue that leads to nothing is neither logical nor useful.
- **Fix (applied):** parts-cues.json "16" and "17": `["cueVlnOne","Vln. I","UP",297,4]` replaced by `["cueVlnOne","Vln. I","UP",329,4]`, Vln I's motive x crescendo from pp (g''4 e''8 e'' g''4 | a'' fis'' a'' | f''' c''' e''' | dis''' b'' r4), the rhythm the trumpets take over at 333. Both parts stay one page.

### 3. The timpani page turn moved into a 5-bar rest between two exposed solos (applied, parts only)
- **Bars:** timpani 241–247.
- **Problem:** c345 left the timpani turn at 236/237, in the 12-bar rest 225–236. c427 removed the stroke at 245, which opened a 5-bar rest 242–246, the shortest the breaker accepts (minimumPageTurnLength 15/4), and the page-turn breaker moved the turn there. The player now plays motive x pp at 241, turns in 5 bars of a poco a poco accelerando, and enters alone pp at 247 with motive x again. Both statements are the ones the finale quotes (IV 143–145).
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 7: "Consider the tempo of the music … It is preferable for a multi-measure rest to be at the end of a page. If this is not possible, be sure that it is in a period of rest and/or an unexposed part of the music."
- **Fix (applied):** parts-gen.py: `TURNS = {..., "23": 236, ...}` with a comment. The turn is back at 236/237 (rest 225–236; the G♯→G muta at 225 is printed on page 1 with 11 bars before the roll at 237). Two pages as before.

### 4. Bass clarinet: the retransition pedal at 245 enters after 96 bars' rest with no cue (applied, parts only)
- **Bars:** B. cl. 245 (rest 149–244).
- **Problem:** The player counts through the whole trio, its poco rit. at 237–240, the key change at 241 and the start of the accelerando, then holds the pp dominant pedal from 245. It is the longest uncued rest in the set.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5; Ex. 4 (PDF 10): the cue follows the tempo change and stands before the entry.
- **Fix (applied):** parts-cues.json "08": add `["cueVla","Vla.","UP",241,4]`, the violas' head of theme A (e'4 b8 b e'4 | g'2 fis'8 f' | e'2.~ | e'2.), written f♯''–a'' in the B♭ part, inside the stave. One page as before.

### 5. Part-combiner directives split the multi-rests in five parts (applied, parts-lib.ily)
- **Bars:** Ob 2 321 (…–320 | 321–…), Cl 2 213, Bsn 1 181 (169–180 | 181–184), Hn 4 177 (169–176 | 177–184) and 345 (341–344 | 345–356).
- **Problem:** `\partCombineSoloI`, `\partCombineAutomatic` and the like steer only the shared score staff. In a single part they sit between two full-bar rests, and merge-mmrs! cannot join rests across them, so the part prints two numbered rests with no letter, tempo word or key change at the split. The player has to add two numbers for no reason, and the split looks like a landmark that is not there.
- **Evidence:** MOLA Ex. 3 and Ex. 4 (PDF 10): rests are numbered as one span and broken only at letters and tempo changes.
- **Fix (applied):** parts-lib.ily: a predicate `combine-force?` (a PropertySet/PropertyUnset of `partCombineForced`, or a ContextSpeccedMusic wrapping one) added to `drop-for-part?`. The split-rest scan now finds splits only at letters, tempo words and "ritmo di quattro battute" (101), plus two in the harp's two-stave rest (333, 359) that are not caused by this. No notes change; Bsn 1 and Hn 4 re-space some systems, page counts and turns unchanged.

### 6. Two shorter cases of Ex. 4: Fl 1 at 172 and C.a. at 41 (applied, parts only)
- **Bars:** Fl 1 172 (rest 133–171, 39 bars, across the link's poco rit., the key change and the Trio tempo); C.a. 41 (rest 1–40, 40 bars).
- **Problem:** Fl 1's entry at 172 is a two-note echo of what the cellos have just played (c349); the player cannot place it without hearing the cellos' T. The C.a. enters f on beat 1 of 41 with Ob 2, whose part has a "Vln. I" cue at 37 for the same entry; the C.a. part has none (its old cue went with the entry move from 44).
- **Evidence:** MOLA Ex. 4 (PDF 10): a cue right after the tempo change, before the entry; MOLA PDF 5. The set's 40-bar rule covers the C.a.
- **Fix (applied):** parts-cues.json "01": add `["cueVc","Vc.","UP",169,3,"8vb"]` (the cellos' gis2 a4 | b2 e'4 | dis'4. fis8 a4, printed an octave up under an 8vb line, as Ob 1's cello cue at 181, c385). "05": add `["cueVlnOne","Vln. I","UP",37,4,"8va"]` (I first tried "Ob. 1"; the PDF showed Ob 1 does not play 37–40, so the cue printed one stray note. Vln I it is, under an 8va line, as Hn 2's cue at 37.)
- **Side effect handled:** the new cues re-spaced Fl 1 and Bsn 1 and the page-turn breaker moved their turns into shorter rests (Fl 1 216/217 in 7 bars, Bsn 1 232/233 in 8). parts-gen.py pins them at their v435 places: `"01": 192` (rest 185–196) and `"09": 152` (rest 149–192). Fl 1 keeps its three pages.
