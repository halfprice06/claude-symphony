## Findings

### 1. 243–245: the French sixth is heard only as a pizzicato tap, and its A♯ never resolves in register (applied)

**Bars.** At 244 the French sixth is:
- Vc div. pizz. C3 + A♯3 and Cb C2 (all crotchets);
- Vla E4 and Hn 1 F♯4, both held.

At 245 the cellos play B2 and the basses B1. Nothing plays B3.

**Problem.** A&S PDF 503: the augmented sixth "resolves by opening out into an octave" on 5̂ by contrary half steps (Ex. 29-2). "The resolution of an augmented 6th to an octave sounds like nothing else in tonal music."
- PDF 504 (Ex. 29-3a) gives the chord's origin: 4̂–♯4̂–5̂ in an upper voice over the Phrygian iv6–V.

In v152 neither is audible:
- The two notes that make the interval, C and A♯, are pizzicato crotchets. At 2. ≈ 70 they decay within the beat.
- The A♯3 goes to B2, a major seventh down. The 6th does not open to an octave; the voice simply leaves.
- The held voices (E, F♯) are the two tones that do not characterise the chord.

This is the movement's only augmented sixth, placed at its largest seam (c062). Its defining motion should be heard.

**Fix (applied, sections/retr.ily).** Two bassoons, pp, sustain the voice exchange and the resolution:
- Bsn 1 has A3–A♯3–B3: the 4̂–♯4̂–5̂ of Ex. 29-3a.
- Bsn 2 has A2–C3–B2.
- C3–A♯3 opens to the octave B2–B3.

### 2. Bar 38: the tail on G is spelled with a remote, wrongly directed A♯ (applied)

**Bars.** 38: Fl 1, Fl 2 and Vln I play `b''2->( ais''8 a''8 | g''4`, over a plain G-major chord.

**Problem.**
- **Direction.** A&S PDF 497, sec. 19 and Ex. 28-23: in a chromatic passing line, "we use raised notes ascending and lowered ones descending", so the passing tone and its goal stand a minor 2nd apart on different staff degrees. A♯→A is an augmented unison on one degree.
- **Remote accidentals.** PDF 497–498, sec. 20–21 and Ex. 28-24, 28-26: in major the preferred chromatics are ♯4̂ and ♭7̂. A♯ is ♯2̂ of G and suggests B major, a remote key.
- **Every other statement is right.** The tail scan shows that all the others follow A&S's chromatic scales:
  - G–F♯–F–E and A–G♯–G–F♯ in E minor (Ex. 28-27);
  - C–B–B♭–A at 42, 66, 70, 274 and 298;
  - B–A♯–A–G♯ with ♯4̂ in E major.
- **Bar 38 is the only exception.** The B♭ also anticipates the G-minor fugato's B♭ at 58. c062 marked the tails "follows" and missed this bar.

**Fix (applied, sections/sA.ily).** `b''2->( bes''8 a''8` in sAFluteOne, sAFluteTwo and sAVlnOne. The sound is unchanged.
