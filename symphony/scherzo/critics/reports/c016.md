The basses are a model of Adler's advice on PDF 105: wherever the cellos have quavers the basses play only the downbeat crotchet.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Coda 340: bass clarinet B1 is below the instrument's standard range
- **Bars:** 340 (`codaBassCl`, `b,,2.\f`).
- **Problem:** B1 sounding is written C♯3 for the B♭ bass clarinet. Adler (PDF 216): "one can reasonably expect every bass clarinet to have a low E♭, but writing all the way to C is still risky, for not all symphony orchestras have instruments with such extensions." The note also lands on the bar that prepares the ff tutti, where a missing or cracked note would be heard. Bars 337–339 (E2, D2, C♯2 = written F♯3, E3, D♯3) are within range.
- **Fix (applied):** the bass clarinet rests at 340 and the contrabassoon, which is silent until 341, takes the B1 and continues to its E1 at 341:
  - `codaBassCl = { R2.*4 | e,2.\mf\< | d,2. | cis,2. | R2.\! | R2.*8 | R2.*36 | }`
  - `codaCbsn = { R2.*7 | b,,2.\f | e,,2.\ff | ...`
  The held B1 in a reed is kept and the line B1→E1 leads into the tutti. This adds no low-brass unison and no new parallels.

### 2. Violin I shifts from first position to the top of the E string with no time, at 44→45 and 340→341
- **Bars:** 44→45 (D4 on the last beat, then D6 accented f on the downbeat; 2.=88, a crotchet lasts 0.23 s) and 340→341 (A4, then E6 ff; Più mosso 2.=96, a crotchet lasts 0.21 s).
- **Problem:** Adler (PDF 82–83) names this case under "Wide skips between extreme ranges" (Bartók, Ex. III-23): "the entire left-hand position has to be completely altered; sometimes strings have to be crossed silently", and "a soloist can execute this shift more accurately than a whole section… there are difficulties in simply hitting the correct pitch." D4 lies on the G or D string in first position; D6 needs the E string in third or fourth position, with two strings crossed. A4 to E6 needs the move from first to fourth position plus a crossing from the D or A string to E. In both places the high note starts a new section (the second idea at 45, the ff tutti at 341), is doubled by Fl 1, and is exposed. My scan found that every other large upward violin, viola and cello leap in the movement (16→17, 40→41, 260→261, 276→277, 280→281, Vln II 100→101 and 108→109, Vla 300→301, Vc 60→61) stays in first position and only crosses strings, which a section plays cleanly.
- **Fix (applied):** a crotchet rest on the last beat in Vln I only, which gives the left hand a beat to move:
  - sA bar 44: `a'4-. fis'-. r4` (was `a'4-. fis'-. d'-.`). Fl 1 and Ob 1 keep the D4, so the descending figure is still heard.
  - coda bar 340: `b'4-.\f fis'8-. fis'-. r4` (was `b'4-. fis'8-. fis'-. a'4-.\f`). The chord's A is held by Bsn 1 (A3). The f moves to the downbeat of 340.
  I considered raising bar 44 an octave instead, which would put 44–45 in third position. I rejected it because it breaks the falling line D5→A4 from 43 into 44, which matches the theme's own fourth bar. The record (themes-scherzo.ly) is unchanged: themecheck reports 41 of 41 statements unchanged.
- **Previous decisions:** cycle 7 asked to keep "coda motive-x build 333–340". Its entries, its bass descent and its crescendo stay. Only Vln I's last upbeat crotchet becomes a rest. Nothing earlier is undone.

### 3. No other changes are needed in the string writing (for the record, not a request)
The reading's other rules are already followed (table above): string ranges, divisi instead of double-stops in the violas, a simplified bass under fast cello figures, enough time for pizz./arco changes (cycle 6), "change bow freely" on the last held G♯5, and no harmonics or multiple stops. The most demanding string passage left is the violas' pp spiccato B3–F♯3 at 334–340, which crosses between the C and G strings, the strings Adler (PDF 86) says "speak a bit more reluctantly". I decided not to change it. It is a single crossing within normal section technique, and the alternatives (an octave higher, or E–B instead of B–F♯) either cross Vln II or break the stretto's entries on the fifth, which is by design.
