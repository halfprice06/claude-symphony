## What else I studied

I read the first two passes on this reading (c036, c126) so as not to repeat them. I looked at the Clarinet 1 and Bass clarinet parts (partsout/) for transposition and key signatures, and at movement II v201's score.ly for its clarinet pitch. I built the unchanged scratch copy to confirm the baseline.

## Findings

None. Every principle in the reading is followed or does not apply, and the scans found nothing that earlier critics have not already fixed or kept for a stated reason. The two passes before this one dealt with what the reading raises: breathing points (c036, c126), the weak low flute octave (c036, c078), the approach to B6 (c126, c166), the ff→p join at 300→301 (c126), and the bassoon's accompaniment at 233 (c126). I made no change.

### Checked, no change
1. **Clarinet throat tones in soft held chords** (Cl 1 G4 = written B♭4 at 29-31; Cl 2 G4 at 37-40; Cl 1·2 G4/E4 at 133-136 and 321-323; Cl 1 E4 at 361-364). Adler calls the throat tones "rather pale" (PDF 210, Ex. VII-56). He also says the clarinet has a full dynamic range in every register (PDF 175, 210). Each case is a background chord at pp-p, and at 133-136, 321-323 and 361-364 a dying close, where a pale colour fits. Moving the clarinets up into the clarion would put them in theme A's register at 29-31 (against c028/c100: accompaniment out of the tune's register). Kept.
2. **Contrabassoon 45-56**: 12 bars at f in its low register (G1-F♯2) with no rest, 8.2 s. Adler asks for "quite a few periodic rests" in the low register (PDF 230). The slur breaks at 46/47, 48/49, 50/51 and 52/53 give four breathing points. The player then rests 57-68. Across the movement the part has long rests between its entries (1-44, 57-68, 73-100, 133-206, 225-280). Kept.
3. **Ob 1 C♯6 swell at 227** (p<mp>p, the codetta's oboe solo). This is the range Adler calls "most beautifully scored ... for a thin, softly piercing effect", though hard to control (PDF 198). A professional oboist can play it. Kept (as c126).
4. **Cl 1·2 across the break** in the slurred F♯4-G4-A4 of theme A at 115/315 and Cl 2's staccato quavers 106-119/306-319 at f-ff. Adler says this troubles only inexperienced players (PDF 210). Kept (as c126).
5. **Bsn 2 E2 sub. p at 121 and 277** (lowest fifth). Adler warns against pp there (PDF 226). This is p, after a crotchet rest, with arco cellos on the same note. Kept (as c126).
6. **"1." rather than "Solo"** on the clarinet T at 153 and the codetta solos 225-236. Adler allows "1." for a single player and "solo" for emphasis (PDF 181). The score labels "1."/"2." consistently and marks each solo "dolce" or "espress.". Kept.
7. **Contrabassoon A2-C♯3 at 213-222** (above its lowest twelfth, PDF 230). This is c114's octave filler at mp-mf, where a paler sound does no harm; c126 kept it. Kept.
