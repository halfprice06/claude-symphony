### 2. For the finale and the record: the Neapolitan-as-IV pivot links the movements in pairs (information; no change)

**Reading.** A&S PDF 494–495 (sec. 14, Ex. 28-19, from the scherzo of Schubert's D. 959): the most frequent use of ♭II as a pivot is "the Neapolitan 6th of a minor key becomes IV6 in the major key a 3rd lower". Review point 11 (PDF 499–500) repeats it.

**What the symphony does.** The movements' key pairs follow that relation. I (C minor) → II (A♭ major): C minor's ♭II, D♭, is IV of A♭. III (E minor) → IV (C major): E minor's ♭II, F, is IV of C. c080 recorded the second relation only. The finale already uses it:
- 272: IV6/4 F–A–C over the C pedal.
- 273–275: theme A's bar 2 (G–F♯–F–E) over I – F♯°7 – iv6/4 – I.

So the F chord that is ♭II in the scherzo (73–86, 331) returns in the finale's last bars as IV over the tonic pedal. That is also the coda use A&S PDF 495 describes (Ex. 28-20: a ♭II-type chord over a coda pedal acting like a plagal IV).

**Consequence for the finale's rework.** Keep F major at 272 as IV6/4. Keep the chain's F♮ (♭6̂ of A minor, 4̂ of C) in the 273–275 quotation. Do not turn 272 into ii or a dominant-function chord. The scherzo needs no change: its ♭II passages already state F at length (73–86) and once more before the last V (331).

### Considered and kept (decided)

- **332, the leading tone in Vln I D♯6 and in Cl 1 + Hn 3 D♯4.** Three of the five F's at 331 go down a diminished third. A&S PDF 488 would let one inner F go to F♯ instead. I tried this on paper with Hn 3 F4→F♯4. It puts Hn 3 in unison with Hn 2's F♯4 entry at 332 and takes D♯ out of the horn chord. Moving D♯ into Hn 2 instead would make Hn 2 leap D♯4→G4 or cross Hn 3 through 333–339. The Cl 1/Hn 3 unison is one line in two colours: the motto tail G–F♯–F–D♯ that has run in Cl 1 since 329. An octave between it and Vln I's D♯6 in a crescendo tutti is a register doubling, not a doubled voice. All three resolve to E at 333. Kept.
- **331→332, Vln II F5→B4 and Vc F3→B2.** These tritone leaps sit inside the motive-x figuration. A&S's objection (PDF 487) is to the tritone in the bass of a root-position ♭II, and here the bass falls C→B by step. Kept (as c180).
- **86, Cl 1 C5–B4–B♭4–A4.** A&S PDF 493 allows either scale over ♭II. This line is the motto tail on A and part of the protected episode (c080, c180). Kept.
- **No ♭II in the trio or the E-major coda.** ♭II in major comes from ♭VI or I6 (PDF 488–490, Ex. 28-14). ♭VI of E is C major, which belongs to the finale. Kept.
- **No second F-major area in the reprise.** c080's 331 gives the restatement A&S PDF 483 describes; a second one in the last 80 bars would weaken it. Kept.
