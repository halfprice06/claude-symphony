## Findings

No finding needs a change to the notes. Under the assigned reading the scherzo's key plan, its dominants and both joins are already what Unit 26 teaches (24 principles, 19 follow, 5 do not apply). ### 1. Theme A bar 2 is now a pitch-fixed link across three movements (bars 14, 26, 106, 118, 254, 266, 306, 318)

Problem: Vln I plays theme A bar 2 as G5–F♯5–F5–E5 in every statement. Movement I (v221/v231, bar 79) has the same four notes at the same pitch in Ob 1 and Vln I. The finale (v217, 273–275) has them again in Vln I, G5–F♯5–F5 as the lower notes under a held C6 and E5 at 275, directly before its quotation of theme A's head at 275–276. scherzo.md records the movement I seed, but it records finale 273–275 only as the motto close "a major third lower" (B4–A♯4–A4–G♯4 at 357–360). It does not say that 273–275 is also theme A bar 2 at the scherzo's own pitch.

Evidence: A&S PDF 448 (§7): in a unified work the succession of events over long spans is as purposeful as within a phrase. PDF 445: a strong arrival comes through 1 or 3 with rhythmic resolution. The finale's line arrives on E5 (3 of C) on the downbeat of 275, and theme A's head follows at once. The link only works if all three movements keep the register. A later scherzo critic who moves a theme-A statement up or down an octave (c009 already moved 107–108) would break it without knowing.

Fix (record only): in coherence/scherzo.md, "What the finale relies on", add: "Theme A bar 2 at pitch, Vln I G5–F♯5–F5–E5 (14, 26, 106, 118, 254, 266, 306, 318): the finale's Vln I plays G5–F♯5–F5–E5 at 273–275 under C6, before the head at 275–276; movement I has it at I 79. Keep the first statement's register (13–16, Vln I) and tell both threads before changing it." Extend the existing line "Tell the movement I thread before changing theme A bar 2" to name the finale.

### 2. Movement I v231's bar 78 now foreshadows the scherzo's bars 1–7 and its last pitch

Problem: since v231, movement I's Cl 1 holds G♯5 (written as G♯, no longer A♭) over an E-major chord on beats 3–4 of bar 78 (Cb E1, violas B3, Vln II E4). Bar 79 then turns to E minor with G5–F♯5–F5–E5. That is the scherzo's opening in two bars: G♯ over E major (1–6), turning to G♮ (7), with theme A bar 2 to follow (14). The G♯5 is also the pitch of the scherzo's last Vln I note (369–376). scherzo.md lists only bar 79.

Evidence: A&S PDF 446 (§5): tonicization by melodic figuration alone, borrowed tones over a slow harmony that hint at a key. I 78–79 hints at the scherzo's E major → E minor turn before movement II has started. PDF 448: tonal plans across the whole work.

Fix (record only): in coherence/scherzo.md, "Themes quoted from earlier movements", add: "Movement I v231 bar 78–79: G♯5 (Cl 1) over E major, then E minor with G5–F♯5–F5–E5 (Ob 1, Vln I): the scherzo's 1–7 pivot and its last Vln I G♯5 in small. Keep the G♯ spelling at I 78 (tell the movement I thread)."

### 3. The join into the finale moves every upper pitch by common tone or semitone in its own octave; only E4→E♭4 is recorded (bar 375)

Problem: the scherzo's last sonority sounds E4–G♯4–B4 (harp harmonics, 375), G♯4 (Hn 1) and G♯5 (Vln I). The finale's bar 1 answers each in the same octave: E4→E♭4 (violas, Vln II, Cl 2, Hn 2), G♯4→A♭4 (Hn 1), B4→C5 (Vln I's lower note, Vln II's top), G♯5→A♭5 (Vln I). scherzo.md records the E4 (c070) and the G♯s. It does not record the harp's B4→C5. Someone could thin the harp chord at 375 to E–G♯ and not know the finale's C5 depends on it.

Evidence: A&S PDF 453 (Ex. 26-14, K. 488): the expanded IV goes to V through an augmented sixth. PDF 454: Beethoven's I – VI ‖ IV–V – I. The symphony takes the same path: C – A♭ (VI) – E (a third further down, = F♭) – German sixth on A♭ (IV 1) – V (IV 2) – I. The E chord goes into the augmented sixth by the shortest voice leading, as the reading's sketches show the bass going 6–5. B→C here is harp to strings across a pause. It is not the trumpets' reserved B→C.

Fix (record only): in coherence/scherzo.md, "What the finale relies on", add after the E4 line: "Harp harmonic B4 at 375 (written b, sounding B4): the finale's C5 (Vln I lower note, Vln II) answers it at the same pitch. Keep all three harmonics E4–G♯4–B4." The finale thread may want the same line in finale.md, item "Link from the scherzo".

### Candidates tested and rejected

- **Ending on the root E in Vln I for a firmer close.** Rejected. PDF 445 allows 3 for an arrival. The G♯ is the pivot the finale takes as A♭ (cyclic rule, do-not-touch since v01).
