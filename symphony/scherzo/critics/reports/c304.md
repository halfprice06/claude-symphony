## Findings

This cycle makes no change. I checked every principle in the assigned reading against the bars it applies to, and the score follows each one. The specific points this range raises were already handled by earlier critics of this reading: the low brass out under the coda peak and back on the last E (c034), the flutes rested before the tutti theme and the tenuto brass downbeats (c124), and Ob 1 kept out of its lowest fifth at the cadence (c214). I considered the points below and decided to keep the score as it is.

### Considered and kept
1. **Bars 169-184: strings alone for T's continuation.** Tchaikovsky (PDF 156) adds a bassoon and timpani under the violins' continuation. The scherzo's strings-only background gives the contrast Adler asks for: after sixteen bars of clarinet over harp, the change of whole choir is the new colour. Adding a held bassoon would weaken the drop to wind colours at 185 (Ob 1 call). Kept.
2. **Bars 189-192: C.a. and cellos in unison on the answer to the oboe call.** Adler (PDF 154) allows accompaniment and doubling by related colours. This is a melody doubling, not a background, and it is the blend Dvořák uses. It was protected by c278/c279 (trio colourings 185-200). Kept.
3. **Bars 105-120/305-320: trumpets on motive x chords inside the fast tutti.** Smetana's rule (PDF 154) is against brass doubling the fast notes. Here the trumpets play motive x (crotchet, two quavers, crotchet), not the theme's quaver line. The brass adhesive is Hn 3·4's slurred tenor line. Motive x is also the only trumpet material the cyclic rule allows. Kept.
4. **Bars 336-338: trumpets hold the hemiola chord with the horns while the trombones and tuba rest.** This is Barber's point 3 (PDF 150): low brass out under the peak. The trumpets' held notes are the adhesive (PDF 154), and they are not a melody. Kept.
5. **Bar 341: Hn 3 enters on the same downbeat as the harp, not after it (Weber, PDF 156).** The general pause at 340 gives the rest before the new colour that Adler names as the other way to make an entry heard (PDF 156). The closing page is protected (c279, c299). Kept.
