## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 193: two "1." labels stacked on the bassoon staff (applied)
- **Problem:** At M+8 (193) Bsn 1 enters alone p< on A3 and the Bn. 1·2 staff prints "1." twice, one above the other (score p. 7, second system). The hand-written `^\markup \bold "1."` from c135 was right when it was added, because the part-combiner then carried its solo state through the rests and printed nothing. The bar now starts with `\partCombineSoloI`, which prints its own "1." (soloText), so both appear. Two identical labels read as a misprint or as "two players"; Flute and Oboe at 197, whose manual labels have no `\partCombineSoloI`, print one.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 8: "equal attention should be given to both the vertical and horizontal lines of music when proofreading". MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11): each shared staff names its players once ("a 2", "I", "II"). I checked the other five manual labels (69, 125, 197 ×2, 297 ×2): each prints once.
- **Fix:** tB.ily, tBBsnOne: `\partCombineSoloI a2.\p\<^\markup \bold "1." |` → `\partCombineSoloI a2.\p\< |` (comment added). One "1." remains; the part is unchanged (the label was already stripped there).

### 2. Percussion is tacet in this movement but neither the score nor the part set says so (applied)
- **Problem:** The symphony uses two percussion players (movement I: cymbals, tam-tam; the finale's cymbal crash). The scherzo has no percussion staff, and its heading says only "Trumpet 3 tacet". Its part set numbers the parts 01–30 like movement I's but skips 24, so the librarian's percussion folder gets nothing for movement III, and nothing tells the players or the conductor that the omission is intended rather than a missing part. Trumpet 3 has a tacet sheet for exactly this reason (c020, parts-gen.py: "a tacet sheet keeps the set complete").
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5: "Tacet until…" is acceptable only when the player takes no further part, so a whole-movement tacet must be stated for that movement. MOLA PDF 6: the front matter identifies the instrumentation, including "a list of all percussion instruments" and how many percussionists are required. Movement I v221's set includes parts-24-percussion.pdf with the header "Percussion (2 players): cymbals, tam-tam".
- **Fix:** score.ly subsubtitle `\small "Trumpet 3 tacet"` → `\small "Trumpet 3 and percussion tacet"` (same number of heading lines; layout unchanged). parts-gen.py, before the harp book:
  ```
  w(r'''\book {
    \bookOutputSuffix "24-percussion"
    \header { instrument = "Percussion (2 players)" }
    \paper { page-breaking = #ly:optimal-breaking }
    \markup \column { \vspace #6 \fill-line { \fontsize #6 \bold "III. Scherzo — tacet" } }
  }''')
  ```
  The parts build now writes parts-24-percussion.pdf (one page, "III. Scherzo — tacet"), and the set runs 01–30 without a gap.

### 3. Bar 357: "espress." touches the rehearsal box W (applied)
- **Problem:** On the Hn 1·2 staff at W (357), Hn 1's "espress." was placed at the same height as the box W and started right at its right edge (gap about 0.3 mm at 250 dpi), so it read as one item, "[W]espress.", and the letter looked as if it belonged to the text. This is the last page and the motto-tail entry, where the conductor's eye goes first.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 8: anchored markings must not end up "positioned" so that they collide or float; Ex. 7 (PDF 12) shows the same fault, "a tempo" sitting on the box F. Elsewhere in the score (C 45, L 169, N 209 in Vln I; M 185 in Ob) the expression word stands under the letter, not beside it.
- **Fix:** coda.ily, codaHnOne: `b'2.(\p\<^\markup \italic "espress."` → `b'2.(\p\<-\tweak outside-staff-horizontal-padding #1 ^\markup \italic "espress."`. The horizontal padding makes the skyline stack the text under the box, as at C, L, M and N. Checked at 250 dpi; the Hn 1 part keeps its gap; page 13 keeps its systems.

### Considered, no change
- **Vla ppp at 349 after ppp at 345; Cb pp at 349 after pp at 341.** Both are reminders where the violas divide and the cellos take the bow; MOLA Ex. 3 prints dynamics where the level changes, but a reminder at a division is usual and harmless.
- **Tempo marks at K and V in the parts start after the key signature, not at the letter** (Bsn 1 p. 2: "Meno mosso" stands over 341–344's multi-rest after the new four sharps). The mark is anchored to the first musical column of 341, as LilyPond places it; the letter and the tempo word are on the same line and in the same bar. Not floating.
- **Page 1 of the score has no page number** (MOLA PDF 7). The first page carries the title block; LilyPond and engraving practice leave it unnumbered, and movement I's numbering starts the same way after its front page.
- **A separate front-matter page with the instrumentation (MOLA PDF 6).** Movement I has one; here it would add a 14th page to repeat what page 1's staff names and heading already give. The percussion tacet (F2) was the only missing item.
- **Vln I part page 1 with 12 systems** (MOLA PDF 7). Checked at 160 dpi: no symbols touch between systems; c135/c165 kept it for the same reason.
