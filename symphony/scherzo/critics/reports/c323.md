## Findings

### 1. Theme A's cadence bar: doubled leading tone in the lowest sounding voice (27, 267). APPLIED

**Problem.** In theme A's fourth phrase the violas' staccato off-beats at 27 (beats 2–3) are D♯4, under Vln I's D♯5 on beat 2. The pizz. B1 on the downbeat has died away by beat 2, so D♯4 is the lowest sounding note: at.py gives D♯4 – A4 – D♯5 on 27.2, a root-position diminished triad on the leading tone with the leading tone doubled, going straight to the tonic at 28. The reprise does the same at 267 (cellos A3 below, so V4/2, still with D♯ doubled into i6 at 268). The parallel bar in the first phrase, 19 (and 259), has B3 in the violas, the root of V.

**Evidence.** Unit 18 §3 (PDF 292–293, Ex. 18-6): when 6/3 and diminished chords are voiced, "do not double the leading tone if the tonic triad follows". §5 (PDF 296): the leading-tone chord works as a neighbour to I in its 6/3 form, not in root position. A doubled leading tone gives two voices that both need to go to E; here the violas rest and Vln I goes up to F♯5, so neither resolves in its octave. c022 made the same correction at 263 (violas B for D♯).

**Fix (applied).**
- sections/sA.ily, sAVla, bar 27: `r4 dis'4-. dis'4-.` → `r4 b4-. b4-.`
- sections/dA.ily, dAVla, bar 267: `r4 dis'4 dis'4` → `r4 b4 b4`

Now 27.2 is B3 – A4 – D♯5 (V7, root under the melody) and 267.2 is A3 – B3 – A4 – D♯5 (V4/2, as 259). The leading tone is only in the melody, and the cadence bar matches the half-cadence bar 19/259. Theme A's melody, motive x and the chromatic tail are unchanged (themecheck 51/51). Comments added above both variables.

### 2. No parallel 6/3 passage, in E major either (201–208, 145–148, 225–240). KEPT

**Problem considered.** Unit 18 §1 (PDF 289–291) offers the 6/3 series as the natural transition. c233 showed that in E minor, G and F a descending series reaches C–E–G. E major has no such chord, so I checked the trio's transitional stretches for a place where a series would improve the flow.

**Result.**
- **201–204** (A – Am – B – B7, after the forte G♯ at 200): the top line A4–C5–B4 is c070/c177's ♭6–5 in its own register. A stepwise bass under it would have to fall C–B with that line, which makes octaves.
- **145–148**: Hn 1's held G♯ is the pivot from movement II and grows into T's head (c051). A moving series under it would take the attention from the pivot.
- **225–240**: the codetta's E pedal and plagal A–E carry the relay of T's head (c053, c157). Unit 18's condition is still met: only the start and the goal are articulated (PDF 291).

The movement already uses the related techniques where they fit: motion in 3rds at 349–355 (Ex. 18-13) and I6–V4/2–I6 at 341–344 (Ex. 18-19a).

**Decision:** no change.

### 3. Coda 329–332, the Phrygian bass without iv6 and its 7–6. KEPT

**What differs.** The bass E–D–C–B is Ex. 18-12's descent through 1–♭7–♭6–5 (PDF 297–298), but over C the score has the Neapolitan 6/4 (c081), not iv6 with a 7–6.

**Why I keep it.** The reason recorded in scherzo.md is stronger than the reading's point: the finale takes this F back as IV6/4 over its C pedal at IV 272. The book calls the 7–6 "almost invariable" only for iv6.

**Decision:** no change.

### 4. The coda's A-major statement over the harp's I6 (341–344). KEPT

**What differs.** Cellos rest until 349 (c267), so on beats 2–3 the harp's C♯3–E3–A3 is the lowest sound. beats.py reads A6/3 – A6/3 – E4/2 – A6/3.

**Why I keep it.** The pizz. A1 alone on each downbeat after the G.P. is heard as the root (§8, PDF 298–299). The less stable 6/3 colour suits a phrase that is itself a transition from IV back to E at 349 (§1, PDF 290). The V4/2 resolves correctly to I6 (Ex. 18-19).

**Decision:** no change.

### 5. Proportions and length. KEPT

**What I checked.** Scherzo 87 s, trio 70 s, reprise 49 s, coda 44 s. The sections shorten toward the end, the reprise leaves out the F-major episode, and the slow coda stage (33 s) holds the cyclic close.

**Why I keep it.** Unit 18 does not bear on large proportions. Its rule that transitions are articulated only at their two ends (PDF 291) is met by the pedal transitions 89–104, 133–152 and 241–252, whose motion is carried by a line (c233, c293). At 4:38 the movement is a light middle weight between the Adagio and the finale.

**Decision:** no change.
