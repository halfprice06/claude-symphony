## Findings

### 1. Bars 119 and 319: a major second inside the horn section on the ff downbeat (applied)
**Problem.** In the tutti theme, bar 119 (and 319 in the reprise) has on beat 1 Hn 2 B3 (crotchet, accented) while Hn 3 and Hn 4 in unison play A3 on the tenor countermelody. Two horns against one, a whole tone apart, in the same register, at ff, form the only true second inside one brass family in the whole movement. Every other dissonance in the brass is split between colours. Hn 2's B3 came from c082, which wanted no D♯ (the third) under the theme's 4–3 appoggiatura. That aim was right; the choice of B3 put the root next to the seventh in the horns.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 102–103: "Discords of the seventh or second are preferably entrusted to instruments of different tone colour" (the diagram on 103 splits the seventh chord between trombones and horns). PDF 103–104: duplication in the brass places the horns "side by side" with the trumpets on the same note. Tpt 2 is on F♯4 in this bar (motive x).
**Fix (applied).** sC.ily, `sCHnTwo` bar 15: `| b4-> r4 dis'4-. |` → `| fis'4-> r4 dis'4-. |`; dC.ily, `dCHnTwo` bar 15 the same. Hn 2 now doubles Tpt 2's F♯4 on the downbeat and moves F♯4–D♯4–E4. There is still no D♯ under the tail's E on beat 1, so c082's purpose holds. The horns read B4–F♯4 over Hn 3·4 A3: a sixth and a fourth, no second. Notes of the theme and of the countermelody are unchanged.

### 2. Bar 339: the last ff chord has no fifth in the woodwind above the trumpets (applied)
**Problem.** The scherzo's final tutti stroke before the general pause is E minor. Above Tpt 1's G4 the woodwinds have Fl 1 E6, Fl 2 E6 (unison), Ob 2 G5, Ob 1 E5, Cl 1 E5 (unison), so only E and G. B is in Hn 4, Vln II and the violas, all at B4 or below. The chord is complete in the strings and brass but hollow in the upper woodwind, and two pairs of winds sit in unison. It lost its B when c044 moved Cl 2 from B4 to G4 so that the seventh resolves. That was right for the seventh, but no other instrument took over the B. c029 had used this very page (PDF 110–111) to fill 333–338 above the trumpets; 339 is the one bar it does not cover.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 110: "If trumpets and trombones take part in a chord, flutes, oboes and clarinets are better used to form the harmonic part above the trumpets"; all three diagrams on PDF 111 give that harmonic part complete triads. PDF 112: the parts in octaves strengthen one another. PDF 505 (appendix): in most of the tutti chords the flutes take two different notes. Dvořák 9/iii's last stroke has B in Ob 1 and Cl 1 over E in the flutes.
**Fix (applied).** coda.ily, `codaFluteTwo`: `| dis'''2. | e'''4-> r4 r4 |` → `| dis'''2. | b''4-> r4 r4 |`. The woodwinds now read E6–B5–G5–E5 above the trumpets. Fl 2's D♯6 falls a third to B5, an inner leading tone going to the fifth at a cadence; it still resolves to E in Vln I, Fl 1, Ob 1 and Cl 1 at their own pitch. The per-player scan shows this removes eight octave pairs D♯→E between Fl 2 and Cl 1, C.a., Hn 3, Ob 1 and Tpt 2. It adds one octave pair between Fl 2 and the violas' lowest note (D♯4–B3), which is a doubling of the same 7–5 motion. The shared flute staff now prints a two-note chord with one fff, so the dynamics are equal.

### Considered and left (my decisions)
- 75–76 and 83 (and 87–88): Bsn 1 lies above Hn 3·4, with the bass clarinet below, so the horns sit inside the bassoon colour (PDF 108 advises against this). Swapping Hn 3 and Bsn 1 at 75–76 would give Hn 3 C4–D4–C4 and Bsn 1 A3–B♭3–A3, but it makes parallel fifths Hn 4/Hn 3 (F3–C4 → G3–D4). The passage is protected (c014, c047, c080). At 87–88 the bassoon on top holds the discords, which PDF 108 recommends. No change.
- 103–104: Tbn 1's seventh A3 lies a seventh above B.tbn B2, inside one colour (PDF 102–103). It resolves to G3 at 105, the cleanest seventh resolution in the brass. Giving the A to a horn would put it a second from Hn 3's B3 or leave it unresolved. No change.
- 45–56, 121–128, 277–284: the clarinets sit inside the horns (PDF 108). Moving them above would cross the second idea's lower octave in Vln II. c014 left 45–56 for this reason, and it applies here too.
- 89–100: PDF 104 says the brass's held octaves "must not be ignored", and a pp horn B could hold the dominant pedal. I did not propose it. The timpani's exposed motive x with pizzicato strings is protected (c097), and the hemiola's brass entry at 101 depends on the brass having been silent (c050).
- 105–120: the woodwinds play the theme in octaves and do not double the brass chord (PDF 106). The chords are short and detached, where resonance is "a minor consideration" (PDF 114). This is Beethoven 9/ii's tutti layout, and c057 thinned the middle on purpose.
