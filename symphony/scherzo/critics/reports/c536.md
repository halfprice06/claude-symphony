## Findings

### 1. Hn 2 at 120–121: the f staccato crotchet runs straight into a subito-piano held entry (applied)

Problem: in the tutti 105–120, Hn 1·2 punctuate on beats 1 and 3. The last stroke, on beat 3 of 120, is a staccato crotchet. On the next downbeat, H (121), Hn 2 alone starts the second idea's held pad, G4 sub. p, for eight bars, with Hn 4 on B3. Hn 4 has two beats' rest before this entry. Hn 2 has none. At 2. = 88 a crotchet lasts 0.23 s, so the only gap is the shortening of the staccato. That is not enough time to change from a forte stroke to a soft held attack. My scan found no other horn entry like this in the movement.

Evidence: Sebesky PDF 32: the horn "speaks" late, which makes trouble when it is combined with other brass in staccato passages. PDF 46 (Ex. 51): Sebesky leaves out the horns' short notes on the upbeat "because of the tendency of the horns to 'speak' a little late. By omitting these notes, the horns have a better opportunity to land squarely on the downbeat". The soft downbeat at 121 is where a late or cracked horn would be heard most, because the string tune enters sub. p on the same beat.

c251 kept the beat-3 crotchets at 112/120/312/320 because they drive into the next phrase and "set up the subito piano" (c063). This fix keeps that stroke. Hn 1 still plays G4 on beat 3 of 120 with the violas' G4 off-beat, and Hn 1 rests from 121 anyway (c046). Only the player who has to enter softly loses the note. The chord on 120.3 was E4/G4 over nothing. It is now G4 with the violas' G4, still the tonic's third, as at 106 and 114, where Hn 1 also plays alone. The reprise (320) goes into four bars' rest, so it needs no change.

Fix (sC.ily, `sCHnTwo`, bar 120): `e'4-- r4 e'4-. | g'2.--(\subP` becomes `e'4-- r4 r4 | g'2.--(\subP`. The part-combined staff now prints "1." over the beat-3 G4 and "2." at 121, which shows clearly who plays.

### 2. Breathing and endurance (checked, no change)

The longest rest-free spans are Hn 1 205–224 (15 s, p–mf, breath at 216), Hn 3·4 and Bsn 1 101–120 (13.6 s, f–ff, breaths at 104 and 116), Cl 1 153–168 and 209–224 (12.6 s, breath at 160/216) and Bsn 2 169–184 (12.6 s, pp). Each long slur ends at a comma (PDF 24, 30). Trumpets never play more than 5.5 s without a rest. The trombones and tuba play only 25 notes each. PDF 70 asks for breathing space and PDF 44 asks that the lead player be rested. Both are met.

### 3. Bass trombone and tuba both on the root (101–120, 332–335, 339) (kept)

PDF 47, and PDF 88 note L of the "incorrect" Ex. 112: "One or the other can handle it easily—not both". Sebesky is aiming for the lean sound of a swing band. Dan asked for weight rather than bite in the brass. The pair is an octave apart, not the full low-brass unison kept for the finale. c266, c356 and c446 kept it, and this reading adds no new reason.

### 4. Horn quavers in motive x at 71 and 299 (kept)

PDF 46 would leave out the quavers. Here the horns are not answering trumpets. They enter a stretto together with the bassoon and cellos, on the beat, and the quavers belong to motive x, the entry's subject. Dropping them would turn the third stretto entry into a different figure. c176 and c446 kept them on Dvořák 9/iii's precedent.

### 5. Bsn 1 doubling Hn 3·4 at ff, 105–120 and 305–320 (kept)

PDF 73 and 106: the bassoon has "very limited projection—no edge—not recommended for use in combination with open brass". At ff under two horns the bassoon adds little that a listener can pick out. It does no harm, though. It softens the horns' attacks on the tenor line, and Adler II PDF 27–30 (row 13 of c251) recommends horns with bassoons and cellos on a tenor counter-line. Taking it out would change nothing a listener could hear, so it stays.

### 6. Clarinet throat tones at 29–39, oboe unisons at 41–43/273–275, Hn 1·2 tenths at 45–52 (kept)

These are the other rules in the chapter that the score does not follow. c356 (finding 2) and c446 (findings 1 and 4) kept them for reasons this reading does not override: a pale pad behind the tune, a mixed unison that gets smoother with each added player (PDF 56 fn 4), and Hn 2 filling an empty tenth above the bass. No change.
