## Verdict on the large form

The form, the proportions and the length (4:36) are right. I change none of them. Every section starts on a 5/3, which is what Unit 18 asks of goals (row 1). Every structural dominant arrives at the head of its hypermetric group (89, 101, 245, 301), as Ex. 18-11 asks. The transitions use pedals where Unit 18 would allow 6/3 series. That is the Dvořák practice, and in the link a 6/3 series would make C–E–G (c053). The one Unit 18 point I found is inside theme A, and it affects how the theme's second half moves.

## Findings

### 1. Theme A bar 10 (22, 114, 262): the whole-step bass neighbour A–G–A under iv. Make it A–G♯–A, V6 of iv (applied)

**Bars.** 21–23 (sA), 113–115 (sC, tutti), 261–263 (dA). 313–315 is not affected: its G is passing (F♯–G–A, c058).

**Problem.** Theme A is 16 bars:

| Bars | Harmony |
|---|---|
| 13–16 | i–V |
| 17–20 | i–iv–V–i |
| 21–24 | iv – **i6** – iv – V♭9 |
| 25–28 | i–i6–V–i |

The first half already sounds the tonic on 13, 14, 15, 17 and 20. The second half is meant to move from the subdominant to the half cadence at 24. Bar 22 puts the tonic back under it instead: G1 pizz. with G4 and B3 off-beats, so i6. The melody E5–D♯5–E5 then turns around a tonic, and the phrase loses its pull toward V.

**Evidence.** Unit 18 §5 (PDF 296) deals with this case directly:
- A bass neighbour "works best when the melodic progression is a half step".
- I6 as neighbour to IV "is usually better in major than in minor, where I6 is generally altered to form an applied dominant, V6 of IV" (Ex. 18-9).

With V6/iv:
- The bass is A–G♯–A, a half-step neighbour.
- 21–24 becomes iv – V6/iv – iv – V, a single motion away from the tonic.
- The melody's D♯ (beat 2) now forms a G♯-minor sonority with the off-beats G♯–B (bass and inner B). In v143 it formed an augmented G–B–D♯ (row 16).

The leading tone G♯ is not doubled in an inner part (PDF 293): the inner voices take B or E. G♯ is already a melodic note of the theme at 18 (A–G♯–G–F♯), so the colour is not new to the movement. The finale quotes only theme A's first two bars, so this change does not reach it.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-143/work).** In each file only the bar-10 entry changes. A c143 comment is added at the top of sA.ily, sC.ily and dA.ily.
