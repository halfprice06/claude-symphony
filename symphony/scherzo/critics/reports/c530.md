## Findings

### 1. The horn call quotes movement I's pitches but not its gesture: tie the E4 over the bar line and resolve after the downbeat (applied)

- **Bars.** 4–6 (Hn 1).
- **Problem.** Movement I's call (I 13–14) is `r4 es4( c'2~ | c'4 b2)`: a short upbeat, then the arrival of the rising sixth tied over the bar line as the longest note, falling by step after the downbeat. Over the G pedal that fall is a 4–3 suspension. The finale's major form keeps the same gesture: `e''2 c'''2~ | c'''4 b''2.` (IV 243). The scherzo has the pitches and the player, but in three equal bar values (`gis2. | e'2. | dis'2.~ | dis'2`). The E4, the call's goal, is its shortest held note, and the D♯4 lands on the downbeat with the bass. That makes the scherzo's call a rhythmic transformation, the kind of change the cyclic rules leave to movements I and IV ("quote the motto, do not develop it").
- **Evidence.**
  - Casanelles PDF 201: denotative meanings make "clear and direct associations".
  - PDF 200: a quotation relies on the audience's background. *A Clockwork Orange* assumes the listener knows Beethoven. Here the listener heard movement I's call about 25 minutes earlier, so the identifying gesture has to be there.
  - PDF 227 shows what a transformation keeps: Zimmer's Superman theme keeps only Williams's opening fifth, and it is heard as a new theme. The scherzo's call already keeps the interval. Restoring the tie and the after-beat fall makes it a quotation.
  - Aldwell & Schachter PDF 346–350: the suspension is a note tied over the bar line that resolves down by step after the strong beat. That is movement I's C4→B3.
  - The scherzo already uses the same tie at 147–148 (c498, Hn 1 G♯–A tied–G♯).
- **Fix (applied).** sections/intro.ily:
  `introHnOne = { gis'2.~\pp | gis'2. | gis2.(\<^\markup \italic "espress." | e'2.~\p\> | e'2 dis'4~ | dis'2)\! r4 | R2.*6 | }`
  - E4 is held for five beats and D♯4 for three. Movement I's proportions are 1:3:2 crotchets; the scherzo's are now 3:5:3.
  - The E4 is tied over the pizz. B at 5 and resolves on beat 3. Against the bass that is a cadential 6/4 (with Vln I's held G♯5) moving to V with the added sixth.
  - D♯4 still ends at 6.2, unresolved, and bar 7's turn to E minor is untouched.
  - Pitches, player, dynamics and the do-not-touch reasons all stay: the G♯ pivot, the rising sixth, the unresolved 7th that the finale resolves at IV 219–220.
- **Alternatives tested and rejected.**
  - `e'2.~ | e'4 dis'2~`, resolving on beat 2 as movement I does: at 2. = 88 the suspension lasts 0.23 s and passes as an ornament. Beat 3 gives 0.45 s.
  - A crotchet upbeat G♯3, `r2 gis4(`, as in movement I: after the 2.7 s fermata a 0.23 s pp horn upbeat is too short to register. It would also take the horn's G♯ off the bar-3 downbeat, where E enters under it.
- **Check.** Build clean. analysis.txt is identical to v530 (one parallel, bar 69, as before). Page 1 engraves without collision, and the ties and hairpins read correctly. themecheck reports only hornCall CHANGED (50/52, the two hornCall lines).
