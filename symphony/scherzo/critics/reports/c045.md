## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 121 (H): the accompaniment still reads ff under an f melody
- **Problem.** At 121 the flutes, oboes, horns and violins are marked f for the second idea in E minor. Cl 1·2, Bn 1·2, contrabassoon, violas, cellos and basses have no mark there, so they keep the ff from 104–105. Their sustained chords and the bass would then play louder than the tune. At the same point in the exposition (45) every part is marked f, and in the reprise (277) every part is marked sub. p.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8: proofread the vertical lines as carefully as the horizontal ones. dynmap.py at 121 shows six parts reading ff and six reading f.
- **Fix (applied).** In sC.ily, bar 17 of the section: `g'2(\f b'4~` (Cl 1), `e'2(\f e'4~` (Cl 2), `e2(\f g4~` (Bn 1), `e,2\f g,4~` (Bn 2), `e,,2(\f g,,4~` (Cbn, Cb), `e'2.(\f` (Vla), `e,2(\f g,4~` (Vc).

### 2. Bar 224: a forte timpani stroke inside the diminuendo
- **Problem.** The timpani's E at 224 is its first note after 14 bars of rest. The last timpani dynamic was f at 209, so this stroke is forte. It falls in the bar where the tune ends with a diminuendo into the pp codetta at 225.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 (vertical proofreading). Every other part at 223–224 is getting softer.
- **Fix (applied).** tC.ily: `e,4\p r4 r4` at 224.

### 3. Bar 193: the violins enter without a dynamic
- **Problem.** Vln I and II come back at 193 after eight bars of rest with only a crescendo hairpin. The violas, cellos, basses, clarinets and bassoons are marked p at 193. The violins still read mp from 169, so the crescendo starts from an unclear level and above everyone else.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8. In Ex. 3 (PDF 10) every entry after rests carries a dynamic (pp at bar 12 after the multi-rest).
- **Fix (applied).** tB.ily: `fis''2(\p\<` (Vln I) and `fis'2(\p\<` (Vln II).

### 4. Bars 87 and 229: one printed dynamic, two levels on the clarinet staff
- **Problem.** The clarinet staff is part-combined and prints one dynamic for both players. At 87, Cl 2 enters pp while Cl 1 is still in its p dolce phrase, and the printed pp reads as both. At 229 it is the other way round: Cl 1 enters p while Cl 2 is repeating its pp E, and the printed p reads as both. The project rule says simultaneous dynamics on a shared staff must be equal.
- **Evidence.** In MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11) each shared staff carries a single set of dynamics that holds for both players. The pair check found these two places on the clarinet staff and no others in the movement.
- **Fix (applied).** sB.ily 86–87: `c''2(\> b'8 bes'8 | a'4)\pp ...`. Cl 1 makes a diminuendo into Cl 2's pp entry, which suits the poco rit. tC.ily 229: Cl 2 `e'2.\p`, rising with Cl 1's entry. Low E on the clarinet stays soft at p.

### 5. Rehearsal letters skip four landmarks
- **Problem.** In the exposition the letters come every 12–16 bars, each at a landmark. From the trio on they come every 32–36 bars and pass over the violins' entry with the trio tune (169), the trio codetta (225), the second idea in the reprise (277, which is lettered C and H in its two earlier appearances) and the motto's chromatic tail and close (357). Movement I letters every 4–22 bars.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 4: rehearsal letters in addition to bar numbers, "their position corresponding to landmarks in the music". In a part, a player counting 36 bars of rest has no letter to come back in at.
- **Fix (applied).** In global.ily, `\mark \default` at 169, 225, 277 and 357. The `s2.*32`, `s2.*28`, `s2.*36` and `s2.*29` runs are split as 16+16, 16+12, 24+12 and 16+13. The letters after K become L (169), M (185), N (209), O (225), P (241), Q (253), R (277), S (289), T (305), U (325), V (341), W (357). The comments in global.ily are updated. No coherence file names a scherzo letter (checked), so nothing outside the score changes.

### 6. Bar 340: the general pause is not labelled
- **Problem.** The silent bar before the Meno mosso has a fermata on every staff but no "G.P.". The fermatas alone do not say that everyone is silent, so a conductor and players counting rests have to check every staff.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 4: indications that apply to everyone go above the top staff and above the first violins, as in Ex. 6 (PDF 11).
- **Fix (applied).** In global.ily: `\mt 96 \tempo \markup "G.P." s2.\fermata`. It prints next to the fermata above the top staff and the strings, and the existing `\mt 96` step stays as it is. I checked the zoomed page: it is clear of the box for V.

### 7. Bars 73 and 141: the harp's pedal text is crossed by barlines
- **Problem.** "Ped.: D C B♭ / E F G A" (73) and "Ped.: D♯ C♯ B / E F♯ G♯ A" (141) were printed between the two harp staves. The harp's barlines run through that space, so they cut through "B♭" and "G♯".
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12): markings must not collide.
- **Fix (applied).** In sB.ily and link.ily the markup moves from the RH note or rest to the LH below the staff: `f,4_\markup \small "Ped.: …"` at 73, and at 141 the LH multi-rest with the same left-alignment overrides. Below the harp nothing crosses it. I kept the settings in the score even though MOLA PDF 5 says not to print harp pedalling. That sentence is in its section on parts. The score settings (c026, from Adler) tell the conductor and the copyist the spelling. The harp part should leave them out.

### 8. Page 1: no duration
- **Problem.** The heading names the work, the movement, the transposition and the Tpt 3 tacet, but gives no duration.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 6: "an approximate duration given for each movement".
- **Fix (applied).** In score.ly the subsubtitle gets a last line, `\small "Duration: about 4½ minutes"`. The MIDI runs 276 s, and the fermatas and G.P. add a little in performance. The heading is one line taller and the page count stays at 13.
