## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The coda's trio tune (349–364) is a fourth statement of T in E with the trio's bass line. Its first half should tonicize IV (A major). Applied.

**Problem.** T sounds complete in E major at 153, 169, 209 and 349. Each time the bass is E E B E | A F♯ F♯ B | C♯ G♯ F♯ A | B B E E. The only differences at 349 are the instruments. So after the scherzo's last E-minor ff (347) and the G.P., the slow coda (36 bars, about 31 s, the last ninth of the movement) repeats a key and harmony the listener has already heard three times. A&S §7 (PDF 448) asks for a purposeful succession of key areas. §11 (PDF 451–452) and Points 9 (PDF 457) say a tonicized IV is "frequent" and unproblematic late in a piece, and moves on to V and I. Dvořák 9/iii's coda turns to the subdominant region (♭II, ii) before its last tonic (harmony.csv 1530–1626).

T's first half allows this without changing a note's rhythm or contour. Played a fourth higher (or a fifth lower), bars 1–8 run A A E7 A | D Bm B7 E. The phrase's half cadence then falls on E: V of A is I of E, a common pivot (A&S §3, PDF 443). E is reached by V7–I with the melody on its 1 on a minim (§4, PDF 445). T's second half (357–364) then confirms E with ii–ii6–6/4–V–I (Points 2). The motto tail's A minor at 367 now answers the coda's A major, the same IV→iv mixture the trio uses at 201–202.

**Fix (applied in sections/coda.ily, length unchanged):**

### 2. The retransition (241–256) reaches V with no II or IV. Bass A at 247. Applied.

**Problem.** The bass is E (241, 243, 245, 247) and then B (249–256). A&S §15 (PDF 456): "When the V comes back, it is often preceded by a II or IV and often supports a 7th." The 7th is present (Cl 2 A4 255–256, Vln I A5 254), but nothing prepares the dominant. Both other approaches to V in this movement do prepare it: 85–88 (A pedal with the Neapolitan) before 89, and 201–202 (A, A minor) before 203. Dvořák 9/iii's retransition puts VI/V before V7 (onsets 1086–1101).

This matters for pacing. From the trio's last PAC (223) to 248 the bass sits on E for 25 bars, about 20 s. A move to the subdominant at 247 makes the last of those bars point forward.

**Fix (applied in sections/retr.ily).** Seventh bar of retrVc `a,4 r4 r4` (was `e4`), and of retrCb `a,,4 r4 r4` (was `e,4`). Over A the bar sounds A (bass), E (Vla's e' on beat 1) and G (Hn 1's held g'), an incomplete iv7. Its G falls to F♯ in Cl 2 at 253. The horn pivot G♯→G (241–247), protected since c003, is unchanged.
