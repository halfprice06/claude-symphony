## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Double basses stay *f* under a *pp* texture, 225–240 (applied)
**Problem.** Cb takes *f* at 209 (tC.ily, `tCCb`, `e,4\f`). At 225 every other string and wind part drops to *pp* (Vc `e4\pp` pizz., Vln II and Vla *pp*, Bsn *pp*). The Cb part has no new dynamic, so the score and the Cb part both read *f* for sixteen bars of pizzicato under the codetta.
**Evidence.** v05 page 7, bar 225: the Vc has *pp* and the Cb does not.
**Reading.** Not a Gould ch. 1 rule. It is the plain requirement that each staff shows the dynamic it plays (MOLA 4: "all instructions … dynamics").
**Fix.** In tC.ily, `tCCb`, bar 17: `e,4\pp r4 r4`.

### 2. Flute 1 *pp* attached to the wrong note, 169 (applied)
**Problem.** `tAFluteOne` has `gis''4( fis''8 gis'' a''4)\pp`. The *pp* prints under beat 3 of 169, after the entry.
**Fix.** `gis''4(\pp fis''8 gis'' a''4)`.

### 3. Two *ff* in one bar on shared staves, and *ff* printed again in the next bar, 104–105 and 308–309 (applied)
**Problem.** At 104 and 308, Fl 1, Ob 1 and Cl 1 take *ff* on beat 2 (their hemiola minim), while Fl 2, Ob 2 and Cl 2 take *ff* on beat 1. Each staff prints *ff* twice in one bar, and Ob 1·2 and Cl 1·2 stack two *ff*s at 105 beat 1 and beat 2. Then every woodwind, string, Bsn, Cbn and timpani part repeats *ff* at 105 and 309, where nothing changes. The only dynamics that actually change at 105 and 309 are those of the brass (*f*), and those get lost among the repeated *ff*s.
**Evidence.** v05 page 4, bars 104–105; page 10, bars 308–309.
**Reading.** Gould PDF 28: one stem direction per beat in a shared staff. The same logic gives one dynamic per staff per event. The staff-level `Dynamic_engraver` in score.ly is there to print a shared dynamic once.
**Fix (applied).**
- In sB.ily and dB.ily, last bar of `FluteOne`, `OboeOne` and `ClOne`: `a''2-\tweak stencil ##f \ff`. The *ff* stays in the MIDI for that voice but does not print.
- In sC.ily and dC.ily, first bar: delete `\ff` from Fl 1·2, Ob 1·2, Cl 1·2, Bsn 1·2, Cbn, Timp and all five string parts.
- Brass *f* at 105 and 309 is kept.

### 4. Part-combined oboes and clarinets in the tutti theme, 105–120 and 309–324 (applied)
**Problem.** Ob 1 and Cl 1 play theme A while Ob 2 and Cl 2 play off-beat crotchets `r4 g' g'`. The part combiner switches mode within the bar: "1." on beat 1 (Ob 2 rests), then a single-stemmed chord on beats 2–3, then two voices. It prints "1." at 105, 108, 109, 113 and 116, and "1. 2." at 112 and 120, while both players are playing. Stems flip direction inside a beat group.
**Evidence.** v05 pages 4–5 and 10–11.
**Reading.** This breaks Gould PDF 28 (keep the stem direction of a beat or half-bar) and the double-stemmed writing of PDF 28–29: two parts on one stave, upper part stems up and lower part stems down.
**Fix (applied).**
- sC.ily and dC.ily, `OboeOne`, `OboeTwo`, `ClOne` and `ClTwo`: `\partCombineApart` at bar 1 and `\partCombineAutomatic` at bar 17.
- In sC `OboeTwo` I wrote `\partCombineAutomatic R2. | R2.*11 |` so that countbars still parses the line.
- Result: Ob 1 and Cl 1 have stems up, Ob 2 and Cl 2 have stems down with their beat-1 rests, and no solo labels print.

### 5. "arco" and "pizz." where nothing changes, and "arco" on the bassoons, 45 and elsewhere (applied)
**Problem.**
- Bar 45: `sABsnOne` carries `^\markup \italic "arco"`. The word prints over the bassoon staff, an instruction a bassoonist cannot follow.
- A redundant "arco" prints for Vc at 57, 193, 293, 305 and 333; for Cb at 305 and 337; and for Vla at 245. The violas are never pizzicato before 257.
- A redundant "pizz." prints for Vc and Cb at 153 (four bars after 149, on the same system) and at 241 (pizz. since 225).

A player reads every arco or pizz. as a change, so a redundant one makes them question what they were doing before.
**Evidence.** My state-tracking script over all sections. v05 pages 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11.
**Fix.** Delete those markups. The pizz./arco changes that do happen are untouched: 3, 45, 89, 101, 149, 169, 193 (Cb), 205, 225, 257, 277, 281, 349, 365 and 383.

### 6. Rehearsal letters and tempo words missing over the strings when Vln I is hidden (applied)
**Problem.** `Mark_engraver` and `Metronome_mark_engraver` sit on the Vln I staff. `RemoveEmptyStaves` hides Vln I in three systems:
- 79–96: "poco rit.", F and "a tempo"
- 134–152: "poco rit." at 149
- 241–256: N, "poco a poco accelerando" and "poco rit."

In those systems the string section shows no mark at all.
**Reading.** MOLA PDF 4: tempo indications go above the top staff and above the first violins, "or similarly positioned staff in the absence of strings".
**Fix (score.ly).** Remove both engravers from the Vln I `\with`. Add them to the strings StaffGroup: `\new StaffGroup \with { \consists "Mark_engraver" \consists "Metronome_mark_engraver" \consists "Staff_collecting_engraver" } <<`. The marks now print above the top visible string staff: Vln II at 87–89 and Vc at 149. Elsewhere the result is the same as before.

### 7. The Trio begins on the last bar of a system, 153 (applied)
**Problem.** v05 page 5 ends its second system with bar 153. That bar carries K, the new four-sharp key, "Trio, Un poco meno mosso" and the first note of the trio tune, and the tune continues on the next page.
**Reading.** Gould PDF 21 asks for warnings before a change at the system's end, and for clefs to separate phrases. A section that starts its first phrase on the last bar of a system does the opposite.
**Fix (global.ily).** `\bar "||" \break \key e \major \mark \default % K 153`. The build stays at 13 pages. Page 6 now opens at bar 131 and its second system starts at K.

### 8. Shared-staff consistency in the horns (applied)
**Problem.**
- Bar 73: "senza sord." prints twice, stacked, on Hn 3·4, because both parts carry it.
- Bars 3–6: Hn 1 has `\<` and `\>` and Hn 2 has no hairpins. The shared staff prints one swell that Hn 2 is not marked to play, which breaks the brief's rule of equal simultaneous dynamics on shared staves.

**Fix.**
- Remove the markup from `sBHnFour`. One "senza sord." on the staff covers both players; the BRIEF rule about the first entry is kept.
- `introHnTwo = { R2.*2 | b2.~\pp\< | b2.~\> | b2.~ | b2.~\! | b2 r4 | R2.*5 | }`. The staff still prints one hairpin and the build gives no warning.
