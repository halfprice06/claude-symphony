## Findings

### 1. Bars 81, 185, 225: harp off-beat chords without "non arpegg." (applied)

Problem. The harp's off-beat chords under the clarinet solo at 81–84 (A3–F4, crotchets on beats 2 and 3) carry no arpeggio indication. The trio's identical accompaniment at 153 and the coda's at 341 print "non arpegg.", and the chords the composer wants broken carry an arpeggio sign (149, 151, 237, 361). So the score's own convention is that plain chords are flat, but the first harp chords in the movement do not say so. At 185 (letter M) and 225 (letter O) the off-beat chords start again after 16 bars' rest at a new section, with no restatement of the 153 instruction.

Evidence. Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 46: "The chords must always be broken (arpeggiato); should the composer wish otherwise he should notify it (non arpeggiato)." By that rule a harpist reads 81–84 as rolled, which smears the light off-beats under the dolce solo and differs from the trio's identical figure. At 185 and 225 the instruction from 153 is 32 and 72 bars old.

Fix (applied). First chord of each entry, text below the RH staff, where the "étouffez" and the trio's pp already sit:
```
sB.ily  sBHarpR: | r4 <a d' f'>4_\markup \small \italic "non arpegg." <a d' f'>4 |          (81)
tB.ily  tBHarpR = { r4 <gis cis' e'>4_\markup \small \italic "non arpegg." <gis cis' e'>4 | (185)
tC.ily  tCHarpR: R2. | r4 <gis b e'>4_\markup \small \italic "non arpegg." <gis b e'>4 |     (225)
```
The LH minim chords at 73–80 stay unmarked, so they are broken by Rimsky's default under the harp's own melody, as a harp solo should be. Placing the text above the RH staff added two pages (15); below the staff the score stays at 13 pages. Sound and parts' notes unchanged.
