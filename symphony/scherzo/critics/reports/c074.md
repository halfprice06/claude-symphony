## Findings

### 1. 121-124 and 277-280: the sub. p bass has four instruments (applied)
**Problem.** After the ff tutti, the second idea starts sub. p (c063, c039). The slurred bass line is played by Vc and Bsn 2 at E2 and by Cb and Cbn at E1. The upper strings are marked sub. p, but the bass is as heavy as it was at f in bar 45. The contrabassoon on a soft slurred line two octaves below the violas thickens exactly the texture that c063 set out to lighten.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 130 (and Ex. V-6, PDF 131): the double bass doubles the cello in tuttis, "when a lighter string texture is desired, the double bass was eliminated", and it comes back for the pedal and the cadence. PDF 154: Tchaikovsky makes his diminuendo by scoring. PDF 137: a crescendo is built by adding octaves. Here the reed 16-foot can be held back until the crescendo starts at 125 and 281, which is also where Bsn 1 (c067) and Ob 1 (c044) join.
**Fix (applied).** sC.ily `sCCbsn`: 121-124 `R2.*4 | a,,2(\p\< c,4~ | ...`. dA.ily `dACbsn`: 277-280 `R2.*4 | a,,2\p\<( c,4~ | ...`. Cb, Vc and Bsn 2 keep the bass. The pitches are unchanged and no theme is touched.

### 2. 177-184: Fl 1 uses the colour that is reserved for T's return at 217 (applied)
**Problem.** At 169 the trio tune is scored as Adler's Brahms 3/iii model: Vln I sul A in its best register, Vln II an octave below, violas div. louré off-beats in a pale register, the cellos' countermelody, pizz. basses. At 177 Fl 1 joins with its heterophony and doubles Vln I's A6 at 179. c069 kept Fl 1 out of 209-216 so that its entry at 217, on the same climb to A6, would be "a colour held back for a specific event". The same colour on the same note has already been heard 40 bars earlier at p. The return therefore gains loudness but no new colour.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 143: the exposition is "assigned to the strings before it is developed, colored, or doubled by other members of the orchestra", and the foreground is introduced before anything is layered on it (Beethoven 7/ii). PDF 129 and 133-135: keep the higher colour for the more important later statement, otherwise "subsequent restatements [are] anticipated". This answers c051's reason for the 177 entry ("added colour saved for the climax"): c069 later moved that climax to 217, and the 177 entry now works against it.
**Fix (applied).** tA.ily `tAFluteOne = { R2.*16 | R2.*8 | R2.*8 | }`. Fl 1's first entry in the trio becomes the forte call at 197, then T at 217. The themecheck reports 41/41 unchanged, because the Fl 1 trio record line is at 217.

### 3. 331: the iv6's bass is doubled in the middle as well as at the bottom and the top (applied)
**Problem.** At the coda's Phrygian iv6 (C-E-A over C), C sounds in Cb and Cbn (C2), Bsn 2 (C3), Cl 2 (C4) and Vln I (C6, the c033 rising line). The root A is in the strings' quavers, Bsn 1, Hn 4 and the violas, but the woodwind middle has C4 and no A. Cl 2 here doubles the bass two octaves up, which hardens the 6/3.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 152 and Ex. V-27: in I6, IV6 and V6 with more than four voices the bass is doubled, but "it is advisable to double it near the bottom and leave the 'open' sound so characteristic of this inversion intact". PDF 151: doublings are thinner higher up, as in the overtone series.
**Fix (applied).** coda.ily `codaClTwo`: 331 `a2.` (A3) instead of `c'2.`. Cl 2 then moves E4-D4-A3-B3, and the A3 resolves up by step into V at 332. The bass C stays in Cb, Cbn and Bsn 2, and Vln I's C6 stays because it is the c033 line. There is no outer-voice change, and the analyser's 7 parallels are the same 7 as before.

### Considered, no change (my decision)
- Vln I's B3 dips at 16, 32, 256 and 272 (Shostakovich's cello octave, PDF 141): the cellos are pizz. on the downbeat, and since c073 Vln II's B3 already supports the note.
- A6 at 50 before the B6 peak (PDF 129): it is part of the second idea's contour (a recorded theme). The peak is still set apart by its one fff, by the tutti, and by B6 appearing only at 336.
- Hn 1's D5 against the second idea's lower octave at 45-46, and the horns at 209-224: Adler PDF 135 allows a background in the melody's register when its colour differs, and c054 has already cleared the one clash.
