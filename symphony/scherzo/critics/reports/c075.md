## Findings

### 1. The scherzo has no instrumental parts (MOLA PDF 5–8)
- **Bars:** whole movement.
- **Problem:** v75 has only the score. parts.ily holds one variable per player, but nothing engraves them. MOLA PDF 5 asks for computer-engraved parts in the transposed key, and PDF 6–8 set their format. Movement I has had a parts build since c185, so three movements of the set would be missing their parts.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-075/work):** four new files next to score.ly. They do not change the score.
  - `parts-lib.ily`: mvt1/v221's copy with five changes. (a) `(define (bar-moment b) (* 3/4 (- b 1)))` and the cue length `(* 3/4 n)` in `partCues`, for 3/4. (b) `pedal-letter-markup?` also drops markups starting "Ped." (the scherzo's harp settings). (c) and (d) are described under F2 and F4. (e) The audit's `last-end` starts at `(ly:make-moment 0)`, so first entries are checked too.
  - `parts-gen.py`: mvt1's generator with this movement's table. Clarinets `\transpose a c'` (instrumentTransposition A); bass clarinet in B♭, treble French notation `\transpose bes c''`; cor anglais and horns in F; horns, trumpets and timpani without key signatures; contrabassoon and basses an octave up. `sec` takes three arguments (name, bars, beats). Trumpet 3 gets a one-page sheet "III. Scherzo — tacet". Paper B4, staff 20.5 pt, `ly:page-turn-breaking`. Cues are read from `parts-cues.json`.
  - `build-parts.sh`: run after build.sh. It writes partsout/parts-NN-name.pdf.
  - Result: 29 parts, 0 warnings. Page counts: Fl 1 2, Fl 2 1, Ob 1 2, Ob 2 1, C.a. 1, Cl 1 2, Cl 2 2, B.cl. 1, Bn 1 2, Bn 2 2, Cbn 2, Hn 1 2, Hn 2 2, Hn 3 2, Hn 4 1, Tpt 1 1, Tpt 2 1, Tpt 3 1, Tbn 1 1, Tbn 2 1, B.tbn 1, Tba 1, Timp 2, Harp 4 (with cover), each string part 3.
- **Decided, no change:** the bass clarinet part reads F♯ major (six sharps) at K and V. It rests through both, and MOLA PDF 5 requires the transposed key at every line.

### 2. No cues in long rests (MOLA PDF 5, Ex. 4; Adler PDF 299 item 6)
- **Bars:** brass enter at 101 after 100 bars and at 301 and 333 after 181–213 bars. The bass clarinet enters at 329 after 180 bars, the contrabassoon at 281 after 149, the harp at 341 after 103, and Ob 2 at 197 after 91.
- **Problem:** a player counting 100 to 213 one-in-a-bar bars at 2. = 88 has nothing to check the count against. MOLA says cues "are expected during long periods of rest", in the reader's key, "audible and obvious".
- **Fix (applied):** 41 four-bar cues, at every entry after 40 or more bars' rest. Each is in small notes with the source labelled. Format: `[quote, label, direction, first bar, bars]` in `parts-cues.json`. Sources were chosen by register, so no cue needs more than three ledger lines in its reader's clef:
  - Vln I: Tpt 1·2 at 97 and 297; Fl 1·2, Ob 1·2 at 193 and 269; Ob 1 at 181; Ob 2 at 37; C.a. at 265; harp at 69 and 337 (3 bars).
  - Vc: Hn 3·4 at 69 and 297; B.cl. at 69, 135 and 325; Cbn at 41 and 277; Tba at 101 and 329; Tbn 1·2 and B.tbn at 329; Bn 2 and Hn 1·2 at 193.
  - Timp: Tbn 1·2 and B.tbn at 97 (the ritmo di tre battute strokes).
  - Ob 1: C.a. at 101 and 185. C.a.: Cl 2 and Bn 1 at 189. Bn 1: Bn 2 and Hn 2 at 269. Hn 1: harp at 145.
  - `\addQuote "cueCA" { \corAnglais }` is added to the quote list.
  - In `insert-cue`, a joined rest whose only articulation is a hairpin stop (Ob 2's `R2.\!` at 201) may take a cue after its first bar; before this change the 269 cue failed. I checked that transposed cues are transposed once: Hn 2's Bn 1 cue at 269 reads F♯3 F♯3 B3 for the concert B2 B2 E3.

### 3. Page turns in playing bars and in rests that are too short (MOLA PDF 6–7; Adler PDF 299 item 5)
- **Bars:** harp 184/185; any turn that only has a 2–4-bar rest.
- **Problem:** the harp's only turn fell at 184/185, inside the trio's unbroken left-hand crotchets (169–208). With a plain cover page it moved to 224/225, again in the left hand. `ly:page-turn-breaking` treats the right hand's rest as a turnable place even while the left hand plays. MOLA PDF 7 also says to consider the tempo. A 4-bar rest at 2. = 88 is 2.7 s, which is too short for a wind player who has to put the instrument down.
- **Fix (applied):**
  - Harp: a cover page (MOLA PDF 6 names this case), `page-breaking = #ly:optimal-breaking` for that book, and `{ \skip 2.*240 \pageBreak }` in the RH staff. Pages 2–3 face each other (1–240). The turn falls in the 96-bar rest 241–336, and page 4 holds 241–376.
  - All parts: `minimumPageTurnLength = #(ly:make-moment 15/4)`, five bars, about 3.4 s at 88 and 3.9 s at 76.
  - Every turn now comes in or before a rest of at least 5 bars: Fl 1 241–252, Ob 1 189–196, Cl 1 241–248, Cl 2 253–268, Bn 1 225–232, Bn 2 and Cbn 289–296, Hn 1·2 289–298, Hn 3 321–330, Timp 261–267, Vln I and Vc 153–168, Vln II and Vla 185–192, Cb 141–148.

### 4. Fermatas lost in the parts (MOLA PDF 5, Ex. 4; Adler PDF 299 item 4)
- **Bars:** 2 (the held G♯ pivot) and 376 (the final fermata).
- **Problem:** `partGlobalOf` joins neighbouring skips of \global so that multi-rests are not split. It also joined `s2.\fermata` into the skips around it and dropped the fermata. Every player resting in 1–12 would read one 12-bar rest with no hold, and one bar ahead of the horn. The G.P. at 340 survived only because a \tempo separated it. Movement I is not affected: its \global has no fermatas.
- **Fix (applied, in `merge-skips!`):** a skip with articulations is never merged.

```scheme
((and (eq? (ly:music-property (car l) 'name) 'SkipEvent)
      (null? (ly:music-property (car l) 'articulations))
      (pair? acc) (eq? (ly:music-property (car acc) 'name) 'SkipEvent)
      (null? (ly:music-property (car acc) 'articulations)))
```

Every part now shows the fermata at 2, "G.P." with a fermata at 340, and the fermata at 376.

### 5. Bsn 2 lacks the diminuendo at 203–204 (MOLA PDF 8: extracted parts are not error-free)
- **Bars:** 203–204.
- **Problem:** Bsn 1 has `b2.\>` at 203 into 205. Bsn 2, on the same staff, has `b,2. | b,2.` with no hairpin. The score shows one hairpin for both, but Bsn 2's own part would hold p while Cl 1·2 and Bsn 1 fade into the E-major return at 209. This was the only real difference I found in the per-player MIDI comparison. The project rule asks for equal simultaneous dynamics on a shared staff.
- **Fix (applied in sections/tB.ily, tBBsnTwo):** `b,2.\> | b,2. | \partCombineSoloI R2.\! |`. The score still prints one hairpin (checked at 160 dpi).

### Considered, no change
- Multi-rest bar ranges ("9–11", MOLA Ex. 3): MOLA calls them "helpful", not required. Each line starts with a bar number, and the letters break rests every 8–24 bars, as in Adler's Ex. XVIII-5. I decided they are not needed.
- Printed down-bows: MOLA PDF 5 allows them for a specific effect. c018's re-taken accented strokes are that effect.
- "Meno mosso" over Vln I's rest at V: at 100 dpi it starts at the first bar after the new key signature. It does not float.
