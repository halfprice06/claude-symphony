## Findings

### 1. Balanced beamed pairs point the wrong way between up-stems (applied)

**Bars.** Cor anglais and Cl 2 at 107 and 307 (`e'4-. b'8-. b'-. g'4-.`, the third bar of the tutti theme); violas at 331 (`a4-. c'8-. c'-. a4-.`, alto clef, the coda's stretto ostinato).

**Problem.** In each bar the crotchets on either side sit below the middle line and take up-stems; the quaver pair sits on the middle line, so LilyPond gives it its default down-stems. The bar reads up–down–up, with the staccato dots of the pair above the staff and those of the crotchets below. At 107/307 the cor anglais and Cl 2 are the lowest of the woodwinds doubling the tune, so the figure looks broken into two voices.

**Reading.** Gould PDF 39: "The context may determine the direction. For example, notes adjacent to the group in question may take stems in one particular direction." The example (PDF 39, second system) is this case: a middle-line beamed group between up-stem groups goes up, "rather than" down. "Where there is no obvious case for either direction, it is the convention to use down-stems." So only groups whose two neighbours agree should change. c095 (stems.ily) applies the same rule to single stems from Gould PDF 27–28, and leaves beamed groups out.

**Scan.** 60 beamed groups in the score are balanced about the middle line. 47 already agree with their neighbours. 8 have a down-stem before and an up-stem after (theme A's B4 pair in Vln I at 15, 31, 255, 271 and Vln II at 107, 307; the fugato head's D3 pair in the cellos at 59 and 291); there is no clear case, so they keep the conventional down-stems. The 5 groups above have up-stems on both sides.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-285/work).** In stems.ily I added a second pass to `middleLineStemEngraver`'s `finalize`, after c095's run loop: for each beamed group with no set direction whose noteheads balance about the middle line, it takes the own direction of the stem just before and just after it (within a bar, with the same `within?` and `c095-own-dir` as c095), and sets every stem of the group UP when they agree on UP (or when only one exists and it is UP). It sets the stems, not the Beam: setting `Beam.direction` in the engraver caused a cyclic `Stem.direction` dependency and a crash at 307. Part-combined voices are not touched (their stems have a set direction). The code, replacing the final `(run (1+ i))))))))))` line:

```scheme
                  (run (1+ i)))))
        ;; c285: balanced beamed groups follow the stems on either side (Gould PDF 39)
        (let bloop ((i 0))
          (if (< i n)
              (let ((b (and (beamed? i) (ly:grob-object (st i) 'beam))))
                (if (and (ly:grob? b) (not (number? (ly:grob-property-data b 'direction))))
                    (let* ((bs (ly:grob-array->list (ly:grob-object b 'stems)))
                           (k (let f ((k i)) (if (and (< (1+ k) n) (memq (st (1+ k)) bs)) (f (1+ k)) k)))
                           (ps (append-map c095-head-positions bs))
                           (balanced (and (pair? ps) (= 0 (+ (apply max ps) (apply min ps)))
                                          (every (lambda (s) (not (number? (ly:grob-property-data s 'direction)))) bs)))
                           (prev (and balanced (> i 0) (within? (mo (1- i)) (mo i)) (c095-own-dir (st (1- i)))))
                           (next (and balanced (< (1+ k) n) (within? (mo k) (mo (1+ k))) (c095-own-dir (st (1+ k)))))
                           (dir (cond ((and prev next) (if (= prev next) prev #f)) (else (or prev next)))))
                      (if (eqv? dir UP) (for-each (lambda (s) (ly:grob-set-property! s 'direction UP)) bs))
                      (bloop (1+ k)))
                    (bloop (1+ i)))))))))))
```

**Result.** A stem-log diff against v285 shows exactly ten stems changed, the five pairs above, all now up; nothing else in the score changed direction. The pairs' beams sit at 3.0 (stem 3¼ spaces from a note on a line, PDF 33) and pass every beam rule. I checked 105–110 at 250 dpi (/tmp/critic-285/cmp107.png, v285 above, new below) and 331 on page 11. Because parts.ly includes the same stems.ily, the parts get the fix too. This extends c095's engraver and does not remove anything from it, so it does not undo c095.

### Checked and kept (decided, no change)

1. **Fl 1 ledger groups 254–266 with 5.5-space stems.** PDF 33 requires one clear stave-line between the beam and the first ledger line, and the PDF 35 example puts such beams inside the stave. That makes the stems long. Keep.
2. **C.a. 189 and cellos 193/195, turn sloped 0.35.** With up-stems the lowest note of the turn is furthest from the beam, so the group is convex and slopes with its outer step (PDF 37); both ends are attached. Ob 1's turn at 185/197 with down-stems is concave and correctly horizontal (PDF 36 (c)). Keep.
3. **Fl 1 256, F5–C5 run, slope 0.48, shortest stem 2.98.** A ¼ slope would leave the last stem at 2.5 inside the stave, under PDF 35's 3-space minimum. Keep.
4. **The 8 middle-line pairs (theme A, fugato head) between a down-stem and an up-stem.** No clear context, so down by convention (PDF 39). Keep.

No other finding. On this fourth pass every other principle in PDF 31–41 that applies to the score is followed.
