### 6. The scherzo's place in the four-movement key plan (A&S PDF 443, 452–454): keep F

- Unit 26's large plans run I – VI – IV – V – I (Ex. 26-14, Beethoven's plan on PDF 454), with VI leading down in thirds to IV and IV reaching V through an augmented sixth. The symphony's movement keys are C – A♭ (VI) – E – C, and the finale opens with the German sixth on A♭ → V. The scherzo's E stands a semitone under IV (F). The symphony's only real IV areas before the finale are inside the scherzo: the F-major episode 73–88 and the Neapolitan 6/4 C–F–A at 331. The finale's IV6/4 over its C pedal at 272 (noted at c280) answers them.
- **Decision.** Keep the F-major episode and the N6/4 at 331 exactly as they are: they carry the symphony's IV. A later critic who proposes cutting the episode or replacing 331 with iv6 (cycle 80 replaced iv6 with it) would remove that link.
