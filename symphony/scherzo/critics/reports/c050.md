## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Coda 329–332: the woodwinds join the crescendo at the strings' level (applied)
**Problem.** At 329 the strings reach mf< on the way to ff at 333. Cl 1·2, the bass clarinet and Bsn 1·2 enter on sustained notes at mf<, the same level as the strings, and the contrabassoon enters mf< at 331. The new group arrives at full weight in the middle of the build. The swell then has nowhere to go but onto the strings' level, and the coda's peak (336, fff, the movement's only fff, kept below the finale's) is reached by adding mass rather than by a faster rise in the new colour.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov, PDF 127, "Amplification and elimination of tone qualities": "While the first group effects the crescendo gradually, the second group enters piano or pianissimo, and attains its crescendo more rapidly. The whole process is thereby rendered more tense as the timbre changes." The horns in the same build already follow this (Hn 3·4 pp< at 331, Hn 1·2 p< at 332, from c039). The woodwinds are the one group that does not.
**Fix (applied, coda.ily).** At 329: `codaClOne … g'2.\p\<`, `codaClTwo … e'2.\p\<`, `codaBassCl … e,2.\p\<`, `codaBsnOne … g2.\p\<`, `codaBsnTwo … e2.\p\<`. At 331: `codaCbsn … c,2.\p\< | b,,2.\f`. The shared staves keep equal dynamics, and the hairpins still end at the ff of 333. No notes change.

### 2. 205–209: the anticipated motto tail does not resolve in its own voice (applied)
**Problem.** At 205–208, Fl 2 and Cl 2 play G♯–A–B, then B–A♯–A. This is T's head running into the motto's chromatic tail B–A♯–A, at the coda's pitch an octave higher (c023, moved to the second players by c036). Both parts stop on A at 208. The G♯ that completes the quotation arrives only as the first note of T in Fl 1 and Cl 1 at 209, in other players. Everywhere else the scherzo states the tail complete in one part: theme A's G–F♯–F–E, and Hn 1's B–A♯–A–G♯ at 357–360. Movement II's clarinet (II 85–89) and the finale's horns (IV 273–275) do the same.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov, PDF 125–126, "Transference of passages and phrases": "In order to connect the phrases on each instrument in the best possible way, the last note of each part is made to coincide with the first note of the following one." Example 237 (PDF 407) shows this with every string group.
**Fix (applied, tC.ily).** Add `tCFluteTwo = { gis''2\f r4 | R2.*31 | }`, and at 209 `tCClTwo = { gis'2\f r4 | R2.*15 | …`. At 209 the second players play G♯ with the first players' entry of T, f with f. I tried carrying the slur across into 209. The part combiner then put the two voices on different contexts and the build gave 4 slur warnings, so the slur still ends at 208 and the G♯ is attacked with T's first note. The build is clean. The engraving is two voices in bar 209 only.

### 3. 357–360, the motto close: a false relation G♯4–G4, and bassoon octaves with Hn 1 (applied)
**Problem.** Under Hn 1's B4–A♯4–A4–G♯4, Vln II has G♯4 at 357 and leaps to C♯4 at 358, while the violas leap from E4 up to G4 at 358. G♯4 in one part and G♮4 in another part in the next chord, at the same pitch, is a false relation at the most exposed cyclic quotation in the movement. Bsn 1 makes the chromatic step G♯3–G3 an octave lower, which softens the clash but does not remove it. Bsn 1 then moves A3–G♯3 at 359–360 in octaves with Hn 1's A4–G♯4. That doubles the motto's last step and thins the four-part voice leading just where the finale picks this close up a major third lower.
**Evidence.** Rimsky-Korsakov, PDF 126–127: the progression of parts must stay "as regular as if there were no leap from octave to octave; this applies specially to chromatic passages in order to avoid false relation." His note (PDF 127) allows exceptions only for "extreme contrast of timbre between two adjacent chords". Here the two chords use the same instruments.
**Fix (applied, coda.ily).** Exchange the inner lines so that each chromatic step stays in one part:
- `codaVlnTwo` 357–364: `gis'2.\p | g'2. | e'2. | e'2. | gis'2.~\pp | gis'2.~ | gis'2.~ | gis'2 r4`
- `codaVla` 357–364: `e'2.\p^"unis." | cis'2. | c'2. | b2. | b2.~\pp | b2.~ | b2.~ | b2 r4`
- `codaBsnOne` 357–360: `gis2.\p | cis'2. | c'2. | b2.` (doubles the violas' C♯–C–B in unison)

Hn 1's line, the bass, the harmony (E – A♯°7 – iv6/4 – E) and the theme record are unchanged. c042's aim is kept: no part touches Hn 1's A4 at 359. The pitches of 361–364 are only exchanged between Vln II and the violas. The analyser shows the same 7 outer-voice parallels as v50.
