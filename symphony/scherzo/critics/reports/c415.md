## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The choirs are not visibly separated on the reduced pages (applied)
**Bars:** every two-system page. The worst cases are 190–210, 341–356 and 359–376.
**Problem:** The full pages (1, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12) keep Adler's "empty line": 12.4 spaces between choirs and 8.3 inside a choir. On the compressed two-system pages the gap is set only by the notes and dynamics, because LilyPond's default clearance is 1 space both between choirs and inside them. At 359 the gap Bn→Hn is 8.8 spaces and Fl→Cl is 9.7. At 190 Bn→Hn is 9.2 and Cl→Bn is 10.6. At 341 Hn 3·4→Harp is 8.8, the same as Ob→Cl, and the woodwind→brass gap is 9.8. A conductor reading these pages cannot see where the brass begins.
**Evidence:** Adler PDF 292: "It is imperative to leave space between the choirs on the score page. Leave an empty line between the winds and the brass…". PDF 291 item 2: the separation of choirs "must be immediately discernable, for a conductor has to read all the lines simultaneously". Ex. XVIII-1 (PDF 293) and Mahler's reduced page in Ex. XVIII-3b (PDF 296) both show the larger gaps.
**Fix (applied, score.ly `\layout`):**
```
\context { \StaffGroup \override StaffGrouper.staff-staff-spacing = #'((basic-distance . 9) (minimum-distance . 7) (padding . 0.5) (stretchability . 5)) \override StaffGrouper.staffgroup-staff-spacing = #'((basic-distance . 10.5) (minimum-distance . 8) (padding . 2.0) (stretchability . 9)) }
\context { \PianoStaff \override StaffGrouper.staffgroup-staff-spacing = #'((basic-distance . 10.5) (minimum-distance . 8) (padding . 2.0) (stretchability . 9)) }
```
The clear space between choirs is now at least 2 spaces. Inside a choir the minimum clearance is half a space, which is still clear at 150 dpi on pages 3, 7, 8 and 13 with no collisions. The full pages are unchanged (stretched as before). Measured after the change: at 341 woodwind→brass is 10.9 (inside the woodwinds 8.3) and brass→harp is 9.0 (inside 7.6). On page 7 Cbn→Hn is 10.2 against 7.4–7.6 for the neighbouring woodwind gaps. I tested the larger values first. Padding 3.5 gave 15 pages and padding 2.5 gave 14 pages, so padding 2.0 with 0.5 inside a choir is the largest setting that keeps 13 pages.
To keep v415's layout I fixed the page and system starts in global.ily: `\tag #'score \pageBreak` at 19, 58, 93, 111, 131, 173, 211, 253, 289, 305, 321, 341, and `\break` at 38, 190, 232. The tag keeps the page breaks out of the parts, and parts-lib already drops `\break`. The page starts are the same as v415's.

### 2. Five shared-staff entries start a system with no player label (applied)
**Bars:** Fl 1·2 at 38 (a2 since 37; the "a2" is on the previous system), Fl 1 at 176 (the "1." is at 172 on page 6), Fl 1 at 217 (the system starts at 211; the last "1." is at 205 on page 7), Cl 1 at 232 (the "1." is at 229, previous system), Hn 4 at 232 (c334's `\sysLabel "4."` is at 233, but the system now starts at 232).
**Evidence:** Adler PDF 295: "it is imperative that all the instruments must be clearly marked in all systems appearing on a page". Ex. XVIII-10a (PDF 301) names the player on the shared staff. This is c235/c325's rule; the system breaks have moved since (c395, c411, c412).
**Fix (applied):**
- sA.ily `sAFluteOne` bar 38: `b''2->-\sysLabel "a2"( bes''8 a''8`
- tA.ily `tAFluteOne` 176: `r4 dis'''8(\p-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." e'''8 fis'''4)`
- tC.ily `tCFluteOne` 217: `cis'''2(\mf\<-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." dis'''4` (a plain score-only label, because 217 is not the first bar of its system)
- tC.ily `tCClOne` 232: `b'2-\sysLabel "1."-\tweak stencil ##f \p) r4`
- tC.ily `tCHnFour` 232: `e2.---\sysLabel "4.")`, with the one at 233 kept in case the break moves back.
All five labels print and touch nothing (checked at 110–200 dpi). The parts are unchanged.

### Considered, not changed
- **A change a listener would hear.** Nothing in ch. 18 or App. A is about sound. It covers score layout, parts, proofreading, ranges and terms. The only performance risks it names are page turns without rests (PDF 299 item 5) and lost lines (item 7). I checked both in the Flute 1 and Harp parts and both are in order. A sounding change would have to come from another lens, not from this reading. score.midi is byte-identical to v415's.
- B6 at 336 without 8va (PDF 300, item 8a). Kept, as c055, c145, c235 and c325 decided: it is the movement's single peak note, and an octave sign would make it look like a lower note to a conductor reading at speed.
- Tempo words above the timpani (Ex. XVIII-1): kept off (c145, crowding).
- Hn 4 in treble clef at 229–236 (E3, three ledger lines): within Adler's four-ledger-line limit. The clef plan stays (c245).
