## Findings

### 1. Horn 4 is four bars late from bar 321 to the end (applied)

Bars 321–380. `dCHnFour` has 24 bars in a 20-bar section (16 bars of music plus eight `R2.` instead of four). Everything after it in Hn 4 is shifted four bars later. Effects in the score (p. 12, v24): Hn 4 is missing from the coda tutti at 333–336 (the Hn 3·4 staff shows "3." alone); it plays B3 dotted minims at 337–342, through the general pause at 340 and under Hn 1's dolce trio tune at 341–342, then an accented crotchet at 343. On the last page the Hn 3·4 staff runs four empty bars past the final barline (bars 377–380). The build gives no warning because the extra bars are whole bars, and countbars.py does not see the error because its regex only checks lines that end in `}` and this line ends in a `% c011` comment.

Evidence from the reading: in every tutti chord of the appendix (PDF 505–507) the four horns fill the middle between trumpets and trombones; at 333–339 the fifth B is held only by Cl 2 and Hn 4, so the missing Hn 4 leaves the fifth thin, and a horn sounding through the G.P. contradicts Rimsky's rule for silence before a re-entry (PDF 136).

Fix (applied in dC.ily):
```
dCHnFour = { ... | dis'4-> r4 dis'4-. | e'4-> r4 e'4-. | R2.*4 | }
```
The kit's counter should also strip trailing comments before matching (the regex `^{sec}(\w+) = {{ (.*) }}\s*$` fails on `} % ...`). I did not edit the kit.

### 2. The reprise tutti 305–320 is a literal copy of 105–120 (applied)

Bars 305–320. Every one of the 23 parts in dC 305–320 is identical to sC 105–120. Rimsky (ch. IV, PDF 117–118) says one idea "will be orchestrated in a different way every time it recurs", and names the means: transfer into other octaves, extension of the range by added octaves, changed details, changed dynamics. His Exs. 205 and 206 (PDF 368–369) show the same Sadko tutti twice: the second time the melody is spread over more instruments and the level rises to fff. Exs. 207 and 208 (PDF 370–374) do the same with the Christmas Night polonaise. In all his tutti examples (204, 205, 206, 208, 209) the flutes double the melody an octave above the oboes and violins. Here both flutes double Ob 1 and the violins in unison at E5–A5, the flute's middle register, so in ff the flutes add little.

Fix (applied in dC.ily): Fl 1 takes the upper octave in the second half of each phrase, 309–312 and 317–320 (top note A6, under the coda's B6). Fl 2 stays with Ob 1, so the E5 line keeps its strength. Fl 1 enters each high limb after a beat's rest (308 beat 3, 316 beat 3), so there is no awkward leap. Bars 305–308 and 313–316 stay as before, because there the violins already lift to B5–C6. The first tutti (105–120) is unchanged, so the reprise is the fuller of the two, as in Ex. 206.
```
dCFluteOne = { e''4-. b'8-. b'-. e''4-. | g''2->( fis''8 f''8 | e''4-.) b''8-. b''-. g''4-. | fis''4-. dis''-. r4 |
  e'''4-. b''8-. b''-. e'''4-. | a'''2->( gis'''8 g'''8 | fis'''4-.) dis'''8-. dis'''-. b''4-. | e'''4-. r4 r4 |
  a''4-. g''-. fis''-. | e''4-. dis''8-. dis''-. e''4-. | fis''4-.( g''8 a''8) b''4-. | c'''2->( b''4) |
  e'''4-. b''8-. b''-. e'''4-. | g'''2->( fis'''8 f'''8 | e'''4-.) dis'''8-. dis'''-. fis'''4-. | e'''4-. r4 r4 | R2.*4 | }
```

### 3. The scherzo's last ff chord (339) has the strings on E only (applied)

Bar 339. This is the single tutti chord that ends the scherzo proper before the general pause. The winds and brass spell E minor, but the strings play only E: Vln I E6, Vln II E5, Vla E4, Vc E2, Cb E1. In Rimsky's appendix of single tutti chords (PDF 505–507), the strings play triple or quadruple stops in almost every full-orchestra chord (chords 1–5, 7, 9–12, 17–20), and in chord 8 (Ivan the Terrible) they play a short sff chord in stops while the winds hold. In this bar the fifth B is only in Cl 2 and Hn 4, and the third G only in Ob 2, Hn 1, Tpt 1 and Tbn 1.

Fix (applied in coda.ily, bar 339 = coda bar 15): Vln I keeps E6, the melody's last note. Vln II plays the standard first-position quadruple stop G3–E4–B4–G5 (open G). Violas play B3–G4–E5 and cellos the fifth E2–B2. All are idiomatic stops for a short accented crotchet.
```
codaVlnTwo: ... fis''4 dis''2 | <g e' b' g''>4->\dnb r4 r4 | ...
codaVla:    ... dis'2. | <b g' e''>4-> r4 r4 | ...
codaVc:     ... b,2. | <e, b,>4-> r4 r4 | ...
```
No C-major triad results, and the outer voices (Vln I/Fl 1 E6 over Cb E1) are unchanged.
