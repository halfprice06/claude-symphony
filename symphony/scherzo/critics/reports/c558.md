## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 253–268: the cellos' countermelody sits at the accompaniment's level, under the bass. Applied.

**Problem.** At the reprise Vln I has theme A at mp leggiero. Under it the cellos play the arco countermelody marked "espress." (G3–A3–B3–E4, T's head in E minor since c539, the tenor line the tutti brings back at 305). It is marked pp, the same level as the pizzicato off-beats of Vln II and violas, and a level below the divided basses' pizzicato p on the downbeats. Hn 3 joins it at 261 at ppp. It has no hairpin in 16 bars, although Vln I swells to its C6 at 263–264 and the same line in the tuttis swells at 110–111 and 310–311 (c468). So the second voice of the reprise is printed at the third level. In the MIDI the cellos sit at velocity 50 against the basses' 57.

**Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 14: the focus levels are the soloist (primary), the background or counter-line (secondary) and the rhythm section with the bass (tertiary). PDF 13: the arranger directs the ear to the primary element without confusing it. Ex. 269 (PDF 205, image; PDF 207 text): the lead violins go pp < … mf. The violas' counter-melody, which "lends the right amount of melodic interest", is p with its own < > and reaches mf with the lead, a level under it and above nothing else. Ex. 2A (PDF 18): the strings under the solo are p with "poco < >" swells. The house rule (c100, Casanelles) is one level under the tune, which here means p, not pp. c359 set pp without giving a reason for that level. c447 placed Hn 3 a level under the cellos, and that relation is kept.

**Fix (applied, dA.ily).**
```
dAVc 253:   g2(\p^\markup \italic "arco, espress." a4 | ...            (was \pp)
dAVc 263-265: a2\< c'4 | b2-\tweak stencil ##f \mp\> a4) | g2(-\tweak stencil ##f \p e'4~ ...
dAHnThree 261: c'2.(\pp^\markup \italic "dolce" | b2. | a2\< c'4 | b2-\tweak stencil ##f \p\> a4) | g2(-\tweak stencil ##f \pp e'4~ ...   (was \ppp, no hairpins)
```
Now the order is lead mp, counter-line p, off-beats pp, with the bass at p. The cello line is in sustained arco in the tenor, so it carries over the plucked bass and stays out of Vln I's octave (Ex. 279–280). At 263–264 both the counter-line and Hn 3 swell with the lead's printed hairpin into the C6. Their hidden peaks are mp and p, so they stay a level under Vln I's mf, as the tutti line swells at 110–111. Notes, slurs, c449's phrase-by-phrase growth (cellos alone, then with Hn 3) and the Fl 1 heterophony from 261 are unchanged. In the MIDI the cellos go from 50 to 57, with 61–63 at 263–264, and Hn 3 from 31 to 37–44. Vln I stays at 67–93. The page prints the new p and the two hairpin pairs under Vln I's own hairpin, with no collisions.
