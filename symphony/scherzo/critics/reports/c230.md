## Findings

### 1. The mockup does not play the closing page's two fades, and its final fermata is shorter than a bar of the rit. (Applied; MIDI only, print identical.)

**Bars 362–364 (Fl 1, Cl 1·2, Vln II, Vla, Vc, Cb), 372–376 (Hn 1, global tempo).**

**Problem.** The printed close has two diminuendi on tied notes:
- the held chord, pp at 361 and > from 362 to its release on beat 3 of 364 (c123);
- Hn 1's last G♯4, pp at 368 and > from 372 to ppp at 375. This is the pivot the finale takes as A♭.

MIDI velocity is set once per Note On, so neither fade sounds in v230. In score.midi, Hn 1 starts G♯4 at velocity 37 at 368 and holds that level for 9.85 s to the end, above Vln I's ppp G♯5 (velocity 31). The chord likewise stops at its pp level. v230's score.midi has no controller 11 at all. The last bar, which carries the fermata, runs at \mt 100 and lasts 1.8 s. Bar 375 of the rit. lasts 1.29 s. So after the 375 chord, the held G♯ sounds alone for about 1.8 s, and the movement's last gesture is clipped in every render.

**Evidence.**
- Casanelles PDF 217 (Fig. 5.8). A dynamic change on a held chord is written in the DAW as continuous controller data under the note, not as a new note. Fig. 5.7 is the same gesture in score notation.
- PDF 210–212. The mockup is how the score is heard and approved. MIDI is the score's temporal equivalent. Dan hears the movement through these renders.
- PDF 211–212. Writers who share one template produce a score that feels like one composer's. Movement II already renders its own morendo on held notes with Staff.midiExpression (its c228, global.ily 57–59) and holds its final fermata bar 6.3 s. The scherzo's matching gesture (a held pivot note fading under a fermata) should render the same way, so that both joins (II → III and III → IV) sound as printed.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-230/work).** In score.ly, after `\include "parts.ily"`:

```
chordFadeMidi = { s2.*361 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.85 s2. \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.7 s2. \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.55 s2.
  \set Staff.midiExpression = #1.0 s2.*12 }
hornFadeMidi = { s2.*371 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.94 s2. \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.88 s2. \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.82 s2.
  \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.76 s2.*2 }
```

`\chordFadeMidi` is added to the staves of Flutes 1·2, Clarinets 1·2, Violins II, Violas, Violoncellos and Double basses, and `\hornFadeMidi` to Horns 1·2. Example: `<< \global \pc \removeWithTag #'part \hnOne \removeWithTag #'part \hnTwo \hornFadeMidi >>`. In global.ily, on the last bar: `\mt 50 s2.-\tweak staff-padding #2 \fermata` (was `\mt 100`). Comments are in both files.

Notes on the fix:
- 0.76 is the ppp/pp ratio of the score's own volume table (0.25/0.33).
- The steps fall on bar lines. A first version with one step per beat added spacing columns and changed page 13. With bar-line steps, all 13 pages are pixel-identical to v230's at 70 dpi.
- MIDI channels are per instrument. Vln II shares a channel with Vln I and is reset to 1.0 at 365, before Vln I enters at 369. Vc and Cb are reset before their pizz. at 375. Hn 3 shares the horn channel and stops at 369, before the horn fade.
- Result in score.midi: CC11 108/89/70 at 362/363/364 and 127 at 365 on the six chord channels; CC11 120/112/104/97 at 372–375 on the horn channel. The last bar lasts 3.6 s. The movement is 278.1 s long (was 276.3 s).
- The parts are not affected (parts.ly does not include score.ly; the global change is a MIDI tempo only).

### Points tested and rejected

- **Give the horn call movement I's long middle note.** Movement I's call has the proportions 1:4:2 (E♭ crotchet, C four crotchets, B two). The scherzo has 1:1:2 bars (G♯3, E4, D♯4 held). I considered holding E4 for two bars. Rejected:
  - Bars 1–7 are protected.
  - The finale relies on the D♯4 at 5–6 with no E4 at 7–8 (IV 219–220).
  - The contour and the rising sixth already name the source. Casanelles PDF 227: Zimmer's Superman theme keeps only its opening interval, and the reference still holds.
- **Fade every staff at 372–376 from global.ily, as movement II does.** Rejected. Vln I's G♯5 is printed ppp without a hairpin. A global fade would also weaken the new p/pp attacks at 375 (harp, pizz., timpani). The fade belongs to Hn 1 alone.
- **A new finale quotation of the trio tune T.** Rejected, as c130 rejected it. The finale's C-major close already carries F1, the chorale, the motto chain and theme A's head. Reserving the tune is the *Annihilation* principle (PDF 245): its effect is to belong to one movement.
- **The coda build 325–336 against Fig. 5.7.** It follows: a pp plateau of four bars, then mf, f, ff and the fff peak, with the steepest rise into 336 and an abrupt end at 339–340. No change (protected since c108).
