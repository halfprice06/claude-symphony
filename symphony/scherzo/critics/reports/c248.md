## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 207–209: the timpani roll stops on the downbeat of 208, a bar before T's forte return (applied)

**Problem.**
- 205–208 is the four-bar dominant pedal before the trio tune returns f at 209 (the trio's main arrival, c033, c179).
- The B-pedal parts crescendo through 208 into 209: violas and cellos `\<` from 207 to mf at 209, Hn 3·4 to p at 208, Bsn 1, Bsn 2 and Cbsn pp< from 207.
- The timpani roll alone peaks early: `b,2.:32\pp~ | b,2.:32~ | b,2.:32\<~ | b,4\mf r4 r4`. The roll is tied into a crotchet marked mf on the downbeat of 208 and the drum is then silent for beats 2–3. The next timpani note is the E at 209, f.
- The result is the loudest timpani point of the passage on the fourth (weakest) bar of the group 205–208, then two beats of silence in the drum at the moment the crescendo should be strongest.

**Evidence.**
- A&S PDF 53: in a four-bar group the first and third bars are strong and the second and fourth weak; the group 205–208 is weak at 208.
- A&S PDF 54: when the end of a group coincides with a goal of tonal motion, that is a phrase; the goal here is 209 (tonic, first bar of T, the next group's strong bar), and the violas' and cellos' crescendo is aimed there.
- A&S PDF 51: "the beginning of a note is heard as stronger than its continuation". The release stroke at 208 (mf, the top of the hairpin) is an attack, so it stands as an accent on a weak bar and contradicts the hypermetre the rest of the orchestra is building.
- Dvořák 9/iii (corpus, above): the B roll crescendos without a break into the E arrival.
- The score's own practice: every other roll that leads into an arrival on another drum (130→131, 286→287, 332→333) runs to the end of the bar before the arrival. 208 was the one exception.
- c056 tied this roll into a closing crotchet for notation reasons (Adler Ex. XII-47, an ending roll). That suits the quiet ending of the codetta at 240, which stays as it is. At 208 the roll does not end the music; it leads into the f arrival, so it belongs with 130/131, 286/287 and 332/333.

**Fix (applied).** tB.ily, `tBTimp`, last four bars:

```
b,2.:32\pp~ | b,2.:32~ | b,2.:32\<~ | b,2.:32 | }
```

The roll continues through 208 with the hairpin from 207, which now ends on the existing `e,4\f` at 209 (tC.ily unchanged), so the MIDI crescendo has a printed target. The tie 207→208 stays; there is no tie into 209 (another drum). A `% c248:` comment is added above `tBTimp`. No other part changes. The engraving on page 7 is clean: the roll and hairpin fill 205–208 and the f stands under 209.
