Verdict on the tunes. Theme A is the movement's most memorable idea: motive x (E–B–B–E) followed by the chromatic tail G–F♯–F–E, with the climb to C6 at 24. It stays in the ear as the Dvořák 9/iii head does. The second idea's hemiola, with its leaps B5–G6 and C♯6–A6, makes a real climax at 50, and its continuation is short. T is a hymn-like, stepwise tune, simple enough to hum. It has its own profile now: the tied A at 162, the 4–3 cadence, the turn to B at 180–184. Its rhythm is plain (minim + crotchet in five of the first nine bars), but earlier cycles already changed what could be changed without weakening it (c001, c081). The trio call's turn E–F♯–E–D♯–E gives the middle part a separate figure (c021, c091). Scored by Rimsky's ch. II, the melodies are almost all correct. One place breaks his rule on string-and-wind unison.

## Findings

### 1. Four winds on one string group at the counterstatement's arrival (41–43; 273–275). Applied.

**Bars.** 41–43 and 273–275: theme A in G (A5 D5 D5 A5 | C6 B5 B♭5 | A5 F♯5 F♯5 D5), f.

**Problem.** Since c107, Fl 1·2 and Ob 1·2 all play the tune a2 at Vln I's pitch. Vln I is the only string group on the line. Vln II plays off-beat C5 and A4 crotchets, which double Cl 1's held C5 and A4. The result is four winds against one string section on a tune that the strings have carried since 13.

**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 76: "In such combinations the strings will predominate provided that the two instruments are of equal power, e.g. when violins are coupled with an oboe... If several wind instruments play in unison with one group of strings, the latter will be overpowered." PDF 66–67: coupling two instruments of one kind adds power but takes away expression. The scan in "What else I studied" found no other passage outside the tuttis where more than three winds stand on one string part. In the tutti at 105–120 Vln II carries the lower octave, and at 333 both violin groups carry the tune. The tune was strings and flutes at 37–40, so at 41 the colour should thicken, not switch to the winds. The off-beats Vln II gives up are not missing: the violas keep them, and Cl 1 already holds the D7's seventh. I keep c107's a2 winds, which took the held notes out of the theme's register. Only the string side is strengthened, so this does not undo c107.
