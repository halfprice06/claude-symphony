## Findings

### 1. Trio, bar 195: the violas' turn is transposed exactly, so A♯ clashes with the violins' A♮ (applied)

**Problem.** At 193–196 the violas repeat the trio call's five-note turn as a two-statement sequence a step apart, under the violins' phrase. At 193 (over F♯ minor) the turn is A–B–A–G♯–A. At 195 (over the cadential 6/4 in E) it is B–C♯–B–A♯–B. A♯ keeps the semitone of the first statement, but it is not in the key. It also produces two chromatic successions split between voices within one beat. Vln I and II play A5/A4 on the half of beat 2 (`b''4.( a''8 gis''4~`), and the violas' A♯3 follows on beat 3. Then Cl 1 enters on A4, the seventh of V7, on the downbeat of 196. The score is careful about this cross relation elsewhere. c042 put F♯ major → F♯ minor at 192–193 into one voice (Cl 1 A♯–A), and c116 damps the harp at 193 so that the A♯ does not ring into the A♮.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 265: "When the pattern repeats in a diatonic sequence, the qualities of chords and melodic intervals will change as needed to conform with the key", as with the half step becoming a whole step in Ex. 17-1. The trio already follows this at 189. There the cellos' answer to the oboe's turn E–F♯–E–D♯–E is D♯–E–D♯–C♯–D♯, with the diatonic whole step C♯, not C𝄪. A&S PDF 394 (Ex. 23-12a): a chromatic succession split between two voices "can sound disagreeable", and here the split runs from the top voice (Vln I) to an inner one. In one voice it is unobjectionable (Ex. 23-12b), which is why c042 moved 192–193 into Cl 1.

**Fix (applied, sections/tB.ily, tBVla bar 195).**
```
b4( cis'8 b a b)        (was b4( cis'8 b ais b))
```
The turn now has the whole-step lower neighbour, as at 189. The violas' A3 on beat 3 rubs a semitone against Bsn 1's G♯3, the mirror of the G♯3 against Bsn 1's A3 at 193, which is the normal sound of a neighbour note against a held chord tone. In the key signature of E major, the A now prints without an accidental. The scan of 185–208 finds no new parallels. The chords, the violins' 7/6–5 suspension (c061) and the 197–200 layering are unchanged.
