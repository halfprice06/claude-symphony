## Findings

### 1. Bars 253–353 are crowded: crotchets nearly as narrow as quavers (applied)

Bars: the five systems 253–270, 271–292, 293–311, 312–331, 332–353 (pages 9–12).

Problem: LilyPond's automatic breaks put 18–22 bars of dense quaver texture (Fl 1 heterophony, the tutti 305–320, the coda ostinato 321–331) on each of these systems. The quavers cannot shrink below about 1.95 spaces (head, beam and accidental widths), so all the compression falls on the longer values. Measured mid-bar (no barline in the span):

| System | quaver | crotchet | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–252 (13 systems) | 1.70–2.25 | 2.85–3.12 | 1.35–1.69 (most 1.5) |
| 253–270 | 1.95 | 2.49 | 1.28 |
| 271–292 | 1.79 | 2.47 | 1.39 |
| 293–311 | 1.95 | 2.25 | 1.15 |
| 312–331 | 1.95 | 2.38 | 1.22 |
| 332–353 | 1.95 | 2.42 | 1.25 |

In five bars the crotchet gets less space than the quaver after it: Fl 1's `g'''8( a''' g'''4 fis'''8 f''')` at 254 and 258 (2.49 against 2.75) and in the tutti at 306, 310, 318 (2.02–2.14 against 2.75). At 306 the crotchet G6 stands as close to its neighbours as the quavers do and reads as part of a quaver run. A test without the natural on F6 gave the same squeeze, so the accidental is not the cause; the system width is.

Evidence: Gould PDF 53: "Durations should be spaced in relative proportion to each other, each value taking only a little more space than the next shortest value" (table: quaver 2½, crotchet 3½ units). PDF 54: "Each longer value ... should have more space than its shorter neighbour (a), or at least as much (b)", with the "not acceptable" example of exactly this squeeze; and "Where the density of the notation increases, the uniform space for a note-value may need to be noticeably different from that of an adjacent system", i.e. fewer bars on a denser system.

Fix (global.ily): system breaks at 269, 289 (letter S, already a double bar), 305 (letter T), 321, 341 (letter V, already a double bar) and 359. I tried six break plans in probe builds; this one is the only one that keeps 13 pages and brings every system to Gould's proportion:

```
  s2.*16 \break        % Q 253: was s2.*24
  s2.*8
  ...
  \bar "||" \break \mark \default % S 289
  s2.*16
  \break \mark \default % T 305
  s2.*16 \break        % was s2.*20
  s2.*4
  ...
  \bar "||" \break \key e \major \mark \default % V 341
  ...
  s2.*2 \break s2.*11 \once \override Score.MetronomeMark...   % W 357: was s2.*13
```

Result: 253–268 1.54, 269–288 1.51, 289–304 1.63, 305–320 1.50, 321–340 1.35, 341–358 1.60, 359–376 1.87. The crotchet at 254 and 258 now has more space than the next quaver, and at 306 and 318 as much (2.84 against 2.91, Gould's case (b)). Seven systems instead of six from 253; the last page now holds two systems (341–358 and 359–376), so the page count stays at 13. Pages 1–8 are identical. The parts are unaffected: parts-lib.ily drops \break from \global.

### 2. Player labels at the new system starts (applied)

Bars 305 and 359. With 305 now a system start, Fl 1·2 printed "a2" twice: once from the part combiner and once from c235's `\sysLabel "a2"`, which had been suppressed while 305 stood mid-system. With 359 a system start, Hn 1's solo (357–366, Hn 2's rests hidden) began the system without "1.". Gould PDF 55: no characters should collide, symbols evenly spaced; Adler II PDF 295 (c235's source): every shared instrument marked in every system.
Fix: dC.ily, `e'''4-.\ff b''8` (the `-\sysLabel "a2"` removed at 305); coda.ily, `| a'2.-\sysLabel "1." | gis'2.)~` in codaHnOne at 359. I checked every shared staff at the other new starts (269 Bn "1." and Cl chords, 289 Bn "1.", 321 Cl and Bn chords, 341 Hn 3 "3."): the combiner already labels them. The existing sysLabels at the old starts (271, 293, 312, 332, 354) now fall mid-system and are suppressed by c235's own test.

### 3. Hn 3 at 343, dotted crotchet against the quaver before D♮: checked, no change

In the old layout Hn 3's `gis'4. b8 d'4~` at 343 gave the dotted crotchet 3.52 spaces and the quaver (followed by the natural on D4) 3.57: equal within 0.05, which PDF 54 (b) accepts. I tested Gould PDF 57's remedy (less space around the accidental) in a small file: `Accidental.extra-spacing-width = #'(+inf.0 . -inf.0)` brings the quaver to 2.54 but puts the natural on the quaver's flag; `#'(-0.5 . 0)` gives 3.23 against 3.38 and clears it. After finding 1 the bar sits on a 1.60 system and no longer needs it, so I made no change.
