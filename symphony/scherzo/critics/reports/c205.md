## Findings

### 1. Ob 2's two rests at 105 and 305 sit at different heights (applied)

**Bars.** 105 and 305, oboe staff (`\partCombineApart`).

**Problem.** Ob 1 plays E5, then B4–B4 quavers, then E5. Ob 2 plays E5 and then two crotchet rests. Its beat-2 rest is pushed down to staff-position −6 (−4.25 to −1.44 spaces) to clear Ob 1's B4 quavers. Its beat-3 rest sits one space higher, at −4 (−3.25 to −0.44). The result is a staggered pair of rests that rises again inside a single bar of the lower part.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 49: when rests leave the centre they move by exact spaces and stay correctly placed against the stave lines. PDF 50: lower-part rests go below the centre line. PDF 51: a rest's level follows the surrounding pitches, and Gould's examples keep one part's rests in a bar at one level unless a note of the other part forces a change. On beat 3 nothing forces a change: Ob 1's E5 is above the centre line. The probe confirms the two heights, and the 200 dpi image of page 4 shows the step.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-205/work).** sC.ily and dC.ily, score only, so the Ob 2 part keeps centred rests as PDF 49 asks:
```
sCOboeTwo = { \partCombineApart e''4-. r4 \tag #'score \once \override Rest.staff-position = #-6 r4 | ...
dCOboeTwo = { \partCombineApart e''4-. r4 \tag #'score \once \override Rest.staff-position = #-6 r4 | ...
```
Both rests now sit at −6, checked at 200 dpi.

### Other points checked and kept (no change)

- **Hn 3·4 drone at 153–168 (three ledger lines, Hn 4's rests at 166 below the stave).** Gould PDF 46 sets three ledger lines as the point where a change becomes worth considering, not as a limit, and PDF 47 says players read four at a glance. The rests at 166 sit at the drone's level, as PDF 51 asks. A bass clef for 16 bars would add two clef changes around the trio's first page for a passage that reads cleanly. Kept.
- **B6 at 336 (Fl 1, Vln I, five ledger lines) without 8va.** PDF 47 allows woodwind and strings up to five lines, and c055 kept this too. Kept.
- **Tuba E1 and harp F♯1 (four lines).** Neither instrument has a lower clef. PDF 47 says four lines are read at a glance. Kept.
- **Cl 2 D♯3 at 87–88 (four lines on a shared staff).** It is within PDF 47's five lines, and a clarinet does not change clef. Kept.
- **Bottom-line whole-bar rests for the second player (Cl 2 at 85/192/210–212, Hn 2 at 357–366).** This is the outer-line practice of PDF 51. Kept.
