## Findings

The music passes the release check. The four findings are about the copy that leaves this loop: its title page, its parts, its record for the other movements, and its comments.

### 1. Title page still says "draft v200" (score.ly header; applied)
Problem. The release copies of movement I and movement II dropped "draft" and give version and date: "Full score in C — v221, 12 October 2026" (mvt1 v221 score.ly) and "Full score in C — v201, 13 October 2026" (mvt2 v201, its c200: "release copy"). The scherzo still prints "Full score in C — draft v200".
Evidence. MOLA PDF 6: the score's cover carries the title as in a concert programme and "a catalog number, print date, or revision date when applicable". Casanelles PDF 222: shared material should read as the work of one composer, which here means the four title pages follow one form (the finale thread kept "draft v201"; that is its decision, and two of the three finished movements use the release form).
Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-200/work/score.ly):
```lilypond
subsubtitle = \markup \center-column { "Full score in C — v201, 17 October 2026" \small "Contrabassoon and double basses sound an octave lower than written" \small "After movement II (Adagio): a pause" \small "Trumpet 3 tacet" \small "Duration: about 4½ minutes" }
```
kit/promote.sh replaces `draft v$PREV"` with `draft v$NEW"`. With this line that sed finds nothing, which is what a release copy needs: promote v201 from this scratch copy and the label stays as written.
