## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Hn 1·2, bars 357–366: Hn 2's rests are printed under Hn 1's solo, and there is no "1." at 357 (applied)
Problem: Hn 1 plays the motto's chromatic tail alone at 357–360. The staff shows Hn 2's bar rests under it, with no player label. At 365–366 Hn 1 holds G♯ alone and Hn 2's bar rests are printed again. Hn 1's crotchet rest at 366 is pushed above the top line. Twelve bars later, at 369, the same staff shows the same player's solo the other way: "1." and no rests. The part combiner could not mark these bars as a solo because Hn 1's tie runs into and out of the bars where Hn 2 plays (361–364).
Evidence: Gould PDF 50–51: separate sets of rests are for two parts with different durations sounding together; otherwise parts share a rest, and an upper-part rest stays just above the middle line, not outside the stave. PDF 13: one convention, applied consistently. The c135 rule for this score is a solo label after rests on a combined staff.
Fix (score only; notes and MIDI unchanged), sections/coda.ily:
```
codaHnOne: ... | R2.*16 | b'2.(\p-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." \<-\tweak outside-staff-horizontal-padding #1 ^\markup \italic "espress." | ... | gis'2 \tag #'score \once \override Rest.staff-position = #2 r4 | R2. | ...
codaHnTwo: ... | R2.*16 | \tag #'score \override MultiMeasureRest.transparent = ##t R2.*4 \tag #'score \revert MultiMeasureRest.transparent | e'2.\pp | e'2.~ | e'2.~ | e'2 r4 | \tag #'score \override MultiMeasureRest.transparent = ##t R2.*2 \tag #'score \revert MultiMeasureRest.transparent R2.*10 | }
```
I tried \partCombineSoloI first. It breaks the ties at 360/361 and 364/365 ("unterminated tie" warnings), so I did not use it. The motto tail's notes, slur and dynamics are unchanged; themecheck reports mottoTail HnOne 357 ok.

### 2. Cb, bar 3: the period of "tutti pizz." touches the barline of bar 4 (applied)

### 3. Bsn 1, bar 269: "scherzando" runs past the final barline of the system (applied)
Problem: the word starts at the last bar of the page-9 top system and its last letters hang beyond the closing barline, into the right margin.
Evidence: Gould PDF 56–57: leave a stave-space at the end of the stave. PDF 55: no collisions. PDF 44 treats the last barline as the limit even for an ottava line.

### 4. Harp RH, bars 149 and 151: the crotchet rest nearly touches the arpeggio sign (applied)
