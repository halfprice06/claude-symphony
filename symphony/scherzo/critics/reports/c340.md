## Findings

### 1. Bars 148–149: the pivot G♯ stops a beat before the drone and the harp take it over. Hold Hn 1's G♯4 into the downbeat of 149 (applied)

- **Problem.** The link plays the introduction in reverse. Hn 1 holds G♯4, movement II's A♭, from 145, and at 147–148 turns it into T's first two notes (G♯–A–G♯, c051).
  - Hn 1, the bassoons and the bass clarinet all stop on beat 2 of 148.
  - Beat 3 of 148 is silent in the whole orchestra.
  - On 149 beat 1, Hn 3·4 enter on E3/B3 (c333), with no G♯.
  - The harp's G♯4 comes on 149 beat 2.

  So the movement's pivot pitch is absent for two beats at the point where it passes into the trio. c333 wrote that the drone enters "as Hn 1's G sharp ends … the horn sound is dovetailed across 148–149". In the notes, and in score.midi (Hn 1 G♯4 ends at 148.67, the harp starts at 149.33), the two are not dovetailed.
- **Evidence.**
  - Adler PDF 190–191, XV-37: a held note is coloured by passing it on, each instrument entering "before the previous one stops".
  - Adler PDF 192, XV-40: where the colour changes, "make certain that the seams don't show", unless a seam is wanted.
  - Here no seam is wanted. There is no double bar until 153, the phrase is 145–152, and c333's own purpose was continuity.
  - Adler ch. 1, PDF 20 ("hearing mentally"): the printed rest on beat 3 looks like an ordinary phrase end, but heard, it is a silent beat in the whole orchestra between Hn 1 and the drone.
  - The scherzo already treats the same pitch this way at both ends of the movement. At 2→3 Hn 1 holds G♯4 straight into the call, and at 360–376 the G♯ passes Hn 1 → Hn 3 → Hn 1 + Vln I with overlaps (c140, c240).
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-340/work/sections/link.ily).**

  ```
  linkHnOne = { g'2.~\pp | g'2.~ | g'2.~ | g'2 r4 | gis'2.~\< | gis'2.~ | gis'2(\p\> a'4 | gis'2.~ | gis'4) r4\! r4 | R2.*3 | }
  ```

  (was `… | gis'2) r4\! | R2.*4 | }`).
  - Beat 3 of 148 is now Hn 1's G♯4 alone, as in bars 1–2.
  - The drone enters under it on 149.
  - Hn 1 leaves on 149 beat 2, the beat the harp plucks G♯4–B4–E5. G♯4 then passes Hn 1 → harp → Cl 1 (153) with no gap.
  - The diminuendo runs to 149 beat 2, so the drone's pp is not covered.
  - Nothing else changes: the bassoons and bass clarinet still stop at 148 beat 2, there is no new pitch, and Hn 2 rests.
  - In score.midi, Hn 1 G♯4 now lasts 148.0–149.33, ending as the harp starts.
- **Build.** 0 warnings and 13 pages. On page 6 the tie and the hairpin cross the 148|149 barline cleanly.
