### Considered and not raised

- **45–46 and 49–50, outer voices 5-8-8.** In the first and third hemiola units the melody and bass meet on the middle minim (B5/B1, C♯6/C♯2) and again on the last (G6/G1, A6/A1), so the outer voices make octaves by contrary motion. A&S PDF 269 prefers 5-10, 10-8 or 10-10 to the empty 8-5 pattern. But the bass is c032's sequential bass (do-not-touch since c102 and c212), the melody is the recorded second idea, and the octaves are filled by Cl 1·2, the violas and the horns in thirds and sixths. A&S PDF 269 says the 8-5 pattern is empty "unless decorated", and it is decorated here. Any fix would rewrite the bass or the theme. Left.
- **49–52, inner voices not transposed from 45–48.** Unit 2 does not repeat unit 1's harmony a step higher (that would bring F♯° at 51 and a C-major triad at 52). It becomes V/V–V4/3 of D–D6–V6/5 of D, into the cadential 6/4 in G. A&S PDF 282 (sequences leading to V, ♯4̂ on the second step) covers this. Inner voices that follow the new harmony instead of the old pattern are correct. Left.
- **330, Vln II E5–B4 over D.** E5 is a ninth over the bass D. It is the ostinato's common tone, and it moves up to F5 at 331. Each diatonic alternative makes octaves: D5 with the basses (E–D), A4 with the cellos (E–A), F♯5 with Cl 1 at 331 (F♯–F). This is the voice-leading corrective A&S PDF 267 describes. Left.
- **331→332, Vln II and Vc F→B in double octaves.** Both leap a tritone from ♭2̂ to 5̂. ♭2̂ is doubled in five parts here. Vln I, Cl 1 and Hn 3 take the normal diminished-3rd descent F–D♯ (A&S PDF 484), and A&S PDF 488 treats a ♭2̂ doubled in an inner voice more freely (it may even go straight to ♮2̂). The leap reaches the root of V on the motive x downbeat (c080, c089). Left.
- **219→220, violas C♯4–F♯3 under Vln I F♯6–B5.** These are 5ths on two off-beats with a rest between them. Vln I passes through C♯6 on the downbeat, and the violas take F♯ by c282's fix (A&S PDF 151). Not consecutive in the sense of A&S PDF 267. Left.
- **68→69, outer octaves E–A at the stretto.** The top voice changes: Vln II ends its entry and rests, and Vln I enters after a rest in a new register. This has been by design since v01. Left.
- **79→80, B♭ of E°7 in harp RH, harp LH and Bsn 1 resolving to A in octaves.** This is the harp melody's own resolution, doubled by its accompaniment, inside the protected F-major episode. Left.
- **121–128 / 277–284.** In minor the composer did not transpose c032's major-key bass (E–G–E | B–A–G, then A–C–B | E–F♯–G). The rise skips ii° and closes iv6–V♭9, exactly as A&S PDF 283 advises for rising from i in minor. Left.
