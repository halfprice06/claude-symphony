## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Player labels are lost at system breaks on shared staves (applied)
**Bars:** 75 (Bn 1·2: Bsn 1), 93 (Ob 1·2: Ob 1), 111 (Fl a2, Ob 1, Cl 2), 131 (Fl 1, Ob 1), 234 (Bn 1·2: Bsn 1; Hn 3·4: Hn 4), 270 (Bsn 1), 312 (Fl a2, Ob 1, Cl 2), 354 (Cl 1).
**Problem:** the part combiner prints "1.", "2." or "a2" only where the state changes. When a solo or a2 runs over a system break, the new system shows a single line with no label. At 93, the first bar of page 4, the oboe staff shows one line; the "1." is on the last bar of page 3. At 111 and 312 the clarinet staff's single line is Cl 2 alone (Cl 1 rests 106–120), next to flutes that are a2 and oboes that are Ob 1 alone, and none of the three is marked. The conductor cannot tell solo from a2, which decides the balance of the tutti theme.
**Evidence:** Adler PDF 295 on reduced scores: "it is imperative that all the instruments must be clearly marked in all systems appearing on a page". PDF 291, item 2: "every instrument appropriately labeled". Ex. XVIII-10a (PDF 301) marks the player ("1.") on the shared staff wherever one player plays.
**Fix (applied):** global.ily defines `\sysLabel`, a score-only post-event (tagged 'score, so the parts are unchanged). Its after-line-breaking callback prints the label only if its note is the first musical column of its system, and drops it anywhere else. If the layout changes later, a label that no longer falls at a system start disappears instead of cluttering mid-system. Its height is ignored in the page-breaking estimate (an unpure-pure Y-extent, pure height zero) but used in final placement, so slurs and accents are avoided and the line and page breaks stay exactly as in v235. Without that, the score went to 15 pages.
```
#(define (c235-first-in-system grob)
  (let* ((col (ly:item-get-column grob)) (sys (ly:grob-system grob))
         (left (and (ly:grob? sys) (ly:spanner-bound sys LEFT))))
    (if (not (and (ly:grob? col) (ly:grob? left)
                  (<= (- (ly:grob-property col 'rank 0) (ly:grob-property left 'rank 0)) 1)))
        (ly:grob-set-property! grob 'stencil #f))))
#(define c235-label-extent (ly:make-unpure-pure-container ly:grob::stencil-height (lambda (grob start end) '(0 . 0))))
sysLabel = #(define-event-function (txt) (markup?)
  #{ -\tag #'score -\tweak Y-extent #c235-label-extent -\tweak after-line-breaking #c235-first-in-system ^\markup \bold #txt #})
```
The labels go on the first note of each listed bar, e.g. sB.ily `sBOboeOne` bar 37 `a''2-\sysLabel "1."->(`, sC.ily `sCClTwo` bar 7 `fis'4-\sysLabel "2."-.)`, sC.ily `sCFluteOne` bar 7 `fis'''4-\sysLabel "a2"-.)`, tC.ily `tCHnFour` bar 26 `e2.-\sysLabel "4."--`, coda.ily `codaClOne` bar 30 `gis''2-\sysLabel "1."--` (14 labels in sB, sC, tC, dA, dC, coda). Checked at 200 dpi: every label prints, none touches a slur, accent or the staff above. If the composer moves a system break, the label belongs on the first bar of the new system.

### 2. Bassoon 1's pp at bar 7 is printed under Bassoon 2's held note (applied)
**Bars:** 4–8.
**Problem:** Bsn 2 holds B2 pp from bar 4. Bsn 1 enters on B3 pp at 7. The two voices are apart, and the pp is printed below the staff, under Bsn 2's tied B2, so it reads as a second pp for Bsn 2. Bsn 1's entry has no dynamic of its own on the page.
**Evidence:** Adler PDF 294: dynamics go below "unless two instruments share a staff and two different sets of dynamics are required". Ex. XVIII-10a (PDF 301) prints Oboe 1's dynamics above the shared staff and Oboe 2's below.
**Fix (applied, intro.ily):** `introBsnOne = { R2.*6 | b2.~-\tag #'score ^\pp -\tag #'part \pp | b2\> r4\! | R2.*4 | }`. The score shows pp above, at Bsn 1's entry. The Bassoon 1 part keeps it below the staff, and its text is identical to v235's. The dynamic level, and so the MIDI, is unchanged.

### 3. The last timpani muta is written below the staff (applied)
**Bars:** 375–376.
**Problem:** "muta: low E in G, G in A♭, B in C, high E in E♭ (for the Finale, in the pause)" is printed below the timpani staff, where it sits nearer the harp than the timpani. The other two mutas in the movement (145, 225) and the stick changes are above the staff.
**Evidence:** Adler PDF 294: the instruction "change to" / "muta in" "should be given well ahead of time and should be written above the player's staff."
**Fix (applied, coda.ily `codaTimp`):** `R2._\markup ...` → `R2.^\markup ...` (same right alignment). It prints above the fermata, clear of Hn 3·4. This touches the protected last page (c214/c215). The reason given there was that the page's layout and collisions are settled. This change keeps that: no note, system or page moves, and nothing collides (checked at 150 dpi). Only the side of the staff changes, to Adler's.
