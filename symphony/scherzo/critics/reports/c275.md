## Findings

I found nothing in this score that breaks the assigned reading, so I propose no change. Below are the points I checked closely and the decision on each.

1. **Bassoons 213–216 (Bsn 1 E4/F♯4 over Bsn 2's moving line).** At 160 dpi Bsn 1's dotted minims seemed to have no stems, which would break Gould PDF 24 (a white note is read by its shape, so a stemless dotted minim reads as a semibreve). The probe and a 500-dpi crop show the stems are there. They are 2½ spaces, the double-stemmed minimum of PDF 28, with the tenuto just clear of the stem end (c125 offset). Keep.
2. **Clarinets 87–88 and 285–287, Cl 2 C♯3/D3: four ledger lines below the treble stave.** PDF 21 says to use up to at least three ledger lines before changing clef. This is one beyond that, but Cl 1 shares the staff, so a bass clef would move Cl 1 too, and the A-clarinet part reads written E3/F3 (three ledger lines). Keep.
3. **Harp LH F1/G1 at 83, 158–163, 218–219: four ledger lines.** There is no lower clef, and octave signs belong to the ledger-line reading (PDF 41–47; c185 kept them). Keep.
4. **Bass trombone and tuba tenutos at the stem end (101–120, 333–335).** They sit far from the E1/E2 noteheads because PDF 28 extends ledger-line stems to the middle line. s125 put them there on purpose so they do not read as an extra ledger line or touch the f (PDF 55–56). The assigned reading does not answer that reason. Keep.
5. **Clef positions (301, 331, 341, 345, and the harp part's 341).** All follow PDF 21–23 (rules 7–13). Keep.
