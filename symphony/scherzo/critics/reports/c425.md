## Findings

### 1. Harp RH, 349–361: the return to treble clef stands at an arbitrary point (applied)
Problem: the harp's right hand plays its last bass-clef chord at 348 and then rests twelve bars, across the system break at 359. The second system of page 13 opened the right-hand stave in bass clef with two bars' rest, and the treble clef appeared in the middle of the system before 361.
Evidence: Gould PDF 23: after a period of rests, return the instrument to its commonest clef and "place the clef change at the end of the system after the player has finished: this is the least disruptive position as regards spacing. It is better than an immediate clef change before rests, which draws unwanted attention to the new clef at an arbitrary point"; it also helps a score reader identify staves by their clefs. PDF 21: a clef for a new system is announced at the end of the previous one. The opposite case (keep an unusual clef until the barline before the entry, c155 at 331) does not apply: here the change is back to the usual clef.
Fix, sections/coda.ily, codaHarpR:
```
old: ... | R2.*3 R2.*9 | \clef treble <e' gis' b' e''>2.\arpeggio | ...
new: ... | R2.*3 R2.*7 \clef treble R2.*2 | <e' gis' b' e''>2.\arpeggio | ...
```
Result: a cautionary treble clef at the end of 358, and the 359 system opens with the harp in treble. Print only; notes, MIDI and layout unchanged.

### The audible candidate
This reading governs how notes are written, not which notes sound. The two rules in it with a consequence for sound are PDF 60 (only strings may put different durations on one stem, because the bow sustains only two notes: a held triple stop would be broken and lose a note) and PDF 52/65 (unisons on a shared staff need two stems or an "a2", or a doubling may be dropped). I checked both across the score: every sustained string chord is a double stop or is divided (169, 209, 349), and every struck multi-stop (72, 300, 305, 339) is a crotchet or carries "non div."; the shared wind staves show two stems or "a2" on their unisons. So the reading gives no reason for a change a listener would hear, and I did not invent one outside it.

Not raised: Vln I B6 at 336 without 8va (PDF 47 allows five ledger lines; c055/c325 kept); bar-length notes 368–376 at bar start (c035).
