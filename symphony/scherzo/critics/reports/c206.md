## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 188–189: the B pedal has no time to return from B♯ to B♮. Applied.
- **Problem.** At 188 the RH plays `<gis bis dis'>` on beats 2 and 3, so all B strings are set to B♯ (c170's spelling of G♯ major's third). At 189 the "B♮" mark sits under the RH's beat-1 rest. But the LH plays `b,,4` (B1) on that same downbeat, and B1 is a B string.
  - The left foot therefore has to bring B♯ back to B♮ in the instant between 188 beat 3 and 189 beat 1.
  - In that same instant the harpist has to damp the B♯3 just struck. Moving a pedal under a ringing string bends its pitch audibly.
  - At 2. = 76 there is no beat between the two attacks. c170 wrote that the change happens "in the crotchet rest at the start of 189", but that holds only for the RH. The LH's B1 was not considered.
- **Evidence.**
  - Adler PDF 111: one pedal moves every B string in every octave, so B♯3 (RH) and B♮1 (LH) cannot follow each other without a pedal change between them.
  - PDF 113: the harpist changes "just a split second before playing the note". The composer must make sure "the tempo will permit time for the changes".
  - Every other change in the part has at least one free beat (190 A♯: two bars; 213 A♯: two beats; 234 C♮: three bars).
- **Fix (tB.ily, `tBHarpL`, bar 189):** `R2.` instead of `b,,4 r4 r4`.
  - The double basses' pizz. B1 on 189 still carries the bass, at pp, and the harp's B1 returns at 190.
  - Beat 1 of 189 is now free in both hands. The RH damps B♯3, the left foot sets B♮, and the RH plays the B-major chord on beat 2.
  - c170's spelling and its pedal marks stay as they are.
  - I chose this over respelling the chord C♮ (Adler allows "practical harp spellings", PDF 113), because a respelling would reverse c170's harmonic reasoning, and this fix costs only one doubled bass note.
  - A c206 comment above `tBHarpL` gives the reason.

### 2. Bars 1, 73, 141, 333: the pedal settings leave naturals unmarked. Applied.
- **Problem.** The settings print "D C B♭ / E F G A" (1, 73), "D♯ C♯ B / E F♯ G♯ A" (141) and "D C♯ B / E F♯ G♯ A" (333). Every change mark in the score prints its ♮: "B♮" at 189, "A♮" at 209 and 216, "C♮" at 234. So within the same part a bare letter could mean natural or could mean "as before".
  - At 141 three pedals stay natural (B, E, A) while four change.
  - At 333 the D pedal moves from D♯ to D♮ and the C pedal from C♮ to C♯.
  - In both places the bare letters are exactly the strings the harpist must check.
- **Evidence.** Adler PDF 112 gives the letter form as "D♭ C♯ B♭ / E♮ F♭ G♯ A♭": every pedal has its sign, including E♮. He calls it "a very clear one".
- **Fix (text only; no notes change):**
  - intro.ily: `"set pedals in the pause:" "D♮ C♮ B♭ / E♮ F♮ G♮ A♮"`
  - sB.ily 73: `"Ped.: D♮ C♮ B♭ / E♮ F♮ G♮ A♮"`
  - link.ily 141: `"Ped.: D♯ C♯ B♮ / E♮ F♯ G♯ A♮"`
  - coda.ily 333: `"Ped.: D♮ C♯ B♮ / E♮ F♯ G♯ A♮"`

  The texts still begin with "Ped." or "set pedals", so `partCleanHarp` still drops them from the part, as MOLA PDF 5 and c135 require. I checked pages 1, 3, 6 and 12: no collisions and no reflow.

## Checked and kept (decided)
- **Close-position off-beat triads (153–168, 185–192, 225–236, 341–348).** Adler (PDF 114) prefers wide three-note chords. Spreading them would push the top note into the solo's register, which c004, c028 and c100 rule out. Kept, as c026 and c116 decided.
- **Low-string resonance in the moving bass (81–84 D2–B♭1–G1–A1; trio LH 209–224).** The wound strings ring (PDF 112–113). In each place the LH plays one note per bar and has the rest of the bar free, so the harpist damps the previous string while placing the next, as normal practice. None of these re-creates a cross relation with the orchestra, which was the test c116 used for its étouffez marks at 87, 193 and 240. No marks added.
- **213 "A♯" and 216 "A♮" in the LH.** At 213 the change falls on beat 2, just after A1 is struck on beat 1. The LH is free to damp A1 before the foot moves, and A♯1 is not needed until 214. A♮ is not needed again until 220. Adler (PDF 113): "the experienced harpist usually will make the pedal change even later than it is marked". Kept.
- **The starting setting in the part.** c116 put it in the part (Adler PDF 112). c135 removed it under MOLA PDF 5, and movement II's harp part also leaves it out. In the part, the key signatures at 73, 149 and 341 give the settings. Kept as is, for consistency between the movements.
- **Harmonics at 375.** The range, the hand split and "o" written an octave below the sound all follow PDF 116. Kept.
