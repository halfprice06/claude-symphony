## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 40: the violas sit above Vln II and on the tune's own note under the dip. Applied.

**Problem.** At 40 the theme falls A4–F♯4–D4 (Vln I, with Fl 1·2 an octave up) onto V of G. Vln II drops to A3 for this bar (c007 moved it below the dip), but the violas stay on D4. That leaves the violas above Vln II, and on beat 3 they play the tune's last note D4 in the same colour. Every other dip of the theme has the order Vln I, Vln II, violas from the top down: 16 (Vln II B3, Vla F♯3), 32 (Vln II C4, Vla F♯3), 276 (Vln II A3, Vla D♯3). Bar 40 is the only one where the off-beat chord is inverted.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 135: keep the background out of the melody's register, "especially when foreground and background are performed by instruments of similar color", and put the background in the palest register. PDF 151 (Ex. V-26): in a string chord "all instruments are given a traditional place in the distribution". c007 applied PDF 135 to the second violins at this bar but left the violas.

**Fix (sA.ily, sAVla, bar 40).**
```
old: | r4 d'4-. d'4-. | r4 fis'4-.\f fis'4-. |
new: | r4 fis4-. fis4-. | r4 fis'4-.\f fis'4-. |
```
The D-major chord keeps its notes: bass D, violas F♯3, Vln II A3, the tune A4–F♯4–D4, winds A4/F♯4/D4. The violas move D4 → F♯3 → F♯4 at 39–41, the same octave shift they make at 32–33. No outer voice changes. A comment line `% c207` is added above the violas' variable.

## Considered, no change (my decisions)

- **Tutti 113–118 and 309–318: viola off-beats in Vln II's octave.** `rub.py` finds seconds between the violas' upper note and Vln II's lower octave of theme A: A4 against G4 at 113, 115, 313 and 315; E4 against D♯4 at 114; A4 against G♯4 at 310; G4 against F♯4 at 118 and 318, which is the motto's chromatic tail. Putting the violas' off-beats below Vln II (E3–C4 at 113 and 115, E3–B3 at 114, 117 and 118) makes consecutive fifths between Bsn 2 and the violas at 112–113 (E2/B3 → A2/E3). Every fix I found costs more than the passing seconds it removes. Those seconds last a staccato crotchet each, and Vln II's line is doubled by the cor anglais and Cl 2. c107 and c177 kept this voicing for other reasons, and both still stand. Kept.
