## Findings

### 1. The scherzo's coherence record is out of date (coherence/scherzo.md)
**Problem.** The other threads read this file to learn the scherzo's state. It says "Current version: symphony/scherzo/v95" and "Updated 2026-10-13 13:00 UTC", but v100 is current. Its list "What the finale relies on" is headed "finale v27, 2026-10-12", but the finale is at v111. It says the timpani play motive x "ppp ... at 363, 367 and 371", but the score has pp at 363 and 367 and ppp only at 371. On the other side, coherence/finale.md checks itself against "scherzo v86". The music has not drifted: themecheck reports 41/41 statements unchanged from v86 to v100, and both joins match (see above). Only the labels are stale.
**Evidence.** The BRIEF's coherence rule: cyclic-review cycles must report any mismatch between the movements' current-state files. Casanelles PDF 222: a team that shares material "routinely" uses version numbers to organize the work, and that shared state is what makes a score feel "seamlessly composed by a single person" (c030 cited this passage as PDF 211–212; the text is on PDF 222).
**Fix (text, coherence/scherzo.md; the composer's file, so not applied in the scratch copy).**
- Header: "Current version: symphony/scherzo/v100 (376 bars, 3/4, about 4:36 MIDI, 13 A3 pages). Updated 2026-10-16."
- Heading "What the finale relies on (finale v111, 2026-10-13)". Its six items are still correct for v111: last G♯, final tuning, theme A head at IV 275–276, motto close at IV 273–275, motive x at IV 143–145, D♯4 resolved at IV 219–220.
- Ending paragraph: "Timpani motive x B–B–B–E pp at 363 and 367, ppp at 371".
- When the finale thread resumes, tell it that the scherzo's exports are unchanged from v86 to v100 (41/41), so its quotation record can be re-dated to scherzo v100 without other changes.

### 2. The movement's title names only the genre; the other three titles give the tempo (score.ly header)
**Problem.** The four title pages read "I. Lento — Allegro appassionato", "II. Adagio", "III. Scherzo" and "IV. Finale: Allegro con fuoco". Only the scherzo leaves out its tempo word. The finale's form, genre and then tempo, is the one that suits a scherzo. The subtitle line "After movement II: a pause (no attacca)" also differs in wording from the finale's "After movement III (Scherzo): a pause".
**Evidence.** MOLA PDF 5–6: the title information of score and parts must be consistent. Casanelles PDF 222: shared material should read as "seamlessly composed by a single person".
**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-100/work/score.ly).**
```
subtitle = "III. Scherzo: Allegro vivace"
... \small "After movement II (Adagio): a pause" ...
```
The title page still fits; the score stays at 13 pages.

No other findings. Every cyclic rule, quotation and join from the reading and the do-not-touch list holds in v100. I looked for a reason to change the timpani's E2 at bar 3 (present since v01) and decided to keep it. It is the timpani's first stroke after the pause, and it answers movement II's last timpani note, A♭2, a major third lower, the same relation as the keys. The B–G–E figure at 9–10 then answers II's E♭–C–A♭ figure. These two links support each other.

## Notes for the finale's rework (no scherzo change)
The finale already quotes the scherzo three times: theme A's head (IV 275–276), the retransition's motive x on G (IV 143–145), and the motto close a major third lower (IV 273–275), which completes the falling chain E♭5 → B4 → G4. My decision is that it should not add a fourth quotation, the trio tune T. T's head G♯–A–B–E–D♯ is the horn call 3–1–7 with the sixth filled in, and the finale resolves that call itself at IV 243–246. Stating T as well would put the same contour into the finale twice and turn quotation into a medley (Sebesky PDF 12–13, economy). One point for the finale thread's own check: the scherzo → finale join moves *up* a major third (E → the A♭-based German sixth). A&S PDF 587 calls the common-tone link most natural *down* a third, which is the scherzo's own opening relation. The finale's ff attack after a pause does not rely on a smooth pivot, so this is consistent with its design. I note it, and I am not asking for a change.
