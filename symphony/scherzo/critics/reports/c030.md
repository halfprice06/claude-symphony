## Findings

### 1. The horn call resolves in major at 5–7. The finale owns that resolution. (Applied.)
**Bars 3–7, Hn 1; Vc and Cb at 6–7.**

**Problem.** Movement I's horn call, E♭3–C4–B3 (3–1–7) at 13–14, stops on B with the B unresolved. The finale resolves it in major at 243–246: Hn 2 E3–C4–B3–C4, with Tpt 1·2 above. That resolution is the payoff of the call across the whole symphony. The scherzo's v30 plays the call as G♯3–E4–D♯4 and then adds E4 at 6–7, so it resolves 7→1 in major, in Hn 1, about twenty minutes before the finale does.

**Why this matters.**
- The reading's rule of reservation (principle 1, PDF 245, 247–248) says a marked event should be held back for its reveal.
- The BRIEF's rule says "quote the motto (… the horn call), do not develop it".
- The added E is not part of movement I's call. It changes a direct reference into a transformation (principle 4, PDF 201).
- The resolution also puts D♯4→E4 into the opening solo, which is the same pitch move as the E♭4/E4 thread that the finale's violas close at 219–220.

**Fix (applied).** The call stops on its seventh over V, as movement I's call does over its V. The bass reaches E only at 7, together with the E-minor chord. The D♯4 still moves to E4 at the same pitch, but in Cl 2 and in E minor (principle 5, PDF 239).

```
introHnOne = { gis'2.~\pp | gis'2. | gis2.\< | e'2.\p\> | dis'2.~ | dis'2\! r4 | R2.*6 | }
introVc = { R2.*2 | e,4\pp^\markup \italic "pizz." r4 r4 | R2. | b,4 r4 r4 | R2. | e,4 r4 r4 | R2.*5 | }
introCb = { R2.*2 | e,,4\pp^\markup \italic "pizz." r4 r4 | R2. | b,,4 r4 r4 | R2. | e,,4 r4 r4 | R2.*5 | }
```

Hn 2's B3 (4–7) is unchanged. It carries the chord into the clarinets' E minor at 7. The hairpins on the shared horn staff still start and end together (4 → downbeat of 6).

**Do-not-touch.** HISTORY lists bars 1–7 as protected, for the reason "the pivot from movement II and into the finale". The change keeps that reason intact: the G♯4 pivot (1–2), movement I's call with its rising sixth (3–5), the turn to E minor at 7 and the timpani from 9 are all unchanged. Only the added E4 is removed. Cycle 10's rising sixth stays.
