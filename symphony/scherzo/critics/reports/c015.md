# Critic 015: v15, lens 5 (engraving and notation)

Assigned reading: Gould, *Behind Bars*, ch. 1 "Ground Rules", Beams and Ledger lines (PDF 31–41, printed pp. 17–27). I read every text page from 31 to 41 and viewed every page as an image at 110 dpi. I also viewed PDF 35 again at 250 dpi to check where the beam sits in the ledger-line examples. I looked at all 13 pages of v15 at 90 and 150 dpi, and at 300 dpi for pages 2, 4, 5, 7 and 12.

To check the beam rules on every beamed group, not only the ones I could see, I added a logging engraver to a scratch build (/tmp/critic-015/audit). It writes each beam's staff, bar, end positions, stem directions and note positions, 363 groups in all. A script then tested each group against the rules below.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Flute beams in the ledger-line heterophony, 169–184 and 257–272 (also 31, 35; applied)
**Problem.** LilyPond extends every beamed stem to the middle line. Fl 1's quaver groups sit two to four ledger lines above the stave (e.g. 262 and 265: A6–B6–A6–G6). Their stems are 6½–7 spaces long, and the beams lie flat on the middle line. The flute line takes the whole stave height, and the slope that should show the contour is gone.
**Reading.** Gould PDF 33: stems close to 3½ spaces, with one clear stave line between the beam and the first ledger line. PDF 35: groups beyond the first ledger line take a ¼ (second) or ½ (wider) slope. The image there places the beam just inside the stave. PDF 36–37: concave groups and groups starting and ending on the same note stay horizontal.
**Evidence.** The beam log shows positions 0.00/0.00 with stem lengths 5.5–7.0 at 175, 178, 258, 262, 263, 265–267, 270, 271.
**Fix (score.ly).** Add a `Beam.positions` callback, `gould-ledger-beam-positions`, to the Voice context. Its definition and comment are just above `\include "global.ily"`. It changes only a group whose notes all lie at or beyond the first ledger line on the beam's far side (staff position ≥ 7 with stems down, or ≤ −7 with stems up). For such a group it:
- places the beam inside the stave at the highest of 1.24/0.76/0.24 spaces (sitting on or hanging from a line) that leaves every stem at least 3 spaces;
- gives it a slope of 0, 0.25 or 0.52 by the PDF 35–37 rules.

Every other group keeps `beam::place-broken-parts-individually`. Fl 1 at 258–271 now has stems of about 3–4½ spaces and beams that follow the line (checked at 300 dpi, page 9).

### 2. Clarinets 1·2 cross in the tutti theme, 105–120 and 309–324 (applied)
**Problem.** `\partCombineApart` (cycle 5) gives Cl 1 up-stems and Cl 2 down-stems. But Cl 1 plays theme A at E4–A4 and dips to B3/G3, while Cl 2's off-beat crotchets sit at G4/A4 above it. The upper-stem part lies below the lower-stem part all bar long. Cl 1's quaver beams (b8 b at 105, 107, 113, 117) run up through Cl 2's down-stems and staccato dots, and the noteheads at 114 and 118 collide.
**Reading.** Gould PDF 41 (overlapping parts) and PDF 38–39 (stem direction follows position on the stave). The upper part takes up-stems.
**Fix.** In sC.ily and dC.ily, swap the first 16 bars of `ClOne` and `ClTwo`. Cl 1 now takes the off-beats `r4 g'4-. g'4-.`, and Cl 2 takes theme A, `e'4-. b8-. b-. e'4-.` and so on. Bar 17 onward is unchanged. The sound is identical. Crossings remain only at 113–116 where the theme's peak rises over A4, and there Cl 1 rests on beat 1 and the beams no longer cut through stems.

### 3. Oboe 1 drops under Oboe 2, 108 and 312 (applied)
**Problem.** Ob 1 `fis'4-. dis'-. b-.` (down to B3, the bottom of the oboe) goes under Ob 2's `r4 a'4 a'4` with stems up. Cycle 9 already moved this dip up an octave in Fl 1 and Vln I.
**Reading.** Gould PDF 41, as in finding 2.
**Fix.** In sC.ily and dC.ily, `OboeOne` bar 4 becomes `fis''4-. dis''-. b'-.`, the same register as Fl 1.

### 4. Unisons and crossing in Fl 1·2 and Ob 1·2, coda 341–346 (applied)
**Problem.**
- Fl 2 holds `b''2.` under Fl 1's `b''8 b''` at 341 and 343, and `dis'''2.` under Fl 1's `dis'''2` at 346.
- Ob 2 holds `g''2.` above Ob 1's `e'' b'8 b'` at 341–343, so the parts cross. It also sounds in unison with Ob 1's `g''2` at 342 and `fis''` at 345–346.

LilyPond sets the unisons as two heads side by side (half against dotted half, quaver against dotted half). At 300 dpi the ff climax in both staves is a cluster of shifted noteheads.
**Reading.** Gould PDF 41: overlapping parts and shared ledger lines. PDF 38: stem direction by position.
**Fix (coda.ily).**
- `codaFluteTwo`: `g''2.\ff | g''2. | g''2. | b''2. | b''2. | b''2. |`
- `codaOboeTwo`: `g'2.\ff | g'2. | g'2. | b'2. | b'2. | b'2. |`

These are chord tones of E minor and B major below the first parts. The outer voices are unchanged, and analysis.txt still lists the same 7 parallels.

### 5. A rest and a missing "2." in shared staves, 200–204 (applied)
**Problem.**
- At 200, Fl 2's diminuendo `bis'2.\>` made the part combiner print Fl 1's whole rest on top of the stave, touching Fl 2's stem. In 197–199 the same staff correctly shows "2." with no rest.
- At 201, Cl 1's `R2.\!` (a hairpin end on a rest) made the combiner print Cl 1's rest and Cl 2's p as two voices. "2." appeared only at 202.

**Reading.** Gould PDF 41: two parts on one stave must read as two parts. Here one part is silent, so the solo label alone should show it.
**Fix (tB.ily).**
- `tBFluteOne`: `\partCombineSoloII` at bar 13 (197) and `\partCombineAutomatic` at bar 17.
- `tBClOne`: `\partCombineSoloII` before `R2.\!` at bar 17 (201) and `\partCombineAutomatic` at bar 21.

Now "2." prints at 197 and 201 and no stray rest appears (checked at 300 dpi).
