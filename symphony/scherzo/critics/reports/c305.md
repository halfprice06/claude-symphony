## Findings

### 1. Dynamics run into down-stems and staccato dots (applied)
Bars: Bn 1·2 69 and 297 (f), Bn 1·2 197 (f), Cl 1·2 209 and 273 (f), Vc 133 and 321 (f), Tbn 1·2 333 and 339 (f); Cb 135 and 323 (staccato dot on "mf").
Problem: the probe found the dynamic's box crossing the stem of a down-stemmed chord or lower part by 1.0–1.46 spaces, and the Cb staccato dot overlapping "mf" by 0.21 space. At 500 dpi the stem end of the bassoon chord at 69 and 197 and of the trombone chord at 333 runs into the top hook of the f, and the dot at 135 sits on the "m". LilyPond places a dynamic against the outline of its glyph, so the narrow top of the f can slide alongside a stem end; I reproduced this in a two-bar test with LilyPond's default settings as well.
Evidence: Gould PDF 55: "Where space is limited, the distance between characters should not be less than ½ stave-space and no characters should collide."
Fix (score.ly, the Staff context that carries the dynamic engravers): `\override DynamicText.vertical-skylines = #ly:grob::simple-vertical-skylines-from-extents`. Each dynamic is now placed by its box and clears the stem end and the dot. After the scratch build the probe finds no dynamic touching any stem, script, head or text in the movement. System breaks are unchanged (same 19 systems), and the page count stays at 13.

### 2. Accidentals ½ space after a barline: checked, no change
Bars 100–104, 108, 114, 123, 125, 130 and others: an accidental on the first note of a bar stands 0.50 space after the barline. Gould PDF 57 allows an accidental to close "to within ½ space of a barline" where space is limited, and these systems hold 18–21 bars of tutti. The stems in the same bars are at least 0.91 space away. No change.

### 3. Quavers wider than the system's quaver in Fl 1's turns, 255–267: checked, no change
The quavers in Fl 1's heterophony take 3.2 spaces against 2.0 for plain quavers in the same system, because nearly every one carries an accidental. Gould PDF 57 (b) says that where notes are very close together extra space for accidentals is better than spacing every note at the same distance. This is that case. No change.

### 4. Crotchet at 338 widened by the oboe offset: checked, no change
Bar 338's first crotchet takes 5.6 spaces against 2.7. The space holds the Ob 1 crotchet and Ob 2 dotted minim side by side (PDF 65–66, kept by c215) and the D♯ that follows in five staves (PDF 57: two or more accidentals need extra space). No change.
