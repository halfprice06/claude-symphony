## Other study

I set the reading's examples beside high-resolution crops of pages 2, 3, 4, 6–10, 12 and 13 of v35. I wrote two scratch checks: a per-player MIDI build to find every bar where one player of a shared staff enters after both have rested, and a parser that compares the timing of the dynamics and hairpins of the two players on each shared staff. I tested LilyPond 2.24's part combiner in a small file. It reprints no "1." when a solo player comes back after a shared rest, and `\partCombineSoloI` does not force the label either.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Single-player entries without "1." (11 places), applied
Bars: Fl 69, 169, 253, 297, 361; Ob 69, 185, 269, 297; Bn 193, 269.
Problem: the part combiner prints a label only when its state changes. Each of these entries follows a rest for both players, so it prints nothing. A conductor cannot tell "1." from "a2". For example, at 69 the Fl and Ob staves start the stretto with no label while the Cl staff below them shows "1.".
Evidence: Adler II PDF 312 lists "1." and "a2" as the terms a shared staff uses, and the Brahms 1 score (Adler II PDF 297) marks "zu 2" at each change. Gould PDF 65 asks that each part's notes are clearly its own on a two-part staff. My scratch test confirmed the combiner behaviour.
Fix: `^\markup \bold "1."` on the entry note, for example sB `a''4-.\f^\markup \bold "1." e''8-. …` (Fl 1 and Ob 1), dA `e'''4-.\pp^\markup \italic "leggiero"^\markup \bold "1."`, and coda `gis''2.\pp^\markup \bold "1."`. At 193–196 (tB) the combiner had also printed Bn 2's bar rests. There I used `\partCombineSoloI` in both parts at 193 and `\partCombineAutomatic` at 197. The same treatment at 205–208, with `\partCombineAutomatic` at the start of tC, moves the "1." from 206 to the downbeat of 205.

### 2. Two staggered hairpins on the shared bassoon staff, 71 and 299, applied
Problem: Bn 1 starts its `\<` on beat 2 (`a4-. e8-.\< e-.`) and Bn 2 starts on beat 1 (`a,2.\<`). The staff-level dynamic engraver prints both, so two hairpins stand side by side under one staff.
Evidence: Gould PDF 55 (one clear symbol per event, none colliding); the brief's rule that shared staves need equal simultaneous dynamics.
Fix: Bn 1 `a4-.\< e8-. e-. a4-.` in sB and dB, which matches Bn 2 and the contrabassoon.

### 3. Bassoon solo at 73: Bn 2's bar rest printed and "1." a bar late, applied
Problem: the `R2.\!` that ends Bn 2's hairpin makes the combiner set the bar apart. Bar 73 shows Bn 1's C4 over a whole-bar rest, and "1." appears only at 74.
Fix: sBBsnOne `\partCombineSoloI c'2.\pp` … `\partCombineAutomatic R2.` at 89; sBBsnTwo `\partCombineSoloI R2.\! | R2.*15 | \partCombineAutomatic R2.*12`. This is the same method already used for the horns at 73.

### 4. "a2 1." run together over one crotchet at 44 and 276 (oboes), applied
Problem: Ob 2's last note (`a'4`) is a unison with Ob 1's first crotchet. The combiner prints "a2" and then, one beat later, "1.". The two labels sit over the same beat and read as one string, "a2 1.".
Evidence: Gould PDF 65: "Two parts with the same note-value may share a notehead as long as they take two stems."
Fix: for that bar only, `\partCombineApart a'4-. fis'-. d'-. | \partCombineAutomatic` (Ob 1) and `\partCombineApart a'4 r4 r4 | \partCombineAutomatic` (Ob 2), in sA and dA. The crotchet now has a shared notehead with two stems and Ob 2's rests. The combiner then prints "1." correctly on Ob 1's solo at 45 and 277.

### 5. "rit." touches "change bow freely", 369–370, applied
Problem: the strings' "rit." at 370 sits on the "w" of "bow" in Vln I's text at 369.
Evidence: Gould PDF 55: at least ½ space between characters, and none may collide.
Fix: global.ily, before that tempo mark: `\once \override Score.MetronomeMark.outside-staff-padding = #1.2`. I also tried moving the text below the staff, but then the Vln I–Vln II span bars cut through it, so I rejected that.

### 6. Last fermata on the incoming tie, 376 (Vln I), applied
Problem: the fermata on Vln I's final G♯5 touches the tie arriving from 375.
Evidence: Gould PDF 55 (no collisions).
Fix: global.ily last bar `s2.-\tweak staff-padding #2 \fermata`. The tie and the fermata are now clear. The fermatas over the other staves' rests sit slightly higher, which is harmless.

### 7. Double barline missing at P (289), applied
Problem: D (57), the start of the development after the cadence in G, has a thin double barline. P (289), the same juncture in the reprise, has none.
Evidence: Gould PDF 53: thin double barlines mark divisions between sections. Using them at one of two identical junctures makes the form read differently on the page.
Fix: global.ily `\bar "||" \mark \default % P 289`. I decided against adding one at G (105) and Q (305): the retransition drives straight into theme A there, and the score is consistent in leaving them out.

### 8. Bar-length notes at the end, 370–375, not applied
Gould PDF 55 places a single note that fills a narrow bar, when no other durations occur, just left of the bar's centre. LilyPond places it at the start. In bars this narrow, the gap is about one notehead. I decided to leave it: a fix needs a custom spacing override for six bars, and readability does not suffer.
