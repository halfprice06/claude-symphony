## Findings

### 1. Cb 207: crescendo hairpin on a single plucked note (applied)

**Problem.** In the trio's B pedal, the basses pluck B1 at 205, 206 and 207. At 207 the pluck carries a hairpin that runs across the rest at 208 to the arco mf at 209 (c033's crescendo into T's forte return). A pizz. note cannot swell after it is plucked, so the hairpin asks for something the player cannot do. It also prints under a rest bar.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 37–38 treats crescendo and diminuendo as functions of the bow. PDF 53–54 treats pizz. as a separate mode with the bow out of use. c179 removed a hairpin from a single pizz. note for this reason (Vln II 271). The crescendo into 209 is still made by the bowed parts at 207–208: cellos b,2.\< and Bsn 2/Cbsn pp< (c179), violas and Bsn 1, the timpani roll (c248), and Fl 1/Cl 1.

**Fix.** Give the third pluck a louder level, so that pp, pp, p is the plucked crescendo:
```
tBCb = { … | b,,4\pp^\markup \italic "pizz." r4 r4 | b,,4 r4 r4 | b,,4\p r4 r4 | R2. | }
```
The print loses one hairpin and gains a p. The MIDI plays the 207 pluck one level louder, instead of at pp with a hairpin that has no effect on a single note.

### 2. Violas at 13: "unis." after movement II's divided ending (applied)

**Problem.** Movement II (v273) ends with the violas divided on E♭3/A♭3. The scherzo's violas first play at 13, on single off-beat crotchets, with no instruction. At bar 3 the composer already prints "tutti pizz." for the basses, because movement II ends with 4 soli (c243). The violas are in the same situation and have nothing.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 30: "When divisi is no longer in order and the part returns to single notes, the word unisoni (or more commonly the abbreviated, unis.) should appear on the part." PDF 32 gives the matching rule for "tutti" after a half section, which is the rule the score follows at 3. Both movements are played from one stand of parts after a short pause, so the players' last instruction was div.

**Fix.**
```
sAVla = { r4 b4-.\pp^\markup \italic "unis." b4-. | …
```
"unis." prints above the stave at 13, clear of the pp and the dots, and it is in the viola part too. It changes nothing in the MIDI.

### Points tested and kept (no change)

- **Vln I 218–220.** a'''4~( through 220 is 7 beats on one bow at ff, about 1.8 s. That is longer than Mendelssohn's six notes at f (PDF 39), but the bow is on a held A6 that fades, which a section can do. Splitting it would put a bow change on the tied peak. Keep.
- **Vla 352–353.** "unis." for two bars between div. at 349–351 and 354–356. Each change is marked, as PDF 30 asks. Keep.
- **Hemiola minims 101–104 / 301–304.** The minim at 103 falls on an up-bow. With one stroke per minim this cannot be avoided, and 104 then ends up-bow, so 105/305 begin down-bow (c085, c148). Keep.
- **Vln I/II 277 ⊓ and Vc/Cb 72 ⊓.** These marks match the natural direction. They are kept so that the whole section attacks together and so that 285 arrives down-bow (the reprise starts its second idea down-bow, unlike the up-bow at 45/121, because it begins sub. p without a swell). Keep.
- **Vla 271.** Two plucks under a hairpin into the arco f at 273. Two notes can make a step, unlike 207. Keep.
