### 2. themes-scherzo.md describes T's first phrase as it was before v322 (composer, text)

- **Problem.** The trioT row says the "first phrase ends 6–5–4–3, the diatonic form of the motto tail". With bar 3 now D♯5–F♯4–B4, the phrase is G♯–A–B–E | D♯–F♯–B | A–G♯. It ends 5–4–3 (B4–A4–G♯4), preceded by a leap. The row also does not say what the new leap is. D♯5→F♯4 is nine semitones, a major sixth. That is the interval of movement I's horn call E♭3→C4, not of the scherzo's own call G♯3→E4, which is a minor sixth. So T now quotes movement I's call interval upside down. cyclic-notes.md line 90 offered "the inverted sixth (falling E♭–G)" to movement II, whose theme A does not use it.
- **Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 227: Zimmer's Superman theme is tied to its model only by "the opening 5th interval and a certain reliance on wide intervals". The interval is the theme's identity, so the record should name it.
- **Fix (text).** In the trioT row, replace "first phrase ends 6–5–4–3, the diatonic form of the motto tail" with "first phrase: bar 3 falls a major sixth D♯5–F♯4 (7–2; movement I's call interval E♭3–C4 inverted, since v322), then B–A–G♯ (5–4–3), the diatonic form of the motto tail". No score change.

### 4. The motto-tail preview in Hn 1 at 205–209 (c324): keep

- **Question.** Under the *Man of Steel* principle (PDF 227), does a preview in the same player and at the same pitch weaken the reward at 357–360?
- **Answer.** No. In *Man of Steel* the drum ensemble is absent from the first climax and present in both the second and the third, and the third is still the reward (PDF 227). The order is absent – present – present, which is what 205–209 and 357 now do. The reward is marked by something other than the colour:
  - At 205–208 the A♯ sits over the B pedal and is swallowed by T's forte return at 209.
  - At 357–360 it sits over A♯°7 and A minor, and the G♯ is then held for sixteen bars.

  PDF 219–220 (one palette per narrative world) supports c324 directly: Hn 1 alone is the cyclic palette (1–6, 357–376). Under the old scoring the motto figure was the only cyclic material in woodwind colour.
- **Decision.** Keep. It also stays on the do-not-touch list from c110 ("motto tail 205–209 and 357–360").
