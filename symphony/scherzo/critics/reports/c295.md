## What else I studied

I read c025, c115 and c205 (the earlier passes over this range) and c245 (which placed Hn 1's rest at 366) so I would not repeat them. I wrote a LilyPond probe (/tmp/critic-295/probe/probe.ily) that logs every notehead and rest with its staff, bar, beat, voice and vertical extent in the laid-out score. I ran it twice. The first run used the score as it is. The second used a copy in which `\pc` sets the two players as plain voices instead of part-combining them. Comparing the two runs gives every pitch the part combiner fails to print. HISTORY's note at c204 says this can happen ("\partCombine can drop a note after shared multi-bar rests; critics should compare shared staves with the parts"). From the same log I counted ledger lines for every note (rows 8–10), measured every rest (rows 13–19), and checked rest–note clearances. No rest comes within 0.4 space of a note of the other part.

## Findings

### 1. Cl 2's B3 at 361–364 is not printed in the score (applied)

**Bars.** 361–364, clarinet staff.

**Problem.** Cl 1 holds E4 and Cl 2 holds B3, tied over four bars with pp and a diminuendo. This is the fifth of the final E-major chord, which c288 tied. The score shows only E4. The part combiner put the pair in "shared" mode and dropped Cl 2's note. The B3 notehead and its ledger line are missing from all four bars. A minimal test file reproduces it (/tmp/critic-295/t/t.ly). The parts and the MIDI still have the note, so the score and the parts disagree.

**Evidence.** The probe comparison finds exactly four missing noteheads in the whole score: Clarinets at 361, 362, 363 and 364, staff position −7 (B3). Gould PDF 41: two parts on one stave share ledger lines, and each part's notes are shown. A missing note is the most basic engraving fault there is.

**Fix (applied).** sections/coda.ily, codaClTwo:
```
| R2.*20 | \partCombineChords b2.~\pp | b2.~\> | b2.~ | b2 r4\! | \partCombineAutomatic R2.*11 |
```
The score now prints `<b e'>` on one stem, with the shared ledger line and a single pp and hairpin (both players have the same dynamics). I checked it at 250 dpi. After the rebuild, the probe comparison finds no missing notes anywhere.

### 2. Cl 2's rest at 209 sits half a space off the stave lines (applied)

**Bars.** 209, beat 3, clarinet staff (Cl 1 A4, Cl 2 rest).

**Problem.** LilyPond pushed the lower part's crotchet rest down 3½ spaces (−4.75 to −1.94). At that height its hooks fall between the lines differently from every other crotchet rest in the score.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 49: rests move "an exact number of stave-spaces up or down, so that they remain correctly positioned in relation to the stave-lines." PDF 50: the lower part's rests go below the centre. This is the same fix c205 made for Ob 2 at 105/305.

**Fix (applied, score only).** sections/tC.ily:
```
tCClTwo = { gis'2\f \tag #'score \once \override Rest.staff-position = #-6 r4 | ...
```
The rest is now exactly 3 spaces down (−4.25 to −1.44), 0.44 space clear of Cl 1's A4. The Cl 2 part keeps a centred rest.

### 3. Hn 1's rest at 366 is raised in a bar that reads as one part (applied)

**Bars.** 366, Hn 1·2 staff.

**Problem.** In 365–366 Hn 2's bar rests are hidden (c245), so the staff shows Hn 1's G♯4 alone. c245 put Hn 1's crotchet rest at staff position 2, one space up, as an upper-part rest. That rule applies only when a second part is visible.

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 49: "Rests remain centred within the stave regardless of the pitches of surrounding notes." Rests move only for beams or for double-stemmed writing (PDF 49–50), and there is no second part on the page here. c245's reason (PDF 49–50, upper-part rest) assumed a second part. Hiding Hn 2's rests removed it.

**Fix (applied, score only).** sections/coda.ily, codaHnOne: `gis'2 \tag #'score \once \override Rest.staff-position = #0 r4 |`. The rest is centred (−1.25 to 1.56). This replaces only c245's rest height. c245's labels and hidden rests stay.
