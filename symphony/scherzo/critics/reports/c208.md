## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 223–224: the horns hold at *p* through the diminuendo into the codetta (applied)
**Problem.** At the end of the trio's return (221–224) Hn 1·2 hold B4/G♯4 and then G♯4/E4 at *p* with no diminuendo. Over 223–224 everything else fades into the codetta's *pp* at 225: Fl 1, Cl 1, Vln I and II have `\>` at 224; Bsn, Cbsn, Vla, Vc and Cb from 223. In the last bar the horns are the only sustained part that keeps its level, so they stand out just as the music dies away. The MIDI shows it: Hn 1·2 stay at velocity 44 through 223–224.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 130: a prolonged diminuendo is made "by the elimination of the instruments in the reverse order (brass, wood-wind, strings)". The brass should go first. The score's other diminuendi already work this way: at 129–140 the brass stop at 132, the woodwind at 136 and the cellos and basses carry on to 139; at 197–201 the horns fade and stop with the flute and oboe while the strings go on. At 225 the horns do drop out and the woodwind solo and strings continue, so the order is right, but the horns leave one bar too late.
**Fix (applied).** tC.ily: Hn 1 `... b'2.--\p | b'2.-- | gis'2.--) | R2.*13 | gis'2.~\pp ...` (was `gis'2.-- | gis'2.--) | R2.*12`); Hn 2 `... gis'2.--\p | fis'2.-- | e'2.--) | R2. | R2. | ...` (was `e'2.-- | e'2.--) | R2.`). The horns end with the tune's arrival on E at 223, and the woodwind and strings make the last bar's diminuendo alone. Bar 224 keeps a complete E-major chord without them (Vla G♯3/E4, Bsn 1 G♯3, Fl, Cl, violins E, Bsn 2/Vc B, Cbsn/Cb E). I tested a horn hairpin over 223–224 first, in four placements (below, above, one bar, invisible stencil). Every one reflowed the score to 15 pages with a compressed page 3, the same problem c198 met with a horn hairpin at 216. Removing the brass is the method the reading itself gives for a long diminuendo, and it keeps 13 pages. Shared staff: both horns stop together, so the dynamics stay equal.

### 2. 277: Hn 3 comes back *p* after 36 bars' rest (applied)
**Problem.** At the reprise of the second idea (277) everyone is marked *sub. p*. Hn 3 enters on a held B3 for 277–280 marked plain *p*, its first note since 240. On the page the horns show two different marks for the same moment: Hn 2 *sub. p* and Hn 3 *p*. In the exposition (121) the same note is held by Hn 4, which was already playing, so *sub. p* was correct there. When c156 moved the line to Hn 3, it became a fresh entry, and the mark was carried over unchanged.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 136: "After a long rest the re-entry of the horns, trombones and tuba should coincide with some characteristic intensity of tone, either *pp* or *ff*; piano and forte re-entries are less successful." *ff* is ruled out here (the passage is soft, and Dan's rule bars loud brass attacks), so the entry should be *pp*. The note is the third of the chord's middle, under Hn 2's G4 and the violins' tune, so a degree under the *sub. p* of the others also fits PDF 133: a basis sits below the melody's dynamic.
**Fix (applied).** dA.ily: `dAHnThree = { R2.*24 | b2.--(\pp | b2.-- | b2.-- | b2.--) | ...`. Hn 4 rests there, so the shared staff has one dynamic. The *mf* at 285 is unchanged.

### 3. 227–228: Cl 2's held E4 stops dead under the oboe's fading phrase (applied)
**Problem.** In the codetta, Cl 2 holds E4 *pp* under Ob 1's "dolce" phrase (225–228). Ob 1 swells and dies away to its rest on beat 3 of 228. Cl 2 holds at full *pp* to the end of 228 and stops, one beat before Cl 1's solo begins on the same staff at 229. So the clarinet's last sound in the oboe's phrase is a cut-off, not a fade.
**Evidence.** Rimsky PDF 130: "Clarinets specialise in diminuendo effects and are capable of decreasing their tone to a breath (morendo)." The score uses this at 7–8, 86–87, 135–136, 147–148 and 361–364 (c108 added the first and fourth). 227–228 is the one soft held clarinet note left without it. The hand-over to Cl 1's solo also needs a beat's clearance on a shared staff.
**Fix (applied).** tC.ily: `tCClTwo = { gis'2\f r4 | R2.*15 | e'2.\pp | e'2. | e'2.\> | e'2 r4\! | R2.*4 | R2.*8 | }` (was `e'2. | e'2.`). Cl 2 now fades with Ob 1's diminuendo and stops with it on beat 3 of 228. The E stays in the harp's chord and in Hn 4's E3 from 229.
