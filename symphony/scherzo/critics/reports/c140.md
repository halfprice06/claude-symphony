### 2. cmajor.py does not see C–E–G inside a seventh chord. A superset scan finds six bars, and none of them is a C-major triad (checked; no score change)

**Problem.** c042 recorded that cmajor.py only flags sonorities whose pitch classes are exactly C, E, G, and asked critics to check seventh chords by eye. I scanned score.midi every semiquaver for any sonority that contains C, E and G (script: /tmp/critic-140/scan/ceg.py; /tmp/critic-140/scan/who.py lists the instruments). It finds bars 34, 113, 115, 261, 313 and 315, and only these.
- At 113, 115, 261 and 315 the sonority is A–C–E–G. Theme A's ninth bar (A–G–F♯) passes through G on beat 2 over iv, which is A minor.
- At 34 the same passing G is a quaver in the flutes and oboes over the winds' A minor.
- At 313 the sonority is F♯–A–C–E with the same passing G, over c057's iiø7 bass F♯.

In every case the root is A or F♯ and the G is an unaccented passing tone that falls to F♯. The ear hears iv7 or iiø7, not C major. The cyclic notes allow A minor (iv) explicitly.

**Decision.** Keep all six bars. Adler (adler1 PDF 22) asks the composer to answer for every chord. Answered: none of them is the finale's C-major sound. Only one voicing comes near it: 113 and 313 put C4–E4–G4 close together in the middle, with Tpt 2 on E4. Even there the A2 bass, the A4 in Tpt 1 and the violas, and the melody's step G→F♯ on beat 3 keep the chord as iv. Rewriting theme A's ninth bar would change an exported theme for nothing audible.

**For the kit.** The composer may copy /tmp/critic-140/scan/ceg.py into kit/ beside cmajor.py, so later critics run the superset check instead of reading by eye. Its expected output on v140 is the six bars above.

### Points tested and rejected

- **The G♯5 is absent at 365–368, between Fl 1 (361–364) and Vln I (369).** Ex. XV-37 overlaps each colour into the next. Here the gap is the point. For four bars Hn 1's G♯4 is (with Hn 3) alone again, as at bars 1–2, and Vln I then colours it from above at ppp, which is XV-37's own move. The closing page is protected for the link, and the change would weaken the mirror of the opening. Keep.
- **Trumpets and timpani are not coupled in the tuttis** (row 4). Their alternation of motive x at 105–120 and 305–320 is a dialogue in the manner of Beethoven 9/ii. The classical coupling is kept for the coda's peak, 333–336. Keep.
- **The second idea contains a rising sixth (B5–G6 at 45–46)** against the rule that new themes do not open with that interval (cyclic-notes, critic 101). The idea opens D6–B5, so the sixth is its second interval. c060 recorded it as the horn call's 3–1–7 in G, a derivation and not a new opening. Keep.
- **The G→G♯ pivot in Hn 1 has a crotchet rest at 144 and at 240** (row 19, seams). The rest is the horn's breath, and the two joins mirror each other. The harp (149, 151) and Cl 1 (153) pass the G♯4 on at the same pitch. Keep.
