### Tested and kept (no change)
1. **Retakes forced by marked down-bows (bow simulation).** The simulation finds down-bows that follow a down-bow at 18/24/26/42, 110/116/118, 258/264/266/274/277, 310/316/318 (Vln I, theme A accents), 72 and 300 (Vln I/II, Vla, Vc), and 333–339 (all strings). In theme A and at 72 the preceding note is a staccato or spiccato crotchet, so the bow is already off the string and the retake costs nothing (Adler PDF 43–46). At 333–339 the composer asks for a series of down-bows for weight (c096, protected), which Adler shows in Tchaikovsky 6/iii, "played quite fast, with the bow raised between each down-bow" (PDF 42, Ex. II-29). The minims before 337 and 339 lose a few hundredths of a second to the lift; Adler's alternative for this case, ⊓ V inside the long note (Coriolan, PDF 38, Ex. II-20, used here at 300), would mean splitting the hemiola minims 336–338 into tied notes and hiding the hemiola in the notation. The current bowing is the better choice; it stays.
2. **Accented downbeats on an unmarked up-bow.** 49/125 are the second idea's crescendo bars, an up-bow by the alternation 45 V – 47 ⊓ – 49 V, matching the crescendo-on-up-bow rule (PDF 38). 103/303 sit inside the alternating hemiola minims 101–104, also a crescendo. No mark needed (PDF 38: mark only to counteract habit).
3. **Vln II and violas, pizz. → arco at 273 (4 beats, 0.91 s).** The shortest return to the bow in the score. Adler asks for "some short time" and warns about the return to arco (PDF 54); 0.91 s with the bow held in the hand is enough, and c175 set it.
4. **Arco → pizz. with one beat at Vc 225 and Cb 205/225.** Each comes from a marked up-bow, which brings the hand to the frog as Adler asks (PDF 54). Protected (c096, c186).
5. **Viola triple stop B3–G4–E5 at 339** without an open string (PDF 29 prefers one). A struck fff crotchet with fingers 2–3–4 in first position; nothing has to sustain.
6. **Viola double-stop spiccato at ff, 105–120/305–320.** Adjacent strings, first position, a rest on every downbeat (PDF 29–30, 46). Protected (c019, c177).
7. **Vln I's last G♯ 369–376** with "change bow freely": the staggered bowing of PDF 40, inside the protected closing page.
