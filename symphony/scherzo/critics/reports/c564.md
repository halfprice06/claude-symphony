## Findings

### 1. Bar 120: the exposition tutti ends on a chord with no fifth, and the trumpets rest on it (applied)

- **Bars:** 120 (sC.ily, `sCTptOne`, `sCTptTwo`).
- **Problem:** Bar 120 is the arrival of the movement's first full tutti: theme A's last tonic crotchet, before the sub. p second idea at 121. Its chord is E1, E2, E3, G3 (×4), E4 (×4), G4, E5 and E6. That is E and G only, with no B at any octave. The trumpets play motive x on the odd bars (105, 107, ... 119) and rest on 120, because 120 is an even bar. So the trumpets are present on the dominant at 119 and absent on the tonic it resolves to, and the arrival has less brass and less resonance than the bar before it. In the exposition the trumpets' B4 is the only fifth on the tonic downbeats. Whenever they rest (112, 120), the fifth is gone.
- **Evidence:** All twenty appendix chords sound root, third and fifth, and in every loud one the brass sounds the complete chord by itself (PDF 505-507, chords 1-5, 9-12, 17-20). In Ex. 204 (PDF 367), the model for this kind of fast ff tutti, every group lands together on the final crotchet, trumpets included. In Ex. 209 (PDF 375) the trumpets and trombones play the attack and only then stop. Ch. IV (PDF 116) says a passage is scored for its place in the form, as a climax or as a step. 120 is the close of the exposition's tutti and 112 is only a mid-phrase cadence, so the two should not be scored the same.
- **Fix (applied):** Tpt 1 B4 and Tpt 2 G4, crotchets with tenuto, then rests:
  `sCTptOne = { ... | b'4-. b'8-. b'-. b'4-. | b'4-- r4 r4 | R2.*20 | }`
  `sCTptTwo = { ... | fis'4-. fis'8-. fis'-. fis'4-. | g'4-- r4 r4 | R2.*20 | }`
  Tpt 1 holds B4 from 119. Tpt 2 rises F♯4–G4. G4 rather than E4 avoids octaves with the tune's F♯5–E5 at 119-120. The trumpets play in tutti rhythm (everyone has a crotchet on 120.1), at the f already marked at 105, with no accent (Dan's soft-brass rule), and the tenuto matches the low brass downbeats. The 120 chord is now complete in the whole orchestra and in the brass: E1-E2-E3-G3-E4-G4-G4-B4.
- **Kept as it was:** 112 (mid-phrase) and the reprise's 320, where the trumpets are tacet by c033.
- **Best-candidate test:** this was the change this reading supports that a listener would hear most clearly. The trumpets' edge now arrives on the cadence instead of falling away one bar before it. I kept it because it breaks no rule or protection. The trumpet rule allows tutti rhythm. c086's layout (horns on beats 1 and 3, trumpets on motive x, single low-brass downbeats) is unchanged elsewhere. c424's upper-brass third stays in Hn 1's G4, which Tpt 2 now doubles. No cue in parts-cues.json involves bar 120, since the trumpets are already playing at 119.

### 2. Bar 56: the G-major close before the fugato has no fifth (applied)

- **Bars:** 56 (sA.ily, `sAVla`).
- **Problem:** 56 closes the second idea after the ff hemiola cadence. The chord is G1, G2 (×3), G3 (×2), G4 (×5), B4 (×2) and G5 (×2), with no D. The violas (and Cl 2) move D4→G4 from 55. The matching closes at 132 and 288 keep their fifth, B3 in Cl 2 and violas.
- **Evidence:** Appendix PDF 505-507: complete chords, as in finding 1.
- **Fix (applied):** violas hold the common tone: `... | d'2. | d'4-. r4 r4 | }` (was `g'4-.`). Cl 2 keeps its G4. The violas next enter at 61 (fugato), so they lose nothing. Same note count, no new parallels.

### Checked and kept (no change)

- **K1, reprise 305-320: tonic downbeats with E and G only.** This is the combined effect of c362 (Hn 4 G3 for the third, made while Hn 1 was on B4) and c424 (Hn 1 B4→G4). With the trumpets tacet (c033), nobody holds B on beat 1. I tested every voice that could take it. Hn 1 B4 would take away the only upper G (G4) and leave the third in G3 only, which is worse, because the fifth is the tone that may be left out and the third is not. Hn 4 B2 makes fifths with the bass at 304–305, and Hn 4 B3 crosses Hn 3 on the shared staff. Theme A brings B on beat 2 of every motive-x bar, so the chord is complete within the bar. Kept.

It would also put back the middle doubling that c057 removed for economy.
- **K3, 336-338 and 339.** These follow A1-A7 (c554's held low brass, c204's Tbn 2 B2, c024's string stops). The harp stays out (A10, c474).
- **K4, 72 and 300.** 72 is a step, with Hn 3·4 senza sord. from 73. 300 is the goal and was completed by c474. Kept.
