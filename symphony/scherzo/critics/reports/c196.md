### Tested and kept (decided; not a request)
1. **Cello F♯4 at 282 in bass clef.** Ex. III-53 (PDF 96) keeps the bass clef up to E4 and starts the tenor clef at F4. F♯4 is the only cello note above E4 in the movement. It is a single minim in a slurred tenor line that otherwise lies between B3 and E4. Switching to tenor clef and back within two bars would cost the reader more than one note on two ledger lines. Kept.
2. **Viola E5/F5 in alto clef at 62, 70–71, 198–199, 294, 298–299 and 339.** PDF 85 allows treble clef "to avoid ledger lines". None of these passages stays on the third ledger line for more than two bars, and each lies between A4 and F5, which violists read in alto clef as a matter of course. This is the third critic to keep it (c016, c106). Kept.
3. **Vln I's legato sixths B5→G6 at 46 and C♯6→A6 at 50** (PDF 82–83, "wide skips legato"). Both are a sixth on the E string, inside a two-bar slur at f. They are shifts to 7th position, which Adler treats as the normal top of the orchestral range (PDF 72). They are not skips between extreme registers, and c166 kept them for the same reason. Kept.
4. **Viola double-stop off-beats 105–120/305–320** (PDF 76, 88). The sevenths B3–A4 at 108, 111, 308 and 311 are fingered 2–4 on the G and D strings in first position, or played against the open A. The sixths are 2–3 or 3–4. They are among the easiest double-stops in Ex. III-33, on a sonorous ff passage, which is the use Adler gives for orchestral double-stops. Kept.
5. **Vc 60→61 (D2 staccato, then D4 pp slurred).** This crosses from the C string to the A string in first position (D4 is the fourth finger), after a staccato crotchet that leaves room for the bow. It is not the same-string or extreme-register case of Ex. III-22/23. Kept.
6. **Vln I's slur at 218–220 (A6 ff, seven beats, about 1.8 s).** Adler PDF 75 says the E string is "forceful, even strident" in its high positions, and a fast ff bow in high position is normal section technique at this length. Splitting the slur would put a bow change inside T's climax note, which c082 ties on purpose. Kept.
