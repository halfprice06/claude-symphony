**Form verdict.** The movement's key plan follows Unit 15's model for a minor-key movement:
- I – ♮VII – III, with III expanded into a key area.
- III to V through IV and the chromatic II6 (the F-major episode leaves on F/A).
- In the da capo, ♮VII7 is turned back to I through V6/5. This is Ex. 15-14, and it uses the double meaning of ♮VII that the book demonstrates on Ex. 15-1.

The proportions and the length are right, and the movement keeps moving. I tested three places against the reading and decided not to change them:
- **The da capo's second idea in E minor (c003).** The composer's reason holds: Dvořák keeps the da capo in the keys it first had, and the G version is heard once.
- **The 288→289 seam.** See row 11.
- **The trio codetta.** See row 10.

## Findings

### 1. Bar 330: the ♮VII of the coda's falling bass is hidden by the tonic drone (applied)

- **Problem.** In the coda's first stage the bass falls E–D–C–B (329–332) under I – ♮VII – N6/4 – V7. This is Ex. 15-15/15-16 (PDF 241–242): ♮VII leads to V7 through a passing 6/3 chord, and its 5th (A, 4̂) prepares the seventh of V7. The design is right: Bsn 1 holds A from 330 into 332, and Cl 1 moves in 10ths with the bass.
  - At 330, however, the cellos (E3–B2) and violas (B3–F♯3) keep the E–B motive-x drone over the basses' D2 and Bsn 2's D3.
  - The chord sounds as D–E–F♯–A–B, with a major 2nd D3–E3 in the tenor. The ♮VII reads as an E-minor sonority over a passing D.
  - c032 already moved the drone to chord tones at 331 and 332 "so no B sounds against the bass C". Bar 330 is the one step of the descent where this was not done.
- **Evidence.** A&S PDF 241–242 present ♮VII as a chord in its own right: "at first as its dominant [of III]; this initial impression is altered as VII moves through IV6 and on to V7". Ex. 15-15 and 15-16 voice it as a plain triad.
  - At 330 this matters for form. The four-bar descent is the coda's harmonic motion from i to V, and each bar should be heard as a new chord.
  - Motive x keeps its rhythm and contour, so the stretto ostinato and the crescendo are unchanged.
- **Fix** (coda.ily, bar 330 only):
  - `codaVc`: `e4-. b,8-. b,-. e4-.` → `a,4-. fis,8-. fis,-. a,4-.`
  - `codaVla`: `b4-. fis8-. fis-. b4-.` → `fis4-. d8-. d-. fis4-.`
  - Vln II keeps E5–B4 above as an upper 9th/6th colour.
  - Voice leading: the violas go B3→F♯3, not B3→A3, which would make 12ths with the bass E2→D2. The cellos go E3→A2→F3, which avoids 12ths with Vln I's A5→C6 at 331 and octaves with Cl 1's F♯4→F4.
- **Result.** I checked every pair of parts with a script over 329–331. The only new motion in octaves is the motive-x figure doubled within bar 330 (Vln I A5–F♯5 with the cellos A2–F♯2). The old version had the same kind of doubling between Vln II and the cellos. No new parallels occur across the bar lines.
- **Applied** in /tmp/critic-083/work/sections/coda.ily, with a c083 comment. This touches the coda build that c007 and c017 asked to keep. Only the pitches of two string parts in one bar change; the build, the ostinato entries and the dynamics stay.
