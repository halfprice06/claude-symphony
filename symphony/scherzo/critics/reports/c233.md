## What else I studied

**Beat scan.** beats.py labels 172 beats of the movement as 6/3 and 374 as 5/3, so 6/3s are 31% of the labelled beats. No two adjacent 6/3 beats have a stepwise bass, so the movement has no parallel 6/3 series at all.

I ran the same scan on Dvořák 9/iii (reference-corpus/musicxml-converted/ly/Dvorak_Sym9_mvt3.ly, compiled to MIDI). It gives 104 beats of 6/3 and 275 of 5/3 (27%), and also no parallel 6/3 series. Its transitions also run over long pedals: bars 8–12 already sit on an E pedal under D. So the main model makes the same choice as this score: 6/3s appear one at a time, with functions from Ex. 18-19, and transitions are carried by pedals and stepwise basses under root-position chords.

**Why the score cannot simply borrow a 6/3 series.** A descending diatonic 6/3 series reaches C–E–G on the chord over E in E minor and in G major. In F major (the episode) it reaches C–E–G on the chord over E, and that triad is the episode's own dominant. The movement avoids it with the rootless V♭9 E–G–B♭–D♭ at 79. So in every scherzo key the series runs into the no-C-major rule. Only the E-major trio could carry one without a C-major triad. Its transitions (the B area at 180–184, the middle 185–208, the codetta 225–240) are protected for other reasons (c093, c153, c157, c053).

## Findings

No finding needs a change to the score. The form, the proportions and the length (4:38) hold against Unit 18, and every point below is a decision to keep. Ranked by how close each came to a change:

### Kept 1. Transitions over pedals, not 6/3 series (89–104, 133–152, 225–252, 357–376)

**Unit 18's claim.** Unit 18 §1 (PDF 289–291) presents the 6/3 series as the natural transition between two stable points. Read narrowly, the movement's four pedal passages (89–104, 133–152, 225–252, 357–376) are its places of least harmonic motion.

**Why I keep them.**
- They are protected (c103, c113, c123, c133, c163).
- A series in E minor or G produces C–E–G (see above).
- Unit 18's own condition is met another way. In each passage only the start and the goal are articulated (PDF 291), and the motion inside is given to a line rather than to chords: the head rising Vla → Vln II → Vln I at 89–100, Hn 1 G → G♯ at 141–148, Hn 1 G♯ → G at 241, the motto tail at 357–360.
- Dvořák 9/iii, the main model, does the same.

**Decision:** no change.

### Kept 2. Coda 329–332 without the Phrygian 7–6 (Ex. 18-12)

**What differs.** The bass E–D–C–B is exactly the stepwise descent Ex. 18-12 describes. The chords differ:
- Over D, the score has ♮VII 5/3 instead of v6.
- Over C, it has ♭II6/4 (F–A–C, c081) instead of iv6 with a 7–6.

**Why I keep it.**
- Restoring iv6 would undo c081. That change was accepted, and c143 kept it.
- A 7–6 into iv6 needs a held B over the C-bass bar. That clashes with the ff motive-x ostinato for one beat, which is c053's reason.
- Unit 18 calls the suspension "almost invariable" for iv6 (PDF 298). It does not ask for it on the Neapolitan.

**Decision:** no change.

### Kept 3. Theme A bar 10 at 313–315 differs from 22/114/262

**What differs.** At 22, 114 and 262, bar 10 of theme A has V6 of iv (bass A–G♯–A, the neighbour 6/3 of PDF 296, applied by c143). In the reprise tutti at 313–315 the bass is F♯–G–A: ii°7 – i6 – ii, the passing II–I6–II6 of Ex. 18-10 (PDF 297).

**Why I keep it.** Both are correct uses of Unit 18. The reprise varies the statement in the same way it varies others (c058, c143).

**Decision:** no change.

### Kept 4. The B area at the end of T's second statement (180–184)

**What it is.** At 183–184 the cellos sustain D♯3, so in the score the B chord looks like a 6/3, and Unit 18 calls the 6/3 a weaker goal than the 5/3 (PDF 290–291).

**Why I keep it.** at.py shows the pizz. B1 in the basses on each downbeat, so the chord is B 5/3 with the 3rd in the cellos. The arrival is a 5/3. It is protected by c093.

**Decision:** no change.

### Kept 5. Length and place in the symphony

**Why I keep it.**
- The movement takes 278 s between a long Adagio and a long finale.
- Its proportions are scherzo 87 s, trio 70 s, reprise 49 s and coda 44 s, which shorten toward the end, so the movement keeps moving.
- Unit 18 does not bear on overall length. The form lens's earlier passes (c013 through c223) found no open form point, and I find none.

**Decision:** no change.
