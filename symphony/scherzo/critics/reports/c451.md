## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 53, 129, 285: the second idea's cadential phrase opens with its own reduction (APPLIED, theme change)

**Problem.** The second idea runs two four-bar sequential phrases. The tied hemiola cell and the rising sixth carry them. Then comes the cadence. Bar 53 is three plain crotchets down the tonic triad over the cadential 6/4: D6–B5–G5 in G (E minor 129/285: B5–G5–E5). The line reaches the tonic G5 on the last beat of 53, a bar before the cadence. It then climbs back to B5 and does 3̂–2̂–1̂ again in the hemiola 54–55. The reduction of 53–55 is therefore 5̂–3̂–1̂ | 3̂–2̂–1̂. The tonic arrives twice, and 4̂ never sounds in the top line. Bar 53 is the only bar of the theme with no figuration of any kind. It is the bar a listener hears just before the theme's goal, and it gives them nothing to keep.

**Evidence.**
- PDF 332–333, Ex. 20-8: in a 5̂–1̂ descent "the passing tone A (over IV) is normally required to secure a stepwise motion to the goal tone". Passing tones are "virtually obligatory in that melodic continuity depends on them". Here the goal is G5 at 55, and the line jumps to it at 53 instead.
- PDF 333, Ex. 20-10: an extended passing motion fills a larger interval. D6–C6–B5–A5–G5 fills the fifth across 53–55.
- PDF 333–334, Ex. 20-11: "The neighbor-note figure … is often treated motivically." An upper neighbour on beat 2 gives bar 53 crotchet, two quavers, crotchet. That is motive x's rhythm, legato and at forte. The second idea now carries the scherzo's main rhythmic cell into its cadence.
- PDF 328, Ex. 20-1: a bare reduction "removes much of the music's individual flavor". In v451, bar 53 is exactly such a reduction.
- PDF 340, Ex. 20-24 bar 2: over V the raised 6̂ is used even in a falling line. In E minor the neighbour is C♯6 over the cadential 6/4. C♮ would be the closer half-step neighbour (PDF 341), but over the 6/4's E and G it sounds C–E–G, which the cyclic rule forbids. C♯ in the E-minor second idea already has a precedent (c091, at 128/284).

The protections hold. Bar 53 was never part of the hemiola: c441 says its crotchets "re-establish the bar so that a hemiola can be heard". A crotchet–two quavers–crotchet bar states 3/4 even more clearly. The cadence 54–55 is untouched: three minims, the tied 9–8 (c441), and the c448 hairpins. The rising sixths, the phrase lengths, the bass and the harmony are unchanged.
