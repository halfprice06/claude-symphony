### Checked against the reading, no change

- **Key plan 13–104.** E – III (G, 37–56) – iii, v, iv by mixture and sequence (57–72) – ♭II over iv (73–88) – V (89–104). This is the K. 457 plan (Ex. 26-8, PDF 449): III as the midpoint, IV as the passing step, the leading tone brought in before the return (D♯ at 95, V7 at 101). Keep (c083's protection).
- **The reprise's G minor at 289** follows an E-minor cadence and has no G major before it, unlike 57. It is not unprepared: the D7 at 273–275 is V7 of G, diverted at 276. G minor at 289 then fulfils it in the minor mode, the way Ex. 26-7's V6/5–VI foreshadows its larger C-minor area (PDF 448). The fugato then repeats Ex. 26-10's procedure (D-minor answer; V of A at 296 before the A entry). Keep.
- **The trio after c093** is Ex. 26-17's plan: V reached (183), extended by VI as upper neighbour (C♯ minor 185 and 197) and by a briefer IV (201–202), then V7 (203–208) into I (209). The middle's I–vi–IV–V is also the K. 488 descent in 3rds (Ex. 26-14). Keep.
- **The coda's A major (341–348)** is a late IV (PDF 451–452: no imbalance this late), entered without a transition after the G.P., as §11 says IV usually enters in ABA forms. It moves to V of E (B7 at 347). T's second half confirms E with ii–ii6–6/4–V7–I (Points 2). From 349 to 354 no D♯ sounds, so for six bars the harmony fits both E and A; but iii–V6 in A is a weak reading, and the cadence at 353–356 settles it. Keep.
- **Link and scherzo close (133–152).** These are the quietest 20 bars of the movement (8–26 attacks per four bars in analysis.txt) and stay on E except for V at 138. Dvořák's link (600–702) moves through iii, III and VI6 because his trio is in VI, a new key. This trio is the parallel major, a change of mode that §16 (PDF 456) allows without a dominant. The horn's G→G♯ (141–145, protected) is the event that carries the passage. A chord change under the held G would need either a 7th that rises (A minor 7 → E) or the A♯°7 that 358 saves for the motto's close. Keep.
- **Length and proportion.** 276 s in the MIDI, about 4:40 with the fermatas and G.P., is right between the 5:45 Adagio and the 7:00 finale. The reprise ratio (0.56) matches Dvořák's (0.60). The slow coda (31 s) belongs to the protected G♯ pivot and cannot be shortened without renumbering 357–376. No cut.
