## Findings

None of the 26 principles is violated in a way a change would improve. Every °7 is approached by neighbour, common tone or passing tone; every 7th falls, is held, or is resolved by another instrument in the same register; every ♭6 in E major reaches 5 in its own part; every chromatic step that touches the bass stays in one line. I tested the points below and keep them.

### Considered, no change

1. **102→103 and 302→303: Bsn 1 A3→B3 against Cl 2 D♯4→F♯4, a tritone moving in similar motion to a P5** (A&S PDF 375, Ex. 22-3c). Ex. 22-3c is about the resolution of vii°7 to I, where the hidden 5ths land on the new chord. Here the harmony is one V7 held for four bars of hemiola (101–104); both parts arpeggiate the same chord, inside a tutti where the outer voices (B pedal, Vln I B5–A5–F♯5–D♯6) carry the line. Nothing resolves, so there is no faulty resolution to hear. Keep (hemiola brass 101–104 protected by c097).

2. **275→276: D in the bass (Cb/Vc D2–D3 on beat 1) and in Vln I (D5 on beat 3), then D♯3 as the new bass in violas and Bsn on 276** (A&S PDF 394, Ex. 23-12a). The chromatic step D→D♯ passes between instruments in the bass register. It is heard as one line, though, because Vln I itself goes D5 → F♯4 → D♯4 inside the same beat-group, which is the "one voice" case of Ex. 23-12b; and the turn ♮VII7 → V6/5 → i at 273–277 is protected (c083). Keep.

3. **104→105 and 304→305: the V7's 7th A leaps to E in Vln I, Vln II and cor anglais** (A&S PDF 375 §3). Cl 1 (A5→G5), the trombones (A3→G3) and the bassoons (A3→G3) resolve it by step in two registers, a transferred resolution (Ex. 22-5, PDF 376). Settled by c052 and c093. Keep.

4. **212→213: the violas' D4, the passing seventh of V7/IV, sits out beat 1 of 213 and falls to C♯4 on beat 2.** The off-beat accompaniment pattern delays the step; the resolution is in the same part and the same register, which satisfies PDF 375. Keep (c089).

5. **No vii°7 or viiø7 of E in the E-major sections** (A&S PDF 381–382, 393, Ex. 22-14, 23-9). The reading presents these as available colours, not requirements. The trio's plainness is deliberate (c089 keeps the first statement plain), and the place where one would go, the codetta over the E pedal 225–236, already carries the mixture through iv at 235 (Ex. 23-8's varied repetition); a D♯–F♯–A–C chord there would replace the protected codetta IV, IV, iv (c170) and anticipate the °7 colour that the close at 358 keeps for itself. Keep.
