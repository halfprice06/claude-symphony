## Findings

### 1. The plagal close, 357–364: the inner lines are re-bowed every bar, and the violas' last diminuendo is an up-bow (applied)
**Bars:** Vln II 357–360, violas 357–364.
**Problem:** Under Hn 1's motto tail (B–A♯–A–G♯, one slur, 357–360) the strings carry the two chromatic steps of the plagal close. Vln II (la metà) plays G♯4–G4–E4–E4, the mixture step. The violas play E4–C♯4–C4–B3, the 6–♭6–5 line that movements II and IV share (c460). The cor anglais doubles that line under one slur at 358–360. Neither string part has a slur, so each takes a new bow on every bar. The semitones G♯–G and C♯–C–B therefore fall exactly on a bow change, while the horn and cor anglais play them legato.

The violas then hold B3 for four bars, 361–364, with a diminuendo from 362 into Hn 1's G♯. Counting from the div. bars at 353–356, the violas reach 361 on an up-bow. The movement's last string fade below the violins is drawn toward the frog, where the stroke grows heavier. The cellos and basses carry a printed V at 358 (c175) precisely so that their same four-bar diminuendo falls on ⊓, and c175's note says the violas do the same. Since c050 and c460 rewrote the violas' line, they no longer do.

This is the movement's most closely heard soft passage: the motto quotation, the cyclic inner line, and the hand-over to the last G♯. It is the change from this reading that a listener would hear most. The bow-change gaps at the chromatic steps are small. The viola B swelling against the horn's fade is the clearer of the two effects.

**Evidence:** Adler PDF 37: "Whenever a passage is slurred, all notes under that slur are performed on one bow … This is called legato playing." Ex. II-19 (Schubert 5/ii) binds each falling step in one bow. PDF 38: "there is a tendency to perform a diminuendo with a down-bow … indicate bow direction only where he wishes to counteract the normal habit". PDF 38–39: when the passage is soft, many notes fit on one bow. Here four dotted minims at 2. = 76 take 3.16 s at pp, shorter than the violas' accepted 3.41 s bow at 242–246. PDF 38 also forbids phrase slurs that are not bowings. This slur is a bowing.
**Fix (applied):** coda.ily. Only slurs are added. Notes, dynamics, scoring and layout are unchanged.
- `codaVlnTwo`: `gis'2.(\pp | g'2. | e'2. | e'2.) |` (was unslurred)
- `codaVla`: `e'2.(\pp^\markup \italic "unis." | cis'2. | c'2. | b2.) | b2.~ |` (was unslurred)

The new count:
- Vln II: ⊓ for 357–360, its natural taper to the rest at 361.
- Violas: V for 357–360, then ⊓ at 361 for the held B and its diminuendo, with the cellos and basses (⊓ at 361 after their V at 358).

Both string lines now move with Hn 1's slur and the cor anglais' slur. A `% c518` comment above `codaVlnOne` records the reason.

**Do-not-touch answered:** c108, c168 and c418 list the closing page 357–376 as do-not-touch, for its notes, levels and the frame G♯. This change keeps all of those. It only restores the bow plan that c175 recorded for this page. c460's line and c490's la metà are unchanged.
