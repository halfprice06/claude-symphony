## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Every timpani roll plays as one stroke in score.midi; the crescendo rolls into 105, 209 and 305 are not heard (applied, MIDI only; a fix for all four movements)

- **Bars:**
  - 101–103 (p < into motive x at 104 and the tutti at 105);
  - 205–208 (pp < into T's forte return at 209);
  - 301–303 (into 305);
  - 129–130, 237–240, 285–286, 332, 336.
- **Problem.** LilyPond's Note_performer ignores stem tremolo, so `b,2.:32` sounds as one note held for the whole value. A chain tied over bar lines (`b,2.:32\p\<~ | b,2.:32~ | b,2.:32`) becomes one note of 9–12 beats. In the v360 MIDI the timpani have exactly eight notes of 3 beats or longer: 101 (9 beats), 129 (6), 205 (12), 237 (10), 285 (6), 301 (9), 332 (3) and 336 (3). The GM timpani sample decays in about a second. So the three built crescendos, whose rolls the score asks to rise to the primary level (Sebesky PDF 14), are silent after their first beat, and the hairpin over them does nothing, because velocity is one value per note.
- **Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 12: a figure "may look great on paper, but when played in the studio will sound wrong". PDF 240–241: compare the score with the recorded result, and ask where the arrangement sags; "music does not exist on paper". Adler II PDF 80: a roll sustains the note to its marked end.
- **Same fault elsewhere.** Movement II has carried this as open since its c244: its rolls are single strokes, and its critique says the fix "would need a MIDI-only score block". Movement I (rolls tied over 9 bars at 116–124, 181–182) and the finale (18 timpani notes of 2 beats or more in v322's score.midi) have the same fault.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-360/work).**
  - New file rolls-midi.ily with a music function `\rollStrokes #n music`. It turns every note or chord that carries a stem tremolo into repeated n-th notes of the same total length. The first stroke keeps the dynamics, hairpins and texts. The roll's tie is dropped, so a tied chain sounds as continuous strokes across the bar line, and a roll tied into a plain note (240–241) ends with a stroke on it.
  - I tested a first version that kept the tie on the last stroke. It silenced the stroke on each downbeat, so I removed that.
  - score.ly: the `<< … >>` becomes the variable `scherzoMusic`, followed by `\score { \scherzoMusic \layout { } }` and `\score { \rollStrokes #16 \scherzoMusic \midi { …unchanged… } }`. Semiquaver strokes are 17.6 per second at dotted minim = 88 and 15.2 per second in the trio.
  - Diffs: /tmp/critic-360/c360-score.diff; the file itself is /tmp/critic-360/work/rolls-midi.ily.
- **Result.**
  - Print unchanged: all 13 pages are pixel-identical to v360 at 50 dpi.
  - score.midi changes only in the timpani track: 8 notes removed, 229 strokes added.
  - The crescendos now sound: velocity 62→83 across 101–103 and 301–303, and 54→82 across 207–208 into 209.
  - A 4/4 test (/tmp/critic-360/rolltest/t.ly: a 4-bar tied roll with a hairpin, a `g,4:32\>~ g,4\!` end, and a chord roll `<c g,>2:32`) also expands correctly with `#32`.
- **Message (movements I and II, finale).** "Scherzo c360: timpani rolls play as single held strokes in every movement's score.midi (stem tremolo is not performed; tied chains merge). symphony/scherzo/v361/rolls-midi.ily (if promoted) gives `\rollStrokes #n music` for a MIDI-only `\score`: put the score's music in a variable, `\score { \music \layout { } }` and `\score { \rollStrokes #16 \music \midi { … } }`. The print is untouched (scherzo: 13 pages pixel-identical; MIDI differs only in the timpani). Choose n so that the strokes run at about 12–18 per second at your tempo. It handles notes and chords and drops the roll's ties. Movement II: this closes your open c244 point."
- **Shared or not:** yes. One copy should live in symphony/coherence/ for all four movements. I do not write there; the composer should copy it there when promoting.

## Weighed and kept (do-not-touch read as reasons)

- **Theme A bar 2 always over E inside the scherzo (14, 106, 254, 306).** Sebesky PDF 19 asks for a melody not to be harmonized the same way twice. Changing this would mean reharmonizing the protected tutti layouts (c304) and moving the inner parts under a pitch-fixed link (I 79, IV 273–275). The variety Ex. 3/3A asks for is already there at symphony level: the finale sets the same four notes over a C pedal, I – vii°7 – iv6/4 – I. Inside III each statement varies from its fifth bar on (18/110, 22/114, 113/313). Kept.
- **357–360, Bsn 1 doubling the violas' C♯4–C4–B3 under the motto tail.** Ex. 1 (PDF 12–13) could argue for thinning it. But the line carries the A♯°7's third and the iv's third, and it is the "close with moving bassoons" (c014). Removing it would leave the chromatic inner line to the violas alone, at pp under a p horn. Kept, as c260 decided.
- **Harp at 361 and again in harmonics at 375.** PDF 11 says to save a colour for its moment. The 375 harmonics are the finale's link (E4→E♭4, B4→C5). The 361 chord is ordinary pp harp under winds and strings, a different colour, and it marks the arrival on E after the motto tail. Both kept (c050, c150, c250, c350).
- **No closing timpani roll (c091), so the ending does not copy movement I's.** Movement I v368 still ends with a C roll ppp (181–182) and a harp chord, so the reason holds.
