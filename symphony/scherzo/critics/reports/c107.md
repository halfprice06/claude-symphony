## Findings (ranked)

### 1. 197–200: Vln II's lower octave of the call tangles with the violas' trio tune. Applied.
**Problem.** At the forte combination, the call (E6 in Fl 1·2 and Vln I, E5 in Ob 1 and Vln II) sounds against the head of T in Cl 1 and the violas (G♯4–E5, f espress.), with Hn 1 mf. Vln II and the violas are the same colour, and their lines share G♯4–F♯5. At 198 beat 3 the violas' E5 lies above Vln II's G♯4. At 199 the violas' D♯5–C♯5 runs above Vln II's A4–B4 in the same dotted rhythm (`a'4. b'8 cis''4` against `dis''4. cis''8 b'4`). At 200 both play `a'4 gis'2`. So the T head, the middleground that c037 added, is covered by a louder string line in its own register and joins it at the end.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 144, rules 3 and 4: separate theme and countertheme in register, and make the countertheme rhythmically different "so they don't interfere with one another". PDF 135: keep the background away from the melody's register when the colour is the same. PDF 142: the layers must reach the listener in their intended order of priority.
**Fix (tB.ily, tBVlnTwo bars 13–16).** Vln II plays the T head in unison with the violas. The call keeps Fl 1·2 and Vln I at E6 and Ob 1 at E5. The strings now lie in three layers: Vln I (call), Vln II with Vla (T head), Vc/Cb (bass). Vln II reaches 197 by step (F♯4 → G♯4), so no shift is needed.

### 3. 37–43 and 273–275: Fl 2 and Ob 2 hold notes inside the theme they accompany. Applied.
**Problem.** In the counterstatement in G, Fl 1 (with Vln I) plays theme A while Fl 2 holds D5 (37–40) and then F♯5 (41–43), and Ob 2 holds D5 under Ob 1 (41–43). Each held note is a pitch the theme strikes: the motive-x quavers D5 at 37, 39 and 41, F♯5 at 43, and D5 at the end of 40 and 43. Fl 1 also crosses below Fl 2 at 39 (B4 under D5). Same colour, same pitch: the theme's quavers fall onto a sustained unison and lose their outline. The reprise has the same notes at 273–275. The chord does not need these notes. Cl, Hn, Bsn, Vln II and Vla already complete G (37–40) and D7 (41–43) below the theme.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 135: background figures in the palest register, away from the melody's register, above all when foreground and background are the same colour. PDF 152, V-28 item 3, and PDF 137: strengthen a melody by doubling it at the unison, and build a crescendo by adding instruments to it.
**Fix.** Fl 2 joins Fl 1 a2 at 37–44, and Ob 2 joins Ob 1 a2 at 41–43. In sA.ily, Fl 1 gets `\p` at 37 so both lines are identical; the combiner prints "a2".
