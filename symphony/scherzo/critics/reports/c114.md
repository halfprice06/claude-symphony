## Findings

### 1. Reprise tutti 305–320: nothing between the bass and the tenor on the downbeats (applied)

**Bars 305–320.** The reprise is a partial tutti: the trombones and tuba are held back for the coda (c033, kept). In 105–120 those four instruments fill the third octave on every downbeat (Tbn 2 E3, Tbn 1 G3). In the reprise nothing takes their place. The downbeat chords now read E1 E2 – E4 at 307 and 317 (24 semitones empty), F♯1 F♯2 – C4 at 313 (18), G1 G2 – E4 at 318 (21), and E2 – G3 at 305, 309, 312 and 320. Below the tenor countermelody, the only sustained voice is Bsn 2 at E2. This happens in ff, under four horns, two trumpets, the woodwind and the strings.

**Evidence.** In all 20 chords of the appendix (PDF 505–507), the octave above the bass is filled: by cellos, by bassoons, or by trombones and horns. Ch. IV PDF 125: "the upper and lower parts of a passage can seldom be widely separated without the intermediate octaves being filled in, for this is contrary to the first principles of proper distribution of chords." The Sadko tutti (Exs. 205–206, PDF 368–369) is in the same 3/4 one-in-a-bar metre. There the octave above the basses is held by trombones, horns and bassoons in dotted minims.

**Fix (applied, dC.ily).** Hn 4 leaves the tenor countermelody, which Hn 3, Bsn 1 and all the cellos still carry. Hn 4 holds the bass an octave above Bsn 2, moving its B and A down to octave 2 so that it never crosses the tenor:
```

```
Hn 4 is on the shared Hn 3·4 staff, so no staff is added. The low brass rule (c033) and the brass crotchets of the first tutti (c086) are untouched. c097 put Hn 3 and Hn 4 in unison on the countermelody because one horn under the first tutti's low brass was covered. With no low brass in the reprise, Hn 3 plus Bsn 1 and the cello section are enough. The first tutti (105–120) is unchanged, so the reprise is now scored differently in its inner detail (PDF 118 d).

### 2. Trio return 213–220: the bass falls to F♯1/A1 with two octaves empty above it (applied)

**Bars 213–220.** In T's forte return the bass is in the double basses, the contrabassoon (unison, c101) and the harp's left hand, all in octave 1. The cellos and Bsn 2 carry the countermelody in octave 3. At 219, the climax bar (f), the chord reads F♯1 – A3: 27 semitones with nothing in them. It is 24 at 218 (G♯1 – G♯3), 21 at 220 and 19 at 213–215.

**Evidence.** Reading notes 1 and 20 (appendix PDF 505–507; PDF 125). c094 left "bass in one octave only at 209–224" open. c101 answered it with the contrabassoon in unison, citing Rimsky PDF 56 ("no phrase of importance in the double basses without the support of cellos or bassoons"). In the appendix that support is always at the octave, never in unison only. c087 took Bsn 2 off an octave doubling because it crossed the cellos at 209, 213 and 216. I checked the octave-up bass line against the cellos bar by bar: from 213 to 222 it lies wholly under them.

**Fix (applied, tC.ily).** The contrabassoon doubles the basses an octave higher at 213–222 (sounding A2, F♯2, F♯2, B2–F♯2, C♯3, G♯2, F♯2, A2, B2, B2). It stays in unison at 209–212, where the octave would cross the cellos, and returns to unison on E at 223. Phrase slurs and dynamics are unchanged:

The largest gap at 219 is now 15 semitones (F♯2 – A3). I first tried the bass clarinet, which is idle here, but its staff added two pages to the score, so I used the contrabassoon, which is already on those systems.

### 3. Bars 104 and 304: Fl 2 sits under the oboe, in unison with the trumpet (applied)

**Bars 104, 304** (the V7 that ends the hemiola build, ff). Fl 2 drops from F♯5 to D♯5, under Ob 1, which rises to F♯5. D♯5 is also Tpt 1's note, and at ff the flute in its fifth-line octave disappears into the trumpet.

**Evidence.** Reading note 3: in no appendix chord is a flute placed under the oboes or inside the trumpets' unison. The flutes always form the top of the wind chord (PDF 505–507). Ch. IV PDF 115: oboe with trumpet is the closest wind match to trumpet tone, so the D♯ belongs to the oboe.

**Fix (applied, sB.ily and dB.ily).** Each instrument keeps its held note: Fl 2 stays on `fis''2.\ff` and Ob 1 stays on `dis''2.\ff`. Both still step to E5 at 105/305. The chord's pitches are unchanged, and the staves no longer cross.
