# Cycle 43, critic B: balance and levels (on the c42 render)

**Method.** Every stem and the master of `cycles/c42/render/` (the final stems after the c42 stem fix; the stems sum to the master to -187 dB). Levels per 0.5 qn on the score grid (`build/parts.json` tempo map), 8192-point frames, mid signal, weighted K (38 Hz high-pass plus a +4 dB shelf above 1.5 kHz), A, and octave bands at 125/250/500/1000 Hz (88-177, 177-354, 354-707, 707-1414 Hz). Delivered loudness: ffmpeg EBU short-term (S) on `mvt2-mockup-c42.mp3`. Delivery simulations: the c42 stems re-summed with level changes and run through the unchanged `tools/deliver_master.py` into my scratch folder (it reproduces c42 exactly: span 19.0, LRA 15.5, limiter 2.6). Simulations ignore the hall (level changes in the volume envelope come after it anyway), so trust them to about 1 dB. Printed marks from `build/mockup-events.tsv` and score pages 1-8. "Measured" means a number from the render or the data; "inferred" means a judgement about what a listener hears.

## Manual notes (B4: May 2021 HO manual pp. 49-52, 202-204, 208; `loop/MANUAL-GAPS.md` read)

| Manual (PDF page) | Where it applies here | Does the render follow it? |
|---|---|---|
| p.49 "Loading With Effects Off": load without the per-patch convolution reverb and use a DAW reverb on an FX bus | every track | **Followed in another way.** Each patch's reverb insert is replaced by one shared true-stereo hall (`mix.json` `reverb`, fx_volume 0.095, low cut 160 Hz, ir_setup 0.7), depth set by per-mic sends. Same result as an FX bus, kept inside Opus. |
| p.50-51 Multi-outputs: each mic position on its own output, mics sent in different amounts to one reverb bus | mic balance and depth (check 3) | **Not used, not needed.** The mic mix is set inside each instance (`mics`, `send`). What matters for check 3 is the balance itself: every family is mixed with Main as the stronger mic (finding 3). |
| p.52 Multi-timbral instances (up to 16 parts per instance) | memory | **Not used.** One instance per track (32), 44-45 GB; a multi-timbral layout would not change levels. |
| p.202-203 Level Amp, Limiter, Limiting Amp (fixed-ratio levelling, ceiling, release 1-400 ms) | sample-level peaks inside a track (finding 1) | **Not used, correctly.** A per-track limiter would flatten the f/ff layers; the one sample swell that sets the delivered maximum is better fixed by a level shape on the notes (finding 1). The delivery limiter is ffmpeg's `alimiter` on the master (2.6 dB in c42). |
| p.204 SSL Bus Compressor (2:1-20:1, 0.1-30 ms attack, auto release) | master span (check 1) | **Not used, correctly.** Loudness range is handled by the slow look-ahead ride in `deliver_master.py`; a bus compressor reacts to single attacks and would reverse swells (kit LEARNINGS, Delivery). |
| p.208 SSL Transient Shaper (Invert subtracts the attack for more body) | accents, harp and pizz. attacks | **Not used.** GENTLE_ACCENT already takes the bite out of accents in the envelope. Nothing in this lens needs it. |
