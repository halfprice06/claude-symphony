## Findings, ranked by what a listener would hear

### 1. The climax order is reversed: bars 79-80 are louder than bars 61-62, and a single flute is the loudest instrument there (measured)

- Where: bars 79-80 (qn 262-268), all tracks; Flute 1 qn 262.6-265.9.
- Evidence: delivered mp3, maximum 3 s short-term loudness: bars 60-64 -11.1 LUFS, bars 78-82 -10.2 (c27). c26: -9.4 against -11.1; c22: -9.4 against -10.9. So the climax was 1.5-1.7 LU over bar 79 in both series 1 renders, and is now 0.9 LU under it (BRIEF item 6 requires bars 61-64 to be clearly the loudest). The master's five highest pre-limiter peaks are all at qn 262.1-265.1 (metrics.md). Over qn 262-268 the loudest stem is Flute 1 at -23.7 dB, above Violins I (-24.8), the celli (-25.3) and Violins II (-26.4); Flute 1 is +7.5 dB on c26 in bar 79. The cause is in `build/roles.json`: Flute 1 carries `[262.6, 263.5, 3]` and `[263.5, 265.9, 7]`, series 1 lifts written when v77 printed f there. v600 prints ff on Flute 1, Flute 2 and Violins I at 262 (mockup-events.tsv), so the CC11 already plays the ff layer and the +7 dB comes on top. The strings in bars 79-80 are 4-6 dB over c26 for the same reason (ff now printed). A flute's B flat 6 above full violins, celli and a2 horns at a tutti ff is not how a hall balances. It reads as a solo instrument close-miked over the orchestra.
- Simulation (stems, plain power, 3 s windows): -6 dB on Flute 1 and Flute 2 over qn 262-268 takes the bar 79-80 maximum from -15.3 to -16.0 dB. Adding -2 dB on Violins I, Violins II, Violas, Violas div, Violoncellos and Double basses over the same span takes it to -17.1, which is 1.3 dB under the climax (-15.7). That restores about the c26 margin.
- Fix: in `build/roles.json` delete Flute 1's `[262.6, 263.5, 3]` and `[263.5, 265.9, 7]` (or set both to 0). Add `[262, 268, -2]` to Violins I, Violins II, Violas, Violas div, Violoncellos and Double basses. Leave the existing Violoncellos rows alone. The c27 port note said Violoncellos `[262,268,2]` and `[262,265,2,0.25]` were dropped, so check that nothing else lifts them. This is level only, so it can be done as a stem fix (`tools/stem_fix.py`) if the cycle renders for other reasons. Then re-list the top master peaks and check the delivered order (bars 61-64 maximum at least 1.5 LU over 78-82). Also check Clarinet 1 in bars 61-63: +6.7/+7.0/+5.5 dB on c26 and the fifth-loudest stem at the climax (-28.1 dB, over Violins II). It may be a dropped series 1 trim; measure it before cutting.

### 2. Every timpani roll plays the loudest (fff) layer turned down, so ppp and f rolls have the same colour (measured; the library fact is read from the manual and movement I)

- Where: Timpani, all rolls: ppp bars 2-3 (qn 3), the pp crescendo rolls at 168, 200 and 261, the 13.5 s ppp roll under Horn 1 in bars 67-70 (qn 226), and the f rolls of bars 79-81 (262, 265, 268).
- Evidence: the Timpani track has no CC1 lane in parts.json (`TIMP_CC1_MAP = []` in `tools/score_to_parts.py`; movement I's values were not ported, per README). p.124: the felt rolls' "CC 1 (Mod Wheel) controls dynamics (8 levels)". Movement I c12 measured that with no CC1 every roll plays V8 (kit LEARNINGS section 20, c11-c15). Here the 1-8 kHz share of each roll body (+0.3 to +3 s) is ppp -11.5 dB (qn 3), pp -11.5 (168), ppp -12.5 (226), against f -13.6 / -13.7 / -12.8 (204, 262, 265, 268). The soft rolls are as bright as the f rolls, or brighter. The coda strokes, which use velocity layers, read -35 to -42. A ppp felt roll should sound like a dark rumble. These sound like a loud roll turned down, which is the clearest "machine" cue in the quiet passages (bars 2-3 and 67-70 are exposed). Also, the f roll of bars 79-81 stays flat at -33 dB from qn 262 to 270 through the printed decrescendo at 268, while the master falls from -15 to -29 dB. The roll does not settle under the texture (BRIEF item 5). In c26 it fell from -31 to -43.
- Fix: copy movement I's c12 values into `build/score_config.py`: `TIMP_CC1_MAP = [(22, 56), (34, 56), (46, 60), (58, 66), (72, 72), (88, 88), (114, 104), (124, 122)]` and `TIMP_ROLL_TRIM_DB = [(22, 17.6), (34, 19.0), (46, 18.6), (58, 12.7), (72, 9.5), (88, 7.3), (114, 3.7), (124, 0.0)]` (`symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/build/score_config.py` lines 519-532; V1 at CC1 5 was nearly silent there, so ppp-pp go to V4). This changes the timpani state only (no new row), and the CC1 then follows the printed curve, so the decrescendo at 268 also darkens the roll. Check on the render: the roll bodies' 1-8 kHz share in dynamic order (movement I: V4 about -31, V6 -22, V8 -13 dB); each roll's level against c27 per printed dynamic within ±1.5 dB (refit `TIMP_ROLL_TRIM_DB` by stem fix if not); the ppp roll at 226 at least 10 dB under Horn 1; the 262-270 roll falling at least 4 dB after 268.

### 3. Brass and clarinets play their middle dynamic layers at every printed dynamic (measured for the timbre; the fix is inferred from movement I)

- Where: Horns 1-4, Trombones, Bass trombone, Tuba, Clarinets 1-2. Most exposed: Horn 1's dolce solo bars 7-14 and 65-70, Horn 3's pp pedal bars 3-6, the brass of bars 61-64, Clarinet 1's coda solo bars 85-89.
- Evidence: `CC1_DYN_TRACKS = ()` here. CC1 on these tracks runs 53-87 (parts.json: Horn 1 53-87, Horn 3 53-81, Clarinet 1 53-87, Trombone 1 53-86), and the KS Masters crossfade dynamic layers on CC1 (p.101; MANUAL-GAPS #1). The tone colour barely changes with level. Horn 3 from pp (bars 3-6) to ff (bars 61-62) rises 21.4 dB in level but only 9.2 dB in 2-4 kHz minus 250-1000 Hz. Horn 1's p solo (bars 7-14) and its bars 55-58 crescendo differ by 3 dB in level and 6.9 dB in that tilt. A real horn brightens much faster than it gets louder. Movement I measured horn 2-4 kHz +12 dB from p to f once CC1 carried the dynamic, against 0-6 dB before (kit LEARNINGS 20, c06-c10). The climaxes of bars 61-64 and 79-81 are therefore loud but not brassy. The pp horn and the ppp coda clarinet carry the mf colour turned down, the same fault as finding 2.
- Fix: `build/score_config.py`: `CC1_DYN_TRACKS = ("Horn", "Trombone", "Bass trombone", "Tuba", "Clarinet ")` (the trailing space keeps the bass clarinet out) and `CC1_HOLD = {"Contrabassoon": 40}`, with the generic `CC1_DYN_MAP`. Movement I's levels moved -5..-10 dB at soft dynamics and +2..+6 at fff, and per-dynamic trims took two cycles to settle. Plan the per-dynamic level check (stems by printed dynamic against c27, along the printed curve) and refit `pitch_trims`, `early_fills`, the Trombone 1 accent swells and the Horns 1-2 a2 per-dynamic level in the next cycle. If the actor wants one controller change per cycle, do finding 2 first: it is smaller and it is Dan's named priority.

### 6. The violas' sotto voce portato eighths swell late and blur in bars 48-51 (measured; the patch remedy is inferred)

- Where: Violas qn 152.5-167.5 (15 off-beat eighths, pp sotto voce, portato) under the cor anglais quotation ("solo, espr.", p-mp).
- Evidence: v600 writes these as separate off-beat eighths with rests. v77 had held notes, so the violas are now 5-10 dB under c26 in bars 48-51. Each note (0.48 qn, about 0.53 s at q=54) reaches its peak at +0.34 s after the score onset (+0.45..+0.52 s on 158.5, 162.5, 163.5). That is at the end of the note, rising 13-17 dB from the gap before it. The peaks sit at -47.7 to -51.7 dB, 9-13 dB under the cor anglais. So each eighth is a swell whose weight comes a sixteenth late (S2), not a gently pressed portato on the beat. The Sus MAX pp attack is too slow for a 0.5 s note (kit LEARNINGS 6: soft string entries reach level in 0.3-1.0 s).
- Fix (test): `build/score_config.py` `ART_SPANS = {"Violas": [{"from": 152.5, "to": 168.0, "art": "Detache"}]}`. Violas KS Master row "Detache", key 30, not loaded now, so this is a full rebuild of the Violas track. p.91: Détaché samples "come to a well defined end" and "work well whether you play the samples to the end or you end the note before that". Use velocity about 50-60 and the Sus MAX stroke lead. If the row does not speak sooner, fall back to Sus MAX with a 0.10-0.12 s extra lead (`extra_leads`) and a 3 dB front fill held 0.2 s (`early_fills`). Pass criterion, set before the render: peak by +0.15 s on at least 12 of 15 notes, a drop of at least 6 dB in the rest between notes, and peaks 7-9 dB under the cor anglais.
