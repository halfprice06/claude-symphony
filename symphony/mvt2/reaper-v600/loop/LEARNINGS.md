# Movement II mockup, series 2 (v600): learnings

Lessons from this series that would help someone mock up a different score with this library and pipeline, especially where movement II differs from `mockup-kit/LEARNINGS.md` and Movement I's `symphony/mvt1/reaper-v621/loop/LEARNINGS.md`. One short dated bullet each under a topic heading; actors append. Skip anything specific to this score's bars.

## Score extraction
- 2026-11-06 (c27 port): a score revised for its own MIDI can carry hidden dynamics (`-\tweak stencil ##f \mp`) and MIDI-only volume settings (`midiMinimumVolume`, `midiMaximumVolume`, `midiExpression`). The hidden marks also end hairpins in the score: dropping them outright let the next hairpin run on to the next printed mark (a 1 qn decrescendo became 8 qn). Replace each by a hairpin stop instead (`build/mockup.ly` `mockVol`).

## Pipeline: porting a mockup to a revised score
- 2026-11-06 (c27): ties the revised score adds merge repeated notes into one held note, which silently disables per-onset re-tongue entries (`REP_RETONGUE_AT`) at the merged onsets and changes the sound: a pp bass clarinet pedal held 11 qn on Sus Non Vib rose 6-7 dB inside the sample against the re-tongued 3 qn notes. Check per-onset tables also against notes that disappear into ties, and compare per-bar stem levels with the previous render after a port.
- 2026-11-06 (c27 port): check per-onset tables against the new score by kind of entry: level shapes depend on the note's pitch, length, printed dynamic and articulation; legato fills and leads also on the adjacent note before; entry leads only on the note itself. Many apparent dynamic changes came from hidden MIDI-only marks; re-run the check after fixing the extraction.

## Strings
- 2026-11-06 (c28): Hollywood Strings Violas Detache plays about 17 dB louder than the Leg Slur MAX first-note layer at the same pp velocity and CC11 (0.48 qn eighths: peaks -31 against -49 dB). Plan a role cut of about -13 dB with an articulation change like this, and measure it on the render.
- 2026-11-06 (c28): an articulation test with `ART_SPANS` silently drops the after-rest entry lead when the row's name contains none of the entry-lead substrings ("Sus", "Leg Slur", "Flautando", "Tremolo"): a Detache test moved 15 notes 0.108 qn later than the Leg Slur notes they replaced. List the tested row in `ENTRY_LEAD_ROWS` so only the row changes; diff note onsets in parts.json after any row test.
- 2026-11-06 (c29): Hollywood Strings Violas Pizz RRx4 has a velocity-layer edge between 46 and 47 (5-8 dB step at one pitch); pp triplets at v41-46 nearly vanished. Floor pizz velocities at 48 and shape pp/p with CC11 and the volume envelope instead (-4 dB put the pp beats 3-5 dB under the p beats).

## Dynamics
- 2026-11-06 (c28): `fit_rebows.py` holds whatever level the stem had before each re-bow, so a sample that swells inside a piece (a celli ppp A flat 2 +7 dB about 2.3 s after its re-bow, outside the 1.5 s fill window) is carried into the next piece's fill. Fix the swell with a level shape before refitting; `--hold-max-after` caps only the hold, not the fill.
- 2026-11-06 (c29): loop-seam correction profiles (fit to one long note's 3.95 s loop) turn into modulation once that note is re-bowed: each new bow restarts the sample, so the profile's phase is wrong. Five such profiles gave 8-11 dB median 1 s level ranges on held pedals; deleting them and refitting the re-bow fills on a stem_fix simulation without them brought those to 3.7-6.5 dB.
- 2026-11-06 (c29): `fit_rebows.py` chains each piece to the level before the note's first re-bow; when that first re-bow falls at the end of a printed decrescendo, the whole chain is lifted to the louder level (+5..+9 dB on a ppp pedal). Skip that re-bow (`--skip`) so the next piece targets the softer level, and level the pieces with constant per-key offsets checked on a simulation.

## Timing
- 2026-11-07 (c31): lead plus a short fill (0 pre, 3-8 dB, hold 0.1-0.2 s) on 14 late soft string and wind entries moved body - 6 dB from +0.10..+0.35 s to -0.00..+0.08 s; one 8 dB fill overshot the body by 4.2 dB (Violins II pp sul tasto) and was cut to 6.3 by stem fix. Simulated peaks from the previous render's envelope predicted all but that one within 1.5 dB.
- 2026-11-07 (c31): a longer entry lead can push a held string note past the 4.5 s automatic re-bow threshold and add an unfitted re-bow (`AUTO_REBOW_SKIP`); a group lead moves the automatic re-bow points of the following held note, so re-key `rebow_moves.json` to keep fitted fills valid.
- 2026-11-07 (c31): shortening a brass first note by 0.05 qn (a smaller entry lead) changed the level of the following legato targets by +2..+3.5 dB on Solo Trombone ff; re-measure the whole slur after any lead change on its first note.
- 2026-11-06 (c28): `auto_fills.py` run over string sections and a bassoon added 108 fills; simulating them on the previous render's stems (fills are level only) found 11 whose 200 ms front rose more than 1 dB to over +2.5 dB over the louder body (one +11.5 dB on a join with a transition spike). Lowering each by (over - 2) dB before rendering kept the median join dip at 3.4 dB (5.1 before) and joins of 6 dB or more at 8 of 140 (40 before).

## Mix, mics and reverb
- 2026-11-07 (c31): a post-hall vol_db fall can be moved into CC7 without changing the planned level: hold vol_db at the level before the fall, write the fall as CC7 steps (0.06 qn), and return CC7 to 127 over 1.5 qn while vol_db takes the same fall slowly. On four string tracks this kept the hall ring of an ff sf (master +2.6 dB at the end of the fall) and removed a one-sample 4.5 dB step that the attack probe had flagged for five cycles; the CC7 step quantization left a median 0.36 dB error.
- 2026-11-06 (from Movement I c36): CC7 and CC11 act inside Opus before its hall, REAPER's track volume envelope acts after it. A sudden drop in the envelope cuts the reverb tail; put subito drops in CC11/CC7.
- 2026-11-06 (c29): CC7 on a Hollywood Strings KS Master is linear amplitude as on the timpani: CC7 28 (127*10^(-13/20)) reproduced a -13 dB envelope role within 0.3 dB, without the post-hall cut of the previous note's tail.
- 2026-11-06 (c29): raising the woodwind hall send 0.35 -> 0.6 (pre-fader sends) raised the solo stems by 0.3-0.8 dB (bass clarinet chalumeau 1.8 dB, clarinet 1.4 dB); plan a small trim on the low winds with such a change.

## REAPER, the bridge and the shared lock
- 2026-11-06 (c28): check the system's default audio devices (`system_profiler SPAudioDataType`) before taking the lock: with Bluetooth AirPods as default input and output REAPER (devices "<default>") opened 24000 Hz / 480 with the headset microphone, and the render had to be abandoned after a 17-minute build and settle. The actor may not change system audio settings; ask the user to switch the headset off first.
- 2026-11-06 (c27): Opus can segfault in a worker thread while a build job loads a KS Master state (Clarinet 1, crash report all Opus frames); the bridge then waits its whole 600 s job timeout before failing. The same data built cleanly after a fresh start and open, all 32 tracks in 59 s. When a job times out, check `pgrep -x REAPER` and `~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports/REAPER-*.ips` before suspecting the data.
