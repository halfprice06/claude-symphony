# Learnings for future mockups (staging; curated into the repo kit)

## Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: CC11 is far from linear in dB. Within sustained notes it moves the level about 5.7 dB per 10 CC at CC 0-40, 3.5 at 30-60, 2.5 at 50-80 and 1.2 at 88-128 (115 notes; winds average 1.8). A crescendo that starts at f barely grows on CC11 alone, so add a dB ramp in the volume envelope across crescendos to f or louder (c06: +4 winds, +3 strings, +2 brass). Release it slowly (1 qn) after the arrival so it does not sound like fp.
- 2026-10-11: Sample level varies by pitch in the solo legato patches at a flat CC11, by 6-14 dB. Measured residuals: Solo Horn F4 +5, G flat 4 -5, A flat 4 -5; Clarinet G5 +10, A flat 5 -5; English Horn A4 +9. Fit each note's body level against its neighbours, average the residual per pitch, and trim per pitch in the volume envelope. A per-note trim fixes one note; a pitch trim fixes every occurrence.
- 2026-10-11: The level per CC11 step differs by patch: Clarinet 0.17, Clarinet 2 0.20, Bass Clarinet 0.26, Bassoon 0.30-0.34 dB/CC. A single soft trim for all winds leaves the bassoons 5-9 dB under the clarinets at pp. Use per-track soft trims with ppp/pp/p anchors.
- 2026-10-11: At pp-p the Basses KS Master sits 5-12 dB under the Celli KS Master at the same CC11. At f the gap is 0-3 dB. Soft passages need about +3 to +5 dB on the basses.
- 2026-10-11: The legato transition time depends on the source pitch. Violas leaving C4 took over 0.22-0.37 s after the onset, against a 0.05 s median for every other transition. Measure legato takeover per source pitch before tuning one lead time per family.
- 2026-10-11: Events on the very first block of an offline render can be dropped: one opening voice at -75 dB in one render, two string sections silent in another, with identical MIDI. Put a silent pre-roll bar before the music, send each track's first keyswitch and controller values in it, and cut the pre-roll off the rendered files.

## REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-11: The pre-roll is easiest to cut exactly when it has a round length: 3 qn at 60 bpm = 3.000 s = 144000 samples at 48 kHz. Read the time back with `TimeMap2_QNToTime` in the render job and cut that many samples from the master and every stem, so analysis scripts keep the score's timeline.
- 2026-10-11: A full 27-instance Opus project holds about 55-57 GB. When other jobs push the Mac into memory pressure, macOS compresses REAPER's idle sample memory: 42 of 57 GB in c06. A 1x offline render then starts every entry 0.2-0.3 s late, on tracks whose data did not change. Before rendering, check `top -l 1 -o mem -stats pid,command,mem,cmprs` and run an attack-timing probe after rendering. A large CMPRS figure is a warning, not a verdict: c08 rendered cleanly at 36 of 49 GB compressed (see the cycle 8 note).
- 2026-10-11: An agent cannot edit `/Library/Application Support/East West/Opus/Preferences.json` (for example PreloadSize): the permission system blocked the edit in c06 even with the user's approval in chat. Ask the user to change the preference in Opus's settings, and check first that it addresses the cause (see the c07 note on HDD preload and RAM).

## Measurement methods
- 2026-10-11: Put level-only shaping (role cuts, note and pitch trims, swells, hairpin gain) in the REAPER volume envelope, and keep CC11 for choosing timbre and layer. Each can then be measured on the post-fader stems against the plan in parts.json.
- 2026-10-11: The Dvorak 9/i Columbia reference has an LRA of 21.8 LU. The mockup went from 25.4 to 23.9 LU when ppp/pp/p were raised 2 dB and f/ff were left alone. Raising the soft levels in the volume envelope narrows the range without changing the CC11 layers.

## Cycle 7 additions
### REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-11: Re-setting an Opus state (`vst_chunk`) on a live track does not free the old instrument's memory. One full 27-track rebuild took a session from about 49 GB (fresh load) to 57 GB, and a later session to 72 GB, with 69 GB of it compressed. For data-only changes, keep the Opus instance: delete and recreate only the MIDI item and envelopes (`build_reaper.py --midi-only`, which compares the generated state with the last one written, byte for byte; `opus_state.build` uses gzip mtime=0, so states are deterministic). 27 tracks update in about 30 s instead of about 15 minutes.
- 2026-10-11: Save, quit and reopen REAPER before a render after any patch reloads: the fresh load had 0 GB compressed, and its render passed all 19 attack checks (the previous session failed 10). Gate renders on `top -stats pid,command,mem,cmprs`.
- 2026-10-11: Opus's HDD preload setting (PreloadSize 0) keeps more of each sample in RAM than the SSD setting (manual, Audio Engine). On a machine near its RAM limit, lateness from memory compression is not fixed by a larger preload.
### Timing
- 2026-10-11: Convert note leads from seconds to qn on the real tempo map ending at the onset, not at the onset's own tempo: after a caesura or a slow rit. step, the onset-tempo conversion made 0.17 s leads 0.25-0.30 s long.
- 2026-10-11: An extra per-onset lead helped loud and mid entries (Vln I 171: 0.38 -> 0.20 s body arrival; Cl1 268: 0.17 -> -0.04) but not every soft string entry (Vln II 42: 0.24 -> 0.45). Measure the shape of the first 0.3 s before adding lead to pp entries; the soft first-note sample may just grow slowly.
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: Per-pitch sample unevenness holds across articulations: Solo Horn F4 is hot on Sus MAX chords as on Leg Slur (+6 to +7 dB), Basses A flat 1 +6 to +8, Bassoon D flat 3 +5. Apply pitch trims to every articulation, not just legato.
- 2026-10-11: Wind portato (tenuto under a slur) at ff re-tongued on Sus left 6-12 dB holes at each note; joining it on Leg Slur with a 10 CC notch, or crossfading Sus notes by 0.03 qn, brought the dips to 4-5 dB. A flute legato transition up to F6 still dipped 10 dB inside the transition itself.
- 2026-10-11 (corrected in c11): A CC11 dip of -7 CC between +0.15 and +0.5 s does not fix the Leg BC MAX (v124) soft bump. It lands inside the transition sag, which comes before the bump, and deepens the sag to 7.5-10 dB holes about 0.5 s long. Moving those joins to Leg Slur MAX with a bow-change notch brought the long ones to 2.5-5 dB.

## Cycle 8 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: Solo wind and horn legato targets (Flute, Oboe, English Horn, Solo Horn KS Masters) often sag 5-12 dB for the first 0.3 s at a flat CC11 and then swell; the shape repeats exactly from render to render. A short volume-envelope lift (0 at the onset, +dB by +0.05 s, held to +0.35 s, gone by +0.6 s; dB = sag - 2, cap 8) brought 30 such notes from 7-10 dB to 2-4 dB. A flat per-pitch trim on a hot pitch deepens its sag; shape the trim (early vs body) instead.
- 2026-10-11: Violas KS Master Leg BC MAX transitions at pp-p dip about 9-10 dB at short slur-to-slur joins, against 4-5 dB for Leg Slur MAX joins. Keeping those joins on Leg Slur MAX (still overlapped, with a small CC11 notch for the bow change) halved the median dip (10.2 -> 6.2 dB). This is patch-specific (c11): applied to all soft joins, it took the violas from a 5.6 to a 3.1 dB median dip, but Violins II went from 4.8 to 6.2 and the celli from 5.1 to 5.3, with new 7-11 dB holes on short joins that had been fine on Leg BC. Measure each section before switching.
- 2026-10-11: Harp Harmonics patch: about 0.55 dB per velocity step around v58-76 (v76 -47.7 dB, v58 -57.7 dB in the mix stem).
- 2026-10-11: Pizzicato level varies by pitch too (Violas F4 +10 dB over the line's median at one velocity); a pitch trim restricted to the pizz. articulation fixes it without touching the legato notes of the same pitch.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-11: A dB ramp across a crescendo must hold until the note *after* the arrival note; releasing it from the arrival made every f/ff arrival fall 3-6 dB in its first beat. Holding it cut the falls to 1-3.6 dB and raised the main climax bar by 2.4 dB.
- 2026-10-11: Cutting the celli's f/ff trims by 1-2 dB did not change the master's loud-frame 125/250 Hz balance at all (-10.3 -> -10.4 dB at 125 Hz); a low-mid deficit at tutti that comes from the voicing is not a mix-trim problem.
### REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-11: After a Mac reset, reopening a 27-instance Opus project loaded only about 1 GB per minute from the external sample drive (30-70 MB/s) and took about 25 minutes to reach 49 GB. A render-wait loop with a fixed cap must refuse to render if memory is still growing, or it renders half-loaded patches.
- 2026-10-11: Memory compression alone does not always make a render late: at 36 of 49 GB compressed (caused by other jobs and file cache, not by REAPER) the 1x offline render passed all 19 attack checks. The c06 failure (42 of 57 GB) came with a session grown by patch reloads; run the attack probe after every render rather than trusting the compression number either way.
- 2026-10-11: Breaths and other tempo-map edits need every item recreated on the new map, but not the patches: write the tempo map, then replace each track's MIDI item and envelopes (`build_reaper.py --midi-only --tempo`).

## Cycle 9 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: Opus channel sends set pre-fader (`SendProcessor.post: false`) still return each instrument's reverb at its seat. Vln I tail R-L was -7.6 dB, the same as with post-fader sends. The panLeft/panRight seat is applied before the send's tap point, or the HO recordings carry the seat already. To get a hall tail that does not follow the seat, use a shared reverb bus outside Opus. (c17, measured: the cause was the shared hall insert's `trueStereo: false`. Loading HO's true-stereo IR `//ir/HOP True Stereo/S. Cal Hall Perc TS FR 3.3s` into the insert, with `trueStereo: true`, brought Vln I tails from -3.9 to +0.3 dB R-L, without a bus.)
- 2026-10-11: The Solo French Horn KS Master's CC15 "Con Sordino" does nothing: the spectra of muted and open notes at the same CC11 matched to 0.1 dB. The ConSordino script is a strings EQ. A second ReaEQ (low shelf 300 Hz -6, bell 1.8 kHz +3, high shelf 4.5 kHz -8) with its Wet automated 0 -> 1 moved the 2-8 kHz to f0..4f0 ratio by 8 dB.
- 2026-10-11: The HO legato scripts join only within an octave. A 16-semitone Leg BC MAX step (celli, ppp) sat 18 dB under its body for 0.8 s. The same note on Sus MAX with a 0.12 s lead reached body level by +0.2 s.
- 2026-10-11: Harp Sus RR is uneven by pitch at one velocity, by about ±4-9 dB. At 0.3-0.6 dB per velocity step, offsets of +5 to +12 and -4 to -8 velocity halved the spread of a broken-chord figure (p10-p90 8-9 -> 5-6 dB).
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-11: When adjacent level-role spans each ramp over 0.5 qn on their own, both are at full value at the shared qn, and the dB values add: +9 dB where +4 met +7, -18.5 where -6 met -8. Crossfade one into the other over about 0.25 qn instead.
- 2026-10-11: A negative note trim that reaches full depth at a legato target's moved onset deepens the transition sag. In three cases the dip went from 3-5 dB to 8-10 dB. Let note trims ramp in (about 0.6 s) and end them at the next note's moved onset.
- 2026-10-11: A dB weight on loud accents (CC11 moves about 1 dB per 10 CC there): +3 dB with a 30 ms rise, on top of the Sus Accent sample, measured 5-8 dB over the neighbouring notes. +2 dB and a velocity of 110 gave 2-4 dB.
- 2026-10-11: An untargeted < followed by > (messa di voce) must return to its starting level. With +22 / -14 CC steps the line ratcheted up by one dynamic level, and the next printed mp then sounded no louder.
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-11: Do not tune the low end to one reference recording. A lossy live Dvorak MP3 had 13 dB less 20-100 Hz than two lossless slow-movement recordings (Brahms 1/ii, 4/ii). Compare band shares (20-100, 100-250, 250-500, 500-2k, 2-6k, 6-20k Hz) against several references.
### REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-11: Compare floats that were rounded for output with a tolerance. A "cut notes that start before X" rule tested against rounded onsets cut the target note itself to 0.02 qn in one render and not in another. Check note durations in parts.json around every new rule before rendering.
- 2026-10-11: A render into a folder that already holds stems of the same names left REAPER idle and the bridge unresponsive (inferred: an overwrite prompt that a script cannot answer). Render into an empty folder; move or delete old stems first.

## Cycle 10 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: The Hollywood Strings Diamond divisi patches (for example `1st Violins KS Master DIV A.oib`) contain only a Close mic channel. The section KS Masters have Close, Mid, Main, Surround and Vintage. The DIV A KS Master's keyswitch map is the same as the section's (24-50). As a solo stand-in at the section's gain plus 1 dB and with SOLO_BOOST (+8 CC), it measured 12 dB over the p accompaniment, so its gain had to come down 7 dB. Start a DIV A solo track about 6 dB under the section's gain. (c13: this does not carry over to other DIV A patches. The Celli DIV A, as the upper half of a divisi answer with no SOLO_BOOST, measured 10-12 dB under its target at the section gain -2 and needed the section gain +4. Set the gain from one render.)
- 2026-10-11: Felt-stick timpani rolls at f/ff were 9-11 dB under the tutti in 50-250 Hz at their attack. A +6 dB volume bump on the attack (10 ms rise, held 0.25 qn, gone by 0.75 qn) brought them to -5..-6 dB without changing the roll body.
- 2026-10-11: Lowering two Solo Trombone Sus Accent notes from v121 to v108/105 cut their peaks by 3-8 dB while their bodies fell only 1-1.3 dB. A high accent velocity adds a transient, not weight.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-11: A divisi or tremolo-chord level cut compensates for the extra full-section samples, so it must start with the chord. Moving it after an sf's attack simulated the chord 10-14 dB louder and pushed the master over 0 dBFS. Starting it exactly on the sf (not 0.1 qn before it) removes the dip ahead of the accent.
- 2026-10-11: A note trim that ramps in over 0.6 s does nothing for a percussion stroke, because the stroke's energy is in the first 0.1 s. Use a level span that is at full value at the onset.
- 2026-10-11: Raising ppp/pp/p by 3/3/1.5 dB in the volume envelope moved the master LRA from 23.8 to 21.6 LU and the delivered mp3 from 16.0 to 14.6 LU, without changing CC11.
### REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-11: The volume envelope is written on a 0.125 qn grid, which smears shapes faster than about 0.15 s (sample-sag fills, attack bumps). Add the shape's own breakpoints to the grid when timing within 0.1 s matters.
- 2026-10-11: With no master FX, the rendered stems sum to the master to -122 dBFS peak. A post-render gain fix on one track can be applied to its stem and the master rebuilt as the stem sum, which equals a re-render, as long as REAPER and the config get the same gain.
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-11: Simulate level-only changes before rendering. Apply each track's new-minus-old volume envelope (in dB, on the score-time grid) to the previous render's stems, sum them, and run the delivery master. In c10 it predicted the delivered LRA (14.6 LU) and limiter (3.6 dB) that the render then measured, and it found two changes that would have raised the peak. It cannot model velocity or articulation changes.

## Cycle 11 additions
### REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-11: REAPER can hang at launch with no window title and flat memory, while the bridge stays silent. `sample <pid>` showed it waiting in `OnStartup_LoadProjects -> CheckFaultyProject -> MessageBox`: reaper.ini had a `faultyproject=` entry, and REAPER asks whether to load that project with FX offline. Without the GUI: kill REAPER, start it with `open -a REAPER --args -new` (an empty project, so the startup script and bridge run), then open the project with a bridge job, `reaper.Main_openProject("noprompt:<path>")`. The job returned in 17 s, and the samples streamed in afterwards. REAPER cleared the entry itself.
- 2026-10-11: A level-only fix found after a render (a changed volume-envelope shape on one or two tracks) can be applied to the rendered stems as the new-minus-old dB envelope, and the master rebuilt as the stem sum (the stems summed to the master to -121.6 dBFS). Update REAPER with the same data (`--midi-only` for those tracks) so the project still matches. This saves a second render and a restart.
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: Solo French Horn Sus MAX attacks on A flat 4 at f (v83) rise slowly: body - 6 dB only at +0.34 s, then the note swells about 3 dB over its body at +0.4 s before it settles. An early fill held to +0.35 s overshot the body by 4-10 dB. Hold a fill only until the sample's own rise begins (here a +8 dB fill held to +0.10 s and gone by +0.35 s gave -5 dB at +0.10 s and a +2 dB peak). Measure the shape out to +0.6 s before choosing the hold.
- 2026-10-11: Opus legato lead tables are not enough for a few pitches and intervals; these are repeatable per patch and need their own leads:
  - Solo Horn legato departures from F4 crossed 0.19-0.31 s late at v124. An extra 0.15 s lead brought them to +0.05..+0.18 s.
  - Celli A flat 3 -> C4 crossed at +0.23 s. An interval-specific +0.15 s brought it to -0.05 s.
- 2026-10-11: Velocity 108 vs 124 did not change the Violins I KS Master legato timing at all (identical crossovers to the millisecond). To move a transition, move its start: per-onset lead offsets of 0.05-0.07 s moved the crossovers by 0.05-0.07 s.
- 2026-10-11: The legato lead cap (0.45 x the previous note's length) makes extra leads useless on fast slurred notes (0.33-0.5 qn triplets): a lead table change there has no effect.
- 2026-10-11: The Hollywood Strings divisi (DIV A) KS Master used as a solo violin legatos faster than the full section at the same lead (crossover median -0.03 s against +0.02 at 0.17 s). A lead of 0.12 s matched the section.
- 2026-10-11: Ending fast slurred Sus MAX string notes (sextuplets at about 0.16 s) 0.01 qn before the next onset at v100, instead of overlapping them by 0.03 qn at v83, did not change how clearly each note sounds (median dominance share 0.27 -> 0.26). Their blur is not from the overlap.
- 2026-10-11: Leg Port MAX (1st Violins KS Master) can be switched in for a single legato target. Send its keyswitch while the previous note sounds and switch back before the next note; the join had no hole (3 dB). A harmonic-sum f0 tracker with 43 ms windows did not resolve the glide: in a section, the source and target pitches dominate every frame. Measure the slide on a short-window f0 track or by ear.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-11: Early fills that hold to the measured end of the hole (+0.15..+0.4 s, dB = dip - 3, cap 7) brought 14 of 15 wind, horn and string joins from 7-11 dB to 3-5 dB. The fixed 0.10 s hold had left most of the longer holes in place.
- 2026-10-11: An accent shape of +4 dB (10 ms rise, held 0.12 qn, gone by 0.3 qn), on a Solo Trombone Sus Accent note at v108, turned an attack 1.7 dB under its body into one 2 dB over it. With a +3 dB role over the note, the ff pickup went from 5.4 to 1.5 dB under the master. The stem peak rose 6 dB, but the master peak did not, because the master's peak is the tutti.
- 2026-10-11: When the instruments of a ff tutti chord arrive from different crescendos, some carry the crescendo's dB gain and some enter cold. The single winds' level spread over 13 dB on one chord. Per-track roles evened it to 5 dB and halved the fall into the next bar (2.2 -> 1.2 dB).
- 2026-10-11: An untargeted swell that overshoots a later printed goal makes the goal sound no louder. Capping the swell 8 CC under the goal and adding +2 dB at mf arrivals raised the goal by only about 1 dB relative to the phrase start; CC11 shaping alone does not make a p-mp -> mf arch audible. Starting from p, cap at the goal itself: an 8 CC margin flattened short solo swells (Horn: CC 68 -> 52).
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-11: The envelope simulation predicted the limiter at 3.1 dB; the render measured 3.5. Lead and articulation changes at the climax (earlier string legato starts) move the peaks, and the simulation cannot model them. Leave about 0.5 dB of margin under the limiter target.

Choosing per join from two renders (one on each articulation) took 16 joins 2.5-5 dB shallower and halved the celli's joins of 7 dB or more (22 -> 11). Render both versions once, then keep a per-join list.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-11: A level trim on a pizzicato or other plucked/struck note must be a step at full value on the attack (0 at onset - 0.03 s, full by - 0.01 s, held to the next onset). A 0.25 qn ramp sampled on the 0.125 qn envelope grid reached full depth just before the next note: an -8 dB trim sat at -1 to -2.5 dB on its own attack and cut the following note. With the step, a viola pizz. figure's spread went from 8 to 5.7 dB and its largest jump from 14.5 to 6.7 dB.
- 2026-10-11: In a long build-up, cycles of crescendo gain and evening roles had raised the first ff climax by 2.8 dB while the second ff (no crescendo hairpin into it, so no hairpin gain) stayed put, leaving it 4.8 LU under the first. Check every ff arrival with no hairpin into it; +2-2.5 dB on the strings, +2-6 dB on the winds that entered cold and -1 dB on the first climax brought the gap to 1.6 LU and lowered the delivery limiter from 3.5 to 3.0 dB.
- 2026-10-11: A pp chord of many sustained parts (horns, clarinets, strings) measured as loud in short-term loudness as the p solo with thin accompaniment that follows it. Cut the loudest stems of the chord (here the horns, 2-4 dB over the rest) rather than the global ppp trim: lowering the ppp trim by 1.5 dB moved the section only 0.3 dB, because most of the chord was marked pp.
### Timing
- 2026-10-11: Phrase-final lengthening by tempo steps (x1.015 in the first half of a phrase, x0.96 and x0.92 on the last two beats, whole-beat steps only) added 0.03-0.11 s per phrase and 1.06 s over 14 phrases. Leads converted on the real tempo map followed without changes, and the attack gate still passed. Skip a phrase that ends where a printed rit. starts, or the tempo jumps back up between them.
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-11: The level simulation on the previous stems (c10 method) under-predicted the delivery limiter again: 2.4 dB simulated, 3.0 dB rendered, after level changes at the climaxes plus a full rebuild of two string tracks. Keep about 0.5 dB of margin.
- 2026-10-11: An attack-hole probe that takes the minimum of 10 ms frames inside a tremolo is noisy to about 2 dB. A 4.3 -> 6.6 dB change crossed the limit while 25 ms envelopes of the two renders matched within 1 dB. Compare envelopes before treating a marginal probe failure as a new hole.

## Cycle 13 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: The Hollywood Strings DIV A KS Masters ship with the Opus main (Master) channel panned: 1st Violins DIV A -0.74, Celli DIV A +0.33. Every section and solo-instrument patch used here had it at 0. The main channel pans after the reverb return, so a seat set on the mic channels does not undo it. A solo track on 1st Violins DIV A measured 21 dB R-L, with its hall on the same side. Centring the main channel (pan 0, panLeft -1, panRight 1) brought it to -5 dB. Check `mixer.channels[main].pan` of every patch you load.
- 2026-10-11: Opus treats CC7 as linear amplitude. Inside timpani rolls the level followed 20 log10(CC7) (fitted slopes 0.8-1.1), so a CC7 lane built as 127 x 10^(dB/40) delivers half of each planned dB change. Putting the other half in the volume envelope, relative to the roll's start, took a p < f roll from +5.3 to +9.9 dB and a decrescendo roll's fall from 4.5 to 8.4 dB, without touching the measured static levels.
- 2026-10-11: Per-pitch sample level in the woodwind KS Masters, measured at a flat CC11 against neighbouring notes: Clarinet A flat 4 (the written B flat 4 throat note) -8 dB; Bassoon E flat 3 -6, G3 -5; English Horn D flat 4 -5. Basses Sus MAX: E flat 2 -5.5, D flat 2 +4 (pizz. even). A +6/+4 dB pitch trim brought each within about 1-3 dB of its neighbours.
### Articulation and divisi
- 2026-10-11: A divisi passage where one voice carries the line is best split onto a half-section divisi patch for that voice, while the other voice stays on the section track. Played as two-note chords on one full-section KS Master, a slurred cello answer was re-attacked as Sus MAX chords and opened with a 10-15 dB hole. On its own Leg Slur track the entry dip was 4.6-6.6 dB. Keep the section track's divisi level cut (-3 dB) for the voice it still plays.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-11: Stacked plan shaping at soft string bow-change joins (an 8 CC notch, a taper on the slur's last note when the line continues, and a bloom-correction ramp that ended on the join) added 2.4-5.2 dB to the sample's own 4.4 dB sag. Do not taper or ramp down into a note that the next note joins, and keep bow-change notches at 3-4 CC.
- 2026-10-11: At f, a horn breath with a 0.2 qn gap (about 0.2 s) dipped only 4-5 dB, because the release and the hall tail filled it. Lengthening the gap to 0.3 qn deepened the dip by only about 1 dB (hall decay about 2.5 s). A loud breath needs a longer gap or a cut in the hall send, not a few hundredths of a beat more.

## Cycle 14 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-11: Hollywood Strings Leg Slur MAX cannot play fast slurred figures. On viola sextuplets of 0.157 s per note, at v127 with a 0.02 qn overlap and no lead, the target pitch dominated only 3% of its slot (Sus MAX with short crossfades: 29-33%). The legato voice held one pitch per group. Below about 0.2 s per note, use Sus MAX, or a run/fast-legato patch if the library has one.
- 2026-10-11: Solo Trombone Marc Lng RRx4 at v94-106 has a body 2-3 dB under Sus MAX (v91, CC11 114), and its attack rises over about 0.1 s with no peak over the body. Played at 0.75 of the written length, it gives the separation of a marcato line but not the attack weight. A +2 dB body lift brought the level back.
- 2026-10-11: A felt-stick timpani stroke (Timpani Flt Lng, v96) placed on the tied note at the end of a crescendo roll, at a CC7 4 dB (40 log scale) over the roll plus a 3 dB volume bump, peaked 6.4 dB over the roll's last half beat and then decayed. MIDI from LilyPond merges a tie into the roll note, so the stroke has to be added by rule.

## Cycle 24 additions
### REAPER / bridge / machine
- 2026-10-12: Most of an Opus project's memory is the preload of rows that are loaded but never played. A KS Master loads about six default rows (Sus Accent, the RRx9/RRx16 staccato and double-tongue rows, Marc Shrt), and a score that plays one to four of them carries the rest. Setting `loaded`/`active` False on every row the track does not play (in the instrument's `layerMatrix.rows`) took a 32-instance project from 61 GB to 43 GB settled after a fresh open, and the render ended at 44 GB with nothing compressed (it had ended at 63 GB with 44 GB compressed). Every stem whose data did not change rendered bit-identical, so unloading rows does not change the sound.
- 2026-10-12: Read memory only after the samples have finished streaming in. 20-21 GB readings taken soon after opening a project were partial loads: the same project settled at 61 GB about 13 minutes after the open. Wait for several equal `top` readings 30 s apart before you record a footprint.
- 2026-10-12: A full delete-and-recreate rebuild of 32 Opus tracks took about a minute of bridge jobs. Reopening the project afterwards (`-new` + `Main_openProject`) took about 10 minutes until memory settled at 43 GB. The 61 GB project before the cut took about 18 minutes.
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-12: Every HO KS Master's `controllerMap` maps CC36 to "Repetition: Reset RR" and CC69 to "Repetition" (manual CC table: CC36 any value 1-127 resets round robins; CC69 0-63 turns the Repetition script off). Sending CC36 at the start of a render changed which round-robin samples the wind tracks with repeated notes played (Bassoon 1, Bass clarinet), with no level change (0.35 dB p95 over 0.5 qn bins). Send it in the pre-roll of every track so each render starts from the same round-robin position.
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-12: Loudness motion can be read from ffmpeg's ebur128 metadata at 10 Hz, over the frames with short-term loudness above -60 LUFS. Two useful figures are the 90th percentile of the 4 s change in short-term loudness, and the std of the 1 s power mean of momentary loudness minus its 20 s power mean. On two slow movements by Brahms they are 8.55/4.56 and 6.29/3.92 dB. This mockup's mp3 measured 5.0/2.8, and a series of balance cuts on solo crests had lowered them over five cycles. Track both figures every cycle.
- 2026-10-12: A notch that lowers a note body also lowers the reference level used by spike detectors on the neighbouring join, so that join can appear as a new spike even though its audio is unchanged. Compare the audio level with the previous render before treating a newly listed spike as real.
- 2026-10-12: A 10 ms threshold onset probe can flip from ok (+0.12 s) to late (+0.34 s) on audio identical to the previous render when a slow string entry has two similar rises: a 0.2 dB change in the pre-onset floor moved the crossing to the second rise. Check flagged attacks against the previous render's audio before treating them as engine lateness.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-12: Lowering an unmarked swell is a better way to make a phrase's printed crest stand out than lowering the crest itself. A -2.5 dB role on a horn solo's p< > swell raised the printed mp crest from +0.9 to +3.4 dB over it. Removing a +2.5 dB swell gain on the violins raised their printed crest from +0.8 to +2.9 dB over the swell. Neither change moved the delivery figures (LRA, span or limiter).
- 2026-10-12: A note trim that ramps in over 0.6 s lets a soft wind entry speak under strings that re-articulate on the same beat: the oboe stood 4.7 dB under the second violins at the onset. A 0.1 s ramp brought it to -0.9 dB.

## Cycle 25 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-12: The HO Basses KS Master's fundamental is uneven by pitch. On C2-G2 it is as loud as harmonics 2-4 (Db2 -0.1, D2 +8.6, F2 +9.5 dB), while on E1-B flat 1 it sits 12-30 dB under them; the tuba, bass trombone and contrabassoon sit 11-24 dB under in the same render. - 2026-10-12: With CC36 = 127 (round-robin reset) and, on the strings, CC69 = 0 (Repetition off) sent in the pre-roll, two renders of unchanged string data were 96-99% the same (50 ms frames, residual under -30 dB). Without the reset most frames differed. Woodwinds that got only the CC36 reset were still 12-20% different (Flute 1, Bassoon 1). - 2026-10-12: One loud sample layer can be hot on its own. A cello A flat 3 at f/ff was 4-6 dB over its neighbouring notes at the same CC11, while the same pitch at p/mp was within 1 dB. When a single note is the loudest stem in a tutti, compare it with the same pitch at other dynamics before you change the whole line.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-12: Old negative note trims can go stale. Trims set against sample swells in early cycles, with a 0.6 s ramp, later made 4-7 dB holes in the middle of printed crescendos once CC shaping had removed the swells. Before adding or keeping a negative trim, measure the note with no trim. On crescendo notes use a step (0.1 s ramp) at a smaller depth; on a printed decrescendo, ramp the trim over the hairpin's length (2.1 s on a 2 qn decrescendo gave a 4.6 dB fall across the bar instead of 75% of it in the first 0.5 s).
- 2026-10-12: When a level offset (a role) ends on the downbeat of a held note, the note drops at once by the role's depth (5.5 dB on a coda clarinet's last note). End lifts at the end of the phrase, or at the printed decrescendo, and release them over its length.
- 2026-10-12: Timpani ppp rolls that follow the generic soft-roll cut and settle can end up 14-23 dB under the orchestra and masked in every band but the fundamental. +3 to +4 dB on the ppp rolls kept them under every other stem while making them audible as a low layer (master change under 0.5 dB).
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-12: A plain-amplitude simulation (stem x dB envelope, master = master + new stem - old stem) predicted ReaEQ and volume-envelope changes within 0.3 dB on band shares and per-pitch fundamentals. On a sustained note with a long trim ramp (3 s) it came out about 2 dB low, probably because the sample's own decay under the trim differed from the simulated shape. Check long-ramp trims against the render.

## Cycle 26 additions
### Opus / Hollywood Orchestra behaviour
- 2026-10-12: A long held string chord (15-25 s, one sample per section) makes every section replay its 3.9-4.0 s sustain loop 4-5 times, and sections that start together loop in step. A periodic inverse level profile removes the level part (c25: 0.5-1.5 Hz movement 2.7 -> 1.3 dB) but not the loop's change in tone colour (centroid autocorrelation at the loop period still 0.39-0.63 per stem). A listener heard it as a slow synchronized bowing effect. Splitting the held notes into staggered same-pitch re-bows of 1.8-4.5 s, at different times per section, took the master's 100-300 Hz loop autocorrelation from 0.58 to 0.24 and the centroid's from 0.39 to 0.19.
- 2026-10-12: A same-pitch re-bow on HS Sus MAX at pp (v41, CC11 about 30) starts slowly: the stem dips 6-15 dB for about 0.9 s, and the new note settles 3-9 dB away from the level of the note it replaces (Sus MAX and Leg Slur MAX sustains differ at the same CC11). Plan a level shape per re-bow (fit it on a render), or the re-bows read as holes.
- 2026-10-12: HO trombone accents: Sus Accent MAX at v105-110 with CC11 122 (the fff layer) put a 2-5 kHz burst 7-13 dB over the note's own body. Sus MAX at v92-96 with CC11 at the printed ff (114) and a volume swell (full at +0.07 s, held to +0.25 s, gone by +0.6 s) gave -1..-9 dB in that band and no master jump (+10 -> +2 dB). The weight then has to come from the swell: +2.5-3 dB left the accents only 0-1 dB over the surrounding marcato eighths; +4.5-6 dB gave 1.5-4 dB.
### Dynamics shaping
- 2026-10-12: An untargeted decrescendo in an inner part (one dynamic level, 14 CC) can leave a held chord over a tune that falls 5-9 dB through its own printed decrescendo. Give it the tutti's arrival dynamic as its target: the chord fell 1-5 dB with the tune.
### Measurement methods
- 2026-10-12: To hear whether a sample loop shows, fold the 100-300 Hz band level and the spectral centroid of the master over the held passage and take the autocorrelation at the loop period; compare with a short chord of the same voicing (0.18 here). Level folding alone misses colour cycles that a level profile has already flattened.
