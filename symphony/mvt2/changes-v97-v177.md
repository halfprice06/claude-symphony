# Movement II: changes from v97 to v177

This covers cycles 97–176, the cycles that turned v97 into v177. The form, length (92 bars plus the attacca bar, about 5:45) and themes are the same as in v97. The changes are detailed revisions. Fifteen of the 80 cycles changed nothing (v114, v130, v132, v144, v160, v162–v164, v170, v172, v175, v178 are relabelled copies). The full log with reasons is in critics/HISTORY.md.

## Changes you would hear most

- **Bass clarinet solo, 31–37 (c140, c111, c149).** The solo moved down an octave, so it now plays movement I's motto at the pitch movement I used. The cor anglais now plays a sixth above it (it was a third below) and leads into theme B with a pickup at 36. Bassoon 2 no longer doubles the bass clarinet's A♭; the contrabassoon holds the pedal alone.
- **First climax, 57–64.** The flute and first violins now reach A♭6 at 58, saving B♭6 for the main climax at 79 (c110). The 7th at bar 60 is held into 61 instead of being anticipated (c172). The contrabassoon adds a low D♭1 at 61 (c133), and trombone 2 fills the brass chord (c104, c119). The allargando now starts at 64, so the trombone quotation's first bar is in tempo (c153). The bass trombone plays sf>p at 63 (c154).
- **Basses, 53–56 and 65–71.** The double basses now rest at 53–56 and return at 57 (c164). At 65–70 four solo basses play, and the full section returns at 71 (c109).
- **Main climax, 77–81.** The flute run at 78 is written out note by note in the flute and violins (c149). The harp adds low octaves at 74, 77 and 78 (c114). Horn 3's F♭ at 80–81 gets a crescendo (c98), and the hemiola at 80–81 has tenutos (c148).
- **Coda, 82–92.** The timpani roll at 89–92 is gone (c120). The cor anglais joins the muted horns at 87–88 and descends F♭→E♭ at 88–90 (c104, c150).

## Harmony

- French sixth at 21 over F♭ in the bass (c152).
- IV7 at 25, iii6 at 78, half-diminished vii7 over C at 39 (c102).
- II6/5 before the cadential 6/4 at 43; the bass goes down to G♭1 there (c112, c122).
- Bar 55 rebuilt with a new bass line E♭–C–A–B♭ (c112).
- Bar 58: the suspension now resolves cleanly, with no voice sounding the resolution note early (c119, c151).
- Cross relations removed at 50 and 59–60, parallel fifths removed at 53–54 (harp) and 62 (violas) (c99, c132, c142).
- Timpani at 53 now lands on D♭ with the cellos, so theme B returns over a root-position tonic (c173).

## Orchestration and texture

- Violas and harp moved off the counter-melody at 25–28 and 74–78 so it stands out (c127).
- Second violins re-voiced at 37–40 and 39 so they do not double the flute's tune (c141, c142).
- Harp right hand re-voiced at 53–56 (c137); bassoon 1 holds a line under the harp at 54–56 (c107).
- Clarinets enter above horn 1 at 57 (c147); clarinet 1 moves from F5 to A♭5 at 61–62 (c167).
- Cellos play on the D string at 44–47 (c121), softer under the oboe (c124).

## Dynamics and expression

- Hairpins and swells added in the strings at 15–22, 25–26, 73–74; niente endings at 3, 66, 81, 82 (c138, c139, c168).
- Diminuendos from the suspended E♭ at 8, 24, 72 (c98).
- "dolce cantabile" for flute 1 at 37, "cantabile" for violins at 52, "dolce" for oboe 1 at 15 and solo violin at 17 (c128, c158).
- Flute entries balanced against the cor anglais and clarinets at 40, 41–44, 77 (c101, c123, c134, c178).
- MIDI levels corrected so playback matches the printed dynamics (c160).

## Playability, bowings and breaths

- New bowings for the violins at 61 and 77–79, cello slurs at 26, 41, 46, 74, 80–81 (c118).
- Breaths added for oboe 1 at 58, horn 4 at 2, bassoon 1 at 77, contrabassoon at 80 (c166, c176).
- Harp: pedal diagram, pedal marks and dynamics (c116). Timpani: stick changes, damping, playing near the rim (c146).
- Slurs in the soft wind accompaniment (c126); flute 2 now tongues at 61–62 (c176).
- Bass clarinet part notes the low C and gives an ossia (c126, c156).
