## Material

- **Theme A** (Vln I, bar 23): `es'8 f'8 | aes'4. c''8 es''4~ | es''4 d''4 des''4 | c''4. bes'8 aes'4 | g'2 es'8 f'8 | aes'4. c''8 f''4~ | f''4 e''4 es''4 | des''4 c''8 bes'8 g'4 | aes'2.` A rising arpeggio to a held note, then the motto's tail E♭–D–D♭–C (5̂–♯4̂–4̂–3̂), harmonised vi7 – V7/V(6) – V7 – I. It does not open with a rising sixth (critic 101's rule) and does not use the S-head rhythm.
- **Theme B** (Fl 1, bar 37): `aes'4 des''4 f''4. es''8 | des''4 c''8 des''8 es''2 | ges''4. f''8 es''4 des''4 | c''2 bes'2 | aes'4 des''4 f''4. ges''8 | aes''4 g''4 ges''4 f''4 | es''4. f''8 des''4 bes'4 | c''2.` Opens with a rising fourth; bar 42 is the motto's tail at the D♭ level.
- **Cyclic quotations (once each):** movement I's second-theme head in minor with pickup, oboe 1, F♯ minor, bars 47–49; movement I's first-theme head, trombone 1, ff, A minor, bars 63–64.

## Rules kept from the cyclic notes

- No tam-tam, no trombone chorale, no C-major material (all reserved for movement III).
- Melodic moments for the instruments movement I kept from melody: horn 1 unsupported (7–14, 67–70, 87–90), bass clarinet (31–37), harp (91–96), timpani (93–98). String-free passages: 3–14, 31–36 (basses only).
- D♭ is the key of the middle section; the movement closes with a plagal D♭–A♭ cadence with D♭–C on top.
- More inverted bass positions than movement I, and a melodic bass counterline (cellos 23–30, 71–82).
- Same orchestra as movement I; percussion tacet.
