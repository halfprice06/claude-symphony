## Findings

### 1. In the score, dynamics touch down-stem ends; the scherzo's s305 override fixes it (APPLIED)

- **Bars:** visible across the score, worst at 79: Ob ff, C.a. ff, Cl ff, B.cl. ff, Bn ff, Cbn ff, Hn 3·4 f, Timp f (on the tremolo strokes), Cb ff. All sit directly under a down-stem, and in a 220 dpi render the stem ends run into the top of the ff/f glyph.
- **Problem:** LilyPond places a dynamic against the outline of its glyph. The top left of an f is open, so the dynamic moves up until the stem end reaches the hook. At the score's 14 pt staff size this reads as a collision.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12) show marks that collide in engraved parts and say they must be proofread out. Gould PDF 55 says no characters should collide.
- **Fix (score.ly, in the Staff context that holds the dynamics; the same line in genparts.py's Staff context):**
  `\context { \Staff \consists "Dynamic_engraver" \consists "Dynamic_align_engraver" \override DynamicText.vertical-skylines = #ly:grob::simple-vertical-skylines-from-extents }`
  With box skylines, every dynamic at 79 now clears its stem end by a small gap. The fixed system breaks and the 8 pages are unchanged, and the only change is a slightly different vertical spacing inside the systems. score.midi is byte-identical. In the parts, which use 21 pt staves and have more room, I found no ink collision in v315. The override moves a few dynamics there by a fraction of a space, and I applied it so that the parts and the score follow the same rule.

### 2. Five entries come after rests that cross tempo changes, with no cue (APPLIED, part only)

- **Bars and parts:**
  - Ob 1 enters at 44 b4 after 21 bars' rest (23–43) across "poco rit." (35), the change to 4/4 and "Poco più mosso" (37).
  - Fl 1 enters at 57 after 12 bars' rest (45–56) across "poco rit." (47), "poco sostenuto" (48) and "a tempo, poco a poco animando" (53).
  - B.cl. enters at 57 after 19 bars' rest (38–56) across the same three tempo words.
  - The harp enters at 71 after 14 bars' rest (57–70) across "poco allargando" (60), "Largamente" (61), "allargando" (64) and "Tempo I" (65).
  - The harp enters at 37 on the change of metre and tempo, after 22 bars' rest with a "poco rit." at 35.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest, the cues being transposed to the reading key of the instrument. Cues must be audible and obvious." Ex. 4 (PDF 10) places a named cue just before the entry, after a rest that crosses letters and tempo words. A rest counted through tempo changes is the case where counting is least reliable (c135's reason for its own cues). c285 declined other cues only because those rests stay in one tempo.
- **Fix (as applied; the score keeps its rests through `\tag #'score`):**
  - bSec.ily, bSecOboeOne: `R1*5 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring #"corAnglaisQ" #DOWN { <>^\markup \tiny "C.a." R1*2 } } | r2 r4 aes'4(…`. The cue is the cor anglais's theme B at 42–43 (A♭4…B♭3). #DOWN puts the oboe's own rest above the low cue notes. With #UP, the whole rest landed on the D♭4 at 43 (Gould PDF 50–51).
  - bSec.ily, bSecFluteOne: `R1*2 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring #"vlnOneQ" #UP { <>^\markup \tiny "Vln. I" R1*2 } } |`. The cue is the violins' theme B at 55–56.
  - bSec.ily, bSecBassCl: `R1*2 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \transposedCueDuring #"vlnOneQ" #UP c { <>^\markup \tiny "Vln. I" R1*2 } } |`. genparts' `\transpose bes, c'` turns the pitch argument `c` into d′, so the cue prints A♭5–C5, one octave under the written pitch, inside the stave and clear of the rest. With `c,` the rest collided with the D4 and E♭4 of 56.
  - retr.ily, new `retrHarpR = { R2.*3 | \tag #'score { R2.*3 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring #"hnOneQ" #DOWN { <>^\markup \tiny "Hn. 1" R2.*3 } } | }`. The cue is horn 1's motto tail at 68–70. #DOWN is for the same reason as the oboe.
  - trans.ily, new `transHarpR = { R2.*3 | \tag #'score { R2.*3 } \tag #'part { \transposedCueDuring #"bassClQ" #DOWN c'' { <>^\markup \tiny "B.cl." R2.*3 } } | }`. The cue is the bass clarinet solo at 34–36, an octave up, as in Fl 1's c165 cue.
  - genparts.py: new `\addQuote "vlnOneQ" { \removeWithTag #'part \vlnOne }`, placed before `bassClQ`, because the bass clarinet's source now contains a vlnOneQ cue. `oboeOneQ` now filters `#'part` only (it filtered `#'(score part)`). With the new score/part pair at 42–43, removing both tags would make the quote two bars short. The Fl 2/Ob 2 cue at 59–60 prints exactly as before.
  - score.ly: `\pc \removeWithTag #'part \oboeOne` and `\removeWithTag #'part \harpR`, so the score never shows these cues.
- I checked every cue pitch against its source part at 300–400 dpi. All parts except the harp stay on one page, and the harp keeps its two pages and its turn at 70/71.

### 3. Horns 3·4 at 79 and Ob 1 at 78: no cue after 13–14 bars that cross "poco allargando" (recorded, optional, not applied)

- Hn 3·4 rest 65–78 and enter f at 79. Ob 1 rests 65–77 and enters ff at 78. Both rests cross the "poco allargando" at 78, so c285's one-tempo reason does not cover them.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 5, Ex. 4.
- **Suggested fix (untested):** add `\addQuote "hnTwoQ" { \removeWithTag #'part \hnTwo }` to genparts.py. In aTwo.ily, give Hn 3 and Hn 4 a two-bar `\cueDuring #"hnTwoQ" #DOWN` before 79, taking horn 2's counter-melody at 77–78, and give Ob 1 a one-bar Vln I cue at 77. I rank this below finding 2 because the entries fall on the tutti climax, which the players cannot miss.

### 4. Harp octave lines in the part (recorded, no change)

- MOLA PDF 5 says to avoid 8va and 8va bassa in parts. The harp LH has 8va bassa at 13–14, 71–78 and 80. HISTORY protects this as c265's "harp octave-lower plan", and a harpist reads 8va bassa in the left hand as a matter of course, so I do not re-raise it.

### 5. String bow marks (recorded, no change)

- MOLA PDF 5 says bowings should be printed only for a specific effect. Every printed bow in this movement serves a stated effect: the up-bow crescendo into the ff down-bow at 60–61 (c085), the bow change on the tie at 61–62 (c118), the fresh down-bow for the B♭6 at 79 (c246), and "change bow at will" on the long pedals. This follows the reading.
