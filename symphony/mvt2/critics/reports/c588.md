## Findings

### 1. Bars 73-74, cellos: the new 9-8 suspension is cut off from its resolution by the slurs (applied)

**Problem.** c469 tied the cellos' C4 from 73 b3 into 74. Over the bass B♭1 it is a 9-8 suspension, and it resolves to B♭3 at 74 b1.5. The slurring was copied from the plain line at 25-26: `aes2\< c'4~) | c'4 bes8( aes8) g4`. The slur ends on the preparation. The suspended C4 at 74 b1 is under no slur, and a new slur starts on the resolution B♭3. On the page, and in a cellist's bow, the dissonance therefore ends one gesture, and the resolution gets a fresh bow stroke and its own attack.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 348 says the resolution "should sound like a relaxation, a decrease in intensity", and that the suspension takes "something of an accent and [its] resolutions are softer". A new bow on the resolution works against this. In the images, every suspension is slurred into its resolution: Ex. 21-1 (Jupiter, PDF 346) ties the preparation to the suspension inside one slur, Ex. 21-17 (Mozart K. 378, PDF 357) slurs each 7 into its 6, and Ex. 21-37 (Beethoven Op. 126/3, PDF 367) slurs the suspended chord into its release. This score already does the same for its other tied suspensions. c338 slurred the violins' tied E♭ into D at 24 and 72 and kept the same bow count. 73-74 is the only tied suspension in the movement whose resolution starts a new slur.

**Fix (applied, sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoVc, bar 73-74):**
```
| aes2\< c'4~-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \> | c'4 bes8) aes8 g4\! -\tweak stencil ##f \p |
```
The slur from 72 b3 now ends on the resolution B♭3. The passing A♭3 and the G3 each take their own bow. Bar 74 still has three bows: the slurred bow carrying C-B♭, then A♭, then G. So the bow directions c118 set are kept, and 75 is still an up-bow crescendo. The slurred bow lasts 4½ beats, about 5 s at ♩=52 and p-mp, which is normal for a cello. The hidden mp and the diminuendo are unchanged. Bar 26 in A keeps its plain, untied line (c469). Suggested source comment: `% c588: slur carried from the preparation through the suspended C4 into its resolution B flat3 (A&S PDF 348; Ex. 21-1, 21-17, 21-37), as c338 at 24/72; bow count in 74 unchanged (c118)`.
