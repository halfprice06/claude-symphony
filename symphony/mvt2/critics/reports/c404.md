### 1. Bars 61-62: the full tutti chord checked group by group (no change)

Sounding notes at 61 b1:
- Woodwind: D♭1 (Cbn), D♭3 (B.cl., Bsn 2), F3 (Bsn 1), A♭4 (Cl 2), D♭5 (Ob 2), F5 (Ob 1, Cl 1), A♭5 (Fl 2), A♭6 (Fl 1).
- Brass: D♭2 (tuba), D♭3 (B.tbn), F3 (Tbn 2), D♭4 F4 (Hn 1·2), A♭4 (Hn 3·4).
- Strings: D♭2 (Cb), A♭3 (Vc), D♭4 F4 (Vla), A♭4 (Vln II), D♭6 (Vln I).

Each group is a complete D♭ triad. The bass octave holds only the root, and the tuba sits under the bass trombone as in chords 1-4 (PDF 505). The brass is in close position and one degree under (f against ff), and the timpani roll f on the root, as in chords 2-3. The woodwind group has a gap from F3 to A♭4, which the horns and violas fill. The tables also fill such gaps with another group (chord 2: horns between the clarinets and bassoons). At 62 b1 (G♭) and 62 b3 (F♭ = E) the woodwind and brass are complete. The strings at 62 b3 have no fifth (c274). Recorded only.

### 2. Bars 79-81: the second peak against chord 4 and Ex. 209 (no change)

At 79 the melody B♭ sounds in four octaves (Fl 1 + Vln I B♭6, Ob 1 + Cl 1 + Vln II B♭5, C.a. + Vla B♭4). The harmony is in Cl 2, Ob 2, Fl 2 and Hn 1-4, and the bass is D♭ in three octaves. That is the layout of Ex. 209 (PDF 375): the melody in octaves across winds and violins, with the harmony in clarinets, bassoons and horns. The string group alone holds only D♭ and B♭. The tables would complete it, but c114 rejected a string chord on 79 and the violin octave plan is protected, so I do not raise it again. The horns at f under the ff winds and strings are one degree under, as in the tables.

### 3. Bar 63: string chord under the trombone quotation (no change)

At 63 the strings hold A1, A2, E4, C5, E5, C6, E6 and A6, with Fl 1 on A6. There is an empty twelfth from A2 to E4. That space is left for trombone 1's quotation (E3-C4, c074), and Ex. 219-221 (PDF 122) leave room in the same way for the one line that carries the music. The tables' rule against gaps applies to a single chord, not to a chord accompanying a solo. Protected.

### 4. Soft wind chords at 3-6 and 31-36 against chords 13, 14 and 16 (no change)

At 3-6 the chord is A♭2, E♭3 (E3 at 5), A♭3, C4 (D♭4 at 5) in B.cl., Cl and Hn 3·4. It is close and complete, as in chord 16 (Christmas Night, pp: winds and brass, low register, close order). At 31-36 the bass is a Cbn/Bsn A♭ pedal under single wind lines. PDF 121 says a pedal does not change the character of a wind tutti, and a soft wind tutti has little power. That suits a link passage. Protected (woodwind choir 31-36).
