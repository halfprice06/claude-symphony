## Findings (ranked)

**F1. Bar 22: the pickup into theme A is spaced at three to five times the width of the same eighths elsewhere on the system. Applied.**
Problem: the Vln I instruction "tutti (gli altri con sord.), espr., sul D" sits on the last eighth-note pair of the system. LilyPond keeps it inside the right margin by widening the last column, so bar 22 is twice as wide as bars 19-21, and the E♭4–F4 pickup (Vln I and II) gets about 130 px at 150 dpi against about 25 px for the eighths in bars 16 and 21. The half note on beat 1 of the same bar gets less space than the eighth on beat 3.5.
Evidence: Gould PDF 54: "Each longer value ... should have more space than its shorter neighbour" and "Notes of equal duration require equal spacing for an entire system"; PDF 53: "Poor horizontal spacing is often the main problem of badly presented music". This is the entry of the theme's first string statement, so it is the bar a conductor looks at.
Fix (aOne.ily, aOneVlnOne, bar 22):
```
es''2) es'8(\mp-\tweak X-offset #-9 ^\markup \italic \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.4) \column { "tutti (gli altri con sord.)," "espr., sul D" } f'8 |
```
In the scratch build bar 22 is now as wide as its neighbours and the eighths match those in 16 and 21.

**F2. Bar 62 b3, Bn 2 F♭2: the tenuto touches the end of the down-stem and reads as a bracket. Applied.**
Evidence: Gould PDF 55: "no characters should collide"; the minimum gap between symbols is half a staff space. On the part-combined staff LilyPond puts the lower voice's tenuto at the stem tip with almost no padding.
Fix (bSec.ily, bSecBsnTwo, bar 62): `ges,2-- fes,2-\tweak padding #0.6 --`. At 300 dpi the tenuto now sits clear of the stem. Fl 2 at 61 (A♭5, D♭6) has the same placement but a visible gap; I left it.

**F3. Bars 1 and 7, harp: the D-string retuning is printed twice in two different wordings. Not applied.**
Bar 1 LH: "tune lowest D string to D♭ (for bar 9)"; bar 7 RH/LH: "(lowest D string tuned to D♭)" beside the pedal diagram. The second is a reminder, but two wordings of one instruction on one page invite the question whether two actions are meant.
Evidence: Gould intro PDF 9-10 (players should not spend rehearsal time on "needless reading difficulties"), PDF 13 ("establishing a convention and adopting a consistent approach").
Fix (aOne.ily, aOneHarpL bar 7): replace the italic line with the shorter `\italic "(D string = D♭)"`, or keep only the bar 1 instruction.

**F4. Bars 52 and 56: dynamic arrivals do not line up across the string section. Not applied.**
At 52 Vln II, Vc and Cb crescendo towards the p of 53 but the violas hold C4 with no hairpin and then stop; at 56 the violins reach mf on beat 1 and the cellos and basses on beat 3 under the same chord. Gould's dynamics chapter is outside the excerpt; the consistency rule of the introduction (PDF 13) applies.
Fix (bSec.ily): bSecVla bar 52 `c'1\<` (or `c'1\>` if the violas are meant to fade out of the texture); bSecVc and bSecCb bar 56 move `\mf` to the first note: `a,2\mf bes,2` and `a,,2\mf bes,,2`.

**F5. Bars 71 and 75, Cl 1·2: the unison A♭4 (Cl 1 dotted quarter, Cl 2 dotted half) prints its two dots stacked vertically (":"). Not applied.**
The noteheads are correctly kept apart with the white one offset right (PDF 65-66), but the two dots sit one above the other after the white head. Gould PDF 68: each dot has its own space and must not suggest another value; the stacked pair suggests a dot for a note in the space above (B♭4). Gould's own rule for dotted unisons (p. 58) is beyond the excerpt. Two options: leave as LilyPond's standard, or write Cl 2 as `aes'4.~ aes'4.` only if the composer accepts the hidden beat 2; I do not recommend the second option. This is minor and I would only note it for the parts.
