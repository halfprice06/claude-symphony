## Findings

### 1. Clarinet 2's crotchet rest at 82 b3 hangs below the stave on top of the pp (applied)

- **Bars:** 82, Cl 1·2 shared staff (page 7, top system).
- **Problem:** Clarinet 1 holds C5 for the whole bar. Clarinet 2 plays A♭4 for a half note and then has `r4`. LilyPond places that crotchet rest entirely below the bottom stave line, and the shared p>pp hairpin ends in a pp just under it. Gould says that when rests in double-stemmed writing move, they move by whole stave-spaces and only as far as needed to avoid the other part (PDF 49-50). A rest outside the stave should sit at the level of that part's surrounding pitches (PDF 51). Clarinet 2's pitches here are A♭4 and F4, inside the stave, and clarinet 1 has no note on beat 3, so nothing forces the rest out. Every other lower-voice crotchet rest in the score sits one space lower, at staff position -4, on the bottom line and half inside the stave: Cl 2 at 77 b3, Bn 2 at 74 b3 and 77 b3. The rest at 82 is a space lower than those, so the clarinet staff does not match the bassoon staff in the same system.
- **Evidence:** page 7 at 220 dpi, Cl 1·2 staff at 82: the rest's top hook is level with the bottom line, and the pp sits half a space under its tail. Bar 77 on page 6 has the rest at the normal position.
- **Fix, sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoClTwo (bar 82); score only, the part is unchanged:**
  ```
  \after 2 \pp aes'2\p\> \tag #'score \once \override Rest.staff-position = #-4 r4 |
  ```
- **Result in /tmp/critic-405/work:** the rest now sits on the bottom line in the same position as Cl 2 at 77 and Bn 2 at 74 and 77, and it is clear of the pp. Pages 1-6 and 8 are pixel-identical to v405 at 60 dpi, and only this rest moves on page 7.
