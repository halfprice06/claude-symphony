## Findings

### 1. Stems are thicker than the stave-lines (applied)
All bars. LilyPond's default stem is 1.3 times the stave-line, and I measured this in the PDF: stave-lines 7-8 px, stems 10 px at 1200 dpi. Gould PDF 27: "Stems should be thinner than the stave-line, but not so thin as to reproduce too faintly." Gould PDF 52: the barline is "conspicuously thicker than a stem", so it stands out in long bars. Now the barline (1.9) is only 1.46 times the stem. Earlier critics (c245, c375) marked the rule as followed without measuring it. Fix (score.ly, `\context { \Score ...}` in `\layout`; the same line in genparts.py's parts `\layout`):
```
\override Stem.thickness = #0.9
```
Measured after the change: stems 7 px, the same as or slightly under the stave-lines. Barlines are now 2.1 times a stem. At A3 and staff size 14 a stem is still about 0.14 mm, so it will not reproduce faintly. Spacing and breaks do not change.

### 2. No stave-space after warning signatures at system ends (applied)
Bars 36 (end of the page 2 system: warning D♭ key signature and 4/4) and 64 (end of the page 5 system: A♭ key signature and 3/4). The warning time signature ends about 0.4 stave-space before the end of the stave lines. Gould PDF 56-57 (Mid-system): "Where a barline comes before the end of the stave, allow a stave-space at the end of the stave". The example shows the warning signature with a stave-space of line after it. LilyPond's default right-edge space is 0.5. Fix (score.ly, Score context):
```
\override TimeSignature.space-alist.right-edge = #'(extra-space . 1.0)
\override KeySignature.space-alist.right-edge = #'(extra-space . 1.0)
\override KeyCancellation.space-alist.right-edge = #'(extra-space . 1.0)
```
The systems keep their bars and breaks.

### 3. Cor anglais slur runs through the double bar into the warning key signature at 36/37 (applied)
Bar 36 b4, C.a. C5 slurred into F5 at 37 (the pickup into theme B). The first half of the broken slur crosses the double barline and ends on top of the first flat of the warning D♭ signature (seen at 800 dpi). Gould PDF 55: "no characters should collide", with at least half a stave-space between symbols. PDF 44 gives the rule for a line that crosses a system break: it runs "as far as the last barline (and not beyond it)". The other broken slurs at system ends (B.cl. 36, strings 64) end clear of the signatures. Fix (trans.ily, transCorAnglais):
```
r2 c''4-\shape #'(((0 . 0) (0 . 0) (-1.5 . 0) (-2.6 . -0.6)) ()) (\p |
```
The slur now ends just before the double bar, and its second half at 37 is unchanged.

### 4. String-name lines end at the end of the note, which Gould marks as incorrect (applied at 51-52 and 62)
Vc "sul G" 51-52 and Vln II "sul A" 62. Both lines are stopped on a spacer `s16` placed just before the barline, so the hook sits at the end of the half note's duration. Gould PDF 44: end the extension line "immediately after the last notehead(s), including duration dots". "Although the practice of some editions, avoid terminating the line at the end of the duration." The printed "incorrect" example is exactly this layout. c305 moved the 62 line off the 63 chord, but its spacer method still ends the line at the end of the duration. Fix (bSec.ily):
```
c1-\tweak bound-details.right.attach-dir #RIGHT -\tweak bound-details.right.padding #0.6 \startTextSpan | d2\< es2\stopTextSpan |
f''2---\tweak bound-details.right.attach-dir #RIGHT -\tweak bound-details.right.padding #0.6 \startTextSpan fes''2--\stopTextSpan
```
The hooks now fall about half a space after the E♭3 and F♭5 noteheads. MIDI is unchanged.
Not applied: Vln I "(sul D)" 1-3. c245 and c305 kept it running to the end of the note because "a string-position bracket governs the sound for its full length". An octave line also governs its note for the note's full sounding length, and Gould still ends it after the notehead. The only exceptions Gould gives are trill lines and repeat bars (PDF 44). So that reason does not hold. The same tweak would end it after the bar-3 notehead, but bars 1-3 are in the protected link, so I leave it to the composer.

### 5. Rehearsal letter L in the strings sits 16 mm left of the winds' L (applied)
Bar 83. In the winds the boxed L and "a tempo" stand over the first musical column. In the strings (`\strMark` on the viola's whole-bar rest, because Vln I rests) they sat at the far left of the system, over the bracket and clef. The X-offset of a whole-bar-rest text is measured from the system's left edge. Gould PDF 9 says the operator must judge the program's placement, and PDF 13 says effective communication comes from "establishing a convention and adopting a consistent approach". MOLA PDF 4 says placement "should be the same throughout the work". Fix (coda.ily, codaVla):
```
<>\strMark "L" \markup \concat { \hspace #1 "a tempo" } R2. |
```
The strings' L is now within about 1 mm of the winds' L. Not changed: at 37 the strings' E is about 4 mm right of the winds' E, because the winds' letter aligns on the new 4/4. This is a small difference and optional.
