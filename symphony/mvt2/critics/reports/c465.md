## Findings

The string notation follows the reading at every point in the table. What remains are three small engraving faults, two at system ends and one in a part.

### 1. Bar 22, Vln II: "espr., sul D" runs into the string group's barline at the end of page 2's first system. Applied.
**Problem.** The text starts at the pickup E flat4 on 22 b3, the last note of the system. Its "D" ends at x = 807.89 pt, on the final barline (807.9 pt). Because the string group's barline is drawn through the gap between the Vln I and Vln II staves, the letter sits on the line. Vln I's copy of the same words was moved left and set in two lines for the same reason (c065, c125); Vln II's copy, added by c438, was not.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 35 (Ex. II-16): the string indication stands at its note and must be read with it; PDF 53: the same for ord. at the point of return. A string name fused with a barline is the kind of misreading that rule is meant to avoid. Gould PDF 55: no characters collide.
**Fix (applied, aOne.ily).** `bes2 es'8(\mp-\tweak X-offset #-2 ^\markup \italic "espr., sul D" f'8 |`. The score.ly Vln II staff has no `\removeWithTag #'part` filter, so the tweak is untagged (a tagged pair printed the text twice in my first test). In the Vln II part bar 22 is mid-line and the shifted text sits clear over the tied B flat3. The "D" now ends 1.2 spaces before the barline.

### 2. Bar 44, Ob 1: "dolce espr." runs into the woodwind barline at the end of page 3's first system. Applied.
**Problem.** Same fault: the entry is the last note of the system and "espr." ends at 807.79 pt, touching the barline that joins the Fl and Ob staves.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 55. This is a wind staff, outside Adler ch. 2; I include it because the scan for Finding 1 found it and the fix is the same.
**Fix (applied, bSec.ily).** `r2 r4 aes'4(\p-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." -\tag #'score -\tweak X-offset #-2.5 ^\markup \italic "dolce espr." -\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "dolce espr." |`. The oboe staff is filtered in score.ly, so the tagged pair prints once in each place. The score copy now ends 8 pt before the barline. The part is unchanged.

### 3. Bars 51-52, cello part: the "sul G" line closes at the line break. Applied.
**Problem.** In the score, 51-52 sit on one system. In the Violoncello part, 51 ends a line. The spanner's override sets `bound-details.right.text` to the closing hook, and LilyPond uses that for the broken end as well. So the part shows "sul G" with an end hook over the single note of 51, and then "sul G" with a second hook at 52. That reads as two one-bar indications, or as sul G ending at 51.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 35, Ex. II-16: the line after "Sul G" shows how far the indication lasts. A closing hook at a line break gives the wrong extent.
**Fix (applied, bSec.ily, Vc 51).** Before `c1`, add `\once \override TextSpanner.bound-details.right-broken.text = ##f \once \override TextSpanner.bound-details.left-broken.text = \markup \italic "(sul G)"`. The part now shows "sul G" running open to the line end, then "(sul G)" with the single hook after E flat3 at 52. The score does not change, since the spanner does not break there. The other two spanners (Vln I 1-4, Vln II 62) do not break in score or parts; the same two overrides would protect them if a later layout breaks them.
