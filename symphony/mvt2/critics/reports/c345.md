## Findings

### 1. Timpani retuning for the scherzo at 92 is in the part only, not in the score (applied)

**Bars:** 92 (timpani staff hidden 87–92).

**Problem.** The timpanist retunes all four drums in the pause (A♭→E, C→G, D♭→B, E♭→E), and the text that says so is tagged `'part` (coda.ily `codaTimp`, c150/c200). The full score prints the clarinets' "muta in A (for the Scherzo)" at 92 but says nothing about the timpani. The conductor decides the length of the pause, and four drum changes take longer than two clarinettists picking up A instruments, so the score should show it.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 291, item 3: "the score must contain every detail for every instrument, including special instructions". PDF 294: the "change to"/"muta in" instruction "should be written above the player's staff". The score already does this for the timpani at 5 ("muta G in A♭, A♭ in C, C in D♭", above the staff) and for the clarinets at 92. Bar 92 is the only muta in the movement that the score leaves out.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-345/work, coda.ily `codaTimp`).** Keep the timpani staff alive in the last system and print the same text above it, score-only; the part's existing text stays as it is:
```
\tag #'score { \set Staff.keepAliveInterfaces = #'(rhythmic-grob-interface multi-measure-rest-interface) } R2.*2 | R2.*3 |
R2.\fermata -\tag #'score -\tweak parent-alignment-X #RIGHT -\tweak self-alignment-X #RIGHT -\tweak extra-offset #'(5 . 0)
  ^\markup \small \italic \right-column { "muta A♭ in E, C in G, D♭ in B, E♭ in E" "(for the Scherzo, in the pause)" }
  -\tag #'part -\tweak self-alignment-X #RIGHT _\markup ... (unchanged) |
```
Page 8 had half a page free, so the extra staff costs nothing: still 8 pages, and the timpani staff sits in its own bracket between Hn 3·4 and the strings. The wording and drum order are the c270-protected text, unchanged; only where it prints changes. This is a printed text at the ending: the composer may tell the scherzo thread, but nothing it relies on changes.

### 2. Player not named at the start of the systems at 7 (horn 1) and 37 (bassoon 1) (applied)

**Bars:** 7 Hn 1·2; 37 Bn 1·2.

**Problem.** At 7 the Hn 1·2 staff starts a new system with the horn theme on a single line and no label. "1. solo" was printed at 6, at the end of the previous system, on page 1's first system, so the system at 7 (the most exposed solo in the movement) shows "Hn. 1·2" with one line and does not say which horn plays it. At 37 the Bn 1·2 staff starts the Poco più mosso system with a single F3 pp; the combiner's "1." was printed at 35, in the previous system. A reader could take both for a2.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 295: in a reduced score "it is imperative that all the instruments must be clearly marked in all systems appearing on a page". Ex. XVIII-3b (PDF 296) labels every player line on each system ("1.Fl.", "1.Clar.", "1.Horn"). c295 applied this rule at six system starts (41, 45, 61, 75, 79, 87); 7 and 37 are the two it did not cover. I checked every other system start (1, 15, 23, 31, 41, 45, 49, 53, 57, 61, 65, 71, 75, 79, 83, 87): each shared staff either has two parts or carries a label.

**Fix (applied; the form c295 used).**
- aOne.ily, `aOneHnOne` 7: `aes4.-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." c'8 es'4~-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \< |`
- bSec.ily, `bSecBsnOne` 37: `f2\pp-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." r2 | R1*7 |`

Both are score-only; parts and MIDI are unchanged.

### 3. Tempo words over the brass on reduced systems and over the timpani (recorded, no change)

Ex. XVIII-1 (PDF 293) repeats "Allegretto" above the woodwinds, brass, percussion (timpani) and strings, and the PDF 294 text asks for a repeat "in the space separating each choir". This score repeats tempo words over the brass only on the four full systems (c215) and never over the timpani. On reduced systems with brass, the words at 53, 65, 78 and 89 are printed over the top staff and Vln I only. I do not recommend a change. Adler's own Mahler pages (Ex. XVIII-3a and b, PDF 296) print "Ganz unmerklich", "etwas zurückhalten" and "Etwas gemächlicher als zuvor" only at the top and at the strings, on the full page and on the reduced page, which is what this score does. The brass staff on a reduced system here is at most four staves below the top (c215's reason), and the timpani are always within three staves of the strings mark. This answers the PDF 294 text with Adler's own example and closes the point.

### 4. Number of drums not stated at the start (optional, not applied)

PDF 292: "The number of drums should be given at the beginning of the score"; Ex. XVIII-1 names the staff "6 Timpani". Here the staff is "Timpani" and the label over bar 1 lists four pitches, so the number can be counted but is not stated. If the composer wants the book's form, change the instrument name in score.ly to `instrumentName = "4 Timpani"` (short name "Timp." unchanged). The longest name ("Contrabassoon") sets the indent, so the layout does not move. Optional because c295 judged the pitch list sufficient and movement I and the scherzo use the same form; change all three together or none.

### 5. Shared clarinet staff at 89 (recorded, no change)

At 89 clarinet 2 enters niente < under clarinet 1's tied pp C5, and the only hairpin printed is below the staff with clarinet 2's down-stem. PDF 294 allows one set of dynamics on a shared staff unless two different sets are needed; clarinet 1 has no new dynamic at 89, so the single hairpin below is correct and the stem direction shows whose it is. Decided at c226; no change.
