## Findings

### 1. Bars 80 b2–81 b2: the chromatic F–F♭–E♭ line is in horn 3 alone at the ff climax (applied in scratch)

**Problem.** At 80 b3 the chord is B♭–D♭–F♭–A♭ (ii-half-diminished seventh, with the F♭ borrowed from minor) before V7 at 81. Its colour note F♭4 is played only by horn 3 at f. Every other tone is doubled several times: A♭ is in Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1, C.a., Vln I, Vln II, violas and Hn 4, and B♭ is in Hn 1·2, Bsn 1·2, Vc, B.cl., Cb and Cbsn. The comment above `aTwoVla` (c042) still says "80 b3 F flat4 (with horn 3)", but since c071 moved the violas onto the theme's third octave they play `bes'4( aes'2) | g'4( es'2)`. That is the same as the cor anglais in unison, and the F♭ has had no string support since then. The comment no longer matches the notes.

**Evidence.** PDF 113 names "the splendid effect of horns doubled by divided violas or 'cellos" as the exception to the rule that strings and brass do not mix in juxtaposition. PDF 108–109 (image): in forte, balance comes from doubling the harmonic parts, not from adding more doublings of one note. PDF 112: parts in octaves reinforce one another, and in a changing succession the orchestrator gets the balance by distribution and grading. Here the one chromatic inner line in the climax has a single instrument, while the theme's B♭4 is already played twice in unison (C.a. + violas).

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-227/work/sections/aTwo.ily, line 48).** The violas divide for one beat and a half. The lower half doubles horn 3 (F4, F♭4 tied, E♭4). The upper half keeps c071's third octave with the cor anglais, so c071's reason (the theme's third octave at the peak) still holds:

### 2. Bar 60: trombone 2 moves with the woodwind line (no change recommended)

PDF 108 says that in a chain of chords the brass should hold the stationary parts and the woodwind should move. At 60, Tbn 2 goes E♭3→G♭3 in half notes. Its G♭3 lands in unison with Bsn 1's and the cellos' G♭3, and the brass shadows the moving line. The alternative, `es1\pp\<` held into F3 at 61, would leave the low brass on a bare A♭–E♭ through the crescendo. The seventh would then come only from the moving voices, and c224 already accepted the root-and-fifth sound only for b1–2 at pp. The trombone's G♭3→F3 at 61 is also a correct resolution of the seventh. Record as follows, no change.

### 3. Bars 79–81: bassoon 1 doubling horns 1·2 at ff (record)

PDF 108 says that in forte the horns overwhelm the bassoons. Bsn 1 adds little to Hn 1·2 here, but it also doubles the cellos in the protected three-colour counter-melody (c104), and removing it would gain nothing audible. Record, no change.
