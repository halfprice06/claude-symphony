## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Violas, bar 74: up-stems and slurs below, in the middle of a down-stem passage (applied)

**Problem.** The violas play six-quaver groups in every bar from 71 to 78, all with down-stems, the beam under the stave and the slurs above. In bar 74 the stems point up, the beam is above the stave (position +3.5), and both slurs drop under the notes; the first slur hangs below the E♭3 under the stave. This is new: c502 changed 74 b1.5-b2 from E♭4-G4 to G3-E♭4. The new group (E♭3 G3 E♭4 | E♭4 B♭3 D♭4) has three notes above and three below the middle line, and LilyPond applied the equal-numbers rule (furthest note E♭3, so up-stems). Nobody chose the flip; it came with the pitch change. The same happens in the viola part (page 28 of the parts book).

**Evidence.** Gould PDF 38 gives the equal-numbers rule, and PDF 39 qualifies it: "The context may determine the direction. For example, notes adjacent to the group in question may take stems in one particular direction", with an example where one group follows its neighbours' direction. Under "visual considerations" (PDF 39): when groups in a passage together span the stave, "maintaining the same stem direction for the whole passage ... allows all slurs ... to remain next to their noteheads". Bars 71-78 are one accompaniment figure spanning the alto stave (positions -3 to +5), so it is exactly this case. c015 and c105 applied the same reasoning to the violas at 37-60.

**Fix (applied, sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoVla, bar 74):**
```lilypond
c'( es' aes') c'( es' aes) | \stemDown es( g es') es'( bes des') \stemNeutral |  % c515 ...
```
After the fix the logger reports the beam at -5.0 → -3.5: it rises with the outside interval E♭3-D♭4 (PDF 36), 1.5 spaces over a six-note group (inside PDF 34's "one or possibly two stave-spaces" for a long group), entirely below the stave, and the shortest stem (E♭3) is exactly 2½ spaces, Gould's minimum outside the stave (PDF 35). The two slurs are above again, as in 71-73 and 75-78. No pitch, rhythm, dynamic or MIDI change.
