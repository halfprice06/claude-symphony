## Findings (ranked)

### F1. Bar 60, Vln I: "poco allargando" touches the up-bow sign (applied)
- Problem: on the Violins I staff the tempo word starts on the right arm of the `\upBow` over the C7 whole note. At 300 dpi the "p" of "poco" touches the V.
- Evidence: Gould PDF 55: "no characters should collide", and nothing should be closer than half a stave-space. PDF 9: a person has to check what the software places. The cause is c128's `extra-offset #'(1.8 . -0.7)`, which lowers the word 0.7 space to keep it below the timpani p. That puts it on the same level as the bow mark.
- Fix (global.ily, bar 60): `\once \override Staff.MetronomeMark.extra-offset = #'(3.0 . -0.7)` (was `1.8`). The word moves 1.2 spaces to the right and keeps c128's height, so it stays clear of the timpani p. The copy above the flutes moves with it and still sits over beat 1's note.

### F2. Bars 59–60, Fl 1·2 and Ob 1·2: second player's whole rest collides with player 1's stems (applied)
- Problem: flute 1 and oboe 1 are forced to `\stemDown` at 57–60 (c015). In 59–60 the part-combiner starts printing flute 2's and oboe 2's whole-bar rests, which it does not print in 57–58. At 59 the oboe 2 rest sits on the bottom line, centred in the bar, and oboe 1's D♭5 quarter on beat 3 has its down-stem running into it. The flute staff is the same. In 60 the rests are drawn under the whole notes, although the "1." label at 57 already says that player 2 is silent.
- Evidence: Gould PDF 50: "A rest moves further from the centre of the stave or to outside the stave should parts otherwise collide". PDF 65: in double-stemmed writing the upper part has up-stems, which is not the case here. PDF 51: parts share a rest wherever possible.
- Fix (bSec.ily, `bSecFluteTwo` and `bSecOboeTwo`): `\tag #'score { s1*2 }` in place of `\tag #'score { R1*2 }`. This is the method c115 used for flute 2 at 63. The parts keep their `R1*2` and the Ob. 1 cue, so the parts book is unchanged. Players 1 keep their c015 down-stems, and 59–60 now read as a continuation of the "1." solo, as 57–58 do.

### F3. Bar 48, Vla: the pp sits on the first eighth's tenuto-staccato (applied)
- Problem: the off-beat eighths at 48 have `-_` below the notehead. The dynamic line that runs from the crescendo at 46 b3 through `fp>` at 47 to the `pp` at 48 is set at the default distance from the staff, so the top of the pp touches the staccato dot.
- Evidence: Gould PDF 55 (no collisions, at least half a space apart). PDF 53–54: the accompaniment must read cleanly at a glance, and here the dynamic hides the articulation.
- Fix (bSec.ily, `bSecVla`, bar 46): `f2 \once \override Staff.DynamicLineSpanner.staff-padding = #3 bes2\< | b1\fp\> |`. The override has to sit at 46 b3, because that is where the dynamic line starts; putting it at 47 has no effect. The pp, the fp and the hairpin all move down about one space, and the line stays straight.

### F4. Bar 63, Tbn 1: the slur end runs into the accent on C4 (applied)
- Problem: the quotation's pickup slur E3→C4 (c148) ends exactly on the `>` above the dotted quarter, so the accent and the slur tip overlap.
- Evidence: Gould PDF 55 (symbols must not collide). PDF 33–37 do not cover slurs, but the same rule applies to every symbol. Bar 63 is the movement's main brass statement (protected), and at ff the marcato accent has to be seen.
- Fix (bSec.ily, `bSecTbnOne`): `r8 \shape #'((0 . 0) (0 . 0) (0 . 0) (-0.7 . -0.9)) Slur e8(\ff^\markup \italic "marcato" … c'4.->)`. The slur now ends at the left of the C4 notehead, under the accent. Pitches, the slur and the accent are unchanged (c148's articulation is kept). I first tried `-\tweak avoid-slur #'inside` on the accent, and it changed nothing.

### F5. Bar 1, Hn 3·4: horn 4 has no rest on beat 1 (not applied; recorded)
- Problem: horn 3 holds C4 on beat 1 (`c'2\repeatTie`) while horn 4 enters on beat 2. The combiner treats beat 1 as horn 3 alone, so horn 4's `r4` is not printed.
- Evidence: Gould PDF 50: "Two parts on a stave with different durations require their own sets of rests."
- Tested: `\partCombineApart` in bar 1 brings the rest back, but it collides with horn 4's ppp and moves horn 3's ppp above the staff, which is worse by PDF 55. The "4." label at beat 2 (c045) and horn 4's own part make the entry clear. I recommend no change. Do not raise again unless someone finds a version that does not collide.

### F6. Bar 78, Cl 1·2: the minim rest is close to the f (no change)
- Cl 2's half rest at 78 b1–2 is placed on a ledger line below the stave, as PDF 51 requires, but it is about 0.4 space above the shared f. That is just under PDF 55's half-space minimum, and it is legible. c144/c145 settled this bar, so I recommend no change.
