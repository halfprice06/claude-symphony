## Findings

### 1. Flute 2's whole-bar rest at 82 hangs below the stave and is cut by flute 1's hairpin (applied)

- **Bars:** 82, Fl 1·2 shared staff (page 7, top system).
- **Problem:** In 82, flute 1 holds A♭5 for the whole bar, p>pp. Flute 2's `R2.` prints as a semibreve rest. It hangs from the bottom stave line, outside the stave with no ledger line, and the p>pp hairpin runs through it. Gould says rests stay centred in the stave (PDF 49). He says parts share a rest wherever possible (PDF 51). He also says a semibreve rest outside the stave sits on a ledger line, far enough out to clear the other part (PDF 51). This rest does none of these. It also tells the reader that flute 2 has a written rest under a bar flute 1 plays alone. On a shared staff that information belongs only in the part.
- **Evidence:** v394 printed no rest here. There flute 2 had `R2.*2` at 81-82, and the part-combiner showed flute 1 alone. c394 gave flute 2 notes at 80 b3-81 b1 (`fes'''4 r2`), and after that the part-combiner prints flute 2's whole-bar rest in 82. This is the same defect c265 removed from oboe 2 at 82: "oboe 2's whole-bar rest hung below the stave with no ledger line... one centred shared rest instead". c265's do-not-touch list protects these shared-rest fixes. So c394 brought the defect back in the flutes.
- **Fix (as c265 did for oboe 2), sections/aTwo.ily:**
  ```
  aTwoFluteTwo = { R2.*8 | f'''2.~\ff | f'''2 fes'''4 \breathe | fes'''4-\tag #'part \> r2-\tag #'part \! | \tag #'score s2. \tag #'part R2. | }
  ```
- **Result in /tmp/critic-395/work:** bar 82 now shows flute 1's dotted half and its hairpin with no rest, as in v394 and as the oboe staff below it. The flute 2 part keeps its `R2.`. c394's notes and c394's part-only hairpins at 81 are unchanged.
