## Findings

### 1. Bars 63–64: the trombone quotation stays ff to its last note while everything around it fades (applied)
Problem: Tbn 1 enters ff, marcato, and no later dynamic follows. In bar 64 the strings have already reached p, the bass trombone diminuendos f → p, the allargando slows to ♩=40, and bar 65 starts pp. So the last 1½ beats of 64 are one trombone at ff alone, cut off against a pp entry: the seam is exposed (reading 7 and 9; PDF 190, 192). In the MIDI the same thing happens.
Evidence: Adler PDF 503 (Ex. XV-35) writes the long notes after a sforzando as "sf > pp"; Dvořák's own model for this passage steps the quotation down from ff to f and writes the brass ends `->\f\>`.
Protected-passage note: HISTORY protects the quotation for cyclic reasons (single statement, ff, low brass, A minor). A hairpin after its last accent changes none of these.
Fix (bSec.ily, `bSecTbnOne`): `a4 gis8 a8 f4.->\> e8\mf |`

### 2. Bar 82: the tutti p chord covers the harp's harmonic pickup into the coda (applied)
Problem: at 82 about twenty parts hold the tonic at p to the barline. The harp's octave harmonics E♭–F enter on beat 3 at the same p, and harmonics are the weakest sound in the orchestra. Then everything stops at once and the strings re-attack ppp at 83. Adler's dovetail rule (PDF 192) and the subito-piano rule (PDF 190: the new layer must already be audible when the old one leaves) both point the same way: thin the old layer before the new colour enters.
Fix (aTwo.ily, every part that sounds in bar 82 except the harp): `\after 2 \pp aes''2.\p\>` and the same for each part's note (for example `\after 2 \pp c'2.)\p\>` in horns 1 and 2, identical in both so the part-combined dynamics stay equal). Contrabassoon: `aes,,2\p\> r4\! |`. Strings: Vla/Vc/Cb then go to the ppp already printed at 83.

### 3. Bars 61–62: the climax has no sustaining direction and the augmented theme has no articulation (applied)
Problem: the arrival is a fermata-like broadening of theme B's head (A♭–D♭–F–F♭ in half notes) in Fl a2, Ob 2, Tpt 1 and Vln II, with only "Largamente" and ff. Franck's climax (PDF 38, Ex. XI-20) prints "f molto sostenuto" in every string part, and that direction is what makes players hold the full value at full weight instead of tapering each half note. Without it, half notes at ff in the brass will decay.
Fix (bSec.ily): tenuti on the theme notes, `aes''2--(\ff des'''2-- | f'''2-- fes'''2--)` (Fl 1 and Fl 2), `aes'2--\ff des''2-- | des''2-- fes''2--` (Ob 2), `aes'2--\ff des''2-- | f''2-- fes''2--` (Tpt 1 and Vln II); text "molto sostenuto" in the five string parts (combined with the existing "non div." in Vln I and Vla).

### 4. Bars 23–24 and 71–72: the violins' theme A has no swell on its held E♭ (applied)
Problem: when horn 1 plays the theme (7–8) the held E♭ gets `\<` and the motto tail `\>`. When the violins take it at 23 and in A′ at 71 the same bars have no hairpin; only the second phrase (27–28, 75–78) is shaped. Berlioz's solo (PDF 188, Ex. XV-33) and Franck's string melody (PDF 38) both mark the peak of each phrase with < >. At 71 the theme is the reprise, and leaving the first phrase flat makes it sound plainer than the horn's first statement.
Fix (aOne.ily Vln I and Vln II; aTwo.ily Vln I and Vln II): `aes'4. c''8) es''4~\< | es''4 d''4(\> des''4) | c''4.(\! bes'8 aes'4 |` (and the octave above for Vln I at 71).

### 5. Bar 47: the oboe's quotation has no expressive word (applied)
Problem: every other solo in the movement has one ("solo, dolce espr." Hn 1 at 6; "solo, espr." BCl at 31; "dolce cantabile" Fl at 37; "espr." Vln I at 70). The oboe's quotation of movement I's second theme, the most exposed solo in the B section, has only "1." and p. Adler PDF 188: the solo is printed "mf espress."
Fix (bSec.ily, `bSecOboeOne`): `cis'4(\p^\markup \bold "1."^\markup \italic "espr."`. The notes of the quotation are unchanged.

### 6. Bar 63: the sforzando and key change rest on string tremolo alone (not applied; composer's choice)
Problem: the ff tutti stops at the barline of 62 and the A-minor bar is attacked only by string tremolo sf (and the bass trombone f). Adler PDF 502–503: "Often an accent is not sufficient to emphasize sforzando"; combine a short sf note in another group (oboes, trumpets, pizzicato) with the long notes. A short chord on 63 b1 would also make the trombone's eighth-note pickup sound like an answer, as Dvořák's brass do.
Suggested fix: Ob 1·2 and Cl 1·2 `<a' e''>8-.\sf r8 r4 r2` / `<c' a'>8-.\sf r8 r4 r2` at 63 (both players of each pair with the same sf), clear of the trombone's register. I did not apply it because it adds sound next to a protected quotation; the composer should decide.
