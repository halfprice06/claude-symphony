## Findings

### 1. Bars 8, 24, 72: the swell peaks on the resolution, not on the suspension (applied)

**Problem.** Theme A holds E♭ over the bar line (7-8, 23-24, 71-72). On the downbeat of 8/24/72 the bass is F (harp/B.cl. at 8, double basses at 24 and 72), so the held E♭ is a prepared 7th over vi7 that resolves down by step to D over B♭ on beat 2. A&S describe this as a suspension with moving bass (PDF 353, Ex. 21-14) and call the 7th of a seventh chord a frequent result of suspension (PDF 362). The score writes `es'4~\< | es'4 d'4\> des'4`. The crescendo runs through the dissonant downbeat, and the diminuendo starts only on the D. So the loudest point is the resolution, which reverses A&S's rule: "dissonant suspensions receive something of an accent and their resolutions are softer" (PDF 348), and the resolution "should sound like a relaxation, a decrease in intensity" (PDF 348).

**Evidence.** The score already does this the other way at 32-33 (B.cl. `es'2.~\< | es'4\mf\> d'4`) and 67-68 (Hn 1 `es'2.(\p\< | d'4\mp\>`). There the peak sits on the downbeat. The theme, which comes back three times, is the one place where the shape is reversed.

**Fix (applied).** Start the diminuendo on the tied note:
- aOne.ily, aOneHnOne bar 8: `es'4~\< | es'4\> d'4 des'4 | c'4.\! ...`
- aOne.ily, aOneVlnOne and aOneVlnTwo bar 24: `es''4~\< | es''4\> d''4( des''4) |`
- aTwo.ily, aTwoVlnOne bar 72: `es'''4~\< | es'''4\> d'''4( des'''4) |`; aTwoVlnTwo bar 72: `es''4~\< | es''4\> d''4( des''4) |`

The notes and slurs are unchanged. This keeps c028's "violin swells on held E♭ at 23-24, 71-72" and only moves the top of the swell to the barline.

### 2. Bars 80-81: the 9-8 suspension in horn 3 has no expressive marking (applied)

**Problem.** Horn 3's F♭4, prepared at 80 b3 and held as a minor 9th over E♭ at 81 b1, resolving to E♭4 on b2, is the dissonance that makes the V♭9 (c082). It is the only inner-voice dissonant suspension in A′. It is marked f and sits inside a tutti ff, and nothing asks for it to be brought out. A&S: suspensions "bring into prominence the voice in which they occur ... Recognizing and bringing out suspensions is of particular importance to the performer" (PDF 348). The resolution tone sounds only in the bass group (B.cl., Bsn 2, Cb, timpani), which is the 9-8 case A&S allow (PDF 352, Ex. 21-12d), so the dissonance is clean and is worth bringing out.

**Fix (applied).** Swell into the suspension. The diminuendo already starts on it.
- aTwoHnThree: `f'2.\f | f'2 fes'4~\< | \once \override Staff.Hairpin.circled-tip = ##t fes'4\> es'2 |`
- aTwoHnFour (shared staff, same dynamics): `aes2.\f | aes2~ aes4\< | ... g2.\> |`

The pitches of the protected V♭9 and hemiola at 80-81 are untouched. The hairpin prints once below the shared staff.

### 3. Bar 42: the 4-3 over D♭ is inside a diminuendo that starts three beats earlier (applied)

**Problem.** At 42 the cor anglais' G♭4 is the 7th of A♭7 on b3. On b4 it is held as a 4th over D♭ and resolves to F on b4.5. This is A&S's suspension with dissonant preparation (PDF 357-358, Ex. 21-23a: "the 7th of V7 is frequently suspended to form a 4-3 over I"). Flute 1 holds E♭5 over the same bass, a 9th that resolves up to F5 a sixth above the cor anglais (PDF 358). The score starts `\>` on 42 b1 in both instruments, so the bar's one dissonant downbeat-to-offbeat figure arrives already fading. A&S PDF 348 asks for the opposite (accent on the suspension, softer resolution).

**Fix (applied).** Keep mp through the bar and start the diminuendo on the suspension. The accompaniment (violas, cellos) keeps its own diminuendo from b1, so the melody pair stands out a little at that point.
- bSecCorAnglais bar 42: `aes'4\mp bes'8 aes'8 ges'4~ ges'8\> f'8 |`
- bSecFluteOne bar 42: `f''4\mp ges''8 f''8 es''4~ es''8\> f''8 |`

This leaves the c082 harmony at 42 as it is. Bar 58 has the same figure inside the 57-60 crescendo. I left it alone: Mozart's 7-6 chain (PDF 355, Ex. 21-17) also lets a crescendo run through suspensions.

### Considered and left alone

- The B♭ at 76 b2 has no local peak, unlike 12 (mp) and 28 (mf). It lies inside the 75-78 crescendo to the f of the deceptive cadence, and A&S PDF 363 (rhythmic weight from a long, high note) is already met by the register. No change.
- Pedal points 31-36, 65-70 and 83-92 all match PDF 367-368. Their dynamics (pp pedals under a p/mp solo, timpani ppp in the retransition) suit their function.
