## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 74 b3-75: the violins' new level falls on the downbeat inside the slur, not on the pickup that starts the phrase (applied)

**Problem.** In A′ the second half of the theme starts with the pickup E♭4-F4 at 74 b3, slurred into the A♭4 at 75. The violins played 71-74 at p (from the p pickup at 70), and the new level mp was printed on 75 b1: `g''2) es'8( f'8 | aes'4.\mp\< c''8)` (Vln I; Vln II the same an octave apart before 74). So the pickup is played at the old level and the step to mp comes on the second note of a slurred group. Every other phrase start in the movement puts its level on the pickup: Hn 1 p at 6 b3, Vln I/II mp at 22 b3 (the same pickup E♭4-F4 into the same theme bar), C.a. p at 36 b3, Ob 1 p at 44 b4, violins p at 52 b4 and 70 b3.

**Evidence.** Sebesky Ex. 269 (PDF 205, image): when the intro is restated at a higher level (pp, then mp) the new mark is under the first note of the restatement, in every part. Ex. 2A (PDF 18, image): each lead entry takes its dynamic and word at its first note. Bar 22 b3 in this score already does this for the same figure.

**Fix (applied, sections/aTwo.ily).** Vln I: `g''2) es'8(\mp f'8 |` and `aes'4.\< c''8)`. Vln II: `g'2) es'8(\mp f'8 |` and `aes'4.\<^\markup \italic "ord." c''8)`. No pitch, rhythm, slur or other dynamic changes. The crescendo still starts at 75 and reaches f at 78. The pickup's MIDI velocity rises from p to mp, which matches the print. The Vln I register plan 74-78 (c299, c309, c341) is untouched.

### 2. Bars 22 b3 and 70 b3: Vln II plays the theme with Vln I but has no "espr." (applied)

**Problem.** At 22 b3 the violins enter the theme in unison (protected unison 23-30). Vln I reads "gli altri con sord., espr., sul D"; Vln II reads only "sul D". At 70 b3 the theme returns in octaves; Vln I reads "espr.", Vln II "sul D". The two sections play one line, and their parts carry different expression words. At 52 b4, the other place where both violin sections take a theme together, c158 gave both sections the same word ("cantabile").

**Evidence.** Sebesky Ex. 281 (PDF 214-215, image): all parts playing one figure carry the same marks. Ex. 2A (PDF 18): the three flutes on one gesture carry identical hairpins and one word. The violas' "espr." at 71 was removed by c058 because they are background (PDF 207). Vln II here is not background; it carries the theme.

**Fix (applied).** sections/aOne.ily, aOneVlnTwo bar 22: `es'8(\mp^\markup \italic "espr., sul D" f'8 |`. sections/retr.ily, retrVlnTwo bar 70: `es'8(\p^\markup \italic "espr., sul D" f'8 |`. Text only. At 140 dpi both fit above the staff without collision, and the systems and page breaks are unchanged.
