## Findings

### 1. Bar 61–62: trombone 2, bass trombone and tuba arrive at mf, two degrees under the climax (applied)
Problem: at 60 the low brass enters pp < under the poco allargando, which is the crescendo chord Rimsky describes (PDF 130). It then arrives at 61 on mf, while the woodwinds and strings are ff, the horn pairs and timpani f. The trombones end the crescendo below the horns that double them.
Evidence: Rimsky marks horns one degree stronger than the trombones only when they are single (PDF 41: "if the trumpets and trombones play pp, the horns should be marked p"; PDF 103: the extra degree is the arrangement used "when it is impossible for the horns to proceed in pairs"). Since c194 every horn part at 61–62 is a pair (Hn 1·2 a2, Hn 3·4 a2), so the pairs and the trombones should carry the same mark. In hard cases the woodwind is marked one degree above the brass (PDF 112), and the appendix chords 2–4 (PDF 505) do exactly that: winds ff/fff, all brass one degree lower. In each crescendo example cited on PDF 130 the trombones enter last and soft and reach the woodwinds' mark or one below the strings (No. 257, PDF 420: Tbn "p cresc." → f with the tutti f; No. 261, PDF 428: Tbn/Tuba pp cresc. → f, strings ff; No. 263, PDF 430: brass p cresc. → sf). None leaves the trombones two degrees under the woodwind. Bar 60 follows the rule (Hn 4 p, single, over pp trombones); bar 61 does not.
Earlier reasons answered: c076 set mf "with horns f (PDF 41)" when the horns at 61 were not all paired; c194 paired them and cited PDF 103 for exactly this point, so c076's reason no longer applies. c060's cyclic reserve is still kept: trumpets stay tacet, nothing is ff, and the chord lasts two tenuto bars under ff strings and woodwinds, so the finale still has the first forte D♭ and the first full-brass chord with trumpets. Tbn 1 still rests until the quotation at 63.
Fix (bSec.ily): `bSecTbnTwo` 61 `f2--\f aes2--` (was `\mf`); `bSecBassTbn` 61 `des1--\f`; `bSecTuba` 61 `des,1--\f`. Tbn 1 rests on the shared staff, so the Tbn 1·2 staff needs no second dynamic.

### 2. Bar 64: the B section ends on the trombone at mf (applied)
Problem: Tbn 1's quotation ends `f4.->\> e8\mf`. At 64 the strings are already p and falling to the pp of 65, and the bass trombone is p. The last sound of the B section is one trombone eighth at mf over pp strings, so the brass is the last group to fade and the section ends at a middle dynamic.
Evidence: diminuendo removes the brass first, then woodwind, then strings (PDF 130; No. 259, PDF 424–425: the trumpets go "smorz." a bar before the clarinets, the strings last). "It is not good to commence or finish any piece of music either mf or mp" (PDF 136). c028, which added the hairpin, wanted the quotation to step down from ff as Dvořák's does; p completes that diminuendo and leaves the accented F3 and the ff entry untouched.
Protected-passage note: the quotation (63–64) and the releases 63–67 are protected for the single ff statement, its pitches and rhythm, and the staged removal of groups (c213). None of these changes; only the target of the existing hairpin does. This touches a quotation, so the composer should tell the other threads (dynamic only, no pitch or rhythm change).
Fix (bSec.ily, `bSecTbnOne`): `a4 gis8( a8) f4.->\> e8\p |` (was `\mf`).

### 3. Bars 31–34: the bass clarinet solo re-enters mp and peaks mf (applied)
Problem: the bass clarinet has rested since 14 (16 bars, about 55 s). It starts the transition's solo at mp, grows to mf at 33 and falls to p at 34, over the cor anglais and bassoon at pp.
Evidence: Rimsky calls mp and mf re-entries "colourless and common-place" and says the remark "is capable of wider application" (PDF 136); the transition is the start of a new section after the A section's pp close at 30. The accompaniment only needs to be of inferior dynamic power to the solo (PDF 133), which one degree gives. The solo is in the chalumeau (c140), which carries at p.
Fix (trans.ily, `transBassCl`): `r4 es,8(\p^\markup \italic "solo, espr." f,8 aes,8 c8 | es2.~\< | es4\mp\> d4 des4 | c2\p bes,4) \breathe |` (was `\mp`, `\mf`). The rest of the line is unchanged.

### 4. Bar 78 b3: Hn 1·2 a2 enter mf< (checked, no change)

### 6. Bars 53 and 75: horns join early in the builds (checked, no change)
