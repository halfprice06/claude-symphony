## Findings

### 1. Clarinets: "muta in A (for the Scherzo)" at bar 92 (requested by the scherzo thread; applied)

Bars: Cl 1 85–90 b2, Cl 2 89–92.

Problem: the scherzo is the only movement with clarinets in A. Movement II gives the players no instruction to change. The task brief assumes Cl 2 stops at 92; in fact Clarinet 2 plays until the last note (E♭4 89–92, morendo, fermata). Clarinet 1 stops on 90 b2 and rests 90 b3–92.

When the change can be made: Clarinet 1 could change from 90 b3, but changing during the ppp morendo would add key and case noise to the quietest bars of the movement. Clarinet 2 cannot change before the last note. Both players can change during the pause between movements: the movement is not attacca, and the scherzo's clarinets have six more bars before their entry at bar 7 (fermata on 2, then the horn call), about 7 seconds. That is enough to pick up the A clarinet, but a cold instrument will be flat for the pp E5 at the scherzo's bar 7, so I added "in the pause" to the text. The scherzo uses the same placement for its own muta.

Evidence: Gould PDF 51 (where parts share a stave, mark things once wherever possible: one text on the shared staff); the timpani muta at 92 already uses this form (c150, c200; MOLA PDF 5: a change is marked at the end of the previous passage).

Fix (coda.ily), applied:
```
codaClOne = { ... c''2.~ | c''2)\> r4\! | R2. | \once \override MultiMeasureRestText.parent-alignment-X = #RIGHT \once \override MultiMeasureRestText.self-alignment-X = #RIGHT \once \override MultiMeasureRestText.extra-offset = #'(5 . 0) R2.\fermata ^\markup \small \italic \right-column { "muta in A" "(for the Scherzo)" } | }
codaClTwo = { ... es'2.~\>^\markup \italic "morendo" | es'2.\ppp\fermata \tag #'part { \textEndMark \markup \small \italic \right-column { "muta in A" "(for the Scherzo, in the pause)" } } | }
```
Score: one text above the Cl 1·2 staff at 92, inside the bar and clear of the barline and fermatas (the extra-offset stops "(for the Scherzo" from crossing the 91|92 barline, which it did when right-aligned on the rest's centre: Gould PDF 43, never cut through other symbols). Cl 1 part: the same text over its last rest. Cl 2 part: `\textEndMark` right-aligned to the final barline, so it reads as applying after the last note, not over 89–91 as a beat-3 markup did in my first attempt. The bass clarinet stays in B♭ (the scherzo's is in B♭) and needs nothing. **Tell the scherzo thread**: done as asked, Cl 1 at 92 over its rest, Cl 2 after its last note; no pitch or ending change. Update coherence/mvt2.md ("Not attacca" section, bar 92 row).

### 2. Ob 1·2 at 82: oboe 2's whole-bar rest hangs outside the stave, and oboe 1's rest is pushed up (applied)

Bar 82. Problem: oboe 2 rests the whole bar, while oboe 1 plays A♭4 (minim) and then rests on b3. The combiner printed oboe 2's semibreve rest hanging from the bottom line below the stave with no ledger line, inside the diminuendo hairpin. It also moved oboe 1's crotchet rest up two stave-spaces, so the stave showed two rests where one would do, one of them outside the stave.

Evidence: Gould PDF 49 (rests stay centred unless a beam or a second part forces them out), PDF 51 ("parts share a rest wherever possible"; a semibreve rest outside the stave "is placed on a ledger line"), example at PDF 51 top. The probe found Ob 1's rest at +2 spaces, the only raised rest in the score that has no second part on that beat. The same fix was accepted for the flutes at 59 and 63 (c115, c155).

Fix (aTwo.ily), applied:
```
aTwoOboeTwo = { R2.*8 | f''2.~\ff | f''2 fes''4 \breathe | fes''4\> r2\! | \tag #'score s2. \tag #'part R2. | }
aTwoOboeOne: ... aes'2)\p\> \tweak staff-position #0 r4\! |
```
Score: one centred crotchet rest on 82 b3. The Ob 2 part is unchanged (one-bar rest at 82). MIDI unchanged.
