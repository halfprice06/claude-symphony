## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bar 63: the bass trombone's CC11 ramp fades trombone 1's ff quotation in the render (applied, MIDI only)

**Problem.** c300 added a MIDI-only CC11 ramp (0.85, 0.72, 0.6 on beats 2–4) to the strings and the bass trombone at 63, so their sf > p is heard under the quotation. The bass trombone and trombones 1·2 both use the GM program "trombone", and fixmidi.py puts them on one channel (6). The bass trombone's ramp therefore applies to trombone 1 as well.

In v368's score.midi:
- Tbn 1's accented C4 (velocity 87) is reduced to 108/127 and then to 92/127 while it sounds.
- The falling B3–B♭3–A3, the part of movement I's P head that c240's slur and portato were written to bring out, plays at 92/127 and then 76/127 (0.6).
- The channel returns to 127 only at 64.

So the only ff statement of movement I's first theme becomes softer as it goes, which is the opposite of what c300 intended. My channel scan finds no other CC11 event that acts on a part sounding on the same channel.

**Evidence.**
- Casanelles PDF 124: CCs "are disassociated from the notes. Their values affect the whole instrument".
- PDF 127: "polyphonic dynamic variation (having several notes with different dynamic shapes) would require two or more MIDI 1.0 instruments". The bass trombone's diminuendo and trombone 1's ff marcato are two different shapes on one MIDI instrument.
- PDF 133–134: the mockup exists to catch mistakes that are easy to miss.
- c228 already warned about shared channels: per-staff fades are unsafe where partners differ.

**Fix (applied, `sections/bSec.ily`, bassTbn).** Replace
`<< a,1\sf\> \tag #'score { s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.85 s4 ... #1.0 } >> |`
with
`a,1\sf\> |`

The strings keep c300's ramp (each string staff that fades has its own channel, and Vln I and II fade together). The bass trombone's A2 now holds at velocity 62 under Tbn 1's 87. Tbn 1 keeps its written level throughout 63. Nothing printed changes. The quotation (protected) is restored to its printed level; it is not altered.

### 2. Bars 32–33: the render reverses the bass clarinet's crest on the motto's E♭ (applied, MIDI only)

**Problem.** In the transition solo (movement I's motto at its own pitch, c140), the printed shape is p at 31, a crescendo across the tied E♭3 to mf on 33 b1, then a diminuendo through D–D♭–C to mp. A held note has one velocity, so score.midi played the E♭ at p (velocity 37) for its whole 1¼ bars, and the D on 33 b2 at mf (49). The listener hears the crest on the D, which is the reverse of the print.

c338 fixed the same problem for theme A's tied E♭ at 8, 24 and 72 with hidden levels, but this statement was missed. I checked the horn's tail at 67–68 as well. There the render (E♭ 44, D 50) matches the print, because the printed mp peak is on the D, the major seventh over the E♭ pedal. I left it unchanged.

**Evidence.**
- PDF 124: one velocity per Note On.
- PDF 142 and Fig. 4.5: the level of a sounding note is set by CC11, because the note message "is not designed to affect a note that is already sounding".
- PDF 130 and Fig. 4.4: a crescendo is a continuous, shaped rise.
- c338 (A&S PDF 348) sets the precedent within this score.

**Fix (applied, `sections/trans.ily`, transBassCl).**
```
| << es2.~-\tweak stencil ##f \mf \< \tag #'score { \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.7 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.85 s2 } >> |
  \set Staff.midiExpression = #1.0 es4\mf\> d4 des4 |
```
The clarinets, which share the bass clarinet's channel, rest at 31–36.

Result in score.midi: the E♭ is attacked at velocity 51 under CC11 89, rises to 108 on b2 and to 127 on 33 b1. The D, D♭ and C then play at 49, 46 and 44.

The printed marks and the 8-page layout are unchanged. The only visible effect is a slight change in horizontal spacing inside the 31–36 system (page 2, third system), with the same breaks. The woodwind choir 31–36 is protected for its pitches and register, and those are untouched.

### 3. Bars 3 and 66: the strings' niente releases cut off at full level in the render (applied, MIDI only)

**Problem.** At 3, every string section fades to niente on a note tied from 1–2. At 66, Vln II, the violas and the cellos fade on notes tied from 65 (c278's ties). The render plays these notes at their pp attack level to the end and then stops dead. These are the movement's first sound after movement I's chord and the cut-back after the climax. c228 proposed this per-staff fade but did not apply it.

**Evidence.**
- PDF 142 and Fig. 4.5: the release is its own part of the sound, and the change of level inside a held note needs CC11.
- PDF 134: the mockup should be close to the performance "in every aspect".

**Fix (applied).**
- `sections/intro.ily`: Vln I, Vln II, violas, cellos and basses at 3 become `<< note\> \tag #'score { \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.85 s2 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.6 s4 } >> | \set Staff.midiExpression = #1.0 R2.\! ...`. The b3 step falls on a column the timpani already have (`aes,2:32 ... aes,8`), so the page is identical.
- `sections/retr.ily`: Vln II, violas and cellos take `\set Staff.midiExpression = #0.75` before the note at 66 and `#1.0` at 67, on barlines only. As c300 found, there is no inner column at 66, and spacers there change the spacing. Vln I rests 65–69, so the shared violin channel fades only Vln II. The basses keep c300's own steps.

Nothing printed changes. The 1–6 link and the retransition 65–70 are protected, and their notes, marks and timing are untouched.
