## Findings

### 1. Bar 73 b3: Vln II arrives on clarinet 2's held A♭4 (applied)
**Problem.** At 73 the theme's lower octave (Vln II) moves `c''4. bes'8 aes'4`. Clarinet 2 has held `aes'2.` since the downbeat, so the melody's goal note on beat 3 is already sounding in a wind colour. This is the same fault c247 removed at 71 and 75. c247 left 73 because E♭4 on beat 1 made compound fifths with the bass (B♭4/E♭2 → E♭4/A♭1).
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 135: when foreground and background are close in colour, keep the background off the melody's register. PDF 144 rule 3: separate theme and countertheme. The scan finds this as the only remaining case where a wind holds a pitch before the theme arrives on it in 71–78.
**Fix (answers c247's reason).** Keep A♭4 on beats 1–2 and move only beat 3 to E♭4. E♭4 is the seventh of the vi7 that c060 placed on 73 b3 (the viola already passes through it), and it falls by step to the D♭4 at 74 (c073). A♭4/A♭1 → E♭4/F1 is not a parallel. c126's slur is unchanged.
```
aTwoClTwo (bar 73):  aes'2.   →   aes'2 es'4
```

### 2. Bar 75 b2: the viola figure strikes the theme's only octave (applied; protected figure, reason answered)
**Problem.** At 75 both violin sections are on A♭4 (c299 put Vln I at pitch for 74 b3–76). The top of the viola figure, A♭4 on beat 2, therefore strikes the melody's only sounding octave in the same string colour while the melody holds it. c177 kept the figure because it "pulses on Vln II's held note" (Adler PDF 262–264, *La Mer*). That was before c299. At that time Vln II carried a lower doubling of the theme and Vln I sounded the lead an octave higher.
**Evidence.** Adler PDF 135 (principle 8). The *La Mer* passage c177 cites (PDF 262) is the oboe pulsing the flute's tune as a doubling in a second colour. It is not a same-colour accompaniment striking the lead. Ex. V-7 (PDF 132–135): Brahms puts the middle-register background below the foreground.
**Fix.** Change one eighth. The figure, its slurs and its rhythm stay the same, and the viola does not double the harp's A♭3–E♭4 for longer than that one beat.
```
aTwoVla (bar 75):  c'(\p\< es' aes') es'( aes f')   →   c'(\p\< es' aes) es'( aes f')
```
**Result.** The edit adds no I6 third (C) on b2 (the c207/c247 rule) and leaves the counter-melody's E♭3 alone.

### Recorded, no change

**R1. Viola figure at 71, 74, 78 b2** (A♭4, G4, A♭4 on Vln II's held note). Here Vln I still carries the lead an octave higher, so c177's reading still applies. If the composer wants principle 8 followed throughout, the same one-eighth change works: 71 `c'8( es' aes) es'( c' aes)`, 78 `aes( fes' aes) ...`; 74 needs `des'( es' g)`. This is untested and I do not recommend it, because the figure is protected and the effect is light.

**R2. B-section top register (PDF 129).** Vln I reaches A♭6 at 58 in the build, and the climax at 61–62 has the same A♭6 (held by Fl 1 and the upper Vln I). The climax is still marked by its length, ff and the brass. Changing 57–58 would break the protected unison-to-octave plan (23, 53, 57, 71), and B♭6 stays reserved for 79. No change.

**R3. Tremolo at 63–64 (PDF 154).** Tchaikovsky keeps the upper strings in a nonbrilliant register under a low tune. Here Vln I has E6–A6 (div.) above Tbn 1. The sf comes before the trombone's entry, and the strings fall to p under its ff, so the tune is not covered. The passage is protected (Tbn 1 over the tremolo 63–64, c294). No change.

**R4. Vln II above the C.a. quotation at 48–51.** The two lines are separated by register and rhythm, as PDF 144 rules 3–4 require, and the dynamics are graded (pp under mp). Same conclusion as c294.

**R5. Vln I divisi at 62 b3 (PDF 152).** It was raised and settled by c059, c092 and c159, and the 62 b3 spacing is protected. No change.
