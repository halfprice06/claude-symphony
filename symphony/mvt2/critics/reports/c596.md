## Findings

### 1. Bar 29: horns 3 and 4 enter pp after 22 bars' rest with no cue in their parts. (applied)

- Hn 4 rests from 6 b3 to 29 b1, about 78 s at crotchet = 52. It then enters pp on written B♭3 (concert E♭3), the lowest note in its part, in the register Ex. X-5 (PDF 303) marks as the dark bottom of the "deep and solid" range. Hn 3 rests from 6 b3 to 29 b3 and enters pp on written A♭4 (concert D♭4).
- Both entries come under strings that are still diminuendo from f (cellos and violins f at 28, p>pp at 30), so the player cannot hear his own attack well. Adler PDF 304: the player "must 'hear' and then 'find' each note in his mind and in his embouchure." The parts give nothing to hear: page 14-15 of v596's parts book show a 6-bar rest (23-28) and then the note.
- No other instrument sounds E♭3 in bar 28; the basses and violas reach E♭ on the same beat as Hn 4.
- Every other horn entry after a long rest already has a cue: Hn 1 at 53 (C.a., c075), Hn 2-4 before 60-61 (Hn 1, c135), Hn 2 at 75 and Hn 1 at 78 (Vc., c466), Hn 3·4 at 79 (Hn 1, c455). Record correction: c466 said every other horn entry was cued; the two entries at 29 were not.
- The cellos supply both pitches: at 27 they play E♭3-F3 (Hn 4's entry note first), at 28 A♭3-F3-D♭4 (Hn 3's entry note last). Transposed for horn in F the cue reads written B♭3-C4 | E♭4-C4-A♭4.

### 2. Bars 53-70: horn 1's endurance before the second solo. (no change)

Hn 1 plays from 53 b4 to the end of 62, about 38 s, rising from pp at written E♭4 to ff at written E♭5, with the line mostly in written C5-F5 (Ex. X-5's "bright and heroic" band). Breaths fall at 56, 58 and 60. It then rests 63-66 (about 16.7 s) and plays the exposed p solo at 67-70 in written B♭4-F4, the middle register. PDF 303 asks for periodic rests after high playing; this order (loud and high first, then a rest, then a soft solo in the middle) follows it. It was settled by c046, c194, c264 and c336, and I do not re-raise it.

### 3. Bars 61-62: horn 4, a low horn, a2 with horn 3 up to written G♭5. (no change)

This departs from PDF 303 (II and IV low). It is protected (c064, c069, c086): with the trumpets tacet, the top line needs two horns. Written G♭5 is well inside Ex. X-4 and the passage is short, tenuto and f. Not re-raised.

### 4. Bars 87-90: muted horn 4 at written C4-C♭4-B♭3, pp. (no change)

PDF 303 says low notes work best sustained or slow; these are dotted minims under one slur at crotchet = 52 slowing to 44, with the cor anglais an octave above. Under a straight mute this is the least secure part of the range, but the writing follows the reading and the close is protected (c466 reached the same result).

### 5. Mute plan, breaths and articulation. (no change)

- "mettere sord." at 82 gives about 18 s before "con sord." at 87 (82 at 44, 83-86 at 52). "via sord." at 91 is followed by "senza sord." at the scherzo's first Hn 3·4 entry, which meets PDF 308.
- Every slur fits in one breath (table, row 20): 15 s or less at p/pp, about 9 s or less at f/ff.
- No pedal notes, stopped notes, cuivré, bells up, glissandos, trills or multiple tonguing are used. None of them would suit an Adagio with this layout, and c328 already turned down stopped horn.
