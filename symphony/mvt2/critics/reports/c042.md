## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 51–53: the cellos double the 7th of A♭7, hold it against its own resolution, then leap away (applied)
**Problem.** At 51–52 the bass is A♭1 (Cb) and the harmony is V7 of D♭ (Vla C4, Vln II E♭4). The cellos hold G♭3 for both bars. The cor anglais sings A♭4–G♭4 (51) and then F4 (52 b1–3), so:
- the 7th is doubled at the octave on 51 b3–4 and 52 b4;
- through 52 b1–3 the resolution F4 sounds above the unresolved G♭3, a major 7th;
- at 53 the cello G♭3 falls a 4th to D♭3, so in that voice the 7th never resolves.

**Evidence.** "As in other seventh chords, the 7th resolves by stepwise descent" (A&S U22 §3, PDF 375). The 7th is best introduced as a passing or neighbour tone (PDF 377). The cor anglais' A♭–G♭–F already does this. The passage is pp, Vc/Cb/Eh/Vln II/Vla alone, so the fault is exposed.

**Fix (bSec.ily, bSecVc).**
`... fis2 b,2 | ges1 | ges1\< |` → `... fis2 b,2 | es1 | es1\< |`

E♭3 is the chord's 5th and steps down to D♭3 at 53. V7 is then complete only when the cor anglais' passing G♭ arrives. The cor anglais quotation and the respellings 47/51 are untouched.

### 2. Bars 78–81: ♭6̂ is cancelled by ♮6̂ at 78 and never resolved; use ♮6̂–♭6̂–5̂ in the repeated cadence (applied)
**Problem.** The ♭VI deceptive cadence at 78 (protected, cycle 23) puts F♭ in the bass and moves it up to F on beat 3. F then stays as ♮6̂ through 79–80 (IV, ii7), and E♭ arrives at 81. This is the order A&S call poor: "Once introduced, ♭6̂ tends to move to 5̂; it will not normally be replaced by ♮6̂ before 5̂ is reached… ♭6̂–♮6̂–5̂ is poor" (PDF 388, 395). Bars 79–80 also repeat the cadence of 75–76 (IV, B♭6 appoggiatura, ii7) unchanged. A&S's tool for varying such a repetition is a mode change (PDF 392, Ex. 23-8), and a common use of ♭6̂ is "to color and intensify IV, II6, II7" (PDF 395, Points 2a).

**Fix.** Keep 78 and the protected peak. On 80 b3, flatten the F of ii7 so the chord becomes iiø7 (B♭–D♭–F♭–A♭), and let F♭ fall to E♭ at 81. That gives F–F♭–E♭ (♮6̂–♭6̂–5̂, the good order) inside the A′ close, ff. The same line returns softly in the coda (87–89), and the movement's E/F♭→E♭ thread now resolves at the main cadence.

aTwo.ily:
- `aTwoVla`: `<f' aes'>2.:32 |` (bar 80) → `<f' aes'>2:32 <fes' aes'>4:32 |`
- `aTwoHnThree`: `f'2. |` (bar 80) → `f'2 fes'4 |`
- `aTwoFluteTwo`: `f'''2. |` (bar 80) → `f'''2 r4 |` (so no F6 sounds against F♭4)

The violas and horn 3 make F→F♭ in one voice, so there is no cross relation (PDF 394, Ex. 23-12b). F♭4/A♭4 move to E♭4/G4 in parallel 3rds. The theme's octaves and the doubling layout of 75–82 (protected) are unchanged; only an inner chord tone changes and flute 2 loses one beat.

### 3. Bar 56: the vii°7/vi of bar 40 has lost its 7th in the B3 statement (applied)
**Problem.** At 40 the chord is A–C–E♭–G♭: Vln II's G♭4 is an upper neighbour F–G♭–F, the A2/G♭4 d7 resolves to a P5, and the G♭4–C5 A4 moves to a P4 with a doubled root. That is a textbook use (PDF 375, 377, Ex. 22-3b, 22-6a). At 56 the violins are on the melody, so only A (bass), the viola arpeggio A–C–E♭ and the melody C5 remain. The chord is a bare root-position diminished triad with its leading tone doubled.

**Fix (bSec.ily, bSecBsnOne, bars 53–56).** `R1*4 |` → `R1*2 | r2 f2(\p | ges2 f2) |`

Bassoon 1 repeats the bar-40 neighbour figure: F3 (chord tone at 55 b3), G♭3 (the 7th), F3. The d7 A2–G♭3 resolves to the P5 B♭2–F3 and the A2 G♭3–A3 resolves to a P4 (PDF 375, 377). It enters at p, the same level as bassoon 2 on the shared staff. Clarinet 1 and horn 1 are already playing from 52, so the strings→woodwinds→brass entry order of 53–61 is unchanged.

### 4. Bars 46–47: B♭→B♮ split between bassoon 2 and violas; the 7th of C♯7 enters by leap (applied)
**Problem.** At 46 b3–4, bassoon 2 holds B♭2 and the violas G♭3. At 47 the violas leap a 4th to B3, the 7th of C♯7 (= C♭, ♭7̂ of D♭). The chromatic B♭→B is split between two voices (PDF 394, Ex. 23-12a). The 7th also enters by leap, the least preferred approach (PDF 377).

**Fix (bSec.ily, bSecVla).** `f1\pp | f2 ges2 | b1 |` → `f1\pp | f2 bes2 | b1 |`

The violas now play B♭3–B3–A3: the chromatic step is in one voice, the 7th is prepared, and it falls to A3 at 48. The G♭ chord at 46 b3 stays complete (G♭ in the bass, B♭, D♭ in the cellos and oboe). The spelling is unchanged.
