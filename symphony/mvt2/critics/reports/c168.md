## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The closing fermata at 92 is missing in 15 parts. Applied.
- **Problem.** The fermata is written only in the parts that play or sit in the last system: strings, Cl 1·2, cor anglais, Hn 3·4, timpani. Flutes, oboes, bass clarinet, bassoons, contrabassoon, Hn 1·2, trombones, bass trombone, tuba and harp end on a plain multi-bar rest (Flute 1: "6 | 4" after K). Those players see "rit." over the rest and then nothing, although the last bar is held at the conductor's discretion and the timpani part even says "during the pause" before movement III.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 5: "Tempo and meter changes must be indicated on all parts, even during periods of extended rest", with Ex. 4 (PDF 10) showing tempo words placed over multi-bar rests. A fermata is a change of tempo. MOLA PDF 8 asks that the part be read "through the eyes of a player".
- **Fix** (coda.ily). Add variables for the instruments that rest through the coda, and split the harp's last rest:
  ```
  codaFluteOne = { R2.*9 | R2.\fermata | }
  % likewise codaFluteTwo, codaOboeOne, codaOboeTwo, codaBassCl, codaBsnOne, codaBsnTwo,
  % codaCbsn, codaHnOne, codaHnTwo, codaTbnOne, codaTbnTwo, codaBassTbn, codaTuba
  codaHarpR = { ... | R2. | R2.\fermata | }   % was R2.*2
  ```
  The `\sec` function picks the new variables up without other changes. In the score these staves stay hidden in the last system (score.pdf pages 1 and 3–8 are pixel-identical to v168's; page 2 changes only by finding 2). In the parts the final bar now splits from the multi-rest and carries the fermata (checked in Flute 1, Trombone 1, Harp).

### 2. Two loose ends in the 15–22 dialogue (Vln II at 19, violas at 19). Applied.
- **Problem.**
  - Vln II 19: `aes'2--(\< g'4--) | r2\! e'4--\pp`. c157 replaced bar 20's held notes with a rest and took the diminuendo with them, so the swell that c139 kept "under the oboe at 15–16, 19–20" is now a crescendo that runs into silence. At 15–16 the same figure is a complete swell.
  - Violas 19: they re-enter after a bar's rest with no dynamic, the only re-entry in 15–22 without one (Ob 1 and the solo violin got their p at 19 and 21 from c068).
- **Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10): every hairpin starts and ends at a level the player can see, and the entry after a rest restates its dynamic (pp after the three-bar rest). PDF 8: proofread the horizontal line of each part.
- **Fix** (aOne.ily):
  - `aOneVlnTwo`: `aes'2--(\< g'4--\>) | r2\! e'4--\pp |`. The swell now peaks on beat 3 and is gone before the oboe's B♭–A♭ arrives on 20, which is what c157 wanted (the oboe's arrival heard alone).
  - `aOneVla`: `f'2\pp e'4 |` at bar 19.

### 3. At the A′ peak (79) the cellos are the only playing staff without a mark. Not applied.
- **Problem.** At 79 every staff prints ff (woodwinds, Vln I, Vln II, violas, basses) or f (four horns, timpani), except the cellos, which carry their f over from 78 (c108 removed a repeated f). The cellos play the counter-melody with Bsn 1 (ff, forced by the shared staff) and Hn 1·2 (f). A conductor reading down bar 79 sees one blank staff and cannot tell whether the cellos are meant to stay under the tutti or were missed. At 61, the other ff arrival, every staff is marked.
- **Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11): at the tutti arrival every staff carries its own mark, in the same place. PDF 8: proofread the vertical lines.
- **Fix, either:**
  - `aTwoVc` bar 79: `des'4\ff c'4 bes4 |` (the strings' level; the tenor-register cellos then carry the counter-melody through the tutti with the horns), or
  - keep the level and say so: `des'4^\markup \italic "sempre f" c'4 bes4 |`.
  - I lean to ff. Either way the A′ dynamic plan (p→ff, c008) is kept. Check page 7 for spacing.

### 4. In the viola part, the tempo words of 47–48 and letter F at 53 float between systems. Recorded; no clean fix.
- **Problem.** In the viola part, "poco sostenuto" (48) is stacked above "poco rit." (47), both over bar 48 and nearer the line above. "F" and "a tempo, poco a poco animando" sit at the height of bar 48's pp in the system above.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12): anchored markings in extracted parts drift into floating positions.
- **Tested:**
  - Normal part spacing for the viola (genparts.py without the c165 exception) pushes the part to two pages. Padding 2 and 3 also give two pages, and `page-count = 1` overflows.
  - Padding 1.5 stays on one page but leaves the stacks as they are.
  - A part-only break at 53 (`{ s4*172 \break }`) puts F at a line start but moves the stack to bar 42.
- **Recommendation.** Leave it, as c165 did. The barlines still identify the bars, and a page turn would be worse (MOLA PDF 7). I restored genparts.py unchanged.
