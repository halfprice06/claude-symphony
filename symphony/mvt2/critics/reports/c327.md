## Findings

All of the reading's rules are kept in v327. I found no violation that would justify a change, so I made no edit and do not recommend one. The points below record what the third pass checked that the first two did not, ranked by how close each comes to a real weakness.

### 1. Bars 87–88: viola + muted horn with the cor anglais above (follows; no change)

The coda's F–F♭–E♭ line is Vla (lower half) + Hn 4 con sord. in unison, with the C.a. an octave above at pp. This is Rimsky's Ex. 92 (PDF 269, *Golden Cockerel* [98]: Violas + Horn, other horns in soft chords). It also follows PDF 79 (Vla + Hn is the closest string–brass pair) and PDF 78 (in two octaves, the string group doubles only the lower one, so the editor's objection to bare wind-over-string octaves does not arise). Ex. 92 also shows something this passage lacks: a moving cello line under the doubled melody. Here the cellos and basses hold A♭ from 83 to 92 apart from the F–D♭ at 87–88. The static bass belongs to the protected plagal close (87–92), so I record the comparison only.

### 2. Bars 57 b3–60: Hn 1 an octave over Vc + Bsn 1 (follows; no change)

PDF 76 allows mixed wood-and-brass octaves, and PDF 76–77 call Vc + Fag a best unison. The layout is a horn octave above a string + wind unison, so every element is a pairing the reading lists. c087 and c187 raised the horn–cello unison point (PDF 79, Ex. 90) and kept the octave. I have no new argument. Recorded.

### 3. Bars 80 b3–81 b1: one oboe above Hn 3 at ff (no change)

PDF 75 says a single horn doubled an octave above needs two winds (2 Cl, 2 Ob or 2 Fl), more in forte. At 80 b3 the V♭9's F♭ is Hn 3 + lower violas (F♭4) with only Ob 2 above (F♭5). There is no free second wind in that register. Fl 2 rests at 80 b3, but F♭6 would be two octaves up. Cl 2 holds the D♭5 of the chord, and Ob 1, Cl 1 and the C.a. carry the theme. The F♭ is already in three instruments over two octaves, the discord is protected (V♭9 at 80–81) and c287 recorded the same layout. Moving Cl 2 to F♭5 would drop the chord's D♭. Not recommended.

### 4. Bars 53–56: the bass in the cellos alone (follows; no change)

Under the violins' unison statement of theme B, the bass (D♭3–C3, B♭2–G♭2, E♭2–C2, A2–B♭2) is the cellos alone. The basses, B.cl. and Bsn 2 return at 57. PDF 78 says a soft low line suits the cellos and need not be doubled. Ex. 91 adds a bassoon only in a tutti ff. The entry of the 16-foot bass at 57 is part of the protected build order 53–61. If the composer ever wants more weight at 55–56 (E♭2–C2 at mf), the fix the reading supports is Bsn 2 in unison with the cellos for those two bars:
```
% bSec.ily, bSecBsnTwo, bars 53–56 (now R1*4):
R1*2 | \partCombineSoloII es,2\p\< c,2 | a,2\mf bes,2 |
```
I did not test it and do not recommend it. It would pre-empt Bsn 2's entry at 57 and blur the change of weight that the build depends on.

### 5. Bars 61–62: three-octave augmented head (follows; no change)

Each octave has a string and a wind: Fl 2 + Vln I (from 61 b3); Vln II + Cl 2 at 61 and + Ob 1 at 62; Vc + Bsn 1 (from 61 b3). The bass is the three-group tutti formula of PDF 80 (Cb + Bsn 2 + Cbn + B.tbn + Tuba). The brass is in normal register order with 2 horns over 1 trombone (PDF 74). This is the textbook layout. Recorded.

### 6. Bars 63–64: trombone quotation undoubled (follows; no change)

PDF 75 forbids woodwinds an octave over a trombone. PDF 74–75 say a wind in unison would give a finer legato. The quotation is kept to a single colour by the cyclic rule, so the second point does not apply. The cello + bass + bass trombone unison at 64 is PDF 79's Vc/Cb + Tbn pairing. Recorded.

### 7. Bars 75–82: counter-melody in three groups (follows; no change)

Vc + Bsn 1 + Hn 2 (Hn 1 from 78) is PDF 79's "Violas (or 'Cellos) + Cl. (Eng. horn) + Horn" with a bassoon for the clarinet. PDF 75 names Horn + Fag. The reading says three groups suit loud passages. The counter-melody starts at mp under a violin melody that has no wind until 77. c217 settled that balance (Hn 2 one level under the cellos, cellos ff at 79). Recorded.
