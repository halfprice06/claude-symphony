## Findings

### 1. Bar 55: the same E♭4 string is plucked twice in a row by the right hand, twice. Applied.

Bars 55 b3 to 56 b1. The RH plays `bes8 c' es' es' bes es' | es' ges' ...`. E♭4 is struck on the last triplet of beat 3 and again on the first of beat 4, and again on the last triplet of beat 4 and the first of 56. At the animando tempo here (♩=61-62) each triplet eighth lasts about a third of a second. The LH rests throughout 53-56, so both repetitions fall to the same hand.

Evidence: Adler PDF 118 gives the two ways a harpist repeats one pitch quickly: alternate the hands on the same string, or tune two strings to the same pitch enharmonically and alternate them (Ex. IV-15). The enharmonic way is not available: D♯ would need the D pedal, which must stay D♭ for the D♭4s in 55 b1-2 and would be moved under a ringing string. PDF 117 (sons étouffés) shows the other side of the problem: a finger placed back on a sounding string stops it, so a same-hand repetition is either a small break in the ring or a buzz. Nowhere else in 53-56 does the figure repeat a string; 54 and 55 b1-2 use the low-middle-high-middle-low-middle shape, and 56 alternates two strings.

Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-396/work/sections/bSec.ily): beat 4 becomes B♭3-E♭4-B♭3.

```
\tuplet 3/2 4 { bes8\p des' es' des' bes des' } \tuplet 3/2 4 { bes8 c' es' bes es' bes } |
```

The pitch content is unchanged (B♭ and E♭ only on beat 4), there is still no C4 on beat 4 (c332's point), E♭4 is still on beat 4 as the violas have it at 39 b4, and the figure leaves B♭3 for the E♭4 at 56 by a fourth. Bar 56 and the rest of 53-56 are untouched. This changes no protected passage, theme, quotation or the ending.

### 2. Bar 41 b4: the A pedal returns to A♭ while the A♮ strings may still ring. Recorded, no change.

The A♮1-A♮2 octave is struck at 40 b1 and the pedal goes back to A♭ in the 41 b4 rest, about five seconds later at ♩=60. Wound low strings are still sounding then, and a pedal moved under a vibrating string bends it audibly. Adler PDF 113 says the composer need only check same-side clashes and the time available; the harpist chooses the moment and damps the string herself. c116 already moved the mark from 40 b3 to 41 b4, the latest rest before the A♭ at 42 b3. A "damp" or "étouffez" sign here would be over-notation. No change.

### 3. Mid-movement pedal settings appear in the score only. Recorded, no change.

The part shows the starting setting at 1 and nothing at 37, 39-41, 53 or 71. Adler PDF 112 asks only for the setting at the beginning; the later changes are each one pedal, in long rests (principle 5), and the accidentals in the part show them. This is c075's protected decision (MOLA PDF 5), and the reading gives no reason to reopen it.

### 4. Movement I to II pedal reset during the attacca. Recorded, no change.

The harp resets four pedals (D and B with the left foot, E and A with the right) in the six bars of the link, about 21 seconds at ♩=52. Adler PDF 112-113 is met: no foot moves two pedals at once and the time is ample.

### 5. Harmonics at 83. Recorded, no change.

Written at the lower octave with "o" and the explanation "sound 8va" (PDF 116), inside the C3-A5 range, a single line spanning a fifth for the left hand, over Vc 4 soli and Cb ppp with the harp's own A♭1 tied rather than re-struck (PDF 116, rule 1). The pickup E♭4-F4 is plucked at pp so it does not stand out above the harmonics. Nothing to change.
