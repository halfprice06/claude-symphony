### 5. The II→III common tone is an inner voice (record; no change)
**Bars:** 87–92 and scherzo bar 1.
**Point:** A&S PDF 587 and 603 say the common tone is "usually in the soprano", and *Widmung* keeps A♭/G♯ in the vocal line (Ex. 32-3, viewed). Here A♭4 is in Vln II under Vln I's C5. I do not recommend a change. C5 on top is the I→II common tone and is protected (c030, cyclic rule). Ex. 32-3 also shows the voice resting between A♭ and G♯. The scherzo's Hn 1 then plays G♯4 alone under a fermata at Vln II's pitch, so the common tone is exposed at the moment the new key arrives, which is what the book requires.
