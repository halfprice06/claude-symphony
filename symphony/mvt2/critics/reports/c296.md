## Findings

### 1. Sticks are never changed back after bar 50 (applied)

Bars 65-85. At 50 the part says "medium sticks" for the f rolls at 60-62 (c146). Nothing cancels it, so the player plays the ppp roll "near the rim" at 67-70 and the exposed pp answer E♭-C-A♭ at 84-85 with medium sticks, and the f roll at 79 has no stick instruction of its own. Adler says the composer should name hard, medium or soft (PDF 61, PDF 80) and that beater instructions belong in score and part (PDF 88); he also says the player must be given time to change mallets (PDF 61 footnote). Movement I and bar 1 here use soft sticks for the soft rolls; the same applies at 67 and 84. Each change falls in rests: 65-66 (two bars), 77-78 (two bars), 83 (one bar after the rest at 82).

Fix, applied in /tmp/critic-296/work:
- retr.ily: `retrTimp = { R2.^\markup \small "soft sticks" | R2. | es2.:32\ppp~^\markup \small \italic "near the rim" | ...`
- aTwo.ily: `aTwoTimp = { R2.*6 | R2.^\markup \small "medium sticks" | R2. | des2.:32\f^\markup \small \italic "ord." | ...`
- coda.ily: `R2.^\markup \small "soft sticks" | es4.\pp c8 aes,4~ | aes,2. | R2. |`

In the score, "soft sticks" prints at 65 and 83. The 77 marking prints in the part only, because the timpani staff is hidden on the 75-78 system. If the composer wants it in the score too, add `-\tag #'score ^\markup \small "medium sticks"` to the 79 roll beside "ord.".

### 2. The D♭ roll at 79-80 is two rolls (optional, not applied)

Bars 79-80: `des2.:32\f | des2.:32 |`. Without a tie the player restrikes at 80. Adler's Ex. XII-48 (PDF 80) writes a roll longer than a bar with ties so that it continues. A restrike may be intended here, since the contrabassoon and basses also re-attack D♭ at 80 b1. If the composer wants one continuous roll under the tied winds and Vln I, write `des2.:32\f~ | des2.:32 |`. If the restrike is intended, leave it; the notation is then correct. No bars or notes would change either way.

### 3. The E♭ roll at 81 stops at the barline (no change recommended)

Bar 81: `es2.:32\>` to niente, then rest at 82. The rolls at 3, 60 and 70 end on a tied stroke (Ex. XII-47). Here the circled hairpin to the barline already shows where the roll ends, as PDF 80 requires. Changing it to `es2:32~\> es8\! r8` would end the roll a beat before the V♭9 resolves at 82, and a tied stroke into 82 would bring back the stroke that c123 removed. I record it so later critics do not raise it again.

### 4. Drum ranges (no change)

A♭2 sits at the top of the 30" (Ex. XII-46: D2-A2) and C3 at the top of the 28" (F2-C3). D♭3 is below the 23" (D3-A3), so A♭2/C3/D♭3/E♭3 on 30"/28"/25"/23" is the only layout inside Adler's ranges. The top notes of a drum sound a little tighter, but both appear only at p or pp (A♭2 at 2-3, 52, 60, 84-85; C3 once, an eighth, at 84). Keep the c270 muta chain.
