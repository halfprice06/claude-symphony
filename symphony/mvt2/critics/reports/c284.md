## Findings

### 1. Flute 1's A6 at 63 b1 sounds ff, alone, over the trombone quotation's first notes. Applied.
- **Bars:** 63, beat 1 (Fl 1 A6, quarter note).
- **Problem:** at the end of 62 every wind stops except flute 1. Flute 1 slurs from its held A♭6 into A6 (c070: the chromatic line resolves in the voice that holds it). The note has no dynamic of its own, so it is still ff from 61. The strings attack the A-minor chord sf and diminuendo to p (c154 made the bass trombone do the same). Trombone 1's quotation starts on the second eighth of the beat. So the loudest sustained sound during the quotation's anacrusis E3 and its first C4 is a top-register flute at ff, the one wind "left alone in its upper reaches" (PDF 151). Adler's examples all support the fix. A very high flute cuts through without any doubling (PDF 152, Ex. XV-4), so it does not need ff to be heard. Instruments that do not carry the statement are marked below it (PDF 147, Ex. XV-1: trombones ppp). A new colour should enter after a rest or anacrusis so that it is clearly heard (PDF 156). The flute ending at 63 also goes against Barber's rule of taking the high woodwinds out before the gesture ends (PDF 150 item 2). Taking the flute out would undo c070, though. Marking the flute's A6 with the strings' sf keeps c070's resolution and makes it part of the falling background.
- **Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-284/work/sections/bSec.ily, bSecFluteOne):**
  `aes'''1( | \once \stemDown a'''4)\sf-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." \tweak staff-position #0 r4 \tweak staff-position #0 r2 | R1 |`
  (was `a'''4)-\tag #'score ...` with no dynamic). Flute 2 has a spacer at 63, so the shared staff carries only one dynamic. The sf prints in the score and in the Flute 1 part.
- **MIDI side effect, also an improvement:** in the part-combiner's "one" voice, the run at 78 b3 (C6 + F6-G6-A♭6) had inherited the ff of 61 (velocity 64). It now starts from 58, the same value as the f printed at 78. The written dynamics there are unchanged.
- **If the composer prefers to end the flute with the other winds** (Barber, PDF 150 item 2): `aes'''1 | R1 |` at 62–63 removes the A6. Vln I still has the A6, but this reverses c070, so I did not apply it.

### 2. Horns 1·2 ff and horns 3·4 f at 61–62. Recorded, no change.
At 61 horns 1·2 hold D♭4/F4 at ff while horns 3·4 (a2), the trombones and the tuba play f. Principle 4 asks for the brass to be marked below the theme. The split is consistent, though. Horns 1·2 are divided (one player per note, c264), and Rimsky PDF 103 gives one degree louder for a single horn as the fallback when pairs are not possible. Horns 3·4 a2 are two players on one note at f. Each pitch therefore has about the same weight, and the brass stays one degree under the ff woodwinds and strings (c218). No change.

### 3. Long unisons (principle 21, PDF 158). Recorded, no change.
The MIDI scan finds these runs of four or more identical notes: Vln I = Vln II at 23–30 and 53–56 (the protected unison plan); Hn 1 = Cl 1 at 53–56 (protected counter-line); Bsn 1 = Vc at 45–47, 57–60 and 75–82, with Hn 1 or Hn 1·2 at 59–60 and 75–82 (c094, c011: the three-timbre blend); C.a. = violas at 79–82 (c187); Hn 3·4 = lower violas at 87–89 (c227, c280); B.cl. = Bsn 2 = Cb on the bass at 57–60. Harp LH also doubles the pizzicato basses note for note at 37–44. That is the standard harp and pizzicato bass blend, not a chamber-style line. Mahler's rule describes one style of scoring. Each of these unisons was chosen on cited grounds, and none is a background line hiding the foreground. No change.

### 4. Trombone colour just before the quotation (PDF 156). Recorded, no change.
Trombone 2 and the bass trombone play f in the tutti at 61–62, so the trombone sound is not new at 63. Trombone 1 itself rests from 37 to 62, and the whole wind tutti stops at the barline. The quotation is therefore set off by the drop in texture and by its own eighth-rest anacrusis, which is what PDF 156 asks for. The brass layout at 61–62 is protected (c014, c021, c124). No change.

### 5. Theme B's colour changes, 37–52 (PDF 146). Recorded, no change.
Fl 1 (37), C.a. (41), Ob 1 + Vc (45) and the C.a. quotation (47) give four colours in eleven bars, which is close to the "constant flux" PDF 146 warns about. Each change falls on a phrase seam, and the background (viola triplets, pizz. basses, harp) stays the same through 37–44. That steady background is what keeps the structure clear. Protected as "theme-B colours 37–60". No change.
