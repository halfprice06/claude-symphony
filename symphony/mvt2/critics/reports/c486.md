## Findings

### 1. Bars 7-12, bassoon 2: 19.6 s at pp with no breath marked (applied)

**Problem:** Bassoon 2 plays `c2.(\pp | c4-- bes,4-- bes,4-- | c2 bes,4~ | bes,2.) | c2( des4 | f2--) r4`. That is 17 beats at ♩=52, or 19.6 s, in the low register with no rest and no comma. It is the longest unprotected stretch in the movement. c126 left a slur break at 10-11 so the player could "re-tongue or snatch air", but no breath is printed there.

**Evidence:**
- Adler PDF 321 says slurred notes are played on one breath, and that a slur's end is a point where the next note is tongued. PDF 176 says the same for woodwinds: a slur works like one bow.
- A slur break tells the player to re-tongue. It does not tell them to breathe. This score marks breaths with `\breathe` in 43 places, so a player will read an unmarked slur break as "no breath intended" and try to hold out to 12 b3.
- Adler PDF 188 asks the orchestrator to plan rest after sustained passages.
- The end of bar 10 is the natural place to breathe:
  - Bsn 1 rests on 10 b3.
  - The bass clarinet and horn 1 breathe at 10 b2.5.
  - The bass clarinet's G2-A♭2 slur and the harp's E♭ hold the bass across the barline.
- So this comma completes the staggered breath plan rather than adding a new gap.
- This answers c126's reason: c126 already meant a breath to be possible here. The comma only prints it.

**Fix (sections/aOne.ily, aOneBsnTwo, line 31):**
```
  c2.(\pp | c4-- bes,4-- bes,4-- | c2 bes,4~ | bes,2.) \breathe |
```
Bsn 2 then plays 13.8 s and 5.8 s, with a second breath at 12 b3 as before.

### 2. Bars 63-64, trombone 1 quotation: no change

The quotation (E3-C4-B3-B♭3-A3-G♯3-A3-F3-E3) is within the range of Ex. X-68 and the strong band of Ex. X-69. Its slide motion is slow enough (Ex. X-71 warns only against fast I-VII alternation). It lasts 9.2 s at ff, which is within one breath for a tenor trombone. Its slurs (`e8( c'4.->)`, `b8( bes8 a8 | a4--)`, `gis8( a8)`) group the notes as movement I's P head does, and the separate notes are tongued (PDF 321). The ff marcato entry after about 233 s of rest is cued from the bass trombone, and the cue's last pitch, F♭2, is the same pitch class as the E3 entry. Nothing to change. A note for the composer: if Tbn 1's B3-B♭3 slur is ever taken at a quicker tempo, the player can use 5th position for B♭3 (IV-V-VI instead of IV-I-II). That is the player's choice, not a notation matter.
