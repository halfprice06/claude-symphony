## Findings

No principle of the assigned reading is violated in v493. I propose no change. The findings below are records ranked by how much they bear on the form; findings 1 and 5 are points no earlier critic on this reading recorded.

### 1. The dominant pedal at 51-52 resolves at 53 without the basses (recorded; follows)

- **Bars:** 51-53.
- **What the score does.** The basses hold A♭1 for two bars (A♭ triad at 51, A♭7 at 51 b3 and 52 b4) and then rest from 53 to 56 (c164). The tonic that the pedal prepares, at theme B's return in the violins, is taken by the cellos (C♯3) and the timpani (D♭3). So the bass register drops an octave and a fifth at the arrival.
- **Why it might be questioned.** A&S PDF 367 places dominant pedals "before a tonic that comes at a recapitulation of the opening material", and Ex. 21-41 resolves the pedal D to G in the bass's own register. A one-note fix was possible: `bSecCb` bar 53 `des,2\pp r2` instead of the first `R1` of `R1*4`, doubling the cellos' C♯3 half note an octave down.
- **Why I do not propose it.** Dvořák does the same thing at the matching point of his Largo. In `bassmL` the basses hold A♭ for three bars (`af'1\ppp~| af~ | af4 r r2`, bars 43-45), and both basses and cellos are silent for the first bars of the C♯-minor section (`R1*6`). The dominant pedal there also prepares an arrival with no bass. In this score the cellos and timpani at least state the root, and c164's reason (Adler part 1 PDF 130-131, 139: leave the basses out under the first string statement and add them for the build at 57) still applies. The basses enter at 57 mf<, which makes the build easier to hear. The one-note fix would weaken that. Recorded, no change.

### 2. Placement and share of the pedals match the book and the model (recorded)

- Tonic pedals stand at the beginning (2-6), at the end of A (30-36) and at the end (83-86, 89-92). Dominant pedals stand before each structural tonic: 51-52 before B's return, 60 before the climax at 61, and 65-70 before A′ at 71. This is PDF 367 point by point.
- Durations: 2-6 17.3 s; 30-36 25.0 s; 51-52 8.9 s; 60 4.0 s; 65-70 20.8 s; 83-86 13.8 s; 89-92 18.7 s. In the first 36 bars, 12 bars (33 %) are on a pedal. Dvořák's bass part keeps about 20 of his first 45 bars on D♭ or A♭ pedals (about 44 %), with the longest (about ten bars of D♭) under the theme's restatement. This score keeps its themes over a moving bass and uses pedals only at section joins. Both fit "tonic pedals occur frequently at beginnings and endings" (PDF 367). The plan's "melodic bass counterline" rule (movement-2-plan.md) asks for this score's choice.
- The one-bar dominant at 60 before the climax at 61 is shorter than the two bars at 51-52 before the lesser return. c223 and c353 recorded this; the chromatic bass rise G♭-G-A♭ at 59-60 and the bass ascent 57-60 are protected. I add nothing.

### 3. Each pedal begins and ends on its chord; 86 is the book's exception (recorded)

From the beat dump: 2-6 I-iv6/4-I; 30-36 I-°7-I (open fifth at 35-36, protected); 51-52 V-iii6 (passing)-V7; 60 V-(F, G♭ passing)-V7; 65-70 V7-passing 6/4 (68 b3)-V7; 89-92 I. At 86 the A♭ pedal lies under D-F-A♭-C and then the bass leaves by F-D♭-A♭ for the plagal close. This is Ex. 21-42 (PDF 368), where Bach's tonic pedal turns into the seventh of a V4/2 that leads away. Follows.

### 4. Upper-part dissonances over the pedals are prepared and resolved (recorded)

PDF 368 requires this. I checked every dissonant interval above the pedal tone:
- **5:** F♭3 and D♭4 are neighbours to E♭ and C, consonant with each other (iv6/4) and back by step at 6.
- **33 b2:** D3-C♭4 (B3) over A♭: both enter by step (E♭-D, C-C♭) and leave by step (D-D♭-C, C♭-B♭-A♭).
- **52 b1-2:** cello D3 against viola C4 is the passing ♯4 in C3-D3-E♭3 (c322).
- **60 b2-4:** passing F, then G♭ as seventh of V7 resolving to F at 61 in horn 1 and violas.
- **79-80:** over the D♭ bass the B♭6 appoggiatura holds to 80 b2 and falls to A♭6; the inner D♭-C-B♭-A♭ (horns 1·2, bassoon 1, cellos) passes by step.
- **86:** clarinet D5 against viola C4 is the chromatic passing tone of the protected motto tail E♭-D-D♭-C and moves on by step.
No dissonance between upper parts is left by leap. Follows.

### 5. Two pedals not recorded before: inner A♭4 at 15-19 and D♭ under the A′ peak at 79-80 (recorded)

- **15-19:** Vln II holds A♭4 under the oboe-violin dialogue, leaving it only for the neighbour G4 at 16 b3 and 19 b3 and F4 at 18 b3, while the cellos move C-D♭-E♭-F-D♭-C. This is the inner-voice pedal of PDF 367. It keeps the tonic audible in the b-phrase until the move to F minor at 20.
- **79-80 b2:** the bass holds D♭ for six beats (cbn, basses, B.cl., Bsn 2, timpani) while the upper voices make the B♭6-A♭6 appoggiatura over ii6 and the inner line descends. A pedal on IV is the book's "other points" (PDF 367). Holding the bass still under the highest melodic point lets the moving upper voices carry the peak. The motion then resumes at 80 b3 (B♭) and 81 (E♭) for the cadence. Follows.

### 6. 65-67: three bars with no motion over the dominant (recorded for the third time; not re-raised)

At 65-66 the E♭7 is held and fades; at 67 only E♭2, E♭3 (timpani) and horn 1's E♭4 sound. By PDF 367's definition this is a held chord, not a pedal with moving voices. c223 and c353 recorded it, and the cut-back at 65-67 and "horn 1 unsupported" are protected. The reason given still holds: Ex. 21-37 (PDF 365) shows a held V7 working like a suspension that delays the tonic, which here is the return at 71. I do not answer the stated reason, so I do not reopen it.

### 7. The three six-bar soft pedal passages (recorded)

1-6, 31-36 and 65-70 each last 6 bars (20.8 s, 21.5 s, 20.8 s) and start at 0 %, 30 % and 70 % of the movement. Equal lengths could sound mechanical. But they differ in degree (I, I, V), in what moves over the pedal (common-tone link and horn 3 neighbour; B.cl. solo and woodwind choir; motto tail in horn 1 over the bassoons) and in how they join (attacca from movement I; phrase modulation into D♭; V7-I into A′). c253 recorded them as the reductions after a tutti. Ex. 21-41 also gives its cadential V and final I pedals similar weight. No change.
