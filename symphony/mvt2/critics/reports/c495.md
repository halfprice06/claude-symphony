## Findings

### 1. Viola part, bar 83: letter L floats above the line it belongs to (applied)

Problem: in the viola part, "[L] a tempo" sits about 2 cm above the last line, directly under the "p" of bar 72 on the line above. A violist reading bars 71-77 sees "a tempo" under bar 72. MOLA Ex. 7 (PDF 12) shows exactly this fault, "a tempo" and letter [F] floating between two lines, and PDF 8 names it: anchored markings in extracted parts lead to "collisions or floating markings". Ex. 4 (PDF 10) puts the letter at its barline on its own line.

Evidence: the cause is the two-line text at 84 b3, `^\markup \column { "con sord.," "div., change bow at will" }`. It overlaps the letter-and-word stencil horizontally, so the letter, which has a higher outside-staff priority, is stacked above both text lines.

Fix (sections/coda.ily, codaVla, bar 84): the score keeps the text above; the part prints it below the staff. This is the last line of the page, and nothing is below it:

```
r2 <c' es'>4~\ppp-\tag #'score ^\markup \italic \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.4) \column { "con sord.," "div., change bow at will" } -\tag #'part _\markup \italic \override #'(baseline-skip . 2.4) \column { "con sord.," "div., change bow at will" } |
```

Result in the scratch build: L now sits right above the 83 rest, level with "poco allargando" of 78. The text reads below the 84-85 chords, after "mettere sord." at 83. The viola part stays on one page, and the page now has about 6 staff spaces free at the bottom. The score is unchanged.

### 2. Timpani part, bar 77: "medium sticks" printed over the clef against the bar number (applied)

Problem: bar 77 is the first bar of the last line of the timpani part, a single-bar rest. The text is centred on that short rest, so it runs left over the bass clef and reads "77 medium sticks", with the bar number and the instruction on one line. MOLA Ex. 7 (PDF 12) shows the same fault, "cresc. molto" printed against the bar number 71. Gould PDF 55 says no characters should collide.

Fix (sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoTimp, bar 77): left-align the text in the part only:

```
aTwoTimp = { R2.*6 | R2.-\tag #'score ^\markup \small "medium sticks" -\tag #'part -\tweak self-alignment-X #LEFT ^\markup \small "medium sticks" | ...
```

Result: in the part the text starts at the rest, clear of the clef and the bar number, and ends over the r2 of 78, under "poco allargando". The score is unchanged.

### 3. Viola part, bar 53: letter G sits on the row of bar 48's "sotto voce" (not applied; open)

Problem: "[G] a tempo, poco a poco animando" stands over the 53-56 rest. As in Ex. 4, the word goes above the "53-56 / 4" rest number. Because of that two-tier number, the letter's baseline lies about 2 cm above line 7 and level with "sotto voce" (48) under line 6. On the page it reads "sotto voce  [G] a tempo, poco a poco animando" as one row under bars 48-49 of line 6. Bar 49 is in "poco sostenuto", so a player can take the "a tempo" four bars early. This is the floating marking of MOLA Ex. 7 (PDF 12).

Tests (all reverted): (a) viola system padding 1.5 gave a second viola page (31 pages in the book), and 1.0 made no visible change; (b) a part-only invisible spacer 3 spaces deep under bar 49 (`_\markup \with-dimensions #'(0 . 0.5) #'(-3 . 0) \null`) moved line 7 down but compressed the page ("compressing over-full page by 4.4 staff-spaces"); 1.5 spaces had no visible effect; (c) a part-only `\break` after bar 50 also compressed the page.

Suggested fix for the composer: finding 1 frees about 6 staff spaces at the bottom of the viola page. Spend them here by moving the part-only "sotto voce" from below bar 48 to above it, where the score prints it (`-\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "sotto voce"`). Then try the spacer under 49 with a depth of 2-2.5 spaces. Accept the result only if pb.log shows no "compressing" warning and the book stays at 30 pages. If neither works, record it as a known limit of the one-page viola part (c165, c200).

### 4. Timpani muta wording differs between score and part (applied)

Problem: the score prints "muta A♭ in E, C in G, D♭ in B, E♭ in E (for the Scherzo, in the pause)" at 92. The timpani part prints "... (for the Scherzo, during the pause)". MOLA PDF 5 requires the parts to match the score, and Ex. 5 (PDF 10) shows a change stated once, in one form. A librarian checking parts against the score should find the same words. Clarinet 2's part also says "in the pause", so "during" is the odd one out.

Fix (sections/coda.ily line 29, codaTimp, part tag): `"(for the Scherzo, during the pause)"` → `"(for the Scherzo, in the pause)"`.
