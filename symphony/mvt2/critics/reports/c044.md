## Findings

The orchestration of v44 already follows almost every principle in this part of the chapter. The three findings below are local, and each one is fixed by a small change.

### 1. Bar 79: the sustained horns 3·4 are marked ff while horns 1·2 play the counter-melody f (applied)

**Problem.** At the A′ peak, c034 lowered horns 1·2 to f so that the counter-melody D♭4–C4–B♭3 (with Vc and Bsn 1) stays under the theme. Horns 3·4 still enter ff on F4/A♭3 and hold them through 80. These are sustained harmony notes, they are in the same register as the counter-melody (A♭3 is its last note, F4 is a third above its first), and they are in the same instrument. So the background horns are now marked louder than the middleground horns around them. With four horns in that octave, the louder pair makes the counter-melody's line less clear, which is what c034's change was meant to prevent.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 184: Schoenberg gives "the principal gestures" a louder dynamic than "the subordinate ones". PDF 179 (*Scheherazade*): foreground, middleground and background each need their own weight. PDF 159, rule 1: melody notes must be more prominent than harmony notes. Here the harmony notes sit one dynamic level above the counter-melody.

**Fix (applied).** In `sections/aTwo.ily`: `aTwoHnThree = { R2.*8 | f'2.\f | ...` and `aTwoHnFour = { R2.*8 | aes2.\f | ...`. Both players change together, so the shared staff keeps equal dynamics. Pitches, the hairpin at 81 and the exit at 82 are unchanged. The chord is still full. Its upper octave keeps Fl 2, Ob 2, violas div. and Cl 2 at ff.

### 2. Bars 45–46: Vln II holds D♭4, which is the first note of the cellos' theme line (applied)

**Problem.** At 45, theme B's head in B♭ minor is in oboe 1 (D♭5) with the cellos an octave lower in tenor clef (D♭4–E♭4–D♭4–C4, B♭3–A♭3–B♭3–D♭4). Vln II enters pp on D♭4 and holds it for two bars. So the melody's first note and its return at 46 b3 are doubled at pitch by a sustained string chord note. Cellos and second violins are the same family, so the cello melody has nothing to set it apart at the very pitch where it starts and ends. The held D♭4 also covers the cello line's movement around it (E♭4, C4).

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 165: "doublings at pitch usually result in the loss of the individual color characteristics of both instruments", and "sustained doublings tire the ear". PDF 159, rule 1. PDF 168–169 (Schubert 8): melody and harmony separate when "scored with opposing timbres and differing designs". The sustained D♭4 has neither: same family, same pitch.

**Fix (applied).** In `sections/bSec.ily`, Vln II 45–46: `f'1\pp^\markup \italic "unis., ord." | f'2 ges'2\< |` (was `des'1 | des'2 des'2\<`). F4 is the fifth of B♭ minor. At 46 b3 the bass falls to G♭, and G♭4 is the root of that chord. It then falls a semitone to E♯4 at 47 (same pitch as F4), as the D♭4 did. The B♭ minor and G♭ chords are now complete in the strings (F3/F4 + B♭ in the bass; B♭3, D♭4, G♭4 over G♭). I checked by hand: no parallel fifths or octaves with the oboe, cellos, violas, Bsn 2 or the bass. B♭3/F4 to D♭4/G♭4 against the cellos is a fifth to a fourth. The swell to mp at 47 (c043) is unchanged.

### 3. Bar 47: the cellos hold C♯4 through the cor anglais' pickup C♯4, the first note of the movement I quotation (applied)

**Problem.** In 47 the oboe ends its phrase on C♯5 (`cis''2. r4`). The cellos, an octave lower, hold `cis'1\mf\>` for the whole bar. On beat 4 the cor anglais enters p on C♯4 with the pickup of the one quotation of movement I's second theme. The cellos are still sounding that pitch (mf diminuendo, not yet pp). So the quotation's first note starts as a unison with a fading string note and is not heard as an entry. The oboe already stops before beat 4; the cellos do not.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 184: Schoenberg "is careful to assign each fragment to an instrument that will be clearly heard in the complex texture". PDF 165: an at-pitch doubling takes away both instruments' colour. PDF 168–169: separate ideas need contrasting timbre and design. A held note of the same pitch gives neither. (c034's PDF 156 point, a rest before a colour enters, says the same.)

**Fix (applied).** In `sections/bSec.ily`, Vc 47: `cis'2.\mf\> r4\! |` (was `cis'1\mf\>`). The cellos now stop with the oboe. C♯ stays in the bass (Cb C♯2) and in the cor anglais. The chord on beat 4 still has E♯4 (Vln II), B3 (Vla) and G♯2 (Bsn 2). The cellos come back on C♯3 at 48 as before.
