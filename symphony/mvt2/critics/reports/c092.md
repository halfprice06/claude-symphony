## Findings

### 1. Bars 59–60: the ♯4̂ bass G falls a major 7th to A♭ instead of rising to it, and Bsn 2 crosses Bsn 1 (applied)

**Problem.** The bass of the build is G♭–G–A♭ (♯4̂–5̂ under vii°7/V → V7). Cb (G2→A♭1), B.cl. and Bsn 2 (both G3→A♭2) all leave the leading tone of V by a descending major 7th. No instrument takes G up to A♭ in the same register. A&S exclude dissonant leaps from plain textures (PDF 82) and allow no leap past the octave (PDF 81). The bass's half-step, the point of a ♯4̂–5̂ approach, cannot be heard. Bsn 2's G3 also sits above Bsn 1's E♭3/F♭3 for two beats on their shared staff, a crossing A&S say to avoid (PDF 88, Ex. 5-30).

**Evidence.** MIDI 59 b3: Cb G2, B.cl. G3, Bsn 1 E♭3, Bsn 2 G3. 60 b1: Cb A♭1, B.cl. A♭2, Bsn 2 A♭2. My crossing check finds this as the only crossing on any shared staff in the movement.

**Fix (applied, bSec.ily).** Take bar 59 down an octave, so that G♭–G–A♭ rises by step into the tuba's A♭1:
- Cb: `ges,,2(\f g,,2) | aes,,1\upbow\<` (was `ges,2(\f g,2)`).
- B.cl. and Bsn 2: `ges,2\f g,2 | aes,1\< \breathe` (was `ges2\f g2`).

This keeps the protected "♯4 bass at 59" and "57–60 bass ascent", and makes the ascent literal.

### 2. Bars 62–63: Vln I's upper half moves E6→A6 in octaves with the bass E→A (applied)

**Problem.** Since c059 Vln I plays undivided F6–F♭6 at 62. It then divides at 63, and the upper half leaps a 4th, F♭6(E6)→A6, while the bass (Cb, Vc, B.tbn.) leaps E→A. These are consecutive octaves in one pair of voices, both moving by leap (PDF 83, Ex. 5-18). A&S say leaps in both voices make such motion more noticeable (PDF 87, rule 7). They also say Bach avoids even hidden outer octaves unless the soprano moves by step (PDF 88, Ex. 5-29b "poor"). Flute 1's G♯6→A6 above is correct; the string choir's top line is not. c052 had the right line (upper half G♯6→A6); c059's undivided bar 62 lost it.

**Evidence.** MIDI 62 b3: Vln I E6, bass E1/E2. 63 b1: Vln I A6 + E6, bass A1/A2.

**Fix (applied, bSec.ily).** Divide on 62 b3 only, so 61–62 b2 stays undivided as c059 wanted:
`des'''1--\ff^\markup \italic "molto sostenuto" | f'''2-- << { aes'''2--^\markup \italic "div." } \\ { fes'''2-- } >> |`
`<< { a'''1:32\sf\> } \\ { e'''1:32 } >> |` (the "div." at 63 is dropped).

Now the upper half goes G♯6→A6 by step in unison with Fl 1, and the lower half keeps the F♭–E thread as a common tone.

### 3. Bars 40 and 56: the leading tone A♮ of vii°7/vi is doubled at the octave by the arpeggio and resolves with the bass (applied)

**Problem.** In bar 40 the viola triplets (A3–C4–E♭4–C4–A3–E♭4), and in bar 56 the harp RH (same notes), put A3 twice over the bass A. The A is the root of vii°7/vi and the leading tone of B♭ minor. A&S call doubling the leading tone of a leading-tone chord "virtually absolute[ly]" wrong (PDF 76, Ex. 5-4). The figure's low note then goes to B♭3 with the bass A→B♭, so the doubling also produces octaves on resolution (PDF 83). In bar 56 the chord also lacks its E♭ (A–C–G♭ only), and A&S say complete chords are best (PDF 76).

**Evidence.** Vla 40: `a8( c' es' c' a es')` → `bes8(...)`, under Cb/harp LH A1/A2 → B♭. Harp RH 56: `a8 c' es' c' a es'` → `bes8...`, under Vc/Cb A → B♭.

**Fix (applied, bSec.ily).**
- Vla 40: `\tuplet 3/2 4 { c'8( es' ges' es' c' es') }`.
- Harp RH 56: `\tuplet 3/2 4 { c'8 es' ges' es' c' es' }`.

The A stays in the bass only. G♭4 completes the chord at 56 and doubles Vln II's G♭4 at 40 at the unison, which stays in place. The harp no longer needs A♮ at 56, so I removed the "A♮" pedal mark at the end of 55 (`... bes f' }`). The composer should update the pedal-plan comment above bSecHarpR. The harp LH still needs A♮ at 40.

One side effect is left: the viola's C4 at 40 b2 and Vln II's G♭4 make a d5 that moves to B♭3/F4. A&S rate d5→P5 "less good" (PDF 83–84, Ex. 5-20b). Here the viola's E♭4 sounds between the two chords, and both voices are inner voices. I judge it much milder than the doubled leading tone.

### 4. Bars 25–26: violas and cellos move C4→B♭3 in parallel unisons (applied)

**Problem.** At 25 b3 the cello counter-melody leaps A♭3→C4 onto the violas' held C4. Both then move to B♭3 at 26. That is a parallel unison (PDF 83, Ex. 5-17, "wrong"), and the leap onto the viola's pitch is an overlap (PDF 88–89).

**Evidence.** MIDI 25 b3: Va C4/E♭4, Vc C4. 26 b1: Va B♭3/E♭4, Vc B♭3.

**Fix (applied, aOne.ily).** Violas at 25: `<aes es'>2.` (was `<c' es'>2.`). The lower viola line becomes C4–B♭3–A♭3–B♭3–C4 (23–27), all by step. The cello now meets the viola's B♭3 by contrary motion. The chord's 3rd stays in the melody (C5), and the cello brings C4 on b3. The cello counter-melody (protected) is unchanged.

### 5. Bars 77–78: violas and cellos move D♭4→C♭4 in parallel unisons at the deceptive cadence (applied)

**Problem.** The viola's last eighth of 77 (D♭4) doubles the cello's D♭4. Both then move to C♭4 on the ♭VI downbeat of 78 (PDF 83, Ex. 5-17). I first tried B♭3 as the viola's last eighth of 77. That made parallel 12ths with the bass (B♭3/E♭2 → C♭4/F♭2), so the fix belongs on 78's first eighth.

**Fix (applied, aTwo.ily).** Vla 78: `aes(\f fes' aes') fes'( c' aes)` (was `ces'(\f ...`). The viola goes D♭4→A♭3, and the cello's C♭4 keeps the 5th of F♭. My scan finds no new parallels.

### 6. Bars 42 b3 and 58 b3: the 7th G♭ of A♭7 is doubled in the viola and rises (applied)

**Problem.** In both bars the melody holds G♭ (cor anglais G♭4 at 42; Fl/Vln/Ob G♭ in octaves at 58) as the 7th of V7, prepared for the 4–3 into D♭. The viola's triplet starts on G♭3 as well, and its next low note is A♭3, so its copy of the 7th rises. A&S: the 7th "must never be doubled" (PDF 76). c042 already removed a doubled G♭ at 53 for the same reason.

**Fix (applied, bSec.ily).** Vla 42 b3: `\tuplet 3/2 4 { aes8 c' es' }`. Vla 58 b3: `\tuplet 3/2 4 { aes8( c' es') }` (both were `ges8`). The viola now holds A♭3 into b4, and the suspension stays the melody's alone.

### 7. Bar 29: horn 3 adds a third copy of the leading tone G (applied)

**Problem.** In the V7 at 29 the leading tone G sounds in the melody (Vln I/II G5), in the violas (G4) and in horn 3 (G3), which then leaps down to E♭3. c052 chose G3→E♭3 to avoid octaves, but the reading (PDF 76) says not to double the leading tone at all. The violas' G4→A♭4 is the clean resolution, so the horn's copy is the extra one. My first version (B♭3→A♭3) made octaves with the melody's passing B♭4→A♭4, so I changed it.

**Fix (applied, aOne.ily).** `aOneHnThree = { R2.*22 | bes2.\pp | c'2. | }` and `aOneHnFour = { R2.*22 | es2.\pp~ | es2. | }`. Bar 29 has E♭3/B♭3 and bar 30 has E♭3/C4. The horns stay in the middle register, the tonic at 30 has its 3rd, and my scan finds no parallels.
