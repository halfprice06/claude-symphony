## Findings

### 1. The Tbn 1 quotation at 63–64 should take movement I's current articulation of the P head (applied; quotation change, tell the other threads)
**Bars:** 63 b3–64 b1.
**Problem:** v240 has `c'4.->) b8 bes8 a8 | a4 gis8( a8)`. The falling chromatic eighths B3–B♭3–A3 are tongued separately, and the repeated A3 is plain. Since its v239, movement I slurs the falling eighths into the repeated note and marks that note portato. v251's tOne has `d''8( des''8 c''8 | c''4--) b'8( c''8)`, and trans and dev are the same. Every other feature of our quotation already copies movement I: the slurred pickup into the syncopated accent (c148), the slurred turn, the rhythm and the intervals. The articulation is now the one difference.
**Evidence:** A quotation works when the listener recognises an "older sound" inside a new one (Sebesky PDF 241–242, the Rochberg passage). By the late-Romantic orchestra "every note, chord, timbre, and nuance" belonged to the composition (Adler PDF 22), and articulation is part of the head's identity (Adler PDF 19: colour gives the material its personality). Movement I's slur over the chromatic descent also groups 63 b3–64 b1 as one gesture, and the portato marks the repeated note as a light upbeat to the turn, not a new stress. I checked the context. The quotation is ff marcato over sf > p tremolo (63–64). The slurred notes B3 (4th position), B♭3 (1st) and A3 (2nd) are a normal legato-tongued descent. The accents on C4 and F3 and the closing diminuendo to p (c218) are unchanged, and the slur ends inside the bar before the turn, so the breath plan is unchanged.
**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-240/work/sections/bSec.ily):**
`r8 … e8(\ff … c'4.->) b8( bes8 a8 | a4--) gis8( a8) f4.->\> e8\p |`
Pitches, rhythm and dynamics are unchanged, and the MIDI is identical. This changes the articulation of an exported quotation, so the composer should update coherence/mvt2.md's quotation row and tell the movement I thread that the match is made. The finale and scherzo do not quote this passage.

### 2. In the render, the last motto tail (85–88) is the softest line under its own accompaniment (applied, MIDI only)
**Bars:** 85–89.
**Problem:** The print gives Cl 1 niente < pp at 85–86 over a ppp pedal and ppp violas, then pp at 87–88 with pp chords. The tail is meant to sit one level above the pedal. score.midi gives the clarinet velocities 22 (E♭5) and 29 (D5, D♭5) against 31–37 for the violas, 37 for the horns, 41 for Vln II, 44–50 for the cellos and 50–57 for the basses. The cause is LilyPond's equalizer: the clarinet range is 0–0.7 and the cello range 0.2–0.8, so the same printed level comes out about 20 velocity points lower in the clarinet. This is the close that the scherzo (B–A♯–A–G♯, its 357–360) and the finale (G–F♯–F–E, its 273–275) answer.
**Evidence:** Sebesky's red-pencil check, comparing the score with the recorded result and asking whether the voicing sounds as intended (PDF 240–241, 242). c160 used the same passage to fix the opposite error here (v56, too loud).
**Fix (applied in coda.ily, nothing printed):** `\set Staff.midiMinimumVolume = #0.2 \set Staff.midiMaximumVolume = #0.9` before Cl 1's E♭5 at 85, and on 88 beat 3 `<< des''2.-- { s2 \unset Staff.midiMinimumVolume \unset Staff.midiMaximumVolume s4-\tweak stencil ##f \pp } >>`. A dynamic placed directly at 89 clashes with Cl 2's hidden ppp on the shared staff and gives two warnings, so the reset is on a spacer at 88 b3. Result: 85 = 47, 86–88 = 54, then C5 at 89 = 29, under Vln I's C5 at 41 as it takes the note over. Cl 2 at 89 is unchanged (22). Side effect: Cl 1's velocities at 76–77 change by 1–3 points (39→38, 40→38, 44→41), because a hairpin there is interpolated toward the next dynamic. That is audible as nothing, and I note it for completeness.

### 3. In the render, the cor anglais's quotation of movement I's S head (47–53) is quieter than its pp bass (applied, MIDI only)
**Bars:** 47 b4–53.
**Problem:** The print gives C.a. p "solo, espr." over a pp accompaniment (c058). score.midi has the C.a. at 37–44, against cellos and basses at 50 and Vln II at 41. The C.a. range 0–0.7 was set by c228, and the cellos and basses have floors of 0.2.
**Evidence:** As Finding 2 (Sebesky PDF 240–241). Adler PDF 19: the colour of a cyclic idea is what makes it recognisable, so it has to be heard.
**Fix (applied in bSec.ily, nothing printed):** `\set Staff.midiMinimumVolume = #0.15 \set Staff.midiMaximumVolume = #0.9` before `cis'4(\p` at 47, then `\unset` both on `f'2` at 53. The C.a. now sounds at 59–66, one step above the pp strings. No other part changes.

### 4. In the render, the double basses keep a p velocity through the ppp pedal at 83–86 (applied, MIDI only)
**Bars:** 82–86.
**Problem:** The basses' A♭1 is tied from 82 (p >) into the coda's ppp (c063, protected tie). A held MIDI note keeps its attack velocity, so the pedal sounds at 57 under the harp harmonics (53) and the timpani answer (54), louder than either cyclic idea it supports.
**Fix (applied in coda.ily):** `\set Staff.midiExpression = #0.75` at 83 and `#1.0` at 87 (the new F1). The bar-91 fade in global.ily resets CC11 later, as before. Nothing is printed.

### 5. The harp's opening pedal setting needs no change (recorded)
Movement I v251 leaves the harp at D♮ C♮ B♭ | E♮ F♮ G♮ A♭ (recTwo: `pedBflatAflat`, then A♮ and back to A♭; no later diagram). Our part-only setting at bar 1 is D♭ C B♭ | E♭ F G A♭ (`"^-^|^--^"`, intro.ily), and so is the first score diagram at 7. With B already on B♭, the harpist changes only D (left foot) and E (right foot), together, during six bars of rest (about 21 s at crotchet = 52). The movement never needs B♮; I checked every harp pitch. Keep the part's "set pedals during the rests" diagram as it is (c116; Adler PDF 112). The crash cymbal removed from movement I does not concern us, since we have no percussion.
