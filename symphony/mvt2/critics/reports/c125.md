## Findings

### 1. Bar 22: a part-only instruction prints in the score and spreads the bar (applied)
**Problem.** `aOneVlnOne` carries `-\tag #'part _\markup "(solo player: mute on, join at bar 24)"`. score.ly removes the `'part` tag from many winds but not from Violins I, so the line prints in the score. It runs from the eighth on beat 3 to the final barline of the system and touches it. To fit the text, LilyPond widened the last column. At 300 dpi, beat 3 → 3½ (an eighth) is 265 px, beat 2 → 3 (a quarter) is 95 px, and the half on beat 1 is narrower than the eighth after it. Bar 22 was 385 px against 150–215 px for the other bars of 15–22 (150 dpi), even though it holds the same rhythms as bar 15.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 54 shows exactly this rhythm as "not acceptable": each longer value should get more space than its shorter neighbour, or at least as much. PDF 54 also says equal durations get equal space across a system, and PDF 55 says no characters should collide. c065 moved the "tutti" text left for the same reason but did not see the part-only line.
**Fix.** score.ly: `<< \global \removeWithTag #'part \vlnOne >>`. aOne.ily: give the score copy of the "tutti (gli altri con sord.), espr., sul D" markup `-\tag #'score -\tweak X-offset #-16`, and keep the part's copy at `-9` with `-\tag #'part`. Bars 15–22 are now 175–240 px each, bar 22 is 205 px, its two eighths are equally spaced, and the text clears the barline. The Violin I part is unchanged.

### 2. Bars 61–62: tenutos sit on the stem ends on the shared wind staves (applied)
**Problem.** At the climax, every stem-side tenuto touches its stem and reads as a bracket ("⌐" or "L"). This happens in Ob 1·2 (62 b1 and b3, both voices), Cl 2 (61 b1 and b3), Bn 1 (61 b1 and b3, 62 b1 and b3) and Bn 2 (62 b1). LilyPond pads tenutos by only 0.2 space. c065's `-\tweak padding #0.6` on Bn 2 at 62 b3 did not visibly separate it either. Inside the staff, the Fl 2 tenutos were already a full space clear.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 55: characters at least ½ space apart, none colliding. PDF 65: on a shared staff, the lower part's marks go with its down-stems, so these tenutos have to sit beyond the stem.
**Fix (score.ly, applied).** A `Script.Y-offset` callback in the Voice context. When a script is on the stem side of a stemmed note and less than 0.5 space from the stem end, it moves out to 0.6 space clear. Inside the stave it moves a whole space, so a quantized tenuto stays in a space.
```lilypond
#(define (stem-side-y-offset grob)
  (let* ((y (ly:side-position-interface::y-aligned-side grob))
         (nc (ly:grob-parent grob X))
         (stem (if (ly:grob? nc) (ly:grob-object nc 'stem) '()))
         (dir (ly:grob-property grob 'direction)))
    (if (and (ly:grob? stem) (>= (ly:grob-property stem 'duration-log 0) 1)
             (eqv? dir (ly:grob-property stem 'direction)))
        (let* ((ref (ly:grob-parent grob Y)) (se (ly:grob-extent stem ref Y))
               (st (ly:grob-property grob 'stencil))
               (ex (if (ly:stencil? st) (ly:stencil-extent st Y) '(0 . 0)))
               (gap (if (> dir 0) (- (+ y (car ex)) (cdr se)) (- (car se) (+ y (cdr ex))))))
          (if (< gap 0.5) (+ y (* dir (if (< (abs y) 2.4) 1.0 (- 0.6 gap)))) y))
        y)))
% in \layout: \context { \Voice ... \override Script.Y-offset = #stem-side-y-offset }
```
A page-by-page image diff shows the only other visible changes are small: the up-bow at Vln I 80 and the string fermatas at 92 sit slightly higher. Notehead-side marks do not move. If the composer prefers local edits, the same result comes from `-\tweak Y-offset` on the eleven tenutos in bSec.ily 61–62.

### 3. Harp RH 7–14 and 71–82: two-note rolls look like quarter rests (applied)
**Problem.** A roll on a third or fourth (`<c es>2\arpeggio`, `<bes, es>2\arpeggio`, `<es aes>4\arpeggio`) is drawn about two spaces tall. That is the height and shape of a crotchet rest. Most of these chords follow `r4`, so bars 7, 10, 11 and 72–73 read at a glance as `r4 r4 <chord>2`, which is four beats in 3/4.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 56: a symbol must not be placed where it can be "mistaken for one" of another kind. Her case is an accidental too near the key signature. PDF 55 asks for evenly spaced, unambiguous symbols.
**Fix (score.ly, Harp RH staff, applied).** Lengthen each RH roll by one space at each end. Cross-staff rolls (12, 78) are unaffected.
```lilypond
\override Arpeggio.positions = #(lambda (grob) (let ((p (ly:arpeggio::calc-positions grob))) (cons (- (car p) 1) (+ (cdr p) 1))))
```

### 4. Hn 3·4 bars 3–6: one held chord printed with stems that flip direction (applied)
**Problem.** Bars 3–6 are one four-bar sustained pair (Hn 3 C–C–D♭–C over Hn 4's A♭3), every note a dotted half. Because only Hn 3's C is tied 3→4, the part-combiner splits 3–4 into two voices (C stem up, A♭ stem down) and then prints 5–6 as one-stem chords (stem up). The same rhythm changes appearance halfway through the phrase.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 60: notes of the same duration combine onto one stem, and separate stems are for different durations. PDF 61: aim for one stem direction "within a phrase, bar or half-bar". Both chords lie below the middle line, so the stem goes up throughout (PDF 60).
**Fix (intro.ily, applied).** `introHnThree = { … | \partCombineChords c'2.~\pp | c'2. | des'2. | c'2 r4 \partCombineAutomatic | }`. In chord mode the combiner draws a tie on Hn 4's re-struck A♭ as well, so the A♭ tie at bar 3 gets a stencil filter (`hnFourNoTie`, defined in intro.ily). Only the C is tied on the page. Side effect: in score.midi, Hn 4's pp A♭ at bar 4 is now held rather than re-struck (bars 1–4 go from 18 to 17 attacks). The printed part for Horn 4 is unchanged.

### 5. Bar 92: the cor anglais rest has no fermata (applied; outside the assigned reading)
The cor anglais staff is on page 8. Clarinets and Hn 3·4 carry fermatas on their final rests, but the cor anglais does not. Fix (coda.ily): `R2.*3 | R2.\fermata |`. This is a consistency point and not from Gould 53–68.
