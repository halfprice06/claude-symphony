## Findings

**1. Bars 29–30: Hn 3's D♭4–C4 is tongued, though it is the resolution of the V7's seventh.** (applied)
Problem: Hn 3 enters on D♭4 at 29 b3 (c094, the seventh of V7) and resolves to C4 at 30, over Hn 4's held E♭3. The two notes have no slur, so the resolution is re-tongued. Everything around it is slurred: cellos, violas and both violin sections, which close p>pp at 30. This is the first time the horns sound under the strings, and the figure is the 4̂–3̂ sigh that the coda's plagal close (D♭–C on top) brings back.
Evidence: Adler PDF 309: "All phrases under one slur will automatically be performed on one breath", and a lyrical line is soft-tongued (Tchaikovsky 5/ii, X-24, every step slurred). Every soft horn choir in the chapter is slurred voice by voice (Humperdinck X-16, "sehr weich", PDF 306; Debussy X-17, PDF 307). c188 made the same change at 80–81 and 87–90 for the same reason.
Fix (aOne.ily, line 110): `r2 des'4(-\tag #'part \pp -\tag #'score -\tweak stencil ##f \pp | c'2.) |`. Pitches, dynamics and the hidden MIDI pp (c160) are unchanged. The slur prints on Hn 3's own voice, because the staff is already split at 29.

**2. Bars 3–6: Hn 3's C4–D♭4–C4 over the A♭3 pedal is tongued note by note.** (applied; inside a protected passage)
Problem: Bars 3–6 are the horns' first soft choir: Hn 3 C4 (tied 3–4), D♭4 at 5, C4 at 6, over Hn 4's A♭3 tied 3–6. The plan calls this the preview of the plagal close, and coherence/mvt2.md says the muted Hn 3·4 echo it at 87–90. Since c188 the echo at 87–90 is slurred; the original at 3–6 is not. So the preview and the echo are articulated differently, and the neighbour note at 5 is re-tongued at ppp–pp under the fading strings.
Evidence: Adler PDF 309 (slur = one breath, soft tonguing for lyrical lines); PDF 306, X-16 (soft four-horn choir slurred phrase by phrase); PDF 307, X-17 (soft horn pairs slurred). At ♩=52 the four bars last about 14 s at pp, one breath in the middle register (PDF 303).
Answer to the protection: bars 1–6 are protected for the common-tone link and its pitches (c001, c290). The wind layout at 3–6 is protected too (c287). A slur changes neither, and score.midi is byte-identical after the change. Hn 4's breath at the end of bar 2 stays.
Fix (intro.ily, line 23): `\partCombineChords c'2.~(\pp | c'2. | des'2. | c'2) r4 \partCombineAutomatic |`. In chord mode the slur prints under the chords and the ties stay. I checked score page 1 at 150 dpi: there is no collision, and Hn 4's ties and "4." label are intact.

**3. Bars 60–61: Hn 4 enters p< while the low brass enter pp<.** (recorded, no change)
At 60, Hn 4's A♭3 is p< and Tbn 2, B.tbn and tuba are pp<, all crescendoing to f at 61. Adler PDF 303 and 311 support a slightly stronger middle-register horn entry against low brass. c218 recorded this as a deliberate exception with a stated reason. The reading gives no new reason to change it.

**4. Bars 61–62: Hn 3·4 in unison on the top line at f, with Hn 1·2 below them at ff.** (recorded, no change)
The voicing inverts Adler's high/low pairing (PDF 303, X-6), and the softer marking is on the higher line. The layout and the brass dynamics at 61–62 are protected (c174, c286, c287, c314). At written E♭5–G♭5, Hn 3·4 are in the "bright and heroic" band of X-5 (PDF 303), so f there balances Hn 1·2's ff in the middle register. No change is recommended.

**5. Bars 63–64: Tbn 1 quotation "marcato" ff with accents.** (recorded, no change)
Strauss marks a loud brass melody "ff molto espr." (X-12, PDF 305), but the quotation is movement I's first-theme head, a motif rather than a cantabile line. It lies in the trombone's "very strong" middle register (X-69, PDF 329), and its slurs B–B♭–A and G♯–A move between neighbouring slide positions (PDF 329, X-70). This is consistent with c158 and c188.

**6. Mute plan 82–91.** (recorded, no change)
"mettere sord." at 82 leaves about 17 s before "con sord." at 87. "via sord." at 91 comes in a rest bar, and the scherzo prints "senza sord." at Hn 3·4's first entry. This follows PDF 307–308 and 321. The coda dynamic (pp con sord., MIDI 0.7) matches Debussy's muted horns at pp (X-17).

**7. Stopped horn.** (recorded, no change)
Adler (PDF 308) recommends stopped horn for single soft notes and `sf>p`. The only `sf>p` in the movement is the B.tbn and string attack at 63, inside the protected quotation (c303: "sf-p at 63 is the sforzando device"). Adding a stopped horn there would change the scoring of a protected bar for a colour the movement does not need. I do not recommend it.
