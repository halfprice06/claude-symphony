## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 7–14 and 31–36 have no bar numbers (applied)
Problem: the bar numbers are engraved on the double-bass staff (`\consists "Bar_number_engraver"` in its `\with`), and `\RemoveEmptyStaves` hides that staff when the basses rest. Two whole systems (page 1, system 2; page 2, system 3) therefore have no bar number at all, not even at the start of the system. These are the horn theme and the bass-clarinet transition, where a conductor is most likely to stop and restart.
Evidence: MOLA PDF 4, "All full scores should include measure numbers … If every measure is to be numbered it should be placed below the system."
Fix (score.ly): keep the double-bass numbers. The lowest staff of each of the two systems carries numbers for its own bars only:
```
bnBelow = #(define-scheme-function (from to) (integer? integer?)
  #{ \with { \consists "Bar_number_engraver"
      barNumberVisibility = #(lambda (n pos) (and (>= n from) (<= n to)))
      \override BarNumber.direction = #DOWN … (same overrides as the Cb staff) } #})
\new Staff = "Contrabassoon" \with \bnBelow 31 36 \with { … }
\new Staff = "Harp LH" \with \bnBelow 7 14 \with { … }
```
Two simpler routes, which I tested and rejected: Score-level numbers with `direction = #DOWN` either sit between the double basses and their dynamics, or follow the skyline unevenly and touch the hairpins; and a `Dynamics` line holding the Bar_number_engraver gives 184 "cyclic dependency" programming errors. If the scoring of 7–14 or 31–36 changes, update the two bar ranges.

### 2. Rehearsal letters and tempo marks are missing above the strings at 37, 45 and 83 (applied)
Problem: the Mark_engraver and Metronome_mark_engraver for the strings sit on the Violins I staff. When the Vln I staff is hidden (37–52, 83–86), "D / Poco più mosso (♩ = 60)" at 37, "E" at 45 and "K / a tempo" at 83 print only at the top of the system. Elsewhere (15, 23, 53, 61, 65, 71) the score prints them twice, so here the string players' position in the score has no landmark exactly where the tempo changes.
Evidence: MOLA PDF 4: "All tempo indications should appear above the top staff and above the first violin line (or similarly positioned staff in the absence of strings)"; rehearsal letters at landmarks.
Fix (global.ily + sections): an event function placed on the highest sounding string staff.
```
strMark = #(define-event-function (letter tempo) (string? markup?)
  #{ -\tweak outside-staff-priority #1000 -\tweak X-offset #-1
     ^\markup \column { \override #'(box-padding . 0.5) \box \bold \fontsize #3 #letter \bold #tempo } #})
% bSec.ily Vln II 37:  <des' f'>1\pp\strMark "D" \markup { "Poco più mosso (" \fontsize #-2 \general-align #Y #DOWN \note {4} #1 "= 60)" } ^\markup …
% bSec.ily Vln II 45:  f'1\pp\strMark "E" \markup \null ^\markup …
% coda.ily Vla 83:     <c' es'>2.~\ppp\strMark "K" \markup "a tempo" ^\markup …
```
The box matches the engraved RehearsalMark. When parts are extracted, these three markups must be dropped from the Vln II/Vla parts, because the parts get the real marks.

### 3. Bar 91: the harp's ppp collides with the cross-staff arpeggio and stems (applied)
Problem: the LH staff prints its dynamics above itself (`DynamicText.direction = #UP`). At 91 the six-note rolled chord's arpeggio line runs through the gap between the staves, and the ppp is printed on top of the squiggle and against the LH stem. At 250 dpi it reads as a smudge.
Evidence: MOLA PDF 8, "placement of any 'anchored' markings … leading to collisions" (Ex. 7, PDF 12: a dynamic printed over notes).
Fix (coda.ily, codaHarpL bar 91):
```
\once \override Staff.DynamicLineSpanner.direction = #DOWN \once \override Staff.DynamicText.direction = #DOWN <aes,, es, aes,>2.\ppp\arpeggio
```

### 4. Bars 57–59: single-beat "a2" labels inside chordal writing (applied)
Problem: in Bn. 1·2 the part-combiner prints "a2" over the unison F at 57 b1, over E♭ at 58 b2 and over G♭ at 59 b1, and goes back to chords on the next beat with nothing to cancel it. Cl. 1·2 does the same at 57 b3. Three labels in three bars, each lasting one or two beats, tell the player nothing about the next chord. The trial with `\partCombineChords` was worse: it printed each unison as two noteheads side by side.
Evidence: MOLA Ex. 6 (PDF 11, Beethoven 9) uses "a 2" only for a passage that is shared for some time; Gould PDF 65: "Two parts with the same note-value may share a notehead as long as they take two stems."
Fix (bSec.ily): `\partCombineApart` at the start of bar 57 in bSecBsnOne, bSecBsnTwo, bSecClOne and bSecClTwo, and `\partCombineAutomatic` at the start of bar 61 in all four. Each unison is now one notehead with stems up and down, and the other beats are two voices. Pitches are unchanged. analysis.txt changes only in track labels (shared → one/two) and the 57–60 density count.

### 5. Bar 1: Hn 4's entry on the shared staff has no player label (applied)
Problem: "3." labels Hn 3's tied C4 from movement I. Hn 4 enters on beat 2 in a second voice with its own ppp but no label, so the staff does not say who takes over (the c030 relief).
Evidence: MOLA Ex. 6, PDF 11: paired staves name their players.
Fix (intro.ily): `introHnFour = { r4 c'2~\ppp_\markup \bold "4." | …`

### 6. No front-matter information: duration and instrumentation (not applied)
Problem: the score's only front matter is the title block. MOLA asks for an approximate duration for each movement, the full instrumentation with the key of each transposing instrument (B♭ clarinets, B♭ bass clarinet with low C, F horns, C trumpets), and an explanation of any non-standard practice. Here that means the concert-pitch score, the Cbn/Cb octave transposition, the harp's D♭1 scordatura and the timpani carried over from movement I.
Evidence: MOLA PDF 6 ("an approximate duration … a list of the full instrumentation … If there are deviations from standard musical notation, an explanation should appear following the instrumentation page").
Fix: this belongs on the symphony's front page, not inside the movement, because adding it here would push the 8-page layout. For the movement header, at most add `\small "c. 5′45″"` to `subsubtitle` (the build's audio is 348 s). Coordinate with movement I's score.

### 7. Inconsistent tempo wording, "poco allarg." vs "poco allargando" (applied)
Problem: bar 60 prints "poco allarg.", while 78 prints "poco allargando" and 63 prints "allargando".
Evidence: MOLA PDF 4 (conventional instructions) and PDF 8 (proofread horizontally).
Fix (global.ily): `\tempo \markup \medium \italic "poco allargando" \mt 62` at 60. The MIDI steps are unchanged.
