## Findings

No finding changes the score. The range is spacing and chord layout, which LilyPond computes from the notation and which forty earlier engraving cycles have already corrected where the composer had overridden it. I checked each rule in the table against the bars listed and measured the spacing rather than judging it by eye. The items below are recorded so the next pass on this range need not repeat them.

### 1. Bar 92: the dotted minims stand at the start of the bar, the rest fermatas at its centre (tested, not recommended)
**Problem.** Bar 92 holds only dotted minims and whole-bar rests. The rests and their fermatas are centred (C.a., Cl 1, Hn 3·4, timpani), while the notes and their fermatas stand about 1½ spaces after the barline. Across the last bar the fermatas are therefore at two different horizontal positions.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 55, "Notes of bar-length duration": when a bar has a single note and no other durations in any part, "the note is placed just left of the centre of the bar". The same paragraph adds: "In widely spaced bars, the note can be placed closer to the barline so as not to appear isolated." Bar 92 is about 33 staff spaces wide (245 px at 150 dpi), so the current placement is covered by that clause. c035 raised the same point for 47-48, 51, 85-86 and 88-92 and did not recommend a change. c125 did not re-raise it.
**Test.** In the scratch copy I added `\once \override Score.NoteColumn.X-offset = #N` in global.ily on bar 92's first skip, trying N = 10, 14 and 18. The build stayed clean with 8 pages. LilyPond counts the offset into the bar's width, though. At N = 18 the notes stand 1½ spaces left of the rest centre, as Gould asks, but bar 92 grows from 245 px to 355 px and takes that space from bars 87-91. Gould says such bars are "by definition, usually fairly narrow". Reducing the note head's and dots' `extra-spacing-width` as well still left the bar at 343 px, because the fermata, stem and dynamic keep their own extents. The setting would also reach the parts, because genparts.py builds partGlobalPlain from the same `\global`. I reverted it. With the offset the bar is too wide, and without it Gould's wide-bar clause already covers the placement, so I do not recommend the change.

### 2. Bars 83-86 against 87-92: four bars, then six of the same rhythms (protected layout)
Gould PDF 54 asks for a similar number of bars on adjacent systems when the rhythms are alike. The coda's two systems carry 4 and 6 bars of minims and dotted minims. As a result a dotted minim takes about 290-320 px at 83-86 and 215-290 px at 87-92 (150 dpi). The break at 87 is on the do-not-touch list (c035, c196: the staves change there, and moving the break would add empty staves to page 8). Within that constraint, each system is evenly spaced, as PDF 54 allows ("the uniform space for a note-value may need to be noticeably different from that of an adjacent system"). No change.

### 3. Double barlines (no change)
Gould PDF 53 warns that thin double barlines at every metre change "will appear to indicate new sections". Here the five double barlines all stand at key changes (36/37, 46/47, 50/51, 62/63, 64/65), and the two metre changes (37, 65) are real section starts with rehearsal letters D and H. The final barline at 92 is correct, because the scherzo follows after a pause and not attacca (coherence/mvt2.md). No change.

### 4. Measured spacing (no change)
These measurements match PDF 53-54. Crotchets are equal across the bars of each system (63 px at 100 dpi in both 41 and 42). Minims keep the same width through the poco rit. and poco sostenuto at 45-48 (195 and 198 px at 150 dpi). Bars 57-60 measure 384, 387 and 381 px, so c035's spacing section at 59-60 still does its job. In bar 63-64 the trombone's dotted crotchet gets more space than the neighbouring quavers, and bars that hold only dotted minims (32, 35) are narrower than bars of crotchets (33, 34), as Gould's unit table implies. No accidental or stem is closer to a barline or key signature than PDF 56-57 allow (E♯4 at 47: 1.5 spaces after the signature; the last sextuplet stem at 60: 2 spaces before the barline).
