## Findings

All three findings concern the parts only. The score PDF and score.midi are unchanged.

### 1. Horn 3 part, bar 29: the minim rest is raised to the fourth line. Applied.
**Problem.** aOne.ily line 112 has `\once \override Rest.staff-position = #2 r2 des'4(` for Hn 3. On the shared Hn 3·4 staff this is correct: horn 4 holds E♭3 through the bar, and Gould PDF 50 puts the upper part's rest above the centre line. The override is not tagged, though, so it also prints in the single-stave Hn 3 part. There the half rest sits on the fourth line above the A♭ with no other voice present.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 48: "the minim rest sits on the centre stave-line"; PDF 49: "Rests remain centred within the stave regardless of the pitches of surrounding notes". The rest only moves "when a beam joins notes across a rest and in double-stemmed writing". The Hn 3 part has no second voice at 29.
**Fix (applied).**
```
aOneHnThree = { R2.*22 | \tag #'score { \once \override Rest.staff-position = #2 } r2 des'4( ...
```
Score page unchanged; Hn 3 part rest now on the centre line.

### 2. Cor anglais part, bar 31: the whole-bar rest sits inside the bass-clarinet cue. Applied.
**Problem.** trans.ily line 23 uses `\transposedCueDuring #"bassClQ" #DOWN c'''`. With cue stems down, the player's semibreve rest goes up and hangs from the top line. The cue figure rises (quarter rest, then three beamed eighths from about D5 to A5 written), so the rest ends up between the second and third cue notes, about a quarter space above the second notehead, with the third and fourth notes higher than the rest. At reading distance the rest looks like part of the arpeggio.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 50: "A rest moves further from the centre of the stave or to outside the stave should parts otherwise collide". PDF 51: a rest's distance from the stave "should be on the same level as surrounding pitches", and a rest displaced by the other part "moves further away". Every other cue bar in the book keeps the rest on the side away from the cue notes. This is the only exception.
**Fix (applied).** The cue notes lie above the centre, so the cue goes up and the rest goes below:
```
\tag #'part { \transposedCueDuring #"bassClQ" #UP c''' { <>^\markup \tiny "B.cl." R2. } }
```
The rest now hangs below the stave centre, clear of the cue. The beam sits above the cue with room below the "B.cl." label. The C.a. part stays one page.

### 3. Harp part, bar 78: the 8 bassa at the new system stands under the half rest, not at the first note. Applied.
**Problem.** genparts.py forces a page break before 78 (c165). The 8 bassa line that started at 71 continues onto the new system. LilyPond starts the continuation at the bar's left edge, so the "8" prints under the LH half rest, two beats before the only LH chord (E♭1–E♭2 at 78 b3).
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 44 (image): "Beginning of a system: place the 8 ... just before or flush with the first note". PDF 42: "Place the numeral just left of the first note to which it applies". Ending the line at 77 is correct by PDF 44, which puts the corner "immediately after the last notehead(s)". A single chord after the break then takes the single-note form "8˩" (PDF 45).
**Fix (applied; part only, the score keeps its continuous line).** aTwo.ily line 131:
```
<es,, es,>2.\mp\arpeggio | \tag #'part \ottava #0 r2 \tag #'part \ottava #-1 <es,, es,>4\f\arpeggio \ottava #0 |
```
Result: in the part, the 71–77 line ends with a corner after the 77 chord. At 78 an "8˩" stands directly under the chord. Pitches and MIDI are unchanged, and the harp part is still two pages.

### 4. Recorded, no change: ledger lines and octave signs elsewhere in the parts.
Rows 10–14 hold in every part. No wind or string part needs more than five ledger lines (Vln I B♭6 at 79 is the extreme). The brass have no octave signs, and Tuba D♭1 and E♭1 at 61–62 are written plainly. Cb and Cbn use plain clefs an octave up. The only octave signs in the parts are the harp's, and they cover whole phrases. The harp harmonics at 83 are written an octave below sounding, the normal convention, and labelled "(sound 8va)". That is not an octave sign, so PDF 45–47 does not apply to them.

### 5. Recorded, no change: rests in the two-voice bars of the parts.
Rows 19–24 hold in all 23 cue bars apart from C.a. 31 (now fixed). The rests hang from the outer stave line on the side away from the cue, and none crosses the centre line. The string divisi bars (Vc 18, 21; Vla 25–28, 80–81, 84–92) have no rest in one voice only: where both halves rest they share one rest (Vla 27 b3, 85 b1–2), as PDF 51 asks.
