## Findings

### 1. Parts: rehearsal letters stacked above the tempo word, and floating under the system above (MOLA Ex. 4, Ex. 7; PDF 5, 8)
Bars 37, 53, 61, 65, 83 in most parts. Ex. 4 prints each boxed letter at the barline with the tempo word beside it on the same line ("[D] … Etwas bewegter", "[E] Tempo I"). Our parts print the letter one line higher than the tempo word. Each stacked pair takes an extra line of height. In the tightly spaced viola part (padding 0.5 since c221) this pushes the markings down into the gap above the next system, so they read as belonging to the line above. Ex. 7 shows this fault ("a tempo" with letter F floating between lines). In the viola v225 part, D (37) sits under bar 27, F and "a tempo, poco a poco animando" (53) sit under bars 48–49, and H/J (65/71) sit under the ff of 61.
Fix (applied, genparts.py only): partMerge now passes the flattened global through `beside-letter`. When a `\mark` is followed by a `\tempo` with no music between them, it inserts `\once \override Score.MetronomeMark.X-offset = #3.6 \once \override Score.MetronomeMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #0.2` before the tempo. Letters are left-aligned on the barline (`\override RehearsalMark.self-alignment-X = #LEFT`). Result: D/Poco più mosso, F/a tempo, G/Largamente and K/a tempo now share a line in most parts (Cl 1, Bsn 1, Hn, low brass, harp, viola G). Where a letter also meets the previous bar's tempo word (G after "poco allargando" 60, H after "allargando" 64), the letter still goes above, as before. Score unchanged.

### 2. Parts: letters not boxed as in the score (MOLA PDF 5 "must match the score"; Ex. 4)
All parts. The score uses `format-mark-box-letters` (c045). The parts used the default plain bold letter, so the conductor's "[F]" and the player's "F" differ in form, and a plain bold F next to a bold "4" multi-rest number is easy to misread. Ex. 4 boxes them in the part.
Fix (applied): `rehearsalMarkFormatter = #format-mark-box-letters` in the parts' `\Score` context (genparts.py header).

### 3. Clarinet 1 part: rest 71–76 split into "4 2" for no visible reason (MOLA Ex. 3–4; c165 rule)
Bar 75. aTwoClOne starts `R2.*4 | \partCombineSoloII R2.*2 |`. The part-combiner switch only matters in the score, but in the part it ends the merged multi-rest, so the clarinettist counts 4 + 2 with nothing printed at 75. The same switches are at 57–58 and 61–62 (bSec.ily 119–132), 79 (aTwo 58), 1 and 87 (intro 19, coda 73).
Fix (applied): partMerge drops `partCombineForced` property sets (`pc-switch?`), as it already drops the MIDI `\mt` steps. Cl 1 now prints J "6". No other part changes its bar count; all remaining splits fall on letters, tempo words or key changes.

### 4. Double-bass part: "change bow at will" (2) collides with the multi-rest number at 4 (MOLA PDF 8, Ex. 7)
Bar 2–4, Cb part line 1: the text runs into the "3" of the rest 4–6 ("change bow at will3"). Text scripts take no horizontal space by default, and the following multi-rest is short.
Fix (applied, intro.ily): `aes,,2.\pp~-\tweak extra-spacing-width #'(0 . 0) -\tweak extra-spacing-height #'(-inf.0 . +inf.0) ^\markup \italic "change bow at will"` (the `\textLengthOn` effect for this one text). In the part the text now ends before the bar line. The score has six bars across A3 on that system, so it looks the same; score.midi is byte-identical.

### 5. Viola part: tempo words still float at the bottom line (MOLA PDF 8, Ex. 7; PDF 7 page turns)
Bar 89 ("rit.") and 53, viola part only. After findings 1–2, "rit." (89) still prints directly under the hairpin of 76–77 on line 7. It sits above two stacked string texts at 85–89 ("div., change bow at will", "lower half sul C", "change bow at will"), and the system gap is at its 0.5 minimum. I tested padding 1, 1.5 and 2: LilyPond goes to two pages (31 total), and with `page-count = 1` it compresses with "too few pages" warnings. Tighter horizontal spacing (`spacing-increment` 1.0–1.1) keeps 8 systems. Making the 89 "change bow at will" score-only did not lower the "rit." either, because "lower half sul C" still overlaps it, so I reverted that.
Recommendation (not applied; it touches c165/c221's one-page viola rule): reconsider the one-page rule. MOLA PDF 7 allows a turn "in a period of rest". The violas rest 31–36 (six bars of Adagio, about 20 s, after "via sord."). A two-page part turning there, with padding 1.5, puts every marking next to its own staff. If the one-page rule stands, the fallback is `\tag #'part` placing "lower half sul C" below the staff at 87 (`_\markup`), which frees the space above 89.

### 6. Parts: "G" below "Largamente" at a line start (Bsn 1, 61) (Ex. 4; Gould: letter is the outermost mark)
Bsn 1 line 4 starts at 61. At a line start the letter sits on the system's left edge and the tempo word goes above it, while every other part prints the letter above. This is the same as v225 and is not made worse by finding 1. Fix if wanted: a part-only `\break` one bar earlier in Bsn 1 is not available (automatic breaks), so either accept it or add `\once \override Score.RehearsalMark.outside-staff-priority = #2000` at 61 for parts. It is minor, so I recorded it and made no change.

Checked and recorded, no change: score pages 1–8 against Ex. 1, 2 and 6 (names, order, brackets, barlines, a2 labels, tempo marks on top and over strings, bar numbers below); cautionaries at line ends in the parts (Ex. 3); all tempo and metre changes and all ten letters in all 25 parts (Ex. 4); mute and muta changes (Ex. 5); cue set (c135/c165).
