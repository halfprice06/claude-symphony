## Findings

The score and parts already follow MOLA on almost every point. What is left are three small vertical-proofreading and placement items. None of them is large.

### 1. Bars 64-65, bass trombone: the diminuendo has no end (applied)

Problem: at 64 b3-4 the bass trombone's hairpin (`d,4\> e,4 <>\!`) runs to the barline and stops with no dynamic and no niente sign. The bass trombone rests from 65. Every other diminuendo in the same bar has an end: Tbn 1 to p on 64 b4.5, cellos and basses to pp at 65 (c428), violas and Vln II to pp at 65. Elsewhere this score marks a fade into a rest with a circled tip (Vln I 1-3, Vln II/Vla/Vc 66, C.a. 90, Hn 1·2 82, timpani 69-70).

Evidence: MOLA PDF 8 asks the proofreader to check the vertical line as carefully as the horizontal one, and here the vertical line shows one hairpin out of six with no end. Adler part 2 PDF 50: a well-marked score shows where a note goes to niente ("n"), and "that is exactly what one must do".

Fix, applied in /tmp/critic-445/work/sections/bSec.ily (bSecBassTbn):
```
a,2\p \once \override Staff.Hairpin.circled-tip = ##t d,4\> e,4 <>\! |
```
The score and the B.tbn part now show the niente circle at the 64/65 barline. MIDI is unchanged.

### 2. Bar 47, oboe 1: mf diminuendo into a rest with no end

Problem: Ob 1 holds C sharp5 `cis''2.\mf\> r4\!`. The other staves sounding with it end their hairpins on pp at 47 b3 (Bn "p > pp", Vln II, Vla and Cb "fp > pp"), and the cellos end on pp at 48. The oboe's hairpin stops on the rest with no dynamic. A player cannot tell whether to fade out or to stop at about p for the cor anglais' p entry on b4.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 8 (vertical proofreading), as in Finding 1. The source comment says the oboe hands the C sharp to the cor anglais (c041), which is a reason to fade out completely.

Fix (not applied; optional):
```
des''2\<) | \once \override Staff.Hairpin.circled-tip = ##t cis''2.\mf\> r4\! |
```
in bSecOboeOne. Ob 2 rests in this bar, so the part-combined staff's rule of equal simultaneous dynamics is not affected.

### 3. Bar 92: "muta in A (for the Scherzo)" floats between the cor anglais and clarinet staves

Problem: on score page 8 the text sits right-aligned about halfway between the C.a. staff and the Cl. 1·2 staff (it is attached above the clarinets' fermata rest, coda.ily line 93). A conductor or librarian reading the bottom page can take it as a cor anglais instruction. The score text also differs from the Cl 2 part, which reads "(for the Scherzo, in the pause)".

Evidence: MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12): anchored markings that float are an error. MOLA PDF 5 and Ex. 5 (PDF 10): an instrument change must say clearly when to change and to what, as "to piccolo" does in Ex. 5.

Fix (not applied; page 8 has plenty of room): name the players and match the part text:
```
... R2.\fermata ^\markup \small \italic \right-column { "Cl. 1·2: muta in A" "(for the Scherzo, in the pause)" }
```
Alternatively, place it below the Cl. staff with `_\markup` and keep the wording. The scherzo thread does not need to know about this: it is a text change, and the change itself (clarinets to A in the pause) stays the same.
