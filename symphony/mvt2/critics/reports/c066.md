## Findings

**1. Violas, bar 40→41: arco to pizz. with no time (applied).**
The bowed, slurred triplet figure runs to the last eighth of bar 40, and bar 41 starts pizz. on the downbeat with triplet eighths at ♩=60. Adler part 1 PDF 54 says cases with no time for the change "should be avoided if at all possible", and that the switch is quickest from an up-bow that ends at the frog. Fix: bar 40 beat 3 plays three eighths on an up-bow and beat 4 is a rest. Vln II's D♭ chord and the harp/bass keep the harmony on beat 4. The tuplet number is shown on that group, because the figure's numbers are otherwise omitted.
```
\tuplet 3/2 4 { a8( c' es' c' a c') } \tweak TupletNumber.stencil #ly:tuplet-number::print \tuplet 3/2 4 { bes8(\upbow des' f') } r4 |
```
The cellos' pizz. to arco at 40→41 has two beats after the last pluck, and the bow stays in the hand (PDF 54). That is enough, so no change.

**2. Cor anglais quotation, 47 b4–53 b2: about 25 seconds with no rest (applied: breath mark only).**
From the C♯4 pickup to the F4 at 53 the line has no rest: one beat at ♩≈55, 48–52 at ♩=54, two beats at 60. Adler part 1 PDF 175 says double-reed players need less air but often have to exhale, which is a reason to mark where. The two slur joins at 49→50 and 50→51 both come without rests. The natural place is after 50: the second half (51–53) is the falling A♭–G♭–F close, and 47 b4–50 is about 14 seconds. Fix in bSec.ily: `cis''2(\mp b'4 a'4) \breathe |`. Pitches, rhythm, slurs and dynamics of the protected quotation are unchanged.

**3. Bassoon 1, bars 32–36: about 17 seconds held at pp (applied).**
`aes2.( | aes4 f2~ | f4 es2~ | es2.~ | es2)` has no breath: 9 beats at 52, 3 at 49 and 2 at 45, about 16.8 s. Bassoon 2 already breathes inside its pedal at 33 b3. Adler part 1 PDF 188 says to give the player time after sustained passages and to stagger long notes between players. Fix in trans.ily, which re-strikes the E♭3 at 35 together with the bass clarinet's A♭3 attack, so the breath is covered:
```
transBsnOne = { R2. | aes2.(\pp | aes4 f2~ | f4 es2) \breathe | es2.~ | es2 r4 | }
```
This does not change the harmony of the protected woodwind choir.

**4. Contrabassoon, 30–33 b2: A♭1 held for about 12.7 s at pp (not applied, for the composer's judgement).**
This is inside the instrument's range (PDF 304), but the low register uses a lot of air. Bassoon 2 takes the same pedal at 31 an octave higher. If players report a problem, end the contrabassoon at 32 b3 (`aes,,2.~ | aes,,2 r4 | R2.*4`) and let bassoon 2 carry the pedal. Otherwise leave it.

No other playability problems found.
