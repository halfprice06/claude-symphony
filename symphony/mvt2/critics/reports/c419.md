## Findings

No finding needs a change to the score. They are ranked by how much each matters to the question "would a conductor believe a master wrote this?"

**1. Comparison with Brahms Op. 77/ii: the opening holds up (bars 1-14).** Brahms starts with a wind band alone and gives the tune to one solo wind. Strings come in only when the solo violin does. This score does the same kind of thing, with a horn solo (7-14) over bassoons, bass clarinet and harp after the wind-only link (3-6). The voicing matches Rimsky's rules for blending brass with woodwind (PDF 108). The horns hold the stationary notes at 3-6, and the bassoons move under the horn melody at 7-14. Clarinets do not sit inside the horns. The accompaniment differs in colour from the tune, as PDF 116 asks. A conductor who knows the Brahms would see the same restraint here: one colour at a time, and the strings saved for a structural moment (15 and 23). No change.

**2. Comparison with Brahms: the dynamic range (61-62, 79-81).** This is the one clear difference from the model. Brahms's Adagio keeps the orchestra in the middle range and does not have the two ff tuttis this movement has. The two peaks are an accepted design (c413: two different peaks, brass held back, PDF 119-121, 136). The reading supports them. A tutti belongs to f and ff (PDF 120), and Ex. 209, the Scheherazade iii allargando ff, PDF 375, is a slow-movement tutti peak that releases at once to solo instruments, as 63-67 and 82-83 do here. Since the model differs but the reading supports the design, I do not reopen c413. No change.

**3. Horn balance in the mixed brass at 60-61 matches Rimsky's balance rule exactly.** At 60, Hn 4 plays alone among trombones and tuba and is marked p against their pp. PDF 103 says a horn that cannot be doubled is marked one degree louder. At 61, Hn 3-4 play a2, so the horn line is doubled where it joins the trombones (PDF 102). The woodwind is a degree louder than the brass (PDF 112). This answers in advance any critic who wants to raise the trombones at 60 or thin the horns at 61. No change.

**4. Each return of a theme is scored a new way, as ch. IV asks (7, 23, 37, 45, 53, 57, 71).** Ex. 190-191 (PDF 357) give one theme first to a solo clarinet over divided violas, then to a solo horn dolce over sustained horns. Ex. 196-198 (PDF 361-363) give one tune three scorings, getting fuller each time. Theme A here follows the same plan: horn, then violins in unison with the cello counter-melody, then violins in octaves with flute added at 77. That covers operations a, c, d and e on PDF 118. Theme B also changes colour on each phrase. No change.

**5. The coda's muted horns with cor anglais and clarinets (87-92) use the blend Rimsky praises.** PDF 107 and 110 say muted or stopped horns resemble oboe and cor anglais in tone, and that the two blend into one chord. Ex. 167 (PDF 336) is a pp chord of muted brass and wind under a legatissimo violin line, which is the texture of 87-90. The fade by withdrawal follows Ex. 143 and 169: the horns die away at 90, the clarinet goes morendo at 91, and the strings end ppp. No change.

**6. The empty middle at 83 departs from PDF 124-125 on purpose.** Rimsky says widely separated outer parts without the middle octaves filled give a "strange" effect. Bar 83 has harp harmonics over Vc/Cb ppp and nothing between. It lasts one bar, it is the deliberate join after the second peak (c063/c124, protected), and the violas fill the middle again at 85. Since the passage is a short join and not a sustained texture, Rimsky's warning does not apply. No change.

**7. v409 to v419: nothing got worse.** The five changes are small and each is grounded in the reading. "molto sostenuto" now prints on all five string staves at 79, as at 61, so the page has more text, but the two peaks are now marked the same way and both have a reason (c418). The Vln II C4 at 39 and "sul G" at 51-52 do not touch the outer voices; analysis.txt still shows 0 parallels. The Bsn 2 breath at 70 is covered by Bsn 1 and the basses. The movement's ending, its exported themes and its quotations did not change, so the other movement threads need no report.
