## Findings

### 1. Bars 70→71: the return of theme A is the one main V7-I joint without contrary semitone motion (applied in scratch)

**Bars:** 70-71. Bassoons 1-2.

**Problem.** At 69-70 the dominant seventh is Cb E♭2, Hn 1 B♭3, Bsn 1 G3 and Bsn 2 D♭3. In v377 both bassoons stop on 70 b2 (`g2 r4`, `des2 r4`), and the timpani stop at b2.5. So under the violins' pickup E♭-F at 70 b3, the dominant is only E♭2 and B♭3. The leading tone G and the seventh D♭ disappear one beat before the A♭ chord. Bsn 2's D♭ reaches C3 at 71 only after a rest, and the G does not resolve at all.

Every other tonic arrival in the movement converges by contrary semitones:
- 30: Vla G4→A♭4 against Hn 3 / Vc D♭4→C4;
- 82: Cl 2 G4→A♭4 against Bsn 1 / horns / Vc D♭4→C4;
- 9, 25 and 73: G→A♭ against D♭→C.

The return at 71 is the movement's main structural arrival, and it is the exception.

**Evidence.**
- Review point 6 (PDF 580) says chromatic contrary motion is organised in 6-10 / 10-6 frames, with the m6-M10 as the most typical. Ex. 31-19 (PDF 564-565) shows that the outward or inward semitone motion between V-type sonorities is what gives the progression its direction, while the other voices hold.
- Here the two bassoons form the tritone D♭3-G3. Resolved by contrary semitones it opens to the minor sixth C3-A♭3: the same convergence as 30 and 82, in two inner voices.
- Exercise 4 (PDF 583) asks for a middle voice that moves where the melody has quick notes. At 70 b3 the melody has the pickup eighths and the middle is empty.
- Rimsky-Korsakov, *Principles*, PDF 125: an empty middle between distant outer parts is kept for special effects. c343's own gap scan listed 70 b3.5 (Cb E♭2 against Vln I F5) as the widest gap in 65-70.

**Fix (applied: /tmp/critic-377/work/sections/retr.ily and aTwo.ily):**
```
% retr.ily, Bsn 1, bar 70 (was: g2-\tweak stencil ##f \pp r4)
g2.-\tweak stencil ##f \pp(
% retr.ily, Bsn 2, bar 70 (was: des2-\tweak stencil ##f \pp r4)
des2.-\tweak stencil ##f \pp
% aTwo.ily, Bsn 1, bars 71-74 (was: R2.*4)
aes2)\pp r4 | R2.*3 |
```
The slur runs from retr.ily into aTwo.ily, as other slurs in the score cross section files. Bsn 2's existing C3 at 71 (`c2.(\pp`) now follows its D♭3 without a rest. Bsn 1's A♭3 (half note, pp) fills the A♭ chord between Vc E♭3 and Vla C4 and stops before the violas reach A♭ at 71 b3.

**Protected material, and why this does not undo it.**
- c014 "horn-bassoon voicing 69-70": the notes, the crossing under the horn and the dynamics are unchanged. Only the length of the last chord changes.
- c153 "thin textures 65-70": the addition is two pp bassoons for one more beat.
- c343 kept the bassoons' b3 stop "so the dominant thins out". It thins out to a bare fifth with a two-octave hole, which c343's own Rimsky-Korsakov reading counts against.
- "A′ texture 71-82": the change adds one half note. The violins, the viola figure, the cello counter-melody, and the horn 2 / Bsn 1 counter-melody entry at 75 are untouched.

**Build.**
- build-warnings.txt is empty; the score is 8 pages.
- analysis.txt shows 0 outer-voice parallels. Its only differences from v377 are the bassoon track counts and the attack count for 69-72 (55 → 56).
- allpairs_par.py gives the same result as on v377. The only difference is a relabelled existing Bsn 2/Cb doubling at 72-73.
- The parts book is 30 pages, 0 warnings, every part on one page except the harp (2).

### 3. No note-against-note M6-m10 motion anywhere (no change; a rule for later cycles)

A&S call the M6-m10 exchange, set note against note, "scarcely possible, because of the poor sonorities produced" (PDF 565, Ex. 31-21a). I checked every contrary semitone pair against the bass. The intervals before and after each move are:
- 21: P8→A6, then A6→P8 at 22;
- 40, 56, 60: d7→P5, the normal °7 resolution;
- 78: P5→M6.

There is no M6→M7 or m9→m10 chain. Later changes should keep it that way. The rule c242 gave for the motto tail (never set it against a rising chromatic bass) covers the most likely place for the mistake.

### 5. Complementary rhythm between melody and middle voice (no change)

Exercise 4 (PDF 583) asks for quick notes in the middle voice against long notes in the melody, and the reverse. The movement does this in three main places:
- **23-28 (and 71-76):** the cello counter-melody moves on the beats where the theme holds (23 b3, 26 b2, 27 b3).
- **53-56:** the Hn 1 / Cl 1 counter-line moves where the theme holds G♭ (54) and B♭ (56).
- **79:** under the held B♭6 (18 parts sounding), only the counter-melody moves (Cl, Bsn 1, horns 1·2, Vc).

Two other bars have one mover: 47 (the C♯7 pivot, fp, poco rit.) and 63 (the trombone quotation, protected). Both are deliberate holds.

### 6. Doubled chromatic lines at 62 and 87-89; correction to c242 (no change)

- **62 b1→b3.** F→F♭ sounds in six parts at once (Fl 2, Ob 1, Bsn 1, Vln I, Vln II, Vc), against a single rising pair, Hn 3·4 B♭4→C♭5. The F at b1 is a suspension prepared at 61 (Ob 1, Cl 1 tied). It goes to F♭ while the bass moves G♭→F♭, so it moves from a 7th to an octave. It is not the 7-6 of Ex. 31-2. c242 listed it under 7-6 chains; that label should not be repeated. The layout is protected (61-62 brass, F♭→E♭ thread), and the reading gives no reason to change it.
- **87-89.** F-F♭-E♭ sounds in C.a. F4, Hn 4 F3 and lower Vla F3, against the bass F→D♭→A♭. This is c030's intended doubling inside the protected plagal close. The only rising semitone that iv→I could supply does not exist (D♭-F♭-A♭ → A♭-C-E♭), so Unit 31 has nothing to add there.

### 7. Devices of the unit that the movement does not use (no change)

The movement uses none of these:
- chromatic 7-6 chains (PDF 552);
- 5-6 sequences in either direction (PDF 554-557);
- augmented-sixth sequences (PDF 557);
- augmented-triad chains (PDF 562);
- extended exchange chains (PDF 567-571);
- octatonic or whole-tone passages (PDF 578-580).

A&S call these transitional or minor resources whose predominance weakens the tonic (PDF 571-572). The movement's transitions (31-36, 45-47, 62-65) are pedals and pivots, and they are protected. Adding a chain would mean writing a new passage, not fixing the texture.
