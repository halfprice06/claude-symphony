## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The trombone 1 part has the wrong cue at 61–62 (applied)
**Bars:** Trombone 1 part, 61–62, just before the quotation at 63. **Problem:** the cue shows nothing in 61 and A♭2 as a whole note in 62. The bass trombone actually plays D♭3 at 61 and G♭2–F♭2 at 62. **Cause:** `\addQuote "bassTbnQ" { \bassTbn }` in genparts.py quotes the bass trombone with both of its tag branches. c135 added a part-only Vc cue (`\tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part {…}`) at 58–59 in bSecBassTbn, so the quote now has two extra bars and runs two bars late. c135 filtered the three quotes it added; this older one from c075 was missed. **Reading:** Adler PDF 299 item 6 says cues exist to make re-entry after a long rest safe, and the cued notes must be the cued instrument's notes. Here the player has 62 bars of rest and enters ff, solo, on the quotation. A wrong cue there is worse than no cue. **Fix (genparts.py):** `\addQuote "bassTbnQ" { \removeWithTag #'part \bassTbn }`. The part now shows D♭3 | G♭2 F♭2, labelled "B.tbn.". The other quotes are correct: corAnglais and bassCl have no tags, and hnOneQ, oboeOneQ and vcQ are filtered.

### 2. Clarinet 2's f stands under a rest at 78 (applied)
**Bars:** 78, aTwo.ily `r2\f g'4`. **Problem:** in the Clarinet 2 part the f is printed under the half rest, and the entry on beat 3 has no dynamic of its own. It is the only dynamic attached to a rest in the movement (I grepped every section). **Reading:** in Adler's Ex. XVIII-10 (PDF 301) the shared staff carries one f, but in the separate parts each player's dynamic sits on that player's own first note (Oboe II's ff on its quarter). PDF 291 item 3 also asks for every detail for every instrument. **Fix (aTwo.ily):** `\tag #'score { r2\f } \tag #'part { r2 } g'4-\tag #'part \f`. The score source is unchanged: the shared f stays on beat 1 and the "2." label on beat 3. I first tried `r2-\tag #'score \f`, but that gives a "conflict with event" warning against Cl 1's f. A plain `r2 g'4-\tag #'part \f` changed the part-combining (a half rest hanging under the staff and no "2."). The tagged-rest form avoids both problems. The MIDI is identical to v145.

### 3. Bassoons 57–58: whole rest on a ledger line and no player label (applied)
**Bars:** 57–58, Bn. 1·2 staff (p. 4, second system). **Problem:** `\partCombineApart r1 | r1` in bassoon 1 prints a whole rest for player 1. At 57 it sits on a ledger line above the staff, because bassoon 2's F3–G♭3 fill the top of the staff, so it looks like a ledger-line note. Bassoon 2's entry has no "2.". Every other single-player entry in the score is labelled (Hn 4 at 60, Cl 2 at 78, Tbn 2 at 60, and so on). **Reading:** Adler PDF 294–295 (Ex. XVIII-2): each instrument "is labeled every time it enters". App. A PDF 312 gives "1., 2." as the standard indication. Gould PDF 55: no symbol placed where it can be taken for another. **Fix (bSec.ily):** bassoon 1 has `\partCombineApart \tag #'score { s1*2 } \tag #'part { R1*2 } | ges2\f …`, and bassoon 2 has `f2\p\<-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "2." ges2 | …`. The score shows "2." and bassoon 2's notes with stems down, with no floating rest. The Bassoon 1 part shows a "2" multi-rest. I tried `\partCombineSoloII` first: it looks the same, but it moves bassoon 2 into another MIDI voice and flattens the p< crescendo (velocities 36–48 became 32–34). The spacer form keeps the MIDI identical.

### 4. Shared-staff "1." labels printed in single-player parts at 67–68 (applied)
**Bars:** Horn 1 part at 67 ("1. solo"), Bassoon 1 part at 68 ("1."), retr.ily. **Problem:** these two labels are untagged markups, so the solo parts print them. Every other "1." in the score is `-\tag #'score` (bSec.ily 20, 35, 42, 113, 114). **Reading:** Adler Ex. XVIII-10b (PDF 301): the extracted Oboe I part drops the score's "1.", because the label only tells the conductor which player of the pair is playing. **Fix (retr.ily):** Hn 1 `-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1. solo" -\tag #'part ^\markup \bold "solo"`, and Bsn 1 `-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1."`. The score is unchanged. Horn 1's part keeps "solo", as at bar 6.

### 5. Tempo words in adjacent rest bars run together in the parts (applied)
**Bars:** 47–48 ("poco rit." then "poco sostenuto") in the Trombone 1 part, and the same in other parts where 47–48 are narrow rest bars. **Problem:** the two directions print on one line with a small gap, so they read as one instruction, "poco rit. poco sostenuto". In parts where the bars are wider (Cl 2, Cbn) they are stacked. **Reading:** Adler PDF 299 item 4 says the player resting through a passage must still know "what is happening every moment". PDF 291 item 1 asks for clear, unproblematical notation. **Fix (genparts.py, parts \Score context):** `\override MetronomeMark.outside-staff-horizontal-padding = #1.5`. The second word now stacks above the first. Every part is still one page (the harp two), and the book is still 30 pages.

### Observations (no change)
- Tempo words are printed at the top and over the strings, not over each choir (Adler PDF 294, Ex. XVIII-1). This is the protected c045/c135 layout, and adding them would change the 8-page layout.
- B♭6 sits on five ledger lines in the Fl 1 and Vln I parts (Adler PDF 300 item 8a). This was closed in c075 under MOLA PDF 5, which prefers no 8va in parts. Not re-raised.
- Bar 60: the p and pp under Hn 4, B.tbn and Tuba sit close to the ledger lines but do not touch at 600 dpi. No change.
- Bar 78: Cl 2's half rest hangs below the Cl staff under Cl 1's A♭4. This is a standard two-voice display and unchanged since v144.
