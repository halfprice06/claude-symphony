Model work: Franck, Symphony in D minor, ii (Allegretto). No lens-9 critic has used it before (searched HISTORY.md). Franck's movement opens with pizzicato strings and harp under a long cor anglais solo. A contrasting cantabile follows, then a light middle episode of soft rapid string figuration with a wind tune over it. The return brings the opening theme back with the middle section's figuration, and the movement ends quietly, with harp arpeggios recalling the motives. The theme comes back in the finale. Sources: UC Davis programme note (https://arts.ucdavis.edu/post/franck-symphony-d-minor), Bru Zane (https://bruzanemediabase.com/en/node/17870), IMSLP score page (https://imslp.org/wiki/Symphony_in_D_minor_(Franck,_C%C3%A9sar)). I worked from these descriptions and from memory. There is no Franck score in the corpus, so I make no bar-level claims about it.

## Would a conductor believe a master wrote this?

Yes, on the evidence of this chapter. The colour plan does what Franck's movement does. A wind solo is carried over plucked harp (7-14, as Franck's cor anglais over pizzicato and harp). One colour is kept for one idea (C.a. for movement I's theme, Hn 1 for the motto tail; Adler PDF 261). A soft accompaniment figure runs under a wind tune in the middle section (viola triplets under flute and C.a., 37-44). The quiet close recalls the opening theme on the harp (83). The end hands material forward to the later movements. Where the two differ, the difference is a recorded choice. Franck's return lays the middle section's figuration over the opening theme. This score's A′ (71-82) does not bring theme B or its triplets back. That was closed at c551 ("recall left to movement III"), and the A′ texture is protected, so I do not reopen it. Against v499, nothing is worse. The stem fixes and c502's viola figure are improvements. c508's cello rebowing is correct in its purpose, but it set up the one small articulation mismatch in F1.

## Findings (ranked)

**F1. Bars 59-60, cellos: the bow groups split the counter-line differently from the horn and bassoon that double it. Applied in scratch.**
- Bars: bSec.ily, bSecVc, 59-60, `ges2(\f es4) fes4 | es4(\< f4) ges2 |` (c508).
- Problem: at 59-60 the cellos, Bsn 1 and Hn 1 play the same counter-line, G♭-E♭-F♭ | E♭-F-G♭, into the ff at 61. Hn 1 and Bsn 1 articulate it `ges'2\f es'4( fes'4 | es'4\< f'4 ges'2)`: the G♭ alone, then one slur from 59 b3. The cellos now slur G♭ to E♭ across that point, then play F♭ alone on a one-beat up-bow, so the chromatic F♭ (the movement's F♭-E♭ thread) gets the weight of a bow change on its own. Before c508 the cellos had three plus three beats, also out of line with the winds. The bow lengths are uneven (3, 1, 2, 2 beats).
- Evidence: Adler PDF 241-243 (Ex. VIII-10): a bassoon doubling the cellos is heard as a new colour *on that line*, which requires the doublers to phrase it alike. PDF 255: in combined writing the line must come through. Bowing need not copy wind slurs, but where the two can coincide at no cost they should.
- Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-509/work): `ges2\f es4( fes4) | es4(\< f4) ges2 |`. The bows are down (G♭, f), up (E♭-F♭), down (E♭-F, crescendo), up (G♭), and 61 is down, so all bows are two beats. The bow change at 59 b3 now falls where the winds' slur begins. c398's down-bow f at 59, c085's down-bow ff at 61 and c508's four bows (no bow carrying the f-ff crescendo for four beats) are all kept. There are no two up-bows in a row (c096). The pitches, ties and hairpins are unchanged, so score.midi does not change.

**F2. A′ does not combine the middle section's figure with theme A, as Franck's return does. Record only.**
Franck's reprise answers Adler PDF 253 ("whenever the choirs are first used separately and then together, a most satisfying sense of completion... results") with material rather than choirs: two ideas heard apart are later heard together. Here 53-56 already combines theme A's head with theme B, so the synthesis happens inside the B section, not in the reprise. The A′ texture (71-82) is protected, and c551 left the recall of theme B to movement III, so I propose no change. If the composer ever reopens A′, the smallest step in Franck's direction is the violas' eighth figure at 75-77 in the triplet rhythm of 37-40. I did not test it, because it would touch a protected texture.

**F3. Ob 1 and Cl 2 in unison on A♭4 at 82 b1-2, p>pp. Follows; record.**
Adler PDF 235 warns against this pair in unison on a soft line. At 82 it is a release chord after the tutti, with Vln II on the same A♭4 and both winds stopping at b3. It is not a melody. Adler's exception for tutti doubling (PDF 257) covers it. No change.

**F4. Comparison with v499: nothing is worse. Record.**
The changes since v499 are c502 (viola 74 b1, a complete V4/3), c505 (stems at 46, 59, 72, 82, which the page images show are correct), c508 (cello bowing at 59-60, refined by F1), and the draft label. The 8-page layout, page breaks and the parts book are as before.
