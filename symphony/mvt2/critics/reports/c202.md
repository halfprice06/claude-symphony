I read every text page in the range (PDF 58-73) and rendered every page that carries examples (PDF 58, 59, 60, 61, 62, 64, 65, 66, 68, 69, 70) at 110 dpi. One point about the assignment's label: READING describes this range as "spacing, doubling, figured bass". The unit itself teaches triad and seventh-chord types, inversion, figured-bass symbols, 6/3 and 6/4 chords as products of melodic motion, the origin and resolution of the seventh, and the difference between harmonic and contrapuntal progression. It has no rules on spacing or doubling; those begin in Unit 5 (PDF 74 onward). I have not attributed Unit 5 rules to this reading.

## Findings

### 1. Bar 49 b1: the F♯-minor tonic has no third on the beat (applied in the scratch copy)

**Bars:** 49 (Vln II), with 48 and 50 for context.

**Problem.** Bar 49 is where F♯ minor is confirmed (c193: prepared 46-47, confirmed 49-50). On its downbeat the sounding notes are F♯2 (Cb), F♯3 (Vc), F♯4 (C.a., the quotation) and F♯5 (Vln II): one pitch class in four octaves. The third, A, comes only in the violas' off-beat eighths (pp, sotto voce, from b1.5). At b2 the bass moves to B, and the viola's A3 at b2.5 (the 7th of iv7, c027) rises to B3 at b3.5 instead of falling.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 61: the character of each mode comes mainly from the quality of the tonic triad, and minor differs from major in its third (PDF 59, Ex. 4-3; PDF 61, Ex. 4-5). A hollow octave on the tonic downbeat of the bar that is supposed to confirm the new minor key leaves its mode to an off-beat pp eighth. PDF 67-68 (Ex. 4-15): the 7th is the active element and resolves down by step; at present the only 7th of the iv7 at 49 b2 goes up. PDF 65 (Ex. 4-11b): motions above a stationary bass (5-6, and by extension 7-6) are kept in one voice. In the corpus, Dvořák 9/ii puts the minor third on the tonic downbeats of its C♯-minor section (46, 51).

**Fix (applied).** Vln II at 49 b1-2: A5 instead of F♯5. Rhythm, slur and bowing are unchanged (c027's held half note, c186's one-bow G5-E♯5).

```
% bSec.ily, bSecVlnTwo, bar 49
fis''2 g''4( eis''4) |      % v202
a''2 g''4( eis''4) |        % c202
```

What this does: A5 is the third of the F♯-minor chord on the downbeat; with the cor anglais it forms a voice exchange (C.a. A4→F♯4, Vln II F♯5→A5 across 48-49), a standard way of prolonging one chord. At b2 over B it becomes the 7th of iv7 and resolves down to G5 at b3 (7-6 over the stationary B, in the same voice). From there the line goes G5-E♯5-F♯5 as before, so the leading tone still resolves to 1̂ at 50 (PDF 61-62). Bar 50 b1 stays F♯-C♯ on the beat: Vln II's E♯ must resolve to F♯, and the C.a. has the quotation, so the third there remains in the violas, which is acceptable once the third has been heard on the tonic downbeat of 49. The viola's A3 at b2.5 now doubles the 7th for one off-beat eighth. If the composer prefers, it can become D4 (`r8 a8-_ r8 d'8-_ r8 b8-_ r8 gis8-_`), but I did not apply this: it is a pp off-beat, and c107 chose same-pitch off-beats.

Page 3 of the scratch PDF shows A5 with one ledger line and the slur and tie layout unchanged.

**Other movements:** none. The quotation (C.a.), the themes, the ending and the exported material are untouched.

### 2. Bar 78 b3: the composer's label "passing I6/4" does not match the sound (comment only, not applied)

**Bars:** 78 b3 (aTwo.ily header, c070 note).

**Problem.** The header calls 78 b3 "a passing I6/4 between flat VI and ii6/5 at 79". What sounds is E♭ (Cb, Bsn, harp) with G (Cl 1 G4, harp G3) and C (Vla, Vc, Bsn, Hn 1·2, Vln II, Vln I, Fl 1). That is a 6/3 on E♭, not a 6/4 (no A♭ until the grace run). It comes from melodic motion: Cl 1 A♭4-G4-F4 and the melody's C over a bass that steps F♭-E♭-D♭.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 66-67 (§9-10, Ex. 4-12, 4-13): name chords from the bass. A roman numeral under a 6/3 or 6/4 that arises from melodic motion misdescribes it. PDF 71-72: in a contrapuntal progression the stepwise lines create the chords. The music is correct as a passing 6/3. Only the label is wrong, and a later critic who reads "I6/4" would look for an A♭ that is not there or "fix" the G.

**Fix.** In the aTwo.ily header, replace "(passing I6/4 between flat VI and ii6/5 at 79)" with "(passing 6/3 E♭-G-C between flat VI and ii6/5 at 79: Cl 1 A♭4-G4-F4 passes over the bass F♭-E♭-D♭; A&S PDF 64-67)". No note changes.
