## Findings

### 1. Bar 47 b4: the quotation's pickup enters p inside a string diminuendo that is still mp (applied)
- **Problem.** Movement I's second-theme head enters on the cor anglais' C♯4 pickup, `cis'4(\p`, on 47 b4. At the same moment, Vln II (E♯4, a third above), the violas (B3, a step below) and the basses are in the middle of `\mp\>` hairpins that reach pp only on the next downbeat. Bassoon 2 does the same. The one cyclic quotation in the middle section therefore starts as the softest, lowest-lying note inside a louder string chord in its own register.
- **Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 13: "It is the arranger's job to see that the listener's ear is directed to the primary element and not diverted or confused by an overbusy background". PDF 14: the background "can ... descend to a tertiary level", and the arranger must "manipulate his ensemble accordingly". In Ex. 269 (PDF 205) every accompanying part reaches its new lower level together with the lead (mf → mp at the bar line), not after it. Here the hand-over from oboe to cor anglais is the focus shift, so the background has to be down before it happens.
- **Fix (bSec.ily).** End the diminuendos on beat 4. This changes dynamics only. The pp at 48 is kept and not reprinted.
  - Vln II: `\after 2 ^\markup \italic "poco rit." \after 2. \pp eis'1\mp\> | fis''1^\markup \italic "poco sostenuto" |`
  - Vla: `f2 bes2\< | \after 2. \pp b1\mp\> | a1 |`
  - Cb: `bes,,2 ges,,2\< | \after 2. \pp cis,1\mp\> | fis,1 |`
  - Bsn 2: `bes,2 bes,2\< | \after 2. \pp gis,1\mp\> | R1 |`

### 3. Bars 61–62: the climax's sustaining parts are articulated three different ways (applied)
- **Problem.** c028 put "molto sostenuto" and tenuti on the Largamente. In v58 the tenuti are on Fl 1·2, Ob 2, Tpt 1 and Vln II only. Ob 1, Cl 1·2, Tpt 2, Tpt 3, all four horns, the trombones, bass trombone, tuba, BCl, both bassoons, contrabassoon, Vln I (div.), cellos and basses play the same half and whole notes with no articulation. The head A♭–D♭–F–F♭ therefore has tenuti in Vln II and Tpt 1 and none in the cellos and Vln I lower, which play it in octaves. On a shared staff, Tpt 1 has tenuti and Tpt 2 does not. Ob 1 has a bare slur and Ob 2 tenuti.
- **Evidence.** In Ex. 281 (PDF 214–215, viewed as images) every saxophone, trumpet and trombone that plays a figure carries the same marks: roof accents, accent and sfz-plus-hairpin attacks on each held chord. A tutti that should sound as one gesture is marked as one. Sebesky PDF 213: a well-made chart "plays itself", meaning the players get the feel of the notes at once. Players who see tenuti in one part and none in the next stand will not phrase the same way.
- **Fix (bSec.ily).** Add `--` on every attack at 61–62 in the sustaining parts that lack it. Tremolo parts (Vla, Vln I upper at 63, timpani) are unchanged. For example: Ob 1 `f''1--(\ff | f''2-- fes''2--)`; Cl 1 `aes'2--\ff des''2-- | bes'2-- ces''2--`; Cl 2 `f'1--\ff | ges'2-- aes'2--`; BCl `des1--\ff | ges,2-- fes,2--`; Bsn 1 `f1--\ff | ges2-- aes2--`; Bsn 2 `des1--\ff | ges,2-- fes,2--`; Cbsn `des,1--\ff | ges,,2-- fes,,2--`; Hn 2 `f'1--\f | ges'2-- aes'2--`; Hn 3 `aes'1--\f | bes'2-- ces''2--`; Hn 4 `aes1--\f | bes2-- ces'2--`; Tpt 2 `f'1--\ff | bes'2-- ces''2--`; Tpt 3 `des'1--\f | des'2-- fes'2--`; Tbn 1 `aes1--\f | bes2-- r2`; Tbn 2 `f1--\f | ges2-- aes2--`; B.tbn `des1--\f | ges,2-- fes,2--`; Tuba `des,1--\f | ges,,2-- fes,,2--`; Vln I `aes'''1--~` and `des'''1--\ff | f'''2-- fes'''2--`; Vc `aes2--\ff ... des'2-- | f'2-- fes'2--`; Cb `des,1--\ff | ges,,2-- fes,,2--`.

### 4. Bars 36–37: the bass clarinet crescendos to mp on the flute's p entry of theme B (applied; adjusts c038)
- **Problem.** `aes4\p\< bes4 c'4 | des'2)\mp` arrives one level above Fl 1's `f''2(\p^"dolce cantabile"` on the same downbeat. c038 chose mp on purpose, "one step above the flute's p entry", to give the open hairpin an end level. The result is that the end of the old line is marked louder than the start of the new theme.
- **Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 13–14: at the moment the second theme begins, it is the primary element, and the bass clarinet's resolution is secondary. Ex. 284 (PDF 218) moves a line out of focus by "the decrease in volume" and the change of colour. Movement I and the Dvořák corpus part both start new themes at a level the accompaniment does not exceed.
- **Fix.** trans.ily: `aes2.~(\> | aes4\pp\< bes4 c'4 |`. bSec.ily: `des'2)\p r2 |`. The hairpin still has a defined end level, so c038's concern is kept. The shape (dim. 35, cresc. 36) is unchanged, and the bass clarinet stays one level above bassoon 1's pp.

### 5. Bar 71: "espr." on the violas' accompanying eighths (applied)
- **Problem.** At the reprise three lines ask to be expressive at once: Vln I "espr." (70), Vc "cantabile" (71) and the violas "unis., ord., espr." at pp on the eighth-note figure. The violas are the background under the theme and the counter-melody. c018 added "espr." together with the needed "ord." and gave no reason for it.
- **Evidence.** Sebesky PDF 12: anything that does not make "a legitimate difference" should be erased. Every other "espr." in this score marks a primary line (horn 6, BCl 31, Ob 45, C.a. 47, Hn 1 53, Vln I 70).
- **Fix (aTwo.ily, `aTwoVla`).** `^\markup \italic "unis., ord."`. The pp level and the slurs are unchanged.
