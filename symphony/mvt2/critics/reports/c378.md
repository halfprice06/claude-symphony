## Findings

I found nothing that the reading shows to be wrong and that a change would clearly improve. Below are the checks that are new in this pass, ranked by how much they bear on the score's expression. All are recorded with no change.

### 1. Climaxes sit where the hypermeter and the tonal goals put them, at three levels (recorded; no change)
- **Reading.** PDF 53: bars 1 and 3 of a four-bar group are strong, and the whole group acts as one large bar. PDF 54: the end of a bar group is often a goal of tonal motion. PDF 50: high, dissonant and chromatic notes are accented. PDF 54–55, Ex. 3-15: the appoggiatura is an accented dissonance.
- **Phrase level.** In each statement of theme A, the dynamic peak falls on the B♭ appoggiatura on beat 2 of bar 6 of the eight-bar phrase:
  - 12: Hn 1, printed mp.
  - 28: Vln I·II, printed f.
  - 76: Vln I·II, with a hidden mf (c228).

  Bar 6 is a weak bar. The tied high F that reaches it starts on beat 3 of strong bar 5, so the peak is carried by a built-in accent (the dissonance) and not by bar position. This is the pattern Ex. 3-15(c) shows.
- **Section level.** The two ff peaks fall on downbeats of hypermeasures that come straight after an eight-bar build:
  - 61: after 53–60. The D♭ tonic, held back since 37, arrives here, so the section's tonal goal and its climax are the same bar. This is the coincidence PDF 54 describes.
  - 79: the eighth bar of 71–78 brings a deceptive cadence (♭VI at 78), which turns 79–82 into a four-bar extension. The ff falls on that extension's strong first bar, and the f at 78 marks the deceptive goal one level below. c331 recorded 61 and 79 as strong bars. The new point here is that 79's strength comes from the extension, and that the climax waits past the cadence the phrase was expected to reach.
- **Register.**
  - The middle section's highest pitch is Fl 1 A6 sf at 63. That is bar 3 of the 61–64 group, a strong bar.
  - The movement's highest pitch, B♭6, is held back to 79 (c299).

  Both registral peaks fall on strong bars and carry printed accents (sf, ff), so the printed accent and the built-in accent agree.
- **Fix:** none.
