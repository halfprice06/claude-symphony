## Findings

### 1. Letters F (45) and K (71) float above the previous tempo word in most wind and brass parts (applied)

Problem: when a part rests through a letter-and-tempo bar and the next letter falls at the end of the same rest, the tempo word runs past the next barline. LilyPond then lifts the next letter one level, above the word. In v455:
- F at 45 sits above "Poco più mosso (♩ = 60)" in Cl 1, Cl 2, Bsn 1, Cbn, Hn 3, Hn 4 and Timp.
- K at 71 sits above "Tempo I (♩ = 52)" in Fl 1, Ob 1, Cl 1, Cl 2, B.cl., Hn 2, Hn 3, Hn 4, Tbn 1, Tbn 2, B.tbn and Tuba. In the trombone and tuba parts K stands over the "(♩ = 52)" of J, so it reads as part of J.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 10, Ex. 4 shows exactly this situation done right. After "C" comes "Etwas bewegter" over a 16-bar rest, then "D" at the next barline on the same line. Then "E Tempo I", then "17 F 20". The rest under the word is as wide as the word. MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12) name floating marks in extracted parts as the error to proofread for. MOLA PDF 5: letters in the parts must match the score, and a letter that floats can be read as belonging to the bar before.

Fix, applied in /tmp/critic-455/work/genparts.py (parts only; the score is not touched). A multi-bar rest that starts under a tempo word, or under a letter and its word, gets `minimum-length` at least as wide as the word:
```
#(define fit-on (make-parameter #f))
#(define (mmr-fit tm extra)
   (lambda (grob)
     (if (< (ly:grob-property grob 'measure-count 1) 2) 0
         (min 48 (+ extra 1.5 (interval-length (ly:stencil-extent
           (grob-interpret-markup grob (markup #:normal-text tm)) X)))))))
```
In `beside-letter` the letter-and-word branch adds `\once \override Staff.MultiMeasureRest.minimum-length = #wid` (with `extra` 18, since the rod also covers the key and time signatures after the letter), and a new branch does the same for a lone tempo word (`extra` 8). `partMergeFit` and `partGlobalFit` (parameterize `fit-on` #t) are used for the wind, brass and timpani parts. The harp and the strings keep `partGlobal`: their pages are full (the viola went to two pages when I tried it), and none of them had a lifted letter. Result: F and K now sit at their barlines beside the rest number in all the listed parts. Single-bar rests are left alone.

### 2. Bar 91 splits the closing rest in 16 parts (applied)

Problem: Fl 1-2, Ob 1-2, B.cl., Bn 1-2, Cbn, Hn 1-2, Tbn 1-2, B.tbn, Tuba, Timp and the harp print 89-92 as "89-90 2", then a single-bar rest at 91, then the fermata bar. Nothing is printed at 91. The split comes from the MIDI-only `\set Staff.midiExpression` steps of the morendo (c228) in global.ily. c165's `flat-seq` drops the `\mt` steps for the same reason, but it does not drop these.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 5 and Ex. 3-4 (PDF 10): rests are combined and carry one range. c165 fixed the same fault at 89-92 ("1 1 1 1").

Fix, applied in genparts.py: a `midi-expr?` test (as `mt-step?`, for the `midiExpression` symbol), added to the drop list in `flat-seq`. The parts now print "89-91 3" and the fermata bar. MIDI and score are unchanged (score.midi is byte-identical).

### 3. Six players re-enter at the 79 climax after 16 bars' rest with no cue (applied)

Problem: Fl 2, Ob 2, B.cl., Cbn (ff) and Hn 3, Hn 4 (f) rest from 63 to 78 and enter on the downbeat of 79. In between come the Tempo I at 65, the 3/4 metre, letter K and "poco allargando" at 78. Every other entry after a long rest in this movement has a cue (c075, c135, c285, c315). c315 recorded the cues for Hn 3-4 at 79 as optional and was not answered. The reason to add them now is that this is the one entry where five tempo and metre events fall inside the rest and the entry is loud and exposed.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest ... Cues must be audible and obvious." Ex. 4 (PDF 10) shows a cue ("Viol. I") placed in the bars before the entry after a long rest.

Fix, applied in /tmp/critic-455/work/sections/aTwo.ily (part-only; score and MIDI unchanged). In each of the six variables, `R2.*8 |` becomes:
```
R2.*6 | \tag #'score { R2.*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring #"vlnOneQ" #UP { <>^\markup \tiny "Vln. I" R2.*2 } } |
```
with "vlnOneQ"/"Vln. I" for Fl 2 and Ob 2 (the violins' rising triplet into B flat 6), "hnOneQ"/"Hn. 1" for Hn 3 and Hn 4 (Hn 1's 77-78 line), and "vcQ"/"Vc." for B.cl. and Cbn.

### 4. Letter A at 7 above "Adagio" in about 14 parts (not applied)

Problem: the same stacking as Finding 1. It happens at the first system, where the 1-6 rest follows the clef, key and time signature (Fl 1, Ob 1, C.a., Bn 1-2, Cbn, Hn 2, Tbn 2, B.tbn, Tuba and others).

Evidence: MOLA PDF 10, Ex. 4, as in Finding 1.

Test: with enough extra width for the first rest (`extra` 10 for a tempo word with no rest before it), A came down to the staff, but Fl 1's line breaks moved and its B.cl. cue at 34-36 was split across two systems with a tie, which is worse. I reverted it. If the composer wants it, the safer fix is the same override with `extra` 10, together with a `\noBreak` in the part-only cue at 34-36 of fluteOne. I did not test that.
