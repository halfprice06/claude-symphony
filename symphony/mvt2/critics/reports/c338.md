## Findings

### 1. Bars 80–81: Hn 3's F♭4 suspension, the dissonance of the V♭9, plays at velocity 37 inside the ff tutti (applied)

**Problem.** At 80 the part-combiner moves horns 3 and 4 off the shared voice that carries their printed f (79) onto separate voices that have no dynamic. In v338's score.midi, Hn 3's F4 at 80 b1 and the F♭4 suspension at 80 b3 sound at velocity 37 (the horns' pp level) while Hn 1·2 are at 62 (f) and the winds at their ff level. The resolution E♭4 at 81 b2 comes out at 54, louder than the suspension. Hn 4's A♭3 and its G3 (81 b1) have the same fault.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 348: "dissonant suspensions receive something of an accent and their resolutions are softer." c098 shaped this exact suspension in print (crescendo into it, diminuendo from it, resolution only in the bass, PDF 352 Ex. 21-12d). The printed intention is correct and score.midi plays the opposite.

**Fix (applied, aTwo.ily; hidden, nothing printed).**
```
aTwoHnThree: f'2.\f | f'2-\tweak stencil ##f \f fes'4~(-\tweak stencil ##f \ff \< | \once \override Staff.Hairpin.circled-tip = ##t fes'4-\tweak stencil ##f \ff \> es'2) |
aTwoHnFour:  aes2.\f | aes2~-\tweak stencil ##f \f aes4(\< | \once \override Staff.Hairpin.circled-tip = ##t g2-\tweak stencil ##f \ff \> es4) |
```
New velocities: F4 62, F♭4 67, E♭4 67 (Hn 3); A♭3 62, G3 67, E♭3 66 (Hn 4). The tied F♭ cannot swell in MIDI, so its attack takes the crescendo's goal. The E♭ now matches the F♭ and is no longer louder. A hidden level on the E♭ would end the printed circled hairpin early, so I left it. Pages 7 before and after are identical.

### 2. Bars 8, 24, 72: theme A's suspended E♭ resolves to a louder D in score.midi (applied)

**Problem.** The printed shape (c098) is right: crescendo on the tied E♭, peak on the barline, diminuendo through D and D♭. In MIDI the tied E♭ has one velocity, its attack. The crescendo has no target, so it overshoots and the D lands at its top. In v338: horn at 8, E♭ 44, D 56, D♭ 50, C 44. Violins at 24, E♭ 63, D 82 (the f level), D♭ 73, C 63. Violins at 72, E♭ 53, D 75, D♭ 69, C 63. At 24 the resolution sounds a dynamic and a half above the suspension.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 348 (resolution softer, "a decrease in intensity"); PDF 353, Ex. 21-14 (7th over the moving bass F→B♭: E♭ is the dissonance, D the resolution). c198 fixed the same overshoot at 58 with hidden levels.

**Fix (applied; hidden levels only).** The crescendo's goal goes on the E♭'s attack, the same level on the barline, and the diminuendo gets a target one step below at the next bar.
```
aOneHnOne 7–9:   es'4~-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \< | es'4-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \> d'4 des'4 | c'4.-\tweak stencil ##f \p bes8 ...
Vln I/II 23–25:  es''4~(-\tweak stencil ##f \mf \< | es''4-\tweak stencil ##f \mf \> d''4) des''4 | c''4.(-\tweak stencil ##f \mp bes'8 ...
Vln I/II 71–73:  (Vln I an octave up) es''4~(-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \< | es''4-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \> d''4) des''4 | c''4.(-\tweak stencil ##f \p bes'8 ...
```
New velocities: horn 50, 48, 46, 44; violins at 24: 73, 70, 66, 63; at 72: 63, 60, 56, 53. Each falls from the suspension to the resolution, and each line ends at its printed starting level. The printed hairpins are unchanged (checked on pages 1, 2 and 6).

### 3. Bars 23–24 and 71–72, Vln I and II: the suspension and its resolution are on separate bows (applied; answers the c018 protection)

**Problem.** `es''4~\< | es''4\> d''4( des''4)`: the tied E♭ takes one bow and the D starts the next, so the bow changes on the resolution. The horn's own statement at 7–8 is slurred through, and so are the motto tails at 33 (B.cl.) and 67–68 (Hn 1). Only the strings re-attack the resolution.

**Evidence.** In the images of the reading, Mozart's Ex. 21-17 (PDF 355) slurs each 7–6 pair, Ex. 21-32 (PDF 363) slurs the long dissonant note to the next one, and Beethoven's Ex. 21-37 (PDF 365) ties and slurs the suspended chord into its resolution. Dvořák 9/ii bars 18 and 39 (corpus, above) slur the dissonance to its resolution under a hairpin. The resolution is meant to be a release (PDF 348). A fresh bow on it is an articulation.

**Why this can change.** c018 protected the bowing of 23–30 and 71–78 because c006 planned the bow directions: the crescendo at 27 on an up-bow, the diminuendos at 28 and 30 on down-bows, and the up-bow at 28 (c118, c258). The fix keeps the number of bows and every direction. The pickup is up, E♭–E♭–D is down (the diminuendo now sits on a down-bow, Adler part 1 PDF 38), D♭ is up, 25–26 is down, and so on as before. The bow is 3 beats, about 3.5 s at p–mf.

**Fix (applied, aOne.ily and aTwo.ily, Vln I and Vln II):** `es''4~( | es''4 d''4) des''4 | c''4.( bes'8 aes'4 | g'2)` (with the hidden levels of finding 2). The parts book is still 30 pages, one page per part except the harp.

### 4. Bars 35–36: bassoon 1 above the A♭ pedal plays louder than the bass clarinet solo in score.midi (applied)

**Problem.** After its breath at 34/35, bassoon 1's E♭3 goes onto a solo voice that has no dynamic, so it sounds at velocity 53. The printed level is pp. The bass clarinet solo descends p>pp over it at 44 and then 32.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 367–369: the pedal and the voices held over it stay static under the moving voice. Pergolesi's continuo (Ex. 21-21, PDF 359) is marked *p sempre* under the singers.

**Fix (applied, trans.ily):** `es2.~-\tweak stencil ##f \pp | es2 r4` in transBsnOne. Velocity is now 25, as at 33–34. Nothing printed changes.

### 5. Bar 78 b3: clarinet 2's pickup into 79 sounds at velocity 41 under an f (applied; same fault as 1 and 4, outside the reading)

**Problem.** In the score the f sits on Cl 2's rest (`\tag #'score { r2\f }`, c145), and the G4 is played by a solo voice that has no dynamic.

**Fix (applied, aTwo.ily):** `g'4-\tag #'part \f -\tag #'score -\tweak stencil ##f \f`. G4 is now 58. A side effect is that Cl 2's printed p< at 75–76 now rises in MIDI (41, 43, 45; it was flat at 38), as printed.

### 6. Bars 75–78: bassoon 1 stays at p in score.midi while bassoon 2 and the cellos crescendo to f (not applied)

**Problem.** Bsn 1's hairpin, mf and f are part-only (`-\tag #'part`), so in the score its voice stays at velocity 32 until 79. It doubles the cello and Hn 2 counter-melody.

**Tried.** A hidden score-only `\<` on 75 b3 and a hidden mf on 77 b3. The build was clean, but the hidden hairpin cut the printed Bsn 2 hairpin on the shared staff short at 75 b3. A hidden `\f` at 78 b1 conflicts with Bsn 2's `\f` (two warnings). I reverted it. A possible route: a hidden `\mf` on 76 b3 or 77 b3 only, checked against page 6.
