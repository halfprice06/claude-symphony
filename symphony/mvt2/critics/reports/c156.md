## Findings

**1. Bass clarinet, bar 8 beat 2: the parenthesised low C has no fallback in the part (applied, part only).**
The sounding B♭1 (`bes,,4`, written C3) is the only note in the movement below the bass clarinet's standard written E♭3. Adler PDF 304 prints the low C in parentheses, as PDF 302 marks the bass's low C "not found on every instrument". The staff name and part title already say "(low C)", which tells the contractor what to book. The part gives a player on an E♭ instrument nothing to play. An octave-higher B♭2 is harmless here: harp LH sounds B♭1–B♭2 and Bsn 2 B♭2 on the same beat, so the bass note stays in the low octave.
Fix in aOne.ily: `f,4( bes,,4-\tag #'part ^\markup \small \italic "(no low C: 8va)" es,4)`. In score.ly the bass clarinet staff now strips part tags (`<< \clef bass \global \removeWithTag #'part \bassCl >>`), as the other wind staves do, so the text appears only in the part.

**2. Violas, bar 48: "sotto voce" is tagged part-only but prints in the score (not applied; outside this lens).**
In bSec.ily, `-\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "sotto voce"` is meant for the part, but the Violas staff in score.ly (like Violins II, Violoncellos, Double basses and Harp RH) does not apply `\removeWithTag #'part`. This is the only part-tagged item on those staves. The word is useful in the score as well, so the simplest fix is to drop the tag and make the source match the printed result: `r8 a8-_\pp^\markup \italic "sotto voce"`. The printed score and the 8-page layout do not change.
