## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 64-65: the bass group does not take part in the diminuendo into the retransition, and the cellos re-strike B flat2 under the held chord (applied)

**Problem.** Bar 64 is the allargando bar where the A-minor sound slips by semitone into V of A flat (E to E flat in the basses, G sharp to G in the violas, B to B flat in the cellos). Vln II and violas carry it as one gesture: `b4(\>\upbow d'4 | des'2.)~\pp` and `gis2(\>\upbow | g2.)~\pp`. The bass line that carries the same move has no hairpin:
- Cellos: `a,2:32\p d,4( b,8 bes,8) |` then `bes,2.~\pp` at 65. The slur stops on the anticipation and the cellos re-strike the same B flat2 on the downbeat of 65, the only string attack on that downbeat.
- Basses: `a,,2\p d,4 e,4 |` then E flat2 pp (4 soli) at 65.
- Bass trombone: `a,2\p d,4 e,4 |` then rests, while Tbn 1 above it diminuendos `f4.->\> e8\p`.

So in performance the lower parts arrive at 65 one level louder than the upper strings and drop at the barline, and the cellos add an attack where everyone else is holding. The E-E flat semitone is the cyclic thread of the movement (HISTORY c390: "E to E flat at 64-65 is in the basses only"), so it is the one join that should not be stepped.

**Evidence.** Casanelles PDF 149-150: dynamic change is a continuous crossfade through the sound, and a stepped change is the "lifeless" result of rendering, not of performance. PDF 152: notation cannot encode the transition, so the hairpin has to be written. PDF 150-153: a same-pitch re-attack with nothing changed is the mechanical effect; under held notes the transition is hidden by overlapping (tying). PDF 290: balance and fade order come from written levels. Rimsky-Korsakov PDF 130: diminuendo by removing brass, then wind, then strings; here the brass stops at 64 b4 still at p while the strings above it are already fading.

**Fix (applied).** No pitch, rhythm or layout change.
- `sections/bSec.ily`, bSecVc bar 64: `a,2:32\p d,4(\>\upbow b,8 bes,8~ |` (up-bow and diminuendo like Vln II and violas; the anticipation tied).
- `sections/retr.ily`, retrVc bar 65-66: `bes,2.)~\pp | ... bes,2.\>\downbow |` (the slur closes at 65 as in Vln II and violas; the new down-bow at 66 for the fade to niente, as c398 gave Vln II and violas, so the bow still changes about every 6 s).
- `sections/bSec.ily`, bSecCb bar 64: `a,,2\p^\markup \italic "arco" d,4\> e,4 |` (ends on the pp at 65).
- `sections/bSec.ily`, bSecBassTbn bar 64: `a,2\p d,4\> e,4 <>\! |`.

This does not undo c398 (it extends its bowing to the cellos) or c278 (65-66 stay tied). The Tbn 1 quotation and the sf>p at 63 are untouched.

### 2. Bar 64: bass trombone re-strikes A2 after its sf> (checked, no change)

The bass trombone holds `a,1\sf\>` at 63 and plays A2 again at p on 64 b1. This is not the mechanical repeat of PDF 150: Tbn 1's tenuto A4 falls on the same beat, the basses enter arco on A1 there, and the string tremolos start their p bar there, so the re-strike marks the allargando bar in octaves with the melody. Keep it.

### 3. Bars 37-40: viola triplet figure identical in 38 and 39, and no hairpins under the flute (checked, no change)

The figure is the same in both bars, but the basses (B flat-G flat, then E flat-C) and Vln II change the chord under it, so the repeat is not "nothing changed" (PDF 150). A flat accompaniment is the "transparent legato" of PDF 155 and agrees with c139 (no swell under a solo). The swells start at 41, where c038 put them. Keep it.

### 4. Bar 86: crescendo written on held notes in violas, cellos and basses (checked, no change)

Violas `<c' f'>2.\<`, cellos and basses tied A flat with `\<` into the pp at 87. This is the "evolution" of PDF 160 and is correct in the score. score.midi renders it as a step at 87 (velocity cannot change inside a held note; PDF 161 on volume automation). The mockup pipeline draws its own CC lanes from the hairpins (c278 row 15), so I made no MIDI change. A spacer-based CC11 ramp in 86 would add columns to a bar of dotted halves and risk the spacing (c300), so I did not test one.

### 5. Balance and fade order elsewhere (follows)

Every solo entry is one level over its accompaniment, and every release except 64 removes brass first, then woodwind, then strings (81-83 brass and double reeds to niente at 82, clarinets and strings later; 89-92 horns out at 90, Cl 2 morendo with the strings to ppp). The two tutti peaks use accent layering (PDF 146) only at 63, where the quotation needs it.
