## Findings

### 1. Score omits the solo violinist's mute before 25 (applied)

**Bars:** 17-25, Vln I.

**Problem.** From 17 the leader plays "solo, senza sord." while the section is muted. At 22 the score says "gli altri con sord., espr., sul D", and at 25 it says only "tutti". The Vln I part has one more line at 22, "(solo player: mute on, join at bar 25)" (`-\tag #'part` in aOne.ily line 67), but the score does not. A conductor reading the score sees "tutti" and cannot tell whether the leader rejoins muted or open. That matters because 23-30 is the first violin statement of theme A, and its colour is chosen.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 291, item 3: "the score must contain every detail for every instrument, including special instructions concerning bowing, tonguing, or articulations." Item 1 adds that nothing should be left for the conductor to work out. App. A, PDF 312, gives "con sordino" and "tutti" as the standard terms. The part already follows c468's plan (the leader has about 8 s to mute), so the music is right; only the score lacks the instruction.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-545/work).** sections/aOne.ily, Vln I bar 25:
```
c''4.(-\tweak stencil ##f \mp ^\markup \italic "tutti, con sord." bes'8 aes'4 |
```
(was `"tutti"`). The same word now appears in the part, where it agrees with the 22 instruction. Only the text in bar 25 changed on page 2. Pages 1 and 3-8 are pixel-identical at 40 dpi, and the systems and page breaks did not move. "ord." at 27 cancels sul D, as it did before.

### 2. Cl 1·2 entry at 60 without a restated dynamic: close the open item (no change)

**Bars:** 59-60, Cl 1·2 (open since c528, repeated at c538).

**Problem raised earlier.** After a two-beat rest at 59 b3-4, the clarinets re-enter at 60 with a crescendo hairpin and no new dynamic mark.

**Assessment against the reading.** Adler restates a dynamic in a part when the player enters after a rest at a new level. In Ex. XVIII-10b (PDF 301), Oboe 1 gets *mf* after its rest because the level changes. Oboe 2 gets nothing extra where it continues at the same level. Exx. XVIII-5/6 (PDF 299) concern long rests and landmarks, not a two-beat breath. Both clarinet parts print *f* at 59, two beats before 60, inside the same phrase, and the hairpin into *ff* at 61 follows. Nothing in ch. 18 asks for a second *f*. I recommend recording the item as closed rather than optional.

### 3. Shared-staff indications at 89-92 (recorded, no change)

**Bars:** 89-92, Cl 1·2.

"morendo" stands above the staff over Cl 1's rest, but it applies to Cl 2's held E♭4. Ex. XVIII-10a puts each player's marks next to that player's voice.
