## Findings

### 1. Bar 60: bassoon 1 leaves the counter-line after one bar (applied)

**Problem.** The horn 1/cello counter-line runs 57 b3–60 (G♭ | F E♭ F | G♭ E♭ F♭ | E♭ F G♭. F). Bassoon 1 joins it at the f of 59 in unison with the cellos, which completes the string + woodwind + brass mix. At 60, though, bassoon 1 holds E♭3 for the whole bar while horn and cellos go on with E♭–F–G♭–F. Its colour is added for four beats and then withdrawn in the line's last bar, the bar that leads into the 61 climax. The held E♭3 adds nothing to the harmony, because trombone 2 already holds E♭3 and the violas sound E♭4 in every sextuplet.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 176: Mozart keeps "an established color intact long enough to carry out its assignment"; "the listener must not be overburdened with shifting colors". PDF 178: in *Meistersinger* each element combines a string, a woodwind and a brass instrument. c087 chose to leave 57–58 without bassoon (c057's economy reason). This fix leaves 57–58 alone. It only stops the colour from being dropped one bar after it was added.

**Fix (applied).** `sections/bSec.ily`, bSecBsnOne bars 59–60:
```lilypond
  \partCombineApart r1 | r1 | ges2\f es4( fes4 | es4\< f4 ges4. f8) \breathe |
```
The bassoon now matches the cellos' slur and rhythm. Bassoon 2's A♭2 keeps the bass. The only new all-pairs entries are bassoon 1 an octave below horn 1 (the doubling itself).

### 2. Bar 13: the harp's D♭4 sits inside the horn's descent (applied; closes the c073 item "harp RH hidden octave 13→14")

**Problem.** At 13 b2 the harp RH strikes G3–B♭3–D♭4 and lets it ring. The horn then descends E♭4–D♭4–C4–B♭3 through that chord. On the horn's D♭4 the harp is on the same pitch. On the horn's C4 (b3) the ringing D♭4 is a semitone above the solo. At 14 b2 the harp's D♭4 then falls to C4 while bassoon 2 falls E♭3–C3, which is the hidden octave c073 named. The horn already carries the seventh D♭ itself.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 179: the background is "sustained harmony" set apart from the theme. PDF 184: Schoenberg makes sure the principal voice is not "made subservient" to a subordinate one and gives each fragment a place where it will be heard. In 7–14 the harp is the only plucked background, and here it is the one part that enters the solo's register on the solo's own pitches. c057's rule (no harp note above the horn at its attack) still holds.

**Fix (applied).** `sections/aOne.ily`, aOneHarpR bar 13: `r4 <g bes>2\arpeggio` (was `<g bes des'>`). The horn line and the harp LH are unchanged, so the protected horn solo is not touched. The C4 at 14 b2 now enters from B♭3, against the bassoon's descent.

### 3. Bars 57–59: the counter-line starts on the bass's own pitches (no change; recorded)

At 57 b3 the cellos' G♭3 is the same note as the bass clarinet and bassoon 2. At 58 b2 they meet again on E♭3, and at 59 b1 horn 1 (G♭4) and the cellos (G♭3) are in octaves with the bass (G♭2). Each of these is approached by contrary motion, so there is no voice-leading fault. The cellos act as Schoenberg's continuity instrument (PDF 187). The line grows out of the bass and becomes independent from 58 b3, and the horn carries it an octave higher throughout. Separating them would mean changing the protected counter-line or dropping the octave doubling of the bass (bassoon 2 at the octave of the basses, which is Mozart's own layout, PDF 176). I recommend no change, and I record this so that a later critic does not reopen it.

### 4. Bar 58: the bass quarters carry no articulation of their own (suggestion, not applied)

**Problem.** At 58 the bass (B.cl., Bsn 2, Cb: B♭–E♭–A♭–D♭ in quarters) moves in the register the cello counter-line has just left. Every other element of 57–60 is slurred. The two lower lines therefore differ only in rhythm.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 178, observation 2: "specific and unique articulation" separates elements. PDF 179: "varied articulations will help the listener immeasurably". In Ex. XV-26 (PDF 177) Wagner's bass is marked "sehr markiert" while the tune is "ausdrucksvoll".

**Fix (optional).** Add tenuto to the four quarters in bSecBassCl, bSecBsnTwo and bSecCb bar 58, e.g. `bes,,4-- es,4-- aes,,4-- \once \stemUp des,4--` in the basses. This is engraving only and the MIDI is unchanged. I did not apply it, because the build 53–61 is on the do-not-touch list (c108) and the gain is small.

### 5. Open item "viola B♭3 at 22": no longer applies (no change)

The violas now rest at 22, and the bar's answer is in the divided cellos (`g8 aes8 bes4 g4` over E♭3). On beat 3 the cello's G3 is a sixth below the violins' upbeat E♭4, and nothing crosses or covers it. c054's concern was an accompaniment note moving under the upbeat to 23 (Adler PDF 245). That is met, and the item can be closed.
