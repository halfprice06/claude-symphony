## Findings

### 1. Bass clarinet part, bar 8 b2: show the alternative for players without low C as a small parenthesised note. Applied in the scratch copy.

Bar 8 (part only). The part prints written C3 (sounding B♭1) inside the slurred figure G3–C3–F3 (written) with the text "(no low C: 8va)". The text does not say which notes move, and moving the whole figure an octave changes its shape. Rimsky-Korsakov's Table B (PDF 35, image) starts the bass clarinet's low "full, dark" register below the clarinet's written E3; by my reading of the image its first note is written C3, so the low C is part of the instrument as the treatise prints it, and it should stay the main note. The alternative is for instruments that stop at E♭, which is what Adler shows with a parenthesised note (adler2 PDF 304, cited in c156). The B♭ is also sounded by the harp LH (B♭0–B♭1) and Bsn 2 (B♭2), so a player who takes the upper note loses nothing from the bass; the upper note is a unison with Bsn 2.

Fix (sections/aOne.ily, aOneBassCl, bar 8), now in /tmp/critic-236/work:
