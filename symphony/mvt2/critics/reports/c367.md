## What else I studied

I read BRIEF.md, LENSES.md, movement-2-plan.md, HISTORY.md (the standing list and cycles 300–366), and the earlier reports on this reading, c227 (accepted: violas div. at 80–81) and c287 (no change). I read every section file of v367 and looked at all 8 score pages at 140 dpi, plus bars 75–78 at 200 dpi. Rimsky's PDF 112 examples point to the layout of Ex. 171. The Antar Adagio there has divided Vln II and violas con sord. sustained, a held flute and horn, a florid clarinet and harp arpeggios. I compared that with bars 37–60 of this score, where the motion is in the violas and the harp and the sustained parts are in Vln II and the winds. In Dvořák 9/ii (reference-corpus/mutopia/DvorakA_O95), the middle section also keeps its motion in the strings: the clarinets and flutes carry the melody over held strings and pizzicato basses. This score's choice is therefore the one the main model makes, not a fault.

## Findings

No change is recommended. All sixteen principles are either followed or not applicable; where a bar departs from the letter of a rule, it has a recorded reason. I made no edits in /tmp/critic-367/work. It is an unchanged copy of v367, whose build-warnings.txt is empty and whose analysis.txt shows 0 outer-voice parallels, so I did not rebuild or regenerate the parts book. The points below are ranked by how close each comes to a real weakness.

### 1. Bar 60: the moving line is partly in the brass (no change; c227, c349)

**Problem.** PDF 108 gives the moving parts of a chord chain to the woodwind. At 60, Tbn 2 moves E♭3–G♭3 in halves and Hn 1 moves E♭4–F4–G♭4 with Bsn 1 and the cellos.

**Evidence.** Hn 1's line is the counter-melody of 57–60, a melodic part rather than harmonic filler, and Rimsky's rule is about harmonic filler. Tbn 2's G♭3 is the chord seventh that resolves to F3 at 61. c227 tested holding E♭3 and rejected it because the low brass would then sit on a bare A♭–E♭ through the crescendo. The pp< entry at 60 is protected (c349). I have no new book reason to reopen it.

### 2. Bars 53–56: harp triplets with no sustained string harmony (no change; c049, c317, c359)

**Problem.** Rimsky's model Adagio texture (PDF 112, Ex. 171) sets moving accompaniment against sustained divided strings. At 53–56 the violas and basses rest. Between the violins' unison melody and the cellos' bass there are only the harp triplets, Bsn 1's D♭3 and the Cl 1 + Hn 1 counter-line.

**Evidence.** I worked out on paper a pp divided-viola layer: `<des' f'>2 <c' es'>2 | <bes des'>2 <bes es'>2 | <bes des'>2 <bes c'>2 | <c' es'>2 <bes des'>2`. It lies on B♭3–F4, which is the register of the Cl 1/Hn 1 counter-line (D♭4, F4, G♭4, C4, D♭4). It would cover that line, against PDF 112's "comparison of one timbre with another". It would also spend the violas' re-entry at 57 (mp, triplets), which c317 lists as one of the four distinct accompaniments to theme B. The thinner bars before the 57–61 build are the better choice. Not applied.

### 3. Bars 3 and 29–30: horns enclosed in a string chord without string doubling (no change; protected link, c287)

**Problem.** PDF 113 says strings and brass are seldom combined by enclosure unless the horns are doubled by divided violas or cellos. At 3, Hn 3·4 C4/A♭3 enter inside the strings' A♭2–C5 as the strings fade. At 29–30, Hn 4's E♭3 is held inside the strings with no string on that note.

**Evidence.** At 3 the strings are dying away (> to niente) while Cl 2 and B.cl. enter. This is a three-group hand-over (PDF 113 C), not a sustained mixed chord, and the link 1–6 is protected. For 29–30, c287 showed that Ex. 171 itself holds a soft horn inside divided strings, and that moving the violas onto E♭3 spaces the chord worse.

### 4. Bars 61–62 and 79–81: forte horn chords (record)

The four horns play alone at forte (PDF 108). The woodwind is superposed above them and doubles the upper harmonic parts (PDF 109 example). Cl 2 sits on the top horn rather than inside the chord. The bassoon sits inside the horns with the discord at 62 b1. Strings are added side by side, as in Ex. 172. This matches the reading closely.

### 5. Bars 87–90: muted horns, cor anglais and divided violas (record)

This passage combines three pairings the reading recommends: horn with C.a. in one chord (PDF 110), horns doubled by divided violas (PDF 113), and winds plus muted brass plus strings at pp (Ex. 174). It is protected (plagal close).

### 6. Bars 67–70: horn solo over two bassoons (record)

The concords go to the horn (B♭3, C4, D4). The discords go to the bassoons: Bsn 1's A♭3 suspension at 68 b3 and Bsn 2's D♭3 seventh at 69. This is exactly PDF 108's crossing rule for soft horn and bassoon harmony.

Nothing in this cycle changes movement II's ending, its exported themes or its quotations.

Do not touch: the wind and brass layouts at 60–62, 67–70, 79–81 and 87–90; the harp-only accompaniment at 53–56 before the violas re-enter at 57; the standing list; the 8-page layout.
