## Findings

### 1. Bars 63–64: the trombone's pickup accent weakens the quotation's syncopation (applied)
- **Problem.** Trombone 1 plays `r8 e8->\ff c'4.-> b8 bes8 a8 | a4 gis8 a8 f4.->\> e8\mf`. The pickup E3 on beat 1½ carries the same accent as the C4 that follows it on beat 2. The C4 starts on the weak beat 2 and is held through beat 3, so it is the syncopation that makes this the P head. With two accents in a row, the conflict with the metre is lost. The strings' sf on 63 b1, just before, adds to the problem. The pickup is also cut off from the note it leads to.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 51: the syncopated note conflicts with the metre because "the beginning of a note is heard as stronger than its continuation". Ex. 3-6 accents only the syncopated note.
  - A&S PDF 52: rhythmic groups often begin on an upbeat. The pickup E3 is the start of the C4's group, not a separate accented event.
  - Movement I, which this quotation must match, never accents the pickup (coda Hn 1 ff, recOne 8 and 31, dev 17/34/44/58). Its ff statements slur the pickup into the accented note and slur the turn: `r8 g8( es'4.->) … | c'4) b8( c'8) aes4.->`.
  - Nothing else changes: the pitches, ff, "marcato", the accents on C4 and F3, the diminuendo to mf, and the single statement in A minor that HISTORY protects.
- **Fix** (bSec.ily, `bSecTbnOne`):
  `r8 e8(\ff^\markup \italic "marcato"-\tag #'part ^\markup \italic "solo" c'4.->) b8 bes8 a8 | a4 gis8( a8) f4.->\> e8\mf |`
  The eighths B3–B♭3–A3 stay detached, as in movement I's unslurred development statements.

### 2. Bars 80–81: the hemiola's second and third groups have no weight (applied)
- **Problem.** The counter-melody (Vc, Hn 1·2, Bsn 1) plays A♭ (half) | B♭ (half, tied over the bar) | D♭ (half), which is c048's 3/2 grouping. Around it, bar 81 has the bass E♭ on its downbeat and the general diminuendo starts on 81 b1. The B♭ on 80 b3 and the D♭ on 81 b2 are the two attacks that create the 3/2. Both enter unmarked, and the D♭ (the seventh of V7) enters a beat into the diminuendo. Without them the counter-melody sounds like syncopated quarters in 3/4, not a broadening into the cadence.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 51–52, Ex. 3-8: the hemiola is heard because the long values on beats that are weak in 3/4 are emphasised.
  - PDF 51: a note held from a weak beat through a strong one (the B♭ tied into 81) is a syncopation and needs to be heard as one.
  - PDF 50: dissonant notes are heard as accented. The D♭ is the V7's seventh and should not be the softest attack of the three.
  - The score already supports the grouping elsewhere: Hn 3's F♭–E♭ 9–8 and the violas' G4–E♭4 also change on 81 b2. Only the counter-melody lacks a mark.
- **Fix** (aTwo.ily; tenutos only; hairpins, dynamics and bowing unchanged, so the shared Bsn and Hn staves still match):
  - `aTwoVc`: `aes2 bes4--~ | bes4\> des'2-- |`
  - `aTwoHnOne`, `aTwoHnTwo`: `aes2( bes4--~ | bes4\> des'2-- |`
  - `aTwoBsnOne`: `aes2( bes4--~ | bes4\> des'2-- |`
  - The tenuto inside the diminuendo is played as weight and length, not as a louder attack. This is the convention the score already uses at 61–62 and in c126's slur-plus-tenuto wind repeats.

### 3. Bar 88: clarinet 1's struck-again D♭5 under a slur has no articulation (applied)
- **Problem.** The coda tail reads `des''2.(\pp | des''2. | c''2.~ | c''2)`: two D♭5s under one slur with neither a tie nor an articulation. Players will read it either as a tie or as a slurred repeat, which is not possible on a wind instrument. The harmony changes under it from IV6 (87) to iv (88, with F♭ in the cor anglais, Hn 4 and violas), so the repeat is deliberate: the D♭ is struck again on the new chord.
- **Evidence.**
  - A&S PDF 55, Ex. 3-17b: a tone struck again instead of held is a separate attack, so it needs its own sign.
  - The score's own convention for soft-tongued wind repeats is slur plus tenuto (c126, aOne 7–14 bassoons; aTwo 72 Cl 2).
  - Here the repeat also marks the chromatic iv, which A&S PDF 50 says is heard as accented.
- **Fix** (coda.ily, `codaClOne`): `des''2.(\pp | des''2.-- | c''2.~ | c''2)\> r4\! |`
  The pitches and the D♭–C on top of the plagal close are unchanged. If the composer meant a held note, write `des''2.~` instead. Either way, the bar must say which.
