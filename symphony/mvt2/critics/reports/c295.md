## Findings

### 1. Player label missing at the start of a system on six shared staves (applied)

**Bars:** 41 Fl 1·2 (Fl 1), 45 Ob 1·2 (Ob 1), 61 Tbn 1·2 (Tbn 2), 75 Cl 1·2 (Cl 2), 79 Hn 1·2 (a2), 87 Cl 1·2 (Cl 1).

**Problem.** On each of these staves one player (or both in unison) continues from the previous system, but the combiner prints "1.", "2." or "a2" only where the mode changes, which was on the previous system or page. A conductor arriving at 61 (new page, Largamente) sees a single line in "Tbn. 1·2" and cannot tell that it is trombone 2 and that trombone 1 enters at 63; at 79 (new page, the ff tutti) "Hn. 1·2" shows one line with no "a2", so it reads as horn 1 alone; at 75 and 87 the Cl 1·2 line has changed hands since the last label (Cl 2 at 75, Cl 1 at 87) and nothing says so.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 295: on a reduced score "it is imperative that all the instruments must be clearly marked in all systems appearing on a page". PDF 294: the cutout score labels each instrument "every time it enters". PDF 301, Ex. XVIII-10a: the shared Oboe 1·2 staff carries "1." at the point where one player continues alone. Appendix PDF 312 gives "1.", "2." and "a2" as the standard terms. The score already follows this at 57 (Fl "1." restated, bSec.ily line 21), at 63 (Fl "1."), at 53 and 57 (Hn "1."); c025 accepted the same restatement for the horns at a page turn.

**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-295/work).** Score-only markups on the first note of each system, the form the score already uses:
- bSec.ily, `bSecFluteOne` 41: `r2 aes''4.(\pp\<-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." bes''8 |`
- bSec.ily, `bSecOboeOne` 45: `des''2-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." es''8 des''8 c''4) |`
- bSec.ily, `bSecTbnTwo` 61: `f2--\f-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "2." aes2-- |`
- aTwo.ily, `aTwoClTwo` 75: `es'2(\p\<-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "2." des'4 |`
- aTwo.ily, `aTwoHnTwo` 79: `des'4\f-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "a2" c'4 bes4) \breathe |`. In unison mode the combiner prints horn 2's scripts, not horn 1's: the same markup on `aTwoHnOne` does not print (it is in the scratch copy too and can be left out).
- coda.ily, `codaClOne` 87: `\tweak Stem.direction #DOWN des''2.(\pp-\tag #'score ^\markup \bold "1." |`

All are tagged `'score`, so the parts (each player's own part, Adler PDF 301) are unchanged. No notes, dynamics or MIDI events change.

### 2. B♭6 at 76 and 79 sits on five ledger lines (not applied; recorded)

**Bars:** Fl 1 and Vln I 76, 79–80; Vln I grace run 78.

**Problem.** Adler PDF 300, item 8a: use 8va "as much as possible to avoid more than four ledger lines". B♭6 is on the fifth ledger line.

**Evidence against changing it.** Gould PDF 47 allows woodwinds and strings up to five ledger lines and says octave signs are unhelpful because players tie fingering to written pitch; MOLA PDF 5 asks for no 8va in parts. Adler's rule is listed as a copying shortcut, "as much as possible", not as a requirement. The point was closed at c025 and c075 ("B♭6 at pitch", standing do-not-touch). Five lines is the limit; nothing goes higher (analysis.txt: Fl and Vln I top B♭6). Keep.

Recommendation: leave as is; if the composer wants one language, change only the sticks markings. This is a consistency point, not an error.
