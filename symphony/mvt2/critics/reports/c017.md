## Findings

### 1. Bars 15–22: two-layer block texture under the oboe–violin dialogue (applied)

**Problem.** In the b-phrase, oboe 1 and Vln I trade two-bar phrases. Under them, Vln II, violas, cellos and basses all play the same half + quarter rhythm in every bar, and the basses double the cellos throughout. When the melody holds a note (bars 16, 18, 20, 22), nothing moves except the beat-3 chord change. The dialogue is end to end with a dead bar between entries, so it never overlaps (the point left open since cycle 7).

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 172 defines orchestral polyphony as three or more simultaneous elements. PDF 175 and 179 say each element needs a distinct rhythm. In *Meistersinger* (PDF 177) and *Scheherazade* (PDF 178–179) the inner layer moves while the theme holds. Principle 2 is not met here: four string parts share one rhythm.

**Fix (applied).** The violas answer in each bar where the melody holds, using the rhythm of the bar just heard: the oboe's `4. 8 4` at 16, Vln I's `8 8 4 4` at 18, an exact octave-lower echo of the oboe's bar 19 at 20, and Vln I's bar 21 rhythm at 22. Every note is a chord tone or a passing/neighbour note. I checked for parallels against Vln I, Vln II and the cellos. One option I tried, `es'8 f'8 es'4 des'4` at 18, made octaves with Vln I's E♭–D♭, so I rejected it. `sections/aOne.ily`, `aOneVla` bars 15–22:

```lilypond
  es'2.\pp | des'4.( c'8 bes4) | f'2 f'4 | c'8( es'8 c'4 des'4) |
  f'2 e'4 | c'4( des'8 c'8 bes4) | aes2 aes4 | g8( aes8 g4 bes4) |
```

The bars where the melody moves (15, 17, 19, 21) are unchanged, so the violas never compete with the tune. 23–30 stays the plain statement (cycle 7 do-not-touch).

### 2. Bars 57–60: horn 1's counter-line is covered and has no string or wind colour (applied)

**Problem.** At 57 the theme B tutti is Fl 1 + Ob 1 + Vln I + Vln II at mf→f, with clarinets, bassoons, horn 2 and the viola triplets. Horn 1's counter-line (A♭–G♭ | F–E♭–F | G♭–E♭ | E♭–F–G♭) is the only second melodic element, and it is played by one horn in its middle register. Meanwhile the cellos, Bsn 2 and basses all play the same bass line, which needs only two of them.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 177–178: in *Meistersinger* the second theme is Vln I + Cl 1, "octave-lower cellos plus Horn I doubled at pitch", and every element combines a string, a woodwind and a brass colour. PDF 184 (Schoenberg m. 429): a principal gesture that "would otherwise be made subservient" is reinforced by doubling. PDF 184 (Bloch): instruments are added to each element as the climax nears.

**Fix (applied).** The cellos leave the bass on 57 beat 3 and double horn 1 an octave lower, "espr.". The bass clarinet, which rests at 53–60, takes the cellos' old bass line and joins Bsn 2 and the basses. It enters at mp to match Bsn 2 and continues into its ff D♭ at 61. On my first attempt the cellos entered on 57 beat 1 (A♭) and made B♭–A♭ octaves with Vln I at 56–57. Beat 1 now stays on the bass F, and the cellos join on G♭. `sections/bSec.ily`:

```lilypond

% bSecBassCl, bars 57-60 (was R1*4)
  f2\mp\< ges2 | bes,4 es4 aes,4 des4 | ges2\f g2 | aes,1\< |
```

The bass line itself is unchanged (Cb lowest), so the outer-voice result is the same.

### 3. Bars 45–46: the middleground stops one statement too early (suggestion, not applied)

**Problem.** The viola triplets, the B section's established middleground, stop at 45 just as theme B's head moves to Vln I and the cellos. For two bars the texture is a melody over sustained notes, and then the quotation enters at 47 over the same sustained notes.

**Evidence.** Adler PDF 176: "keep an established color intact long enough to carry out its assignment". PDF 181: the background figure carries the passage forward.

**Fix (optional).** `bSecVla` 45–46: `\stemDown \tuplet 3/2 4 { f8(\pp bes des' bes f bes) } \tuplet 3/2 4 { f8( bes des' bes f bes) } | \tuplet 3/2 4 { f8( bes des' bes f bes) } \tuplet 3/2 4 { ges8( bes des' bes ges bes) } | \stemNeutral` then `b1` from 47 as now. I did not test this, and the composer may prefer the thinner texture before the quotation.
