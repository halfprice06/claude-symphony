## Findings

### 1. Flute 1's bass clarinet cue at 34–36 is a crotchet late, and 35–36 print as a "2" rest under cue notes (APPLIED)

- **Bars:** Fl 1 part 34–36. The same fault would hit any new cue from the bass clarinet.
- **Problem:** genparts.py has `\addQuote "bassClQ" { \bassCl }`, with no tag filter. At bar 8 `bassCl` holds both the score's `\tag #'score bes,,4` and the part-only ossia chord `\tag #'part <bes,, \parenthesize bes,>4`. The quote therefore has four crotchets in bar 8 (a test build reports "barcheck failed at: 1/4" at aOne.ily:17), and everything quoted after bar 8 is one crotchet late. In the v285 Flute 1 part, bar 34 shows a crotchet D♭ and then the half note C4 (the solo's C2–B♭ shifted by a beat). Bars 35–36 come out as a compressed "35–36 / 2" rest with the cue notes hung under it, and "poco rit." stands over that rest instead of over 35.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 5: cues "must be audible and obvious to the musician reading the part". PDF 8 and Example 7 (PDF 12) warn that extracted parts are not error-free and must be proofread.
- **Fix (genparts.py):** `\addQuote "bassClQ" { \removeWithTag #'score \bassCl }`. The cue now reads C4 half, B♭3 | A♭3 dotted half tied | A♭3 B♭3 C4, which is the bass clarinet's 34–36 an octave up as c165 intended. Bars 34, 35 and 36 are separate, with "poco rit." over 35. I also gave `corAnglaisQ` a `\removeWithTag #'part` filter, because finding 3 adds a part-only cue inside `corAnglais`. Without the filter, Horn 1's C.a. cue at 49–50 would run a bar late.

### 2. Viola part: "poco allargando" of bar 78 prints under bar 65, beside "Tempo I" (APPLIED)

- **Bars:** viola part 65 and 78 (open since c269; c275 and c276 left it with no fix).
- **Problem:** The word belongs to the system that starts at 78. It was printed about four spaces above that system and half a space under the "pp" of bar 65 on the system above. A violist reads "Tempo I … poco allargando" at 65, thirteen bars early. It sat so high because its right end overlapped the up-bow sign at 80 in this tightly spaced line.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 5 (tempo changes must be indicated on all parts); PDF 8 and Example 7 (PDF 12), floating markings in extracted parts. Example 3 (PDF 10) prints the tempo word at a line start above the clef.
- **Fix (sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoVla, part only):** before bar 78, `\tag #'part { \once \override Score.MetronomeMark.X-offset = #-4 \once \override Score.MetronomeMark.extra-offset = #'(0 . -0.8) }`. The word moves left over the clef and key, clear of the up-bow, and down onto its own system, about one space above the 78 slur. The score is unchanged.

### 3. Four players wait 21–52 bars with no cue (APPLIED, part only)

- **Bars and players:**
  - Clarinet 1 rests 1–52 and enters pp on the pickup at 53 b4, after five tempo changes.
  - Clarinet 2 rests 7–56 and enters mp at 57.
  - The cor anglais rests 1–31 and enters p at 32.
  - Violin I rests 31–51 and enters with theme B's pickup at 52 b4.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 5: "Logical cues are expected during long periods of rest, the cues being transposed to the reading key of the instrument." Example 4 (PDF 10) shows a named cue in small notes before the entry. c135 cued ten players but not these four.
- **Fix:**
  - **bSecClOne, 51–52:** `R1*6 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \transposedCueDuring #"corAnglaisQ" #UP c' { <>^\markup \tiny "C.a." R1*2 } } |`
  - **bSecClTwo, 55–56:** `R1*2 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \transposedCueDuring #"clOneQ" #UP c' { <>^\markup \tiny "Cl. 1" R1*2 } } |`. This needs a new quote in genparts.py, `\addQuote "clOneQ" { \removeWithTag #'part \clOne }`, and score.ly now filters clOne: `\pc \removeWithTag #'part \clOne …`.
  - **bSecVlnOne, 50–51:** `R1*4 | R1 | \tag #'score { R1*2 } \tag #'part { \cueDuring #"corAnglaisQ" #UP { <>^\markup \tiny "C.a." R1*2 } } |`
  - **transCorAnglais, 31:** `\tag #'score R2. \tag #'part { \transposedCueDuring #"bassClQ" #DOWN c''' { <>^\markup \tiny "B.cl." R2. } } |`. The solo is printed two octaves up, B♭4–C5–E♭5–G5, instead of five ledger lines down.
- **Notes:** genparts' `\transpose bes c'` does not reach quoted notes, but it does transpose the pitch argument of `\transposedCueDuring`. So `c'` prints the clarinet cues at written pitch: Cl 1 B♭4 A♭4 | G4 A♭4, and Cl 2 C5 A♭4 | D4 E♭4. I checked every cue note against the parts at 250 dpi. All four parts stay on one page.

### 4. Score page 2: the system separators print over bar numbers 15, 23 and 31 (APPLIED)

- **Bars:** 15, 23, 31 (score p. 2; the other pages have room).
- **Problem:** `\slashSeparator` is drawn at the left margin, halfway between systems. On page 2 the three systems are close together, and the bar number of each system's first bar, below the system above, falls on the slashes. At the podium "15" and "23" are half covered.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 4 (bar numbers below the system, in one position throughout); PDF 8 (proofread the vertical as well as the horizontal).
- **Fix (score.ly \paper):** `system-separator-markup = \markup \translate #'(-4 . 0) \slashSeparator`. The slashes move about 5 mm into the margin (they stay about 10 mm from the paper edge), and every number is clear. Breaks and the 8-page layout are unchanged. The slash separators themselves (c055) are kept.

### 5. Viola part: "poco sostenuto" of 48 is stacked under the hairpin and pp of 44–45 (APPLIED)

- **Bars:** viola part 47–48 (open since c275).
- **Problem:** "sotto voce" (48) sat on the staff, so "poco sostenuto" stacked above it, half a space below the system above's hairpin end. It read as a mark for bar 44.
- **Reading:** MOLA PDF 8, Example 7 (PDF 12).
- **Fix:**
  - **bSecVla bar 48:** `\pp -\tag #'score ^\markup \italic "sotto voce" -\tag #'part _\markup \italic "sotto voce"`. The score is unchanged; in the part, "sotto voce" goes below the staff and "poco sostenuto" sits level with "poco rit.".
  - **genparts.py:** the viola padding goes from 0.2 to 0.5 (it was 0.5 at c276). With findings 2 and 5 the page has room for that. I tested 0.6, which goes to two pages, so 0.5 is the limit.

### 6. Violin II part: "Largamente" stands four lines above the staff, away from letter G (not applied)

- **Bars:** Vln II part 60–61.
- **Problem:** "poco allargando" (60), "molto sostenuto" and "sul A" (61–62) stack, and "Largamente (♩ = 54)" ends up above all of them, while the boxed G sits low by the barline. MOLA Example 4 (PDF 10) prints the letter and the tempo word side by side on one line.
- **Suggested fix (untested):** in bSecVlnTwo at 61, put "molto sostenuto" below the staff in the part (`-\tag #'part _\markup …`, score unchanged, as in finding 5), so the stack loses a line.
