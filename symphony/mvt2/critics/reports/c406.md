## Findings

### 1. Bar 54 b2-b3: B flat3 struck twice in a row by one hand (applied)
**Problem.** The RH triplet figure at 54 is `ges8 bes ges' bes ges bes | bes8 es' ges' es' bes es'`. The last note of beat 2 and the first of beat 3 are the same B flat3 string, plucked twice in succession at triplet-quaver speed (about three notes a second at crotchet 60 with "poco a poco animando"). A finger placed on a string that is still vibrating buzzes; the harp repeats a pitch by alternating hands or by using an enharmonic string (Adler PDF 118, trills and tremolos). c396 removed exactly this at 55 b3-4 and 55-56 for the same reason, and 54 was missed. The rest of 53-56 has no repeated string between beats.
**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-406/work/sections/bSec.ily).** Swap the first two notes of beat 3; same pitches, no new pitch, no C4 or D flat4 (c137, c212, c332 kept), and beat 4 still ends on E flat4 before 55's B flat3:
```
\tuplet 3/2 4 { ges8 bes ges' bes ges bes } \tuplet 3/2 4 { es'8 bes ges' es' bes es' } |
```

### 2. Bars 82-83: the pickup strings ring under the L.H. harmonics (applied)
**Problem.** At 82 b2-3 the RH plucks A flat3, C4 (rolled dyad), then E flat4 and F4 at pp. At 83 the LH plays the head of theme A in harmonics on the same A flat3, C4 and E flat4 strings. Nothing damps the pickup, so the plucked fundamentals keep sounding an octave below the harmonics: C4 until 83 b2½, E flat4 until b3, and F4 through the whole bar, a sixth over the tonic A flat. Adler PDF 116: harmonics "are very soft and must be accompanied very lightly or played a cappella to be effectively heard." The orchestra obeys this (only Vc/Cb A flat ppp), but the louder accompaniment is the harp's own ringing strings. c298 already noted that a harmonic sounds much softer than a plucked note at the same marking, which makes the ringing pickup the stronger sound. The RH is free at 83, so it can damp (the hand-damping of sons étouffés, PDF 117) at the moment the LH places its palm for the first harmonic; the LH's tied A flat1 is not affected.
**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-406/work/sections/coda.ily).** One text below the first harmonic, in score and part:
```
aes4.(\p-\flageolet_\markup \small \italic "R.H. étouffez"^\markup \line { \italic "dolce," \small \italic "harm., L.H. (sound 8va)" } c'8-\flageolet es'4-\flageolet)
```
The text prints between the harp staves at 83 b1, under the p; the score stays 8 pages and the harp part 2 pages. This does not touch the pickup (c116, c266) or the harmonics (c241, c266, c298).
