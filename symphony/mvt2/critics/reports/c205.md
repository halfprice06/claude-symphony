## Findings

### 1. Bars 57–58, bassoon 2 alone with forced down-stems (applied)
**Problem.** Bassoon 2 plays alone at 57–58 (F3–G♭3 | B♭2–E♭3–A♭2–D♭3) with every stem down, because the staff is in `\partCombineApart` from 57 and bassoon 1 has invisible spacers (c145). There is no upper part, no rest for it, and the "2." label is a manual markup. At 58 B♭2 and A♭2 (below the middle line) are stemmed down while the bass clarinet and double basses one and two staves away have the same notes stemmed up.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 65: in double-stemmed writing "the upper part always takes up-stems, the lower part down-stems"; down-stems mark the lower of two parts, so a lone down-stemmed line reads as a part with its partner missing. PDF 60–61: a single line takes the stem direction of its notes, kept consistent within the bar. c145's reason (no whole rest on a ledger line, a "2." label) is kept: solo mode prints no bassoon 1 rest and the combiner prints "2." itself (soloIIText).
**Fix (bSec.ily, bSecBsnTwo):**
```
\partCombineSoloII f2\p\< ges2 | bes,4 es4 aes,4 des4 | \partCombineApart ges,2\f g,2 | aes,1\< \breathe |
```
(the score-only `^\markup \bold "2."` is dropped; apart mode still starts at 59, where both bassoons play, as c045 wanted). Checked in the scratch PDF: "2." printed once at 57; stems at 58 now up–down–up–up, matching B.cl. and Cb.

### 2. Bar 90, clarinet 2 written half tied to quarter (applied)
**Problem.** `es'2~\> es'4~\!` writes a full-bar E♭4 as a half tied to a quarter, only so that the hairpin can stop on beat 3. Two noteheads and an extra tie appear in a soft morendo line where one dotted half is the normal notation; the parts book carries the same.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 67: the dot is the standard way to add half a value; the reading's examples show a dotted minim filling a 3/4 bar, with no tie (PDF 67 first example, PDF 68 chord examples). The hairpin end does not need a notehead.
**Fix (coda.ily, codaClTwo):** `\after 2 \! es'2.~\> |` in place of `es'2~\> es'4~\! |`. Same sound and same hairpin length; score.midi duration unchanged. Checked in the scratch PDF: one dotted half, tie into 91, hairpin ends on beat 3.

### 3. Bars 30 (Hn 3·4), 69 and 82 (bassoons): equal values on separate stems (recorded, not applied)
**Problem.** At 30 Hn 3 C4 and Hn 4 E♭3 are both dotted halves, at 69 Bn 1 G3 and Bn 2 D♭3, at 82 Bn 1 C4 and Bn 2 A♭2; each prints as two stems. Gould PDF 60 asks for one stem; c125 applied this at 3–6.
**Why not applied.** I tested `\partCombineChords` at 30 and 69: Hn 4's tie from 29 and Bn 1's slur from 68 start in separate voices and cannot end in a chord (build warnings "unterminated tie", "cannot end slur", and a knock-on "already have slur" at 79). Gould PDF 65 allows double-stemmed writing for two independent parts, so these bars are correct as they stand. No change.
