## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Bars 12 and 28: theme A's peak has no named level, and at 28 the strings peak one beat before the appoggiatura (applied)
**Problem.** The high point of theme A's second phrase is the B♭ appoggiatura (F–B♭–A♭; c001, c012). In the horn statement at 12, `f'4~\< | f'4 bes'4\> aes'4` gives the swell no top level, and the hairpin ends at 14 with `\!` and no level. In the string statement at 28, `f''4\mf\> bes''4( aes''4)` in Vln I/II, with the matching marks in Vla, Vc and Cb, puts the mf on the tied F on beat 1. The diminuendo starts there, so the appoggiatura arrives already fading.
**Evidence.** PDF 148: for a horn, p and mf are different colours, so the peak needs a named level, not just a hairpin. PDF 149: a hairpin is only defined by its end levels. PDF 155: the swell lands on the note of arrival. Dvořák 9/ii bars 17–18 put the f on the arrival note, not before it.
**Fix (applied, `aOne.ily`).**
- Hn 1, 12–14: `f'4 bes'4\mp\> aes'4 | g'4 es'8 des'8 c'8 bes8 | aes2.)\p`
- Vln I and II, 28: `f''4 bes''4(\mf\> aes''4)`. The crescendo from 27 now runs through the tie to the B♭.
- Vla, 28: `<des' f'>4 <des' f'>4\mf\> <des' f'>4`
- Vc, 28: `\after 4 \mf \after 4 \> aes2( des'4)`. Cb, 28: `\after 4 \mf \after 4 \> des,2 bes,,4`

Pitches and slurs are unchanged, so the protected horn line and the 23–30 bowing are untouched. Bar 76 is left alone, because there the appoggiatura sits inside the long crescendo to f at 78.

### 2. Bars 41–44: the accompaniment does not share theme B's swell (applied)
**Problem.** Flute 1 and cor anglais go p< (41) to mp> (42) to p (44). The violas' triplets stay at pp, and the new cello tenor line (c037) stays flat at pp for four bars. Only the melody breathes.
**Evidence.** PDF 149–150: the crescendo and diminuendo are the life of the sound, and a layer that never moves sounds mechanical. Dvořák 9/ii bars 13–14 and 17–18: the accompanying violins take the cor anglais' hairpins.
**Fix (applied, `bSec.ily`).** The accompaniment moves one step below the tune:
- Vla, 41: `aes8(\<` on the first triplet. 42: `aes8(\p\>`. 44: `ges8(\pp`.
- Vc, 41: `des1(\pp\<`. 42: `des4\p\> ...`. 43: `bes,2(\pp es2~`.

Pizzicato bass and harp stay as they are.

### 3. Open-ended hairpins at 47 (Vln I) and 36–37 (BCl) (applied; closes the open "Vln I 47 hairpin target")
**Problem.** At 47, Vln I `cis''1\>` dies into a rest with no end level. At 36, the bass clarinet's `p\<` runs into the D♭ downbeat at 37, where `des'2)\!` gives it nothing to reach.
**Evidence.** PDF 149: a hairpin is only defined by its end levels. At 47 the end is silence, so it should be marked as niente.
**Fix (applied, `bSec.ily`).**
- 47: `\once \override Staff.Hairpin.circled-tip = ##t cis''1\>`. The override has to be in the Staff context, because this score moved the Dynamic_engraver to Staff; a Voice override draws no circle.
- 37 BCl: `des'2)\mp`. The arrival is one step above the flute's p entry and the bassoon's pp.

### 4. Bars 53–56: horn 1 and clarinet 1 in unison at the same marking (applied)
**Problem.** Both instruments enter p, crescendo together and reach mp at 55 on the same notes. Neither one leads.
**Evidence.** PDF 147–148: two solo instruments layered on one line tend to be heard as "two distinct entities". PDF 290: an orchestra has no fader, so the balance between them has to be written. c019 asked for the clarinet to add a finer legato to the horn, which is a colour, not a second voice. Marking the clarinet lower does that job.
**Fix (applied, `bSec.ily`).** Cl 1, 53: `aes8(\pp bes8`. 55: `ges'2\p`. The horn keeps p<mp. From 57 the clarinet's mp< is unchanged.

### 5. Bars 86–87: low strings step from ppp to pp with no preparation (applied)
**Problem.** Violas, cellos and basses hold ppp from 83, then mark pp at the iv chord at 87 with no hairpin. The arrival of the plagal iv chord is the coda's one harmonic event, yet the written level just jumps.
**Evidence.** PDF 148–149: ppp and pp are two colour regions, and the move between them should be shown as a motion. The Dvořák 9/ii coda chords move by hairpins between ppp and pp.
**Fix (applied, `coda.ily`).** Add `\<` at 86 on Vla `<c' f'>2.\<`, Vc `aes,2.\<` and Cb `aes,,2.\<`, so they grow into the existing pp at 87. Pitches and the 87–92 close are unchanged.

### 6. Bar 79: timpani f inside the ff tutti is right (no change; closes the open point "timpani ff at 79")
**Evidence.** PDF 290–291 and Fig. 6.6: a sustained, constant level sounds more present and thicker than a peak at the same marking, and a roll is exactly that. At f, the D♭ roll already carries under the ff strings and winds. At ff it would cover the horns' f counter-melody, which c034 deliberately set below the theme.
**Recommendation.** Keep `des2.:32\f` and record the point as closed.
