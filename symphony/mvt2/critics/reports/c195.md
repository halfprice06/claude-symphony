## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Horns 1·2, bar 67: "1. solo" printed twice (applied)
**Problem.** The score shows two stacked "1. solo" labels over Hn 1's E♭4 at 67. v194 printed one. c194 gave Hn 1 an a2 with Hn 2 at 61–62; since then Hn 2 no longer has the last "2." label before 67, so the part-combiner now prints its own soloText (`\set Staff.soloText = "1. solo"`, already in retrHnOne) and the score-tagged markup c046 added for the case where the combiner stayed silent prints it again. c035 had settled on one label.
**Evidence.** A part-combining label is an instruction to a player; two identical labels read as an error. Same kind of duplicate as the "2." at 71 and the harp p at 83 that c015 removed.
**Fix (applied, sections/retr.ily, retrHnOne):** drop the score markup, keep the soloText and the part's "solo":
```
\set Staff.soloText = "1. solo" es'2.(\p\<-\tag #'part ^\markup \bold "solo"^\markup \italic "dolce" |
```
The Horn 1 part still reads "solo" (checked).

### 2. Violin II, bar 78 b3: beam slopes 1.36 spaces through the bottom stave-line (applied)
**Problem.** The run C5 + triplet F5–G5–A♭5 is stemmed down, and its beam climbs from −2.18 to −0.82, crossing the bottom line with the semiquaver beam. The three semiquavers are about 2.7 spaces apart. It is the steepest beam in the score, in the bar before the ff peak, and it sits under Fl 1 and Vln I's horizontal beams for the same figure.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 34: shorter groups do not cross more than one stave-space ("beam angle 1, and not 2"), and notes closer than three spaces "take only a slight angle (¼ or ½ space) regardless of the interval"; steep angles through stave-lines make the lattice effect shown there. PDF 35: matching angles.
**Fix (applied, sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoVlnTwo):** `aes'2\f \once \override Beam.damping = #5 c''8( \tuplet 3/2 { f''16 g'' aes'' } |`. The slope is now 0.36 (−2.18 to −1.82); the shortest stem is about 3 spaces (PDF 35 minimum inside the stave). The Violin II part (stems up) is unaffected.

### 3. Flute 1 and Violin I, bars 77, 78 b3, 81: ledger-line beams horizontal (applied)
**Problem.** At 77 and 81 the falling line E♭6–D♭6–C6–B♭5 and at 78 b3 the rising C6–F6–G6–A♭6 lie wholly beyond the first ledger line, and LilyPond sets all six beams flat on the middle line. The same falling figure in Ob 1, Cl 1 and Vln II on the same beats slopes 0.82, so the tutti shows flat and sloped beams for one line in octaves.
**Evidence.** Gould PDF 35 (image): when all notes fall outside the first ledger line the beam "takes only a slight slope, regardless of the pitches": ¼ space for seconds, ½ for wider intervals; the outside interval here is a fourth (77, 81) and a sixth (78). PDF 36: the beam slopes in the direction of the outside interval. PDF 35: matching angles outside the stave. PDF 33: one clear line between beam and first ledger line (kept: the beam stays at the middle line).
**Fix (applied, sections/aTwo.ily, aTwoVlnOne and aTwoFluteOne):** before `es'''8 des'''8` at 77 and 81: `\once \override Beam.positions = #'(0.18 . -0.18)`; before `c'''8( \tuplet 3/2 { f'''16` at 78: `\once \override Beam.positions = #'(-0.18 . 0.18)`. Each beam now slopes 0.36 space in the line's direction, both ends on the middle line (sit/hang, PDF 31–32).

### 4. Violas, bar 41: tuplet numbers at the pizzicato entry (applied; answers c035's reason)
**Problem.** c035 left numbers only at 37, 53 and 59 because "the slurred groups make the rhythm plain after that". At 41 the figure turns pizz., the slurs go, and bar 41 opens a new system in the score (41–44) and a new line in the viola part. The player sees twelve unslurred beamed quavers in 4/4 with no 3.
**Evidence.** The reason for omitting them does not apply to 41–44. Gould PDF 39 ties the passage-wide presentation of these groups to the slurs that sit by the heads; without slurs only the beam carries the grouping. This is the one place in 37–60 where a new articulation, a new system and no slurs coincide.
**Fix (applied, sections/bSec.ily, bSecVla bar 41):** `\undo \omit TupletNumber \tuplet 3/2 4 { aes8\p\<^\markup \italic "pizz." … } \tuplet 3/2 4 { ges8 … } | \omit TupletNumber`. Numbers print on all four beats of 41 (as at 37) and are omitted again from 42. Score and part checked. This changes one bar of the c105 "tuplet numbers" do-not-touch item, for the reason above.

### 5. Violas, bar 43 b3 (c192): steepest triplet, 1.00 space (no change)
