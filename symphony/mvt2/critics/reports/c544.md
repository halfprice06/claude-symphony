## Findings

The score meets every principle of this chapter that applies. I found no new defect that needs a change, so I applied nothing. The findings below record orchestration checks under this reading that a conductor might query, ranked by how much each matters.

### 1. Bars 32-34: cor anglais in its low register a sixth above the bass clarinet solo. Recorded, no change.

Rimsky PDF 38 calls the C.a. "fairly penetrating" in its low register, and Table B (PDF 35, image) labels the bass clarinet's middle register "dull". At 32-34 the B.cl. solo peaks on E♭3 (written F4, middle register) while the C.a. holds C4 and moves C4-C♭4-B♭3-A♭3 above it (written G4 down to E♭4, its low register). The upper of two parallel lines draws the ear, so the risk is that the C.a. takes attention from the solo. The dynamics answer it: B.cl. mf at its peak, C.a. p "dolce", a full degree and a half apart. Adler PDF 205 confirms the low C.a. carries. The C.a. was pp until c276 (playability) raised it to p. The two lines are the common-tone diminished seventh at 33 and movement I's first two motto instruments together (c330), both recorded as settled (c178, c330). No fix is recommended. If a reading shows the C.a. covering the solo, raise the solo, not lower the C.a.: in `transBassCl` bar 32, keep `\mf \<` and add `-\tag #'score ^\markup \italic "in rilievo"`.

### 2. Bar 78 b3: harp f stroke inside an f tutti. Recorded, no change.

PDF 47: "At least three, if not four harps in unison are necessary, if they are to be heard against a full orchestra playing forte." At 78 b3 the harp plays `<g es'>` over `<es,, es,>` f while strings, flute, clarinets and bassoons are f and the horns mf<. The stroke adds an attack on the low E♭ more than an audible chord, and the harp is out for the ff at 79-81, which is what Rimsky's rule asks for. c244 and c344 recorded this stroke under this reading. Nothing has changed, so I leave it.

### 3. Bars 87-90: muted horns 3·4 with the cor anglais. Follows; do not change.

PDF 44: muted horns are "tender and dull in piano", the resonance is reduced, "a timbre resembling that of the oboe and Eng. horn is approached", and "brass instruments, when muted, produce an effect of distance". Hn 3·4 con sord. pp play A♭3 over F3-F♭3-E♭3 with the C.a. F4-F♭4-E♭4 an octave above Hn 4, and the muted violas inside. Muted horn and C.a. are the pairing Rimsky describes, and the distance effect suits the last echo of the 61-62 horn chord. This is one of the best-scored passages in the movement.

### 4. Bars 37-44: plucked bass group. Follows; do not change.

PDF 44-45 treats pizzicato strings and the harp as one "new and independent group with its own particular quality of tone". At 37-44 the Cb pizz. is doubled by the harp's left-hand octaves on every note, and at 41-44 the violas join pizz. in triplets under the C.a. The basses are independent here only as part of this plucked group, which PDF 27 allows. Table D (PDF 49) puts the dry, unresonant pizzicato notes high on each instrument; the viola's top pizz. note is F4 and the basses stay low, so nothing needs a woodwind double. The Cb pizz. A1 at 40 (open string) was raised by c204 and rejected; it is one note at p.

### 5. Bars 60-62: horn and trombone balance. Follows.

PDF 41: in piano the horns should be marked one degree stronger than the other brass, and in forte two horns balance one trombone. At 60 Hn 4 is p< against Tbn 2, B.tbn and tuba at pp<. At 61-62 Hn 3·4 a2 at f stand against each trombone at f, and the single Hn 1 and Hn 2 are ff, a degree above, on the upper chord notes. The tuba doubles the bass trombone an octave lower (PDF 42). This is Rimsky's layout.

### 6. Bars 79-81: horns at f in the ff second peak. Follows.

Without trumpets or trombones at 79 (protected), the horns are the only brass. Hn 1·2 a2 at f double the cello and Bassoon 1 counter-line, which is two horns on one line, the doubling PDF 41 asks for in forte, and Hn 3·4 hold the chord. The horn's "round and full" upper middle (PDF 42) carries over the woodwinds without covering the violins' B♭6. c273 recorded the mf< entry at 78 (after c088 and c218) and asked that it not be re-raised.
