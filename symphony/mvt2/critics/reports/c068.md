## Findings (ranked)

### 1. Re-entries in the 15–22 dialogue have no dynamic (bars 19, 21). Applied.
- **Problem.** Oboe 1 returns at 19 after two bars' rest, and the solo violin returns at 21 after two bars' rest. Each starts with a bare `\<`, so the hairpin has no starting level. Their first entries (15, 17) were p. In the score the reader can work that out; a player reading the part cannot.
- **Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10): every entry after a rest has a dynamic (mf, ff, pp), and every hairpin starts from a printed level. MOLA PDF 5 asks that a part be readable on its own. The answer phrases are meant to be equals in an overlapping conversation (c017, c067), so they should state their level as the first statements do.
- **Fix** (aOne.ily):
  - `aOneOboeOne`: `c''4(\p\< des''8 c''8 bes'4 |`
  - `aOneVlnOne`: `f''8(\p\< g''8 es''4 d''4\! |`

### 2. The two solo statements of the motto's tail leave their peak unnamed and break the diminuendo in two (B.cl. 32–35, Hn 1 67–70). Applied.
- **Problem.**
  - B.cl. 32–33: `es'2.~\< | es'4 d'4\> des'4 | c'2\! bes4) \breathe | aes2.~(\> | aes4\pp`. The crescendo has no target. The diminuendo stops at `\!` on C, and after the breath a second diminuendo starts with no level.
  - Hn 1 67–69: `es'2.(\p\< | d'4\> des'4 c'4\! | bes2.~\> | bes2)\pp`. Again there is no peak level, and a second diminuendo starts from nothing.
  - In both cases a player cannot tell how loud the D (the ♯4̂ of the tail, the expressive point) should be, or whether bar 69/34 starts again from a new level.
- **Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10) writes each swell between printed levels (pp < > mp). Ex. 7 (PDF 12) shows what MOLA calls unclear marking: marks detached from where they apply. The swells themselves were accepted at c058 and stay. Only the levels are added, and the horn's two diminuendi are joined into one.
- **Fix:**
  - trans.ily, `transBassCl` bar 33: `es'4\mf\> d'4 des'4 | c'2\p bes4) \breathe |`. Peak mf over the pp accompaniment, then p before the breath, so the diminuendo at 35 starts from p.
  - retr.ily, `retrHnOne`: `es'2.(\p\< | d'4\mp\> des'4 c'4 | bes2.~ | bes2)\pp r4 |`. The horn peaks mp on the D, then makes one diminuendo to pp at 70, under the timpani's ppp roll and the bassoons' pp.

### 3. Vertical mismatches in the B-section build (bars 52 and 56). Applied. Closes the open item "Vla crescendo at 52 and Vc/Cb mf at 56" (c065).
- **Problem.**
  - At 52, Vln II, Vc, Cb and timpani swell from pp into the p at 53, but the violas hold C4 flat and then stop.
  - At 56, the violins reach mf on beat 1 while the cellos and basses reach it on beat 3. The two halves of the string body arrive two beats apart, although they are playing one build.
- **Evidence.** MOLA PDF 8 says to proofread the vertical lines. Ex. 6 (PDF 11) shows a tutti attack marked identically, in the same place, on every staff.
- **Fix** (bSec.ily):
  - `bSecVla`: `… | c'1 | c'1\< |` then `R1*4\! |`
  - `bSecVc`: `es2\< des2 | a,2\mf bes,2 |`
  - `bSecCb`: `es,2\< des,2 | a,,2\mf bes,,2 |`

### 4. "a tempo" at K (bar 83) collides with the string bracket. Applied.
- **Problem.** The strMark for bar 83 (on the violas, because Vln I rests) prints "a tempo" with X-offset −1. At a system start with no clef or key change, the "a" sits on the top hook of the strings' bracket. A `-\tweak X-offset` before `\strMark` does not override the tweak inside the function (tested).
- **Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 7 (PDF 12) is exactly this case: "a tempo" printed into rehearsal letter F. PDF 8: positions of "anchored" markings must be checked.
- **Fix** (coda.ily, `codaVla`): `R2.\strMark "K" \markup \concat { \hspace #2 "a tempo" } |`. Checked at 250 dpi: the text now clears the bracket and the K box is unchanged.
- **Not applied, related:** at 60, "poco allargando" (above Vln I, with the harp staff hidden) sits right under the timpani's p and reads as part of it. A small `\once \override Score.MetronomeMark.padding` in global.ily at 60 would separate them. I left this to the composer because it risks the 8-page layout (c054: tight pages).

### 5. Bassoon 1's pickup at 78 has no dynamic in its own part. Not applied.
- **Problem.** `aTwoBsnOne` is `R2.*7 | r2 c'4( | des'4\ff …`. After seven bars' rest the pickup has no level. In the score, Bsn 2's f on beat 1 of the shared staff covers it. In a separate Bsn 1 part (MOLA PDF 7: no wind parts with two players on one staff) it is unmarked.
- **Evidence.** MOLA Ex. 3 (PDF 10), and PDF 5 on parts.
- **Fix.** `r2 c'4(-\tag #'part \f |`, and in score.ly feed the bassoons as `\pc \removeWithTag #'part \bsnOne \bsnTwo`, as is already done for the cor anglais and horn 4. This keeps the shared staff to one f, so equal simultaneous dynamics hold.

### 6. Close the open item "mixed English/Italian directions" (c066). No change.
- MOLA PDF 4 lists English, Italian, German and French as acceptable languages. The score uses Italian for all expression and tempo words, and English only for set-up and technique (tuning, sticks, "change bow at will", harmonics). That split is consistent and common in modern published scores.
- Changing "change bow at will" into an invented Italian phrase would read worse than the English. I recommend recording this item as closed.
