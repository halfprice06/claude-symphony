## Findings

All three are faults in score.midi (the source of score.mp3) where the render contradicts the printed balance. Nothing printed changes. They are the same kind of fault c160, c300 and c338 fixed: a hairpin with no written goal, which LilyPond resolves to an arbitrary level.

### 1. Bars 15-16 and 19-20, Vln II: the inner A flat4 pedal swells louder than the oboe solo it accompanies (applied)

**Problem.** Vln II holds an A flat4 pedal under the oboe and solo violin (c061). Its swells under the oboe are printed one level below the oboe: Vln II pp<>, oboe p<> (c138, c139). The crescendos have no written goal. In v458's score.midi Vln II reaches velocity 60 and 70 on the A flat at 15 b3 and 16 b1, and 70 on the G4 at 19 b3. The oboe peaks at 57 on its F5 at 16 b1 and its A flat4 at 20 b1. In the render the accompaniment is a dynamic and a half above the solo, which reverses the print.

**Evidence.** A&S PDF 367-369: a pedal "remains static while the other voices move", and inner-voice pedals are named there. In Pergolesi's Ex. 21-21 (PDF 357, image) the sustained part under the suspensions is marked *p sempre*. Dvořák 9/ii bars 13-14 (corpus, above) swell a held inner note from pp and stay under the cor anglais.

**Fix (applied, aOne.ily, aOneVlnTwo; hidden, nothing printed).**
```
aes'2--(\pp\<\upbow^\markup \italic "con sord." aes'4--) | aes'2--(-\tweak stencil ##f \p \> g'4--\! -\tweak stencil ##f \pp ) | aes'2--( aes'4--) | aes'2--( f'4--) |
aes'2--(\< g'4-- -\tweak stencil ##f \p \>) | r2\! e'4--\pp\upbow | f'4--( c'4-- bes4--~) |
```
New velocities: 41, 49, 53 (15-16 b1), 41 at 16 b3 and through 17-19 b1 as before, 53 at 19 b3. The pedal's peak is now under the oboe's 57. The hidden pp at 16 b3 is needed: without it the diminuendo ran on to 24 through 17-19, below the printed pp.

### 2. Bars 25 and 73, cellos: the counter-melody's C4 plays at velocity 74, above the theme (applied)

**Problem.** `aes2\< c'4)\>`: the cello counter-melody swells to C4 on beat 3 as the violins relax (c138). Neither the swell nor the following diminuendo has a written goal. score.midi plays the C4 at 74 (the cellos' f level is 75). At 25 the violins are at 63 there. At 73 they are at 53, so the counter-melody is 21 velocity steps above the returning theme. The diminuendo then stays high (70, 67, 63 at 74) and meets the violins' mp pickup at 74 b3 from above.

**Evidence.** At 25 b3 the C4 is the 7th of IV7 over the basses' D flat. A&S PDF 348: a dissonance already "brings into prominence the voice in which [it] occurs" and is played with "something of an accent", not two dynamic levels louder. The resolution B flat3 at 26 should then be softer (PDF 348), but in MIDI it was 68, still above the theme. At 73 the bass is F, so the C4 is consonant and has no reason to stand out at all. The cellos are marked p and the theme mp/p (23, 71), so the print means the counter-melody to stay level with the tune or below it.

**Fix (applied, aOne.ily aOneVc and aTwo.ily aTwoVc, the same text in both).**
```
es2\p^... aes8( g8 | f2) g4( | aes2\< c'4)-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \> | bes4( aes4) g4\! -\tweak stencil ##f \p |
```
New velocities at 25-26 and 73-74: 57, 63, 61, 59, 57. The swell peaks at the cellos' mp. At 25 that is level with the violins (63). At 73 it is one level above the violins' p, which is what the print shows. The line returns to p before the next phrase.

### 3. Bars 17-18, solo violin: the p< reaches velocity 81 on E flat5 (applied; outside the reading, the same fault as 1)

**Problem.** `aes''8(\p\< bes''8 g''4 f''4 | es''2\> des''4)\!`, solo, senza sord., dolce. The crescendo has no written goal, and score.midi plays 53, 58, 62, 72, 81. That is the violins' ff level, for one player in a p dialogue whose oboe phrases peak at 57. Its answer at 21 peaks at 63.

**Fix (applied, aOne.ily, aOneVlnOne):** `| es''2-\tweak stencil ##f \mp \> des''4)\! |`. New velocities: 53, 55, 56, 60, 63, 53, the same shape as bar 21.
