## Findings

### 1. Viola part, bar 89: "rit." floats under bars 76–77 of the line above (open item from c225). Applied.
Bars 87–89, viola part only. In v235 the viola part stacks three texts above 87–89: "lower half sul C" (87), "change bow at will" (89) and the tempo word "rit." (89) on top. With the part's system padding at 0.5 (c221, needed for one page) the "rit." lands directly under the hairpin of 76–77 on line 7, so a player reads it as a rit. at 76. Gould PDF 43: tempo markings are the outermost layer and the information that changes is placed closer to the stave; the point of the layering is that each mark is read with the music it belongs to. c225 tested more padding (two pages) and tighter spacing without success.
Test 1 (c225's fallback): "lower half sul C" below the staff in the part only. The "rit." still sat above "change bow at will", halfway between the systems. Not enough.
Test 2 (applied): both string texts below the staff in the part, as c227 already does for div./unis. at 80–81:
```
<f aes>2.\pp-\tag #'score ^\markup \italic "lower half sul C" -\tag #'part _\markup \italic "lower half sul C" | <fes aes~>2. |
<es aes>2.~-\tag #'score ^\markup \italic "change bow at will" -\tag #'part _\markup \italic "change bow at will" | ...
```
Result: "rit." prints directly above bar 89 on the last line; the two string texts print below 87 and 89 with nothing else there but the ties and the 91 hairpin. Viola part stays one page; parts book 30 pages. No page turn at 31–36 is needed, so c165/c221's one-page rule stands.

### 2. Score: part-only viola texts print in the score too (div./unis. twice at 80–81 since c227). Applied.
Bars 48, 80–81 (and 87, 89 after finding 1). score.ly feeds `\vla` to the score without `\removeWithTag #'part`, unlike the wind staves. So c227's `-\tag #'part _\markup "div."`/`"unis."` printed in the score below the staff as well as the score versions above it: "div." and "unis." each appear twice at 80–81 on page 7 of v235. Finding 1 would have added a second "lower half sul C" and "change bow at will" at 87/89. Gould PDF 43: a mark must not be duplicated or cut through others; duplicated text reads as two instructions.
Fix (applied): score.ly line 167 `<< \clef alto \global \removeWithTag #'part \vla >>`. The only other part-tagged viola mark was "sotto voce" at 48, which printed in both score and part; I removed its tag (bSec.ily line 218) so it still prints in both. Score page 7 now shows one div. and one unis.; nothing else in the score changes. score.midi is byte-identical.

### 3. Harp part, bars 1–7: "attacca from movement I" runs through the barline of 7 and under the first chord. Applied.
Harp part page 1. The text hangs from the short 6-bar multi-rest and runs across the barline into bar 7, between the hands ("attacca from m|ovement I"). Gould PDF 43: notation "must never cut through other symbols". The other 24 parts have wider first bars and are clear.
Fix (applied, genparts.py only): a harp copy of the part global, in which the text takes its own width:
```
partGlobalHarp = << \partGlobalPlain { \once \textLengthOn <>_\markup \small \italic "attacca from movement I" } >>
```
and the harp RH uses `\partGlobalHarp`. The harp part stays two pages. The first line now ends at 12 instead of 13, so the 8 bassa line crosses the break at 12/13 with an 8 at the start of the new system, as PDF 44 shows.

### 4. Bass clarinet part, bar 8: the "(no low C: 8va)" ossia does not say which notes it covers. Not applied.
Bar 8 b2, part only (c156). The text sits over the written C3 (sounding B♭1) inside the slurred figure F–B♭–E♭ (written G3–C3–F3). Taken literally, one note goes up an octave and the figure becomes G3–C4–F3. Gould PDF 47–48: change octave between phrases and keep each figure's pitches at the same octave so the intervals stay visible. PDF 45 says an octave sign over one note needs an end corner so that only that note is affected. As written, the player cannot tell whether the note, the beat or the bar moves. Fix: show the alternative as a parenthesised small note, which is the practice c156 cited from Adler (adler2 PDF 304):
```
f,4( \tag #'score bes,,4 \tag #'part << { \voiceTwo bes,,4 } \new CueVoice { \voiceOne \parenthesize bes,4 } >> es,4)
```
(with the part text cut to "(no low C)"). Low rank, so I did not test it.

### 5. Viola part, bars 47–48: "poco sostenuto" floats under bar 43 of the line above. Tested; not applied.
In the part, "poco rit." (47 b3) and "poco sostenuto" (48) overlap horizontally, so they stack, with the part-only "sotto voce" (48) under them. The top word, "poco sostenuto", ends up just under the 41–43 hairpin on line 4, horizontally under bar 43. This is the same fault as finding 1 (PDF 43; MOLA Ex. 7). I tested "sotto voce" below the staff: it then ran into "a tempo, poco a poco animando" of line 6 ("sotto voce a tempo …"), which is worse. I also tested it as part of the dynamic, `ppSottoVoce = #(make-dynamic-script (markup #:line (#:dynamic "pp" #:normal-text #:italic " sotto voce")))`. That kept one page but reflowed the whole part, and "poco sostenuto" still sat about midway between the lines. I reverted both. If the composer wants it fixed, the cheapest clean option is to leave "sotto voce" out of the part at 48 (the dynamic is pp and the eighths are marked portato), which removes one layer. Record it at least.

### 6. Ledger lines and octave signs: no change needed (recorded).
