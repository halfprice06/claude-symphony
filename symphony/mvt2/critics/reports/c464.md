## Findings

I found nothing in v464 that breaks a principle of the assigned reading and is not already settled with a stated reason. I recommend no change to the score. The findings record what I checked, ranked by how hard the reading presses on each place. One of them corrects a factual statement in c407. Nothing was applied in /tmp/critic-464/work (an unchanged copy of v464). I rebuilt it to confirm the baseline (result at the end).

**1. Bar 87: the third of IV6 is doubled in the middle register, not only at the bottom. Record; corrects c407.**
c407 (its finding 8) says that "the third of IV6 at 87 is doubled only at the bottom (V-27, PDF 152)". The score does not do that. At 87 b1 the sounding pitches are: F1 (Cb), F2 (Vc), F3 (Vla lower half and Hn 4), A♭3 (Vla upper half and Hn 3), F4 (C.a.), A♭4 (Vln II), D♭5 (Cl 1). The bass F is in four octaves and is also the C.a.'s note in the middle of the chord. The root D♭ sounds once, on top. Adler's V-27 (PDF 152) marks exactly this as "not good": the third doubled near the bottom keeps the inversion's open sound, while the third doubled inside the chord closes it.
It should still not change. The F3 and F4 are the first notes of the F-F♭-E♭ line (Vla/Hn 4 and C.a.). That line answers 79-81 and bar 5 and is protected (c020, c104, c150, c194). A&S also gives F as the tone to double in this chord read as the Neapolitan sixth of C minor (c080). A doubled third that moves on by step in a melodic line is a different case from Adler's static block chord. Earlier cycles recorded the doubling for this reason ("IV6 at 87 with tripled-plus F", "IV6 third in five melodic voices at 87-88"). Only c407's wording is wrong. Recommended: the composer should note in HISTORY that c407's sentence on 87 is superseded by this record. No score change.

**2. 27 b3-28 b3: the strings are spaced against the overtone series. Record (c207, c347 already recorded the gap).**
PDF 151 (V-26): leave more space between the lower instruments than between the upper ones. At 27 b3 the strings are D♭3 (Cb), F3 (Vc), A♭3/D♭4 (Vla), F5 (violins). At 28 b2 they are D♭3, F3, D♭4/F4, B♭5. A major third sits at the bottom and a twelfth at the top, with no wind in the gap (Hn 3·4 enter only at 29). The reason is the line: the unison violins climb to the B♭5 appoggiatura, the phrase's peak, while the violas stay in the register of the plain first statement. The gap lasts two beats and closes at 29 (G5 over E♭4/G4). 23-30 is protected as the plain statement, and the same gap at 75-76 is filled by Cl 2 and the harp, so the return is the fuller of the two, as Adler's Haydn and Brahms models require (PDF 129, 135). No change.

**3. The viola is never foreground. Record.**
Adler's first two lessons (PDF 125, 128, V-4) are that Vln II, viola and cello are equal partners, and that the viola can carry the tune "above the two violins" with "a glorious sound." In this movement the cellos have three melodic stretches (counter-melodies at 23-30 and 71-78, theme B's minor head at 45-46) and Vln II has its line over the quotation at 48-51. The violas have only middleground: the answers at 16 and 20, the theme's lowest octave at 79-81 (with the C.a.), and the F-F♭-E♭ line at 87-88 with the muted horns. I looked for a place where giving them the lead would not undo a protected decision, and found none. The tenor-register melodies belong to the horn, bass clarinet, cor anglais and cellos by the cyclic plan (melodic moments for horn and bass clarinet; C.a. quotation at 47-53). The middleground role meets Adler's equal-partner test. No change.

**4. The double bass follows Haydn's "+Bass / -Bass" plan. Record.**
PDF 130-131 (V-6): drop the bass for the lighter texture and bring it back at the dominant pedal and the cadence. The basses rest at 15-20 under the oboe/solo-violin dialogue and enter at 21 for the approach to the half cadence. They rest at 53-56 under the first string statement of theme B (c164) and enter mf at 57 for the build. They hold the E♭ dominant pedal alone at 65-70, four soli. At 23-30 they play in their upper register (A♭2-E♭3 sounding), so the plain statement keeps no 16-foot depth until the cadence at 29-30 (E♭2, A♭1). At 71 they take the bass an octave lower (A♭1), with the harp's 8 bassa, so the return is heavier at the bottom from its first bar. That is the accumulation of PDF 129. No change.

**5. Range reserved for the end (PDF 129). Record (c324 R2, c451).**
Haydn holds back the octave transposition until the last variation. Vln I reaches A♭6 at 58 and 61, A6 in the 63 tremolo, and B♭6 only at 79. The A' peak is one semitone above the B-section peak. The B♭6 is kept for the one place, approached by the run at 78, and both peaks have different scoring (brass at 61-63, none at 79-81). The reading presses here, but the register plan was settled for reasons Adler's passage does not override: 57-58 belongs to the protected unison-to-octave plan. No change.

**6. The final diminuendo is made by scoring, but the bass does not drop. Record (c407, protected close).**
PDF 154 (V-29): Tchaikovsky's closing diminuendo uses registers where soft playing is easy, and the bass drops out each time it reaches the tonic. At 87-92 each string sound is in a soft-capable register: Vln I C5 sul D, Vln II A♭4, violas E♭3/A♭3, cellos A♭2, basses A♭1 four soli, Cl 2 E♭4 morendo. The thinning comes from the C.a. and the horns leaving at 90 and Cl 1 handing its C5 to Vln I at 89. The bass is kept because the A♭ pedal under the plagal close is on the standing list. No change.

**7. Climax 61-62: not bass-heavy. Record (c414, c424).**
PDF 151 warns that plain string scoring is bass-heavy when loud. At 61 the strings alone would be: Vln II A♭4 and cellos A♭3 on the augmented head, violas D♭4/F4 tremolo, basses D♭2, Vln I D♭6. The top is carried by Fl, Ob and Cl at D♭6/A♭5 and the horns sit one level under, so the violins' weaker middle register is covered by winds. The cellos rise above the violas at 61 b3-62 on the A string (Haydn's crossing, PDF 127). No change.

No change touches movement II's ending, its exported themes or its quotations, so the other threads need no notice.
