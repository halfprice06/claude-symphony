## Findings

The texture already follows nearly every principle in this chapter. Earlier cycles on the same reading (c067, c167, c217, c307) made the changes the chapter supports. None of my findings below is applied.

### 1. Bar 77: flute 1 and clarinet 1 enter at mp< on violin lines that are also around mp (tested, reverted; no change)

**Problem.** PDF 164 says the doubling wind takes "a lesser volume marking ... than I do for the strings." At 77, Fl 1 (G6, then Vln I's line) and Cl 1 (Vln II's line) enter at mp<. Vln I and II carry a crescendo from mp at 75 toward f at 78, and the hidden MIDI level at 76 b3 is mp. So in the MIDI the winds start at the strings' own level.

**Evidence and reason for no change.** I tested `g'''4(\p\<` in `aTwoFluteOne` and found that it undoes c178. c178 moved the flute from p to mp with a reason from another book: G6 is the first note of Adler's "a bit shrill" register (part 1 PDF 185), and a p entry there is not reliable. c178 also put all three wind entries in the bar at the same level. Sebesky's point is about balance, and the printed music already meets it: the violins are partway through a crescendo from mp to f, so at 77 they are above the winds' starting mp. Clarinet 1 cannot change on its own either, because it shares a staff with clarinet 2 and shared staves need equal dynamics. I reverted the test. One beat of G6 alone above Vln I's G5 remains (c341, recorded by c344). Sebesky gives no reason to reopen the settled Vln I register plan.

### 2. Bars 79-81: winds and strings at equal ff on the theme (no change)

At the A′ peak, Fl 1/Vln I (B♭6), Ob 1, Cl 1/Vln II (B♭5) and C.a./Vla (B♭4) are all ff. Row 5 would put the winds one level under, but ff is the top level here and nothing is marked above it. PDF 168 asks for the opposite help in a tutti: voice the strings simply and high. The score does this with three string octaves on the theme and cellos at ff (c217). No change.

### 3. Bars 57-61 and 77-82: flute 1 doubles Vln I instead of answering it (no change)

Ex. 214's last system (PDF 167, image) has the solo flute that gave the tune to the violins come back with short figures where the violins hold. Here Fl 1, theme B's first soloist (37), returns at 57 and at 77 only as a doubling of Vln I. The gaps are already filled by other voices. At 54 b3 and 56 b3 the Hn 1/Cl 1 counter-line moves under the violins' held notes. At 58 and 60 the horn and cellos move under the held G♭6 and C6. At 79-80 the cellos and horns move under the held B♭6. These are the places a flute fill would go, so another answering line would crowd them. The doubling also follows PDF 164 (emphasis in a build, mf and louder) and Ex. 213. No change.

### 4. Bars 63-64: a string chord under the trombone quotation (no change; c217's option stands)

PDF 173 drops the string chords when the brass come forward, keeps one high violin note, and rests the cellos "to avoid clutter." Here the strings attack sf, fade to p before the trombone's figure develops, and keep the violins high. The violas' E4/C5 sits just above Tbn 1's E3-C4. c217 offered an option: violas rest from 63 b2 to 64 b2 and re-enter sul C on G♯3 at 64 b3. Bars 61-65 are protected (c180) and the sf tremolo is the colour of the A-minor arrival. I agree with c217 and add nothing.

### 5. No clarinet on a cello or viola line (no change)

PDF 166's woody single clarinet on a unison cello or viola line is not used. Bassoon 1 doubles the cellos at 45-47 and 75-82, and the horns at 57-60 and 75-82. The Ob 1/Bsn 1/Vc octaves at 45-47 make the minor episode darker, which a clarinet would brighten, so the bassoon is the better choice there. The cello counter-melody 23-30 and 71-74 stays strings-only on purpose (c007, c167 protected). No place needs the clarinet colour.

### 6. Bar 87: the strings sound IV6 without the root (no change)

Under Ex. 214's internal-resonance rule (PDF 168), each choir should be complete within itself. At 87 the strings have F and A♭ only (Cb, Vc, Vla, Vln II), and the D♭ is only in Cl 1. At 88 the strings are complete again (D♭3 in Vc). This is one bar inside the protected plagal close 87-92 (c207: IV6 third in five melodic voices). The winds at 87 are complete (Cl 1 D♭5, C.a. F4, Hn 3·4 A♭3/F3). Recorded only.
