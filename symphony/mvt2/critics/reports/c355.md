## Findings

### 1. Parts: letters and tempo words are not on one line, and some tempo words float. Applied.
Bars: D 37, G 61, H 65 and K 83 in most of the 25 parts; F 53 in some.

Problem: Ex. 4 prints each boxed letter at the barline with its tempo word beside it on the same line ("[E] Tempo I"). c225 aimed for this by moving the MetronomeMark 3.6 spaces right. The letter and the word are still two separate grobs, though, and each is stacked against its own skyline:
- At D and H the letter falls on a key or metre change. The tempo word is anchored on the time signature, its gap to the letter was only 2.7 pt, and the letter's horizontal padding of 1 space pushed it a full line above the word. Most parts print "[H]" over "Tempo I".
- In other parts a text in the following bar lifted the word above the letter. Examples: Hn 1-4 at D; Vln I at H, where "Tempo I" stood about 30 pt over [H] and right of it; Cb at H; Bsn 1 at G (c225 finding 6); Vln II at G and H (c285 finding 6); B.tbn and Vla at K. In the viola part "a tempo" sat between the systems over bar 84, closer to the hairpin of the line above than to [K]. That is the "a tempo ... [F]" fault Ex. 7 shows.

Evidence: from bounding boxes in v355's parts book, 36 of 102 letter/tempo pairs lay on one line (word right of the letter, baseline difference under 6 pt). MOLA PDF 8: "the placement of any 'anchored' markings may not remain positioned as they were in the score, leading to collisions or floating markings (Example 7)".

Fix (genparts.py only; the score is not affected): when a `\mark` is followed by a `\tempo` with no music between them, the tempo text and metronome figure are drawn inside the letter's own stencil, 1.2 spaces to the right of the box on the box's baseline. The separate MetronomeMark is not printed. The pair is then one object and cannot split:
```
#(define (tempo-markup ev)   ; text (bold, or the \medium \italic markup as given) + "(♩ = n)" from tempo-unit and metronome-count
   ...)
#(define (letter-with-tempo tm)
   (lambda (grob)
     (ly:stencil-combine-at-edge (ly:text-interface::print grob) X RIGHT
       (grob-interpret-markup grob (markup #:fontsize -2 #:normal-text tm)) 1.2)))
; in beside-letter, in place of the c225 X-offset:
#{ \once \override Score.RehearsalMark.stencil = #proc \once \override Score.MetronomeMark.stencil = ##f #}
```
The full diff is in /tmp/critic-355/genparts.diff. Result: 104 of 104 measured pairs now sit on one line, including the line starts (Fl 1 D, Vla H, Bsn 1 G), Vln I H, Vln II G/H and Vla K. The viola part stays on one page and the parts book stays at 30 pages. This replaces c225's finding 1 mechanism with the same intent. It does not undo it. The scherzo and finale generators may use the same X-offset; if so they have the same fault.

### 2. Violin I part: "mettere sord. (not the solo player)" hangs over bars 2-3, where the player is still holding C5. Applied.
Bar 4 (part only).

Problem: the text is attached to the rest at bar 4. In the part, that rest merges into the 4-6 multi-bar rest. Multi-bar rest text is centred on the rest, and this text is about three times wider than the rest, so it starts over bar 2. The player reads the mute instruction during the tied, diminuendo C5 of 2-3, before the bow has stopped. In the score it already starts at bar 4.

Evidence: MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7: anchored marks that float away from their place after extraction. PDF 5 and Ex. 5: a change is marked where the player stops.

Fix (sections/intro.ily, introVlnOne; the score keeps its own copy, so the score PDF is pixel-identical):
```
R2.\!\stopTextSpan -\tag #'score ^\markup \small \italic "mettere sord. (not the solo player)"
                   -\tag #'part -\tweak self-alignment-X #LEFT ^\markup \small \italic "mettere sord. (not the solo player)" | R2.*2 |
```
The text now starts at the barline of 4 and runs over the 4-6 and 7-14 rests.

### 3. Recorded, no change
- Short name "Cb." beside the full name "Double basses" (Ex. 2 uses "D.B."). "Cb." is an accepted abbreviation and is used throughout the score, so this is not a fault.
- No letter at 31. MOLA PDF 4 asks for letters at landmarks. The c055 open item was closed, and c135, c225 and c285 found A-K adequate. Adding a letter would rename D-K in every part, so I do not raise it again.
- Harp starting pedal diagram in the part. This is against the letter of PDF 5, but c116's reason (Adler PDF 112: the starting setting after an attacca) answers it.
- Score pages 1-8 checked against Ex. 1, 2 and 6 (order, names, brackets, barline breaks, a2 and player labels), against PDF 4 (tempo marks on top and over the first string staff present) and against bar-number placement. No faults found.
- Parts checked against Ex. 3-5 (cautionaries at 36/37, 46/47, 50/51, 64/65; ranges on rests; tempo and metre in all parts; mute and muta changes). No other faults found.
