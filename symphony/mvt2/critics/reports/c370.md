## Findings

### 1. The theme record reports a change at B.cl. 31-34 that did not happen (tools; coherence)

- **Bars:** 31-34 (trans.ily, `transBassCl`).
- **Problem:** On v370, `themecheck.py themes-mvt2.ly` reports `17/18 unchanged, 1 CHANGED` for `mottoTailBcl`. Its readout is `D#3/3 D#3/1` where the record has `D#3/4`. The printed notes are unchanged from v368: E♭3 is held from 32 and tied into 33. c368 (v369) wrapped the note in `<< es2.~ ... \tag #'score { CC11 spacers } >>`. lyparse.py keeps only the first token of a `<< >>` block, so it drops the `~` that follows `es2.`, and it reads the tie as two notes. Because of this, mvt2.md's statement "18/18 and 11/11" no longer holds when the check is run on the current version. A real change to the motto tail would now be hidden among the false alarms that the other threads have learned to ignore.
- **Evidence:** Casanelles PDF 31-32 says MIDI is a set of instructions parallel to the score, not the score itself. A MIDI-only layer should leave the score-reading tools unchanged. PDF 17 and 33 say a non-linear, multi-team process depends on communication tools that work. The links in this symphony are kept by four threads through these records. I ran the check on v366: it gives 18/18 there, so the failure starts with c368's construct.
- **Fix (tooling, not score):** in lyparse.py, keep a tie written straight after the first note of a `<< >>` block:
  ```python
              first = inner[:1]
              # c370: keep a tie written straight after that first note (bar 32 B.cl., c368's CC11 spacer branch)
              if inner[1:2] == ['~']:
                  first = inner[:2]
              out += events(' '.join(first))
  ```
- **Test:** with the patched copy, v370 gives 18/18 and 11/11, and v366 still gives 18/18. The scherzo record (themes-scherzo.ly) and the finale record (themes-finale-quotes.ly) give the same results with and without the patch, so the patch changes nothing for them.
- **Score-side alternative:** none. Writing `es4~ es2` would change the printed notation.
- **Next step:** lyparse.py is shared and lives in the coherence folder, so the composer should pass the patch to whoever maintains it rather than edit it in place.
- **Applied:** in /tmp/critic-370/work/c370-tools/ (lyparse.py, themecheck.py, lyparse-c370.patch). No score file was changed.

### 2. Record only: both ends of the movement are voiced as harmonic series (PDF 30-31)

The last chord at 89-92 is partials 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 10 of A♭1, with no added note. Bar 1 is movement I's C-major chord with E4 below G4. This matches the glossary's "the first four harmonics generate a major chord". It is also why the plain triad lets the finale's German sixth on A♭ act on it (mvt2.md). Keep both as they are.

### 3. Record only: each cyclic idea has one fixed colour (PDF 17, 23, 31)

The book's "sonically specific in its aim to create precise meaning" fits how the movement assigns its links:
- the motto tail starts in Hn 1, returns in B.cl. at movement I's own pitch and instrument, comes back to Hn 1 alone at 67-69, and ends in Cl 1;
- each quotation has a single instrument, used once;
- the timpani answer stays on the timpani into the scherzo.

No idea moves to a colour that would blur its meaning. Nothing to change.

### 4. Record only: register and loudness at the joins (PDF 31)

At 92 the low A♭s are ppp, and low notes sound softer than high ones at the same amplitude. The pitches heard on top are therefore C5 (the pitch shared with movement I) and A♭4 (the scherzo's first note). The scherzo enters pp on G♯4 after the pause, one step up from ppp, so the dynamic rises slightly across the pause.

### 5. Record only: the rendering cannot judge the joins (PDF 18-19, 30, 32)

score.midi re-strikes the \repeatTie notes at bar 1, and its fades at 91 change only volume. score.mp3 is therefore not a fair test of either attacca. This is not a score change, and it should not lead to one.
