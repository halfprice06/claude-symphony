## Findings

### 1. Bar 63: in the render, the strings stay at sf under the whole of the trombone quotation (applied, MIDI only)

**Bars:** 63 (Vln I, Vln II, Vla, Vc, B.tbn).

**Problem.** The print has the tremolo chord and the bass trombone's A2 sf > p across 63, so trombone 1's quotation of movement I's P head (ff marcato, entering on 63 b1½) comes through as the chord decays. That is the one place in the movement where movement I's first theme is heard. A single-note tremolo renders as one held MIDI note, and a held note keeps its attack velocity. In score.midi, Vln I's E5/C6/E6/A6 stay at 83, the cellos' A2 at 75, and the violas and bass trombone at 62 through beats 2–4. The trombone is at 87–67. So the render plays an unbroken sf chord over four octaves under the falling B3–B♭3–A3, the part of the head the slur and portato were made to bring out (c240). The p level only arrives with the new attacks at 64.

**Evidence.**
- Sebesky PDF 240–241: compare the score with the recorded result and mark what is "too muddy".
- Sebesky PDF 13: the ear must be directed to the primary element. At 63 that element is the quotation.
- Precedent: c228's CC11 fade at 91 solved the same problem, a diminuendo on held notes.

**Fix (applied in sections/bSec.ily; nothing printed).** In each of the five parts, the bar-63 note becomes `<< note \tag #'score { s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.85 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.72 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.6 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #1.0 } >>`. For Vln I it reads:
`<< <e''' a'''>1:32\sf\>^\markup \italic "trem." \tag #'score { s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.85 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.72 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.6 s4 \set Staff.midiExpression = #1.0 } >>`
- Vln II, the violas, the cellos and the bass trombone take the same wrapper around `<e'' c'''>1:32\sf\>…`, `<e' c''>1:32\sf\>`, `a,1:32\sf\>…` and `a,1\sf\>`.
- The tag keeps the spacers out of the parts. Untagged, they shortened the printed hairpin in the parts.
- CC11 returns to 1.0 at 64 b1, where the p attacks start, so 63 b4 (83 × 0.6 ≈ 50) leads straight into 64 (53).

### 2. Bars 66–70: in the render, the bass pedal is louder than Horn 1's motto tail (applied, MIDI only)

**Bars:** 65–71 (double basses).

**Problem.** At 67–69 Horn 1 plays the motto tail alone, p < mp > pp. It is one of the cyclic rules' unsupported horn moments. Under it are the basses' E♭2, pp 4 soli, tied from 65, and the timpani ppp. In score.midi the basses sound at velocity 50 and the horn at 44 (E♭4), 50 (D4), 48 and 46 (D♭4, C4) and 44 (B♭3). So the pedal is the loudest line for most of the solo. The cause is the volume table: the basses have a floor of 0.2, so their pp comes out higher than the horn's p. c240 fixed the same problem for the coda's ppp pedal (83–86) with CC11 0.75, but the retransition pedal was not changed.

**Evidence.**
- Sebesky PDF 240: "Do the voicings sound the way they were intended to?"
- Sebesky PDF 13–14: the soloist is the primary focus level, and the bass a tertiary one.
- c240 Finding 4 (same means, same instrument).

**Fix (applied in sections/retr.ily and sections/aTwo.ily; nothing printed):**
`retrCb = { es,2.\pp~^\markup \italic "4 soli, change bow at will" | \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.88 es,2.~ | \set Staff.midiExpression = #0.75 es,2.~ | es,2.~ | es,2.~ | es,2. | }`
Then `\set Staff.midiExpression = #1.0` before the first note of aTwoCb at 71.
- The steps sit on barlines. A spacer ramp inside bar 66 changed the printed spacing of that system, so I did not use one.
- The pedal now sounds at about 44 at 66 and 38 at 67–70, under the horn and above the ppp roll (31).

### 3. Bars 3–14 reuse movement I's A♭ colours: a deliberate link, keep it (recorded)

mvt1.md (v321) lists three seeds of our opening:
- I 73–76 is the same A♭-pedal chord with F♭ mixture and Hn 4 A♭3 as our 3–6.
- I 45–47 hands Cl 1's E♭4 to Hn 1's E♭3 pickup, as our 6 does.
- I 46–52 is S in A♭ in Hn 1 with the cellos over harp broken chords, pp–p, dolce. Our theme A at 7–14 is Hn 1 over a harp, p, dolce espr., also in A♭, and fills the same E♭3–C4 span.

Because I runs attacca into II, the listener hears this about six minutes after I's S.

Under Sebesky PDF 15 (changing combinations), this is close to the same colour twice. I read it as Sebesky PDF 11 and 241–242 describe instead: "a variation on a motive already used", "older sounds arranged in slightly new proportions". Three proportions are new:
- the strings are out (4–14), so there are no cellos on the line;
- the wind chord (B.cl., Bsn 1·2) supplies the harmony, and the harp only adds off-beat articulation;
- the tune is an arpeggio and the motto tail, not the S head with its rising sixth, which stays reserved for the quotation at 47.

No change. The horn theme 7–14 is protected anyway.

If the movement I thread ever moves its S accompaniment to woodwinds, the two passages would become one colour. Ask it to keep the cellos on its line.

### 4. The soft stretch 31–52 does not sag (recorded)

Sebesky's question "where does it sag?" (PDF 240) points at 31–52. That is 88 s, a quarter of the movement (103.8 s to 191.7 s in score.midi), at p or below, mostly one wind solo at a time:
- B.cl. 31–36;
- Fl 1 37–40;
- C.a. 41–44;
- Ob 1 45–47;
- the C.a. quotation 47–53, under a poco rit. and poco sostenuto.

The analysis profile has 3.8 to 7.2 voices here.

It holds because the colour changes every four to six bars (Sebesky PDF 15) and the harmony moves D♭ → B♭ minor → C♯7 → F♯ minor → D♭. The stretch also prepares the build at 53–64 (50 s, up to 15.8 voices). That is the logical growth Sebesky asks for: the reserved colours (Tbn 1, tremolo) enter only at its top.

No change. The tempo plan 53–65 (c288) and the background plan 37–60 (c124) are protected.

### 5. Harmonic variety of the motto tail (recorded)

Sebesky PDF 19 asks that a melody not be harmonised the same way twice. The tail E♭–D–D♭–C has six statements in this movement, in four settings:
- vi7 – V7/V – V7 – I at 8, 24 and 72;
- A♭ pedal with the common-tone diminished seventh at 32–34;
- E♭ pedal with 8-7-6-5 over 6-5-4-3 at 67–69;
- A♭ pedal, then IV6 – iv – I with F–F♭–E♭ inside, at 85–89.

The identical setting at 8/24/72 is closed: c157 gave the reason and asked that it not be raised again.

Across the symphony, the three plagal motto-tail closes (II 85–89; III 357–360 B–A♯–A–G♯; IV 273–275 G–F♯–F–E) share the ♮6–♭6–5 line on purpose, a major third apart. Even so, they are not set alike: II has a half-diminished chord at 86 and IV6 over F, while III and IV use a full diminished seventh. That satisfies Ex. 3/3A (same resting place, different chords). Keep.
