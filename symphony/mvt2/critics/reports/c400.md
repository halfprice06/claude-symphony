## Findings

### 1. Bassoon 1 is flat p in score.midi at 75–78 (carried open since c338). Applied and tested
**Bars:** 75–78.
**Problem:** Bsn 1 doubles the counter-melody of the cellos (mp< … f) and Hn 2 (p< … mp). Its part reads p< at 75, mf at 77 b3 and f at 78, but those marks are tagged part-only, because the score's shared staff shows Bsn 2's p< … f. Dynamic_performer works per voice, so in score.midi every Bsn 1 note from 75 b1 to 78 b3 has velocity 32. Over the same bars Bsn 2 rises 32→50, Hn 2 44→50 and the cellos 63→75. c338 listed this as open and c340 carried it. No later cycle closed it.
**Evidence:** Sebesky PDF 241 says to compare the written score with the result and to mark what did not work. A&S PDF 348 is the reason the c338 levels exist: the MIDI should play what is printed. The printed score already has the crescendo, so only the MIDI is wrong.
**Fix (applied in /tmp/critic-400/work):** I first tested hidden dynamics tagged `'score`. That shortened Bsn 2's printed hairpin on the shared staff, ending it at 76 b1, so I dropped it. The tested version adds a `'midi` tag that only the MIDI `\score` keeps:
- sections/aTwo.ily, `aTwoBsnOne`: `aes2-\tag #'midi \mp des'4 | bes2 des'4-\tag #'part \mf -\tag #'midi \mf) \breathe |`
- score.ly: `bsnOneX = \removeWithTag #'(part midi) \bsnOne` before the layout `\score`, `bsnOneX = \removeWithTag #'part \bsnOne` before the MIDI `\score`. The bassoon staff now reads `\pc \bsnOneX \bsnTwo`.
- genparts.py line 176: `\removeWithTag #'(score midi)`.
At 78 I left out a hidden f, because it collided with Bsn 2's printed f ("conflict with event", one warning). Bsn 1 now plays v32 (75), v38 (76–77 b2), v44 (77 b3–78) and v54 (79), one level under Bsn 2 and the cellos it doubles (Sebesky PDF 164). **Result:** build 0 warnings, 8 pages, analysis.txt identical to v400 (0 outer-voice parallels). ### 2. Four A&S citations give printed pages as PDF pages; the reading also gives a cyclic reason for bar 33's C♭. Comments applied
**Bars:** 33, 52.
**Problem:** The c220 comment in trans.ily cites "A&S PDF 553-554" for the common-tone °7 at 33. The c322 comment in bSec.ily cites "PDF 553, Ex. 30-19", "PDF 554, Ex. 30-20d" and "PDF 556, Ex. 30-23". Those are printed page numbers. The pages are PDF 543, 544 and 546, and PDF 553–554 is Unit 31 parallel motion. HISTORY c220 and mvt2.md (v221 line, "A&S PDF 554") carry the same error.
**Evidence:** A&S PDF 544–545 (Ex. 30-21 viewed). "Quite frequently … a minor 3rd substitutes for the augmented 2nd. … In the Brahms, the immediately preceding movement is a Scherzo in C minor; the E♭ carries some of the minor quality into the Finale." Bar 33 spells its common-tone °7 over A♭ with C♭ (♭3), not B♮ (♯2), straight after a C-minor movement I. It is also movement I's stop chord at movement I's own spelling (mvt1.md: I 73 C♭4 over A♭1). This is the cyclic reason for the spelling, and no cycle had recorded it.
**Fix (applied, comments only):** trans.ily c220 comment: "A&S printed pp. 553-554 = PDF 543-544 … PDF 544-545, Ex. 30-21: the minor-3rd spelling, here C flat, carries the minor of the preceding C-minor movement into this one". bSec.ily c322 comments: PDF 543, 544, 546. For the owner: correct HISTORY c220 and the mvt2.md v221 line to PDF 543–545, and add the Op. 87 reason to the bar 33 row of the stop-chord table. Do not change the spelling.
