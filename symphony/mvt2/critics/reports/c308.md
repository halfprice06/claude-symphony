## Findings

The expression marking of v308 passes every check the reading supports. The two findings that change anything are part-only collisions. The rest are recorded so later critics do not reopen them.

### 1. Viola part: the dynamic touches the first triplet number at 37 and 57 (APPLIED, part only)

- **Bars:** 37 (pp, first triplet of the theme-B figure) and 57 (mp<, first triplet of the build).
- **Problem:** in the extracted viola part, the down-stem triplets put their "3" below the beam at the height of the dynamic. At 37 the part reads "pp3" with no gap, and at 57 "mp3". A player reads a dynamic joined to a tuplet number as one sign. The score is not affected, because its wider spacing keeps them apart.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 8 and Ex. 7 (PDF 12): marks anchored correctly in the score collide in the extracted part, so the part must be proofread on its own. Gould PDF 55: no less than ½ stave-space between characters, and no collisions.
- **Fix (applied in sections/bSec.ily, part view only; the score keeps its plain marks):**
  - 37: `aes8(-\tag #'score \pp -\tag #'part -\tweak extra-offset #'(-0.9 . 0) \pp ^\markup \italic "senza sord., unis." ...`
  - 57: `aes8(-\tag #'score \mp -\tag #'part -\tweak extra-offset #'(-0.9 . 0) \mp \< des' f' des' aes des')`
  - Checked at 300 dpi: both now stand clear of the "3". The viola part stays on one page.

### 2. Timpani part: "medium sticks" reads as part of the cue label at 50 (APPLIED, part only)

- **Bars:** 50–51 (Vc cue before the A♭ roll at 52).
- **Problem:** in the part, "medium sticks" sits directly under the small "Vc." cue label, above the cue notes. It reads as "Vc. medium sticks", as if it described the cue.
- **Evidence:** MOLA PDF 5 and Ex. 5: a change instruction belongs to the player and is placed where the player can act on it. MOLA Ex. 7: floating marks in parts.
- **Fix (applied in sections/bSec.ily, part tag only):** `<>-\tweak padding #1.5 _\markup \small "medium sticks" \cueDuring #"vcQ" #DOWN { <>^\markup \tiny "Vc." R1*2 }`. The instruction now prints below the staff at 50, apart from the cue label. The score text at 50 is unchanged.

### 3. Recorded: entries and hairpins pass the Ex. 3 tests (no change)

Every entry after two or more beats' rest prints its dynamic in its own part. The exceptions are the f clarinets at 60 (a half-bar breath inside their f) and the two niente starts (Cl 1 at 85, Cl 2 at 89). Of the 47 hairpins without a printed end:
- most are swells that return to their level (15–20, 23–25, 37–39, 71–73);
- some are niente circles (3, 66, 70, 81–82, 90);
- some fall into rests;
- the rest are the hidden MIDI targets at 58 (c198) and 76, where the printed mp < > < f reads as a swell.

There are no contradictions: no crescendo ends lower and no diminuendo ends higher. The Hn 3·4 swell from f at 80–81 into niente is a swell in Ex. 3's sense; keep it.

### 4. Recorded: vertical proofreading (no change)

Every simultaneous dynamic spread in the score is one already decided:
- Cb mf under f strings at 28 (c229);
- the fp pivot at 47 (c298);
- winds one level under the string line at 57 (c307);
- brass and timpani f under the ff tutti at 61 and 79 (c084, c273, c287);
- accompaniment p under the mp violins at 75.

All shared staves have equal simultaneous dynamics.

### 5. Recorded: the timpani D♭ restrike at 80 (closes c296's optional point)

From the expression side, the restrike at 80 b1 falls on the bar where the contrabassoon, bass clarinet, Bsn 2 and basses also re-attack D♭, and the cellos and violas move. It underlines the bass, not the violins' tied B♭6. A tie (`des2.:32\f~`) would blur that attack. Keep the notation as it is; I recommend closing the point.

### 6. Recorded: Vln II part "Largamente" placement at 61 (c285's open point)

At 61 the Vln II part stacks "Largamente", "poco allargando" (60), "molto sostenuto" and "sul A" above the staff. In the 130 dpi render they are four separate lines, each clear of the others and in the right bar. The stack is tall but nothing collides, and lowering it would push "sul A" into the notes. No change; the point can be closed.
