# Reference books

These are the books Claude consulted while composing and critiquing the symphony. Each critic had to cite passages from them. The books themselves (PDFs and extracted page text) are not in this repository because they are under copyright; obtain them from their publishers or a library.

| Book | Author | Edition used | Short name in the project's notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| *The Study of Orchestration* | Samuel Adler | 2nd edition, W. W. Norton, 1989 (in two parts) | `adler1`, `adler2` |
| *Harmony and Voice Leading* | Edward Aldwell and Carl Schachter | 3rd edition, 2003 | `aldwell-schachter` |
| *Principles of Orchestration* | Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov | Kalmus reprint of the Agate translation (the file was named "principles of form") | `rimsky-principles` |
| *The Contemporary Arranger* | Don Sebesky | 1979 | `sebesky` |
| *Behind Bars: The Definitive Guide to Music Notation* | Elaine Gould | Faber, 2011 (excerpt: front matter, chapter 1 and the start of chapter 2) | `gould-behind-bars` |
| *The Hyperorchestra* | Sergi Casanelles | 2024 | `hyper-orchestra` |
| *Guidelines for Music Preparation* | Major Orchestra Librarians' Association (MOLA) | 2017 revision | `mola` |

`INVENTORY.md` is the guide Claude wrote to these books: chapter-to-page maps, the quality of the extracted text, and where to look for each topic. It refers to a `pdfs/` folder and a `reference-books/text/` folder of per-page text; both were removed from the repository and its history. Citations in the critics' reports (for example "adler1 p. 132") use the short names above with PDF page numbers, and `INVENTORY.md` gives the offset to printed page numbers.

`symphony/reading/orchestration-books.md` is a wider reading list that was compiled at the start of the project.
